### Papers

All three are now child pages under Papers, with rows at the top of the index.

- **[new] Bitune: Leveraging Bidirectional Attention to Improve Decoder-Only LLMs** (Kopiczko, Blankevoort, Asano; [arXiv:2405.14862](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14862), May 2024, v2 Aug 2025; **EMNLP 2025 main**). The causal mask exists for generation, but the prompt is fully available when processed, so masking it discards information for free. Two passes over the prompt, causal and bidirectional, with **separate K/V projections** so the model can treat the two feature types differently, mixed per block with learned coefficients. Generation stays causal. Consistent gains on commonsense reasoning, arithmetic and language understanding; compatible with PEFT and full finetuning. Costs roughly double prompt-processing compute, which is why it has not spread at long context.
- **[new] LLM Modules: Knowledge Transfer from a Large to a Small Model using Enhanced Cross-Attention** (Kolomeitsev; [arXiv:2502.08213](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08213), Feb 2025). Frozen Qwen2-1.5B feeding a trainable GPT-Neo-125M through inserted cross-attention layers, as a composable alternative to distillation. Author reports quality comparable to distillation after 15 epochs on Bespoke-Stratos-17k. Filed with an explicit caution: single author, tiny scale, qualitative evaluation against his own baseline. Written up as a pointer to Flamingo and CALM rather than as evidence.
- **[new] Trained Persistent Memory for Frozen Encoder-Decoder LLMs: Six Architectural Methods** (Jeong; [arXiv:2603.16413](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16413), Mar 2026). Memory in the frozen model's **continuous latent space** rather than in text, so writes and reads are differentiable; six variants across three injection points and four write mechanisms, with the bank accumulating at inference without gradients. LoCoMo forgetting curve: stateless baseline exactly zero, all six adapters positive at 10x capacity, weaker at 1x. Self-described pilot study; no RAG or text-memory baseline, single backbone.

### The through-line

The three arrived as an unordered list, but they are one idea at three attachment points: **keep the pretrained model frozen and add trainable structure around it**. Bitune adds a second attention pass over the prompt, LLM Modules adds a small model reading the frozen one's internals, and Trained Persistent Memory adds a differentiable memory bank. Noted on each page under Connections so the batch stays legible later.

Quality is uneven and the pages say so. Bitune is peer-reviewed and the other two are small single-author preprints, so their pages lead with what the evidence does and does not support rather than burying it.
