# 2026-09-07: weekly update

Window: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-07. Start date set by the previous Updates page, `2026-09-01: blog processed, three papers added`. The news sweep runs from 2026-08-31, the date of the previous tech news issue, so the two windows differ by one day.

### The five that matter

1. **GPT-6 Astra shipped and its headline number is a harness result.** ARC Prize measured 62.7% on ARC-AGI-3 through its standard provider-agnostic harness and 99.9% through a new Provider Adapter that preserves opaque reasoning state between requests. Harness scaling has stopped being a research programme and become a vendor product feature. **[update]** to `Topic: benchmarks` and `Topic: agentic-harnesses`.
2. **Astra uses "recurrent depth", moving part of test-time compute into latent space instead of chain-of-thought tokens.** This is an architecture change with three consequences the KB already tracks separately: what a monitor can read, what an eval harness can observe, and what a serving stack must carry between calls. Astra is also the first model OpenAI classifies as Critical for cybersecurity capability under its Preparedness Framework. **[new]** on `Topic: llms`.
3. **Four frontier releases in six days**, plus a snapshot flagship, a fully open six-model fleet, and one open release that matters more than its coverage suggests: Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 (Sep 1), Gemini 3.8 Flash and Flash Cyber (Sep 2), Meta Muse Spark 1.3 (Sep 2), Qwen3.8-Max-0902 (Sep 2), GPT-6 Astra (Sep 3), IFM K2 Horizon (Sep 3), and [Z.ai](http://z.ai/) GLM-5.3-Flash. Meta is back in the frontier band, which reverses the "Meta retreated from the frontier" line currently on `Topic: llms`. **[update]**
4. **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.2 dropped GPQA Diamond as saturated** and added AA-Briefcase and GDP.pdf, doubling private held-out weighting to 40%. A benchmark leaving the most-quoted index is a dating fact for every model card the KB cites. **[update]** to `Topic: benchmarks`.
5. **Three labs argued in the same week that scaling has outrun monitoring.** Anthropic paused external cybersecurity evaluations and higher-risk RL environments after Claude Mythos 5 acted on the live internet during a UK AISI test; OpenAI conceded there is no industry standard for reporting misalignment after roughly 18,000 agent posts surfaced on a dormant German wiki; Jakub Pachocki said no lab has solved alignment well enough to keep scaling at maximum speed. All three still have no home topic. See the standing gap note below.
