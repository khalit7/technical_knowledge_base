#### Production notes

**Two costs the published cut could not have known about, and both are spent at the head of a beat.** A focus fades 0.25 seconds per handle lighting the parked map before it draws anything, and a handle is an item as well as a column heading, so this map of four columns and fifteen items is nineteen handles and 4.75 seconds gone from the front of split, vector and convergence. And a points heading is a reveal of its own: it is drawn at t=0, where it cannot lead, and still takes a share of the drawing budget, so it pushes every row below it one place later. check_leads models the focus delay now and did not when this episode shipped, which is most of the difference between a report that looked clean and one with eleven leads in it.

**The cheap fixes covered seven of the eight beats, and they made the episode better rather than merely legal.** Reserves first: map to 7.5, split to 4.0, vector to 4.5, convergence to 4.8, close from 2.0 to 4.5. Then rows, where no legal reserve could reach: split went from five reveals to seven, vector from six to eight, convergence from seven to nine. Every added row is a sentence the beat already speaks and that had nothing to land on, which is why the panels read better than they did: the compression claim buried inside the scan sentence, the put-it-on-a-replica conclusion that closes the trade, Elasticsearch adding dense vectors, feature stores turning out to be two databases. Two headings went as well. The vector panel lost its heading outright, because that beat says two things inside its first eight seconds and the heading was taking the slot the first of them needed. The convergence heading became the panel's last row, which is where the narration says it anyway.

**Only the map's words moved, ten of them, and none were cut.** A columns panel has exactly one reveal per column, so it cannot gain a row without gaining a column, and the map is the axis of the episode. With the reserve already near the parked ceiling the only lever left was the order of the words. The fourth column's category name now arrives after its gloss rather than before it, which is eleven words of runway, and the second column gains a sentence in front of it. One thing worth having written down: a column's reveal is timed from the first of its labels the narration says, head or item, so moving the product names in front of the category name buys nothing at all, because S Q Lite simply becomes the thing said too early. That take was rerolled once. The first came back at a character error of 0.062 with Relational and Document silently dropped and in your process heard as and a server, directly contradicting the no server at all it follows. Neither gate would have failed it; reading the transcript did.

**The reserve was swept against both gates at once rather than fitted to the leads and checked afterwards.** A bigger reserve always buys lead margin and always spends still-frame margin, and the two limits are 3.0 seconds and 6.0 seconds. Fitting the leads alone put the map at 8.3, just under the 8.4 parked ceiling, and it rendered a 5.95 second still frame against a 6.0 second limit. That passes and it is not a margin. Sweeping instead, and taking the value furthest from either limit, put it at 7.5: a 5.15 second still frame and a worst lead of 1.9. Measured on this episode's own render, still runs about reserve plus 0.4 on an ordinary beat and reserve minus 2.35 on the parked one, and both held to within a twentieth of a second on the re-render.
