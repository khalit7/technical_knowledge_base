### Papers

- **[new] WSM: Decay-Free Learning Rate Schedule via Checkpoint Merging for LLM Pre-training** (Tian et al., Ant Group Ling team, [arXiv:2507.17634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17634), July 2025). Establishes a formal equivalence between LR decay and checkpoint merging: merging with weights `c_j` is the same as applying gradient weights `w_i=\sum_{j\ge i}c_j`, and the map inverts uniquely, so any monotone decay curve becomes a merge recipe. Constant LR plus merging beat a matched WSD decay by ~1.3 points average on a 16.3B/1.4B-active MoE over 400B tokens, and the advantage survived SFT. Merge duration dominates checkpoint interval and merge count; EMA merging is the weakest, consistent with convex decay curves being the worst shape. Added a row at the top of the Papers index.
