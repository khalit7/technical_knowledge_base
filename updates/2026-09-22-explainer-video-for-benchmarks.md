#### Production notes

Nothing was invented for narrative shape. A 5,859-word page with a seventy-row master table does not fit in six minutes, so a great deal was cut rather than compressed, and the script's docstring records what. Two whole episodes are sitting in what was left out and are worth naming: the cost axis, which spans five orders of magnitude on the ARC family alone, 44% on ARC-AGI-1 for about sixty-seven cents against the $18,817 and $26,098 Astra runs, with EdgeBench and cost-per-hour beside it; and the integrity crisis, where Berkeley RDI's scanning agent reached near-perfect scores on eight agent benchmarks by reward hacking. Also cut: tau2-bench and pass^k, which is a genuinely new metric and the omission that hurts most; SWE-rebench; the retrieval-index result; LiveCodeBench and the live/rolling family; HarnessDev; reward-hacking activation monitors; and the two vendor claims the page carries as unverifiable.

The inventory axis came from the cut of this episode that was cleared when the method changed, and it was the one thing worth keeping from it. Sorting the map by subject instead, knowledge then maths then coding then agentic, is the page's own taxonomy block read aloud: twelve branches, nothing said about any of them, and a map that is a list rather than an argument. The first draft of this cut made exactly that mistake and had to be rebuilt.

Two defects were found by the checks rather than by watching. **A screen reference pointed at an empty frame.** The map beat opened on "Here is the whole board", and `check_references` pulled that frame: the title strip and nothing else. A beat's reveals are spread across its whole length, so its first line cannot point at its own panel, and `reserve` holds back the tail rather than the head. The line lost its deixis. **A chart implied a ranking nobody made.** The ARC Prize bars were drawn as context against verified, which says the adapter run is the real score; ARC Prize's published position is that the two measure different questions. Both bars are now measured-number and machinery colours, so the colour carries the distinction without the judgement. That one was found by pulling frames out of the silent preview, which is what frames are still for now that the layout audit catches collisions.

On length, which is a deviation worth stating. The skill says plan at about 150 words a minute and aim to land under six; the narration lands at 5.99 minutes and the file at 6:09 and 4.44 MiB, so it delivers at 1080p, one rung down the rate-factor ladder. Two further cuts were considered and refused: dropping the third-column beat leaves a map column named and never toured, which is the one thing an overview's home frame must not do, and dropping the convergence removes what the format calls its most valuable half minute.
