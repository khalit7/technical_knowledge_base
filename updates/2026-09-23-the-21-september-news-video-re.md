#### Production notes

**Thirteen of the fourteen were fixed without touching a spoken word, which is the whole point of doing the arithmetic before reaching for the microphone.** check_leads --reserves named the eight beats a reserve could not reach, and the vocabulary did the rest. Two points panels lost their heading: a heading is drawn at t=0 and still takes a share of the drawing budget, so on s2_credit the first real row landed 8.9 seconds after the line naming it, and moving that heading down to be the last row put it where the narration actually says it. s2_cost gained a third compare side, s3_bugs a fifth row and the coda a sixth, so each beat's closing sentence has a reveal to arrive with instead of overrunning the reserve. Total cost: one manim render, no GPU.

**On a claim or a stat the note is the only lever, and the rule is that it has to paraphrase the beat's last sentence.** Five of them paraphrased a sentence from the middle instead, which is unfixable by any reserve, because reveal n lands at beat minus reserve whatever n is. Re-pointing the note is the same class of edit as adding a row and costs the same nothing. s4_open is the clearest: the study's shape, eight worlds and ten agents and sixteen days, is said eleven seconds in and was sitting in the note, twelve seconds behind the words; it is now in the caption, which is drawn with the number at the top of the beat, and the note carries the sentence the beat actually ends on. Two of the five, on s1_caveat and on the close, were leads the checker never reported at all, because the note was never said as a contiguous run and so was listed as untimed. Untimed is not passed, and both were real: 11.7 and 12.6 seconds, hand-timed.

**One beat needed words, and the arithmetic says exactly why no cheaper fix existed.** s1_horizon says twelve hours nine seconds before the beat ends. The last reveal always lands at beat minus reserve, so closing that wanted a reserve of 6.25 against a still-frame ceiling of about 5.6, and the usual escape, adding a row, is closed off here because a fourth bar would make the line the bottom bar is a hundred and eighty times the top point at the wrong bar. So its closing sentence was trimmed by five words. Trimming a beat after its last item is named is lead-safe by construction, because every reveal moves earlier while the words naming them do not move at all, and it is the only word edit that cannot open a new lead somewhere else. One beat re-rendered, one reseed, 152 words a minute, character error 0.026.

**What the checks say now.** check_leads: every timed reveal is named at or after the moment it is drawn. Ten reveals cannot be timed, because the narration never says the label as a contiguous run; six of those are the first reveal of their beat and are drawn at t=0, so they cannot lead by construction, and the other four were hand-timed and are between 1.0 and 6.7 seconds on the safe side. check_timing: no overlap, no silence over four seconds, 149 words a minute overall with no beat above 164, and every still frame between 2.5 and 5.88 seconds against a limit of six. check_structure and the layout audit are clean. check_references returns twelve hits, of which most are ordinary non-deictic English; the real pointers were read on their frames, and watch the bars now lands with the first bar drawn rather than two seconds before it.

**One pre-existing defect is left alone and is worth writing down.** s1_caveat says take the direction of that chart seriously, and the chart left the screen two beats earlier. That is a screen reference rather than a lead, it was in the published cut, and fixing it means re-rolling a clean take for something outside this repair. The card under it now reads take the direction; hold each value loosely, which is the sentence the line is making, so the frame at least says the thing the words say.
