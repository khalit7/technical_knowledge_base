### Papers

- **[new]** [Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer (T5)](../papers/2019-10_t5/summary.md) (11 min read · +4h 12m resources). Raffel et al., Google, October 2019 / JMLR 2020. Full paper-page treatment: text-to-text framing, the encoder-decoder baseline and its three lasting architecture changes, span corruption, and the ablation grid (architecture, objective, C4 and its filtering, data repetition, fine-tuning strategy, multi-task mixing, scaling), then the 11B final models. Indexed in date order between Scaling Laws (2020) and ZeRO (2019) rather than at the top of the table, since this is a backfill of an older paper and not a new release. ### Topic pages patched (added later the same day)

Khalid asked for the fold-in after the Papers work was done, so this happened in a second pass.

- **`llm-training-and-post-training`**** / **[Pretraining](../topics/llm-training-and-post-training/pretraining.md) (7 to 8 min read). The Objective section listed "Span corruption / UL2: T5-style denoising" with no explanation of what the objective does, which is exactly the pattern the 2026-08-31 decision bans. Now explained (sentinel replacement, why targets are short, what UL2 generalises), plus a new dated paragraph on what the objective comparison is actually worth: large gaps between objective families, near-zero gaps within the denoising family, so choose on compute cost. Topic root's Key papers list also gained a T5 line.
- **`data-curation-and-datasets`**** / **[Pretraining corpora: lineage and current landscape](../topics/data-curation-and-datasets/pretraining-corpora.md) (8 to 9 min read). The lineage table had C4 as a row but the page nowhere recorded the evidence its own "what separates the generations" argument depends on. Added the unfiltered-C4 result (8x more text, worst on every task) as the founding measurement for filtering over volume, and the data-repetition result (64 repeats harmless, 1,024 costs about 4 GLUE points with training loss falling) as the origin of the token-horizon rule.
- [Topic: llms](../topics/llms/summary.md). Added to Related papers rather than the body, since that page is a map of who builds what today: T5 as the encoder-decoder fork, with the note that every family in the table is a decoder despite the evidence of the time pointing the other way, and that the reasons are about in-context learning and serving cost rather than quality.
Estimate lines updated on both deep dives, the data-curation topic root (both places it links to pretraining-corpora), and the KB root (whole-KB prose 32h 44m to 32h 46m, llm-training subtree 1h 36m to 1h 37m, data-curation subtree 40 to 41 min).
