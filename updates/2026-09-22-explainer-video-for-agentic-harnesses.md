#### Production notes

Nothing was invented for narrative shape. A 5,838-word page does not fit in seven minutes, so a great deal was cut rather than compressed, and the script's docstring records what: Z.ai's feedback-design account and Nous Research's 1,393 subagents, which together are the page's running-agents-at-scale section; the skills-and-environments supply chain of WikiSkill, Repo-To-Skill, Terminal-Universe and HarnessDev; JIT-Agent; Sierra's 23.9 against 82.2; Fugu as orchestration sold as a model; Origin; and the AGENTS.md settlement. This page arguably wants two episodes, one on the shelf and one on harness scaling, and it got one. Research scaffolds, the fifth group in the page's own taxonomy, are deliberately not on the map, because SWE-agent and OpenHands are not products you choose between and a fifth column drives the map's labels below what the delivery encode can show.

Three defects were found by the checks rather than by watching, and all three were silent. **Two beats were read too fast.** The voice renderer rejects a take above 165 words a minute however clean it scores, and it reseeded four times on both before shipping one at 179. The fix was in the writing, not the audio: the commas became full stops and nothing was cut, and both beats came back at 155. **A screen reference pointed at an empty frame.** The listener opened the scores beat with "who measured these", one second in, before the chart existed; `check_references` pulled that exact frame and it was blank. A beat's first line cannot point at its own panel, so the question lost its deictic. **Two takes said something the script did not**, and the error rate hid both by averaging. "That turns the whole question into review" came back as "Zed turns the whole question into review", a single substituted word that misattributed cloud review to an editor and scored a word error rate of 0.01; and a line ending on a bare "ninety nine point nine" grew an invented syllable after it. Both were caught by reading the transcripts in `video/out/audio/<episode>/verification.json` as text, which is the honest substitute for listening, and both were fixed by ending the line on a word the model cannot run past.

One thing to carry to the next overview: at seven minutes the delivery encode steps down two rungs of the ladder, from a rate factor of 28 to 34, to fit Notion's cap. It stays at 1080p and the file is 4.56 MiB, but the picture is softer than it needs to be, and the fix is a shorter episode rather than a coarser one.
