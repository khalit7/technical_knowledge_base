#### Production notes

**The focus delay is what the first cut could not see.** A beat carrying focus spends a quarter of a second per map handle at its head before it draws anything, and this map registers seventeen handles, five, six and three items plus three headings, so thinking, serving and convergence each lose 4.25 seconds off the front. The first cut was fitted before the tool modelled that. Re-measured on a fresh render of the unchanged script, convergence's first real row, not total parameters, landed 4.3 seconds after the words, and close had 10.1 and 6.8 second leads the old report had already shown.

**No reserve could reach either beat, so each gained two rows for sentences it already spoke.** check_leads --reserves asked for 14.5 and 11.3 seconds against a still-frame ceiling of 5.5. Both beats ended with sentences that had nothing drawn under them: convergence's the smallest active count in the frontier band and it keeps ninety eight percent of the score, and close's whole Sakana orchestrator sentence and its last line. Those became rows, the smallest active count and it keeps 98% of the score, then Sakana: an orchestrator and which model stops mattering. Eight reveals instead of six re-spaces every landing on the beat, and because the last reveal always lands at the beat's length minus its reserve, the rows had to name sentences near the end of the line; a row in the middle would have left the final landing where it was.

**Then every reserve went to the midpoint between the lead floor and the still-frame cap.** That is what --reserves suggests now, rather than the bare minimum. Map 7.5 to 6.6, question 5.0 to 3.2, serving 5.0 to 3.3, convergence 5.0 to 2.8, close 5.0 to 3.9; thinking and leads stay at 5.0, where they already sat inside both gates.
