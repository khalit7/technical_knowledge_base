WHAT TO DO, in every tab of the page (Reading and every other tab except Further reading, which stays as it is):
1. **Split layout.** Every section (or group of sections sharing one visual) that has a visual becomes `.s3sp` with the text in `.s3L` and the visual in `.s3R` (animated stage or animation card first in the DOM; a static figure or chart card after the text, inside `.s3side`). A section with no visual stays full width. Do not invent a visual to fill a column. Copy `.claude/skills/explainer-page/build/templates/02_split.html` (and `25_js_s3.js`, renderer v2, when the page has a stage) into `src/parts/` unchanged. Include `02_split.html` after the page's own CSS parts and before the header (in some pages `02_css.html` or `01_css.html` closes `<head>`; that is fine). If `build.sh` lists parts explicitly (a `JS=` or `HTML=` list), add `02_split.html` to the HTML list there and `25_js_s3.js` to the JS list after the data and engine parts, before the scene files. A lab tab splits too: its notes on the left, controls plus chart on the right; a lab with no prose stays full width. Grep the page's CSS before adding any class.
2. **3D where reasonable, never forced, in the v2 style.** Read `visuals/three-d.md` in full, then the reference scene `technical_knowledge_base/whats_new/papers/attention_is_all_you_need_transformer/src/parts/33_js_sc_att.js` (Fig. 3: a few large objects per step, the camera gliding in close with `view` when a step is about one item, glass plates for matrices, flowing dots wherever data moves, signed bars on white plinths). Khalid likes this motion and style; every stage you build should look and move like it. For each visual, ask whether the concept has spatial structure that depth would show better (stacks, grids, state spaces, value surfaces over a grid, trees of search, tensors, many matrices as bar fields, agents moving in an environment). Where yes, build a 3D stage with the template `25_js_s3.js` (copy unchanged) and one scene file per stage, driven by the page's own data or engines (real numbers the page already computes; never invented values). Keep 2D what reads best flat (learning curves, regret curves, scatters, log axes, heatmaps, tables). Khalid prefers having a 3D visual to not having one: aim for at least the hero and the one or two most spatial mechanisms in 3D when they genuinely fit, and say in the report why other visuals stayed 2D.
3. **Long replays (more than about 12 frames: an episode walked state by state, hundreds of pulls or episodes) are never one step per frame** (2026-11-06). Either (a) the motion is the point: group it into at most about 8 explained stretches, one `ol.sx` item per stretch, each stretch playing its frames during its step, with the normal autoplay rules (the four-learner stage on Model-free prediction and control); or (b) the outcome of a run the reader drives is the point (seeds, a learning rate, a 500-episode curve): it is a widget, with no autoplay and no step list, opening on an informative frame and explained by the paragraph on its left. A live per-frame caption may sit under either, but is never the only explanation.
   **Every animation and stepper is explained on the left** by an `ol.sx` step list, one item per step (a bold short title, then what the step shows and why), linked to the visual: `S3.stage({list:...})` for 3D, `RD.anim({..., list:...})` from the template `21_js_rd_common.js` for 2D, or the same behaviour added to the page's own controller (paper pages use `makeAnim` in `11_js_ui.js` and `90_js_tabs.js`; tech-news issues `10b_js_anim.js`; some pages their own, such as `U.anim` or `22_js_rd_common.js`): copy `bindList`, `reset` and the autoplay state from `RD.anim` in `21_js_rd_common.js`, and have the controller set `data-playing` on its card while it plays. Move the per-step caption text into the list rather than duplicating it.
4. **Motion rules:** every animation and stage plays by itself once at least half in view, pauses off screen, resumes on return, stops for good on any reader control or at the last step, never moves by itself under reduced motion. Replace `21_js_rd_common.js` and `99_js_tabs.js` with the templates (keep the page's storage key) or give the page's own controllers these rules. Invert any never-autoplay assertion in the page's own check scripts.
5. **Improve the explanation** where the new visual changes what the text should say (a paragraph a visual now carries becomes a caption or a step; a step list replaces a caption carousel). Keep every non-fat ledger unit (ledger check problems 0; update a key when you reword its sentence). Update the header's reading minutes if they change (measure with `html_utils/audit_pages.mjs`).
6. **Checks** (all must pass before you report): `sh src/build.sh`; `sh html_utils/checkpage.sh <page>` ends `fail=0, emdash 0, errbox 1, clipped 0` (it now also shoots 1400 px light); the page's own check scripts; your own puppeteer script that, at 1400 px light, 920 px light and 390 px dark, scrolls each stage and animation into view and confirms it plays (S3: `data-frames` rises; RD.anim: the play button reads Pause), steps each to its last step, confirms its step list has one item per step, and with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` confirms nothing moves by itself; no errors, NaN, undefined or sideways scroll. Look at the screenshots: every tab at 1400 light and 390 dark, every stage at a mid step; fix overlapping labels, scenes floating in empty space (raise the camera, set `aspect`), clutter on phones.
7. **Report** additionally: a table of sections (tab, section, layout split or full width, visual kind 3D / 2D / image / none, step list yes or no), the 3D stages built and what data drives them, visuals deliberately kept 2D and why, and the screenshot paths of each stage mid-step at 1400 light and 390 dark (put copies under `{{SCRATCH}}/<page slug>/`, outside the repo).
