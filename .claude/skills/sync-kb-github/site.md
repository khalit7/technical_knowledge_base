# The reading site

Since 2026-10-23 the knowledge base is read on a public static site built from this repo: https://khalit7.github.io/technical_knowledge_base/ (GitHub Pages). Khalid chose public: anyone may read it; only he can change it, because only he can push to the repo. Notion keeps an index that links to the site, plus the videos.

`html_utils/build_site.py` writes the site (a build output, gitignored), Pagefind adds full-text search, and `.github/workflows/site.yml` builds and deploys it on every push to main that touches a page. Publishing a page is therefore: build it, check it, commit, push.

## What the build does
- Copies every page's `index.html` (and its `img/` folder) to `<out>/<path under technical_knowledge_base>/index.html`.
- Adds a small bar at the top of each page: home, breadcrumb (topics by their name, with a dot in the section colour), search.
- Rewrites links to Notion pages that are in `pages.json` into site links (same tab). Notion URLs left inside script data go through `p/<id>/`, a redirect page; ids not in `pages.json` redirect back to Notion.
- Writes the home page (1120 px wide, prose capped near 68ch, system fonts): a header with the title, a one-line tagline, a count line (pages, topics, sections, hours of core reading, computed by the build) over a static soft glow, Pagefind as the main search (`/` focuses it), the learning map in a panel with a faded dot grid, and the whole tree by section in a closed block "All pages by section" (section cards in two balanced columns). Dark mode adds a very light static grain behind everything.
- Topic names: a topic page titled `Topic: llm-architecture` shows as "LLM architecture" on the home page, in the map and in the page bar (`learning_map.topic_name`: acronyms LLM, ML, RAG, GPU, CUDA, JAX, TPU, SWE, PyTorch in their usual case; `NAME_FIX` for names the rule cannot make, such as "Reinforcement learning").
- Section colours (home page and page bar only; teaching pages have their own palette): light `#3072c1 #b0540e #1c8742 #8559b2 #ae4976`, dark `#7cb4fc #ee9a69 #76c788 #c39dee #ed90b5` (blue, orange, green, purple, rose), defined in `learning_map.CSS` and `build_site.SEC_LIGHT`/`SEC_DARK`; the check holds them to 4.5:1 as pill text and 3:1 as lines and bars in both themes.
- The second argument is the base path: `/technical_knowledge_base/` on GitHub Pages.

## The learning map
The home page opens with a map of the order to study in, built by `html_utils/learning_map.py` from the `"prereqs"` field of `technical_knowledge_base/pages.json`.
- **Data**: every topic page (status `html_only`, not Papers or Tech news) has `"prereqs"`: the ids of the pages a reader cannot understand its core without (strict; a subset of its "You need" line, never every link). A new page must get a `"prereqs"` entry when it is registered (`[]` when it needs nothing).
- **What it shows**: the overview is a layered graph of the topics, in study order from top to bottom. Topic A sits in a layer below topic B when at least a third of A's pages build on B (a page of B is in the page's prerequisite closure; the share is the constant `STRONG` in `learning_map.py`), and a solid line joins them, thicker when a larger share of A's pages do (tooltip "x of n pages build on it"); weaker links (some pages, under a third) are not drawn until a topic is focused, then dotted, with the card's "Some pages also use" list. Layer 1, "Layer 1: start here, in any order", holds every topic that builds on no other this way: those with no strong link either way in its upper rows, and the topics later layers build on in its bottom row next to the lines, so no line crosses a card. Layers order topics; stages order pages. Each card gives pages and hours and its pages per stage as small pills under the stage numbers (a page's stage is its longest prerequisite chain across all topics, so a topic in a later layer can still have stage-1 pages); hovering or tapping a card lights it, what it builds on and what builds on it, draws the lines into it and runs dots along them from the topics it builds on until the focus leaves. Clicking a topic grows the card into its pages by stage (pages from other topics it needs as a dashed first column); "Path to" gives any page's prerequisite closure in a valid study order with total and per-topic minutes, stepped only when the reader presses Step or Play. From about 1080 px the page uses a wider drawing of the same overview (built twice at build time, chosen by the panel's width, swapped on resize). Below 900 px the same layers are rows of cards (layer 1 lists its linked topics last) with "Builds on" text. Everything is laid out at build time (layers, barycenter and exhaustive crossing reduction); the page carries one inline SVG and a small JSON, no library.
- **Motion**: on each page load, when the map is on screen, the home page plays one short reveal (layers rise top to bottom, cards 25 ms apart within a layer and layers 120 ms apart, the strong lines draw from upper to lower topic, then the key; about 1.1 s, then still, and any click, key or hover finishes it; a reader who lands scrolled past it sees it finished, and returning from a topic never replays it). Cards answer the pointer: hover or keyboard focus grows a card to 1.025 on a gentle spring (CSS `linear()`, about 3 % overshoot) and fades in a deeper pre-drawn shadow, a press shrinks it to .985, focus adds a ring in the section colour, and after a 60 ms hover intent unrelated topics fade to .38 (no blur). Opening a topic grows the card's outline into the view and its columns stagger in left to right; the current path step gets one soft halo. On touch there are no hover effects, only the press. No idle motion: nothing moves after the reveal until the reader acts. This one-time reveal is Khalid's explicit choice for the landing page only; the teaching pages still never autoplay. Everything else moves only when the reader acts (focus, opening a topic, Step and Play), each change under about 600 ms and interruptible; `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all movement (no reveal, growth, press, dots or staggering) and keeps the colour and opacity feedback as instant state changes.
- **A new topic or section**: a new topic page appears on the map by itself; check its name on the map reads well (names come from the `Topic: ...` title through `learning_map.topic_name`; add any new acronym to `ACRONYMS`), and a new section needs an entry in `SECTIONS` (both `learning_map.py` and `build_site.py`) and a colour token `--sN` in both themes in `learning_map.py`, checked for contrast by `check_learning_map.mjs`.
- **Check before pushing**: `python3 html_utils/learning_map.py check`. An unknown id, a self-loop or a cycle fails the site build (and so the deploy); a page without `"prereqs"`, an old id (alias), a redundant edge, or a prereq the page's own "You need" line no longer links are warnings. After changing the map's code, run `node html_utils/check_learning_map.mjs <scratch>` (builds to scratch, checks every card's layer and numbers against the rule recomputed from pages.json, that layer 1 holds every topic with no strong link and no other band exists, that every strong link is drawn and no weak one before a focus, the stage pills and their labels, the focus sets and that focus dots stop on blur, links, path order and topic strip, that the load reveal ends in exactly the reduced-motion layout and nothing moves after it until a click, that reduced motion animates nothing, moves nothing on load and does not grow a hovered card while the fade stays; topic names on every card, phone card and topic view and no slug text; hover growth to 1.025 and the .38 fade, restored on leave; no animation after the reveal; the wide drawing at 1280 px with every strong line card to card; section colour contrast in both themes; saves screenshots, including reveal and mid-step frames).

Preview locally exactly as deployed:
```
python3 html_utils/build_site.py <scratch>/site/technical_knowledge_base /technical_knowledge_base/
(cd html_utils && NPM_CONFIG_USERCONFIG= npx -y pagefind@1.3.0 --site <scratch>/site/technical_knowledge_base)
(cd <scratch>/site && python3 -m http.server 8732 --bind 127.0.0.1)   then open http://127.0.0.1:8732/technical_knowledge_base/
```

## Notion
Notion keeps the same tree; each page is one link line to `p/<id>/` on the site, plus its video and child pages ([SKILL.md](SKILL.md)). The `p/<id>/` redirect pages are written by the build for every page in `pages.json`, so a Notion link never breaks when a page moves in the repo.
