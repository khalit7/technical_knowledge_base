# Visual and text trim candidates, 2026-11-19

**Nothing here is removed or changed until Khalid approves that page's list.** Each line is a candidate, not a decision; approve, strike or edit lines page by page, then a build session acts only on the approved lines (ledger-checked, so no knowledge is lost).

## Method

Generated by `python3 html_utils/visual_candidates.py --out audit/2026-11-19_visual_candidates.md` (rerunnable; the criteria and thresholds are documented at the top of the script). It parses each page's built `index.html` statically, the JS in `src/parts` that draws each figure, `src/viz_ideas.md`, `OWNERS.md` and the layout rollout record. Kinds:

1. **Step list repeats prose**: a default step list against the paragraphs beside it (its section or its split's left column, whichever is smaller). A sentence counts as restated when 70% of its content words, weighted by how rare each word is on the page, occur on the other side. Flagged when one side's restated sentences hold 40 words or more and make up half the paragraphs or 35% of the list. Saved minutes are that side's restated words / 230; the action cuts the restated paragraph sentences, or shortens restated steps to their titles (the split layout needs a list that explains the figure).
2. **Table repeats a figure**: a table made mostly of numbers whose distinctive numbers (decimals, or 3+ digit integers that are not years or round) are drawn by a figure on the same tab (60% and at least 4 in the same section, or 80% and at least 6 anywhere). The table is the candidate; the figure keeps the data.
3. **Figure on two pages**: two pages' figures share rare distinctive numbers (seen in figures on at most 8 pages) and, mostly, alike captions. The copy on the page that does not own the concept (OWNERS.md) is the candidate; without an owner row Khalid picks.
4. **Figure echoes its text**: a static, non-stage schematic whose label words are 90% in the text beside it, with at most 2 numbers missing and no chart, measurement or 3+ digit number in its caption.
5. **3D step to fix**: steps the rollout record calls faded, faint, pale, busy, crowded, cluttered or dense. Fixes, not removals; no minutes. Polish-notes items may already be fixed by the cosmetic pass: check first.
6. **Closed blocks**: no single closed `<details>` reaches 10 minutes (the largest is about 8), so pages whose closed blocks add up to over 20 minutes are listed with their largest blocks. These are depth minutes (only readers who open them pay), kept apart from core minutes.
7. **Numbers only in the figure or table** are on each candidate's "Move first" line: they go into the caption or text before anything is removed.
8. **3D not needed (convert to 2D or drop)** (Khalid's addition): a stage whose scene header, caption or viz_ideas row speaks of lanes, parties, a sequence or timeline, a pipeline, a request's path or a map, or calls it schematic or illustrative, and names no structure that depth carries (tensors, matrices, shapes, heads, layers, stacks, surfaces, heights or bars, GPUs, chips, racks, topology, memory, grids, planes, points; scenes with gridded glass plates are skipped). "Strong sign" means lanes, parties, a sequence or a path; "weaker sign" means only the word schematic or illustrative. Not a removal; no minutes.

Never flagged: visuals with real, recorded or published data, reproduced or contradicted published figures, labs with more than one control, predict-then-reveal, the page hero, a concept owner's visual. The build-time viz_ideas score is shown only to sort; it decides nothing. Looking at a figure is assumed to take 0.5 min (figure text is not counted in reading time). Page minutes are a static estimate of the Reading tab's core text.

Limits: tables and step lists that JavaScript builds at run time are not seen (static parse); a figure's numbers come from its static text and the JS around its ids, so a figure fed from a data file is missed by kinds 2 and 3 (they undercount); the word matching is lexical, so a close paraphrase is not counted as a repeat; kind 8 reads descriptions, not geometry, so a stage whose header does not describe its layout is not judged.

What the numbers say: the candidates found here are modest (a minute or two per page at most). The 2026-11-16 measure already leaves hidden alternative-mode step lists out, and most of the growth since October was in those lists; what remains is sentence-level restating between a section's paragraphs and its step list, listed under kind 1.
