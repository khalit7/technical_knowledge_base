# 2026-08-31: tech news

Covering roughly 2026-08-25 to 2026-08-31.

## Top stories

**Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion.** Reported Aug 26, one week after the
Business Insider story that Hugging Face was weighing offers above $13B (covered in the last
issue). Terms are not signed and the deal could still collapse, and Nvidia has not commented,
which is itself notable given how quickly it usually pushes back on reports it considers
wrong. The price is roughly 3x the company's $4.5B 2023 valuation. The obvious question for
anyone who depends on the Hub is neutrality: the default distribution point for open weights,
and its default inference and training integrations, would sit inside the company that sells
the hardware those weights run on.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-closes-in-on-hugging-face-acquisition/)

**Z.ai open-sourced GLM-5.3-Flash, and it turned out to be Ox Alpha.** Released Aug 26 under
MIT: a 320B total / 18B active MoE, natively multimodal (image *and* video in),
1,048,576-token context, weights on the Hub. Z.ai says it beats GLM-5.2 across its own
evaluations at roughly a tenth the cost, listing at $0.15 / $0.50 per million tokens. It is
the same model that appeared unattributed on evaluation platforms the week before as the
stealth entry Ox Alpha, which last week's issue flagged as unidentified: ship anonymously,
collect clean third-party numbers, then claim it.
[MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/26/z-ai-releases-glm-5-3-flash-a-320b-a18b-natively-multimodal-moe-with-a-1m-token-context/),
[SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/26/z-ai-open-sources-ox-alpha-model-as-glm-5-3-flash/)

**Harness scaling became a research programme, and ARC-AGI-3 fell twice.** Prime Intellect's
[Prime Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552) (Aug 24, open source) took ARC-AGI-3 RHAE
Best@1 from 30% to 95.5% by giving the model a persistent IPython REPL, recursive subagents,
and versioned prompts/memories/skills that survive across trajectories. That is the same 30%
baseline Nvidia's Agentic Variation Operators moved to 100% the week before, from an
unrelated direction. Two days later [JIT-Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25593) attacked the
problem from the training side, using a 27B model to synthesise a task-specific harness on
the fly and gaining +7.7 to +8.8 average points across nine agent benchmarks at 14.9-54.1%
*lower* cost. Both are written up in full in [papers/](../papers/INDEX.md).

**Nvidia's quarter said the AI capex cycle has not turned.** Reporting Aug 26 on roughly $92B
of quarterly revenue, close to double a year ago, with Jensen Huang guiding to about 70%
revenue growth for fiscal 2028, well above consensus. CFO Colette Kress said shipments of
approved older parts to China were under 1% of data-center revenue, which quietly removes
China from the growth story entirely. Hyperscaler spot pricing for leading-edge Nvidia
instances was up 7.9% quarter over quarter in August.
[CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html)

**Over a hundred companies signed a joint call on AI-enabled cyber attacks.** OpenAI,
Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft signed alongside CrowdStrike, Okta, Fortinet, financial
institutions, and infrastructure firms (Aug 27), warning that "in the coming months,
AI-enabled cyber attacks will become far more widespread and sophisticated," and asking for
public-private coordination on defence. Read it next to OpenAI's Aug 18 post on pacing
releases against cyber capability thresholds: the labs are converging on the same framing.
[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/openai-anthropic-google-and-100-other-companies-call-for-action-to-defend-against-rogue-ai/)

## Models & releases

- **GLM-5.3-Flash** (Z.ai, Aug 26): see above. MIT, 320B-A18B, 1M context, natively
  multimodal. [Release notes](https://docs.z.ai/release-notes/new-released)
- **DFlash2** (z-lab): a speculative-decoding *drafter* for Qwen3.8-27B reporting up to 3.43x
  output throughput in vLLM or SGLang, with MLX support. It drafts a whole block per forward
  pass using block diffusion, keeps the top candidates at each position, and runs a light
  selector over the lattice; verification is unchanged so decoding stays mathematically
  lossless. First production-shaped use of text diffusion in the serving stack.
  [Model card](https://huggingface.co/incoai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2)
- OpenAI retired the official **DALL-E GPT** inside ChatGPT on Aug 30, pointing users at
  ChatGPT Images; custom GPTs with image generation are unaffected.

- **Google DeepMind ran the first double-blind evaluation of a proprietary model** (Aug 27),
  with the Singapore AI Safety Institute, OpenMined, AVERI, and MLCommons. Weights and
  benchmark prompts go into the same hardware-encrypted enclave, so the lab never sees the
  questions and the evaluator never sees the model. It makes non-contamination a property of
  the execution environment rather than a promise in a contract. Pilot ran on Gemini Flash
  Lite.
  [DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/piloting-the-worlds-first-double-blind-ai-evaluations/)
- **VoiceMem** (Aug 26, 170 upvotes): dual-brain streaming memory for speech language models,
  improving retrieval accuracy in real-time interaction.

## Talk of the town

- **"MCP was declared dead"** was the week's recurring headline, arguing that the 2026-07-28
  spec's removal of the initialize handshake and the `Mcp-Session-Id` header leaves something
  indistinguishable from a plain HTTP API. The practical effect is real and good (any request
  can hit any instance, so sticky routing and shared session stores stop being protocol
  requirements), and the KB already documents the change in
  [topics/protocols/mcp.md](../topics/protocols/mcp.md); the argument is about whether the
  remaining surface justifies a separate protocol.
  [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/mcp-stateless-gateway/)
- **Grok's "cryptographic context injection" is still unpatched**, 11+ weeks after Adversa AI
  disclosed it on June 3. A page carries an AES-encrypted payload that static content filters
  cannot read, Grok decrypts it in its own Python runtime, then follows the revealed
  instructions and appends the user's chat history, name, coarse location, and subscription
  tier to an attacker-controlled URL. About a 40% success rate over 20 attempts, triggered by
  nothing more than asking for a summary of the page. Missed in last week's issue and included
  here because the technique generalises: any agent with a code interpreter can be made to
  decrypt its own exploit. Written up in
  [topics/agentic-harnesses/harness-engineering.md](../topics/agentic-harnesses/harness-engineering.md).
  [Adversa AI](https://adversa.ai/blog/cryptographic-context-injection-grok-data-theft/),
  [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/20/grok-chat-duped-into-swallowing-injected-instructions/)
- **The AI Overviews traffic data everyone was passing around** (Aug 28): across 54
  advertisers, organic search sessions fell 10.5% while referrals from AI platforms rose 163%,
  and AI-sourced visitors completed key events at roughly 1.5x the organic rate, with
  AI-driven key events up 335%. Less traffic, better traffic, is the summary most people took
  from it.
