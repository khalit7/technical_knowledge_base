## Top stories

**Nvidia agreed to buy Hugging Face for $12.9 billion.** Reported Aug 26, one week after the Business Insider story that Hugging Face was weighing offers above $13B (covered in the last issue). Terms are not signed and the deal could still collapse, and Nvidia has not commented, which is itself notable given how quickly it usually pushes back on reports it considers wrong. The price is roughly 3x the company's $4.5B 2023 valuation. The obvious question for anyone who depends on the Hub is neutrality: the default distribution point for open weights, and its default inference and training integrations, would sit inside the company that sells the hardware those weights run on. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-closes-in-on-hugging-face-acquisition/)

**Z.ai**** open-sourced GLM-5.3-Flash, and it turned out to be Ox Alpha.** Released Aug 26 under MIT: a 320B total / 18B active MoE, natively multimodal (image *and* video in), 1,048,576-token context, weights on the Hub. Z.ai says it beats GLM-5.2 across its own evaluations at roughly a tenth the cost, listing at $0.15 / $0.50 per million tokens. It is the same model that appeared unattributed on evaluation platforms the week before as the stealth entry Ox Alpha, which last week's issue flagged as unidentified: ship anonymously, collect clean third-party numbers, then claim it. [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/26/z-ai-releases-glm-5-3-flash-a-320b-a18b-natively-multimodal-moe-with-a-1m-token-context/), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/26/z-ai-open-sources-ox-alpha-model-as-glm-5-3-flash/)

**Harness scaling became a research programme, and ARC-AGI-3 fell twice.** Prime Intellect's [Prime Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552) (Aug 24, open source) took ARC-AGI-3 RHAE Best@1 from 30% to 95.5% by giving the model a persistent IPython REPL, recursive subagents, and versioned prompts/memories/skills that survive across trajectories. That is the same 30% baseline Nvidia's Agentic Variation Operators moved to 100% the week before, from an unrelated direction. Two days later [JIT-Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25593) attacked the problem from the training side, using a 27B model to synthesise a task-specific harness on the fly and gaining +7.7 to +8.8 average points across nine agent benchmarks at 14.9-54.1% *lower* cost. Both are written up in full in the KB's Papers section.

**Nvidia's quarter said the AI capex cycle has not turned.** Reporting Aug 26 on roughly $92B of quarterly revenue, close to double a year ago, with Jensen Huang guiding to about 70% revenue growth for fiscal 2028, well above consensus. CFO Colette Kress said shipments of approved older parts to China were under 1% of data-center revenue, which quietly removes China from the growth story entirely. Hyperscaler spot pricing for leading-edge Nvidia instances was up 7.9% quarter over quarter in August. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q2-2027-live-updates.html)

**Over a hundred companies signed a joint call on AI-enabled cyber attacks.** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft signed alongside CrowdStrike, Okta, Fortinet, financial institutions, and infrastructure firms (Aug 27), warning that "in the coming months, AI-enabled cyber attacks will become far more widespread and sophisticated," and asking for public-private coordination on defence. Read it next to OpenAI's Aug 18 post on pacing releases against cyber capability thresholds: the labs are converging on the same framing. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/openai-anthropic-google-and-100-other-companies-call-for-action-to-defend-against-rogue-ai/)

## Models & releases

- **DFlash2** (z-lab): a speculative-decoding *drafter* for Qwen3.8-27B reporting up to 3.43x output throughput in vLLM or SGLang, with MLX support. It drafts a whole block per forward pass using block diffusion, keeps the top candidates at each position, and runs a light selector over the lattice; verification is unchanged so decoding stays mathematically lossless. Cohere's own ParseBench numbers (79.2) put it *behind* GPT-5.5, Opus 4.8 and Gemini 3.5 Flash on accuracy; the pitch is explicitly price per page at enterprise volume, not the top score. ## Talk of the town

- **"MCP was declared dead"** was the week's recurring headline, arguing that the 2026-07-28 spec's removal of the initialize handshake and the `Mcp-Session-Id` header leaves something indistinguishable from a plain HTTP API. The practical effect is real and good (any request can hit any instance, so sticky routing and shared session stores stop being protocol requirements), and the KB already documents the change on the MCP page; the argument is about whether the remaining surface justifies a separate protocol. [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/mcp-stateless-gateway/)
- **Grok's "cryptographic context injection" is still unpatched**, 11+ weeks after Adversa AI disclosed it on June 3. A page carries an AES-encrypted payload that static content filters cannot read, Grok decrypts it in its own Python runtime, then follows the revealed instructions and appends the user's chat history, name, coarse location, and subscription tier to an attacker-controlled URL. About a 40% success rate over 20 attempts, triggered by nothing more than asking for a summary of the page. Missed in last week's issue and included here because the technique generalises: any agent with a code interpreter can be made to decrypt its own exploit. [Adversa AI](https://adversa.ai/blog/cryptographic-context-injection-grok-data-theft/), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/20/grok-chat-duped-into-swallowing-injected-instructions/)
- **The AI Overviews traffic data everyone was passing around** (Aug 28): across 54 advertisers, organic search sessions fell 10.5% while referrals from AI platforms rose 163%, and AI-sourced visitors completed key events at roughly 1.5x the organic rate, with AI-driven key events up 335%. Less traffic, better traffic, is the summary most people took from it.

### Research and papers

- **WikiSkill** (Google Research, Aug 29): co-evolves an agent's skills with a persistent wiki compiled from its own past runs, over three layers (Raw traces, a Wiki layer of distilled patterns that never resets, a Skill layer of active procedural instructions), with a Wiki Maintainer, a Skill Proposer and a gate that validates a proposed skill edit before it lands. Averages across five benchmarks (maths, web search, spreadsheets, document QA, virtual environments): Gemini-3.5-Flash 49.5% to 68.1%, Qwen-3.6-27B 39.4% to 63.3%, with LiveMath moving 33.0% to 72.6% for Gemini. Smaller models with WikiSkill match larger models without it. This is the third harness-scaling result of the fortnight and the one that most directly targets the gap Prime Agent named, so it is folded into the harness-scaling section of `Topic: agentic-harnesses`. [arXiv 2608.27454](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27454), [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/google-gives-ai-agents-their-own-wiki-so-they-can-learn-from-mistakes-and-successes/)
- **ContextPilot** (Tencent, Aug 28, EMNLP 2026 main track): trains agents to manage their own context proactively rather than relying on a compaction heuristic in the harness. Adds planning, long-term memory and soft context offloading as tools, then uses fine-grained RL that keys on context and entropy variation to find the decisions where an edit actually matters. Reports stronger results on long-context QA and deep search with a smaller working context. A 14B checkpoint on the Qwen3-14B base ships with the code. [arXiv 2608.28476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28476), [GitHub](https://github.com/Tencent/ContextPilot)
- **Anthropic on recursive self-improvement**: an early empirical look at AI automating AI development, combining public benchmarks with internal company data. Over 80% of code merged into Anthropic's production codebase was authored by Claude as of May 2026; engineers shipped 8x more code per quarter than in 2024; a 76% success rate on open-ended coding tasks; roughly 52x speedup on code optimisation against 3-4x for skilled humans; and an end-to-end autonomous run that recovered 97% of the performance gap on an AI safety problem. The stated limit is direction-setting: "large performance gaps persist when it comes to Claude exercising judgement in choosing goals", plus modest transfer to production-scale models. Read it against Tencent's claim above that Hy4 helped optimise its own training. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/institute/recursive-self-improvement), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/an-anthropic-researcher-just-gave-us-a-peek-at-self-improving-ai/)
