# TLS and certificates: the handshake, PKI, mTLS and post-quantum

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3c65c17b0d0d8105a37ff2d2f602a421 (child of Topic: protocols)

Recorded locally and read-only against public hosts on 2026-10-05 (`src/raw/`, rerun with `src/run_all.sh`). Tabs: Reading, Handshake, decrypted (every record of TLS 1.3, mTLS and TLS 1.2, observer and endpoint views), Certificate dissector (twelve real chains, checks with faults), Further reading.
