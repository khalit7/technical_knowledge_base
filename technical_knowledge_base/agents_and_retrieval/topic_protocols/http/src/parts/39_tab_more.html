Primary sources

{{RFC 9110: HTTP Semantics|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110}}

The one to read: methods, status codes, fields, intermediaries. Sections 6 to 9 and 15 first. 4 h (2 h for the core)

{{RFC 9111: HTTP Caching|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9111}}

Freshness, validation, Cache-Control. 1 h 30 min

{{RFC 9112: HTTP/1.1|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9112}}

Message syntax, body length rules (section 6), security considerations on smuggling (section 11). 1 h

{{RFC 9113: HTTP/2|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9113}} and {{RFC 7541: HPACK|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7541}}

Frames, streams, flow control, request reliability (8.7); header compression and its security (section 7). 2 h 30 min

{{RFC 9114: HTTP/3|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9114}} and {{RFC 9204: QPACK|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9204}}

HTTP over QUIC streams; compression that tolerates reordering. 2 h 30 min

{{RFC 8470: Using Early Data in HTTP|https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8470}}

0-RTT replay and 425 Too Early. 20 min

Best explanations

{{MDN: HTTP|https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP}}

The canonical reference for headers, status codes, caching and negotiation. 1 h for the core pages

{{High Performance Browser Networking (Ilya Grigorik)|https://hpbn.co/}}

Free book; TCP, TLS, HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/2 performance explained from the ground up (written before HTTP/3). 9 h; HTTP chapters 2 h

{{HTTP/3 explained (Daniel Stenberg)|https://http3-explained.haxx.se/}}

Short free book by curl's author on QUIC and HTTP/3. 1 h

{{Cloudflare: HTTP/3, the past, the present, and the future|https://blog.cloudflare.com/http3-the-past-present-and-future/}}

Deployment view of HTTP/3 and QUIC (2019). 20 min

{{PortSwigger: HTTP request smuggling|https://portswigger.net/web-security/request-smuggling}}

The attack class explained with labs, plus {{the HTTP/2 downgrade research|https://portswigger.net/research/http2}}. 1 h

{{Stripe: Designing robust and predictable APIs with idempotency|https://stripe.com/blog/idempotency}}

The classic treatment of retries with idempotency keys. 15 min

{{Cloudflare: HTTP/2 Rapid Reset, technical breakdown|https://blog.cloudflare.com/technical-breakdown-http2-rapid-reset-ddos-attack/}}

How a protocol feature became a record DDoS. 15 min

{{Claude API: errors|https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/errors}} and {{rate limits|https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/rate-limits}}

Status codes, long requests, rate-limit headers for one real LLM API. 20 min
