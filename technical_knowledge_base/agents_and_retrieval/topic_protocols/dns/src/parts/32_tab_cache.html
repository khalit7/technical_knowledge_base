illustrative model: every resolver cache is assumed to be kept warm by steady traffic, with fetch times spread evenly over one TTL; a real cache that sees little traffic refetches later and flips sooner. The rule it encodes is exact: a cache flips when the entry it held at the moment of the change expires, and an entry keeps the TTL it was fetched with. The orange step is the lab's recorded run from the Reading (section 5): one cache, TTL 30, which kept the old address until its entry expired.
