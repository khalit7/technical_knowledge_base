Pod resolver lab: every packet, two runs side by side

Each pane replays one recorded getaddrinfo call from a lab pod: the exact /etc/resolv.conf it had, and every DNS packet it sent and received, captured with tcpdump inside the pod's own network namespace (measured, 5 October 2026; glibc 2.41 on Debian 13, musl 1.2.6 on Alpine; a real CoreDNS 1.12.4 as the cluster DNS, Unbound 1.25.2 behind it). Pick a preset or any two runs; the bar chart below draws both durations to one scale. Section 6 and 7 of the Reading explain the rules; this tab is the evidence.

Times are from the pod's packet capture, relative to the first packet of the call; the duration is what getaddrinfo itself took. Rows marked "wait" are gaps of more than half a second with no packet: the library sitting on its timer. Addresses are on the lab's private network; .test names exist only there. The lossy runs used a relay that dropped the first AAAA query (or every query, for "dead"); its log is under each pane.
