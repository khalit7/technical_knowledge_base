One gRPC call, frame by frame

Every HTTP/2 frame of three real gRPC calls, recorded on 5 October 2026 by a logging proxy between a grpcio client and server (this page's src/lab/frame_proxy.py): each direction parsed into frames, header blocks decoded with HPACK as the receiving side would. Step through, or click any frame. Section 4 of the Reading explains what to look for.

measured: times are milliseconds since the TCP connection opened, as seen by the proxy (client → server and server → client are read by two threads, so frames that cross within a fraction of a millisecond may print slightly out of order). Bytes are exact. Protobuf inside DATA frames is decoded by this page with the schema in llm.proto.
