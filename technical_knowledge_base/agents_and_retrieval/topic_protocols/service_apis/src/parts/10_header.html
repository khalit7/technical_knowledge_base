Service APIs: REST, gRPC, GraphQL and load balancing

How programs call each other behind an LLM API: REST at the front door, protobuf and gRPC inside (the bytes, deadlines, status codes, retries, connections), GraphQL and JSON-RPC, and how calls get spread over replicas at L4, at L7 and in the client. Taught from zero on the root page's streaming LLM request; every gRPC exchange, failure and balancer run recorded locally on 5 October 2026. About 40 minutes for the Reading, 15 for the two labs.
