SSH: keys, certificates, jump hosts and tunnels for cluster work

How SSH turns one TCP connection into an encrypted, authenticated pipe that carries shells, file copies and port forwards; how keys, agents, host keys and certificates decide who trusts whom; and the jump-host, multiplexing and tunnel patterns that make a GPU cluster behind a bastion pleasant to use. Taught from zero, with every behaviour recorded on 5 October 2026 in a small container cluster (a bastion, two compute nodes, a notebook server) driven from a laptop with ssh -vvv.
