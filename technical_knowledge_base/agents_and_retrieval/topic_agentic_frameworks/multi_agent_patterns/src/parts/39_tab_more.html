Primary sources

{{How we built our multi-agent research system|https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system}}

Anthropic, 13 Jun 2025. The case for: lead and subagents on research, briefs, token economics, rainbow deployments. 25 min.

{{Don't Build Multi-Agents|https://cognition.com/blog/dont-build-multi-agents}}

Cognition (Walden Yan), 12 Jun 2025. The case against: shared context and conflicting implicit decisions. 15 min.

{{How and when to build multi-agent systems|https://www.langchain.com/blog/how-and-when-to-build-multi-agent-systems}}

Harrison Chase, 16 Jun 2025. Reads parallelise, writes do not. 15 min.

{{Single vs Multi-Agent System?|https://www.philschmid.de/single-vs-multi-agents}}

Philipp Schmid, 20 Jun 2025. A compact decision guide. 10 min.

{{Building effective agents|https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents}}

Anthropic, 19 Dec 2024. Workflows against agents; orchestrator-workers and evaluator-optimizer as workflows. 25 min.

{{Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems|https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296}}

Kim et al., 260 configurations; when coordination helps or hurts. 60 min.

{{Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail? (MAST)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657}}

Cemri et al.; 14 failure modes in 3 categories. 45 min.

{{Improving Factuality and Reasoning through Multiagent Debate|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325}}

Du et al., May 2023: three agents, two rounds; the protocol section 8 reproduces. 40 min.

{{Debate or Vote|https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17536}}

Aug 2025: majority voting accounts for most of the gains attributed to debate. 40 min.

{{Should we be going MAD?|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17371}} and {{Rethinking the Bounds of LLM Reasoning|https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.18272}}

Smit et al. (Nov 2023) and Wang et al. (Feb 2024): debate against strong single-agent baselines. 40 min each.

{{Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning|https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171}}

Wang et al., Mar 2022: sample several reasoning paths, take the majority. 30 min.

{{The Hearsay-II Speech-Understanding System|https://mas.cs.umass.edu/Documents/Erman_Hearsay80.pdf}}

Erman, Hayes-Roth, Lesser and Reddy, Computing Surveys, June 1980: the first blackboard system. 60 min.

{{Orchestrate teams of Claude Code sessions|https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-teams}}

Agent teams: shared task list with file-locked claims, mailboxes, when teams beat subagents. 20 min.

{{OpenAI Agents SDK: handoffs|https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/handoffs/}} and {{orchestrating multiple agents|https://openai.github.io/openai-agents-python/multi_agent/}}

Handoffs as transfer tools, input filters, agents as tools, code-driven orchestration. 15 min.

{{Formalizing Fermat's Last Theorem|https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem}} and {{Refactoring Hermes with 1,393 agents|https://nousresearch.com/refactoring-hermes-with-1393-agents}}

Two 2026 scale runs: a shared proof graph with a perfect verifier, and massive parallel writing with tests that missed regressions. 20 min each.
