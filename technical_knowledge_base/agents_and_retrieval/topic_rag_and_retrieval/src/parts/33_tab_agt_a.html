Agentic lab

Who drives retrieval: a fixed pipeline that searches once, or the model itself, deciding what to search for next. Five ways of answering the same 200 two-hop questions, measured with a small local model: what each gets right, how many searches and tokens it spends, and how long it takes. Then a router that picks between them, long context instead of retrieval, where in a long prompt the evidence gets ignored, a small GraphRAG, and memory.

The running example on this tab
HotpotQA (distractor dev set): 200 questions (160 bridge: find one thing, then a fact about it; 40 comparison: two things compared), each needing two "gold" Wikipedia paragraphs, over a corpus of 9,760 paragraphs. The generator is Qwen3-1.7B (and Qwen3-4B for comparison), 4-bit GGUF in llama.cpp on an Apple M1 Pro, greedy decoding, thinking switched off. The search tool is bge-small-en-v1.5 (exact search, run on the CPU so it does not compete with the generator for the GPU). Every number marked measured was produced here in October 2026; published numbers come from the paper named beside them; constructed marks an input written for this page. Terms (embedding, recall@k, BM25) are defined in the {{Reading|#t-read}} tab; the one-shot pipeline's stages are measured in the {{Pipeline lab|#t-pipe}}.
