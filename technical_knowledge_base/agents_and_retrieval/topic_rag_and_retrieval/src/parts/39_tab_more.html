Best outside resources

Manning, Raghavan and Schütze: Introduction to Information Retrieval

The textbook: inverted indexes, TF-IDF, evaluation (chapters 1, 2, 6, 8). about 3 h for those chapters

BEIR (Thakur et al. 2021)

The zero-shot retrieval benchmark the running example's SciFact and NFCorpus come from; why BM25 is a strong baseline. 45 min

Dense Passage Retrieval (Karpukhin et al. 2020)

The bi-encoder with in-batch negatives that started modern dense retrieval. 40 min

Qwen3 Embedding technical report

How a current open embedder and reranker are trained: synthetic pairs, filtering, merging. 40 min

MTEB leaderboard

Filter by the retrieval tasks closest to your domain; read the zero-shot column. 10 min to explore

HNSW (Malkov and Yashunin)

The layered graph index, with the neighbour-selection heuristic that does the real work. 45 min

The Faiss library (Douze et al. 2024)

The vector-search toolkit's design space: IVF, PQ, HNSW, and how to choose. 60 min

Hugging Face: embedding quantization

int8 and binary quantization with rescoring, measured. 15 min

Reciprocal rank fusion (Cormack, Clarke and Büttcher, SIGIR 2009)

Two pages behind RRF and k = 60. 10 min

ColBERT and ColBERTv2

Late interaction and its compression. 40 min each

Anthropic: Contextual Retrieval

Context written per chunk, with BM25 and a reranker; vendor numbers. 15 min

HyDE (Gao et al. 2022)

Search with a generated answer instead of the question. 30 min

Is Agentic RAG worth it? (Ferrazzi et al. 2026)

The controlled pipeline against agent comparison, quoted on Agentic retrieval, long context and memory. 45 min

IRCoT and Adaptive-RAG

Retrieval interleaved with reasoning, and a router that decides how much to retrieve. 40 and 30 min

From Local to Global: GraphRAG (Edge et al.)

The GraphRAG pipeline and its global search. 45 min

Microsoft: LazyGraphRAG and LightRAG

The two cheaper graph variants; the first is vendor claims. 12 and 40 min

Lost in the Middle and NoLiMa

Position and literal-match effects in long contexts. 30 min each

Long Context vs. RAG: an evaluation and revisits (Li et al.)

The head-to-head study, quoted on Agentic retrieval, long context and memory. 45 min

pgvector README

HNSW and IVFFlat in PostgreSQL, filtering and iterative index scans. 30 min
