A pipeline is a funnel. The first stage looks at all 5,183 abstracts and keeps about a hundred candidates; a reranker reads the best few dozen of those closely and reorders them; only the top k (here 3) reach the generator's prompt. A relevant abstract that drops out at any stage is gone for good: the generator never sees it. Watch one real SciFact claim go through, then switch the pipeline and run the same claim again.
