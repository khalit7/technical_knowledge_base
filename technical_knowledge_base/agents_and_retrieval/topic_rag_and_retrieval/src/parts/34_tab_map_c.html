Which store?

Choosing a store is mostly choosing an operating model, not an algorithm. Answer six questions; the chooser ranks the stores in the atlas with the reasons that moved each one, every reason linked to the card that sources it. The rules are rules of thumb written for this page from the atlas facts (they are not a benchmark): read the reasons, then measure your top two on your own data, at your own recall target, the way the section above does. For the database-engine side of "stay in Postgres or not", the {{Search and vector databases|n:3ef5c17b0d0d815eb4caef9b4cd4d732}} page has the memory arithmetic and the measured pgvector curves.
