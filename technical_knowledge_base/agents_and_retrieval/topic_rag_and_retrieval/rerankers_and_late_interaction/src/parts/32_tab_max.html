MaxSim lab: one claim and one abstract, token by token

A real pair from SciFact with the real ColBERT vectors (answerai-colbert-small-v1, 96 dimensions per token, stored by the setup). Before: a single-vector model compares one vector per text (Qwen3-Embedding's cosine). After: late interaction, where each of the claim's 32 token vectors finds its best match among the abstract's token vectors and the maxima are added up. Shading is the dot product of the current claim token with every abstract token; the red outline is its maximum.
