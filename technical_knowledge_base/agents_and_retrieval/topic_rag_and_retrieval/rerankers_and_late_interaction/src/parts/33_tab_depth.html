Depth chooser: quality, latency and compute for every reranker and K

Every point is a real measurement on SciFact's 300 test claims: nDCG@10 after reranking the top K (recorded scores), against the time to rerank one claim's K candidates in one batch on this laptop (bench lock, 3 repeats). Choose the first stage and a point; the cards say what it buys and what it costs, and what the same work would take on faster hardware.

x: milliseconds per claim to rerank K candidates (log scale; measured where a timing exists, otherwise interpolated linearly in K from the measured points and drawn hollow). y: nDCG@10. The first stage alone is the dashed line; ColBERT times are MaxSim on one CPU thread over stored vectors. measured here (e_depth.py, e_time.py, e_colbert.py)

The hardware slider rescales only the cross-encoders' compute: time = K × GFLOP per pair / achieved TFLOP/s, with GFLOP per pair from the token counts (section 1 of the Reading tab). The first position is this laptop's measured throughput for each model. Higher positions are assumptions for illustration, labelled derived: real servers add batching, padding and transfer overheads, and gain from lower precision.
