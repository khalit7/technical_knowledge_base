Design question: add reranking to a RAG service at 200 queries per second, p95 under 300 ms end to end (interview rehearsal)
Budget each stage (query embedding about 10 to 50 ms, ANN search a few ms, generation's first token separately). Rerank the hybrid top 20 to 30 with a 0.6 B cross-encoder on GPUs: about 0.4 TFLOP per pair, so 200 × 30 × 0.4 = 2.4 PFLOP/s of work; at a realistic 100 to 200 TFLOP/s achieved per GPU that is 12 to 24 GPUs, so consider a smaller distilled reranker, fewer candidates (K 10 to 20 after measuring the quality curve), shorter chunks, int8 or fp8, and dynamic batching across concurrent queries with a batch timeout. Cache scores for repeated (query, chunk) pairs. Fall back to the first-stage order if the reranker times out. Log scores to recalibrate thresholds; evaluate offline on a golden set before each model change. Derived arithmetic; the throughput figure is an assumption to replace with a measurement.
