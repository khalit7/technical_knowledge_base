# src: GraphRAG and structured retrieval

Build: `python3 build_data.py && sh build.sh` writes `../index.html` from `parts/`. `build_data.py` turns `data/*.json` (and the root Agentic lab's committed `../../src/agent/out/strategies.json` and, once it exists, `graphrag.json`) into `parts/22_gr_data.js` (what the JavaScript reads, `window.GR`) and `facts.json`; numbers in the prose are written `@@key@@` and filled from `facts.json` by `build.sh`, so prose and data cannot disagree. Results of runs that are still pending are absent from `data/`; their facts read "pending" and the page shows a dashed pending box with the command until the file exists. Checks: `$RAG_SCRATCH/venv/bin/python -I -B check_data.py` (embedded data equal the recorded data, no unfilled fact, a sample recomputed independently from the shared artefacts and the root's committed results, privacy over the whole folder), `node check_page.mjs ../index.html <shots dir>` (every control at 390 px dark and 920 px light; puppeteer from html_utils via createRequire; npm with `NPM_CONFIG_USERCONFIG` pointing at an empty file), and `sh html_utils/checkpage.sh <this page folder>` from the repo root.

Shape: a child page of Topic: rag-and-retrieval (method `.claude/skills/explainer-page/page-kinds/topic-child.md`), rebuilt to the teaching contract on 2026-10-24 (below). Reading tab in the subject's order (the idea in one screen with the hero; what top-k cannot answer; knowledge graphs; extraction; entity resolution; communities; GraphRAG's query engines; cheaper graphs; multi-hop measured; cost; text-to-SQL; tables and Cypher; choosing; depth), then the Cost calculator tab and Further reading. Prefix `gr` for ids, classes and data keys (`gb-`, `ba-`, `sq-` for the widgets moved from the old labs, `cc-` in the calculator); storage key `gr-tab`. Departures from the method: no "in production now" section of real configs, because GraphRAG deployments do not publish theirs; the graphrag 3.3.0 defaults read in source stand in for it. The page is about 630 KB: the Before/after widget embeds all 200 questions' top-5 lists and the 1.7B's answers to them (about 150 KB), the Text-to-SQL widget every generated query of the 1.7B under the four prompts (about 60 KB) and the global-search stepper the reports and answers; cutting to samples would hide the paired wins and losses they exist to show. Two images (the GraphRAG paper's Figure 4, 76 and 78 KB WebP) are files in `../img/`, credited in `../img/CREDITS.md`.

## Scripts and what they produce (`data/`)
Deterministic, CPU, no lock (done): `s_nlp_extract.py` -> `nlp.json`; `s_communities.py` -> `communities.json`; `s_entities.py` -> `entities.json` (hand labels in `inputs/merge_labels.json`); `s_ppr.py` -> `ppr.json`; `s_examples.py` -> `ba.json`, `build.json`; `s_prompt_tokens.py` -> `prompt_tokens.json`; `s_extract.py dry` -> `extract_dry.json`; `s_lightrag.py stub 20` -> `lightrag_stub.json`; `s_sql_sample.py` -> `sql_set.json`, `spider_schema.json`; `s_schema_ret.py` -> `schema_ret.json`; `s_kuzu.py` -> `kuzu.json`.
Generator (bench lock): run on 2026-10-15 02:02 to 04:56 under the lock with `scripts/run_pending.sh` steps (the 1.7B extraction capped at the first 150 subset paragraphs and the 4B at 40, LightRAG at 40, to keep the slot to about three hours on a loaded machine; every call is cached under `$RAG_SCRATCH/gr/gen/`, so extending a cap reruns only the missing paragraphs). They write `extract_<model>.json`, `global_4b.json`, `qa.json`, `sql_runs.json`, `cypher_runs.json`, `lightrag_4b.json`, and with `post` `entities_<source>.json`, `communities_<source>.json`; then rerun `s_examples.py` (adds the LLM graph to the build animation), `python3 build_data.py && sh build.sh` and the checks. Prose quoting those results uses @@facts@@; sections 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 11 were reread against them on 2026-10-15.
