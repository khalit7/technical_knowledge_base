# src: GraphRAG and structured retrieval

Build: `python3 build_data.py && sh build.sh` writes `../index.html` from `parts/`. `build_data.py` turns `data/*.json` (and the root Agentic lab's committed `../../src/agent/out/strategies.json` and, once it exists, `graphrag.json`) into `parts/22_gr_data.js` (what the JavaScript reads, `window.GR`) and `facts.json`; numbers in the prose are written `@@key@@` and filled from `facts.json` by `build.sh`, so prose and data cannot disagree. Results of runs that are still pending are absent from `data/`; their facts read "pending" and the page shows a dashed pending box with the command until the file exists. Checks: `$RAG_SCRATCH/venv/bin/python -I -B check_data.py` (embedded data equal the recorded data, no unfilled fact, a sample recomputed independently from the shared artefacts and the root's committed results, privacy over the whole folder; part 3, the independent recomputation, reads the shared artefacts under `$RAG_SCRATCH`, so on a machine without that folder only parts 1, 2 and 4 can run, and part 3 waits for the shared setup to be rebuilt as the parent's `src/data/README.md` describes), `node check_page.mjs ../index.html <shots dir>` (every control, chip and step at 390 px dark and 920 px light; puppeteer from html_utils via createRequire; npm with `NPM_CONFIG_USERCONFIG` pointing at an empty file), `node check_stages.mjs [shots dir]` (each 3D stage and the SQL animation play once scrolled into view, step to their last step with one list item per step, never move under reduced motion, and stop redrawing off screen; the Before/after widget never plays by itself; the split is two columns at 1400 px), and `sh html_utils/checkpage.sh <this page folder>` from the repo root. Without `$RAG_SCRATCH`, `check_data.py` now skips the recomputation from raw artefacts and says so.

Shape: a child page of Topic: rag-and-retrieval (method `.claude/skills/explainer-page/page-kinds/topic-child.md`), rebuilt to the teaching contract on 2026-10-24 (below). Reading tab in the subject's order (the idea in one screen with the hero; what top-k cannot answer; knowledge graphs; extraction; entity resolution; communities; GraphRAG's query engines; cheaper graphs; multi-hop measured; cost; text-to-SQL; tables and Cypher; choosing; depth), then the Cost calculator tab and Further reading. Prefix `gr` for ids, classes and data keys (`gb-`, `ba-`, `sq-` for the widgets moved from the old labs, `cc-` in the calculator); storage key `gr-tab`. Departures from the method: no "in production now" section of real configs, because GraphRAG deployments do not publish theirs; the graphrag 3.3.0 defaults read in source stand in for it. The page is about 630 KB: the Before/after widget embeds all 200 questions' top-5 lists and the 1.7B's answers to them (about 150 KB), the Text-to-SQL widget every generated query of the 1.7B under the four prompts (about 60 KB) and the global-search stepper the reports and answers; cutting to samples would hide the paired wins and losses they exist to show. Two images (the GraphRAG paper's Figure 4, 76 and 78 KB WebP) are files in `../img/`, credited in `../img/CREDITS.md`.
