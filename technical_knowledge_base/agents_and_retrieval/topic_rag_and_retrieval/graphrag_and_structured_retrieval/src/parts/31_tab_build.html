Graph build lab

One question's ten paragraphs from the root Agentic lab's subset, taken through graphrag 3.3.0's fast indexing path step by step: extract, merge, prune, link, cluster. Every phrase, survivor, edge and community is the real output of graphrag's own functions over the whole 394-paragraph subset (s_nlp_extract.py, s_communities.py); the layout is networkx's spring layout with seed 0. Then the whole subset's community hierarchy, and global search over it.

The community hierarchy of the subset

Hierarchical Leiden over the pruned subset graph (@@sub_pn@@ phrases, @@sub_pe@@ edges): level 0 holds the broad communities, and every community of 10 or more members is split again one level down. Pick a level-0 community to see its children. Communities are what GraphRAG summarises; the top members (by degree) show what each is about, and where pruning left a hub in.
