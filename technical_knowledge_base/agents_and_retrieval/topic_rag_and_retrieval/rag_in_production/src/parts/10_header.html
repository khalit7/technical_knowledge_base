RAG in production: freshness, access control, latency and cost

Operating a retrieval system once it has users, taught from zero: keeping the index fresh and swapping embedding models without breaking it, deleting for real, enforcing who may read what at retrieval rather than in the prompt, the latency of every stage, caches and the wrong answers a semantic cache serves, cost per query from published prices, monitoring, injection through retrieved documents, and scale. Measured on an Apple M1 Pro with the topic's running example (SciFact) in pgvector and local Qwen3 models, with published numbers kept apart. Data as of October 2026.
