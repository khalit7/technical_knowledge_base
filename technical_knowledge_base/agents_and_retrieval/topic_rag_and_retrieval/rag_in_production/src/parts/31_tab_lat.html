Latency waterfall: one request, stage by stage

Each bar is a stage of the read path, drawn where it starts and as long as it lasts, from timings recorded on this laptop by this topic's runs (measured here, Apple M1 Pro; source and load average of each under the chart). Change a knob and the request is re-assembled. The defaults are the request of the Reading tab's section 7. Stages without a direct timing are derived (work divided by a measured rate) and say so. The line under the chart compares the sum of the separately recorded stages with one end-to-end timing of the whole path in one process: they differ, because the stages share one GPU.
