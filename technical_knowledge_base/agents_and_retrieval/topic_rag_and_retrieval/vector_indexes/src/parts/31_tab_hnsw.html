What to look for
  • Build, HNSW: most inserts stay on layer 0; about one in M reaches layer 1 (the level is drawn as floor(−ln U / ln M), hnswlib line 209). The red dashed candidates are those the heuristic refuses because an already-kept neighbour is closer to them than the new node is: they would be reached through that neighbour anyway, so the link budget goes to other directions.
  • Build, Closest-M: the same candidates, but the M closest are kept whatever their direction, so links bunch up inside a dense corner and a cluster can end up reachable only through one or two nodes.
  • Search: the walk descends the sparse upper layers greedily (one node at a time), then runs a best-first search on layer 0 keeping the ef best seen. Its cost is the number of distances computed; exact search would compute 240.
  • At 240 points the layers save little: the lab shows the mechanism, and the Reading tab's section 6 shows what each half buys at 5,183 and 25,039 vectors, measured.
