3. Layout-aware parsers: let a model look at the page

The idea. A human finds the columns, the header and the table by looking at the page, not by reading coordinates. Layout-aware parsers do the same: render the page to an image, run a layout detection model (an object detector, the kind that draws boxes around cars in photos, trained on pages labelled with boxes such as Text, Title, Section-header, Table, Picture, Caption, Formula, List-item, Page-header, Page-footer and Footnote, the eleven classes of IBM's DocLayNet dataset), then fill each box with text from the PDF's text layer where it is good and from OCR where it is not, order the boxes, and send table boxes to a table-structure model. Page headers and footers are then dropped by class, not by guessing.
