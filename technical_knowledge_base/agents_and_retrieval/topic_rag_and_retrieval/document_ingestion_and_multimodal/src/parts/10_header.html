Document ingestion and multimodal retrieval: PDFs, tables, images

The stage before chunking, taught from zero and measured: what a PDF really contains, how rule-based extractors, layout-aware parsers and OCR turn it into text, where reading order, headers, hyphens and tables break, the metadata a citation needs, re-indexing only what changed, and retrieving page images directly with late-interaction vision models. Runs on real arXiv papers from the topic's QASPER set, an olmOCR-bench sample and three ViDoRe sets, on an Apple M1 Pro; published results kept apart and labelled. Data as of October 2026.
