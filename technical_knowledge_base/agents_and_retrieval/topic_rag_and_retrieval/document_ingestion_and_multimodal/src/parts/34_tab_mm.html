Page-image retrieval: parse the page, or embed the picture?

Three ViDoRe sets, chosen to span the range: infographics (InfoVQA), scientific figures (ArxivQA) and text-heavy industrial reports (Energy). Each page was retrieved three ways on this machine: OCR text (Tesseract) ranked by BM25, the same OCR text ranked by a dense text embedder, and the page image itself ranked by a small ColPali-family model with late interaction, each over the same 200-page subset of each set. The queries, the pages and the relevance labels are ViDoRe's.
