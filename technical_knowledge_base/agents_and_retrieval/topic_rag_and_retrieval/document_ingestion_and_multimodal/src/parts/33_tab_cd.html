Re-indexing lab: one real revision, four ways to chunk

A paper from the running example's QASPER set was revised on arXiv. The ingestion pipeline sees the new version, parses it, chunks it, hashes every chunk and embeds only chunks whose hash it has not seen. How much it re-embeds depends almost entirely on where the chunk boundaries fall.

Try: play the fixed-size version, then content-defined on the same paper. Each square is one chunk of the new version, its width proportional to its words; green chunks reuse a stored vector, orange ones are embedded again. The dashed line is the floor: the share of words the revision really changed.
