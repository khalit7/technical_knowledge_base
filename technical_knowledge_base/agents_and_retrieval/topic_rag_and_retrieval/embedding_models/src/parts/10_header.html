Embedding models: training, choosing and fine-tuning

How a text becomes one vector (a transformer, a pooling step and a normalisation, read in the sentence-transformers code), how contrastive training shapes the space (in-batch negatives, temperature, hard and false negatives, batch size), what instructions, prefixes and truncation do, why the MTEB rank does not carry over to your data, and a protocol to choose and fine-tune a model on a few hundred labelled queries with confidence intervals. Measured on the topic's SciFact and NFCorpus running example on an Apple M1 Pro; published numbers kept apart and labelled. Data as of October 2026.
