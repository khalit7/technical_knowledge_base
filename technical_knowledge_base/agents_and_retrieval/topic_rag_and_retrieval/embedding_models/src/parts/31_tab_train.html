Training lab: the tiny fine-tune from the inside

The question: what does contrastive training actually do to the vectors? Twelve SciFact test claims (never seen in training), each with its labelled abstract, its three highest-scoring unlabelled abstracts under the starting model, and twelve random abstracts. Every 4 training steps the run re-encoded these 72 texts with the current weights. The picture is exact, not a projection: around the chosen claim, each abstract sits at distance 1 − cosine from the centre, at a fixed angle, so moving in means "more similar to the claim". Play the steps; switch the training arm to run the same points through a different training (before/after on the same input).
