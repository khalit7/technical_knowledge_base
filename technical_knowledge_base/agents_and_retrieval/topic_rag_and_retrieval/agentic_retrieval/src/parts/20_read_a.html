Agentic retrieval in one picture

Agentic retrieval moves the decision of what to read next from the engineer to the model (or to a router, or to nobody, with the whole corpus in the window). It pays only when the next query can name what the last search found, and every extra call re-reads everything before it.

Every way of deciding lands within a few points of one good search; the tokens differ tenfold

the engineer (one search, fixed k)a fixed programthe model, at every stepan evaluator or routernobody: everything in the window

F1 against tokens billed per question (every call's whole prompt plus output), Qwen3-1.7B on the topic's 200 two-hop questions measured on Apple M1 Pro. The agent loop (ReAct, @@f1_react@@) is level with the top-5 search (@@f1_single_k5@@) at 3.3 times the tokens; the best point is long context with the question's own 10 paragraphs (@@f1_longctx@@), and the same idea with 8k tokens of near-misses is among the worst (@@f1_longctx_tokens8000@@). No retrieval at all scores @@f1_closed@@ (off the chart).

You need: one search done well, {{The retrieval pipeline|n:3c65c17b0d0d819899fde889e1e8b782}}; the agent loop and native tool calling down to tokens, {{Building a harness|n:3c65c17b0d0d817ca14fc32338707c2f}}. The parent, {{Topic: rag-and-retrieval|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b89145c37dfe8a3b0b}}, introduces these strategies in its section 7 and measures them in its Agentic lab; this page reuses those recorded runs and adds its own.

hop, bridge question
one gold paragraph to read; a bridge question names the first thing but not the second (Billy McFarland, then the Fyre Festival)
observation
a tool's result, appended to the conversation before the model is called again
step budget
the most tool calls the harness allows before it forces an answer
retrieval evaluator
a separate judgment of whether the results are good enough (CRAG's T5, a cross-encoder, the model itself)
router
a rule, classifier or small model that picks a strategy once, before anything runs
prefill
running every prompt token through the model before the first output token
read-time curation
keeping raw history and selecting from it once the current task is known
The running example
200 questions from HotpotQA's distractor dev split (160 bridge, 40 comparison), each needing two gold paragraphs, over a corpus of 9,760 Wikipedia paragraphs ({{the root's data README|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b89145c37dfe8a3b0b}}). Search: bge-small-en-v1.5 (33 M parameters), exact. Generator: Qwen3-1.7B at 4 bits (Q4_K_M) in llama.cpp's llama-server on the laptop's GPU, temperature 0. Scores: HotpotQA's official exact match (EM) and token F1. Small models answer worse than frontier ones: read the absolute numbers as a small-model case and the gaps and costs as the finding. This page's own runs (IRCoT, Self-Ask, FLARE, CRAG- and Self-RAG-style checks, routers, budgets, memory, lost in the middle by length, the 4B block) were made on 2026-10-15 with prompts chosen on held-out questions; src/scripts/run_pending.sh reruns them from cache.
