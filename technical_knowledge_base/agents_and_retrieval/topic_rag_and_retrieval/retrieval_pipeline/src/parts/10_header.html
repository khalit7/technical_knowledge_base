The retrieval pipeline: chunking, query rewriting, hybrid search, filtering

Everything between a document collection and the passages a model reads, taught in depth: how text is cut into chunks (and what the libraries really do), BM25's knobs, learned sparse retrieval, how and why rank fusion works, understanding and rewriting the query, metadata filters, removing duplicates, and assembling the prompt. Every stage measured on the topic's running example (SciFact, NFCorpus, QASPER, HotpotQA) on an Apple M1 Pro, with published results kept apart. Data as of October 2026.
