Query-rewrite lab

What a rewrite does to the retrieved list, on real SciFact claims. The rewrites were written by the local generator (Qwen3-1.7B, greedy): HyDE passages and multi-query rewordings by the root's Pipeline lab, step-back questions by this page. Retrieval: bge-small over all 5,183 abstracts (exact search; the rewrites were embedded again on CPU for this lab). The ten claims shown are the ones, among the first 120 test claims, where a rewrite moved the relevant abstract the most, so this lab shows the effect when there is one, not the average (the averages over all 300 claims are in the {{Reading tab|#t-read}}).

Rows: rank, title, cosine (or RRF score), and the row's rank in the other list ("o3" = third in the original list; "–" = not in its top 10). Green = the annotators' relevant abstract. measured

Decomposition on multi-hop questions

HotpotQA questions need two paragraphs. The generator split each into sub-questions (examples below); retrieving for each and fusing the lists helps when both entities are named in the question (comparison questions) and cannot help when the second hop depends on the first answer (bridge questions). Counts over all 200 questions are in the {{Reading tab|#t-read}}.

Conversational rewrites (follow-up questions that only make sense with the chat before them) are measured on eight constructed chats in the root's {{Pipeline lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b89145c37dfe8a3b0b}}; not repeated here.
