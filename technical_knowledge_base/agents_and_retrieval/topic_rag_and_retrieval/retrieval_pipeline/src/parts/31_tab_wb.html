Chunking workbench

Two real QASPER papers, cut live in your browser by the same chunkers the topic measures (a line-by-line port of the root Pipeline lab's chunkers.py, checked equal to the Python output on both papers by src/check_js.mjs: 18 of 18 configurations give identical spans). Sizes are counted in Qwen3 tokens, as everywhere on this page. Move the size and overlap, switch the strategy, and watch two things: whether an annotated evidence paragraph (green underline) survives whole inside one chunk, and whether the chunks a retriever would hand over within a token budget cover it.

Try: fixed windows at 256 tokens, then the same size with the recursive splitter: count the questions whose evidence stays intact. Then untick ". " (LangChain's default separators have no sentence separator) and watch long paragraphs get cut mid-sentence.
