2. The action boundary: the turn must end at the call

The problem. A language model continues text. After it has written an action, the likeliest continuation of a transcript of actions and results is a result, so a model that is not stopped writes one. The harness must make the model's turn end exactly at its action, and must never read anything generated after it. We call that point the action boundary.

ProtocolWho ends the turn at the actionWhat can go wrong
Provider API with tools (Anthropic, OpenAI)The provider: generation stops with stop_reason: "tool_use" / finish_reason: "tool_calls"Nothing at the boundary; a reply cut by max_tokens can hold an incomplete call (section 7)
Open model through its chat templateThe model, by writing its end-of-turn token (<|im_end|>, an end-of-sequence id), and the server, by stopping thereA raw-text endpoint, a misconfigured stop list, or a template whose end token is not in the server's EOS list
Your own text protocol (the Loop lab's ACTION {json} line)You: a stop sequence such as the result header your harness writes, or cutting the reply at the first action lineEverything, if you forget: the parent's recordings below

The rule
Enforce the boundary yourself whatever the model usually does: use the provider's tool calls, or keep the server's end-of-turn stop, or add a stop sequence and also cut the reply at the first action; never parse past it; and treat a model that writes the other side's turn as a harness bug, not a model quirk. A model that behaves on one route (the local model in a chat API) can misbehave on another (the same model on a raw endpoint, or Haiku through a CLI without stop sequences).

Local model: Qwen3 4B Instruct 2507 (4-bit) on mlx-lm 0.32.0, temperature 0.7, seeds 1 to 5 per condition. Text protocol: the Loop lab's step-1 system prompt verbatim, sent through the chat endpoint, at turn 1 and at turn 2 (after a real read of textstats/core.py), with and without stop: ["--- turn"].
