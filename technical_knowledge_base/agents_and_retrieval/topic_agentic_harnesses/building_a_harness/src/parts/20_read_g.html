7. Error recovery: every way a turn can go wrong

The rule. A harness never crashes on a bad turn and never hides one. Each failure is either turned into an observation the model can act on, or, if it is the infrastructure's fault rather than the model's, retried quietly and kept out of both the transcript and the score. The difficulty is telling the two apart, and noticing some failures at all.

Retry budgets. Every retry needs a ceiling: errors in a row (the Loop lab ended a run after three malformed replies), retries per edit (one in our bench), and the run's overall caps (section 8). Anthropic's docs describe Claude's own behaviour with invalid tool calls: it "will retry 2-3 times with corrections before apologizing to the user" ({{Handle tool calls|https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/agents-and-tools/tool-use/handle-tool-calls}}). Retrying the infrastructure belongs in the client or a gateway; the frameworks root's {{Production stack|n:3c65c17b0d0d81ab8432c54d65b7f8c7}} tab measures LiteLLM's retry and fallback rules.
