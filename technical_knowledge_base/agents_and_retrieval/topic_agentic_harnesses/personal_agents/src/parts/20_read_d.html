Derived: P(no wrong step) = pn, and the length at which it falls to one half is ln 0.5 / ln p. At 95% and 20 steps, 0.9520 = 0.358; the half-way length is 13.5 steps. Illustrative: it assumes independent errors and no recovery; real agents notice and fix some mistakes, and their errors are correlated. Measured reliability over repeated trials (pass^k) and task-length horizons are {{Agentic benchmarks|n:3c65c17b0d0d811fb926d7b91ef8234e}}' subject.

The consequence for a personal agent: a mistake is only cheap if it is reversible or caught before it leaves. Sections 4 to 7 are about making both true.
