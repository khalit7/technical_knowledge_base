Gate designer

Every action the recorded models proposed on this page (all scenarios, both models), re-decided by a policy you choose. The policy is plain code, as in section 6: it sees only the tool, its class (what it would take to undo it) and who started the run (Sam, or a heartbeat or scheduled job with nobody watching). No model is involved, and nothing is re-run.

One exception is built in, as in the recorded gate: drafts and messages to Sam stay allowed on unattended runs under "This page's gate", since they only reach Sam.

Read this as a decision table, not a re-run

When the recorded gate held an action, the model's later steps reacted to that (it told Sam the reply was waiting). Under another policy the same model might have continued differently. The counts show what each policy would have decided for the actions that were actually proposed. The "known wrong" actions are the booking replies to the garage in the correction scenario (section 4), proposed before the agent read Sam's correction.
