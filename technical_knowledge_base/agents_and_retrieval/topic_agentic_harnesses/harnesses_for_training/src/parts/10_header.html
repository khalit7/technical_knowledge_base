Harnesses for training and evaluation: scaffold sensitivity, RL environments, trajectories, self-improving harnesses

The same harness that runs an agent is also the ruler that scores it and the gym that trains it. This page builds all three from zero on one small task set: real rollouts of a local 4B model and Claude Haiku 4.5 in four harnesses on a ten-task environment made for this page, with a verifier in a container, rewards, group advantages, loss masks and a training-data pipeline, then compares the 2026 papers that let a model improve its own harness.
