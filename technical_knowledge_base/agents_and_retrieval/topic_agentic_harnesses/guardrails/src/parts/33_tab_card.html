Guard model scorecards

Every published number for the current open guard models that this page could transcribe from a primary source, kept apart by benchmark, by metric and by who ran it. A guard's score depends on the threshold, the mapping from its categories to the dataset's labels and the prompt template, all chosen by whoever ran it; the same model on the same dataset can differ by 50 points between runners. Nothing here is averaged across runners or metrics.

How to read a bar

Colour is the runner: own the model's makers; rival a competitor's card or paper that re-ran it; copied a competitor's table quoting someone else's number; independent no vendor; this page the runs in the Pipeline lab. Hover or tap a bar for its source and note.

One model, every number

Transcriptions with every table and URL are in src/inputs/research_guard_models.md (read 4 October 2026). Models whose makers publish no comparable numbers (NemoGuard 8B's card, Llama Guard 4 against rivals) appear only in others' runs.
