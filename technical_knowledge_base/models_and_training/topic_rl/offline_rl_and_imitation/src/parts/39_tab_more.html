Best resources

{{Levine, Kumar, Tucker and Fu: Offline RL tutorial, review and perspectives|https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01643}}

The standard survey: why offline RL is hard, importance sampling, dynamic programming with constraints, model-based methods, open problems. (about 2h 30m)

{{Ross, Gordon and Bagnell 2011: DAgger|https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.0686}}

The reduction to no-regret learning, the T² and T bounds, and the Super Tux Kart and Mario experiments. (about 1h)

{{Ho and Ermon 2016: GAIL|https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.03476}}

From maximum-causal-entropy IRL to occupancy matching to the adversarial algorithm, in nine pages. (about 1h)

{{Kumar et al. 2020: Conservative Q-Learning|https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.04779}}

The penalty, both lower-bound theorems, and the variants. (about 1h 30m)

{{Kostrikov, Nair and Levine 2021: Implicit Q-Learning|https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06169}}

Expectile regression in eight pages, with the clearest discussion of v0 against v2 results. (about 45 min)

{{Fujimoto and Gu 2021: A Minimalist Approach to Offline RL|https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06860}}

TD3+BC, and a table of how much other methods change their base algorithms. (about 40 min)

{{Chen et al. 2021: Decision Transformer|https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01345}}

Return-conditioned sequence modelling, with the %BC comparison that started the debate. (about 1h)

{{Brandfonbrener et al. 2022: When does return-conditioned supervised learning work?|https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.01079}}

The conditions, and the counterexamples, for Decision Transformer-style methods. (about 1h)

{{Tarasov et al.: CORL|https://github.com/corl-team/CORL}}

Single-file implementations of BC, TD3+BC, CQL, IQL, AWAC, ReBRAC, SAC-N, EDAC, DT, Cal-QL, with logged benchmark runs; the paper's tables are on the D4RL results tab. (about 30 min for the README and one file)

{{HumanCompatibleAI: imitation|https://github.com/HumanCompatibleAI/imitation}}

Clean implementations of BC, DAgger, GAIL, AIRL and preference comparisons. (about 30 min)

{{Sergey Levine, CS 285 lectures on imitation and offline RL|https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/}}

On the current schedule, Lecture 2 (behavioural cloning, DAgger) and Lectures 17 and 18 (offline RL), with slides and videos. (about 4h)

{{Gottesman et al. 2018: Evaluating RL in observational health settings|https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.12298}}

How offline evaluation misleads in practice, with worked examples on sepsis data. (about 1h)
