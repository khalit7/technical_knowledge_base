Best resources

{{David Silver's UCL course, Lectures 4 and 5|https://www.davidsilver.uk/teaching/}}

"Model-Free Prediction" and "Model-Free Control"; the near-crash analogy for MC against TD is from Lecture 4. Slides: {{Lecture 4|https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/lecture-4-model-free-prediction-.pdf}}, {{Lecture 5|https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/lecture-5-model-free-control-.pdf}}. (3h)

{{Sutton and Barto, chapters 5 to 7|http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html}}

Monte Carlo methods, TD learning, n-step bootstrapping: every experiment tab on this page reruns one of their figures. (2h 15m)

{{Sutton and Barto, chapter 12|https://incompleteideas.net/book/RLbook2020.pdf#page=309}}

Eligibility traces in depth: the λ-return, TD(λ), true online TD(λ) and dutch traces, SARSA(λ), Watkins's Q(λ) and tree backup. (about 1h 30m, estimated)

{{Lilian Weng, "A (Long) Peek into Reinforcement Learning"|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-02-19-rl-overview/}}

The sections on MC, TD, SARSA and Q-learning: compact equations for everything here. (about 15 min)

{{Karpathy, GridWorld TD demo|https://cs.stanford.edu/people/karpathy/reinforcejs/gridworld_td.html}}

Watch TD learn without a model, with eligibility traces you can switch on. (about 10 min)

{{Zhang, Python reproductions of the book's figures|https://github.com/ShangtongZhang/reinforcement-learning-an-introduction}}

Short scripts for the random walks, cliff walking, maximisation bias and the blackjack examples; useful to compare with this page's own implementation. (about 30 min)

Primary papers, in the order the Reading tab meets them

{{Singh and Sutton 1996, "Reinforcement learning with replacing eligibility traces"|https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00114726}}

First-visit against every-visit Monte Carlo, and replacing against accumulating traces. (about 1h)

{{Sutton 1988, "Learning to predict by the methods of temporal differences"|https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00115009}}

TD learning, TD(λ) and the random walk. (about 1h)

{{Dayan 1992, "The convergence of TD(λ) for general λ"|https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00992701}}

Convergence of tabular TD with probability 1. (about 45 min)

{{van Seijen and Sutton 2014, "True online TD(λ)"|https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/seijen14.html}}

The online backward view made exactly equal to the forward view, with dutch traces. (about 45 min)

{{Tsitsiklis 2002, "On the convergence of optimistic policy iteration"|https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v3/tsitsiklis02a.html}}

The partial answer to whether Monte Carlo ES converges. (about 45 min)

{{Singh, Jaakkola, Littman and Szepesvári 2000|https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1007678930559}}

"Convergence results for single-step on-policy reinforcement-learning algorithms": SARSA under GLIE. (about 1h)

{{van Seijen, van Hasselt, Whiteson and Wiering 2009|https://doi.org/10.1109/ADPRL.2009.4927542}}

"A theoretical and empirical analysis of Expected Sarsa", the source of Figure 6.3. (about 30 min)

{{Watkins 1989, "Learning from delayed rewards" (PhD thesis)|https://www.cs.rhul.ac.uk/~chrisw/new_thesis.pdf}} and {{Watkins and Dayan 1992, "Q-learning"|https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00992698}}

Q-learning and the λ-return; then the convergence proof. (about 40 min for the 1992 paper)

{{van Hasselt 2010, "Double Q-learning"|https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2010/hash/091d584fced301b442654dd8c23b3fc9-Abstract.html}}

Maximisation bias and the double estimator. (about 30 min)

{{Munos, Stepleton, Harutyunyan and Bellemare 2016, "Safe and efficient off-policy reinforcement learning"|https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.02647}}

Retrace: multi-step off-policy returns with truncated importance ratios. (about 1h)
