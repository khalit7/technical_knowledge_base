Bandits and exploration

This page: the first of the eight.

{{Dynamic programming|n:3c65c17b0d0d81baada7e1c05ce69d01}}

Planning with a known model: policy evaluation, policy and value iteration. No exploration needed, because the model is given.

{{Model-free prediction and control|n:3c65c17b0d0d81898a0cd4c05c744969}}

Monte Carlo, TD, SARSA and Q-learning, where ε-greedy and GLIE do their work; cliff walking shows on-policy exploration's cost.

{{Value-based deep RL|n:3ee5c17b0d0d818689b2c0dbce434ece}}

DQN and its fixes, including NoisyNet inside Rainbow.

{{Policy gradients and actor-critic|n:3ee5c17b0d0d8101a262d3620e75747c}}

The general case of the gradient bandit; entropy bonuses, SAC's maximum-entropy objective.

{{Model-based RL and planning|n:3ee5c17b0d0d8116be5fd39e10bd94c5}}

UCT and Monte Carlo tree search, where the UCB index chooses moves.

{{Offline RL and imitation|n:3ee5c17b0d0d81d4995ec47e428de9b3}}

When the agent may not explore at all: learning from a fixed dataset.

{{RL for LLMs|n:3c65c17b0d0d818c9bcff7177325fe56}}

GRPO, RLVR, DAPO: sampling temperature, group size and clipping as the exploration controls of language-model RL.

Best resources

{{Sutton and Barto, chapter 2: Multi-armed Bandits|http://incompleteideas.net/book/RLbook2020.pdf#page=47}}

The source of the testbed, every strategy in sections 2 and 3, and the exercises. (about 1h 30m)

{{David Silver, Lecture 9: Exploration and Exploitation|https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/lecture-9-exploration-and-exploitation.pdf}}

Slides: regret, optimism, probability matching, information state search, contextual bandits, and the MDP extensions. (about 45 min)

{{Lilian Weng: The Multi-Armed Bandit Problem and Its Solutions|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-01-23-multi-armed-bandit/}}

A compact walk through ε-greedy, UCB and Thompson sampling with code. (about 20 min)

{{Lilian Weng: Exploration Strategies in Deep RL|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2020-06-07-exploration-drl/}}

A map of count-based, prediction-based and memory-based exploration (section 6's families, with many more). (about 50 min)

{{Russo et al.: A Tutorial on Thompson Sampling|https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.02038}}

When and why probability matching works, with examples beyond Bernoulli arms. (about 2h)

{{Lattimore and Szepesvári: Bandit Algorithms|https://tor-lattimore.com/downloads/book/book.pdf}}

The reference text: regret decomposition, UCB and KL-UCB, lower bounds, linear and contextual bandits. Chapters 4 to 10 and 15 cover this page's section 4. (about 6h for those chapters)

{{Slivkins: Introduction to Multi-Armed Bandits|https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.07272}}

A shorter book-length survey, good on contextual bandits. (about 4h)

{{Li et al. 2010: LinUCB on Yahoo! news|https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.0146}}

The contextual bandit paper behind section 5, with its offline replay evaluation. (about 40 min)

{{Burda et al. 2018: Random Network Distillation|https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12894}}

A short, clear paper on the novelty bonus most used in deep RL. (about 40 min)

{{Ecoffet et al.: First return, then explore|https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12919}}

Go-Explore's full version: detachment, derailment, and the archive. (about 1h)
