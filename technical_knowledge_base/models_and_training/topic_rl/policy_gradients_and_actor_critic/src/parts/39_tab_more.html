Best resources

{{Sutton and Barto, chapter 13|http://incompleteideas.net/book/RLbook2020.pdf#page=343}}

The policy gradient theorem with its proof, REINFORCE, baselines, actor-critic, continuing tasks and Gaussian policies; the short corridor comes from here. (about 1h 30m)

{{OpenAI Spinning Up|https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/spinningup/rl_intro3.html}}

"Intro to Policy Optimization" derives the gradient in code-ready notation; the algorithm pages (VPG, TRPO, PPO, DDPG, TD3, SAC) have reference implementations. (about 3h for the core pages)

{{Lilian Weng, "Policy Gradient Algorithms"|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-04-08-policy-gradient/}}

The best single derivation chain from REINFORCE through TRPO and PPO to SAC and TD3. (about 45 min)

{{Huang et al., "The 37 Implementation Details of PPO"|https://iclr-blog-track.github.io/2022/03/25/ppo-implementation-details/}}

Mandatory before implementing PPO or GRPO, with line-by-line video tutorials. (about 50 min)

{{Berkeley CS285 (Sergey Levine)|https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/}}

Lectures 5 to 9 cover policy gradients, actor-critic, value methods and advanced policy gradients (natural gradient, TRPO, PPO) at lecture depth. (course, about 25h in all)

{{David Silver's UCL course, Lecture 7|https://www.davidsilver.uk/teaching/}}

Policy gradient methods: finite differences, Monte Carlo and actor-critic policy gradients, compatible function approximation, natural actor-critic. (about 1h 30m)

{{Zhang and Bondariev, short_corridor.py|https://github.com/ShangtongZhang/reinforcement-learning-an-introduction/blob/master/chapter13/short_corridor.py}}

The reproduction of Figures 13.1 and 13.2 this page's live tab follows (with the differences listed there). (about 10 min)

Primary papers, in the order the Reading tab meets them

{{Williams 1992, REINFORCE|https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00992696}}

"Simple statistical gradient-following algorithms for connectionist reinforcement learning". (about 1h 30m)

{{Sutton, McAllester, Singh and Mansour 1999|https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/1999/file/464d828b85b0bed98e80ade0a5c43b0f-Paper.pdf}}

The policy gradient theorem with function approximation, compatible features and convergence. (about 40 min)

{{Greensmith, Bartlett and Baxter 2004|https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume5/greensmith04a/greensmith04a.pdf}}

Variance reduction for policy gradients: the optimal baseline, and why the true value function is not always the best critic. (about 2h)

{{Barto, Sutton and Anderson 1983|https://doi.org/10.1109/TSMC.1983.6313077}}

The first actor-critic, balancing a pole. (about 45 min)

{{Schulman et al. 2015, GAE|https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02438}}

Generalised advantage estimation, with TRPO on 3D locomotion. (about 1h)

{{Mnih et al. 2016, A3C|https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.01783}}

Asynchronous methods for deep RL. (about 1h)

{{Kakade 2001, natural policy gradient|https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2001/file/4b86abe48d358ecf194c56c69108433e-Paper.pdf}}

The covariant gradient and the Fisher metric. (about 40 min)

{{Schulman et al. 2015, TRPO|https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05477}}

The monotonic improvement bound and the trust region algorithm. (about 1h 30m)

{{Schulman et al. 2017, PPO|https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347}}

The clipped and KL-penalty objectives, the ablation and the hyperparameters. (about 45 min)

{{Engstrom et al. 2020, "Implementation Matters in Deep Policy Gradients"|https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.12729}}

Code-level optimisations, PPO against TRPO, and the trust region that the clip does not keep. (about 1h)

{{Andrychowicz et al. 2021, "What Matters In On-Policy Reinforcement Learning?"|https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.05990}}

250,000 agents, more than 50 design choices, and recommendations. (about 1h 30m)

{{Silver et al. 2014, deterministic policy gradients|https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/silver14.html}}

The deterministic policy gradient theorem. (about 1h)

{{Lillicrap et al. 2015, DDPG|https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.02971}}

Continuous control with deep RL. (about 45 min)

{{Fujimoto et al. 2018, TD3|https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.09477}}

Addressing function approximation error in actor-critic methods. (about 1h)

{{Haarnoja et al. 2018, SAC|https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290}} and {{SAC algorithms and applications|https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05905}}

Maximum-entropy actor-critic, then automatic temperature and real robots. (about 2h for both)

{{Henderson et al. 2018|https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.06560}} and {{Agarwal et al. 2021|https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264}}

"Deep RL that matters" and "Deep RL at the edge of the statistical precipice": seeds, variance and how to report. (about 2h for both)
