Sources this page leans on

{{Bellman 1957, "A Markovian Decision Process"|https://doi.org/10.1512/iumj.1957.6.56038}}

The MDP as a discrete stochastic control problem. (about 30 min)

{{Dreyfus 2002, "Richard Bellman on the Birth of Dynamic Programming"|https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.50.1.48.17791}}

Bellman's own account of the name and the early years. (about 15 min)

{{Ye 2011, policy iteration is strongly polynomial|https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.1110.0516}}

Policy iteration and simplex for fixed discount. (about 1h)

{{Singh and Yee 1994, loss from approximate value functions|https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00993308}}

The 2γε/(1 − γ) bound on acting greedily on an approximate V. (about 20 min)

{{de Farias and Van Roy 2003, the LP approach to approximate DP|https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.51.6.850.24925}}

Linear programming with a function approximator. (about 1h)

{{Mahajan, Stochastic control notes: linear programming|https://adityam.github.io/stochastic-control/mdps/linear-programming.html}}

The primal and dual LPs and occupancy measures, written for costs. (about 20 min)

Best resources

{{David Silver, Lecture 3 "Planning by Dynamic Programming"|https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/teaching/}}

The clearest walkthrough of policy iteration against value iteration, with the gridworld and the contraction proof (slides: {{PDF|https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/lecture-3-planning-by-dynamic-programming-.pdf}}). (1h 30m)

{{David Silver, Lecture 2 "Markov Decision Processes"|https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/lecture-2-mdp.pdf}}

Markov process to MRP to MDP and the student example used on this page. (1h 30m)

{{Sutton and Barto, chapter 4 "Dynamic Programming"|http://incompleteideas.net/book/RLbook2020.pdf#page=95}}

Policy evaluation to GPI, with the proof sketch of the policy improvement theorem, Jack's car rental and the gambler. (45 min)

{{Sutton and Barto, chapter 3 "Finite Markov Decision Processes"|http://incompleteideas.net/book/RLbook2020.pdf#page=69}}

The canonical treatment of MDPs, returns and the Bellman equations. (1h)

{{Csaba Szepesvári, RL Theory lecture notes: planning in MDPs|https://rltheory.github.io/lecture-notes/planning-in-mdps/lec3/}}

Value and policy iteration with exact iteration counts, error bounds and lower bounds. (1h 30m)

{{Andrej Karpathy, REINFORCEjs GridWorld: DP|https://cs.stanford.edu/people/karpathy/reinforcejs/gridworld_dp.html}}

Interactive; watch values propagate and edit rewards. (about 10 min)

{{Lilian Weng, "A (Long) Peek into Reinforcement Learning"|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-02-19-rl-overview/}}

A one-page compression of the foundations, MDPs to DP. (about 35 min)

{{OpenAI Spinning Up, "Key Concepts in RL"|https://spinningup.openai.com/en/latest/spinningup/rl_intro.html}}

A crisp notation reference for states, policies, returns and Bellman equations. (about 15 min)
