Further reading

The Reading tab is a short, intuitive overview of RL on one through-line: every method estimates the expected return in the Bellman equation and differs in what stands in for the expectation. The tabs on this page classify and look up the methods; the pages under this one own the mechanisms, derivations and worked examples. Links open in a new tab, with reading times.

On this page

{{Taxonomy|#t-tax}}

Every way of classifying an RL method in depth: model-based or model-free, value, policy or actor-critic, on-policy, off-policy or offline, Monte Carlo or bootstrapping, tabular or approximate, episodic or continuing, observability, multi-agent, reward source, exploration. Behind the three axes on the Reading tab.

{{Method atlas|#t-atlas}}

Every method on one grid: what it stores, what stands in for the expectation, model, data, action space, year and paper. The Reading tab's spine table, extended to the whole field.

{{Milestones and benchmarks|#t-miles}}

The results that made the field, from TD-Gammon to DeepSeek-R1, with sourced scores. Behind the families on the Reading tab.

Best resources for the topic as a whole

All optional; the Reading tab is self-sufficient.

{{Sutton and Barto, Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd ed., free PDF)|http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html}}

The reference text; chapters 1 to 3 cover the foundations (about 2h), the whole book about 14h. (about 14h)

{{David Silver's UCL course (10 lectures)|https://www.davidsilver.uk/teaching/}}

Still the best structured path through classical RL, slides plus videos; lectures 1 and 2 (about 3h) are the source of this page's framing and the student example. (about 15h)

{{OpenAI Spinning Up|https://spinningup.openai.com}}

The best practical bridge from theory to deep RL code; "Key Concepts in RL" alone is a crisp notation reference (about 15 min). (about 3h for the core pages)

{{Nathan Lambert, RLHF Book|https://rlhfbook.com}}

The reference for the LLM side: reward models, PPO and GRPO for language models, RLVR, DPO and evaluation. (about 6h)

{{Sergey Levine, Berkeley CS285: Deep Reinforcement Learning|https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/}}

The deepest course on the deep RL layer: policy gradients, actor-critic, value methods, model-based and offline RL, with homework. (course, about 25h)

{{Hugging Face Deep RL Course|https://huggingface.co/learn/deep-rl-course/unit0/introduction}}

Hands-on units from Q-learning to PPO and RLHF, with notebooks you can run. (course, many hours; unconfirmed total)

{{Lilian Weng, "A (Long) Peek into Reinforcement Learning"|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-02-19-rl-overview/}}

The best single-page compression of the classical core. (about 35 min)

{{Lilian Weng, "Policy Gradient Algorithms"|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-04-08-policy-gradient/}}

Every policy-gradient method from REINFORCE to SAC, derived in one notation. (about 45 min)

{{Lilian Weng, "Exploration Strategies in Deep RL"|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2020-06-07-exploration-drl/}}

Count-based bonuses, curiosity, RND, Go-Explore. (about 50 min)

{{Lilian Weng, "Reward Hacking in Reinforcement Learning"|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2024-11-28-reward-hacking/}}

From classic specification gaming to RLHF and LLM agents. (about 1h)

{{"The 37 Implementation Details of PPO"|https://iclr-blog-track.github.io/2022/03/25/ppo-implementation-details/}}

What PPO's results actually depend on, line by line against the reference code. (about 50 min)

{{Andrej Karpathy, "Deep Reinforcement Learning: Pong from Pixels"|https://karpathy.github.io/2016/05/31/rl/}}

A policy gradient in 130 lines of Python, explained from scratch. (about 30 min)

{{Alex Irpan, "Deep Reinforcement Learning Doesn't Work Yet"|https://www.alexirpan.com/2018/02/14/rl-hard.html}}

The honest 2018 list of why deep RL is hard (sample efficiency, reward design, instability); most items are the open problems of this page. (about 40 min)

{{Andy Jones, "Debugging RL, Without the Agonizing Pain"|https://andyljones.com/posts/rl-debugging.html}}

Probe environments and practical checks for when an agent will not learn. (about 30 min)

{{Hugging Face, "Illustrating RLHF"|https://huggingface.co/blog/rlhf}}

The RLHF pipeline drawn step by step. (about 20 min)
