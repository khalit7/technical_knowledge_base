Dyna lab

Sutton and Barto's three Dyna experiments (Examples 8.1 to 8.3), run live in your browser with the same engine as the Reading tab and checked against a Python implementation (src/recompute.py). Change the number of planning steps, the step size, exploration and the bonus, and rerun. Section 2 and 3 of the Reading tab explain what the curves mean.
