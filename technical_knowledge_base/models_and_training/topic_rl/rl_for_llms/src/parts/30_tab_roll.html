Real rollouts: 160 answers, three verifiers, one reward model

The data behind every real number on the Reading tab. {{Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct}} answered ten {{GSM8K|https://github.com/openai/grade-school-math}} test problems, 16 times each, at temperature 1 with every other sampling processor off and a 512-token cap, prompted to "reason step by step, and put your final answer within \boxed{}". Each answer was checked by three verifiers (Reading, section 5) and scored by a real reward model, {{Skywork-Reward-V2-Qwen3-0.6B|https://huggingface.co/Skywork/Skywork-Reward-V2-Qwen3-0.6B}}; every token's log-probability was recorded under the policy and the base model. Pick a problem; click an answer to read its ending.

Columns: tokens (cut = truncated at 512), the three verifiers' verdicts, the reward-model score, the three group advantages of sections 3 and 7 (GRPO, Dr. GRPO, RLOO) and the mean per-token KL estimate against the base model. Scripts: src/rollouts/rollouts.py (sampling, log-probabilities, verifiers), rm_score.py, mc_values.py, mk_data.py (this page's extract). Apple M1 Pro, PyTorch on MPS and CPU, seeds fixed; one sample of 16 per problem, so pass rates carry sampling noise of up to about ±12 points (one standard error at 50%).
