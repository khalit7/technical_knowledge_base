Shape drill: predict the shape, the count, the bytes

Questions drawn from nine real configs and the tiny GPT: the shape of a stored weight or of a tensor in the forward pass, the parameters of one layer or of the whole model, the KV cache per token, the forward FLOPs per token. Commit to an answer; the reveal shows the formula with the config's numbers. Wrong options are the usual mistakes (a transposed weight, query heads where the KV heads belong, a tied table counted twice, a forgotten SwiGLU gate). Configs: the root's saved config.json files (read 2026-10-18); every total below matches the Hugging Face safetensors count after the stored non-parameters named in the Reading tab's section 10, checked by src/zero/accounting.py.
