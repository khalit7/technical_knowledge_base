Shapes and counts: every weight and activation of ten real configs

The tool for every section of the Reading tab: pick a model and a context length, and read each stored weight of one layer with the shape the checkpoint stores, every activation through the layer, the parameter total against the Hugging Face count, the KV cache per token and the forward FLOPs of one token. The formulas are section 10's; the configs are the root's saved config.json files (read 2026-10-18), and every total matches the safetensors count after the stored non-parameters named in section 10, checked by src/zero/accounting.py. Predict first: in one layer of Llama 3.1 8B, is k_proj the same shape as q_proj, and which matrix is largest?

Shapes follow each checkpoint's convention: nn.Linear stores (out, in) and computes x WT; GPT-2 stores its linear layers as Conv1D with weights (in, out). With grouped-query attention, k_proj and v_proj have Hkv × hd outputs, not H × hd: using H here is the classic GQA mistake. Qwen3 adds per-head q and k RMSNorm gains (hd each); OLMo 2 normalises q and k over the full width and puts its norms after the sublayers. Forward FLOPs: 2 per weight touched (every block matrix and the output layer; the embedding lookup is free) plus 4 × L × T × H × hd for the query against T keys and the weights against T values. The scores row is why fused attention kernels never store the (T, T) matrices, and the logits row (B, T, V) is often the largest single activation in training, which is why some trainers compute the loss in chunks. KV cache per token here is one line of arithmetic; sizing at a context length, with every attention design, is the {{Long context|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81059ee1ce20c0326dc5}} page's KV-cache calculator.
