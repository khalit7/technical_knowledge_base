Ablation explorer: what each piece of the block buys

Every run here is gpt_zero.py with one switch turned off, trained by src/train/train_zero.py with the running example's loop: seed 0, 1,500 steps of 32 × 256 tokens (12.3M), learning rate 3e-3 with 100 warm-up steps and cosine decay, AdamW, clipping 1.0. Measured on Apple M1 Pro (MPS), 2026-10-18; samples and per-token losses evaluated afterwards with eval_zero.py. The baseline row of the root's runs (tiny_gpt.py, the same model) is shown where it helps: the seed spread of the baseline is about 0.02.

Loss on the 20 fixed held-out windows, every 100 steps (the root runs every 250 or 100). Dashed: the unigram cross-entropy (a model that ignores context) and the bigram cross-entropy (one token of context, counted from the training split), both calculated by src/train/curve.py.
