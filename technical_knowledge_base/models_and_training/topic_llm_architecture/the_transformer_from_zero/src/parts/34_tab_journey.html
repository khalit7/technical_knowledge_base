The whole journey, one line

lookup + position → norm → attention (8 heads) → add → norm → FFN → add → 5 more blocks → final norm → scores against the table → sample → the next token starts again.

Where every number comes from
  • The model. gpt_zero.py's baseline, retrained for this game with train/train_zero.py (seed 0, 1,500 steps, Apple M1 Pro, MPS) because the page's checkpoint is not kept: held-out loss , against 2.2757 for the run the other tabs quote (MPS reruns differ in the third decimal). src/journey/make_journey.py runs it once on the running sequence and records position 30; it asserts that its hand-written attention, FFN and logits equal the model's own forward pass.
  • The backpack shows 8 of the 256 numbers (): the 8 with the largest size relative to their vector's RMS along the journey. Colour is each number against its vector's RMS (blue positive, orange negative); the bag's size in the room is the RMS.
  • Attention is block 1: scores q·k / √32 and softmax shares of all 8 heads at position 30, over tokens 0 to 30. The head you walk with is the most peaked head whose top token is neither <eot> nor yourself.
  • "Trained without ..." lines are the page's recorded ablation runs (held-out loss after 1,500 steps, same data and seed; {{Ablation explorer|#t-abl}}), and their greedy continuations of evaluation prompt 1.
  • Exit doors are the 40 most likely next tokens (top-k 40, as Reading section 9 samples), door size their probability after dividing the real logits by the temperature; the dice draws from exactly those probabilities.
