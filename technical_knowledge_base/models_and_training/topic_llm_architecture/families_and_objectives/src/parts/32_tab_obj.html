3. A causal model is trained on 256-token windows. Is it trained to predict token 1 from token 0 alone?

Skip

Yes: every position is a separate prediction with its own context length, from 1 to 256 tokens, all computed in one forward thanks to the causal mask. That is why the loss is higher in the first half of a window () than in the second (), measured on the reference model.
4. Masked diffusion at rate t = 0.9 against BERT at 0.15: which gets more loss terms, and which is easier per term?

Skip

About 230 terms against 38, each predicted from almost no context, so much harder; the 1/t weight then scales the sum down. Measured on our adapted model: top-1 on masked positions at 15% hidden, at 90% (Diffusion lab).
5. DeepSeek-V3 trains one MTP depth with λ = 0.3. At inference, what happens to the module?

Skip

Dropped or kept as a draft: "we can directly discard the MTP modules and the main model can function independently and normally" (DeepSeek-V3 section 2.2). transformers 5.20.1 drops it on load.
