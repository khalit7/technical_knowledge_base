Fill in the blanks: both sides against the left side

Top-1 accuracy on the hidden positions of 16 held-out windows. The diffusion model reads the corrupted window in both directions; the causal model reads the true tokens to the left. The crossing is the reason BERT masks 15% and why a diffusion sampler's first steps (almost everything masked) are its least certain.

The adaptation run

Held-out loss bound (Reading section 8) on 64 windows with a fixed masking draw, every 100 steps of train_diffusion.py --shift: the reference checkpoint plus one [MASK] embedding row, bidirectional mask, LLaDA loss, AdamW, 16 windows of 256 per step, CPU: steps at lr 1e-3 (50 warmup steps, cosine decay), then 3,000 more at 5e-4 on new windows (--resume). The page's diffusion results use the final model; the 1,500-step model's are in src/lab/out/diffusion_eval.json. Not a perplexity: the 1/t-weighted bound is always above the true per-token loss.
