7. Multi-token prediction (MTP)

Next-token prediction asks each position about one token, so a model can do well on local patterns without planning; and at inference the decoder makes one forward per token, each limited at small batch by reading the weights from memory, not by arithmetic ({{Performance math|n:3c65c17b0d0d81faad1ef390b0e54d08}}). Multi-token prediction adds loss terms for tokens two or more places ahead: a denser training signal, and a ready-made guess for the next forward to check.

Two ways to predict the token after next: the sequential module is told the next token

  • Parallel heads ({{Gloeckle et al., arXiv 2404.19737|https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19737}}, April 2024): n independent one-layer heads on a shared trunk and unembedding; head k predicts t+k from the trunk's state at t alone. Heads run forward and backward one at a time, so peak memory stays O(V + d) instead of O(nV + d). vendor (Meta): at 13B, 12% more HumanEval and 17% more MBPP problems solved; "worse than the baseline for small model sizes, but outperform the baseline at scale"; n = 4 best for token-level code; as drafts, 3.0x faster decoding on code and 2.7x on text.
  • Sequential module (DeepSeek-V3, {{arXiv 2412.19437 section 2.2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19437}}): depth k takes the previous depth's state and the embedding of the known token ti+k, so the chain stays causal (the paper compares it to EAGLE). DeepSeek-V3 uses D = 1 and weights the loss by λ = 0.3 for the first 10T tokens and 0.1 for the remaining 4.8T.

Cost. One extra block in training, nothing at inference: the module can be discarded. DeepSeek-V3's MTP layer is 13.46B parameters, mostly its own stored copies of the embedding and output head, which is why the Hub says 685B where the paper says 671B (bill in the closed block). Benefit, published: DeepSeek-V3 Table 4 shows real but small single-run gains at 15.7B and 228.7B (and MMLU 0.9 lower at large scale); as a draft in serving, the second-token guess has "between 85% and 90%" acceptance and gives "1.8 times TPS" (section 5.4.3).
