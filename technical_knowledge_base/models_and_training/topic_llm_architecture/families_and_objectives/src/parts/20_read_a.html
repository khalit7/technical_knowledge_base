The idea in one screen

Every model on this page is the same stack of transformer blocks with two dials set differently: the attention mask (which positions each position may read) and the objective (which tokens it is asked to predict, from what input). The mask decides what can be cached and whether the model can generate; the objective decides how much training signal each sequence gives. Multi-token prediction and diffusion attack the decoder's one-token-per-forward loop from opposite sides.

You need: the decoder block and its causal attention ({{The transformer from zero|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8174980ddde4a20c7b3c}}) and what a KV cache holds ({{Attention variants|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8120859ecc2d0236e846}}). Width, depth, the attention variant and {{mixture of experts|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81fda06bf955af7c2b58}} are shared by every family; recurrent mixers ({{Beyond attention|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8142bc9ecac1fc366966}}) have no bidirectional form. The root, {{Topic: llm-architecture|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8172b490dc9f9ac281df}}, puts the three families in one table in its section 10; this page owns the masks, the objectives, MTP and diffusion LMs.

Causal, bidirectional mask
position i reads 0 to i; or every position reads every position.
Cross-attention
a decoder layer whose keys and values come from the encoder's output.
Prefix LM
a decoder whose prompt is read both ways and continuation causally.
MLM, span corruption
predict hidden tokens (BERT); predict hidden spans, each replaced by one sentinel (T5).
MTP
multi-token prediction: extra loss terms for tokens two or more places ahead.
Masked diffusion LM
a bidirectional model trained to unmask text at every masking rate, which then writes by unmasking many tokens per forward.
