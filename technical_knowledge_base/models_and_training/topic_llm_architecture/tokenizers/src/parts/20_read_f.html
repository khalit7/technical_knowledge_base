What current models use

Read from each open model's own tokenizer file (downloaded 2026-10-18, revisions in src/tok2026/fetched.json, read with tokenizers 0.23.3), three patterns hold:

  • Tokenizers are inherited, not redesigned. DeepSeek-V4 keeps V3's 128,000 ordinary tokens exactly and only adds special tokens; Gemma 4 keeps 262,125 of Gemma 3's 262,144 entries; GLM, MiniMax and Olmo equal their predecessors (the full list is in the table below). A new tokenizer invalidates every embedding and output row of the predecessor and every cached tokenization of the data, so labs change it only with a new pretraining run and a measured reason.
  • The exception is the multilingual push. Qwen3.5 grew from 151,669 to 248,070 entries and shares 131,586 of Qwen3's ordinary tokens; Llama 4 grew from 128,256 to 201,135 and shares 100,438 with Llama 3. Both cut Hindi sharply (Qwen3 56 to Qwen3.5 27 tokens on the same FLORES sentence, Llama 3 32 to Llama 4 22) while English stays at 16 to 17 tokens in every tokenizer of the table.
  • Sizes cluster at 128K to 262K. 128K (Llama 3, DeepSeek, Nemotron 3 Super at 131,072), 150K to 165K (Qwen3, GLM, Kimi), 200K (Llama 4, MiniMax, OpenAI), 248K to 262K (Qwen3.5, Gemma). Vocabularies grew from 32K (LLaMA, 2023), mostly for multilingual and code compression, and the 32K vocabularies of 2023 are gone from new models; the {{Vocabulary timeline|#t-voc}} tab puts 41 models from 2018 to 2026 on one axis.

Bigger vocabularies barely shorten English; the other 203 languages carry almost all the saving

Tokens for the same text against vocabulary size, for the ten tokenizers of the {{Same text, ten tokenizers|#t-lang}} tab, relative to GPT-2. Try switching to one language.

On the same English text (FLORES-200, 259,170 characters) every tokenizer except Llama 2's, from GPT-2's 50K to Gemma 3's 262K, lands between 4.82 and 4.96 characters per token; across all 204 FLORES languages the total falls from 47.4M tokens (GPT-2) to 22.6M (GPT-4o) and 20.6M (Gemma 3).

Llama 3's own compression figure against FLORES, and where its tokenizer came from (sources)

Llama 3 reports its English compression rising "from 3.17 to 3.94 characters per token" over Llama 2's 32K SentencePiece vocabulary, a 24% gain on Meta's own sample ({{Llama 3 section 3.2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783}}); on FLORES English the same two tokenizers give 4.28 and 4.91, a 15% gain (different text, so the absolute numbers differ). Llama 3's tokenizer is "100K tokens from the tiktoken tokenizer with 28K additional tokens to better support non-English languages" (same section), and its regex (digits in threes) is the one Llama 4, SmolLM3 and others inherited.

The 2026 tokenizers, read from their files (measured, table)

The full inheritance list: DeepSeek-V4-Flash and V4-Pro keep V3's 128,000 ordinary tokens (128,815 to 129,280 entries); Gemma 4 keeps 262,125 of Gemma 3's entries; GLM-5.3 equals GLM-5; MiniMax-M3 equals M2 apart from added specials; Olmo 3 equals OLMo 2; SmolLM3 uses Llama 3's tokenizer; Qwen3.5-397B equals Qwen3.5-0.8B. Gemma 1 and 2 already used 256,000 entries, Gemma 3 and 4 use 262,144.

Entries are what the tokenizer can emit (ordinary tokens plus special tokens); config is the padded vocab_size of the model; digits is how the string 12345678 is cut; shared is how many ordinary entries are identical to the predecessor's.

measured from the files (src/tok2026/analyze.py). "BPE, bytes" = byte-level BPE (GPT-2's byte mapping); "BPE, byte fallback" = SentencePiece-style BPE on characters that falls back to 256 byte tokens for anything unseen (Gemma). NFC = the tokenizer normalises Unicode to composed form first, so two encodings of "é" give the same ids. Kimi K3 ships a tiktoken file (163,584 ranked tokens) and reserves 256 special ids in tokenization_kimi.py, 163,840 in all, its config's size exactly. Closed models (GPT-4o, GPT-5: o200k_base, 200,019 entries in tiktoken 0.14.0) and gpt-oss (o200k_harmony, 201,088, {{gpt-oss model card section 2.3|https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10925}}) are in the {{Vocabulary timeline|#t-voc}} tab.
