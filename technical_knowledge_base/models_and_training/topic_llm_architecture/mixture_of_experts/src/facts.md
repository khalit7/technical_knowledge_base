# Verified facts used on the MoE page (2026-10-18)

Published (sources read 2026-10-18; the paper pages' saved texts in `reference/papers/*/src/inputs/` or arXiv HTML):
- Switch (arXiv 2101.03961 v3): aux loss alpha N sum f_i P_i, eq. 4 to 6; alpha 1e-2 after a sweep 1e-1 to 1e-5, "found 10^-2 balanced load quickly without interfering with training loss"; Shazeer et al. "conjectured that routing to k>1 experts was necessary in order to have non-trivial gradients to the routing functions" (section 2.1); No-Token-Left-Behind "we found no empirical benefits" (appendix B); selective precision: router in float32.
- ST-MoE (arXiv 2202.08906 v2): z-loss (1/B) sum (log sum e^x)^2, c_z = 0.001; Table 4 baseline stable 4/6 (quality -1.755), update clipping 3/3 (-4.206), z-loss 3/3 (-1.741); "The train capacity factor is 1.25 and the eval capacity factor is 2.0"; top-2 +0.004 over top-1 at train CF 1.25 (Table 8); recommends "top-2 routing with 1.25 capacity factor".
- Shazeer 2017 (arXiv 1701.06538): G(x) = Softmax(KeepTopK(H(x), k)), H(x) = x W_g + StandardNormal() Softplus(x W_noise).
- GShard (arXiv 2006.16668): top-2 with random routing of the second expert; expert capacity.
- Expert choice (arXiv 2202.09368): "over 2x faster training convergence in a 8B/64E" model vs GShard top-2; "might not immediately apply to auto-regressive text generation".
- Hash Layers (Roller et al. 2021) arXiv 2106.04426 (title checked).
- DeepSeekMoE (arXiv 2401.06066): Table 1 Pile loss at 2B total, 100B tokens: dense 0.2B 2.060, Hash Layer 1.932, Switch 1.881, GShard 1.867, DeepSeekMoE 1.808 (GShard and DeepSeekMoE 0.3B active); shared expert disabled + one more routed: 1.808 to 2.414; 1/2/4 shared: 1.808/1.806/1.811; device-level balance loss eq. 15 to 17; DeepSeekMoE 16B "comparable" to LLaMA2 7B "with only about 40% of computations".
- DeepSeek-V2 (arXiv 2405.04434): expert-, device- and communication-level balance losses, alpha 0.003, 0.05, 0.02; device-level token dropping at a budget "equivalent to 1.0", lowest affinity first, about 10% of sequences never dropped.
- Aux-loss-free (arXiv 2408.15664, DeepSeek): Table 2 1B ppl 9.56 to 9.50, MaxVio_global 0.72 to 0.04; 3B 7.97 to 7.92, 0.52 to 0.04; sigmoid chosen "since we find that the sigmoid baseline performs better than the softmax baseline" (appendix C); MaxVio definition.
- DeepSeek-V3 report (arXiv 2412.19437 v2 text): sigmoid with normalisation among selected (2.1.2); bias update rule; gamma 0.001 for first 14.3T tokens, 0 for the last 500B; alpha 0.0001 sequence-wise; no token dropping; at most 4 nodes (M = 4); NVLink 160 GB/s, about 3.2x IB (50 GB/s), up to 13 experts; 64-way EP over 8 nodes, 16-way PP; prefill unit 4 nodes 32 GPUs, 32 redundant experts, re-planned about every 10 minutes; decode unit 40 nodes 320 GPUs, one expert per GPU, 64 GPUs for redundant and shared experts; section 4.5.3 validation losses 2.258/2.253/2.253 (1B), 2.085/2.080/2.080 (3B); Table 5: aux-free better on most benchmarks, large-model MMLU 68.3 to 67.2 (worse), small-model MBPP 36.6 to 35.8 (worse).
- Global-batch LBL (arXiv 2501.11873, Qwen): "approximately 0.1 in pre-training PPL and 2 in benchmark scores"; up to 42.8B parameters and 400B tokens. Qwen3 report: 128 experts, 8 active, "excludes shared experts", global-batch LBL.
- MegaBlocks (arXiv 2211.15841): block-sparse, "never drops tokens".
- GLaM (arXiv 2112.06905): 1.2T parameters, "1/3 of the energy used to train GPT-3"; MoE every other layer, 2 of 64 experts.
- Mixtral (arXiv 2401.04088 v1): G(x) = Softmax(TopK(x W_g)) (section 2.1); "Surprisingly, we do not observe obvious patterns in the assignment of experts based on the topic" (section 5). Mistral blog 2023-12-11: 46.7B total, 12.9B active.
- gpt-oss-120b card: "post-trained with MXFP4 quantization of the MoE weights, making gpt-oss-120b run on a single 80GB GPU".
- Nemotron 3 Super card: LatentMoE, "tokens are projected into a smaller latent dimension for expert routing and computation"; config moe_latent_size 1024, d 4096, 512 experts, top 22, routed_scaling_factor 5.0. Kimi K3 card: "Stable LatentMoE framework that activates 16 out of 896 experts"; config routed_expert_hidden_size 3584, d 7168, 2 shared, sigmoid, noaux_tc.
