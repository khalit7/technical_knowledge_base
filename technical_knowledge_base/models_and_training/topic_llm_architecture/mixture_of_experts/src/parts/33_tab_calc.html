What one decode step reads, against batch size

Decoding is bound by weight bytes read per step. Each of B running sequences picks k of E experts in every MoE layer; with uniform, independent routing about E(1 − (1 − k/E)B) distinct experts are touched per layer. The input embedding is excluded (a lookup of B rows, not a full read); the output head and every dense part are read once per step.

What one token sends under expert parallelism

Split every expert m ways (the fine-grained move)

E → m · E experts, k → m · k per token, expert width → width / m. Parameters and FLOPs barely move (only the router grows); the combinations, the dispatch fan-out and the matrix shapes change.
