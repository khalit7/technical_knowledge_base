A mixture-of-experts (MoE) layer stores E feed-forward networks, the experts, but sends each token through only the k that a small router picks, so the model has many parameters but each token pays for k experts. The page is the price of that one discrete choice: the router learns only through what it picked, traffic must be kept balanced, buffers overflow, and the experts must live on many GPUs.

One token, one layer: 8 experts stored, 2 computed

Recorded, not drawn by hand: token 1 of the running sequence ("Once") in layer 1 of the trained moe8top2 tiny GPT. The router scores all 8 experts, keeps the best 2 and mixes their outputs by the renormalised scores. Memory pays for all 8 experts, compute for 2: total parameters (what must sit in memory) and active parameters (what one token computes with) come apart. Under expert parallelism the experts sit on different GPUs, and the token travels to them and back (section 7).

You need: the feed-forward block each expert copies, and SwiGLU ({{The transformer from zero|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8174980ddde4a20c7b3c}}); the running example, the root's tiny GPT on TinyStories ({{Topic: llm-architecture|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8172b490dc9f9ac281df}}). What an all-to-all is and how expert parallelism composes with the other parallelisms in training are on {{Distributed Training|n:3c65c17b0d0d8103a916c4824abbd82e}}.

Expert
one of E ordinary FFNs in an MoE layer, narrower than the dense FFN it replaces.
Router (gate)
a d × E linear layer that scores every expert for each token; the k best are used.
Gate weight
the weight gi a chosen expert's output gets, usually its score renormalised over the k chosen.
Shared expert
an expert every token uses, outside the router.
Fine-grained experts
many narrow experts and a larger k instead of few wide ones, at the same compute.
Collapse, MaxVio
traffic piling onto a few experts; MaxVio = (busiest load − mean load) / mean load, 0 when balanced.
Capacity factor
a fixed buffer per expert as a multiple of its average load; assignments past it are dropped.
Expert parallelism (EP)
experts split over GPUs; tokens move to them and back by two all-to-alls per MoE layer.
