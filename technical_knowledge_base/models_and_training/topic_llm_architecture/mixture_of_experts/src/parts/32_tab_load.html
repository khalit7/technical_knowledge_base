How expert load evolves while the tiny GPT trains: the training log records, every 10 steps, how many of the batch's 16,384 assignments (8,192 tokens × 2) went to each expert in each MoE layer, plus the auxiliary loss and the dropped fraction. Same data, same seed, same schedule for every run; only the balancing differs. measured on Apple M1 Pro (MPS), the root's train.py through the batched MoE forward of src/runs/fast_moe.py.

Heat map: one layer over the whole run

Rows are experts, columns are logged steps (every 10), colour is the expert's share of that step's assignments: white-ish is 0, the darkest is half the batch or more. Balanced routing shows as eight bands of the same even intensity (1/8 each).
