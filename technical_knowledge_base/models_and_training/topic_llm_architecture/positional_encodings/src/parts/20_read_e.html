9. Recipes in production


No current model trains its whole run at its final window. The pattern: pretrain short with a base chosen for the long target, extend in a short continued-pretraining stage on long documents with the positions rescaled, and check that short-context quality recovers. Llama 3.1 went from 8K to 128K in six stages over about 800B tokens; DeepSeek-V3 needed 1,000 steps at 32K and 1,000 at 128K; Gemma 3 rescaled "at the end of pre-training". The training side is on {{Data mixing, curricula and continued-pretraining ratios|n:3c65c17b0d0d819ea5d6c59471e66875}} (sections 5 to 7, which hold the former CPT page) and {{Pretraining|n:3c65c17b0d0d814f82cffd0d5c0dd5ba}}; whether a model then uses its window is the {{Long context|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81059ee1ce20c0326dc5}} sibling's subject.

10. Beyond RoPE


NoPE learns order from the causal mask (section 1: the probe finds position in its middle blocks). Kazemnejad et al. found NoPE "outperforms other explicit positional encoding methods" on their length-generalisation tasks and showed it "can represent both absolute and relative PEs, but when trained with SGD, it mostly resembles T5's relative PE attention patterns" ({{NeurIPS 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19466}}). At scale, NoPE alone "lags behind" RoPE on standard benchmarks, although in needle tests it put the most attention on the needle ({{Yang et al. 2025|https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18795}}). Hence the hybrids in section 4: Cohere's RNoPE-SWA (RoPE in sliding-window layers, NoPE in one full-attention layer of four) reached 256K with "almost no loss" on needle tests and trains "nearly 2x faster at 128k", shipped in Command A; Llama 4 adds a position-dependent temperature on its NoPE layers ("inference time temperature scaling", from {{Scalable-Softmax|https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19399}}); SmolLM3 found that removing RoPE from every 4th layer "improves long context performance without affecting short context capabilities". In 2026 hybrids (Kimi K3, Nemotron 3 Super, Granite 4.0-H) the attention layers carry no positions at all, because the recurrent layers between them already see tokens in order. What a long context costs to serve (windows, KV compression, sparse attention) is on {{Long context|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81059ee1ce20c0326dc5}} and {{Attention variants|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8120859ecc2d0236e846}}, the benchmarks on {{Long-context and multimodal benchmarks|n:3ef5c17b0d0d810bb4dfc98919c9be8a}}.


CoPE, FoPE, HoPE and wavelet positions: research not yet used at scale (depth)


    

  • CoPE (Contextual Position Encoding, Meta, 2024). Positions are counted, not indexed: each key gets a gate gij = σ(qi·kj), the position of key j from query i is the sum of the gates between them, and fractional positions interpolate between learned integer embeddings (§4). A head can therefore measure "the i-th sentence" or "the i-th verb" instead of tokens. It solves the selective copy, counting and Flip-Flop tasks where standard encodings fail and improves perplexity on language modelling and code ({{Golovneva et al.|https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18719}}). Not run here: it needs a trained model and a counting task to mean anything.
    • 

  • Fixing the spectrum. {{FoPE|https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.17739}} (Fourier Position Embedding, ICML 2025) treats each dimension as a Fourier series and "zero-outs the destructive frequency components", those insufficiently trained because of truncation. {{HoPE|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21216}} (2024) argues "long-term decay is outdated" and keeps "only high-frequency signals", replacing the others with position-independent components, which is close to what Gemma 4's proportional RoPE ships. {{Wavelet-based positions|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02004}} (ICLR 2025) capture several window sizes at once "without limiting the model's attention field".

11. Depth: common mistakes and design questions


All closed; the measured mistakes run on the tiny GPT. The pairing-layout and sinusoid-scale mistakes are in sections 3 and 2.


Running a learned-position model past its table
It fails loudly, which is the good case (the IndexError in section 2). The fix is a scheme without a table, or retraining the table at the new length; there is nothing to stretch.


Copying another model's rope_theta into a checkpoint's config


Setting original_max_position_embeddings to the new length


Turning on YaRN for every request


"The config has rope_theta, so the model uses RoPE"
Nemotron 3 Super's config says rope_theta 10000; transformers 's modeling_nemotron_h.py defines apply_rotary_pos_emb and calls it nowhere (counted by src/code/code_refs.py: ). Read the modeling code of hybrids before quoting their position scheme.


"NTK scaling means a base like 500,000"
NTK-aware scaling for s = 4 at d = 128 takes 10,000 to 40,890. 500,000 is Llama 3's pretraining base, chosen before training (ABF, Xiong et al.), not a scaling applied to a trained model.


"factor × original length = the context window"
Llama 3.1's 8 × 8,192 = 65,536 is half its configured 131,072; the model was trained at 128K in its long-context stage, so the rescaled spectrum does not have to cover the window on its own. Gemma 3's 8 × 32,768 = 262,144 is twice its 131,072. DeepSeek-V3's 40 × 4,096 = 163,840, Phi-3's 32 × 4,096 = 131,072, DeepSeek-V4-Pro's 16 × 65,536 = 1,048,576 and Mistral Large 3's 36 × 8,192 = 294,912 do match their configs' max_position_embeddings.


"LongRoPE is behind Phi-3's 2M-token context"
The 2,048K result is the LongRoPE paper's, on LLaMA2 and Mistral. Phi-3-mini uses LongRoPE for 128K.


"NoPE layers are worse at retrieval"
The old version of this page said so. In Yang et al.'s needle tests the NoPE variant put the most attention on the needle; its weakness was on standard benchmarks, which is why it is used in one layer of four rather than everywhere.


Design: you must ship a 128K model, have budget to pretrain at 8K, and must not lose short-context quality. What position recipe?
RoPE with a base chosen for the long target at pretraining (ABF: 500,000 or more at 8K), optionally NoPE or a fast-pair-only RoPE in a few global layers; then a continued-pretraining stage on long documents at increasing lengths with YaRN or Llama 3 scaling (a few billion to hundreds of billions of tokens), mixed with short data; ship the scaling in the config, prefer dynamic scaling at inference so short prompts run unscaled, and verify with needle and multi-hop long-context evaluations plus the short-context suite at every stage.


Design: an agent must handle 1M-token contexts on a hybrid. Where do positions go?
Let the linear or SSM layers carry order (they are recurrent), keep the few full-attention layers position-free or fast-pairs-only so nothing goes out of range at 1M (Kimi K3, Nemotron 3 Super, Gemma 4's global layers), and spend the remaining effort on what long context really costs: KV memory and effective use, owned by the {{Long context|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81059ee1ce20c0326dc5}} page.


Design: how would you check that RoPE scaling is configured correctly on a checkpoint you were handed?
Print the resolved rope parameters (transformers 5 standardises them into config.rope_parameters), compare original_max_position_embeddings with the paper's pretraining length, compute the inverse frequencies with the library's function and with your own, and measure loss per position band on long held-out documents with and without the scaling: a correct setup costs a little below the original length and saves a lot beyond it (section 8).
