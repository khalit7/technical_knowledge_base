Best resources

{{How LLMs scaled from 512 to 2M context|https://amaarora.github.io/posts/2025-09-21-rope-context-extension.html}}

Aman Arora, September 2025 (third-party): the best single walkthrough of RoPE, Position Interpolation, NTK-aware, dynamic NTK, NTK-by-parts and YaRN, with code. (50 min)

{{Rotary Embeddings: A Relative Revolution|https://blog.eleuther.ai/rotary-embeddings/}}

EleutherAI, April 2021 (third-party, with the RoPE authors' input): the canonical explainer with derivation and comparisons against learned and T5 positions. (30 min)

{{Train Short, Test Long (ALiBi)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.12409}}

Press, Smith, Lewis 2021 (maker): the linear bias, and the measurement of how far sinusoidal, rotary and T5 positions extrapolate. (45 min)

{{YaRN|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00071}}

Peng et al. 2023 (maker): NTK-aware, dynamic and by-parts scaling formalised, the temperature, and the experiments. (45 min)

{{Position Interpolation|https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15595}}

Chen et al. 2023 (maker): why extrapolation fails, the interpolation bound, 32K in 1,000 steps. (30 min)

{{The Impact of Positional Encoding on Length Generalization|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19466}}

Kazemnejad et al. 2023 (maker): the NoPE case. (40 min)

{{Rope to Nope and Back Again|https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18795}}

Yang et al. (Cohere) 2025 (maker): the RoPE and NoPE hybrid behind Command A, with attention-mass analysis. (35 min)

{{Round and Round We Go!|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06205}}

Barbero et al. 2024 (third-party analysis): what a trained model actually uses each RoPE frequency for. (40 min)

{{transformers modeling_rope_utils.py (v5.20.1)|https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/v5.20.1/src/transformers/modeling_rope_utils.py}}

Hugging Face (implementer): every RoPE type the configs on this page name, as code. (20 min)
