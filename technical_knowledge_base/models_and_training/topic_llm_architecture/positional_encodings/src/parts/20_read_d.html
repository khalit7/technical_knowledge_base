6. What breaks past the trained length

A model trained on length L has seen relative distances below L only, so pair i has seen angles from 0 to Lθi. A fast pair (wavelength shorter than L) has gone round, often many times, and every angle it meets later is familiar. A slow pair (wavelength longer than L) has only covered a wedge; the first distance beyond L pushes it to angles no training example produced, and the scores it feeds are untested. Position Interpolation's authors describe the result as "catastrophically high attention scores that completely ruin the self-attention mechanism" ({{Chen et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15595}}); ALiBi's authors measured rotary models gaining for only 100 to 200 tokens past their training length ({{Press et al. §2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.12409}}). Relative does not mean unbounded.

Correction to the old page
It said RoPE fails past L "because high-frequency dims see unseen rotation angles". It is the other way round: the low-frequency pairs, whose wavelength exceeds L, are pushed into unseen angles, which is why YaRN leaves high frequencies alone and interpolates the low ones fully ({{YaRN §3.2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00071}}). High frequencies matter for the opposite reason: squeezing them (as Position Interpolation does) blurs neighbouring tokens. On the tiny GPT the slow pairs are pairs 7 to 15, wavelengths 353 to 35,333 tokens.

7. Stretching RoPE: the context-extension methods

Every method rewrites the frequencies θi (equivalently, the positions they multiply) of a model that already uses RoPE, then usually fine-tunes briefly at the new length, with s = Lnew / L:

  • Position Interpolation (PI), Meta, June 2023: divide every position by s, so all angles stay inside the trained range. LLaMA 7B to 65B reached 32,768 tokens "within 1000 steps" of fine-tuning, with "minor degradation" on the original 2,048-token window, regressions of up to 2% on standard benchmarks ({{Chen et al.|https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15595}}). The cost: the fast pairs are slowed by s too, so neighbouring tokens look s times closer. Gemma 3 uses it: pretraining at 32K, then "a scaling factor of 8" on the global layers for 128K ({{Gemma 3 §5.3|https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786}}); Hugging Face's type is linear.
  • NTK-aware, bloc97, June 2023: raise the base instead, b′ = b · sd/(d−2) ({{YaRN Eq. 16|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00071}}), "scaling high frequencies less and low frequencies more": pair 0 is untouched and the slowest pair is slowed by exactly s. For the tiny GPT (d = 32) at s = 8, b′ = 91,896 (recompute.py). Good with no fine-tuning, but some pairs end slightly past their trained angles. Dynamic NTK (emozilla) sets s = max(1, l′/L) from the current length l′ at every step, so short inputs run unchanged.
  • YaRN, Peng et al., August 2023: keep pairs that turn more than β times within L (32 for Llama), divide by s those that turn less than α times (1), ramp between, and sharpen the logits with a temperature (formula below). It needed "10x less tokens and 2.5x less training steps than previous methods": Llama 2 7B and 13B were fine-tuned 400 steps at s = 16 (64K) and 200 more at s = 32, which then extrapolated to 128K ({{Peng et al.|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.00071}}). DeepSeek-V2, V3 and V4, Qwen3, gpt-oss, Olmo 3, Kimi K2 and Mistral Large 3 ship it in their configs, and SmolLM3 at inference.
  • Llama 3 scaling (the llama3 rope type, July 2024): the same idea with boundaries set by wavelength. Pairs with a wavelength under L/4 = 2,048 tokens are kept, those over L = 8,192 are divided by 8, and a smooth blend covers those between: 29 kept, 6 blended, 29 divided in Llama 3.1 8B (recompute.py, from its {{config|https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct/blob/main/config.json}} and ). The Llama 3 paper does not describe this scaling; it describes the long-context stage (section 9).
  • LongRoPE, Microsoft, February 2024: one rescaling factor per pair, found by evolutionary search, extending "to an impressive 2048k tokens, with up to only 1k fine-tuning steps at within 256k training lengths" on LLaMA2 and Mistral ({{Ding et al.|https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13753}}). Phi-3-mini's 128K version uses it.

Each method is a rule for how much to slow each pair: PI all of them, NTK a straight line, YaRN and Llama 3 only the slow ones
