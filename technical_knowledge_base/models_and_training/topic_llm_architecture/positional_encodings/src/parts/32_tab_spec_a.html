Defaults reproduce: gpt-oss-120b's 9 kept, 9 on the ramp and 14 interpolated pairs, and its logit factor of about 1.347 squared, are the {{Architecture Gallery|n:3c65c17b0d0d81be8a07f2562fa2030a}}'s figures, recomputed here independently from the config with the ramp ported from Hugging Face's code. DeepSeek-V3's 40 × 4,096 = 163,840 and Phi-3's 32 × 4,096 = 131,072 equal their configs' max_position_embeddings (independently); Qwen3's 4 × 32,768 = 131,072 is the card's 131,072 by construction. Llama 3.1's 65,536 is half its 131,072 window (see Reading, section 11); DeepSeek-V4-Pro's 16 × 65,536 = 1,048,576 and Mistral Large 3's 36 × 8,192 = 294,912 equal theirs. Mistral Large 3's logit factor is shown as 1 because its config switches the YaRN temperature off. Every port agrees with transformers 5.20.1's own functions (src/code/hf_check.py). Gemma 3's trained length of 32K is from its report, not its config. Mirrors are used where the official repository is gated, and the values were checked against the papers where they state them.
