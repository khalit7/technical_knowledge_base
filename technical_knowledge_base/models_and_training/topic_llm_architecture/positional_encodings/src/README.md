Source of the interactive HTML on the Notion page "Positional Encodings" (child of Topic: llm-training-and-post-training).

Build: `sh build.sh` runs `export_data.py` (which runs `recompute.py` and writes `parts/21_js_data.js`), then writes `../index.html` from `parts/` (the parent's slot scheme: 20_read*.html, 3x_tab_*.html, every *.js in its own script, 99_js_tabs.js last; links as {{text|url}}, n:<notion id> or #t-<tab>), and fills in the reading time and the best-resources total.

Tabs: Reading (in one screen, absolute or relative, six schemes on one query, the relative family, RoPE, variants read from configs with layer strips, what breaks past the trained length with the before/after animation, the extension methods, recipes in production, beyond RoPE, common mistakes), Stretch the spectrum (per-pair slowdown of every method on seven configs, and the configs side by side), Further reading.

Shape: no method file covers deep-dive child pages of a stage-type topic; built from the brief. The page is ordered by the reader's path from "how does a model know where a token is" to "what happens past the trained length and how is it fixed", because that second question is what the practical choices are about. The RoFormer, Attention, T5 and Llama 3 paper pages and the Architecture Gallery already own the RoPE dial, the eight-pair dials, Figure 2's decay, toy extrapolation models, the 2017-to-2025 spectrum morph, the sinusoid heatmap, T5's bucket chart and the YaRN bands for gpt-oss, DeepSeek V3 and Kimi K2; those are linked, not rebuilt (see viz_ideas.md).

Files:
- `live.md`: the old Notion text, verbatim (fetched 2026-10-03; Notion's copy dated 2026-09-20), copied from the session transcript by `save_live.py`. `mk_coverage.py` writes `coverage.json` (every fact of live.md, where the page carries it, checked against the built page; 68 of 68 found, 4 corrected).
- `recompute.py` -> `inputs/recompute.json`: ports of Hugging Face's RoPE scaling functions and T5's bucket function, per-config counts (kept, ramped, interpolated pairs; unseen pairs past L), temperatures, ALiBi slopes, layer patterns. The page's JS (`parts/22_js_rope.js`) is the same port; `check_page.mjs` compares the table and the animation's counters with recompute.json.
- `check_page.mjs` (node, from the page folder; needs html_utils/node_modules): every control in light 920 and dark 390 (all presets x methods x steps of the animation, all views of the score chart and the spectrum tab), scanning for NaN, undefined, Infinity, null and errors, plus screenshots `x-*.png` in `.shots/`.
- `inputs/`: config.json files (Llama 3.1 8B, Llama 4 Scout, Qwen3-8B, Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen2-VL-7B, DeepSeek-V2, DeepSeek-V3, gpt-oss-120b, Phi-3-mini-128k, Phi-2, Gemma 3 27B, GLM-4.5, GPT-NeoX-20B, Command A, SmolLM3-3B, BLOOM, MPT-7B, GPT-2, BERT-base, Mistral 7B; gated ones from named mirrors), `t5_base_relative_bias.json` (T5-base's three relative-bias tensors, read by byte range from the released safetensors file), `qwen3_8b_README_long_texts.md`, and `code_extracts.py` (verbatim: transformers' RoPE scaling functions, T5 bucketing, Llama 4 NoPE temperature and layer types, DeepSeek-V3's YaRN softmax scale, Command A's architecture lines).
- `viz_ideas.md`: candidates scored, chosen and rejected.
