LLM Architecture Gallery (rasbt) and the architectural deltas that matter

How to read a 2026 open model's card and config.json in thirty seconds, and the few choices inside the decoder block that actually differ between models: what each token stores, windows, linear layers, experts, position and stack shape. Every model of Sebastian Raschka's LLM Architecture Gallery is rebuilt layer by layer from its own config, checked against the gallery's figures, against transformers 5.20.1's own parsing and against the root page's Config calculator. Configs parsed on an Apple M1 Pro (CPU); no model was run. Data as of October 2026. Reading: 30 min, plus 3 in closed details.
