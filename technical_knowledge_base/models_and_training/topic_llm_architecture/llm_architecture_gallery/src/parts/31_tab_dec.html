The same layers under two conventions

The decoder walks the stack one layer at a time, adding what each layer keeps for one token. The left counter is the card's convention (every growing layer at full cost); the right one credits windows at 131,072 tokens, so a windowed layer adds only its window's share. Skip: show the answer

24 KiB. Only every fourth layer is attention (12 of 48); each keeps 2 × 2 × 256 × 2 = 2,048 bytes per token, so 12 × 2 KiB = 24 KiB. The other 36 layers are Gated DeltaNet and add a fixed state, not a cache. All 48 as attention would be 96 KiB.
