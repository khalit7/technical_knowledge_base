Stability explorer: learning rate × norm placement × QK-norm

Every cell is a real training run of the running example's tiny GPT (6 blocks, d_model 256, LayerNorm, learned positions, 1,500 steps of 32 × 256 tokens, seed 0, AdamW, 100 warmup steps then cosine, gradient clipping at 1.0 unless the row says otherwise), measured on Apple M1 Pro (MPS). The number is the held-out loss at the end (20 fixed validation windows); the unigram floor, the loss of a model that ignores context, is {%unigram%}. Runs from the root's {{Block lab (on Topic: llm-architecture)|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8172b490dc9f9ac281df}} are marked Block lab and were logged every 10 steps without the attention probe; this page's runs record the largest attention logit and the output log Z every 10 steps on a fixed probe batch.
