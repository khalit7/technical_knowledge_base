Papers and reports (primary sources)

{{Xiong et al. 2020, On Layer Normalization in the Transformer Architecture|https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04745}}

Third-party. Why post-norm needs warmup. 40 min

{{Zhang and Sennrich 2019, RMSNorm|https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07467}}

Maker (of RMSNorm). 30 min

{{Wortsman et al. 2023, Small-scale proxies for large-scale Transformer training instabilities|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14322}}

Third-party (Google DeepMind). Attention-logit growth and output-logit divergence reproduced in small models; the most useful paper for this page. 60 min

{{Dehghani et al. 2023, Scaling Vision Transformers to 22 Billion Parameters|https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05442}}

Maker. QK normalisation, section 2. 15 min for section 2

{{Zhai et al. 2023, Attention entropy collapse|https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06296}}

Third-party (Apple). σReparam. 40 min

{{Kim et al. 2025, Peri-LN|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02732}}

Third-party (NAVER). Pre, post and peri-norm at 400M to 3.2B. 45 min

{{Takase et al., Spike No More|https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16903}}

Third-party. Small sub-layers, large shortcut. 40 min

{{Sun et al. 2024, Massive Activations in Large Language Models|https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17762}}

Third-party. 40 min

{{Xie et al. 2025, mHC: Manifold-Constrained Hyper-Connections|https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24880}}

Maker (DeepSeek); {{Zhu et al. 2024, Hyper-Connections|https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.19606}} is the method it constrains. 45 min

{{Wang et al. 2022, DeepNet|https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.00555}}

Maker (Microsoft). DeepNorm. 30 min

{{PaLM, sections 5 and 5.1|https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02311}}

Maker. z-loss and the rollback-and-skip recipe. 10 min for those sections

{{Gemma 2 report, section 2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00118}}, {{Gemma 3 report|https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786}}

Maker. Soft-capping, then QK-norm. 10 min each

{{Yang et al. 2022, Tensor Programs V (μP)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03466}}

Third-party for these models (Microsoft). Hyperparameter transfer across width. 60 min
