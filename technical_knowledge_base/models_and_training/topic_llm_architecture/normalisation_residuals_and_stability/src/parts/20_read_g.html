4. Attention-logit growth: QK-norm, soft-capping, qk-clip

The problem. An attention logit is the score before the softmax, s = q · k / √dh = |q| |k| cos θ / √dh (dh = 32 in the tiny GPT). The norm before attention fixes the size of the input x, but q = Wq Norm(x) and k = Wk Norm(x), and nothing stops Wq and Wk from growing. When a few logits get large, the softmax becomes one-hot: its entropy (−Σ p log p over the keys, about {%uniform_ent%} nats for uniform attention averaged over this sequence's positions, 0 for one-hot) collapses, the softmax's gradient to every other key vanishes, and the head stops learning. ViT-22B's team saw "divergent training loss after a few thousand steps" at about 8B parameters, "caused by extremely large values in attention logits" that gave "(almost one-hot) attention weights with near-zero entropy" ({{Dehghani et al. 2023, section 2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05442}}); {{Zhai et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.06296}} name it attention entropy collapse; {{Wortsman et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14322}} showed the same instability "also appear[s] in small models when training at high learning rates", and that in their sweeps "all points with attention logits above 1e4 diverged" (section 3.3).

The fix. QK-norm normalises each head's query and key vectors before the dot product, so |q| and |k| are set by the norm's learned gains instead of by the projection weights. In the tiny GPT (RMSNorm per head, before RoPE, as in Qwen3):

Where real models put it. Qwen3 normalises per head before RoPE; OLMo 2 normalises the whole query projection (all heads at once) before splitting into heads; Llama 4 Scout uses a weightless L2 norm, only in its RoPE layers, and Maverick turns it off (use_qk_norm false in its config; the code comment says "the 128E model does not use qk_norm"):

QK-norm does not cap the logit at a small number. With unit gains an RMS-normalised head vector has length √dh, so s ≤ √dh = {%sqrt32%} here. But the gains are learned: {%qk_gain_meas%} The ML-fundamentals page measures the same on Qwen3-0.6B (ceilings 184 to 12,897 from its trained gains, real largest logits 12.5 to 48) ({{Normalisation and initialisation, section 6|n:3c65c17b0d0d81369e1cc38ed4e11d48}}). What QK-norm removes is growth through the projection weights and the stream; the gain vector is small and moves slowly.

Soft-capping instead

Gemma 2 squashes the logits themselves: s ← c · tanh(s / c), "50.0 for the self-attention layers and 30.0 for the final layer" ({{Gemma 2 report, section 2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00118}}). tanh is the identity near 0 and flattens at ±1, so logits well under c pass unchanged and none can exceed c. Gemma 3 "replace[d] the soft-capping of Gemma 2 with QK-norm" ({{Gemma 3 report|https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19786}}); Gemma 4's config keeps the final-layer cap at 30 and has no attention cap, with QK-norm in every attention layer (configs read 2026-10-19):

{%cap_meas%}

Without QK-norm: MLA and qk-clip. Multi-head latent attention never materialises per-head keys at decode (they are absorbed into the query, see the {{Attention variants|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8120859ecc2d0236e846}} child), so a per-head key norm has nowhere to go. DeepSeek-V3 instead "employs additional RMSNorm layers after the compressed latent vectors" ({{DeepSeek-V3 report, section 4.2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19437}}). Kimi K2 keeps the forward pass untouched and, after each optimiser step, rescales a head's query and key weights when that head's largest logit passes a threshold (qk-clip, part of MuonClip); the {{Moonshot AI: Kimi|n:3c65c17b0d0d81f5abf5dbdbac821a12}} page animates it.

When it matters and how to check. Log the largest attention logit per layer on a fixed batch every few hundred steps (one extra forward with the scores computed explicitly, as ns_model.py does; fused attention kernels never return them). Steady growth past a few hundred, together with falling entropy in the same layers, predicts trouble before the loss shows it; Wortsman et al. used exactly this to predict that a 4.8B model "will be unstable at LR 1e-2", and it was.
