9. In production now

The pattern. RMSNorm everywhere; pre-norm as the base; since 2024 a second control on the attention logits in most new families (QK-norm in Qwen3, Gemma 3 and 4, OLMo 2 and 3, GLM-4.5, MiniMax-M2, Llama 4 Scout; qk-clip in Kimi K2; a cap in Gemma 2), and a few departures from the plain identity residual (Gemma's and OLMo's normalised branch outputs, MiniMax-M1's DeepNorm, DeepSeek-V4's four mixed streams). The ML-fundamentals page measures the residual streams of six released models ({{Depth and placement tab|n:3c65c17b0d0d81369e1cc38ed4e11d48}}); the root's {{Architecture map|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8172b490dc9f9ac281df}} places these choices on the 2017 to 2026 timeline.
