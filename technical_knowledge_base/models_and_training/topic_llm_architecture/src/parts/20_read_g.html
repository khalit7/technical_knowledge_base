Optional: across the whole block

A design question that needs every part of the map: 128K context, 32 sequences, one 80 GB GPU

Budget first: an 8B-class model's BF16 weights take about 16 GB, leaving about 64 GB for 32 × 131,072 = 4.2M cached tokens: about 15 KB per token across all layers. Llama 3.1 8B's GQA needs 128 KiB per token (512 GiB in total), 9 times over. So change the mixer, not the size: for example 32 layers of which 8 are full attention with 2 KV heads of 128 and an FP8 cache (4 KiB per token, 16 GiB in total) and 24 linear or SSM layers (a fixed state of about 24 MiB per request, 768 MiB in total), leaving room for activations. Name the price: exact recall over 128K rests on 8 layers, so measure it with a long-context retrieval benchmark (wall 4), and train the long-context phase with the target RoPE scaling (wall 3). Alternatives: MLA (36 KiB per token at this shape in BF16, still too big alone at batch 32 without FP8 and fewer layers), or GQA with sliding windows on 5 of 6 layers (derived in src/read/derive.py).

Real failures from the labs: errors and silent bugs in a hand-written block
  • KV heads not expanded (8 query heads, 2 KV heads, PyTorch 2.14.1 SDPA without enable_gqa): RuntimeError: The size of tensor a (8) must match the size of tensor b (2) at non-singleton dimension 1.
  • Expanded with repeat instead of repeat_interleave: no error, the shapes match, but each query head reads the wrong KV head; the trained GQA-2 model's held-out loss goes from 2.103 to 2.991.
  • Keys not transposed (q @ k): RuntimeError: Expected size for first two dimensions of batch2 tensor to be: [8, 32] but got: [8, 256].
  • Learned positions past the table: IndexError: learned positions stop at 256; got position 256 (the tiny GPT raises it on purpose).
  • Silent one-line attention bugs on the trained tiny GPT, loss on the running sequence 1.96 when correct: scores not divided by √dh 2.88; heads split with .view but no transpose 3.64; heads merged without transposing back 5.03 ("Lily saw a little girl saw a little girl was the girl"); causal mask left out 3.64, and a model trained without it learns to copy the next token, held-out 0.21 with the leak and 7.02 once the mask is back (sections 1 and 3 of {{Families and objectives|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8105b616c17c1439f766}}).
  • Post-norm at the default learning rate: stuck at 5.943, the unigram loss (section 7).
  • MoE without balancing: one expert takes 48% of a layer's assignments and 14 of 48 experts get under 1% ({{Mixture of experts|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81fda06bf955af7c2b58}}).

A tiny decoder-only GPT trained here from scratch: d_model 256, 6 layers, 8 heads of 32, context 256, learned positions, a GELU FFN of width 1,024, pre-norm LayerNorm and tied embeddings, 5,836,032 parameters (4,721,920 outside the embeddings). It trains on TinyStories (CDLA-Sharing-1.0; one shard of 122M tokens) with its own byte-level BPE of 4,096 tokens, 1,500 steps of 8,192 tokens (12.3M tokens, about 5 minutes on the laptop's GPU), and reaches a held-out loss of 2.30 (2.16 with RoPE instead of learned positions, the setting the attention runs use). The running sequence is 256 tokens of a held-out story, Roxy the rhinoceros and the icy hill, never seen in training; names repeat ("Roxy" 11 times), which attention, copying and cache demos need measured.
