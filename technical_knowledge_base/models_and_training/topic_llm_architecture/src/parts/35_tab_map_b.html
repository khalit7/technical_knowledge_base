2. Same model, two eras

Take a real model's width, depth and head count, build it the 2018 way (post-norm LayerNorm, a learned position table, one K and V head per query head, a 4d GELU feed-forward), then apply the shifts that model adopted, one at a time, in the order the field adopted them. The drawing is one block; the counters are that model's whole cache and one layer's parameters, recalculated at every step from its config.json. Choose a model, then step or play.

How the counters are made: src/map/shifts.py. KV per token counts every cache-growing layer at full cost (bf16); the 131,072-token sequence credits sliding windows (a windowed layer keeps its last W entries) and lists a hybrid's fixed recurrent state apart. Attention parameters are for one attention layer; FFN parameters for one FFN layer (for MoE: total, and active per token including the router). For MoE targets the dense FFN shown before the MoE step has the same active width as the final MoE layer, so that step shows parameters multiplying at constant compute. DeepSeek-V3's MHA baseline uses 128-dimension heads, as the {{DeepSeek page|n:3c65c17b0d0d81a2ae7ec6796947a235}} draws it. These are calculations from configs, not measurements; full model sums are in the {{Config calculator|#t-calc}}.
