Pre-norm against post-norm: on the Normalisation page

Where the norm sits decides what happens to the residual stream this walk traces: under pre-norm (the tiny GPT and every current LLM) the stream only grows, block after block; under post-norm it is reset to a fixed size after every sub-layer. The matched runs on this token and this model (the same seed and budget at five learning rates, the gradient reaching each block, and why post-norm at the default rate learns only token frequencies) are section 2 of {{Normalisation, residuals and stability|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8163b50dc5e9d9240e89}}, which steps through four placements on the same trace.
