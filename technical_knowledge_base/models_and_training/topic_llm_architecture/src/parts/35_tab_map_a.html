Architecture map

Every major open decoder from GPT-2 (2019) to Kimi K3 (July 2026) on the same axes: attention, position, normalisation, feed-forward, sequence mixer, depth and width. 46 models, each row read from its own config.json where one is public, with dates, licences and published parameter counts linked to the model card or paper. KV-cache figures are calculations from the configs (bf16, 2 bytes per number), not measurements.

What this tab is for
Use it to place any model you meet: which of the big shifts (pre-norm, RMSNorm, RoPE, gated FFN, GQA, sliding windows, mixture of experts, MLA, QK-norm, hybrids, trained sparse attention) it adopted, and what each choice costs in cache and parameters. The mechanisms themselves are taught in the {{Reading|#t-read}} tab and the child pages; the per-layer detail of every model in Sebastian Raschka's gallery lives on the {{LLM Architecture Gallery|n:3c65c17b0d0d81be8a07f2562fa2030a}} page; full parameter and FLOP breakdowns are in the {{Config calculator|#t-calc}}.
