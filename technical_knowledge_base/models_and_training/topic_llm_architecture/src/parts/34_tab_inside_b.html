Attention maps: what each head reads

The problem. "Attention lets every token look at every earlier token" says what a head may do. A trained model uses that freedom very unevenly, and the patterns it settles on are what the architecture choices on this page are really about: a head that only ever reads the previous token needs no long cache; a head that parks most of its weight on token 0 is why streaming caches keep that token.

How to read the explorer. The grid has one cell per head of SmolLM2-135M (rows are layers 0 to 29, columns heads 0 to 8), coloured by the score you pick. Click a cell to draw that head's map: row i is query token i, column j key token j, darker means more weight, and everything above the diagonal is empty because of the causal mask. Click a token in the text to see what it reads in that head.

What the explorer shows (scores defined under the grid, all on the running text unless noted):

  • Previous-token heads put most of their weight on token i−1. They exist from layer 1 on in SmolLM2; the strongest is (score ). They are the first half of an induction circuit (next section): they copy "what came before me" into each token's residual stream, so a later head can search for it.
  • First-token heads appear abruptly at layer 12: below it, the mean weight on token 0 is at most in every layer; from layer 12 on it is to per layer (mean over the 9 heads, queries from position 8). That is the attention sink (section 4).
  • Spread is the head's entropy divided by the most it could be (log of the number of visible keys): 1 means uniform reading, near 0 means one key. Layer 0 heads are the broadest; they average over the recent context.

When it matters. Head patterns decide what can be pruned, cached or windowed. Gemma 3 and gpt-oss make most layers local (a sliding window) because most heads read nearby tokens; they keep some global layers for the few heads that do not ({{Architecture map|#t-map}}). How to check it on any model: eager attention, a few hundred tokens, and the three scores above: about 20 lines (head_scores in src/inside/make_page_data.py).
