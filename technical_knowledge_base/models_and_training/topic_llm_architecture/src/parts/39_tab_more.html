Best outside resources

{{Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCc8FmEb1nY}}third-party

Andrej Karpathy, January 2023. A decoder written live in PyTorch, from bigrams to self-attention, residuals and LayerNorm. Pairs with {{nanoGPT|https://github.com/karpathy/nanoGPT}} and the newer {{nanochat|https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat}}. about 2 h

{{The Annotated Transformer|https://nlp.seas.harvard.edu/annotated-transformer/}}third-party

Harvard NLP (2018, updated 2022): the original paper interleaved with runnable code, encoder-decoder and all. about 1 h 30 min

{{The Illustrated Transformer|https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/}}third-party

Jay Alammar, 2018. Pictures of Q, K, V and multi-head attention; the gentlest first read. about 30 min

{{How to Scale Your Model: All the Transformer Math|https://jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book/transformers/}}maker

Google DeepMind authors, 2025. Parameters, FLOPs and KV cache per component, worked; the best companion to section 8. about 1 h

{{Transformer Inference Arithmetic|https://kipp.ly/transformer-inference-arithmetic/}}third-party

kipply, 2022. KV cache, memory-bound decode and FLOP counting for serving. about 40 min

{{Transformer Math 101|https://blog.eleuther.ai/transformer-math/}}third-party

EleutherAI, April 2023. The 6N rule, memory for training, worked numbers. about 25 min

{{The Big LLM Architecture Comparison|https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/the-big-llm-architecture-comparison}}third-party

Sebastian Raschka, from July 2025, kept updated, with the {{LLM Architecture Gallery|https://sebastianraschka.com/llm-architecture-gallery/}}: what changed inside the block from DeepSeek-V3 onward, with diagrams. about 1 h

{{The Transformer Family, Version 2.0|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2023-01-27-the-transformer-family-v2/}}third-party

Lilian Weng, January 2023. A survey of attention, position and efficiency variants up to 2022; a map of names. about 1 h 30 min

{{A Mathematical Framework for Transformer Circuits|https://transformer-circuits.pub/2021/framework/index.html}}maker

Anthropic, December 2021. The residual stream as a communication channel, heads as independent read-write operations; with {{In-context learning and induction heads|https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/in-context-learning-and-induction-heads/index.html}} (2022). about 2 h for both

{{interpreting GPT: the logit lens|https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/AcKRB8wDpdaN6v6ru/interpreting-gpt-the-logit-lens}}third-party

nostalgebraist, 2020. Decode the residual stream after every layer. about 20 min

{{Rotary Embeddings: A Relative Revolution|https://blog.eleuther.ai/rotary-embeddings/}}third-party

EleutherAI, 2021. RoPE derived and compared, from the team that first trained it at scale. about 25 min

{{A Visual Guide to Mixture of Experts|https://newsletter.maartengrootendorst.com/p/a-visual-guide-to-mixture-of-experts}}third-party

Maarten Grootendorst, 2024. Routers, top-k, capacity and balancing in pictures; his {{visual guide to Mamba|https://newsletter.maartengrootendorst.com/p/a-visual-guide-to-mamba-and-state}} does the same for SSMs. about 40 min each

{{State Space Duality (Mamba-2)|https://tridao.me/blog/2024/mamba2-part1-model/}}maker

Tri Dao, May 2024, four parts. How SSMs and linear attention are the same computation, by Mamba's co-author. about 1 h

{{Stanford CS336: Language Modeling from Scratch|https://cs336.stanford.edu/}}third-party

Course with lectures on architectures, hyperparameters and MoE, and assignments that build and train a transformer. a course: tens of hours; the architecture lecture about 1 h 20 min
