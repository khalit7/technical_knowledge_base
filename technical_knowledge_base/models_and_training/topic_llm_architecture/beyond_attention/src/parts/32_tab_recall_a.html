Recall probes

Three measurements of the weakness a fixed state is known for, each on fixed, seeded inputs: two pretrained models trained on the same data, the topic's tiny GPTs at the same budget, and toy models trained only on recall. Scripts in src/recall/; outputs in src/recall/out/. measured on Apple M1 Pro

1. The running sequence, token by token: attention against SSM

Per-token loss of the 256-token Roxy story under two tiny GPTs trained at the same budget. Colour: how much worse (orange) or better (blue) the second model is than the first on that token. Boxed tokens are pieces of " Roxy". Step through to see where the difference piles up.

2. Pythia-160M against Mamba-130M: copying and a phone book

Both trained by their authors on the Pile (300B tokens) with the GPT-NeoX tokenizer: the closest public same-data pair of attention and an attention-free SSM. Copy: L random common one-token words, a blank line, the same words again; the score is the share of the second copy's tokens predicted exactly (argmax, teacher forcing), 12 lists per length. Phone book: N lines "First Last: ddd-dddd", two example lookups from the book, then a name; the greedy answer must contain the number; 20 books per size. fp32 on CPU, transformers 5.20.1 (Mamba on its reference PyTorch path, section 12 of Reading). Pythia's KV cache and Mamba's state are equal in size at 32 tokens (18,432 numbers per token against 589,824).

3. A made-up name, recalled after a gap: the tiny GPTs

"Once upon a time, there was a little dog named Zubo." then d filler tokens from validation stories, then " One day, Zubo". Score: loss on the name's pieces after its first piece (" Z" is free to guess; "u" "bo" need the earlier mention). Solid: the name was mentioned; dashed: control, a different name was mentioned. 10 names × 8 fillers per point.

4. Multi-query associative recall, trained from scratch

Zoology's MQAR on the topic's tiny GPT code: n (key, value) pairs, then every key again in random order; 256 key ids and 256 value ids, so chance is 1/256. Two layers, dmodel 128, 8,000 steps of 64 sequences at lr 1e-3, seed 0; accuracy on 2,048 fresh sequences. Models: two attention layers (RoPE), two SSM layers with state 16 or 64 per channel (NoPE), and SSM then attention.
