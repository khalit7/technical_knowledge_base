Beyond attention: SSMs, Mamba, linear attention, RWKV and hybrids

Sequence mixers that keep a fixed-size state instead of a growing KV cache, derived from attention itself: linear attention as a recurrence, S4 to Mamba to Mamba-2's state space duality, RWKV, the delta rule (DeltaNet, Gated DeltaNet, Kimi Delta Attention) and the hybrids that ship in 2026 (Jamba, Nemotron-H, Granite 4.0-H, Falcon-H1, Qwen3-Next, Qwen3.5, Kimi Linear, Nemotron 3 Super). Measured on the topic's tiny GPT, Pythia-160M against Mamba-130M and trained toys on an Apple M1 Pro; state and cache from real configs; published results kept apart.
