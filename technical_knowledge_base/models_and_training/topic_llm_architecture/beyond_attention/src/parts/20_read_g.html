The average hides the recall gap. Without a single attention layer the SSM model matches attention on held-out text (2.152 and 2.146 over two seeds against 2.160 and 2.175) and beats attention without positions (2.389) by a wide margin: order comes free from the recurrence. It is behind on the running sequence (2.024 and 2.126 against 1.741 and 1.859). One attention layer in six (2.089 held-out, 1.688 on the running sequence) beats full attention on both; two in six give 2.082 and 1.629. The hybrids are the best runs here, as Jamba, Waleffe et al. and Kimi report at scale; on held-out text the gaps are a few hundredths of a nat, larger than the seed spread for the hybrids, within it for the SSM. What the SSM costs here, and the training curves (depth)

The SSM has 879,840 more parameters (its mixer has 409k parameters per layer against attention's 262k) and trains about 2.4 times slower here, in its quadratic training form on MPS. At equal wall-clock time on this Mac attention would have seen more tokens; on a GPU with SSD kernels the ratio is different (not run here). Tokens per second are rough (shared machine) and the SSM's quadratic training form is not Mamba's kernel: the losses and the cache arithmetic transfer to a datacenter GPU, the timings do not.
