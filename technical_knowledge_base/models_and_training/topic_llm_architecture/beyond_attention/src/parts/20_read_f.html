7. Hybrids and their ratios

The problem a hybrid solves. A recurrent layer is cheap but cannot look things up exactly; an attention layer can but costs a growing cache. Most of a model's layers do work that a recurrence handles (local syntax, running summaries); a few must retrieve. So keep a few attention layers and make the rest recurrent. The cache then grows only in the attention layers, and the recurrent layers add a fixed state. Read from each model's config (layer counts by type, cache in bf16, state in fp32, one sequence):

ModelRecurrent layersAttention layersRatioKV per tokenState per sequenceKV = state atCache + state at 128K

derived from the configs with the root Config calculator's formulas (window.CC, reused unchanged) plus two additions for Kimi Linear's KDA and Qwen3.5 (src/mem/statecalc.py, checked against the page's JavaScript). "KV = state at" is the context at which the attention layers' cache has grown to the size of the recurrent state: below it the state is the larger memory cost. Llama 3.1 8B, for comparison, keeps 128 KiB per token and 16 GiB at 128K. More models and any context in {{State vs KV cache|#t-mem}}.

How the ratios were chosen (published).

  • Jamba (AI21, {{2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19887}}): 1 attention per 7 Mamba layers, chosen because at 1.3B and 250B tokens "There is no substantial difference between 1:3 and 1:7 ratios" (HellaSwag 65.1 both; NQ 16.5 against 16.0) and 1:7 is cheaper. The same study shows why attention is kept at all: on IMDB, QuAC and NarrativeQA pure Mamba scores 48.8, 20.2, 27.7 against attention's 84.1, 27.9, 45.8 and the hybrid's 90.9, 26.6, 43.7, because "the pure Mamba model often does not follow the correct format" (answering "Very Good" or "3/10" where "Positive" or "Negative" was asked). They conjecture that "the lack of an attention mechanism in the pure Mamba model makes it difficult for it to learn in-context" and found 12 induction heads in the hybrid's attention layers. authors
  • NVIDIA: {{Waleffe et al. 2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07887}} trained 8B Mamba, Mamba-2, Transformer and a hybrid of "43% Mamba-2, 7% attention, and 50% MLP layers" on the same data; the pure SSMs "lag behind Transformers on tasks which require strong copying or in-context learning abilities (e.g., 5-shot MMLU, Phonebook)", the hybrid "exceeds the 8B Transformer on all 12 standard tasks we evaluated (+2.65 points on average)". Nemotron-H follows it: "roughly 8% of the total layers", 4 attention layers of 52 in the 8B (4 attention to 24 Mamba-2 among mixers), "up to 3x faster at inference". Nemotron 3 Super (March 2026, 120B total, 12B active, up to 1M context) keeps 8 attention layers of 88 (counted from its config), as "global 'anchors'". authors
  • IBM Granite 4.0-H: "Mamba-2 layers and conventional transformer blocks sequentially in a 9:1 ratio" (36 to 4), "over 70% reduction in RAM needed to handle long inputs and multiple concurrent batches". vendor
  • Kimi Linear swept the ratio of KDA to MLA layers at equal FLOPs (Table 1, validation perplexity, lower is better): 3:1 5.65, 1:1 5.66, 7:1 5.70, 15:1 5.82, and pure attention (0:1) 5.77. "A higher ratio (e.g., 7:1) produced a comparable training loss but led to significantly worse validation performance, while a lower ratio (e.g., 1:1) maintained a similar validation loss but at the cost of increased inference overhead." Qwen made the same 3:1 choice. authors
  • Parallel instead of interleaved. Falcon-H1 (TII, {{2025|https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22448}}) runs attention and Mamba-2 side by side in every layer and concatenates their channels, SSM : attention : MLP channels "roughly following 2:1:5"; "Having more attention channels significantly degrades the performance". Hymba (NVIDIA) also fuses "attention and SSMs in parallel" heads. Every Falcon-H1 layer therefore has both a cache and a state (the table's 72 + 72). authors
  • The counter-example. MiniMax-01 used lightning (linear) attention with "one transformer block with softmax attention ... every seven transnormer blocks" (80 layers). MiniMax-M2 went back to full attention: at small scale the hybrid "looked just as good", but "the price paid became obvious at a larger scale: the model had clear deficits in complex, multi-hop reasoning tasks" ({{MiniMax, 30 October 2025|https://huggingface.co/blog/MiniMax-AI/why-did-m2-end-up-as-a-full-attention-model}}). vendor

Positions. Jamba: "positional embeddings or mechanisms like RoPE are not necessary, and so we do not use any explicit positional information" (adding RoPE changed nothing measurable, their Table 8); Nemotron-H: "We do not use any position embeddings"; Granite 4.0-H: NoPE; Kimi Linear: NoPE in MLA. The recurrent layers already see order. In transformers 5.20.1, modeling_jamba.py and modeling_nemotron_h.py each define apply_rotary_pos_emb and never call it (a pattern search in src/code/quotes.py: 1 definition, 0 calls; the {{Positional Encodings|n:3c65c17b0d0d81a5b7c9e578fcdfac00}} child found the same), so a config's rope_theta there is dead. Qwen3-Next and Qwen3.5 keep partial RoPE (64 of 256 dimensions) in their attention layers; Falcon-H1 uses RoPE with base 1011.
