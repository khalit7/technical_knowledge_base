7. Hybrids and their ratios

The problem a hybrid solves. A recurrent layer is cheap but cannot look things up exactly; an attention layer can but costs a growing cache. Most of a model's layers do work that a recurrence handles (local syntax, running summaries); a few must retrieve. So keep a few attention layers and make the rest recurrent. The cache then grows only in the attention layers, and the recurrent layers add a fixed state that is reserved from the first token. Step a real request through both:

How the ratios were chosen. Three findings set the one-in-four-to-eight rule:

  • Attention is kept for in-context learning. In Jamba's ablations (AI21, {{2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.19887}}) pure Mamba scores 48.8 on IMDB against attention's 84.1 and the hybrid's 90.9, because "the pure Mamba model often does not follow the correct format" (answering "Very Good" or "3/10" where "Positive" or "Negative" was asked); the hybrid's attention layers hold 12 induction heads. authors
  • Few attention layers suffice. Kimi Linear swept KDA : MLA at equal FLOPs (Table 1, validation perplexity): 3:1 5.65, 1:1 5.66, 7:1 5.70, 15:1 5.82, pure attention (0:1) 5.77. Jamba found "no substantial difference between 1:3 and 1:7 ratios" and took the cheaper 1:7; Qwen chose 3:1. authors
  • Too few fails at scale. MiniMax-01 used one softmax block per seven linear (lightning attention) blocks; MiniMax-M2 went back to full attention because at scale "the model had clear deficits in complex, multi-hop reasoning tasks" ({{MiniMax, 30 October 2025|https://huggingface.co/blog/MiniMax-AI/why-did-m2-end-up-as-a-full-attention-model}}). vendor
The three findings in full: Jamba's ablations, Kimi's sweep, MiniMax's account (depth)

Jamba (1.3B, 250B tokens): HellaSwag 65.1 for both 1:3 and 1:7, NQ 16.5 against 16.0, "There is no substantial difference between 1:3 and 1:7 ratios". On IMDB, QuAC and NarrativeQA pure Mamba scores 48.8, 20.2, 27.7 against attention's 84.1, 27.9, 45.8 and the hybrid's 90.9, 26.6, 43.7. They conjecture that "the lack of an attention mechanism in the pure Mamba model makes it difficult for it to learn in-context" and found 12 induction heads in the hybrid's attention layers. authors

Kimi Linear on the sweep: "A higher ratio (e.g., 7:1) produced a comparable training loss but led to significantly worse validation performance, while a lower ratio (e.g., 1:1) maintained a similar validation loss but at the cost of increased inference overhead." Qwen's blog on its 3:1: "When we mix Gated DeltaNet with standard attention at a 3:1 ratio ... the model consistently outperforms any monolithic architecture". authors

MiniMax-01 used lightning (linear) attention with "one transformer block with softmax attention ... every seven transnormer blocks" (80 layers). For M2, at small scale the hybrid "looked just as good", but "the price paid became obvious at a larger scale: the model had clear deficits in complex, multi-hop reasoning tasks". vendor

Positions. Jamba, Nemotron-H, Granite 4.0-H and Kimi Linear's MLA layers use no positional encoding (NoPE): the recurrent layers already see order. Qwen3-Next and Qwen3.5 keep partial RoPE (64 of 256 dimensions) in their attention layers; Falcon-H1 uses RoPE with base 1011. Measured here at the same budget: the tiny GPT with one attention layer in six (layer 3, NoPE) and two in six (layers 3 and 6), section 9.

NVIDIA's, IBM's and TII's ratios, and the RoPE that is never applied (depth)
  • NVIDIA: {{Waleffe et al. 2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07887}} trained 8B Mamba, Mamba-2, Transformer and a hybrid of "43% Mamba-2, 7% attention, and 50% MLP layers" on the same data; the pure SSMs "lag behind Transformers on tasks which require strong copying or in-context learning abilities (e.g., 5-shot MMLU, Phonebook)", the hybrid "exceeds the 8B Transformer on all 12 standard tasks we evaluated (+2.65 points on average)". Nemotron-H follows it: "roughly 8% of the total layers", 4 attention layers of 52 in the 8B (4 attention to 24 Mamba-2 among mixers), "up to 3x faster at inference". Nemotron 3 Super (March 2026, 120B total, 12B active, up to 1M context) keeps 8 attention layers of 88 (counted from its config), as "global 'anchors'". authors
  • IBM Granite 4.0-H: "Mamba-2 layers and conventional transformer blocks sequentially in a 9:1 ratio" (36 to 4), "over 70% reduction in RAM needed to handle long inputs and multiple concurrent batches". vendor
  • Parallel instead of interleaved. Falcon-H1 (TII, {{2025|https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22448}}) runs attention and Mamba-2 side by side in every layer and concatenates their channels, SSM : attention : MLP channels "roughly following 2:1:5"; "Having more attention channels significantly degrades the performance". Hymba (NVIDIA) also fuses "attention and SSMs in parallel" heads. Every Falcon-H1 layer therefore has both a cache and a state (the table's 72 + 72). authors

The quotes on positions: Jamba, "positional embeddings or mechanisms like RoPE are not necessary, and so we do not use any explicit positional information" (adding RoPE changed nothing measurable, their Table 8); Nemotron-H, "We do not use any position embeddings". In transformers 5.20.1, modeling_jamba.py and modeling_nemotron_h.py each define apply_rotary_pos_emb and never call it (a pattern search in src/code/quotes.py: 1 definition, 0 calls; the {{Positional Encodings|n:3c65c17b0d0d81a5b7c9e578fcdfac00}} child found the same), so a config's rope_theta there is dead.
