3. SmolLM2-135M: retrieve one number, 1K to 16K tokens

The pass-key prompt of the {{Eviction lab|#t-evict}}, full cache, at seven lengths and five needle depths, four seeds per cell (140 prompts). SmolLM2-135M's config says max_position_embeddings 8,192 with RoPE base 100,000; its model card's evaluation table names the base model "SmolLM2-135M-8k" (read 2026-10-18); the SmolLM2 paper describes a 2K to 8K extension for the 1.7B model and does not say whether the small models had one ({{Allal et al. 2025|https://arxiv.org/html/2502.02737}}, vendor). Cells give correct answers out of 4.

Read it as a wall, not a slope: inside 8,192 tokens the misses are near-misses (at 1K and 2K always the first digit; at 8,192 only with the number 8K tokens back), past it every answer is broken text ("a1...." for 66226 at 12,288). Short prompts are not easier for this 135M model (1K: 75%), a reminder that 20 prompts per length is a coarse instrument. A real model would also need its rope_scaling set to go past its length; SmolLM2-135M ships with none.

4. How to measure effective context on your model

  • Loss by position on long held-out documents (section 1): cheap, shows the position wall at once, but averages hide retrieval failures (the Eviction lab's new-story loss barely moved while retrieval collapsed).
  • Retrieval at length and depth (section 3): a needle grid is the minimum; it is a string-matching test, so a pass says little about reasoning over the context. For anything past retrieval use the suites on {{Long-context and multimodal benchmarks|n:3ef5c17b0d0d810bb4dfc98919c9be8a}} (RULER, NoLiMa, MRCR), at your length, with your prompt format.
  • Read the config: max_position_embeddings is an allocation, not a training fact (Qwen3 says 40,960, MiniMax-M1 10,240,000: the root's findings), rope_scaling says whether an extension method is switched on and from what original_max_position_embeddings.
