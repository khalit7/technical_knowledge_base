A transformer's cost per token grows with everything it has already read: the KV cache (the keys and values of every earlier token, kept so they are not recomputed) grows linearly with the context, prefill attention grows with its square, positions past the trained length were never seen, and a model that clears all three may still not use what it read. This page takes those four walls in turn: what each fix shrinks, what it costs in quality (measured on SmolLM2-135M and the topic's tiny GPT on this machine, and from the papers), and how to check it on your own model. It is a child of {{Topic: llm-architecture|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8172b490dc9f9ac281df}}, whose section 9 is this page in one paragraph. Reading time about 22 minutes (measured from the words outside widgets at 200 a minute), plus about 4 for the folded mistakes and answers; the three labs add about 25 with their controls.

Three numbers carry the page. Llama 3.1 8B needs 128 KiB of cache per token, so one 128K-token request holds as much memory as the model's weights, and an 80 GB GPU serves 3 of them (section 1, {{Memory wall|#t-wall}}). Eviction to a quarter of SmolLM2's cache keeps its unseen-story loss within a few percent while a question-agnostic policy drops pass-key accuracy from 97% to 0% (section 3, {{Eviction lab|#t-evict}}). And the context a model accepts is not the context it uses (section 7, {{Effective context lab|#t-eff}}).
