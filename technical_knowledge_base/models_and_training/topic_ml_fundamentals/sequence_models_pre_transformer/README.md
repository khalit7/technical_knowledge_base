# Sequence models: the pre-transformer lineage

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3c65c17b0d0d818da4ded61ace6847ce
Site: https://khalit7.github.io/technical_knowledge_base/models_and_training/topic_ml_fundamentals/sequence_models_pre_transformer/

Interactive page: `index.html`, built from `src/` (`sh src/build.sh`) and published to Notion as an HTML-only page. How sequences were modelled before the transformer, one fix at a time: static word vectors (word2vec, GloVe), recurrent networks and backpropagation through time, the LSTM and GRU gates, seq2seq and the attention that removed its bottleneck, and WaveNet's dilated convolutions. Every animation runs real trained weights or real GloVe vectors. Part of Topic: ml-fundamentals. Tabs: Reading, Word vectors, Further reading.
