Sequence models: the pre-transformer lineage

How sequences were modelled before the transformer, one fix at a time: static word vectors (word2vec, GloVe), recurrent networks and backpropagation through time, the LSTM and GRU gates, seq2seq and the attention that removed its bottleneck, and WaveNet's dilated convolutions.
