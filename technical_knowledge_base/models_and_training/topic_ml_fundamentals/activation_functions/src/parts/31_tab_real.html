Inside real models

What the activation actually outputs in three released models, measured on every feed-forward hidden unit, every layer and every token of the same text: OPT-125m (ReLU), GPT-2 small (GELU, tanh form) and SmolLM2-135M (SwiGLU).

Try: pick a model and a measure. Move the threshold that counts a value as "near zero", or the activity below which a unit counts as dead, and watch which layers respond. Tap a layer's bar to see how often each of its units is on.

How it was measured

  • Text: WikiText-2 (raw), test split, the whole of it: 283,287 tokens for OPT and GPT-2 (they share a tokenizer), 301,948 for SmolLM2 (Salesforce/wikitext), in chunks of 511 tokens after the model's start token, which is left out of every count.
  • What is recorded: the input of the down projection, captured with a forward hook: ReLU(xW1 + b1) for OPT, GELU(xW1 + b1) for GPT-2, SiLU(xWgate) ⊙ xWup for SmolLM2; in float32 on CPU with PyTorch 2.14 and Transformers.
  • Near zero compares each value with the root mean square of its own token's hidden vector, so that one threshold means the same in every layer and model; exact zeros count as near zero. Values are binned in fifths of a decade from 10−5, and the page interpolates within a bin.
  • On means: OPT, output above 0; GPT-2, pre-activation above 0 (where GELU is close to the identity); SmolLM2, gate pre-activation above 0 (where SiLU passes most of u). A unit that is never on in GPT-2 or SmolLM2 still outputs small values and still receives a small gradient; in OPT it outputs exactly 0 and receives none.
  • Caveats: one text from one domain; Voita et al. used over 20 million tokens from many domains and up to 66 billion parameters, so a unit that is never on here may fire on other text. Script: src/real_models.py; results in src/inputs/real_models.json.
