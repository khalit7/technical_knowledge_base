Further reading

Best resources

{{GLU Variants Improve Transformer (Shazeer 2020)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.05202}}

The four-page paper behind GEGLU and SwiGLU, with the 2/3 width rule and the line about divine benevolence. (20 min)

{{Gaussian Error Linear Units (Hendrycks and Gimpel 2016)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08415}}

GELU's definition, its stochastic-regulariser motivation, and both approximations. (45 min)

{{Searching for Activation Functions (Ramachandran, Zoph and Le 2017)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05941}}

The automated search that found Swish, x σ(βx), and its comparisons against ReLU. (45 min)

{{PyTorch: non-linear activations|https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/nn.html#non-linear-activations-weighted-sum-nonlinearity}}

Exact formulas, defaults and variants; the definitions this page's checks use. (15 min)

{{Neurons in Large Language Models: Dead, N-gram, Positional (Voita, Ferrando and Nalmpantis 2023)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.04827}}

Dead neurons and token detectors across the OPT family, 125m to 66b; what the Inside real models tab checks. (40 min)

{{ReLU Strikes Back (Mirzadeh et al. 2023)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04564}}

Activation sparsity in OPT, and turning SiLU and GELU models back into ReLU models for faster inference. (40 min)

{{Efficient BackProp (LeCun, Bottou, Orr and Müller 1998)|http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/publis/pdf/lecun-98b.pdf}}

Sections 4.3 to 4.5: why inputs and activations should be zero-centred, and the scaled tanh. (30 min)
