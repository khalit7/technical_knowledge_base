Activation functions

The nonlinearity between two linear maps: the saturating classics (sigmoid, tanh), the ReLU family and its dead units, the smooth ReLUs (GELU, SiLU, Mish), the gated units that every current LLM uses (GLU, ReGLU, GEGLU, SwiGLU) with the 2/3 width rule, the output layer, and what these functions actually do inside three released models. Every curve on this page is computed in your browser and checked against PyTorch; the dead-unit and real-model numbers were measured offline. Sources are linked inline; checked 3 October 2026.
