Defaults across models: what landmark models actually used

Twenty-one models from AlexNet (2012) to Kimi K2 and SmolLM3 (July 2025), on the same twenty building blocks: activation, normalisation, initialisation, optimiser, learning-rate schedule, weight decay, dropout, clipping, loss tweaks, batch and precision. Every cell gives its source (paper, official code or official config.json), the source's date, and whether the value is published, derived here by arithmetic (formula shown), inherited from a predecessor the paper says it follows, or unconfirmed. A cell the maker left empty says not disclosed and is coloured, because what a lab stays quiet about is part of the picture.

Each column header links to the child page that explains it: {{Activations|n:3c65c17b0d0d819f8049c255e6f206e2}}, {{Normalisation and initialisation|n:3c65c17b0d0d81369e1cc38ed4e11d48}}, {{Optimisers|n:3c65c17b0d0d817080bbc90954681d09}} (optimiser, learning rate, clipping, batch), {{Regularisation|n:3c65c17b0d0d81e988b5c3cb5e197f98}}, {{Loss functions|n:3c65c17b0d0d8161a72bc8c572f37d55}}, and for number formats {{Quantization and Precision|n:3c65c17b0d0d817b8f41ff66fba4cd0e}} on the training topic.

How to read it

  • One checkpoint per row. Settings are never spliced across sizes or versions: GPT-3 is the 175B, LLaMA the 65B, Llama 2 the 70B, Llama 3 the 3.1 405B, OLMo 2 the 7B (November 2024), ViT the H/14 pretrained on JFT-300M, CLIP the ViT-L/14. Other sizes appear only in notes.
  • Config files describe checkpoints, not runs. A config.json's dropout, initializer_range and torch_dtype are what a library needs to load the model; DeepSeek-V3's says 0.02 where its report says the run used 0.006. Training configs (OLMo 2, SmolLM3) and official training code (BERT) are trusted for training values; release configs only for architecture.
  • Inherited is not stated. Where a paper says it "largely follows" an earlier one (GPT-2 after GPT, GPT-3 after GPT-2, Chinchilla after Gopher), the value is the predecessor's, in grey italics, with the predecessor's source.
  • Peak learning rates are not comparable across optimisers, batch sizes and widths; they are recorded, not ranked. For the data and compute of each training stage, see {{Open recipes compared|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b6876ee72b7056793b}} on the training topic, which owns them.
  • Dates. Each source carries its own date; everything was read on 3 October 2026. The two 2025 MoE rows (Qwen3, Kimi K2) rest on technical reports that may be revised.
