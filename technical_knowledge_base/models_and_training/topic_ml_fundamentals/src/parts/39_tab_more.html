Key papers

Each links to its page in the Papers database, which checks the paper's claims and usually runs a toy of it; each page states its own reading time.

{{Attention Is All You Need (2017)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81999af7f16f8ed8ee9e}}

Where the sequence lineage ends; post-norm, Adam with warmup and inverse-square-root decay, label smoothing 0.1; its Then and now tab turns the 2017 block into a 2026 one.

{{BERT (2018)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81e5ad9bd09cbf18ad7c}}

An encoder trained by masked-token prediction; behind BERTScore and the encoder half of contrastive learning.

{{CLIP (2021)|n:3c65c17b0d0d8194a85dfc5f3bf3f799}} and {{ViT (2020)|n:3c65c17b0d0d811fa231cfcde6c1954d}}

Symmetric InfoNCE over a batch of 32,768, run live in a tiny CLIP; and the vision transformer that most contrastive and self-supervised backbones now use.

{{WSM (2025)|n:3c75c17b0d0d8105b452d2c1bc1d087a}} and {{Chinchilla (2022)|n:3c65c17b0d0d8116b7ddffba5c599f3f}}

Replacing the learning-rate decay by merging checkpoints; and why a cosine schedule must match the run's length.

{{OLMo 2 (2025)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81fb9857fb956165ae1c}}, {{Llama 3 (2024)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81aca58ccb9d720b474e}}, {{DeepSeek-V3 (2024)|n:3c65c17b0d0d815fb8dac9ba1e35ab81}}

Three disclosed 2024 to 2025 recipes: initialisation scale, norm placement and QK-norm, warmup-cosine, constant-then-decay, clipping.

{{Mamba (2023)|n:3c65c17b0d0d8179984fc3a1fee4c36a}}

Recurrence returns as a selective state-space model: the postscript to the sequence lineage.

Best resources for the whole topic

Andrej Karpathy, A Recipe for Training Neural Networks

Why training fails silently, and the habits that catch it: the practical companion to {{When training goes wrong|#t-debug}}. (about 35 min)

Google Research, Deep Learning Tuning Playbook

A systematic method for hyperparameter tuning and debugging, from the folded "Debugging training" page. (about 2h for the main document)

Lin et al., Focal Loss for Dense Object Detection

The focal loss of step 3 and the reference for training on extreme class imbalance. (45 min)

Sebastian Ruder, An overview of gradient descent optimization algorithms

The canonical survey, SGD through Adam and Nadam: the families of step 5 in full. (about 40 min)

Lilian Weng, Lil'Log

Surveys spanning contrastive and self-supervised learning and beyond. (about 3h for the core surveys)

Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville, Deep Learning

The textbook behind every stage of the step. (about 20h)
