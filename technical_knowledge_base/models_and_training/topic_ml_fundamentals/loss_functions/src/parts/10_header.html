Loss functions

What a loss makes a model estimate, and what it does to the gradient: regression losses as noise models (mean, median, quantile), what outliers and leverage points do to a fit, cross entropy and its margin-based relatives, the losses that act on the logits themselves (z-loss, soft-capping), and the adversarial loss.
