{{scikit-learn: choosing a scoring function|https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html}}

A practical guide to strictly consistent scoring functions: pick the functional first, then the loss. (20 min)

{{Small-scale proxies for large-scale Transformer training instabilities (Wortsman et al. 2023)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14322}}

Output-logit divergence reproduced in small models, and z-loss as the fix. (40 min)
