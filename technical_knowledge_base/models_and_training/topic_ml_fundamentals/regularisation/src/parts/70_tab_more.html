Best resources

{{Dropout paper (Srivastava et al. 2014, JMLR)|https://jmlr.org/papers/v15/srivastava14a.html}}

The original, still the clearest statement of the ensemble interpretation; Appendix A is the practical guide (retention 0.5 hidden, 0.8 inputs). (1h 30m)

{{Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization (Loshchilov and Hutter 2017)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101}}

The AdamW paper: why L2 in the loss is not weight decay for adaptive optimisers. (45 min)

{{Deep Learning book, chapter 7: Regularization (Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville)|https://www.deeplearningbook.org/contents/regularization.html}}

The systematic treatment: norm penalties, constraints, augmentation, early stopping as L2, dropout. (1h 30m)

{{Least Angle Regression (Efron, Hastie, Johnstone and Tibshirani 2004)|https://hastie.su.domains/Papers/LARS/LeastAngle_2002.pdf}}

The lasso path algorithm this page runs, on the diabetes data of the Coefficient paths tab. (1h)

{{Regularization and variable selection via the elastic net (Zou and Hastie 2005)|https://hastie.su.domains/Papers/elasticnet.pdf}}

The lasso's three limits and the grouping effect. (40 min)

{{Understanding deep learning requires rethinking generalization (Zhang et al. 2017)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03530}}

Networks fit random labels; explicit regularisers help but do not explain generalisation. (40 min)

{{mixup: Beyond Empirical Risk Minimization (Zhang et al. 2018)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.09412}}

Mixup, its ImageNet and corrupted-label results. (30 min)

{{CutMix (Yun et al. 2019)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.04899}}

CutMix, and one table comparing a dozen regularisers on ResNet-50 and PyramidNet. (30 min)

{{Optimal Regularization Can Mitigate Double Descent (Nakkiran et al. 2020)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01897}}

Tuned L2 removes the interpolation peak; the random-features experiment repeated in section 8. (40 min)

{{Why Do We Need Weight Decay in Modern Deep Learning? (D'Angelo et al. 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04415}}

Weight decay as a change to training dynamics, from ResNets to LLMs. (45 min)

{{Dropout as a Bayesian Approximation (Gal and Ghahramani 2016)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02142}}

MC dropout: uncertainty from the spread of thinned networks. (45 min)
