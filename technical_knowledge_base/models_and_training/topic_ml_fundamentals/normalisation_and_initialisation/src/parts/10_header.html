Normalisation and initialisation

How inputs are put on a common scale (feature scaling), how layers inside a network re-impose a fixed scale at every step (BatchNorm, LayerNorm, RMSNorm, GroupNorm, InstanceNorm, QK-norm) and where in the block they sit, and how the starting weights are scaled so the signal neither vanishes nor explodes (Xavier, He, LeCun, residual scaling, μP). Every normalisation on this page is computed exactly in your browser and checked against torch.nn; the depth figures are measured in six released models.
