Best resources

An Introduction to Statistical Learning (ISLR / ISLP)

Free book; the standard reference for everything on this page, with R and Python labs. about 15 h

StatQuest (Josh Starmer)

Video playlists on trees, boosting, SVMs and PCA: the fastest intuition refreshers. about 6 h for those playlists

XGBoost: introduction to boosted trees

The second-order objective, leaf weights and split gain of section 3, from the source. about 25 min

scikit-learn user guide

Algorithm-by-algorithm reference with practical caveats; this page's models match its implementations. docs, about 1 h for the core pages

The Elements of Statistical Learning

The deeper companion to ISLR: boosting as forward stagewise additive modelling, random forests' variance formula, SVMs and kernels. Free PDF. chapters 9, 10, 12, 15: about 8 h

TabArena and BeyondArena leaderboards

The living version of section 4, by data size, split type and tuning budget; check the date. 15 min

Why do tree-based models still outperform deep learning on tabular data? (Grinsztajn et al. 2022)

The three inductive-bias experiments (smoothness, uninformative features, rotation). 1 h

Random Search for Hyper-Parameter Optimization (Bergstra and Bengio 2012)

The low-effective-dimension argument, with the original figures. 45 min

How to Use t-SNE Effectively (Distill)

Live t-SNE runs showing what the plots do and do not mean. 20 min
