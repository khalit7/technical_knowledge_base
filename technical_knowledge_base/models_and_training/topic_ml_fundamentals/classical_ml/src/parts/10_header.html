Classical ML

The methods before and beside deep learning: linear models and naive Bayes, trees and their ensembles, support vector machines, nearest neighbours, clustering, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction, and how their hyperparameters are searched. Every model on this page runs live, written to match scikit-learn and checked against it; the tabular benchmark evidence is dated.
