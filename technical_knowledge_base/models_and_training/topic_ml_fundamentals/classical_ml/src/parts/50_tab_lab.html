Boundary lab

Seven classifiers on the same 2-D data, two side by side. The shading is the model's probability of the blue class (for the SVMs, a squashed decision value); the solid line is where it decides; filled points are the 70% it was trained on, hollow points the 30% it never saw. Every model is this page's own code, written to do exactly what scikit-learn does (see the check line under the panels).

Try: Circles with logistic regression against the RBF SVM (a straight line cannot, a kernel can). XOR with a depth-1 tree, then depth 2. kNN with k = 1 against k = 31 (memorising against smoothing). A random forest with 1 tree, then 100 (the jagged edges average out). Gradient boosting at 5 stages, then 300 with depth 5 (watch the test accuracy fall while training accuracy reaches 100%).
