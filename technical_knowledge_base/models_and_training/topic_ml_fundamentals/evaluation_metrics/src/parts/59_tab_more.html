Best resources

Google ML Crash Course: classification

Precision, recall and ROC with interactive thresholds. about 45 min

The Relationship Between Precision-Recall and ROC Curves (Davis and Goadrich 2006)

The imbalanced-data argument, formally; dominance and interpolation in PR space. 45 min

On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks (Guo et al. 2017)

Reliability diagrams, ECE, and temperature scaling. 40 min

scikit-learn: metrics and scoring

Exact definitions of every classification metric, averaging options and their edge cases. docs, about 40 min

The advantages of the MCC over F1 and accuracy (Chicco and Jurman 2020)

Why one number should use all four cells. 30 min

BLEU (Papineni et al. 2002)

The original definition: clipping, geometric mean, brevity penalty, corpus level. 30 min

A Call for Clarity in Reporting BLEU Scores (Post 2018)

Why BLEU numbers differ across papers; sacreBLEU. 20 min

ROUGE (Lin 2004)

ROUGE-N, L, W and S defined. 30 min

METEOR (Banerjee and Lavie 2005)

Staged alignment, recall-weighted Fmean, fragmentation penalty. 30 min

chrF (Popović 2015)

Character n-gram F-score. 15 min

BERTScore (Zhang et al. 2019, arXiv:1904.09675)

Embedding-based text evaluation. 45 min

MoverScore (Zhao et al. 2019)

Earth mover's distance on contextual embeddings. 40 min

WMT22 Metrics Shared Task: Stop Using BLEU (Freitag et al. 2022)

Overlap metrics against learned metrics, judged by expert MQM annotation. 60 min

To Ship or Not to Ship (Kocmi et al. 2021)

2.3 million human judgements on which metric to trust for system comparisons. 45 min

Hugging Face: perplexity of fixed-length models

Computing perplexity with a sliding window, and what the stride does. 15 min

Adding Error Bars to Evals (Miller 2024)

Standard errors, clustering and paired differences for LLM evaluations. 40 min

Statistical significance tests for MT evaluation (Koehn 2004)

The paired bootstrap for comparing two systems. 20 min

Hugging Face evaluate docs

Implementations of BLEU, ROUGE, METEOR, perplexity and friends. docs, about 30 min
