Threshold lab: one real classifier, every metric at once

The 872 SST-2 validation sentences, each scored by three real classifiers trained on the SST-2 training set (section 1 of Reading). Move the threshold and every number on the page moves together: the four cells, the ratios built from them, and the point on each curve. Click a cell to read the sentences in it.

Try: switch to "Rare positives" and press "Best F1", then "Best MCC": the two metrics want different thresholds. Then pick naive Bayes and look at the reliability diagram.
