Collapse lab: what stops every input mapping to the same point

One small encoder, trained eleven ways on two augmented views of real hand-written digits, with no labels. Each objective below either has a defence against collapse or has had one removed. Pick a pair and play training forward: the left run keeps its defence, the right run loses it. The labels are used only to measure, never to train.

Try: start with "SimSiam, stop-gradient": both runs have the same encoder, predictor and loss, and the only difference is one detach(). Watch the embedding std on the right fall to zero while the loss on the right keeps improving. Then try DINO without centering and without sharpening: two different ways to collapse, told apart by the teacher's entropy.
