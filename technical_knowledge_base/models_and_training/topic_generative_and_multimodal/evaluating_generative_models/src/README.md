# src: Evaluating generative models (prefix ev)

Child page 9 of Topic: generative-and-multimodal. `sh build.sh` writes `../index.html` from `parts/`.

## Shape
Part B of `html_utils/methods/topic_pages.md` (a child page): Reading organised by the subject's own logic (in one screen, the problem, FID from zero, sample count, alternatives, pitfalls, prompt alignment, human preference, VLM benchmarks, video and audio, mistakes, interview questions), three tabs (Metric comparator, Pitfall lab, Which metric?), Further reading. No departure from the method. Departures from the papers' setups, each said on the page: FD-DINOv2 with ViT-B/14 (paper: L/14), CMMD sweep on CLIP B/32 (paper: L/14 at 336 px, checked on 1,000-image subsets), precision/recall in Inception and DINOv2 space (paper: VGG-16), VQA checks with SmolVLM-500M and hand-written questions.

## Runs (`runs/`; GPU steps under the shared bench lock, PYTORCH_MPS_HIGH_WATERMARK_RATIO=0.45, LOW 0.4)
Run with `GM_SCRATCH=<shared setup folder> nice -n 15 $GM_SCRATCH/venv/bin/python -I <script>`; a GPU step goes through a wrapper that runs the guard, acquires `lock.sh acquire bench ev`, runs one process and releases.
1. `ev_gen.py` (MPS): eight sets of 10,000 images from existing checkpoints (DDPM 8,000 steps from Diffusion from zero; the Flow lab model; the Latent lab VAE), real and constructed sets -> `GM_SCRATCH/ev/sets.npz`, `out/gen.json`.
2. `ev_feats.py inception|dino|clipB|clipL` (MPS): features of the reference, the eight sets and the pitfall variants (clipL, the CMMD paper's model, on 1,000 images per set only: it ran at about 10 images/s, `out/clipL_full_attempt.json`) -> `GM_SCRATCH/ev/feat_*.npz`, `out/feats_*.json` (with checks: the setup's reference statistics and 2.54 floor; the Vision encoders page's DINOv2 vectors).
3. `ev_metrics.py` (CPU): FID, KID, FD-DINOv2, CMMD, classifier FD at six N, precision/recall, pitfalls -> `out/metrics.json` (with checks against scipy's sqrtm and torch-fidelity's KID).
4. `ev_sd.py` (MPS): SD 1.5 fp16, 8 prompts x 2 seeds x 4 guidance scales -> `GM_SCRATCH/ev/sd/*.png`, `out/sd_gen.json`.
5. `ev_clipscore.py` (CPU): CLIPScore B/32 and CLIP L/14-336 cosine -> `out/clipscore.json`.
6. `ev_vqa.py` (MPS): SmolVLM-500M yes/no questions -> `out/vqa.json`.
7. `make_data.py` (CPU; reads `out/reading.json`, the rendered word count written by `check_page.mjs`) -> `parts/20_js_ev_0data.js`, `out/page_data.json`; `check_data.py` proves the page embeds it; `check_page.mjs <shots>` clicks every control at 390 dark and 920 light.
