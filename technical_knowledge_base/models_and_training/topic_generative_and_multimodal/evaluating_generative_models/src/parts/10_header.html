Evaluating generative models: FID, CLIPScore, preference and VLM benchmarks

How a generated image is scored, taught from zero: what FID actually computes and why it moves with sample count, compression and resizing; the alternatives (KID, precision and recall, FD-DINOv2, CMMD) measured side by side on the same eight sets of samples; prompt alignment (CLIPScore against question-answering checks) on Stable Diffusion with and without guidance; human preference arenas and reward models; vision-language benchmarks; video and audio in brief. Runs on the topic's tiny Fashion-MNIST models, Stable Diffusion v1.5 and SmolVLM on an Apple M1 Pro; published results kept apart. Child page of Topic: generative-and-multimodal.
