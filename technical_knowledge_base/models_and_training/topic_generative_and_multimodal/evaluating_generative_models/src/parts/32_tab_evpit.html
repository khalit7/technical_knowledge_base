Pitfall lab

Each case processes images one way or another before they reach Inception and reports the FID against the floor (what "nothing wrong" scores with clean processing). The first cases use the real training images as the "model", so any change from the floor is pure artefact. measured on Apple M1 Pro: 10,000 images per set, reference = the 10,000 test images, torch-fidelity 0.4.0 Inception features.
