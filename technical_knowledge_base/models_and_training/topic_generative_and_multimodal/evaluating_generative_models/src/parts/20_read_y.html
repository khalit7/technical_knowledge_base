Depth: mistakes, design questions and provenance

Four mistakes the sections above do not show

Fewer samples than feature dimensions. With N below 2,048 the Inception covariance is singular (below 128 for the classifier's features). Scoring 20 samples in the classifier space during this page's CPU smoke test, the topic's own fid.py printed LinAlgWarning: Matrix is singular. The result might be inaccurate or the array might not have a square root. The number still comes out, and means little.

Taking the first N samples of a class-sorted set.

Mixing CLIPScore conventions. 2.5 · cos with ViT-B/32 (Hessel et al.), 100 · cos with ViT-L/14 (many text-to-image papers), with or without "A photo depicts": three scales for one idea. The same SD image scores .

Reading a small FID change as progress. FID went from 9.1 to 8.4 after your change: same reference set, N, resize, file format and code? Several seeds? A 0.7 difference between single runs can be noise. Check precision and recall, the samples, and a second metric with different features (FD-DINOv2 or CMMD).

Two design questions (interview rehearsal)

You ship a new image model weekly. Design its evaluation. Fixed, versioned harness. Fidelity and diversity: FD-DINOv2 and FID (torch-fidelity, PNG, clean resize) on 30k to 50k samples against a fixed reference, plus precision and recall, with seed spread. Prompt following: GenEval-style detector checks and VQA-based scores on a fixed prompt suite, broken down by skill (counting, text, spatial). Preference: a held-out reward model that is never the training reward, plus a weekly blind human side-by-side against the current model (a few hundred pairs, Bradley-Terry with intervals). Safety and regressions: a red-team prompt set. Gate releases on human preference, not on any single automatic metric; track automatic metrics for regressions between human rounds.

How would you measure and reduce VLM hallucination? Measure with POPE-style balanced yes/no questions (random, popular, adversarial absent objects; report yes-rate and F1), a caption hallucination rate, and a blind baseline. Reduce with higher resolution or more image tokens, negative examples ("no") in instruction data, preference tuning against hallucinated answers; re-measure on held-out images.
