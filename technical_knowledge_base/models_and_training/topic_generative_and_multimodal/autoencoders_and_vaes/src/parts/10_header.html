Autoencoders and VAEs: latents and image tokenizers

How images are squeezed into the small continuous latents that diffusion models work in and the discrete tokens that autoregressive models predict: the ELBO in working form, posterior collapse, why reconstructions blur, VQ-VAE and its training tricks, FSQ and LFQ, VQGAN's perceptual and adversarial losses, the autoencoders inside real image generators, and how to evaluate a tokenizer. Tiny models trained on an Apple M1 Pro, nine pretrained autoencoders run on the same 16 images, published results kept apart. Data as of October 2026.
