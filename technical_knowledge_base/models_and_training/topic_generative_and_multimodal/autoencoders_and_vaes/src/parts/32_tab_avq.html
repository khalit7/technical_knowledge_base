Every way to turn a latent into tokens, trained on the same images with the same encoder and decoder: VQ in four variants, FSQ and LFQ, at about 128 and about 1,024 codes. First a 2-D toy you can watch, then the eleven trained tokenizers. Extends Reading sections 5 and 6.

How each quantizer cuts the space (a 2-D toy)

600 fixed points stand for encoder outputs; each colour is one code's share. VQ's 16 codes start in a tiny square at the origin and are trained by moving each code to the mean of its points; restarts re-seed unused codes on data points; FSQ and LFQ have fixed grids. illustrative, computed in the page (the encoder is frozen here; in real training it moves too).
