12. Evaluating an autoencoder or tokenizer

Fidelity to the image

PSNR: 10 log10(2552 / MSE) in dB; pixel-exact, so it prefers blur to plausible detail. SSIM: local means, contrasts and correlations in small windows, 1 is identical. LPIPS: distance between deep features weighted by a model fitted to human judgements, 0 is identical; tracks what people notice best.

Realism of the set

rFID: Fréchet distance between Inception features of reconstructions and originals over a set, usually the 50,000 ImageNet validation images at 256 × 256 (LlamaGen: "r-FID, reconstruction-FID on 256×256 ImageNet 50k validation set", {{arXiv 2406.06525v1|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06525v1}}). Punishes blur, needs thousands of images; protocols differ (FSQ uses the training set, {{arXiv 2309.15505v2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15505v2}}).

The tokens themselves

Codes used and perplexity (section 8); tokens and bits per image (section 10); encode and decode time and memory; for a continuous latent, its per-channel statistics (section 11).

What it is for

Generation FID of a generator trained on the latents, at fixed compute: the only measure of "easy to model", and it can move against reconstruction (sections 10 and 11).

Same PSNR, different picture: the SD reconstruction against a blur tuned to the same pixel error

For each image a Gaussian blur of the original whose strength was searched until its PSNR equals the SD 1.5 reconstruction's. Feature distance: cosine distance between the 2,048-d Inception pool features (torch-fidelity's inception-v3-compat), a stand-in for LPIPS (root src/latent/perc_demo.py); lower is closer. 160 × 160 crops. measured (CPU)

On the cat and the Hubble field the reconstruction is far closer in deep features than the equal-PSNR blur (cat against ; Hubble against ): the blur erases faint galaxies and fur the decoder redraws. SSIM does not agree everywhere (on the Hubble field it prefers the blur, against ). Comparing papers: LPIPS scales and rFID protocols differ (depth)

LPIPS as computed here (torchmetrics 1.9.0, AlexNet, v0.1 weights) ranges from to across the nine, yet SDXL's Table 3 reports LPIPS of 0.88 to 0.96 and BFL's table 0.27 to 1.67, without saying how they were scaled. NVIDIA's Cosmos report re-ran rivals under its own protocol and got different rFIDs from their own papers (LlamaGen at f = 16: 1.657 in Cosmos's Table 8 against 2.19 in LlamaGen's). Numbers compare only at the same resolution, dataset, image count and metric implementation; re-measure the candidates you are choosing between on your own images.

How to check a tokenizer you are about to use. Round-trip a few hundred of your own images; look at full-resolution crops of text, faces and texture; compute PSNR, SSIM and LPIPS; compare float16 with float32 once; check the normalised latent's mean and std are near 0 and 1; count codes used; and train a small generator on the candidates before committing.
