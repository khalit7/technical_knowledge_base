Papers

Neural Discrete Representation Learning (VQ-VAE), 2017maker

The codebook, the straight-through estimator, the two extra losses, EMA updates in the appendix. 40 min

Taming Transformers for High-Resolution Image Synthesis (VQGAN), 2020maker

Perceptual and patch-adversarial losses with an adaptive weight; a transformer over the codes. 45 min

Finite Scalar Quantization: VQ-VAE Made Simple, 2023maker

FSQ, its recommended levels, and VQ against FSQ across codebook sizes. 30 min

Language Model Beats Diffusion: Tokenizer is Key to Visual Generation (MAGVIT-v2), 2023maker

Lookup-free quantization and its entropy penalty; vocabulary size against reconstruction and generation. 40 min

Improved Variational Inference with Inverse Autoregressive Flow, 2016maker

Where free bits comes from (its appendix). 20 min for the free-bits part

Variational Lossy Autoencoder, 2016maker

Why a powerful decoder ignores the code, and how to choose what the code keeps. 40 min

The Perception-Distortion Tradeoff, 2018maker

Why sharper and lower pixel error are different goals. 30 min

The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Deep Features as a Perceptual Metric (LPIPS), 2018maker

LPIPS and the human-judgement data behind it. 30 min
