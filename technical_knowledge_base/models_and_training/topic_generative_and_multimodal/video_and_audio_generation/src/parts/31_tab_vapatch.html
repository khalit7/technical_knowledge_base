How many tokens a clip becomes and what one denoiser pass and one whole clip cost, for any of eight video models at any size, length and step count. Presets are each model's own defaults, read from its diffusers config and pipeline (or paper). Extends Reading section 1 and section 4.

One pass: linear layers (2 P N, blue) against self-attention (4 L N2 D, orange)

Every model at this resolution, by duration

Formula: tokens = latent frames × (H / cs / p) × (W / cs / p), latent frames = 1 + (F − 1) / ct (Movie Gen: F / 8); FLOPs per pass = 2 P N + 4 L N2 D, checked against PyTorch's FLOP counter on Wan 2.1 1.3B (Reading section 4). Text tokens, cross-attention, the VAE and the text encoder are not included. Parameter counts from the configs on the meta device (Movie Gen: stated). Sizes the VAE and patch cannot divide are rounded down, as the latent grid would be, and flagged.
