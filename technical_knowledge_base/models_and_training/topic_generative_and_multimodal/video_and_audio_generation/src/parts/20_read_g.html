9. How video and audio are evaluated

Human judgement is still the reference for both media; every automatic number measures one slice of quality and has a known blind spot. The Fréchet scores (FVD, FAD), their sample-count and preprocessing traps and the evidence on how well they track people are taught once, on {{Evaluating generative models|n:3fc5c17b0d0d81c890f4f62543312faa}} (its section 9); this table keeps what is specific to each medium.

MeasureWhat it doesBlind spot
FVDThe Fréchet distance on features of I3D, an action-recognition network, so each clip is scored as a whole ({{Unterthiner et al. 2018|https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01717}})Mostly scores single frames: a motionless video can score well (content bias, {{Ge et al. 2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12391}}) independent
VBench16 dimensions (subject consistency, motion smoothness, temporal flickering, dynamic degree, spatial relationship and others), each scored by its own model ({{Huang et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17982}}); its total is what Wan's 86.22% refers toEach dimension inherits its scoring model's errors; a total hides which dimension moved
Human A/B, arenasMovie Gen, finding that FVD did not track human scores, evaluated with human A/B tests on its public 1003-prompt Movie Gen Video Bench; blind pairwise arenas (the Artificial Analysis Video Arena, Elo from human votes) are the closest thing to an independent rankingArenas move weekly, so this page does not copy them; vendor A/B tests choose their own prompts
MOS, MUSHRAListening tests: MOS (mean opinion score, 1 to 5) for speech naturalness; MUSHRA (0 to 100, with a hidden reference and low-pass anchors) for codecs, as in the EnCodec and Mimi tables of section 7Slow and costly to run
WER, speaker similarityFor speech generation: word error rate of an ASR model on the output (VALL-E used HuBERT-Large, F5-TTS Whisper-large-v3) and speaker similarity between output and prompt from a speaker-verification network (WavLM-based in both)Say nothing about prosody or naturalness
FAD, CLAP scoreFAD (Fréchet Audio Distance) is the Fréchet recipe on VGGish audio embeddings ({{Kilgour et al. 2018|https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.08466}}); CLAP score measures text-audio agreement with a contrastive audio-text model, the audio cousin of CLIPScoreModerate agreement with listeners; both depend on the feature network

The two numbers measured on this page (spectral error and SNR) are sanity checks of a codec on one clip, not quality scores.
