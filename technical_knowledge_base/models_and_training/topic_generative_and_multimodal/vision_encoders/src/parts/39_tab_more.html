Knowledge base

The paper page: a toy ViT trained live, ViT against a ResNet, the data-scale experiment and the paper's tables. about 40 min

Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision (CLIP)

A toy CLIP, softmax against sigmoid on a toy batch, zero-shot against few-shot, the tables rebuilt. about 45 min

Contrastive and self-supervised learning

InfoNCE, collapse and its defences (BYOL, DINO, VICReg), MAE's real reconstructions and I-JEPA. about 35 min

Embedding models: training, choosing and fine-tuning

The text-side counterpart: how retrieval embeddings are trained and judged. about 30 min

Document ingestion and multimodal retrieval

Page-image retrieval for documents, where vision encoders meet RAG. about 35 min

Long-context and multimodal benchmarks

How VLMs, and through them their encoders, are scored. about 30 min

RoFormer: rotary position embedding

The rotary embedding that 2-D RoPE in Qwen2.5-VL's encoder and DINOv3 extends to rows and columns. about 30 min

Papers (primary sources for this page)

DINOv2 (Oquab et al. 2023)

Curated LVD-142M, self-distillation plus iBOT, distillation into small models; Tables 4 and 10 are the frozen-feature references. about 60 min

Vision Transformers Need Registers (Darcet et al. 2023)

High-norm outlier tokens, where they appear, and the four-token fix. about 30 min

DINOv3 (Siméoni et al. 2025)

A 7B self-supervised ViT, Gram anchoring for dense features, distillation into a family. Long report; read sections 3 to 5. about 75 min

SigLIP 2 (Tschannen et al. 2025)

Sigmoid loss plus captioning, self-distillation, masked prediction, NaFlex and active data curation; Table 1 for zero-shot and retrieval. about 40 min

SigLIP (Zhai et al. 2023)

The pairwise sigmoid loss, chunked implementation and batch-size study. about 30 min

Masked Autoencoders (He et al. 2021)

75% masking, encoder on visible patches, linear probing against partial fine-tuning (§4.3). about 40 min

DeiT (Touvron et al. 2020)

ViTs trained on ImageNet only, with a distillation token. about 30 min

LiT: locked-image tuning (Zhai et al. 2021)

Freeze the image tower, train only the text tower. about 30 min

DataComp (Gadre et al. 2023)

A benchmark where only the data varies; the filtering baselines. about 45 min

MetaCLIP: Demystifying CLIP Data (Xu et al. 2023)

CLIP's curation rebuilt: metadata, substring matching, balancing. about 35 min

LAION-5B (Schuhmann et al. 2022)

The open CLIP-filtered dataset; §3 describes the 0.28 filter. about 40 min

ALIGN (Jia et al. 2021)

Scale over cleaning: 1.8B noisy pairs. about 25 min

NaViT (Dehghani et al. 2023) and FlexiViT (Beyer et al. 2022)

Native aspect ratio by sequence packing; one model for many patch sizes. about 30 min each

Perception Encoder (Bolya et al. 2025)

Contrastive training's best features sit in intermediate layers; alignment tuning to bring them out. about 50 min

Qwen2.5-VL report and PaliGemma (Beyer et al. 2024)

Two VLMs' encoder choices: a native-resolution ViT trained in the VLM, and a frozen-then-tuned SigLIP So400m. about 30 min each for the encoder sections
