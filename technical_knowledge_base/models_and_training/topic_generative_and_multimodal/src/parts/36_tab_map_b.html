One prompt, six pipelines: what changed from Stable Diffusion 1.5 to Z-Image

The same text prompt passes through the same four boxes in every open text-to-image model: a text encoder turns words into vectors, a denoiser turns noise into a latent image step by step, guided by those vectors, a scheduler decides the steps, and a VAE decoder turns the latent into pixels.
