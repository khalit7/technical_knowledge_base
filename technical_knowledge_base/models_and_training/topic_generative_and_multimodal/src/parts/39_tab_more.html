This page

{{Reading|#t-read}}

The whole topic from zero: the five families, latents, diffusion and flow side by side, latent diffusion and DiT, vision encoders, VLMs, native multimodal models, video and audio, evaluation, choosing, mistakes and interview questions. about 30 min, plus 10 for the folded answers and code

{{Latent lab|#t-latent}}

A tiny autoencoder and VAE trained on Fashion-MNIST, the same image through each encoder and decoder, a latent-space explorer, the beta trade-off, codebooks, the Stable Diffusion VAE and its scaling factor. time set when the lab is finished

{{Diffusion lab|#t-diff}}

A tiny class-conditional DDPM, the forward process, schedules, the same noise through 1 to 250 steps with DDPM and DDIM, guidance from 0 to 10, and Stable Diffusion v1.5 for comparison. time set when the lab is finished

{{Flow lab|#t-flow}}

Flow matching on a 2-D toy and on the running example, paths before and after reflow, ODE solvers, flow against diffusion at equal steps. time set when the lab is finished

{{Vision lab|#t-vision}}

Images as patches and tokens, CLIP and SigLIP 2 embeddings, zero-shot classification, similarity matrices, the contrastive loss, a typographic attack. time set when the lab is finished

{{VLM lab|#t-vlm}}

SmolVLM taken apart: image tokens at each resolution, the exact prompt, answers with their errors, a hallucination probe, time and memory per image. time set when the lab is finished

{{Model map|#t-map}}

Image, video and audio generators, vision encoders, VLMs, unified models and libraries with dates, architecture family and licence; a timeline and a picker. time set when the tab is finished

Best outside resources

Sander Dieleman, "Generative modelling in latent space" third-party

15 April 2025. Why every image, audio and video generator works in a latent, and what makes a good one; the best single companion to sections 2 and 4. The author is a DeepMind research scientist writing on his own blog. about 60 min

Lilian Weng, "What are Diffusion Models?" third-party

July 2021, updated since. Diffusion from the forward process to guidance and fast samplers, with the equations in one place. about 45 min

Holderrieth and Erives, "An Introduction to Flow Matching and Diffusion Models" third-party

2025 MIT course notes: flow matching and diffusion as one framework, with exercises. about 4 h

Lipman et al., "Flow Matching Guide and Code" maker

December 2024, by the authors of flow matching (Meta), with a reference PyTorch library. about 3 h

Jay Alammar, "The Illustrated Stable Diffusion" third-party

The text encoder, U-Net and decoder of section 4 in pictures. about 25 min

Diffusion Explainer (Georgia Tech Polo Club) third-party

Interactive: Stable Diffusion's steps and guidance on real prompts, in the browser. about 15 min

Hugging Face, "The Annotated Diffusion Model" maker

DDPM implemented line by line in PyTorch, by the makers of diffusers. about 60 min, longer if you run it

Sebastian Raschka, "Understanding Multimodal LLMs" third-party

3 November 2024. The token approach and the cross-attention approach of section 6, then about ten models (Llama 3.2, Molmo, NVLM, Qwen2-VL, Pixtral, Emu3, Janus) placed on them. about 30 min

Lilian Weng, "Generalized Visual Language Models" third-party

9 June 2022. The earlier history: joint training, frozen-LM prefixes, cross-attention fusion, training-free stitching. 24 min (the page's own estimate)

Chip Huyen, "Multimodality and Large Multimodal Models" third-party

October 2023. CLIP, Flamingo and the research directions that followed, for engineers. about 45 min

Kingma and Welling, "An Introduction to Variational Autoencoders" maker

2019, by the VAE's inventors: the model, its estimators and its extensions. about 2 h

clean-fid and its paper third-party

Why FID numbers from different code disagree, and a library that fixes the resizing. about 30 min
