Fusion explorer: where the image enters, what it becomes, who attends to whom

Each design on this page, drawn on one toy request: two text tokens, an input image (shown as 4 positions), two more text tokens, and, where the design can draw, an output image (4 positions). The grid is the attention mask: row = the position computing its output, column = a position it may read. Colour says what kind of position it is; the letter in the last column says which loss trains that position. Pick one design, or two to compare side by side.

Click once to show a design on the left; with "compare" on, the next click fills the right.

Loss letters: T = next-token cross-entropy on text; C = next-token cross-entropy on image codes; M = cross-entropy on masked image codes; D = diffusion (denoising) loss; blank = no loss at that position. Masks follow each design's paper or config as quoted in the Reading tab (Chameleon, Janus: causal throughout; Transfusion: "causal attention to every element in the sequence, and bidirectional attention within the elements of each individual image"; Show-o's omni-attention; Gemma 4 12B Unified's config: "use_bidirectional_attention": "vision"). The input image's loss differs by recipe (Chameleon trains on all tokens, LLaVA-style VLMs on the answer only), so the letters show the common case, not every recipe.
