Token generator lab: a tiny autoregressive model over image tokens

A 3.07M-parameter transformer that writes a Fashion-MNIST image as 64 tokens from 1,000 codes, one token per call, conditioned on the class. The tokenizer is the sibling page {{Autoencoders and VAEs|n:3c65c17b0d0d814da93bdb159b7c1e11}}' FSQ [8, 5, 5, 5] autoencoder; the training compute equals the root Diffusion lab's DDPM (Reading section 5). Everything here is recorded output of real runs on an Apple M1 Pro (MPS), 1,000 samples per setting (100 per class), scored like the Diffusion and Flow labs: FD in the shared classifier's feature space against the 10,000 test images (lower is better; 1,000 real validation images score ), the classifier's accuracy on the asked-for class, and diversity (mean distance between same-class samples; real images ). Every setting reuses the same random numbers, so moving a control shows the same draw under different settings.
