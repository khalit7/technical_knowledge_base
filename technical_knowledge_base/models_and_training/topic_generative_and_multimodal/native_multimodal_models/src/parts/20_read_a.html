The idea in one screen

A native multimodal model makes every modality positions in one token sequence of one transformer; the designs differ in what an image becomes on the way in (encoder vectors, latent patches, discrete codes or raw patches) and how an image is written out (not at all, one code per call, a few parallel rounds, or diffusion inside the same network), and each choice is paid for in quality, sequential calls and training stability.

Early, late fusion
modalities share one network from the first layer and the start of training, or are processed by separately trained networks and combined later.
Native
a vendor word for "trained on several modalities from pretraining onward"; it says nothing exact about the architecture.
Unified, any-to-any
one model both reads and writes a modality; any mix of modalities in and out.
Soft token, code
a continuous vector placed in the sequence (readable only), against an integer id from a tokenizer's codebook (readable and writable with the softmax).
Attention mask
which positions may read which; unified models mix causal (text) and bidirectional (inside one image).
Mixture of transformers (MoT)
separate weights per modality or task, one shared self-attention over all tokens.
Cascade
speech recognition, then a text model, then text-to-speech, as three models in a row.
What was measured here, in one paragraph

A 3.07M-parameter transformer trained at the training FLOPs of the topic's DDPM run (root Diffusion lab recordings) on Fashion-MNIST images as 64 FSQ tokens each scores FD without guidance and with s = 1.5; the tokenizer alone, reconstructing real images, scores ; the DDPM reaches but needs times the sampling FLOPs. Measured on Apple M1 Pro (MPS); sections 2 to 5.
