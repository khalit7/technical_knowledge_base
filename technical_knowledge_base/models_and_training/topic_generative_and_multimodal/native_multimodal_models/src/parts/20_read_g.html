8. The closed systems: what their own documents say, and what they do not

For GPT-4o and Gemini there are no architecture papers, only system cards and reports: everything here is the vendor writing about its own system vendor, nothing was measured independently, and where a document is silent the table says so.

Gemini 1.5 (report v5)"up to at least 10M tokens"; needle recall in "9.7M tokens in the audio modality (up to 107 hours); 9.9M tokens in the video modality (up to 10.5 hours)"; "a sparse mixture-of-expert (MoE) Transformer-based model" ({{arXiv 2403.05530v5|https://arxiv.org/html/2403.05530v5}})Per-second token rates (the figures imply about 25 audio tokens a second and about 260 per video frame: derived, not stated).

What the Gemini API meters per modality (vendor units, used by Token economics)

What the API meters. Google's API documentation states the per-modality prices in tokens: an image up to 384 px is 258 tokens, larger images are cut into 768 × 768 tiles of 258 tokens each, audio is 32 tokens a second and video 263 tokens a second at one frame a second ({{Gemini API token docs, updated 2026-10-13|https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/tokens}}) vendor. These are the units the {{Token economics|#t-nmtok}} tab uses for its Gemini row. Note that 258 tokens per frame is the same number the 2.5 report's "66 instead of 258" refers to.

How to read these claims

"Autoregressive" for GPT-4o images says the image is produced token by token; it does not say there is no diffusion decoder after the tokens, and the document gives no way to tell. "Natively multimodal" for Gemini says the model was trained on all modalities from the start; it does not say images go in as discrete tokens (the report credits Flamingo, CoCa and PaLI for its visual encoding, all continuous encoders). Latency figures (GPT-4o 232 and 320 ms, Moshi 160 and 200 ms, Qwen3-Omni 234 ms) are each self-reported under different conditions, and cannot be ranked against one another.
