7. Interleaved data and the balance between modalities

A model that shares weights across modalities has to be told, three ways, how much each modality matters: by the data mix (what share of tokens is text, image-caption pairs, or interleaved documents, where images sit at their place in running text), by the loss weights, and by which weights each modality may use. Only interleaved documents show the model several images with the text between them, the shape of the mixed answers these models are asked to write; a caption pair is one image and one sentence.

Llama 4, BAGEL and Gemini describe their mixes without numbers per modality (quotes)

The other large mixes are described, not tabulated: Llama 4's "more than 30 trillion tokens" include "diverse text, image, and video datasets", pretrained "on up to 48 images" per sample (Meta, 5 April 2025); BAGEL's corpus "comprises 2.5T tokens" of "large-scale interleaved multimodal data"; Gemini "support[s] interleaved sequences of text, image, audio, and video as inputs" (Gemini 1.0 report, Figure 2). vendor

Loss weights. Transfusion trains on "50% text and 50% image data" with L = LLM + λ LDDPM, λ = 5 "following preliminary experiments". The weight is needed because the two losses count differently: the "LM loss is computed per token, while diffusion loss is computed per image", one term spread over 256 patches. In a Chameleon-style model there is one cross-entropy over one vocabulary, so the balance is set by the data mix alone, and the modalities fight inside the shared softmax and norms (section 6).

Modality-specific weights. The cure for competition that does not touch the data is to stop sharing everything: Mixture-of-Transformers "decouples non-embedding parameters of the model by modality" (the feed-forward networks, attention projection matrices and layer norms), keeping "global self-attention over the full input sequence" ({{arXiv 2411.04996v2|https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04996v2}}). BAGEL is this design with two experts, one for understanding (text and ViT tokens) and one for generation (VAE tokens): about twice the parameters, "identical FLOPs during both training and inference" as its dense baseline.
