What the model saw: every token of four images, clickable

The exact pixels each model receives and the tokens it makes from them, for four images: the root’s 512 × 512 cat, two photos at their native 3:2 sizes (scikit-image’s chelsea 451 × 300 and rocket 640 × 427; CC0 and public domain) and a tall synthetic receipt (400 × 1,000). The views are decoded back from the pixel_values the processors produced, so stretching and squashing are visible as they happened. Click any token to see where it came from, its position (1-D or (t, h, w)), and its encoder patch-norm ratio; Reading sections 1, 2 and 2a explain what to look for, and section 2 steps through the rocket photo under both models. measured on the real processors and models, 2026-10-17

The prompt the language model reads

With shading on, shaded tokens on the grid: the largest encoder patch-norm ratio (norm divided by the view’s median, last block before the final LayerNorm) among the patches merged into that token is above 2.5; darker is higher, dark red is 5 or more. Unshaded tokens are below 2.5. Scripts: runs/smol_cpu.py (SmolVLM, CPU) and the saw phase of runs/run_vlm2.py (Qwen3.5, GPU).
