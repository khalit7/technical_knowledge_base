How many tokens an image or a video becomes in four VLMs, and what share of the context that is. The resize and tiling rules are JavaScript ports of the processors (transformers v5.20.1) and are checked against the real processors on 24 image sizes. rules checked on the processors, 2026-10-17

The cache, in one line. Image tokens cost cache exactly like text tokens, so multiply these counts by the model’s bytes per token: one 576-token image holds 302 MB on LLaVA-1.5-7B (524,288 bytes per token), against 40,960 on SmolVLM-500M (measured here, equal to the config arithmetic), 12,288 on InternVL3-1B (2 × 24 × 2 × 64 × 2, config) and 12,288 on Qwen3.5-0.8B’s 6 attention layers plus a fixed state. SmolVLM-500M, Qwen3.5-0.8B and LLaVA-1.5-7B are presets in the {{KV-cache calculator|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81059ee1ce20c0326dc5#t-kv}}; section 8 of the Reading tab has the measured cache.
