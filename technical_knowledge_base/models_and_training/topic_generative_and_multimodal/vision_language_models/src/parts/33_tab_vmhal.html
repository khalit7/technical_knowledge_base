Hallucination probe

The root lab’s 72 yes/no object questions (“Is there <object> in the image? Answer yes or no.”) on the 16 test images: 25 about objects that are there, 16 about unrelated absent objects (random) and 31 about absent objects that often appear with what is there (adversarial). Each is scored by P(yes), the probability on yes tokens divided by the probability on yes plus no tokens at the first answer position, for both models and four conditions: the real image, a mid-grey image, no image at all, and the leading phrasing “There is <object> in the image, isn’t there?” on the real image. Move the threshold to see how many answers flip. measured, bfloat16 on MPS, 2026-10-17
