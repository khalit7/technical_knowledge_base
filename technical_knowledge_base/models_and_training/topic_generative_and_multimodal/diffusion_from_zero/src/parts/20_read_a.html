The idea in one screen

A diffusion model is one network, the denoiser, trained to undo a known amount of Gaussian noise at every noise level; generating is starting from pure noise and calling that network again and again. Four choices around the network decide whether it works: how noise is added (the schedule), what the network is asked to output (the target and its loss), how its answers are chained into a path (the sampler and its step count), and how hard each step is pushed toward the prompt (guidance).

Training destroys an image in one line of arithmetic; sampling rebuilds one by calling the network many times

Training: add noise in closed form (any t in one step; the schedule, section 2)
Denoiser (one network, every t): sees xt and t, outputs the noise ε (or x0, or v; section 3). Loss: mean squared error against what was really added.
Sampling: start from fresh noise, call the denoiser 20 times (shown: its guess of the clean image so far; the sampler, section 5; guidance, section 6)

New terms

  • āt ("alpha bar"): share of the image's variance left at step t.
  • SNR, λ: signal-to-noise ratio ā/(1 − ā) and its log.
  • Target: what the network outputs: noise ε, clean image x0, or a mix v.
  • Sampler: the rule that chains predictions into a path; NFE, network calls per image.
  • Deterministic (ODE) / stochastic: whether the sampler adds fresh noise as it goes.
  • CFG: classifier-free guidance, extrapolating from the unconditional prediction.
  • Distillation: training a second model to take one to four big steps.

The root's tiny diffusion model (TinyUNet, parameters, 4,000 training steps on Fashion-MNIST, 28 × 28 grey clothing in 10 classes, on an Apple M1 Pro) with the checkpoint, noise and scoring of the topic's diffusion runs (root src/diff), so the tiny-model numbers agree across the topic's pages; Stable Diffusion v1.5 at real scale. FD is the Fréchet distance between 1,000 samples and the test set in a Fashion-MNIST classifier's feature space (lower is better; 1,000 real images score ); accuracy is how often that classifier recognises the requested class (real ); diversity, the mean feature distance within a class (real ).
