0. In one screen

A diffusion model is one network, a denoiser, trained to clean up images that have had a known amount of Gaussian noise added. Generating is running it repeatedly from pure noise. Everything else on this page is one of five decisions around that network, and each one can make a good model look broken.

DecisionWhat it setsStable Diffusion 1.5's choiceWhat goes wrong
Noise schedule (section 2)How much signal is left at each of the 1,000 training stepsscaled-linear, β 0.00085 to 0.012; 6.8% of each value's amplitude survives the last stepThe average brightness survives too, so the model can never make very dark or very bright images
Target and loss weighting (section 3)Whether the network outputs the noise, the clean image or a mix (v), and how much each noise level counts in the lossnoise (ε), plain mean squared error, timesteps drawn uniformlyε cannot work where no signal is left; the loss spends most of its weight on nearly clean images

What this page adds to the root. The root's {{Diffusion lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d817ab6ade318917bff55}} trains the topic's tiny diffusion model on Fashion-MNIST (28 × 28 grey clothing, 10 classes) and already shows the forward process, the three schedules, ε against v, the same noise through 1 to 50 steps, four samplers, guidance, and Stable Diffusion v1.5's steps and timings. This page uses the same checkpoints, noise and scoring, so its numbers line up with the lab's, and goes one level down on each decision. The derivations (the ELBO, why predicting noise is right, the DDIM and guidance formulas) live on {{Variational inference and generative-model maths|n:3f05c17b0d0d81aa9857eb5fa1bac530}} (sections 12 to 17) and the paper page {{Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models|n:3c65c17b0d0d811481e4e36333454f7a}}; this page links them instead of repeating them.

Measured here, in one list

  • On the tiny model, the stochastic sampler beats the deterministic one because the model hedges between classes: an exact-score toy reproduces the pattern (section 4), and adding back noise in steps (DDIM's η) moves FD from (η = 0) to (η = 1) at 50 steps measured.
  • Stable Diffusion 1.5's per-channel latent average keeps a signal-to-noise ratio of at the last training step, about the per-value SNR of step of 1,000 derived: the brightness leak behind "medium brightness only" (section 2).
  • Guiding only the middle 19 of 25 steps (the guidance interval) brings SD 1.5's colour saturation at scale 20 from to and saves 6 of 50 U-Net passes; CFG rescale at 0.7 barely moves it () on this ε-model (section 5) measured.
  • Autoguidance with a weak copy of the tiny model gives the best tiny-model FD on the page at 50 steps without CFG's loss of diversity (section 5) measured.
  • LCM-LoRA makes a sharp SD 1.5 image in 1 to 4 steps where DPM-Solver++ at 4 steps gives a soft one, but it drops the lighthouse the prompt asked for (section 6) measured.
