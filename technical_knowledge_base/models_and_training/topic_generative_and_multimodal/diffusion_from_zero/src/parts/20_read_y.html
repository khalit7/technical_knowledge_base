What ran, and sources

Runs (all measured on Apple M1 Pro, MPS, one GPU job at a time under the topic's shared lock; torch 2.14.1, diffusers 0.41.0; code in this page's src/runs/, recorded outputs in src/out/):

  • gmm_toy.py (CPU, seconds): the exact-score toy, 20,000 samples per setting; check_toy.mjs proves the page's JavaScript port equal to it.
  • dz_train.py: the lab's ε-model resumed for 4,000 more steps ( min), and the weak model for autoguidance (TinyUNet base 16, 262,385 parameters, 1,000 steps, min).
  • dz_sample.py: every sampler, the η sweep, EMA against raw weights, the bugs, the 8,000-step model, and the guidance variants on the tiny model, 1,000 samples each with the lab's noise and scoring; it first reproduces two of the lab's numbers ().
  • dz_sd.py: SD 1.5 (fp16, attention slicing, peak GiB of MPS memory): CFG rescale, negative prompts, the guidance interval with a hand-written loop (), zero terminal SNR on the ε-model, LCM-LoRA. images in all.
  • recompute.py: every derived number (schedules, the brightness leak, σ ranges, Karras spacing).
