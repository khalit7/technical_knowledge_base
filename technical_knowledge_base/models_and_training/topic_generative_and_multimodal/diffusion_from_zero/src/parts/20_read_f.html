8. When it goes wrong: the real outputs

Each bug below was run on the tiny ε-model (DDIM, 50 steps, the lab's noise and scoring) or on SD 1.5, so the symptom is what you would actually see. Three more are shown where they belong: a v-model sampled as ε (section 3), "leading" timestep spacing at few steps (section 5) and guidance_scale ≤ 1 meaning no guidance in diffusers (section 6).

The same ten noise images, sampled correctly and with each bug: nothing crashes, the images are just wrong

Grey fog: a σ-space scheduler started from unit noise

Cause. Euler, Heun, DPM-Solver with Karras sigmas and the other k-diffusion-style schedulers work on x̃ = xt / √ā, whose starting noise has standard deviation σmax ( for the tiny model's schedule with trailing spacing, about 14.6 for SD 1.5). A hand-written loop that skips x = x * scheduler.init_noise_sigma starts with noise times too small, and scale_model_input then shrinks it again. Check: print scheduler.init_noise_sigma; the pipeline does the multiplication at {{pipeline_stable_diffusion.py line 713|https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/v0.41.0/src/diffusers/pipelines/stable_diffusion/pipeline_stable_diffusion.py#L713}}.

Noise or blobs at every step count: the timestep in the wrong units

Cause. The network was trained with t / T in [0, 1) (the shared TinyUNet) and is fed the integer t, or a diffusers U-Net trained on integer t is fed a fraction. The time embedding then describes a noise level the network never saw. Check: compare the range of t in the training loop with the sampler's call.

Plausible but wrong: a schedule mismatch between training and sampling

Cause. Sampling with ā from a different schedule than the model was trained with (a cosine scheduler config on a linear-schedule model, or SD 1.5's scaled-linear on a model fine-tuned with something else). Each step's conversion from ε̂ to x̂0 then uses the wrong noise level. Check: diff the scheduler config against the training config (beta_schedule, beta_start, beta_end, num_train_timesteps, rescale_betas_zero_snr). In diffusers the scheduler's prediction_type and schedule come from the checkpoint's scheduler/scheduler_config.json; swap samplers with from_config so they inherit it.

NaN or black images: zero terminal SNR switched on for an ε-model

Cause. rescale_betas_zero_snr=True makes ā at the last step exactly 0, and turning ε̂ into x̂0 divides by √ā. Zero terminal SNR is for v-prediction models fine-tuned on that schedule (section 2).

Clipping that helps pixels and breaks latents: clip_sample

Cause. diffusers' DDPMScheduler and DDIMScheduler default to clip_sample=True, clamping x̂0 to [−1, 1] at every step. That suits pixel models trained on [−1, 1] images; a latent model's latents are not bounded that way, which is why SD 1.5's scheduler config sets clip_sample false.

Blotchy samples from a model whose loss looks fine: sampling the raw weights

Cause. The training loop's own weights are noisier than their EMA; checkpoints often hold both.

"DDIM is always better than DDPM"

True for a well-trained model at 10 to 100 steps (DDIM Table 1), false for an under-trained one (section 5). A sampler ranking measured on one checkpoint does not transfer to another; re-run the sampler sweep after each training run that matters.
