Best resources, in reading order

Karras et al. 2022, Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models (EDM)

The paper that separates schedule, preconditioning, sampler and stochasticity; sections 2 to 5 are this page's spine. about 90 min

Lin et al. 2023, Common Diffusion Noise Schedules and Sample Steps are Flawed

Zero terminal SNR, trailing timesteps and CFG rescale; eight pages. about 30 min

Kingma and Gao 2023, Understanding Diffusion Objectives as the ELBO with Simple Data Augmentation

Every weighting and noise distribution of section 3 on one λ axis. about 60 min

Lu et al. 2022, DPM-Solver++

The exponential-integrator view and why guided sampling needs the data-prediction form. about 45 min

Karras et al. 2024, Guiding a Diffusion Model with a Bad Version of Itself

Autoguidance, with a 2-D toy that explains why CFG loses diversity. about 45 min

Song et al. 2023, Consistency Models

The idea behind LCM and most one-to-four-step models. about 60 min

Sander Dieleman, The geometry of diffusion guidance (2023)

A clear essay on what guidance does geometrically; his blog's "noise schedules considered harmful" and "perspectives on diffusion" posts are worth the same time. about 25 min

diffusers schedulers documentation (v0.41.0)

Every scheduler class used here, with its options (spacing, Karras sigmas, thresholding, zero terminal SNR). about 20 min
