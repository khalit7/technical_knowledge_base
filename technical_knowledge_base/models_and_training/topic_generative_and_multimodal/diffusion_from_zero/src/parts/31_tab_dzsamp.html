Sampler workbench

Every sampler on this page and the lab, on one trained model, from the same noise. Pick a sampler and a step count to see one sample per class (the same ten noise images everywhere) and the scores over 1,000 samples. FD is the Fréchet distance in the shared Fashion-MNIST classifier's feature space (lower is better; 1,000 real images score ); accuracy is how often that classifier recognises the requested class (real images ); diversity is the mean feature distance between samples of a class (real images ). measured on Apple M1 Pro (MPS); DDPM, DDIM, DPM-Solver++ and Euler at 1, 4 to 1,000 steps are the {{Diffusion lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d817ab6ade318917bff55}}'s own recordings.
