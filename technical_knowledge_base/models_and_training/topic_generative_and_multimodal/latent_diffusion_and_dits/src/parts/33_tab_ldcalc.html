What one image costs in denoiser compute, for any of the eleven denoisers on this page at any size: tokens from the VAE factor and patch, FLOPs per pass from the counted formula, then steps and passes. Extends Reading section 9.

FLOPs per pass against image size, all eleven

The formula: FLOPs(N, T) = k0 + k1N + k2T + k3N2 + k4NT + k5T2, N = image tokens (or the U-Net's top-level latent positions), T = text tokens, with the six coefficients fitted to counts on meta tensors at 10 square sizes and 1 to 5 text lengths per model (src/arch/flops_all.py). It reproduces every counted point to within 10−12 relative, except Z-Image (2%: its code pads both sequences to multiples of 32). Non-square sizes use the same N; for a U-Net that is exact for the convolutions and the attention. Not included: the text encoder, the VAE decoder, and anything the formula cannot see (kernel efficiency: attention FLOPs run at very different speeds depending on the kernel). Throughput is yours to set: the default is the effective rate measured here for SD 1.5's U-Net at 512 × 512 in float16 on the M1 Pro's GPU; a datacenter GPU's sustained rate must be measured, and its vendor peak (NVIDIA lists 989 TFLOPS of dense BF16 for an H100 SXM, {{H100 page|https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/h100/}}) is an upper bound no denoiser reaches. derived
