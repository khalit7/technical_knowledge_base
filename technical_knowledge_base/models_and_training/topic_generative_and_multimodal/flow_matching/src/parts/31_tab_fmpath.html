A workbench for the whole page: the 2-D toy (noise in the plane, a ring of 8 Gaussian blobs as data) with the exact field for three paths (Reading, sections 2 and 6), stochastic sampling (section 12), four reflow rounds (section 8) and three one-step students (section 9). Two panels run on the same starting noise, so every comparison is a before and after. Nothing moves until you press play or step.

Try: each preset sets both panels; then change one thing at a time. DDIM is defined for the exact fields (predict both ends, re-noise at the next time); on the straight path it equals Euler. Midpoint costs two network evaluations per step (NFE shown under each panel). ε adds fresh noise each step with the same marginals (Reading, section 12). A one-step student ignores the step count.

What each model is
  • Exact field: the closed-form marginal velocity of the ring for that path (Reading, section 2); no training, no error except the solver's.
  • 1-RF, 2-RF: the root Flow lab's random-pair and reflowed networks (3 → 48 → 48 → 48 → 2 MLP, int8 weights). 3-RF, 4-RF: further rounds trained by src/runs/fm_toy.py, same recipe.
  • Student of k-RF: one call, z + v(z, 0), trained on k-RF's (noise, end point) pairs (Reading, section 9).

Scores under each panel are computed live on the 300 particles shown (2,000 in the recorded tables, so they differ slightly). "On a blob": within 0.75 of a centre. Straightness: straight-line distance over path length.
