Not run here: training at SD3 or FLUX scale (published numbers instead); logit-normal training of the image model (a 15-minute run whose difference would sit below the noise of a 1,000-sample FD, as the root also decided; the toy shows the mechanism); LPIPS distillation (L2 used; Liu et al. used LPIPS for one step); a 3-RF image student (a third pair pass); adaptive ODE solvers (torchdiffeq is not in the shared environment); consistency, shortcut and MeanFlow training (published only; the sibling page Diffusion from zero covers consistency models).
