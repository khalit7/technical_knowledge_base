Measured here (Apple M1 Pro, 2026-10-17): the toy experiments on CPU (src/runs/fm_toy.py), the exact empirical field and copies (fm_memo.py, CPU), coupling distances on images (fm_ot_images.py, CPU), the scheduler demo (fm_sched_demo.py, CPU), and on MPS: reflow pairs (fm_pairs.py), 3-RF and the two one-step students (fm_train.py), scoring and SDE sampling (fm_score.py). The objective's code, the 1-RF and 2-RF models, the solver comparison, the t = 1 measurement, the convention bugs and the guidance sweep are the root topic's Flow lab runs (src/flow/ in the root), read from its recordings by src/make_data_more.py; the diffusion rows are the root Diffusion lab's. What transfers to a datacenter GPU: everything counted in network evaluations, rounds and steps; what does not: seconds.

Not run here: training at SD3 or FLUX scale (published numbers instead); logit-normal training of the image model (a 15-minute run whose difference would sit below the noise of a 1,000-sample FD; the toy shows the mechanism); LPIPS distillation (L2 used); a 3-RF image student (a third pair pass); adaptive ODE solvers (torchdiffeq is not in the shared environment); consistency, shortcut and MeanFlow training (published only).
