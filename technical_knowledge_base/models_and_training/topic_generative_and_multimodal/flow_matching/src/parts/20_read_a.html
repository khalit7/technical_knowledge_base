The idea in one screen

Flow matching trains a network to output the straight-line velocity from a noise sample to a data sample; because many such lines pass through every point, what it actually learns is their average, the marginal velocity field, whose paths bend, and every few-step method is a way to make that field easier to follow.

You need

Regression with a squared error (it predicts the conditional mean of its target) and Gaussian noise; the page builds the rest. The diffusion side (forward process, samplers, guidance) is on {{Diffusion from zero|n:3c65c17b0d0d81cf9bf0d94e57ce9164}}; the derivations (why the per-pair loss has the marginal loss's gradient, the probability-flow ODE) on {{Variational inference and generative-model maths|n:3f05c17b0d0d81aa9857eb5fa1bac530}}, sections 15 to 18; the transformers that production flow models train on {{Latent diffusion and diffusion transformers|n:3fc5c17b0d0d814fbcb5e0c8df5289f3}}.

New terms

  • Velocity field v(x, t): an arrow for every point and time; sampling follows it from t = 0 (noise) to t = 1 (data).
  • Conditional / marginal field: one pair's line velocity / the average over every pair through x.
  • Path (at, bt): how a pair is mixed, xt = at x1 + bt x0.
  • Coupling: which noise is paired with which image in training.
  • k-RF, reflow: the k-th rectified flow, trained on the (k−1)-th flow's own (noise, output) pairs.
  • NFE: network evaluations per sample, the real cost. Straightness: straight-line distance over path length (1 = straight).
  • Shift: a warp of the time grid that moves steps towards the noise end.

Running example. The root topic's 2-D toy (noise in the plane; data a ring of 8 Gaussian blobs, radius 3, spread 0.25; small 3 → 48 → 48 → 48 → 2 networks, parameters) and its Fashion-MNIST model (a -parameter U-Net trained steps in s on an Apple M1 Pro). Image scores are the root's: FD, the Fréchet distance between 1,000 samples and the test set in a Fashion-MNIST classifier's feature space (lower is better; 1,000 real images score ); class accuracy; and diversity, the mean feature distance between same-class samples (real ). On the toy, "on a blob" means within 0.75 (3 spreads) of a centre.
