A flow-matching model makes an image by starting from random noise and following a learned velocity (an arrow for every point and every moment saying which way to move) until it arrives at data. The root's {{Flow lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d817ab6ade318917bff55}} (a tab on Topic: generative-and-multimodal) built that idea from zero, trained a tiny model on Fashion-MNIST, and compared it with diffusion at equal steps. This page goes underneath it: what exactly the network is trying to learn, what would happen if it learned it perfectly, and every knob the field has turned since 2022 to get from 50 steps to one.

In one screen
  • The target is an average. Training regresses on one straight line per (noise, image) pair, but the network can only output one arrow per point, so it learns the average of all lines through that point: the marginal vector field. On the page's 2-D toy that field has a closed form, and of the trained network's loss is irreducible noise around it (section 1).
  • Curvature belongs to the problem, not the network. The exact field's own paths are as bent as the trained network's (straightness against ). Few-step errors come from the path, so fixing them means changing the path, the pairing or the step placement (section 2).
  • A perfect model would only copy. On a finite training set the exact optimum sends every noise to a training image: measured here, of samples are copies, while the trained network's samples sit as far from the training set as held-out test images do. Generalisation comes from the network failing to reach the optimum (section 3).
  • Path, coupling, time sampling and sampler are separate choices. Straight against variance-preserving paths, independent against optimal-transport pairing, uniform against logit-normal t, ODE against SDE: each is measured on the toy here and set beside the published ablations of SD3 and SiT (sections 4, 5, 8, 9).
  • Reflow and distillation buy steps and cost quality. A second and third rectification round on the toy and on the image model, plus one-step students: few-step quality rises, many-step quality falls, as Liu et al. warned (sections 6, 7).
  • Production is flow matching with logit-normal t and a resolution shift. Real scheduler configs of FLUX.2, Qwen-Image, Z-Image, Wan, HunyuanVideo and LTX-Video, read from their repositories (section 11).
