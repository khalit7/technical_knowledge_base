8. Reflow: retrain on the flow's own pairs

The problem. Sections 3 and 7: the field of random pairs bends, and better pairing helps little on images. Reflow (Liu, Gong and Liu 2022) changes the pairing after training: push many noises z through the trained flow to get X1 = flow(z), then train again, same loss, on the fixed pairs (z, X1). Paths of an ODE "cannot cross each other" (their words), so the new pairs do not cross, and the field that averages them is close to each pair's straight line. Names: the first model is the 1-rectified flow (1-RF), the model trained on its pairs 2-RF, and so on.

9. Distillation to one step

The problem. Even a rectified flow is integrated in steps. Distillation trains a student T(z) to output the teacher's ODE end point X1 = flow(z) directly, in one call. Which teacher should it copy? Liu et al.'s recipe regresses T(z) onto the k-RF's pairs (for one step they "replace the L2 loss function with the LPIPS similarity", a perceptual distance). Here the student is the teacher's own network evaluated at t = 0, initialised from the teacher, with a squared error: z + v(z, 0) → X1, on the pairs the next reflow round uses (the last comparison in the card above).

Published one-step and few-step methods for flows (dated)
  • InstaFlow (Liu et al., arXiv 2309.06380, Sep 2023): reflow then distillation on Stable Diffusion; one step, FID 23.3 on MS COCO 2017-5k, 0.09 s per image, 199 A100 GPU-days of training (abstract) published.
  • Latent adversarial diffusion distillation, LADD (Sauer et al., arXiv 2403.12015, Mar 2024): SD3 (8B) to SD3-Turbo, matching strong generators "using only four unguided sampling steps"; FLUX.1 [schnell] (12B rectified flow, "1 to 4 steps") is distilled with it per its model card published.
  • Shortcut models (Frans et al., arXiv 2410.12557, Oct 2024): one network conditioned on the step size as well as the time, trained in one phase, sampling in 1 or many steps published.
  • MeanFlow (Geng et al., arXiv 2505.13447, May 2025): learns the average velocity over an interval; "FID of 3.43 with a single function evaluation (1-NFE) on ImageNet 256x256 trained from scratch", with "no pre-training, distillation, or curriculum learning" published.
  • Guidance distillation: FLUX.1 [dev]'s transformer takes the guidance scale as an input (diffusers v0.41.0, pipelines/flux/pipeline_flux.py line 847, if self.transformer.config.guidance_embeds:), so one call replaces classifier-free guidance's two.
