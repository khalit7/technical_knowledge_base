12. Adding noise back: stochastic sampling with the same marginals

The problem. The flow ODE is deterministic, and section 10 found the stochastic DDPM sampler beating every deterministic run at many steps. Can a flow model sample stochastically too, and should it?

How it works. A velocity also gives the score (the gradient of the log density, pointing to more likely data): on the straight path E[x0 | x] = x − t v, so score = −(x − t v)/(1 − t) derived. For any noise level ε ≥ 0 the stochastic differential equation dx = (v + ε/2 · score) dt + √ε dW has the same distribution at every t as the ODE (the extra drift cancels the spreading the noise causes). Albergo, Boffi and Vanden-Eijnden's "Stochastic Interpolants" (arXiv 2303.08797, Mar 2023) build flows and diffusions as one family "with an adjustable level of noise" published; the ODE is ε = 0, and the {{2-D flow playground|#t-fmpath}} has an ε control.

With a perfect field, noise only costs steps: every ε reaches the data with enough steps, and more noise needs more. Noise helps when the field is imperfect: each injection lets the sampler forget part of the error it accumulated. SiT measured that on ImageNet: SDE sampling beats the ODE for every path (FID 39.8 to 37.8 on VP, 34.8 to 33.6 on the straight path, 34.6 to 32.9 on GVP; Table 5, many steps) published, and their best SiT-XL result (2.06) uses an SDE sampler.

13. Flow matching in production

The pattern. Since SD3 the major open image and video generators train a transformer with flow matching on the straight path and logit-normal t, then sample with Euler (or a multistep solver) on a shifted grid whose shift grows with resolution or with fewer steps: SD3 a fixed 3.0 at 1024 px; FLUX a dynamic eμ from 1.65 to 3.16; Z-Image-Turbo a fixed 3.0; HunyuanVideo a fixed 7.0. What differs is the shift rule (fixed, or linear in token count), whether the grid stops short of the data end (shift_terminal), and the distillation behind few-step variants (LADD for FLUX.1 [schnell] and SD3-Turbo, guidance distillation for FLUX.1 [dev]). The networks themselves are on {{Latent diffusion and diffusion transformers|n:3fc5c17b0d0d814fbcb5e0c8df5289f3}}; video systems on {{Video and audio generation|n:3c65c17b0d0d8198bd7be111a3dc4c3a}}.
