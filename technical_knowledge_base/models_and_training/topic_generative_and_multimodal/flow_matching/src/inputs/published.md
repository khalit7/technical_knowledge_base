## Theory
- Lipman et al. 2210.02747 (HTML), Theorem 2: "Assuming that p_t(x) > 0 for all x in R^d and t in [0,1], then, up to a constant independent of theta, L_CFM and L_FM are equal. Hence, grad L_FM(theta) = grad L_CFM(theta)". Also: "The FM (equation 5) and CFM (equation 9) objectives have identical gradients w.r.t. theta."
- Liu, Gong, Liu 2209.03003 (HTML): "the different paths following a well defined ODE ... cannot cross each other at any time t in [0,1)"; Rectify "converts an arbitrary coupling into a deterministic coupling with lower convex transport costs"; "It is not recommended to apply too many reflow steps as it may accumulate estimation error on v^X"; distillation: "for k = 1, we replace the L2 loss function with the LPIPS similarity [96] since it empirically brings better performance"; a k-step generator samples "t in {0, 1/k, ..., (k-1)/k} during fine-tuning".
- Albergo, Boffi, Vanden-Eijnden, "Stochastic Interpolants: A Unifying Framework for Flows and Diffusions", arXiv 2303.08797, v1 15 Mar 2023 (v4 9 Oct 2025). Abstract: "A class of generative models that unifies flow-based and diffusion-based methods"; "a family of forward and backward Fokker-Planck equations with tunable diffusion coefficient"; "both deterministic and stochastic generative models based on probability flow equations or stochastic differential equations with an adjustable level of noise"; drift coefficients "characterized as the unique minimizers of simple quadratic objective functions".
- Pooladian, Ben-Hamu, Domingo-Enrich, Amos, Lipman, Chen, "Multisample Flow Matching: Straightening Flows with Minibatch Couplings", arXiv 2304.14772, v1 28 Apr 2023. Abstract: "reduce gradient variance during training", "obtain straighter flows", "transport maps with lower cost in high dimensions".
- Gu, Du, Pang, Li, Lin, Wang, "On Memorization in Diffusion Models", arXiv 2310.02664, v1 4 Oct 2023. Abstract: "the typical training objective of diffusion models, i.e., denoising score matching, has a closed-form optimal solution that can only generate training data replicating samples"; "memorization behaviors tend to occur on smaller-sized datasets".
- Kamb, Ganguli, "An analytic theory of creativity in convolutional diffusion models", arXiv 2412.20292, v1 28 Dec 2024. Abstract: "optimal score-matching theory suggests that these models should only be able to produce memorized training examples"; "two simple inductive biases, locality and equivariance"; median r^2 "0.95, 0.94, 0.94, 0.96 for our top model on CIFAR10, FashionMNIST, MNIST, and CelebA"; "a locally consistent patch mosaic mechanism of creativity".

## Production (papers)
- Movie Gen (Polyak et al., 88 authors), arXiv 2410.13720, v1 17 Oct 2024: "a 30B parameter transformer trained with a maximum context length of 73K video tokens"; "We use the Flow Matching training objective"; "we sample t from a logit-normal distribution where the underlying Gaussian distribution has zero mean and unit standard deviation"; inference "linear-quadratic sampler ... with 50 steps (emulating 250 linear steps)"; "follows the first 25 steps of an N-step linear schedule and then approximates the remaining N - 25 steps with 25 quadratically placed steps"; guidance scale 7.5.
- HunyuanVideo (Kong et al.), arXiv 2412.03603, v1 3 Dec 2024: "over 13 billion parameters"; "we employ the Flow Matching framework"; "we first sample t in [0,1] from a logit-normal distribution"; Figure 11: "For our shifting stragty, we set a larger shifting factor s for a lower inference step" (sic).
- Wan (Wan Team), arXiv 2503.20314, v1 26 Mar 2025: 1.3B and 14B; "We leverage the flow matching framework"; "a timestep t in [0,1] sampled from a logit-normal distribution"; "x_t = t x_1 + (1 - t) x_0".
- LADD (Sauer et al.), arXiv 2403.12015, 18 Mar 2024: SD3-Turbo "using only four unguided sampling steps".
- Shortcut models (Frans, Hafner, Levine, Abbeel), arXiv 2410.12557, v1 16 Oct 2024: "condition the network not only on the current noise level but also on the desired step size"; "a single network and training phase".
- MeanFlow (Geng, Deng, Bai, Kolter, He), arXiv 2505.13447, 19 May 2025: "average velocity"; "FID of 3.43 with a single function evaluation (1-NFE) on ImageNet 256x256 trained from scratch"; "requires no pre-training, distillation, or curriculum learning".

- schedulers/scheduling_flow_match_euler_discrete.py 348-351: dynamic shifting `sigmas = self.time_shift(mu, 1.0, sigmas)` else the fixed shift; 354 `if self.config.shift_terminal:`; 646-647 `_time_shift_exponential`: `math.exp(mu) / (math.exp(mu) + (1 / t - 1) ** sigma)`; 279 stretch to terminal.
