Token-budget planner

Give the planner your domain pools (unique tokens after filtering and dedup), the stages of the run (tokens per stage) and the weights in each stage. It returns the tokens each domain supplies, its epochs, its effective data under Muennighoff et al.'s repetition law (Reading, section 2), and warnings; then it animates the same tokens in two orders: one static mixture for the whole run, and your stages.

Edit any white cell. Weights in a stage are normalised to sum to 100%; the sum you typed is shown under each stage. A pool left empty means "not published": epochs are not computed for it.

Same tokens, two orders

The static mix uses each domain's share of the whole run, so both orders contain exactly the same tokens per domain and the same epochs; only where they fall on the token axis differs. The learning-rate line is a warmup-stable-decay schedule with the decay over the last stage illustrative shape.

Try: on the illustrative preset, play "One static mix", then "Your stages", and watch the counter for maths in the final 10% of the run. Then raise the decay stage's maths weight until the planner warns that maths passes 4 epochs.
