Pair
Method

measured shingles, hash values and signatures computed with datatrove 0.10.1's own MinhashDedupSignature (seed 1, the FineWeb default), checked equal to the signature it writes; hash values are shown as thousandths of their range (261−1); text passed through the running example's PII formatter. Both pages are site chrome (forum software, a shop template, a network sign-up banner), not anyone's writing.

The same pair, hashed 2,000 times

MinHash is a random estimate: a different set of hash functions gives a different signature. To see the randomness directly, we took 24 real pairs from the running example, from Jaccard 0.30 to 0.96, and hashed each with 2,000 independent families of 1,024 functions (datatrove's arithmetic, seeds 1,000 to 2,999). Each family gives one estimate (the share of equal minimums) and one yes/no for "becomes a candidate".

Setting

measured dots: share of the 2,000 hash families in which the pair became a candidate (bands are consecutive slices of the signature, as datatrove's np.split); derived line: 1−(1−sr)b. 26 × 11 is Dolma 3's web setting; 20 × 5 and 9 × 13 are two other shapes for comparison.
