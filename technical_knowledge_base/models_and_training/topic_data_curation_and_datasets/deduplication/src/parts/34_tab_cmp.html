measured each square is one document, in WARC order. Exact: SHA-1 of the UTF-8 text, first copy kept. MinHash: datatrove's 14 × 8 signatures, candidate pairs and union-find exactly as run0 (root kept). The check removes a member only if its true Jaccard with the root is at least 0.8 (Dolma 3's README). SimHash: 64-bit fingerprints of the same 5-word shingles, Hamming distance ≤ 3, lowest index kept. SemDeDup: arctic-embed-m [CLS] vectors (512 tokens), 20 k-means clusters fitted on the survivors, NeMo Curator's "hard" ranking. Suffix array: Google's deduplicate-text-datasets (4e9888ac) on the survivors at 230 bytes (about 50 GPT-2 tokens at the 4.68 bytes per token measured on these documents); "remove every copy" is what the tool's ranges mean, "keep one" leaves the first occurrence.
