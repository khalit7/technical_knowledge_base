Papers

Lee et al., Deduplicating Training Data Makes Language Models Better (2021)

NearDup and ExactSubstr, the memorisation tables; Appendix B for suffix-array costs (60 min).

Kandpal, Wallace, Raffel, Deduplicating Training Data Mitigates Privacy Risks (2022)

Memorisation grows super-linearly with copies (30 min).

Carlini et al., Quantifying Memorization Across Neural Language Models (2022)

Three log-linear laws; Section 5.2 on deduplicated models (40 min).

Hernandez et al., Scaling Laws and Interpretability of Learning from Repeated Data (2022)

Why a few heavily repeated items hurt (40 min).

Penedo et al., The FineWeb Datasets (2024)

Section 3.4 and Appendix E: per-crawl vs global dedup (30 min for those parts).

Li et al., DataComp-LM (2024)

Appendix L: BFF, Tables 17 to 23 on dedup methods and shards (40 min).

Abbas et al., SemDeDup (2023)

Semantic dedup on LAION and C4 (40 min).

Tirumala et al., D4 (2023)

SemDeDup plus prototype pruning, and repeating selected data (45 min).

Broder, On the Resemblance and Containment of Documents (1997)

Where min-hashing comes from; 9 pages (30 min).

Manku, Jain, Das Sarma, Detecting Near-Duplicates for Web Crawling (2007)

SimHash at 8 billion pages and the permuted-table index (40 min).

Penedo et al., FineWeb2 (2025)

Section 4.5: rehydration weights from cluster sizes (20 min).

Tools and guides

datatrove minhash.py (v0.10.1)

The four MinHash stages FineWeb ran; 700 lines (40 min).

google-research/deduplicate-text-datasets

The suffix-array tool; the README walks through a full run (30 min).

datasketch lsh.py (v2.0.0)

MinHashLSH and the (b, r) rule the Band designer reimplements (15 min).

text-dedup

Reference implementations of MinHash, SimHash, suffix arrays and Bloom filters, with benchmarks (20 min).

NeMo Curator

GPU fuzzy and semantic dedup on Ray (20 min for the dedup docs).

TxT360 blog

A global dedup of 99 crawls described step by step, with sizes and days (25 min).

Leskovec, Rajaraman, Ullman, Mining of Massive Datasets, chapter 3

The textbook treatment of shingling, MinHash and LSH (60 min).
