Deduplication: exact, MinHash, suffix arrays, semantic

How a pretraining pipeline finds copies among billions of documents, and whether removing them helps: the evidence, exact hashing, MinHash and LSH from zero, union-find clusters, SimHash, suffix arrays, Bloom filters, semantic dedup (SemDeDup, D4), costs at scale and what dedup does to contamination. Measured on the topic's running example (one Common Crawl WARC of CC-MAIN-2024-30) on an Apple M1 Pro CPU, with published results kept apart.
