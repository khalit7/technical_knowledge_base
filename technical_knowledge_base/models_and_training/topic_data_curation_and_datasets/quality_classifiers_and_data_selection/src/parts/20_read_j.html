11. Whom filters disadvantage: topic, format, dialect

A quality filter is a choice about which writing counts. The question for an engineer is not whether a filter is biased (every one is) but which kinds of page it under-keeps, by how much, and whether that matters for the model you want. Three ways to measure it, all used on this page:

  1. Label the pool, then compare keep rates per label. WebOrganizer (Wettig et al. 2025) released classifiers for 24 topics and 24 formats; run here on all 2,617 run0 documents, they agree with the hand labels on @@wo_topic_agree@@ of topics and @@wo_format_agree@@ of formats (200 documents; the hand labels used the same category names) measured. Good enough for keep rates by category, not for single documents.
  2. Controlled edits: change one property of the same text and rescore (section 4: typography, spelling, layout, and LLM rewrites of the same page).
  3. Proxies at scale: top-level domain as a rough stand-in for country, on 50,000 pages.

Topics measured, keep rate at each filter's own cut among topics with at least 30 run0 documents. FineWeb-Edu (keeps @@bias_topic_edu_all@@ overall) keeps most of @@bias_topic_edu_up@@ and least of @@bias_topic_edu_down@@. DCLM (keeps @@bias_topic_dclm_all@@) keeps most of @@bias_topic_dclm_up@@ and least of @@bias_topic_dclm_down@@. Formats: FineWeb-Edu keeps most of @@bias_format_edu_up@@, least of @@bias_format_edu_down@@; DCLM most of @@bias_format_dclm_up@@, least of @@bias_format_dclm_down@@. The direction matches WebOrganizer's whole-corpus measurement (FineWeb-Edu up-weights Science & Tech. 3.8x and History 4.0x, cuts Entertainment to 0.2x; DCLM up-weights Q&A Forum 3.2x and Comment Section 1.5x, cuts Product Page to 0.2x, Table 9, published).

Region and dialect. On 50,000 FineWeb pages DCLM keeps @@tld_us@@ of .us and @@tld_com@@ of .com pages but @@tld_uk@@ of .uk, @@tld_au@@ of .au and @@tld_nz@@ of .nz; .in pages are kept at @@tld_in@@. The spelling swap (section 4) shows a causal but small part of that gap. Published audits found larger, systematic effects with GPT-3-style filters: school newspapers from richer, more educated, urban places score higher (Gururangan et al. 2022), and self-descriptions of realtors, quilters and crafters are dropped more than those of journalists and data scientists (Lucy et al. 2024).

What the hand labels add. In the uniform random sample of 200, @@lab_yes@@ documents were judged worth training a general model on, @@lab_unsure@@ unsure and @@lab_no@@ not (labelled by the agent that built this page, first 1,000 characters each, 2026-10-14). The root's stratified 100 labels found that about a third of the documents both FineWeb-Edu and DCLM drop are fine, half of them news; the {{Threshold explorer|#t-qcth}} shows precision and recall of every classifier against the random sample, and the {{Bias browser|#t-qcbi}} lists the labelled documents behind every cell.

What this does and does not show
Keep rates per category are measured; whether under-keeping a category hurts a model is not, and the published ablations suggest it often does not hurt benchmarks (FineWeb-Edu's gains come with heavy topic skew). It matters when the model's users write or ask about the dropped categories: a model trained on DCLM-baseline sees less British-spelled and typographically formatted text, a model trained on FineWeb-Edu sees little entertainment, sport or commerce. Decide which skew you accept and check it, rather than inheriting one.
