Threshold explorer: where to cut, and what each cut keeps

Every classifier on this page ranks the same 2,617 run0 documents. Choose one, move the cut, and see what survives: how many documents and words, how the kept set compares with the hand labels and with Llama 3's educational grades, and how much it overlaps with what every other classifier would keep at the same rate. All measured on Apple M1 Pro (CPU), 2026-10-14 and 15.

Hand labels: precision and recall as the cut moves

Against 200 documents drawn uniformly at random and read by the agent that built this page (Claude), first 1,000 characters each: precision = share of kept labelled documents judged worth training a general model on ("yes"); recall = share of all "yes" documents that are kept. Unsure labels count as neither. With @@lab_yes@@ "yes" documents in the sample, each point has a binomial standard error of up to about 6 points: read the shape, not the second digit.

Overlap with every other classifier at the same keep rate

Jaccard = documents kept by both / documents kept by either. Grey tick: what two random selections of this size would share. Two classifiers with the same idea of quality would sit near 100%.

Against Llama 3's grades (50,000 of the 232,000 FineWeb-Edu annotation pages, seed 1)

Precision and recall of "Llama 3 score ≥ 3" (FineWeb-Edu's definition of educational) when each classifier keeps the same top share of the 50,000 annotated pages; AUC over all of them. The FineWeb-Edu classifier and the root's fastText were trained on these labels (the first on rows that include these), so their numbers are not out of sample.
