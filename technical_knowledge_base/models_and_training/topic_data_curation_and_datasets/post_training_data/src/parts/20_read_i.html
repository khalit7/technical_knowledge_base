9. Quality control for post-training data

The pretraining pipeline's steps (dedup, decontamination, filtering) all apply, with three differences: rows are few, so each one matters; prompts are reused on purpose across stages; and the evaluations that will be reported are known in advance, so contamination is both easier to check and more damaging.

That is by design: one prompt pool serves SFT, preference and RL, each stage with a different "what good looks like". Filtering by judge, and its cost

Three filters recur in the published recipes, each measured somewhere on this page: correctness (keep a reasoning trace only if a checker accepts it; section 4: the checker you choose changes what survives), a judge's rating (UltraFeedback-style 1 to 5 scores, ties dropped; section 6: the judge's taste, including length, becomes the data's), and difficulty (keep RL prompts the model sometimes solves; section 7). Plus string filters for teacher artefacts (identity, knowledge-cutoff sentences, repetition), which the root's {{Post-training data lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d811fa5ecda8d365ab00f}} runs on the Tulu 3 sample: Ai2's own filters remove 30 of 5,000 rows there, while two plausible extra rules (English only, no refusals) would remove whole subsets that exist on purpose.
