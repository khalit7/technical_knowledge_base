Pair inspector

Eight preference sets, measured the same way measured: lengths of the final chosen and rejected answers in Qwen3 tokens, ties and identical pairs, how the share of "chosen is longer" changes with the size of the length gap, who wrote the answers, and the pairs themselves. A share of 50% in a gap bucket means length carries no information about the label there.

Length ratio, chosen over rejected

log2 of (chosen tokens + 1) / (rejected tokens + 1), clipped at ±4; right of the line, the chosen answer is longer.

Chosen is longer, by gap

Gap = |log2 ratio|; 1 means one answer is twice as long. Pairs with equal lengths or identical texts left out.

The pairs

A seeded sample per set: half drawn at random, half among pairs where the rejected answer is longer (to show both sides), trimmed. HH-RLHF harmless-base contains offensive requests by design; read with that in mind.

UltraFeedback: four binarization rules on the same ratings

Raw openbmb/UltraFeedback evol_instruct subset, every prompt with four rated answers. overall_score is the critique score Argilla found unreliable; the other rules use the mean of the four aspect ratings. The animation in Reading, section 6 shows single prompts.
