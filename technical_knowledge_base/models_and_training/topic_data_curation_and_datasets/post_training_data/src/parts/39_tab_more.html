{{Tulu 3 (arXiv 2411.15124)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15124}}

The most complete open description of a post-training data pipeline: Tables 7, 8, 15, 22. 2 to 3 hours; data sections 1 hour.

{{OLMo 3 (arXiv 2512.13961)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13961}}

Dolci: thinking and instruct mixtures, delta learning, offline difficulty filtering. Data sections about 1 hour.

The bootstrapping loop and the ROUGE-L filter. 30 min.

{{LIMA (arXiv 2305.11206)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11206}}

1,000 rows and the Superficial Alignment Hypothesis. 25 min.

{{UltraFeedback (arXiv 2310.01377)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01377}}

AI-feedback preference data; read with Argilla's cleaned card. 30 min.

{{HH-RLHF (arXiv 2204.05862)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05862}}

Human preference collection at scale. 1 hour; section 2 for the data.

{{A Long Way to Go: length correlations in RLHF (arXiv 2310.03716)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03716}}

How much of RLHF's gain is length. 30 min.

{{DeepSeek-R1 (arXiv 2501.12948)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948}}

Rejection-sampled reasoning SFT data, Appendix B.3.3 and Table 5. Data parts 30 min.

{{IFEval (arXiv 2311.07911)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07911}} and {{IFBench (arXiv 2507.02833)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02833}}

Verifiable instruction constraints and training on them. 20 min each.

{{HelpSteer2 (arXiv 2406.08673)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08673}}

Human ratings on five attributes, verbosity among them; about ten thousand response pairs. 30 min.

{{HardTests (arXiv 2505.24098)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24098}}

How weak tests mislead code RL, measured. 30 min.

{{SWE-Gym (arXiv 2412.21139)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.21139}}, {{SWE-rebench (arXiv 2505.20411)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20411}}, {{SWE-smith (arXiv 2504.21798)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21798}}

Three ways to build SWE environments: by hand, mined, synthesised. 30 min each.
