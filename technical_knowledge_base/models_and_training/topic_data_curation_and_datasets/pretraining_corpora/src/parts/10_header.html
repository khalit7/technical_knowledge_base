Pretraining corpora: lineage, licences and what to use

Where pretraining text comes from (Common Crawl), how the open corpora descend from it from C4 to FineWeb, DCLM, Dolma 3 and Nemotron-CC, the code, maths, papers, books and multilingual corpora beside them, what their sizes and licences really mean, and which to use for a budget. Samples measured side by side on an Apple M1 Pro; published numbers kept apart. A child of Topic: data-curation-and-datasets. Data as of October 2026. Reading: 28 min, plus 15 in closed details.
