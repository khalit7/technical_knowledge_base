Pretraining corpora: lineage, licences and what to use

Where pretraining text comes from (Common Crawl from zero: crawls, WARC, WET and WAT files, the index, robots.txt), how the open corpora descend from one another from C4 to FineWeb, DCLM, Dolma and Nemotron-CC, the code, math, books, papers and multilingual corpora beside them, what each one's licence allows, what their published ablations show once checked against their own tables, and which to train on for a given budget and goal. Samples of the corpora measured side by side on an Apple M1 Pro; published numbers kept apart and labelled. Data as of October 2026.
