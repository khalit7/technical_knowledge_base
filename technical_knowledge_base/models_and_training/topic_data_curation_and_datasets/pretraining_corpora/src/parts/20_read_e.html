Three things to take from it. The bulk of the loss happens before any learned filter: FineWeb's rules, language ID and deduplication already drop about nine pages in ten (the topic's {{Pipeline lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d811fa5ecda8d365ab00f}} measures where, stage by stage). An educational filter at threshold 3 keeps @@kp_edu_of_fw_pct@@ of what FineWeb kept, so such a corpus is small by design: the 1.3T against 18.5T of section 3. And moving the cut from 3 to 2 multiplies what survives by about four (the toggle), the trade Nemotron-CC made explicit: for a long run, more medium-quality tokens can beat repeating a small high-quality set.

Nemotron-CC keeps a different set. Only @@nm_both_pct@@ of the Nemotron-CC pages found from this file are also in FineWeb: different extraction (jusText instead of trafilatura) and heuristic filters applied only to its lower buckets keep different pages. Its total is lower than FineWeb's @@kp_fw@@, not higher, because Nemotron-CC deduplicated globally across its 99 crawls, so a page already captured earlier survives under the earlier crawl's record. A per-file view measures what each pipeline did with one crawl, not how big each corpus is.
