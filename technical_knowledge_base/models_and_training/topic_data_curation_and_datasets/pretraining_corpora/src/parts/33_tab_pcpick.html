What to train on: a starting mix for your budget and goal

Choose the model size, the training budget in tokens, the goal and the licence constraint. The tool proposes a starting mix assembled from published open recipes (each share names the recipe it comes from), then checks the one thing a mix most often gets wrong: whether each corpus is big enough for the tokens you will draw from it. Epochs = tokens drawn from a corpus divided by its published size; past about 4 epochs, repeated data is worth markedly less than fresh data ({{Muennighoff et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16264}}; the {{Data mixing|n:3c65c17b0d0d819ea5d6c59471e66875}} page takes the curve apart). When a corpus would be repeated too often, the tool moves the excess to the next larger corpus of the same kind that your licence choice allows.

A starting point, not an optimiser. The shares are published choices, not fitted to your goal; sizes are each corpus's own published count in its own tokenizer (Reading section 2: within a few percent for English web text, more for code and other languages). Run your own small ablation before a large run.
