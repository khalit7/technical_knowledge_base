0. In one screen

A pretraining corpus is the fixed, versioned set of documents a language model reads during pretraining: trillions of tokens, mostly web pages, plus code, maths, papers and books. Almost every open corpus starts from the same raw material, Common Crawl, a free monthly snapshot of a few billion web pages, and differs in what it keeps: how it pulls text out of HTML, which language and quality rules it applies, how it removes duplicates, whether a learned classifier picks "good" pages, and what it adds besides the web. This page follows that family from C4 (2019) to Dolma 3 and Nemotron-CC v2 (2025), reads each corpus's card for its source, size (and in which tokenizer), recipe and licence, checks the published comparisons against their own tables, and measures samples of @@ms_n@@ corpora side by side on a laptop.

Common Crawl (WARC)→C4, The Pile (2019 to 2020): rules, hand-made mixes→RefinedWeb, RedPajama, Dolma (2023): web only, open recipes→FineWeb, FineWeb-Edu, DCLM, Nemotron-CC (2024): ablated steps, learned filters→Dolma 3, Nemotron-CC v2, FinePDFs, FineWeb2, Common Pile (2025 to 2026): pools, synthetic text, PDFs, licences

Seven ideas that carry the page

1. One raw file, a tenth kept

Of the running example's @@kp_html@@ HTML pages from one July 2024 WARC file, FineWeb kept @@kp_fw@@ (@@kp_fw_pct@@) and FineWeb-Edu @@kp_edu@@ (@@kp_edu_pct@@). Run here on CPU, the published classifier picks @@kp_mine_ge3@@ pages at its threshold (section 4).

2. "Tokens" needs a tokenizer

FineWeb counts GPT-2 tokens, DCLM GPT-NeoX tokens, C4's 156B are spaCy words. On English web text the tokenizers agree within a few percent; on French they differ by a quarter and on code by @@tok_code_ratio@@ times (section 2).

3. Learned filters won, at a price

A fastText classifier trained on instruction-like text beat every other filter in DCLM (30.2 Core against 27.5); FineWeb-Edu's LLM-distilled classifier lifted MMLU 33 to 37. Both keep under a tenth of the web, and both select for what the benchmarks test (sections 3 and 5).

4. Every corpus wins its own paper

The one third-party run of all three ranks Nemotron-CC-HQ, DCLM, FineWeb-Edu at 0.66, 0.57, 0.56, mostly on MMLU and CommonsenseQA; the harness alone can swap DCLM and FineWeb-Edu (section 5).

5. Budget decides the filter

Strict filters suit runs up to about 1T tokens; at 10T+ they repeat too much, which is why Nemotron-CC keeps medium buckets and rewrites low ones, and why recipes switch pools between stages (sections 3 and 9).

6. Licences come in four layers

Content, crawler terms, dataset licence, access gate: ODC-By licenses the compilation, not the pages; NVIDIA's agreement forbids redistribution; only Common Pile is openly licensed throughout, with about 15B tokens of web text (section 7).

7. The crawlable web is shrinking

Measured here on Common Crawl's own robots.txt captures, sites blocking GPTBot from the whole site rose from @@rb_2023_23_gptbot@@ (May 2023, all of it from "block every crawler" rules) to @@rb_2026_39_gptbot@@ (September 2026), and CCBot from @@rb_2023_23_ccbot@@ to @@rb_2026_39_ccbot@@ (section 1).
