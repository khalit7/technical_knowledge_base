Lineage: who descends from whom

Every corpus on the page as one row, oldest at the top, in the column of its kind. A line joins a corpus to what it was built from: solid when it is a filtered subset of the parent, dashed when it re-implements or extends the parent's recipe on new data, dotted when it takes the parent whole as one component of a union. Circle area follows the published token count (log scale; a hollow circle has no comparable token count), the ring colour is the licence class. Tap a row for its full card: source, size and in which tokenizer, recipe, licence verbatim, who trained on it, the published evidence checked against its own tables, and its status in October 2026.

Try: press Play (or step with the arrows) to replay the field year by year and watch the counters; filter to Code or Math; tap FineWeb-Edu and follow its line up to Common Crawl.

Data: src/make_corpora.py (every field sourced; checked 2026-10-14). Token counts are as published, each in its own tokenizer, so circle sizes compare orders of magnitude, not exact sizes (Reading section 2 measures how much tokenizers differ).
