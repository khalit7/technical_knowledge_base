Contamination checker

Pick a benchmark item and see every training document a published rule flags for it: which corpus, which rules fire, how much of the item each document covers, the matched text, and what the document really contains.

Try: MMLU, category "copy": high-school maths questions sitting in Tulu 3's RLVR prompts with their answers. Then category "passage": history items whose quoted speeches fill FineWeb-Edu. Then MATH-500 "diagram": the same plotting code, different problems.

measured on the running example. A rule's "copies" are pairs labelled copy; variant and source are the grey zone; passage, phrase and diagram are false positives. datatrove's column counts documents it removed (only some were also flagged by the other rules and read).

Tokens as the matcher sees them (datatrove's simplify_text, numbers kept); dotted underline: the question part, the rest is the answer or options. Long items show 120 tokens around the match. Identical matched windows are shown once, up to four per item, worst category first. Documents from raw (unscrubbed) corpora show only the matched window, which is the text they share with the item.
