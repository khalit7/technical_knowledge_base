Storage and loading at scale: formats, shards, streaming

How a curated corpus is stored and fed to a training run: documents in JSONL, Parquet, Arrow and shard formats; tokens in flat memory-mapped arrays; packing documents into fixed-length windows; sharding, shuffling and streaming from object storage; data loaders, resuming mid-epoch, and what it costs in bytes and bandwidth.
