The idea in one screen

A generator model turns seeds (a topic, a real web page, a problem with a checker) into text a student model learns from faster. It pays only while the text stays grounded in real documents or checked, and only when it is added to the real data, never put in its place: a model refit on its own output loses the rare things first (model collapse).

Three ways to make synthetic text, one gate, and the loop to avoid

Colours as everywhere on this page: real, rephrased or transformed, generated from seeds. Where the facts come from decides the risk: the generator's memory (it invents and narrows), the real page (the web's variety survives), or a checker (only what passes is kept). Seed
what goes into the prompt to steer one output: a topic, three random words, a persona, a real web page.
Rephrasing, transformation
rewriting a real page in another style; a transformation also changes its structure (questions and answers, a list of facts).
Distillation data
outputs of a stronger model collected to train a weaker one.
Tails
the rare events of a distribution: uncommon words, rare facts, unusual styles.
Replace, accumulate
each new model trains only on the newest generated data, or on all the real data plus everything generated so far.
