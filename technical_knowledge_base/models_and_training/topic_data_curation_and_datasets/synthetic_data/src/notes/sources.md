# Verified sources (read 2026-10-14)

Checked by reading the papers' own tables (arXiv HTML or PDF text), dataset cards (raw README) and blog sources. Version read in brackets. "Discrepancy" = the source disagrees with itself; the page shows both.

## Generated from scratch
- **TinyStories** (arXiv 2305.07759 v2, 2023-05-24). GPT-3.5 and GPT-4; vocabulary "a typical 3-year-old child would understand" (abstract: 3 to 4-year-olds); ~1500 basic words split into nouns, verbs, adjectives, "3 words are chosen randomly (one verb, one noun, and one adjective)" plus a random subset of features (dialogue, plot twist, bad ending, moral value); without it "a very repetitive dataset" even at high temperature. Models under 10M parameters, or one transformer block. GPT-4 grades grammar, creativity, consistency (~50 beginnings, 10 completions each). Card: cdla-sharing-1.0; 2,141,709 rows (train 2.12M, validation 22k); V2 = GPT-4-only. Token count not in the paper (the 470M figure is Gerstgrasser et al.'s).
- **phi-1** (2306.11644 v2). ~6B filtered code tokens (The Stack + StackOverflow; GPT-4 annotated ~100k samples for "educational value", random forest on codegen embeddings), <1B tokens GPT-3.5 textbooks, ~180M tokens exercises (also "less than 180M", "<200M": discrepancy). 1.3B params; HumanEval 50.6%, MBPP 55.5%; phi-1-base 29%; 8 A100 for 4 days; ~8 passes over 7B tokens (51B tokens, 770 GPU hours, Fig 2.1). Unfiltered 350M saturates 12.19% HumanEval vs 17.68% filtered. Contamination: pruning 42.5K to 354K of 879.5K exercises (tau 0.95 to 0.8) leaves HumanEval 50.6/45.1/46.3/45.1 vs StarCoder-Prompted 41.5 (Table 3).
- **phi-1.5** (2309.05463 v1). 7B phi-1 data + ~20B synthetic textbook tokens; "20K topics to seed"; web samples in prompts for diversity; 150B training tokens, 80% synthetic, 20% phi-1 data. Table 1 says data size 30B (prose 7B+20B: discrepancy). Table 2: phi-1.5 WinoGrande .734 ARC-E .756 ARC-C .444 BoolQ .758 SIQA .526; phi-1.5-web-only .604 .666 .329 .632 .414; Falcon-rw-1.3B .607 .633 .282 .632 .405.
- **phi-2** blog 2023-12-12: 2.7B, 1.4T training tokens, 96 A100 14 days. 250B data tokens only on the HF card.
- **phi-3** (2404.14219 v4): mini 3.8B on 3.3T; "data optimal regime"; phase 2 adds synthetic.
- **phi-4** (2412.08905 v1, 2024-12-12). 14B, ~10T tokens. 50 synthetic dataset types, "about 400B unweighted tokens" (Sec 2.2). Table 5: Web 15% 1.3T unique 1.2 epochs; Web rewrites 15% 290B 5.2; Synthetic 40% 290B 13.8; Code 20% 820B 2.4; Acquired 10% 580B 1.7 (400B vs 290B+290B: discrepancy). "Models trained only with synthetic data underperformed on the knowledge-heavy benchmarks and demonstrated increased hallucinations." Table 3 (13B, no web): Synthetic TQA -14.8; Synthetic + Web Rewrites TQA -7.7 vs phi-3-medium. Techniques: multi-agent prompting, self-revision, instruction reversal. Decontamination: hybrid 13-gram and 7-gram.
- **Cosmopedia** blog 2024-03-20 (Ben Allal, Lozhkov, van Strien). Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1; "over 30 million files and 25 billion tokens". Curated seeds Stanford (250,000 units), OpenStax (16,000), Khan Academy, WikiHow; 4 audiences x 3 styles "up to 12 times"; web seeds "over 80% of the prompts": 145 clusters, 112 topics kept (card: discard 35 of 145 = 110: discrepancy); 23 million web prompts. Duplicates: changing only audience "insufficient"; openings "Once upon a time", "The sun hung low in the sky"; MinHash finds "less than 1%" duplicates. >10k GPU hours; "most of the time ... spent on meticulous prompt engineering". Decontamination: 10-gram then SequenceMatcher > 0.5; web+stanford+openstax removed ARC 49 (16 unique), BoolQ 386 (41), HellaSwag 6 (5), PIQA 5 (3). Card rows: web_samples_v1 12,426,348; web_samples_v2 10,345,867; stories 4,992,964; auto_math_text 1,949,895; stanford 1,020,024; wikihow 179,191; openstax 126,332; khanacademy 24,123 (sum 31,064,744). Columns prompt, text, seed_data, text_token_length, format, audience. apache-2.0. cosmo-1b: card says 1.8B; 180B tokens (6 epochs of 30B).

SmolLM2 (2502.02737): Cosmopedia v2 only in stage 4 at 4%.

## Rephrasing
- **WRAP** (2401.16380, ACL 2024). Frozen Mistral-7B-Instruct; styles easy, medium (Wikipedia), hard, Q/A; prompts App. G; max 300 output tokens; 1:1 real:synthetic; C4 (~170B); 128M/350M/1.3B; 1.3B for 300B tokens. Abstract ~3x speed-up; intro "5x lesser data, or 3x lesser compute" (figures only). Pile perplexity: abstract ">10%", body "~50%" (discrepancy). Table 1 avg: Half C4 47.4, Full C4 47.3, RW-160B 47.6, RW-320B 47.5, Pythia 47.0, TinyLlama 47.4, Synthetic 49.4, Synthetic+C4 49.4. Table 2: Half C4 43.1, Full C4 43.5 (prose mislabels 43.5 as the 85B run), RW160 44.1, RW320 44.3, Pythia 44.6, TinyLlama 45.6, Synthetic 45.0, Synthetic+C4 45.5; MMLU ~24-25 everywhere. "synthetic data can not impart 'new knowledge'. It can only help pre-train faster". Qwen-1.8B rephraser comparable (figure). Cost: 3M tokens/hour on one A100 (Mistral-7B); 85B tokens ~25K GPU hours (arithmetic gives ~28K).
- **Nemotron-CC** (2412.02595 v2). Mistral NeMo 12B Instruct, FP8, top-p 0.9, temperature 0.5. Low quality: Wikipedia-style (WRAP prompt). High quality: diverse QA, distill, extract knowledge, knowledge list. Chunk limits (fn 11): 512 Wikipedia, 2,000 Distill, 1,400 Extract, 1,000 QA and List (incl. prompt). Post-processing: drop incomplete, strip markdown and "Here is a paraphrased version:", drop < 50 tokens; QA pairs shuffled and appended after the source segment. Table 3 (B tokens): low Wikipedia 336.3; high Wikipedia 372.9, Diverse QA 499.5, Distill 157.6, Extract 303.6, List 203.2 (~1.87T). Table 10 (8B, 1T tokens, avg / MMLU): LQ-Base 52.5/48.2, LQ-Synthetic 54.0/47.1, HQ-Base 55.8/53.4, HQ-Synthetic 56.7/53.6.
- **Nemotron-CC-v2** card (2025-08-18): Qwen3-30B-A3B rephrasing; English CC 3359.8B; synthetic CC 1257.3B; Diverse QA 692.9B; translated Diverse QA 558.2B (15 languages); generator split Mistral-Nemo 1629.1B, Qwen3-30B-A3B 879.1B. Flow-down notice for Qwen and DeepSeek licences.
- **BeyondWeb** (2508.10975, DatologyAI, vendor). Table 1 14-task avg (1B/3B/8B): RPJ 50.7/53.5/56.6; QA WRAP 52.5/55.3/58.4; Cosmopedia 52.2/55.8/58.6; Nemotron-Synth 54.3/58.8/61.1; BeyondWeb 57.4/60.8/63.7. 8B matches RedPajama's 180B-token score at 23.2B tokens (7.7x). - **REWIRE** (2506.04689 v3, Meta). Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct with reasoning before rewrite; rewrite all of DCLM-RefinedWeb pool; keep top 10% by fastText. Table 1 CORE: 1B raw 0.289, raw+rewritten 0.299, rewritten only 0.270, 2x pool 0.294; 7B raw 0.420, raw+rewritten 0.445, 2x pool 0.425. ~82% of rewritten docs come from pages that would have been discarded.
- **Kang et al.** (2510.01631, EMNLP 2025): Mistral-7B-Instruct generator; rephrased alone not faster than web; ~1/3 rephrased + 2/3 web 5-10x faster to the same validation loss at larger budgets; textbook-only worse; Llama-3-70B generator not better than 8B. Metric is validation loss.

## Model collapse
- **Shumailov et al.**, Nature 631, 755-759, published 2024-07-24 (preprint 2305.17493, 2023-05-27). Author Correction 2025-03-21 (alpha/beta notation). OPT-125m fine-tuned on wikitext2; 64-token blocks; five-way beam search; generation 0 + 9 generations; 5 seeds. Setting A: 5 epochs, no original data; Setting B: 10 epochs, random 10% of original kept. Model 0: perplexity 34 (zero-shot 115). Setting A loses "20 to 28 perplexity points" (figure only); B "minor degradation". Measured perplexity of generated sequences under generation-0 model: more low-perplexity sequences and a longer tail. Repetition penalty 2.0 doubles perplexity. Three error sources (Nature): statistical approximation, functional expressivity, functional approximation (preprint names two). Discrete case: Markov chain with delta absorbing states. Gaussian: variance to zero almost surely.
- **Gerstgrasser et al.** (2404.01413, ICML 2024 workshop). TinyStories (470M tokens), GPT-2 9M, Llama-2 12M/42M/126M, new model each iteration. Table 2 test cross-entropy (t=1 / t=4 acc / t=4 replace / t=10 replace): GPT-2 9M 1.82/1.74/2.39/2.91; temp 0.3: 1.82/1.75/5.82/9.85; Llama-2 12M 2.06/1.94/2.72; 42M 1.90/1.76/2.52; 126M 1.71/1.59/2.23. Linear regression: replace error grows linearly in n; accumulate bounded by pi^2/6 times the first-iteration error. VAE: accumulate still degrades slowly.
- **Kazdan et al.** (2410.16713): replace, accumulate, accumulate-subsample (fixed compute); subsample degrades more than accumulate, less than replace, often plateaus higher. Gemma 2 SFT: number of real examples matters more than proportion; below ~1024 real examples a little synthetic helps.
- **Dohmatob et al.** Tale of Tails (2402.07043, ICML 2024): cut tails change scaling laws (plateau); loss grows linearly with generations; mixing clean data gives a plateau then recovery. Strong Model Collapse (2410.04840, ICLR 2025): v2 "as little as 1%", proceedings "1 per 1000" (discrepancy).
- **Feng et al.** (2406.07515 v2): verification. Eigenvalues: greedy 20.2%, oracle best-of-50 60.4%; self-selection by perplexity ~19-20%. XLSUM: oracle-selected 12.5% beats training on 100% real labels.
- **Alemohammad et al.** MAD (2307.01850): fully synthetic, synthetic augmentation (fixed real data only delays degradation), fresh data loop (enough fresh real data prevents it); sampling bias keeps quality but kills diversity faster.
- **Bertrand et al.** (2310.00429, ICLR 2024): stable if model class is close to data and enough real data reused (Theorem 1: lambda(1+L eps/alpha) < 1/2; conclusion writes < 1: discrepancy).
- **Seddik et al.** (2404.05090), **Briesch et al.** (2311.16822): diversity falls in self-consuming loops; fresh data limits it.
- **AI text on the web**: Graphite (SEO vendor) 2026-05-15 update: ~50% of new English articles in Common Crawl sample AI-written, plateaued (commercial detectors). Ahrefs (vendor) 2025-05-19: 74.2% of 900k new pages contain some AI content. Brooks et al. (2410.08044): >= 4.36% of new English Wikipedia articles (Aug 2024). FineWeb blog: "delve" style phrases rise steeply after CC-MAIN-2023-14; no filtering applied. No peer-reviewed estimate for Common Crawl as a whole found.
