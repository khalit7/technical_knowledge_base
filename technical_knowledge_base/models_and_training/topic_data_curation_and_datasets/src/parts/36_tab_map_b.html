Licences: what a corpus lets you do

Three layers of rights stack on every web corpus: the original pages' copyright (which no dataset licence can waive), Common Crawl's Terms of Use for the crawl itself, and the dataset's own licence for the compilation (the selection, cleaning and metadata). ODC-By (Open Data Commons Attribution) licenses only the compilation and asks for attribution; it says nothing about the pages inside. Gated datasets add a click-through agreement on top.
