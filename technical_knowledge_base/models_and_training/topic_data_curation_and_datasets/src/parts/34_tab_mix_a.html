A pretraining run draws its tokens from several domains (web, code, math, papers, encyclopedias, ...) and the mixture says how many tokens come from each, and when. This lab gives the overview: what the weights mean, how temperature sampling and repetition trade off on the running example's real domain sizes, how labs choose weights with small proxy models, how a short final stage changes the same token budget, and why aggressive filtering makes long runs a mixing problem.
