Start here (outside)

FineWeb: decanting the web

Hugging Face, 2024. The best end-to-end account of building a web corpus, every stage ablated. Self-reported. about 1 h 30 min

DataComp-LM (DCLM)

Li et al., 2024. A benchmark for data curation at fixed compute; extraction, dedup and filter comparisons (Tables 3, 4, 16). Self-reported for its own filter, third-party for the others it reruns. about 1 h 30 min

Nemotron-CC

NVIDIA, December 2024. The quality-against-quantity trade-off, classifier ensembles and rephrasing. Self-reported. about 45 min

Pretraining progress is mostly data

Dwarkesh Patel and Jerry Han, 2026-09-08. Recipes crossed with corpora from 2019 to 2025. Third-party, small scale. about 25 min

Tulu 3

AI2, November 2024. The open reference for post-training data: SFT mixture, on-policy preferences, RLVR, decontamination. Self-reported. about 1 h 30 min
