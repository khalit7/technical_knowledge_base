What "quality" means here: three recipes

A quality filter is a model that gives each document a number, plus a rule that keeps documents above a cut-off. There is no definition of quality behind it other than what the model was trained to prefer, so the first question about any filter is: preferred over what, by whom?

Do the two classifiers agree?

Two ways to ask it. Rank correlation (Spearman) asks whether they order all documents the same way. Top-slice overlap asks what a filter actually does: if each keeps its top k%, how much of what one keeps does the other keep? The overlap is shown as intersection over union, the measure Nemotron-CC's Table 8 reports.
