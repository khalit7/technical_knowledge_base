Exact dedup: hashes of documents, lines and sentence spans

Why bother at all (the Reading tab's {{section 1|#t-read}} table and {{Deduplication|n:3f95c17b0d0d8171ac17e43ab5ea276f}}, section 1, have the evidence): repeats waste training compute, make a model memorise (a 1.5B model trained on C4 emitted memorised text in 1.9% of its output tokens, 0.19% after near-duplicate removal; published Lee et al. 2021, Table 4), and copy benchmark items. Muennighoff et al. found up to about 4 repeats nearly as good as fresh data, so dedup is mostly about not spending the budget twice on the same boilerplate.

The idea. Compute a hash (a short fingerprint, here 64 bits, so equal inputs give equal fingerprints and different inputs almost never collide) of each unit, sort or group by it, keep one per value. The unit decides what you catch:

measured Three run0 stages: extracted (19,241 documents straight out of Trafilatura, every language), filtered (2,692, after language ID and the Gopher, C4 and FineWeb rules) and final (2,617, after MinHash). "Normalised" hashes datatrove's simplify_text (lowercase, no punctuation, numbers normalised). Lines follow the Dolma toolkit's paragraph loop: split on newlines, skip blank ones, keep the first copy. No URL appears twice in one WARC file: Common Crawl deduplicates URLs within a crawl, so URL dedup only matters across crawls.

The lines most often repeated across different sites

measured on the 19,241 extracted documents; only lines seen on at least 5 different hosts are listed, so none is one site's own text. 3,503 distinct lines qualify, with 111,609 occurrences between them. Short lines are navigation and table markup; among lines of 40 characters or more, consent-plugin text dominates: the extractor kept the cookie banner as if it were content.

C4's rule: every repeated three-sentence span

C4 "discarded all but one of any three-sentence span occurring more than once" (Raffel et al. 2020). datatrove implements it as SentenceDedup: split each document into sentences, hash every window of 3 consecutive sentences after normalising, mark windows seen before, cut them, then drop documents left with fewer than 3 sentences or 50 words.

measured datatrove 0.10.1 SentenceDedupSignature, SentenceFindDedups and SentenceDedupFilter with n_sentences=3, min_doc_words=50, on run0's filtered (before MinHash) and final (after MinHash) stages. Of the 70 documents it drops before MinHash, 64 are also among MinHash's 75 and all 50 exact copies are among them: on whole-document duplicates the methods agree; the span rule adds the 24 documents it shortens.
