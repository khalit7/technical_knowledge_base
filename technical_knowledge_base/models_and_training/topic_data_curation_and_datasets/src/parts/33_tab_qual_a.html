Quality lab: which documents are worth training on, and who decides

Every modern web corpus runs a model that scores each document and keeps a top slice. This lab runs the two published scorers that define today's open corpora, the FineWeb-Edu classifier and the DCLM fastText classifier, plus CCNet's perplexity filter and a fastText classifier trained here, on the same documents: the 2,617 pages of our Common Crawl file that survived the {{Pipeline lab|#t-pipe}}'s heuristic filters and the {{Dedup lab|#t-dedup}}'s MinHash step (run0). Then it reads what they drop. Everything below was measured on an Apple M1 Pro (CPU, 4 threads, low priority on a shared machine) on 2026-10-14, except two runs on its GPU on 2026-10-15 that say so, unless it says published.
