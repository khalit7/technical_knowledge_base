Primary sources for this page

The FineWeb Datasets

Penedo et al., 2024. Sections 3.2 to 3.6: WARC against WET, base filtering, C4 ablations, how the custom filters were found. 60 min

The RefinedWeb Dataset

Penedo et al., 2023. Section 3 and Appendix G: the UT1 blocklist and its curation, URL word lists, line-wise corrections, the 0.65 threshold. 45 min

Scaling Language Models: Methods, Analysis & Insights from Training Gopher

Rae et al., 2021. Appendix A: MassiveWeb's quality and repetition rules and Table A1's thresholds. 20 min (appendix)

Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning (T5)

Raffel et al., 2020. Section 2.2: C4's rules. With TFDS's c4_utils.py. 15 min

DataComp-LM

Li et al., 2024. Section 4.2 and Appendix J: resiliparse against trafilatura against WET, measured downstream and in throughput. 40 min (those parts)

Nemotron-CC

Su et al., 2024. Why jusText and fewer filters: token yield against quality. 40 min

Dolma

Soldaini et al., 2024. Section 5 and Appendices G and H: language-filter audit, Jigsaw toxicity classifiers and their thresholds. 45 min

Documenting Large Webtext Corpora: C4

Dodge et al., 2021. What the bad-words list removed, and whose English. 40 min

FineWeb2

Penedo et al., 2025. Section 4: GlotLID against lid.176 and per-language thresholds. 40 min (section 4)

GlotLID

Kargaran et al., 2023. Language ID for 1,665 languages; low-resource failure modes. 40 min

Bag of Tricks for Efficient Text Classification

Joulin et al., 2016. The fastText model behind lid.176, GlotLID and many quality classifiers. 20 min

OpenWebMath

Paster et al., 2023. Extracting LaTeX from Common Crawl's raw HTML when MathJax has not run. 30 min

Web Content Extraction Benchmark

Bevendorff et al., SIGIR 2023. Code, 3,985 annotated pages and the results table used in section 3. 20 min

Trafilatura (ACL 2021 demo paper)

Barbaresi, 2021. The library's design in its author's words. 20 min
