Extraction and filtering: from WARC to clean text

How a raw Common Crawl record becomes a training document: HTML extraction (trafilatura, resiliparse, jusText, readability) read from their source and measured on the same pages, tables, code and maths, language identification (fastText lid.176 against GlotLID), every heuristic rule with its origin and its hand-labelled mistakes, URL, adult and toxicity filters, line-level against document-level edits, and the library defaults that quietly change a dataset.
