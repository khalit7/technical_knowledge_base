Comparison Summary
Hy3 is amongst the leading models in intelligence and reasonably priced when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also faster than average, however somewhat verbose. The model supports text input, outputs text, and has a 256k tokens context window.
Hy3 scores 25 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 18). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 170M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 140M.
Pricing for Hy3 is $0.14 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $0.30) and $0.55 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $1.15). On average, it costs $0.07 per task to evaluate Hy3 on the Intelligence Index.
At 88 tokens per second, Hy3 is faster than average (69).

Total Parameters
The total number of trainable weights and biases in the model, expressed in billions. These parameters are learned during training and determine the model's ability to process and generate responses.
Active Parameters at Inference Time
The number of parameters actually executed during each inference forward pass, expressed in billions. For Mixture of Experts (MoE) models, a routing mechanism selects a subset of experts per token, resulting in fewer active than total parameters. Dense models use all parameters, so active equals total.
Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about Hy3
When was Hy3 released?
Hy3 was released on July 6, 2026.
Who created Hy3?
Hy3 was created by Tencent.
How intelligent is Hy3?
Hy3 scores 25 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among other open weight models of similar size (median: 18).
How fast is Hy3?
Hy3 generates output at 87.6 tokens per second (based on the median across providers serving the model), which is above average compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 68.9 t/s).
What is the latency of Hy3?
Hy3 has a time to first token (TTFT) of 3.17s (based on the median across providers serving the model), which is at the higher end compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 2.24s).
How much does Hy3 cost?
Hy3 costs $0.14 per 1M input tokens (very competitive, median: $0.44) and $0.55 per 1M output tokens (very competitive, median: $1.68), based on the median across providers serving the model.
What is Hy3 API pricing?
Hy3 costs $0.14 per 1M input tokens and $0.55 per 1M output tokens (based on the median across providers serving the model). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $0.11 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider. Compare provider pricing
How verbose is Hy3?
When evaluated on the Intelligence Index, Hy3 generated 170M output tokens, which is somewhat higher than average compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 140M).
Is Hy3 a reasoning model?
Yes, Hy3 is a reasoning model. It uses extended thinking or chain-of-thought reasoning to work through complex problems before providing an answer.
What input modalities does Hy3 support?
Hy3 supports text input.
What output modalities does Hy3 support?
Hy3 supports text output.
Can Hy3 process images?
No, Hy3 does not support image input. It can only process text.
Is Hy3 multimodal?
No, Hy3 is not multimodal. It only supports text input.
What is the context window of Hy3?
Hy3 has a context window of 260k tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.
Is Hy3 open source?
Yes, Hy3 is open weights. The model weights are publicly available and can be downloaded for self-hosting.
How many parameters does Hy3 have?
Hy3 has 299 billion parameters (21 billion active).
What are the active parameters of Hy3?
Hy3 is a Mixture of Experts (MoE) model with 299 billion total parameters, but only 21 billion active parameters are used during inference.
What is the license for Hy3?
Hy3 is released under the Apache 2.0 license. Commercial use is allowed with restrictions. View license
How does Hy3 perform on benchmarks?
Hy3 achieves a score of 25 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This composite benchmark evaluates models across reasoning, knowledge, mathematics, and coding.
