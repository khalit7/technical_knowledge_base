Compare labs on the same axes

What it shows: every lab on this page in one row, measured on the same eight axes: the constraint it optimises, its headline release, how open the weights are, size, architecture, price, the one independent index score where one exists, and who measured the headline claims. Try: filter to one constraint, or to closed models only; sort by active parameters or by the index; click a row for the one-line reason to track it and a link to its section.

Weights: fully open means weights, training data and code; permissive MIT or Apache 2.0; restricted a custom or non-commercial licence. Evidence: vendor only means every headline number comes from the lab or its launch partners; independent means a third party (here, Artificial Analysis) measured the model; mixed means some of each. AA is the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3 (methodology v4.3.2), read on 1 October 2026 ({{leaderboard|https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models}}); a dot means the model is not on it. Prices are list prices for the lab's own API.

Scale and sparsity

What it shows: every model on this page with published parameter counts. Over time puts release date against total parameters, dot area set by active parameters: Xiaomi and Tencent were releasing open models at this scale long before their current lines, and September 2026 (shaded) is crowded. Total against active puts each model on the sparsity lines: dense models sit on the diagonal, and Hy4's 16× sits well inside Step 5's 22× and DeepSeek V4 Pro's 33×.
