Multimodal LLM Architectures

A different axis of LLM design: how deeply other modalities are integrated. Shallow (tools translate audio or images to text, losing whatever the converter discards), modular (a frozen encoder plus a small trained projector into the token embedding space, the LLaVA and BLIP pattern) or unified (trained jointly end to end, as in Gemini, GPT-4o and GPT-5, Qwen-VL, Kimi K3 and MiniMax M3), the most expensive route and the only one to genuinely cross-modal reasoning. (6 min)

Encoder-Decoder Gemma and T5Gemma 2 (2025 to 2026)

The encoder-decoder case for compressing long context, on serving grounds. (9 min read)

GPT-3: Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (2020)

Scaling a dense transformer makes task adaptation happen in the prompt, with no gradient update. (10 min read)

Switch Transformers (2021)

One expert per token: parameter count can grow without per-token compute, and the load-balancing problems every later MoE inherits. (10 min read)

Mixtral of Experts (2024)

The first widely deployed open MoE: 8 experts with 2 active, about 47B total and 13B active. (9 min read)

The Llama 3 Herd of Models (2024)

Dense 8B, 70B and (in Llama 3.1) 405B models, the small ones trained far past compute-optimal on purpose. (15 min read)

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report (2024)

The template the open frontier follows: 671B / 37B, compressed attention cache, aux-loss-free balancing, FP8 training. (12 min read)

DeepSeek-R1 (2025)

Qwen3 Technical Report (2025)

One checkpoint with a fast mode and a thinking mode under a caller-set budget, across a dense and MoE ladder. (12 min read)

Mamba (2023)
