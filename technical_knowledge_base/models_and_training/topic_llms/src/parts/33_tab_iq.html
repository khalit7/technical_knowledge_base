Capability against price

Each point is a model on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3 (a composite of many evaluations), against what it costs. The dashed staircase is the efficient frontier: the models no other model beats on both axes at once. Anything below and to the right of it buys less capability for more money than something on it.

Try: switch the horizontal axis from cost per task to price per token and watch the frontier change. Price per token rewards cheap tokens; cost per task also counts how many tokens a model spends thinking, the point of the Reading tab's section on cost per task. Then tick show effort settings: the same model at higher effort climbs and moves right, buying capability with tokens.

How to read it

  • One index version only. Every value is v4.3, read on 1 October 2026. Figures published before the September rescaling, such as launch-day scores of 57 to 62, are on an earlier scale and cannot be placed on this chart.
  • Cheap tokens can mean an expensive task. Claude Sonnet 5.5 at max effort costs half as much per token as Opus 5.5 but more per index task ($7.62 against $5.98), because it spends more tokens.
  • Open weights sit on the cheap end of the frontier. MiMo-V2.6-Pro reaches 46.3 for about $0.13 a task; the closed leaders reach the mid-fifties for several dollars.
  • A composite hides the spread. The Benchmarks tab shows how differently the same models rank task by task.
