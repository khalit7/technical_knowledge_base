How to read it

  • Who ran it comes first. Nine columns are Artificial Analysis's own runs: eight of the ten evaluations inside its Intelligence Index v4.3 (the index itself is the grey first column; SciCode and GDP.pdf are left out to keep the grid readable) and Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1, which is outside the index; FrontierMath is run by Epoch AI, ARC-AGI-2 by the ARC Prize Foundation, DeepSWE by its maker Datacurve in one shared harness, and LMArena ranks blind user votes. Every value was read on 1 October 2026.
  • Colour is relative to the column. Each column is shaded from its lowest to its highest score across all 42 rows, so a strongly coloured cell means "near the top on this test", not a high absolute number. GDPval-AA, AA-Briefcase and LMArena are Elo ratings; AA-Omniscience runs from -100 to 100 and punishes wrong answers more than abstaining.
  • Labs score themselves higher. Where a lab's figure and an independent one exist for the same version, the lab's is usually higher, often because of a different harness or effort: Opus 5.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 is 66.4% in Anthropic's table and 59.6% in Artificial Analysis's run; Grok 4.7 is 38.0% from xAI and 25.8% from AA. The dot marks these cells; the detail shows both. Hatched cells are not on an independent footing: on DeepSWE the two highest cells, Gemini 4 Argon at 77.9% and Opus 5.5 at 74.2%, are both from Google's own table, while the best independent run is Gemini 3.8 Flash at 73.8%.
  • Versions never mix. DeepSWE is v1.1 only, FrontierMath is the v2 private set, Terminal-Bench is 4.0 (not 2.1). The labs' AutomationBench percentages are a different score from AutomationBench-AA, so they appear only in the detail, not on the AA scale.
  • Effort is part of the model. As on the Capability vs price tab, each model shows its highest benchmarked effort; tick show effort settings for the extra rows Artificial Analysis lists (Opus 5.5 at xhigh and medium, Sonnet 5.5 at medium, Muse Spark 1.3 at xhigh). An asterisk means the independent tester ran a different effort or checkpoint from the row's.
  • Preference is not correctness. LMArena measures which answer people prefer on everyday prompts; it can disagree with the other columns: Muse Spark 1.3 at max effort is fourth on LMArena (1495) but tenth of the 42 rows on the index, and Gemini 4 Argon leads LMArena (1525) while sitting sixth on the index.
