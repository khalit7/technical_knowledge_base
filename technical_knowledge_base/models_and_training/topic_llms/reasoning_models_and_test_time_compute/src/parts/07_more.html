{{Multimodal LLM Architectures|n:3c65c17b0d0d8139bd6cd4137dd2a4d8}} (~6 min)

A different axis of LLM design: how deeply other modalities are integrated. Shallow (tools translate audio or images to text, losing whatever the converter discards), modular (a frozen encoder plus a small trained projector into the token embedding space, the LLaVA and BLIP pattern) or unified (trained jointly end to end, as in Gemini, GPT-4o and GPT-5, Qwen-VL, Kimi K3 and MiniMax M3), the most expensive route and the only one to genuinely cross-modal reasoning. The component anatomy and tokenisation trade-offs are there.
