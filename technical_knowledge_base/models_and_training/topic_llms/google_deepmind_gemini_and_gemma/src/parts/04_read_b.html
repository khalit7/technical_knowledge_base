The 3.x Flash, Flash-Lite and 3.1 Pro models take text, images, video, audio and PDF in a 1,048,576-token window and return up to 65,536 tokens. Cached input is a tenth of the input price on the Flash models ($0.075 on 3.8 Flash). The 2.5 models are still served but, since 18 September 2026, only to projects that already used them; new projects are pointed to 3.5 Flash-Lite or 3.8 Flash ({{release notes|@log}}). How the Flash list price moved generation by generation is charted on the Cost per task tab.

How thinking is controlled

Gemini models think dynamically by default: the model decides per request how much reasoning a prompt needs. The caller steers it with one parameter, thinking_level, in the generation config: minimal, low, medium or high. Google's guidance is minimal or low for fact retrieval and classification, the default for comparisons and creative reasoning, and high for advanced coding, mathematics and multi-step planning ({{thinking guide|@think}}). Four details are Google-specific and are where code breaks:

  • Levels and defaults vary by model, so a request written for one model is not portable to the next. Try it below.
  • The token budget is gone. The 2.5 models were steered by thinking_budget, a token count. The current guide describes only thinking_level, including for 2.5 Pro, 2.5 Flash and 2.5 Flash-Lite (the last ships with thinking off by default). Code that still sends a budget was written against an API that has moved.
  • You pay for thinking you do not see. Thinking tokens are billed as output, and the bill counts every thought token generated even when only a summary returns: "Pricing is based on the full thought tokens the model needs to generate". Summaries are controlled by thinking_summaries; by default only the final answer returns.
  • Thought signatures must go back. Each response carries encrypted thought signatures, a representation of the model's reasoning state, present even after minimal reasoning. If you manage history yourself, every thought block must be resent exactly as received, or multi-turn tool use loses the reasoning that led to the call. With server-side state (previous_interaction_id) Google handles them.

Try: pick the level your code sends. Models that reject it are struck out; the ringed cell is each model's default. minimal fails on 3.8 Flash, 3.7 Flash and 3.1 Pro.

From the per-model table in the {{thinking guide|@think}}. The guide lists the 2.5 Pro and 2.5 Flash defaults only as "on", with no level, and still lists gemini-3-pro-preview, which was shut down on 9 March 2026.

The two outer designs this sits between (a caller-set token budget, and a hidden router that decides for you) and the research behind them are on {{Reasoning models and test-time compute|n:3c65c17b0d0d8190b9b4cd0acbcb66df}}; Anthropic's equivalent, adaptive thinking with an effort setting, is on {{Anthropic: Claude family|n:3c65c17b0d0d814cb979d0eabca59a45}}.

How media is counted against the window

Native multimodality makes media a token cost like any other, so "will this fit, and what will it cost" is arithmetic. On the Gemini 3 models a video frame costs 70 tokens at the default, low and medium media resolution and 280 at high; frames are sampled at 1 per second; audio costs 25 tokens per second at every resolution; an image costs 1,120 tokens at the default (280 low, 560 medium, 2,240 ultra high, set per item); a PDF page 560 at the default ({{media resolution|@media}}). For a video with its soundtrack:

Try: the defaults are the worked example, one hour on 3.8 Flash. Switch to high resolution and the hour overflows the window before you have typed a question; switch the rates to Gemini 2.5's to see the report's "1 hour versus 3 hours". Add images or PDF pages to fill the rest of the window.

Window 1,048,576 tokens for every 3.x model ({{models|@models}}). Prices from {{pricing|@pricing}}; 3.1 Pro's rate for the whole prompt doubles above 200K tokens. The 2.5 rates: 258 tokens per frame by default, 66 at low ({{Gemini 2.5 report|@g25}}), audio 32 tokens per second (the generic {{tokens page|@tokens}}, which also still quotes 263 video tokens per second for older models).

Worked example: one hour on 3.8 Flash. Default resolution: 3,600 × (1 × 70 + 25) = 3,600 × 95 = 342,000 tokens, 32.6% of the window, costing 342,000 / 106 × $0.75 = $0.2565 (about 51 cents from 2027). At high: 3,600 × 305 = 1,098,000 tokens, over the window by 49,424. The longest video that fits is 1,048,576 / 95 ≈ 11,038 seconds (3 h 4 min) at default and 1,048,576 / 305 ≈ 3,438 seconds (57 min) at high. So "an hour of video" is a third of the window at the default and does not fit at high. The 2.5 report made the same point with its own figures: dropping from 258 to 66 tokens per frame took the window from about 1 hour of video to about 3 ({{Gemini 2.5 report|@g25}}).

Agentic video processing
On 1 September 2026 Google added agentic video understanding for 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite ({{release notes|@log}}): instead of every frame at 1 per second, the model loads only the transcript, frames or audio it needs, so "a 1-hour lecture that would use ~1.08M tokens in static mode might use ~108K tokens", depending on prompt and content ({{tokens page|@tokens}}). The formula above is the static case, and the ceiling.
