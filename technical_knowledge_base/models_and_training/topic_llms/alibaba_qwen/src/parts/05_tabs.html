Configs and cache

Eight Qwen checkpoints, recomputed from their own config.json. For each one: the memory one request needs at a chosen context, and where the parameters live. Every figure on the Reading tab's MoE and attention sections comes from this table.

Try: start at 262,144 tokens, the native context of every model since Next.

  • Qwen3-235B needs 47 GiB of cache for one request, and Qwen3-32B, a dense model a seventh the size, needs 64 GiB.
  • Every hybrid needs under 25 GiB including its DeltaNet states. The 2.4T flagship needs 23.3 GiB, half of Qwen3-235B's cache at ten times the parameters.
  • Slide the context down to see where each state overtakes its cache, then switch the state to 32-bit.

The formulas are the Reading tab's:

  • KV bytes per token = Lfull × 2 × Hkv × dh × 2 (16-bit cache).
  • DeltaNet state = Llinear × Hv × dk × dv numbers.
  • Expert parameters = L × E × 3dmodeldff, where E counts the shared expert when there is one.

The defaults reproduce each headline independently from the configs:

  • Experts are 97 to 99% of every MoE's headline total; the rest is attention, DeltaNet, embeddings and norms.
  • Flash-Next's 121.0B of experts sits inside its 125B, which excludes the 51B n-gram table and 4B of MTP.

Two limits on the figures:

  • The 32-bit state is an assumption from the config field mamba_ssm_dtype: float32 (Qwen3.5 and 3.8 only); how serving engines store it varies.
  • Flash-Next's QSA layers also keep indexer keys, which are not counted here.

Sources: {{Qwen3-32B|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-32B/blob/main/config.json}}, {{Qwen3-30B-A3B|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B/blob/main/config.json}}, {{Qwen3-235B-A22B|@c235}}, {{Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B|@nxcfg}}, {{Qwen3.5-397B-A17B|@c35cfg}}, {{Qwen3.8-27B|@c27cfg}}, {{Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B|@c24cfg}}, {{Qwen3.8-Flash-Next|@fncfg}}. 1M means 1,000,000 tokens; GiB is 230 bytes.

Lineage

Dates, sizes, licences and what changed at each release; the mechanisms are explained on the Reading tab. Filled dots are open weights and rings are closed, hosted models. Colour is the licence; dot size follows total parameters where published.

Try: click a dot to jump to its release. Watch the colours: green (Apache 2.0) takes over from Qwen3 on, then the flagship and preview tiers turn to custom licences in August 2026.

Dates are announcement dates where a dated announcement was found. Otherwise they are the date the Hugging Face repository was created, which can precede the public release by a few days.

Qwen (1.0)

Qwen-7B August 2023 (repository 3 August); Qwen-72B November 2023 (repository 26 November) · Tongyi Qianwen License
  • The first open-weight Qwen models, base and chat ({{GitHub|https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen}}).
  • The licence asks a product above 100 million monthly active users to request a licence, and forbids using the model or its outputs to improve any other large language model ({{licence|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-72B/blob/main/LICENSE}}).

Qwen1.5

February 2024 (repositories 30 January); Qwen1.5-MoE-A2.7B 28 March 2024 · Tongyi Qianwen License
  • The dense ladder again, plus the first Qwen MoE: 14.3B total and 2.7B active, matching 7B dense models at lower cost ({{Qwen blog|https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen-moe/}}).

Qwen2

June 2024 (repositories 28 May to 4 June) · Tongyi Qianwen at 72B, Apache 2.0 at 7B and below
  • The first generation with Apache 2.0 below the flagship size: Qwen2-7B-Instruct is Apache 2.0, Qwen2-72B-Instruct Tongyi Qianwen ({{7B|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2-7B-Instruct}}, {{72B|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2-72B-Instruct}}).

Qwen2.5, Qwen2.5-Coder and QwQ

Qwen2.5 19 September 2024; Coder-32B November 2024; QwQ-32B-Preview 28 November 2024; QwQ-32B March 2025
  • A dense family from 0.5B to 72B trained on 18T tokens ({{Qwen blog|https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen2.5/}}), plus Coder, VL, Audio and Math variants.
  • Most sizes are Apache 2.0. The 72B is under the Qwen License, with the 100-million-user clause and a "Built with Qwen" notice for models trained on it. The 3B is research only ({{72B licence|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct/blob/main/LICENSE}}, {{3B licence|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct/blob/main/LICENSE}}).
  • Qwen2.5-72B and Qwen2.5-Coder-32B (Apache 2.0) became default open bases.
  • QwQ-32B was the first Qwen reasoning model. It was notable for showing that a 32B model could reach reasoning behaviour previously seen only in far larger ones ({{QwQ-32B-Preview|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/QwQ-32B-Preview}}). The March 2025 QwQ-32B is Apache 2.0.

Qwen3

29 April 2025 ({{Qwen blog|https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen3/}}) · {{Qwen3 report|@q3r}} · Apache 2.0
  • Dense 0.6B, 1.7B, 4B, 8B, 14B and 32B; MoE 30B-A3B and 235B-A22B (128 experts, 8 active, no shared expert).
  • Single checkpoints with think and no-think modes and thinking budgets; 36T-token pretraining in 119 languages.
  • From here on every open Qwen language checkpoint until Qwen3.8's flagship is Apache 2.0.

Qwen3-2507 and Qwen3-Coder

21 July to 5 August 2025 · Apache 2.0
  • The 2507 updates (the suffix is the date) split Instruct and Thinking checkpoints at 235B-A22B, 30B-A3B and 4B ({{235B Instruct|@i2507}}, {{235B Thinking|@t2507}}).
  • Qwen3-Coder-480B-A35B, released 22 July, routes 8 of 160 experts over 62 layers, with 256K native context ({{MarkTechPost|@mtpcoder}}, {{config|@coderc}}).

Qwen3-Next

September 2025 (repositories 9 September) · {{blog|@nxb}} · Apache 2.0
  • 80B-A3B, the efficiency testbed:
    • the 3:1 Gated DeltaNet and gated-attention hybrid;
    • 512 experts, 10 routed plus 1 shared;
    • multi-token prediction;
    • 15T training tokens.

Qwen3-Max and Qwen3-VL

Preview 6 September 2025, release 24 September 2025 · closed
  • More than 1T parameters, closed: the first Qwen flagship held back from open weights ({{MarkTechPost|@mtp3max}}).
  • Qwen3-VL-235B (Instruct and Thinking, 23 September 2025) carried open vision ({{Presenc|@pres}}).

Qwen3.5

16 February 2026 ({{CNBC|https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/17/china-alibaba-qwen-ai-agent-latest-model.html}}) · {{card|@c35}} · Apache 2.0
  • Qwen3.5-397B-A17B, open weights: the Next layout at flagship scale (60 layers, 512 experts), with a vision encoder and 201 languages, aimed at the "agentic AI era". It was benchmarked on par with US frontier models of the moment.
  • Its hosted twin is Qwen3.5-Plus.

Qwen3.6

April 2026 · Apache 2.0 at 35B-A3B and 27B; closed Plus and Max preview
  • Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (16 April) was the first open-weight model of the generation, a sparse multimodal MoE with 3B active, under Apache 2.0 ({{MarkTechPost|@mtp36}}). Qwen3.6-27B followed (repository 21 April, Apache 2.0; {{card|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B}}).
  • Qwen3.6-Plus (proprietary) and a Qwen3.6-Max Preview kept the top tier closed ({{Presenc|@pres}}).

Qwen3.7

Qwen3.7-Max 20 May 2026; Qwen3.7-Plus 2 June 2026 · closed
  • Qwen3.7-Max was announced at the Alibaba Cloud Summit: closed-weight and text-only, with a 1M-token context ({{MarkTechPost|@mtp37max}}).
  • Qwen3.7-Plus is multimodal and served on Alibaba's Bailian platform (Model Studio internationally) ({{MarkTechPost|@mtp37plus}}). The Flash-Next paper's Table 11 gives it 397B parameters with 17B activated; it is the "397B-A17B predecessor" Flash-Next is measured against ({{paper|@fnp}}).

Qwen3.8-Max and the open 2.4T-A95B

Preview 19 July 2026; launch 3 August 2026 ({{Alibaba|@ali38}}); open weights 12 August 2026 · Qwen3.8-Max License (open) / closed (hosted)
  • A 2.4T-parameter sparse MoE with 95B active and hybrid attention, multimodal, with a 1M-token context. At launch it was fifth on Text Arena, second on Vision Arena and fourth on Frontend Code Arena ({{Alibaba|@ali38}}).
  • On 12 August Alibaba open-weighted Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B, the first open Max-class flagship. That was a strategy change: the Max tier had been the one thing kept closed. The open release is text-only and thinking-only ({{card|@c24}}).

Qwen3.8-27B

14 August 2026 ({{Willison|@sw}}) · {{card|@c27}} · Apache 2.0
  • Dense feed-forward layers with the 3:1 hybrid attention (64 layers, 48 of them Gated DeltaNet), native image and video understanding, and 262K context extensible to 1M.
  • Simon Willison found it excellent but prone to "wildly overthinking" at its default effort ({{Willison|@sw}}). XDA handed it a reverse-engineering job it finished in about 30 minutes ({{XDA|@xda}}).

Qwen3.8-Flash-Next

26 August 2026 ({{TechNode|@tnfn}}) · {{card|@fn}} · {{paper|@fnp}} · Qwen Community License 1.0
  • An open-weight preview of the Qwen4 architecture, released early so developers can prepare for the full Qwen4 family:
    • 125B total and 6B active, a causal LM plus a vision encoder;
    • 262,144-token native context, extensible to 1M;
    • Gated DeltaNet plus QSA.

Qwen3.8-Max-0902

2 September 2026 ({{TechNode|@tn0902}}) · closed
  • A post-trained snapshot of the 2.4T flagship rather than a new version. It keeps the 1M-token context and was further post-trained on coding and Cowork-style agentic tasks. Its Frontend Code Arena score rose 22 points to 1,691, first on that leaderboard at release.
  • The convention matters more than the model. Alibaba now ships dated snapshots instead of incrementing version numbers, so "Qwen3.8-Max" is no longer a single artifact, and any reproducible result must pin the snapshot id.

Qwen-Image-2.1

September 2026 (repository 14 September; licence dated 20 September) · Qwen Research License
  • The open image line moved from Apache 2.0 (Qwen-Image) to research-only ({{licence|@img21}}). Detail is on {{Topic: generative-and-multimodal|n:3c65c17b0d0d817ab6ade318917bff55}}.
