Mozilla has partnered with AI company Mistral AI to expand Firefox's AI capabilities.
Mozilla, the developer of Firefox, has entered into a partnership with Mistral AI, a French-based AI development company. As a result of this partnership, Firefox's AI feature, '
Smart Window,
' will incorporate Mistral AI's '
Mistral Small 4
' model.

Firefox
has demonstrated
a proactive stance in developing AI features, and has already implemented features such as '
translation using local AI
' and '
a chat AI that displays in the sidebar to perform conversations and summaries
.'
In addition, development is underway on 'Smart Window,' an AI-focused window that will be added to the regular and private windows. Smart Window offers features such as 'analyzing tab content and automatically grouping it,' 'chatting with AI,' and 'asking AI questions to perform web searches,' and testing has begun for users in the United States, Canada, and France.
This partnership allows users to select Mistral AI's 'Mistral Small 4' as an AI model available for use in Smart Window. Mozilla explained that they chose Mistral Small 4 because of its 'multilingual support capabilities in the beta version of Smart Window.'
In addition, Mozilla has added French as a supported language for Smart Window and plans further expansion in Europe during 2026.
Mistral Small 4 is developed as an open model, and the model is distributed at the following link. The license is the Apache License 2.0.
