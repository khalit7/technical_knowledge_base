Best resources

{{MiniMax-01 paper|@p01}} (1h 30m, long tech report)

Lightning (linear) attention at 456B scale, the 7:1 hybrid and its small-scale retrieval comparisons: the family's founding architecture bet.

{{MiniMax-M1 paper|@pm1}} (45 min)

Hybrid-attention reasoning, CISPO and its comparison with DAPO and GRPO, the FP32 LM-head fix and the $534,700 RL bill.

{{MiniMax Sparse Attention paper|@pmsa}} (40 min)

The MSA index branch, its KL training, the KV-outer kernel, the FLOP model of eq. 12, and ablations against full attention and sliding windows.

{{Why did MiniMax M2 end up as a full attention model?|@full}} (15 min)

The reversal in MiniMax's own words: the evaluation gap, the circuits that form early, and the missing infrastructure.

{{The MiniMax-M2 series report|@pm2}} (1h)

M2 to M2.7, the sliding-window ablation tables charted in M2's reversal, MTP, CISPO in agentic RL and the Forge RL system.

{{MSA kernel code on GitHub|@msagh}} (repo, ~15 min for the README)

The open-source KV-outer sparse attention kernels behind M3.

{{MiniMax M3 launch coverage (Fireworks)|@fw}} (~10 min)

Launch-partner write-up of M3 and its kernel speed claims.
