Inspect an open run: Olmo 3's training log and data mix

What this is: the raw material a fully open release gives you (Ai2 OLMo, artefacts 2 and 5), drawn from Ai2's public logs and published mixes. Try: switch on the open-weights view to see what a typical release shows of the same run; then compare the data-mix stages.

The whole pretraining run, as the logs record it

Ai2 publishes the raw telemetry of its runs on Weights & Biases ({{ai2-llm|https://wandb.ai/ai2-llm}}): every launch of the job, every public checkpoint, the training loss and the quick evaluations Ai2 ran inside the training loop, for Olmo 3 32B and 7B.

Sources: W&B projects {{Olmo-3-1125-32B|https://wandb.ai/ai2-llm/Olmo-3-1125-32B}} (69 runs) and {{Olmo-3-1025-7B|https://wandb.ai/ai2-llm/Olmo-3-1025-7B}} (39 runs), sampled history read through the public API on 1 October 2026; checkpoints from the Hugging Face refs API for {{32B|https://huggingface.co/api/models/allenai/Olmo-3-1125-32B/refs}} and {{7B|https://huggingface.co/api/models/allenai/Olmo-3-1025-7B/refs}}. Each W&B run is one launch of the job; a new launch resumes from the last saved checkpoint, so some steps are trained twice. The logs do not label why a run stopped. The loss curve is the median per bin of W&B's sampled points, so single-step spikes may not show. In-loop evaluations are Ai2's fast 5-shot versions (MMLU averaged over its four groups), not the report's final scores. Run names in the logs are internal code names (stego32, olmo25_7b).

After pretraining

The data mix, stage by stage

The mixture is the part labs guard hardest and the part Ai2 publishes in full. Each stage draws a mix from a larger pool: a source sampled at more than 1x is repeated or upweighted, one below 1x is thinned. Compare the stages to see the curriculum: broad web first, then a small high-quality and synthetic mix while the learning rate decays, then long documents.
