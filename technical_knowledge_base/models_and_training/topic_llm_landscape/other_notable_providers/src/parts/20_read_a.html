Total, active, sparsity
A mixture-of-experts (MoE) model stores every expert but runs a few per token. Total parameters set the memory, active parameters the compute per token; total ÷ active is the sparsity.
Fully open, open weights
Fully open: weights, training data and code. Open weights: the final weights under MIT or Apache 2.0, or under a custom or non-commercial licence. Otherwise API only or closed.
Hybrid
Mostly state-space (Mamba-2) or convolution blocks with a few attention layers kept for exact recall.
Cost per task
What Artificial Analysis (AA) measures one task on its Intelligence Index costing: list price times the tokens a model actually spends.
Who measured it
Vendor only, independent (here, Artificial Analysis) or mixed. Most numbers on this page are the lab's own and are labelled so.
