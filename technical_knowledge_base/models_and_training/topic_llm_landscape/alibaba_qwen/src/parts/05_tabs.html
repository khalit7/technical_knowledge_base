Configs

Eight Qwen checkpoints, recomputed from their own config.json: where the parameters live, stored and active. Every expert figure on the Reading tab's MoE section comes from this table.

The cache, in one line. Only Qwen's full-attention layers keep a cache that grows, so the 3:1 hybrids stay under 25 GiB per request at 262,144 tokens including their DeltaNet states: Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B needs 23.3 GiB, half of Qwen3-235B's 47 GiB at ten times the parameters (16-bit state). All eight checkpoints are presets in the {{KV-cache calculator|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81059ee1ce20c0326dc5#t-kv}}, with the context slider and the 32-bit state toggle. Qwen3 models support 128K context (with YaRN).

Expert parameters = L × E × 3dmodeldff, where E counts the shared expert when there is one. The defaults reproduce each headline independently from the configs:

  • Experts are 97 to 99% of every MoE's headline total; the rest is attention, DeltaNet, embeddings and norms.
  • Flash-Next's 121.0B of experts sits inside its 125B, which excludes the 51B n-gram table and 4B of MTP.
