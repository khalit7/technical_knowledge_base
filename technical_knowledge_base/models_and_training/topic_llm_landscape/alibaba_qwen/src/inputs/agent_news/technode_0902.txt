 Alibaba upgrades Qwen3.8-Max with a new 0902 snapshot

 Credit: Qwen
 Alibaba has released Qwen3.8-Max-0902, an upgraded snapshot of its Qwen3.8-Max foundation model. The update was post-trained for coding and Cowork-style tasks and is available through Alibaba’s Qwen services and API channels.
Alibaba said the model’s front-end CodeArena score rose by 22 points to 1,691, placing it first on the leaderboard. The model retains a context window of up to 1 million tokens; its official model page is available through QwenCloud. [TechNode reporting]
