'''SpaceXAI LLC''' (formerly '''xAI''') was an American [[artificial intelligence]] company founded by [[Elon Musk]].<ref name="OpenCorporates">{{Cite web |title=X.AI Corp. |url=https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_nv/E28915122023-8 |access-date=2023-07-27 |website=[[OpenCorporates]] |archive-date=August 5, 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230805051958/https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_nv/E28915122023-8 |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name=":1" /><ref name=":3" /> In February 2026 it became a subsidiary of [[Space industry|spaceflight]] company [[SpaceX]], also founded by Musk. 

xAI was founded by Musk and 11 researchers including [[Kyle Kosic]] in March 2023, at the outset of the [[AI boom]];<ref name="OpenCorporates" /><ref name=":1" /><ref name="Fernholz-2026">{{Cite web |last=Fernholz |first=Tim |date=March 13, 2026 |title='Not built right the first time' — Musk's xAI is starting over again, again |url=https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/not-built-right-the-first-time-musks-xai-is-starting-over-again-again/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260314001659/https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/not-built-right-the-first-time-musks-xai-is-starting-over-again-again/ |archive-date=March 14, 2026 |access-date=March 14, 2026 |website=TechCrunch}}</ref><ref name="Bueno Rebolledo-2026">{{Cite news |last1= Bueno Rebolledo |first1= Natalia |last2= Thomas |first2= Chris |date=February 10, 2026 |title=Two co-founders of Elon Musk's xAI resign, joining exodus |url=https://www.reuters.com/business/two-co-founders-elon-musks-xai-resign-joining-exodus-2026-02-11/ |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260211173928/https://www.reuters.com/business/two-co-founders-elon-musks-xai-resign-joining-exodus-2026-02-11/ |url-status=live |archive-date=February 11, 2026 |access-date=March 14, 2026 |work=Reuters}}</ref> Grok 1 was released that November. In March 2025, it was combined with [[X Corp.]], which owns the social network [[X (social network)|X]], under '''X.AI Holdings Corp'''.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Bensinger |first=Greg |date=March 28, 2025 |title=Musk's social media firm X bought by his AI company, valued at $33 billion |url=https://www.reuters.com/markets/deals/musks-xai-buys-social-media-platform-x-45-billion-2025-03-28/ |website=Reuters}}</ref> In February 2026, xAI was acquired by SpaceX at a valuation of US$250 billion, and rebranded as SpaceXAI in July. That August, SpaceXAI acquired the [[AI coding assistant|AI coding]] company [[Cursor (company)|Cursor]].<ref name="8K-Close">{{cite web |title=Form 8-K |url=https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0001181412/e0cb26ac-2a22-4159-a32c-6f1900299e2d.pdf |publisher=U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission |date=August 14, 2026 |access-date=August 14, 2026}}</ref><ref name="Bloomberg-Close">{{cite news |title=SpaceX Completes Its $60 Billion Cursor Acquisition |url=https://news.bloomberglaw.com/mergers-and-acquisitions/spacex-completes-its-60-billion-cursor-acquisition |work=Bloomberg Law |date=August 14, 2026 |access-date=August 14, 2026}}</ref><ref name="Cursor-X">{{cite web |title=Cursor is now part of @SpaceX |url=https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2088249881718919393 |website=X (formerly Twitter) |author=Cursor |date=August 14, 2026 |access-date=August 14, 2026}}</ref>

SpaceXAI's primary product is the [[Grok (chatbot)|Grok]] family of [[Large language model|large language]], [[Text-to-image model|text-to-image]], and [[text-to-video]] models. It operates a [[data center]] business centered on its [[Colossus (data center)|Colossus]] cluster;<ref>{{Cite web |last=Kolodny |first=Lora |date=June 5, 2026 |title=Google to pay SpaceX $920 million a month for compute capacity at xAI data centers |url=https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/google-to-pay-spacex-920-million-a-month-for-xai-compute-capacity.html |access-date=2026-06-06 |website=CNBC }}</ref><ref name=":2" /> [[Anthropic]] and [[Google]] are major customers.<ref name=":4">{{cite web |last1=Howley|first1=Daniel|title=Anthropic to rent all AI capacity at SpaceX's Colossus data center|url=https://finance.yahoo.com/news/anthropic-to-rent-all-ai-capacity-at-spacexs-colossus-data-center-180327774.html|website=Yahoo Finance|access-date=May 6, 2026}}</ref><ref name=":4" /> In ''[[Musk v. Altman]]'', Musk stated Grok was partly developed via [[Knowledge distillation|distillation]] from [[OpenAI]] models; in August 2026 OpenAI announced it will stop providing its models to Cursor, citing distillation concerns. Integrated with X, Grok has [[Grok (chatbot)#Controversies and criticisms|drawn controversies]] including for [[Grok (chatbot)#July 8, 2025, hate speech and harassment|espousing Nazism]] and [[Grok sexual deepfake scandal|generating sexual deepfakes]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=March 20, 2026 |title=Grok Imagine Is Dead For Free Users (Jun 2026) |url=https://alloypress.com/news/grok-imagine-is-dead-for-free-users |access-date=2026-08-27 |language=en-US}}</ref>

== Environmental impact ==
{{See also|Environmental impact of AI}}
In June 2024, the [[Greater Memphis Chamber]] announced xAI was planning on building [[Colossus (supercomputer)|Colossus]], the world's largest supercomputer, in [[Memphis, Tennessee]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Musk to Locate xAI Supercomputer in Memphis |url=https://www.memphisflyer.com/musk-locates-xai-in-memphis |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240929080046/https://www.memphisflyer.com/musk-locates-xai-in-memphis |archive-date=September 29, 2024 |access-date=2024-06-06 |website=MemphisFlyer |date=June 5, 2024 }}</ref> After a 122-day construction period, the supercomputer went fully operational in December 2024. Local officials voiced concerns regarding the increased usage of electricity, 150 [[Megawatt|megawatts]] of power at peak, and while the agreement with the city was being worked out, the company deployed 14 VoltaGrid portable [[gas-fired power plant|methane-gas powered generators]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Edwards |first=Benj |date=July 24, 2024 |title=Elon Musk claims he is training "the world's most powerful AI by every metric" |url=https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2024/07/elon-musk-claims-he-is-training-the-worlds-most-powerful-ai-by-every-metric/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240725065546/https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2024/07/elon-musk-claims-he-is-training-the-worlds-most-powerful-ai-by-every-metric/ |archive-date=July 25, 2024 |access-date=2024-07-25 |website=Ars Technica |language=en-us}}</ref> 

Environmental advocates said that the gas-burning turbines emit large amounts of air pollution, and that xAI was operating the turbines illegally, because it did not have the necessary permits.<ref>{{Cite news |date=August 28, 2024 |title=Musk's xAI operating gas turbines without permits at data center, environmental group says |url=https://www.reuters.com/business/environment/musks-xai-operating-gas-turbines-without-permits-data-center-environmental-group-2024-08-28/ |work=Reuters}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Environmentalists urge health department to take action on xAI |url=https://dailymemphian.com/subscriber/article/45989/environmental-groups-urge-shelby-county-health-dept-action-xai-turbines-memphis |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241001222514/https://dailymemphian.com/subscriber/article/45989/environmental-groups-urge-shelby-county-health-dept-action-xai-turbines-memphis |archive-date=October 1, 2024 |access-date=2024-09-10 |website=Memphis Local, Sports, Business & Food News {{!}} Daily Memphian }}</ref> ''[[The New Yorker]]'' reported in May 2025, that [[Thermal imaging|thermal-imaging]] equipment used by volunteers flying over the site showed at least 33 generators producing heat, indicating that they were all running. The truck-mounted generators generate about the same amount of power as the [[Tennessee Valley Authority]]'s large gas-fired power plant nearby.<ref>{{cite magazine |last=McKibben |first=Bill |url=https://www.newyorker.com/news/the-lede/how-is-elon-musk-powering-his-supercomputer |title=How Is Elon Musk Powering His Supercomputer? |magazine=[[The New Yorker]] |date=May 6, 2025 |access-date=May 8, 2025 |archive-date=May 8, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250508001707/https://www.newyorker.com/news/the-lede/how-is-elon-musk-powering-his-supercomputer |url-status=live }}</ref> 

In July 2025, the Shelby County Health Department granted xAI an air permit for the project.<ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Taft |first=Molly |title=Despite Protests, Elon Musk Secures Air Permit for xAI |url=https://www.wired.com/story/xai-data-center-air-pollution-permit/ |access-date=2025-07-03 |magazine=[[Wired (magazine)|Wired]] |language=en-US |issn=1059-1028 |archive-date=July 3, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250703090702/https://www.wired.com/story/xai-data-center-air-pollution-permit/ |url-status=live }}</ref> In November 2025, xAI announced plans to build a solar farm near Colossus with an estimated output of 30 megawatts of electricity, which is 10% of the data center's estimated power use.<ref>{{Cite news |title=Musk's xAI to build small solar farm adjacent to Colossus data center |url=https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/26/musks-xai-to-build-small-solar-farm-adjacent-to-colossus-data-center/ |last=De Chant |first=Tim |date=November 26, 2025 |access-date=December 31, 2025 |work=[[TechCrunch]] |archive-date=December 8, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251208230647/https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/26/musks-xai-to-build-small-solar-farm-adjacent-to-colossus-data-center/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

The NAACP and other civil rights groups sued xAI in April 2026, alleging that the Colossus 2 data center was operating illegally because it did not have the necessary federal [[Clean Air Act (United States)|Clean Air Act]] permits.<ref>{{Cite news |date=April 15, 2026 |title=NAACP sues Musk's xAI, alleging illegal operation of gas turbines |url=https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/climate-energy/naacp-sues-musks-xai-alleging-illegal-operation-of-gas-turbines-2026-04-14/ |work=Reuters}}</ref> ''Reuters'' found that xAI's Colossus 2 data center had gas turbines with potential emissions that were "far beyond the threshold" that requires federal permits, in "predominantly Black communities already estimated to be suffering disproportionately high rates of lung disease".<ref>{{Cite news |last=Raychaudhuri |first=Disha |last2=Volcovici |first2=Valerie |date=July 14, 2026 |title=Pollution from Musk’s unpermitted xAI power project hits hardest in Black communities |url=https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/pollution-musks-unpermitted-xai-power-project-hits-hardest-black-communities-2026-07-14/?utm_medium=Social&utm_source=twitter |work=Reuters}}</ref>
