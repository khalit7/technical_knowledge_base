Training log

Ai2 publishes the raw telemetry of its runs on Weights & Biases ({{ai2-llm|@wbent}}). This is the whole pretraining run of Olmo 3 32B and 7B as the logs record it: every launch of the job, every public checkpoint, the training loss and the quick evaluations Ai2 ran inside the training loop. Switch on the open-weights view to see what a typical release shows of the same run.

Sources: W&B projects {{Olmo-3-1125-32B|@wb32}} (69 runs) and {{Olmo-3-1025-7B|@wb7}} (39 runs), sampled history read through the public API on 1 October 2026; checkpoints from the Hugging Face refs API for {{32B|@hf32ref}} and {{7B|@hf7ref}}. Each W&B run is one launch of the job; a new launch resumes from the last saved checkpoint, so some steps are trained twice. The logs do not label why a run stopped. The loss curve is the median per bin of W&B's sampled points, so single-step spikes may not show. In-loop evaluations are Ai2's fast 5-shot versions (MMLU averaged over its four groups), not the report's final scores. Run names in the logs are internal code names (stego32, olmo25_7b).

After pretraining

Before and after the OLMo 2 stability fixes

OLMo 1.7 7B (April 2024) against OLMo 2 7B (November 2024), from the same W&B entity: gradient norm and loss across each pretraining run. The fixes (reordered norm, QK-norm, z-loss, initialisation and others, see OLMo 2) were designed to remove exactly the spikes the first run shows.

Projects {{OLMo-7B|@wbo1}} (the OLMo 1.7 runs) and {{OLMo-2-1124-7B|@wbo2}}. Spike score as the {{OLMo 2 report|@o2r}} defines it (section 3.2), applied here to W&B's sampled points (about one per 19 steps for OLMo 1.7 and one per 62 for OLMo 2), not to every step. The x-axis is each run's own step count; both use 4,194,304-token batches. Reproduces the report's qualitative claim (frequent spikes in OLMo-0424, almost none after the fixes) independently from the logs; the report's own spike figure has no numbers to match.

Data mix

The mixture is the part labs guard hardest, and the part Ai2 publishes in full. Each stage draws a mix from a larger pool: a source sampled at more than 1x is repeated or upweighted, one below 1x is thinned. Compare the stages to see the curriculum: broad web first, then a small high-quality and synthetic mix while the learning rate decays, then long documents.
