The core idea. Every serious lab now ships the same skeleton, a sparse mixture-of-experts model that reasons before it answers and reads about a million tokens of context, so the labs differ on three axes only: where the thinking goes (and who controls it), what a token costs to serve, and how open the release is. On one independent scale the closed labs lead on capability and the open labs on price.

You need nothing beyond what an LLM is. How the models are built (attention, mixture of experts, the KV cache) is on {{Topic: llm-architecture|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8172b490dc9f9ac281df}}; this page compares the labs and links there for each mechanism.

New terms on this page (each defined again where it is used):
Intelligence Index: Artificial Analysis's independent composite of ten evaluations; versions do not compare.
Test-time compute: computation spent at inference before answering; a reasoning model is trained to use it.
Total / active parameters: what must sit in memory / what one token multiplies through (they differ in MoE).
Sparsity ratio: total ÷ active; 1 for a dense model.
Open weights / fully open: downloadable weights / weights plus data, code and recipe.
Harness: the code around a model when it is run (prompt, tools, retries, kept state).
Cost per task: the dollars a task really costs, thinking tokens and caching included.

1. Who leads, and on what

The best closed models (Claude Opus 5.5 at 58, Meta's Muse Wonder Full at 56, GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 4 Argon at 53) sit 7 to 12 points above the best open model, MiMo-V2.6-Pro at 46, and those with an API price cost 15 to 46 times as much per task. The open frontier is a near tie: MiMo at 46, DeepSeek V4.1-Flash-1017 and GLM-5.3 at 45, Kimi K3 and Step 5 Preview at 44, while Meta's new 816B Muse Spark Open scores 39. Epoch AI's Capabilities Index, which combines more than 50 benchmarks, has the same top three within two points: Opus 5.5 167, Astra 166, Argon 165 (an early estimate), with Claude Fable 5.1 at 165 too ({{Epoch AI|https://x.com/EpochAIResearch/status/2110693857742717372}}).
