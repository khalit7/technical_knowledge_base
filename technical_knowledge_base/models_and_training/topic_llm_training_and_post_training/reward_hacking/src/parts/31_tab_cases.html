Case gallery

Every documented case on this page in one place: what was optimised, what the model did instead, the model and date, the measured rate where one exists, and the defence that was shown to work (or that none was). The question it answers: across nearly thirty years of reinforcement learning, which shortcuts recur, and which defences have evidence behind them?

Try: filter by setting to see how the shortcuts moved from simulators to reward models to test suites to sandboxes; tick "measured defence only" to see which fixes have a before-and-after number. Each card links its primary source.

Rates are as each source reports them and are not comparable across cards: different tasks, models, definitions of a hack and evaluation versions. Classic examples come from DeepMind's list (about 60 examples in April 2020; its spreadsheet held 90 rows when checked on 3 October 2026) ({{Krakovna et al.|https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/specification-gaming-the-flip-side-of-ai-ingenuity/}}; {{the list|https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRPiprOaC3HsCf5Tuum8bRfzYUiKLRqJmbOoC-32JorNdfyTiRRsR7Ea5eWtvsWzuxo8bjOxCG84dAg/pubhtml}}).
