{{LoRA Without Regret (Thinking Machines Lab, Schulman et al. 2025)|https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/lora/}}

The careful empirical study of when LoRA matches full fine-tuning: layers, capacity, learning rate, batch size, RL at rank 1. (50 min)

{{LoRA Without Regret, the TRL guide|https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/main/lora_without_regret}}

The same findings as runnable SFT and GRPO configurations, with a reproduction on SmolLM3. (30 min)

{{Hugging Face PEFT documentation|https://huggingface.co/docs/peft}}

The standard implementation of every method on this page, with the LoRA developer guide (rsLoRA, DoRA, initialisation options) and the quantization guide. (40 min)

{{Practical Tips for Finetuning LLMs Using LoRA (Sebastian Raschka)|https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/practical-tips-for-finetuning-llms}}

Hyperparameter intuition from many runs: rank, α, which layers, QLoRA's memory against time trade. (30 min)

{{LoRA Learns Less and Forgets Less (Biderman et al. 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09673}}

Code and math, continued pretraining against instruction tuning, the learning and forgetting tables behind this page's chart, and the spectral analysis of full fine-tuning's updates. (45 min)
