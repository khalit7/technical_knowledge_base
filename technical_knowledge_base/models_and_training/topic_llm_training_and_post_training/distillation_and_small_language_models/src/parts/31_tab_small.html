Small models compared

Every open-weight model of at most 16B parameters that Artificial Analysis lists, placed by size and by Artificial Analysis's own runs of each benchmark, not the labs' numbers. Press Play to watch the best score at each size move up quarter by quarter.

Try: start with GPQA Diamond, the measure Artificial Analysis has run on almost every model; its floor is 25%, the score of guessing among four answers. Then switch to the Intelligence Index: most small models sit at its floor, and the ones that do not are recent. Ring colours mark how a model was made, where its makers say so; tap a point for details.

Reading this chart

  • Independent, but not complete. Every score here is Artificial Analysis's run, read on 3 October 2026 from the data behind its model pages. Models it has not evaluated (SmolLM3, OLMo 2 1B) are absent, and some evaluations were not run on older models.
  • Estimated index values. Artificial Analysis marks some Intelligence Index values "estimated"; in its data, every such model has an empty list of v4.3 evaluation results, so the value is not a run of the current suite. Those points are hollow. Most small models are estimated, and many estimates are exactly 4.8, which looks like a floor of the estimate rather than a measurement (measured values go lower: K2 Horizon 0.9B scored 3.0). Measured values are filled.
  • Sizes are total parameters as Artificial Analysis lists them, so Gemma 4 E2B counts its per-layer embeddings (5.1B) and the MoE entries count all experts.
