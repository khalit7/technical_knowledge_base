Source of the interactive HTML on the Notion page "Distillation and Small Language Models" (deep-dive child of Topic: llm-training-and-post-training).

Build: `python3 recompute.py` (writes `parts/20_js_data.js` and `inputs/recompute.txt`), then `sh build.sh` (writes `../index.html`; `rtime.py` fills the reading time). Checks: `sh html_utils/checkpage.sh <page folder>` and, from the repo root, `node <page folder>/src/check_page.mjs` (every control in light 920 px and dark 390 px with reduced motion, card screenshots in `.shots/`).

Data scripts (outputs committed in `inputs/`; big inputs and checkpoints never):
- `real_logits.py`: `HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 uv run --no-project --with torch --with transformers python real_logits.py` (needs Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct and openai-community/gpt2 in the Hugging Face cache) writes `inputs/real_logits.json`.
- `aa_small.py`: saves nothing from the web itself; first `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0" https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/qwen3-8b-instruct-reasoning -o <scratch>/aa.html`, then `python3 aa_small.py <scratch>/aa.html` writes `inputs/aa_small.json` (all open models of at most 16B parameters with Artificial Analysis's own fields).
- `toy/`: the toy experiment. `world.py` (task: routes in a fixed 32-node graph; exact data policy), `train.py` (teacher, then seven student methods x three seeds: `uv run --no-project --with torch --with numpy python train.py teacher`, then `train.py all 1`, `all 2`, `all 3` in parallel, two threads each, about 40 minutes), `mk_toy.py` (writes `inputs/toy.json`). Curves are logged to `toy/runs/*.json` (committed, small); checkpoints (`*.pt`) are not committed (rerun to regenerate; seeds fixed).
- `inputs/source_extracts.txt`: quotes behind every constant typed into `recompute.py`, with URLs. `live.md` is the old Notion page (fetched 2026-10-03, last edited 2026-09-22); `coverage.json` maps every fact in it.

Shape: built from the brief (no method file for deep-dive child pages), like the PEFT and Alignment siblings. Reading tab by question (four levers and three flavours, soft targets, forward or reverse KL, whose text, distil or RL, prune then distil, the small tier, whose terms, practical notes); two tabs (Distil a toy student, Small models compared); Further reading. Details owned by the parent (stage comparison, Open recipes) and siblings (Quantization, PEFT) are linked, not repeated.

Parts: `01_head.html`, `02_css.html`, `10_header.html`, `20_read_a/b/c.html` (Reading), `30_tab_toy.html`, `31_tab_small.html`, `39_tab_more.html`; JS `20_js_data.js` (generated), `21_js_common.js` (the parent's RD.anim controller, copied, plus PF helpers), `22_js_dk.js` (real-logit temperature view, predict boxes), `23_js_kl.js` (forward/reverse KL animation), `24_js_fl.js` (graph drawing, whose-text animation), `25_js_ts.js` (toy method names, Reading summary table), `26_js_rl.js` (R1 and Qwen3 bars), `27_js_bd.js` (token budgets, tokenizer table), `30_js_toy.js`, `31_js_small.js`, `99_js_tabs.js`.
