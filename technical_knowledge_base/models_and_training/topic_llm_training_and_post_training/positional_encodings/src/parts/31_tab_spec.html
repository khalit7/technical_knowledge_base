Stretch the spectrum

Extends Reading's Stretching RoPE section: the same methods, every pair at once, on the real configs. Each line shows how much a method slows each rotation pair (θ / θ′, log scale) against the pair's wavelength. A pair at ÷1 keeps its trained speed; a pair at ÷s is fully interpolated.

Try: on Llama 3.1, YaRN and Llama 3 scaling agree at both ends and differ only in where the blend sits (YaRN by turns per pair, from L/32; Llama 3 by wavelength, from L/4). NTK-aware is a straight line on this chart: the base change slows pair i by si/(d/2 − 1). Pick Phi-3: LongRoPE's searched factors rise unevenly, in jumps rather than a smooth curve, and reach ÷64.8 on its slowest pair, twice its factor of 32, while its "short" factors stay near ÷1 to ÷3. Llama 4's config sets low_freq_factor and high_freq_factor both to 1, which collapses the blend into a single step at 8,192 tokens.
