Key papers

Each links to its page in the Papers database, which checks the paper's claims; times are each page's own reading time.

{{Scaling Laws (Kaplan et al., 2020)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b08a1debb0c15cd252}} and {{Chinchilla (2022)|n:3c65c17b0d0d8116b7ddffba5c599f3f}}

Loss as a power law in parameters, data and compute; then the corrected allocation, about 20 tokens per parameter. (16 min and 20 min)

{{T5 (2019)|n:3d45c17b0d0d81ec8ebfec02e953a7fd}}

The controlled comparison of objectives, architectures, corpora and fine-tuning schemes; mostly negative results that still hold. (18 min)

{{ZeRO (2019)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81879a7ed90bca007699}} and {{Megatron-LM (2019)|n:3c65c17b0d0d8133ae92fbe90a1f308e}}

Sharded data parallelism and tensor parallelism. (19 min and 20 min)

{{LoRA (2021)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81018f15edd19fa5c28a}} and {{QLoRA (2023)|n:3c65c17b0d0d815a8f4edc33f9583671}}

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and fine-tuning on one GPU. (19 min and 29 min)

{{InstructGPT (2022)|n:3c65c17b0d0d8180b958d8299996a063}}, {{Constitutional AI (2022)|n:3c65c17b0d0d815a9b31e9443961c700}}, {{DPO (2023)|n:3c65c17b0d0d818bb1d8cafd30e20f9e}}, {{DeepSeekMath (2024)|n:3c65c17b0d0d817f9fc5cb9a9fbcbee5}}, {{DeepSeek-R1 (2025)|n:3c65c17b0d0d813faca4f7a51eaa0c65}}

The alignment lineage: RLHF, AI feedback, direct preference optimisation, GRPO, and reasoning from RL. (23, 18, 19, 24 and 35 min)

{{RoFormer (2021)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81cfa5e9f54459720098}}

Rotary position embeddings: attention that depends only on relative distance, with no extra parameters; the base every context-extension method rescales. (19 min)

{{Switch Transformers (2021)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81a8b541c9babbf40c94}}

Top-1 expert routing: parameters grow without a matching rise in FLOPs per token; the origin of expert parallelism. (19 min)

{{FlashAttention (2022)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81e4a3e8e7a2c4771381}}

Exact attention computed in tiles that stay in on-chip memory: faster training and memory linear in sequence length. (20 min)

{{OLMo 2 (2025)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81fb9857fb956165ae1c}} and {{Llama 3 (2024)|n:3c65c17b0d0d81aca58ccb9d720b474e}}

A fully open recipe and the most detailed semi-open account of a frontier-scale run. (26 min each)

{{DeepSeek-V3 (2024)|n:3c65c17b0d0d815fb8dac9ba1e35ab81}}

FP8 training at 671B and the most-quoted training bill, with what it includes. (26 min)
