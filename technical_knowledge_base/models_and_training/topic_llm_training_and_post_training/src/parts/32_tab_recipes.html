Open recipes compared: every disclosed stage, model by model

Twenty training recipes that their makers wrote down, from T5 (2019) to MiMo-V2.6-Pro and K2 Horizon (September 2026), on the same stage-by-stage columns: pretraining, mid-training and context extension, supervised fine-tuning, preference tuning, reinforcement learning, distillation, compute and openness. Every cell carries its source, the source's date, and whether the number is published, derived here by arithmetic (formula shown), or unconfirmed. A cell the lab left empty says not disclosed, and is coloured, because which stages a lab stays quiet about is itself the finding.

Try: sort by Disclosed to see who tells you the most, then switch the stage view to Post-training: the hatched cells pile up there. Tick two or three rows (Llama 3.1, DeepSeek-R1 and Olmo 3 are ticked to start) to line their recipes up stage by stage. Click any cell for its source, date, formula and caveats; click a model name for its whole recipe.

How to read it

  • One checkpoint per row. Numbers are never spliced across sizes or versions: Llama 3.1 is the 405B, OLMo 2 the 7B (its post-training is the best described), Olmo 3 the 32B Think, Tulu 3 the 70B. Other sizes appear only in the notes.
  • Inherited is not disclosed by this lab. Tulu 3, DAPO and DeepSeek-R1 start from someone else's (or their own earlier) base model, so their pretraining columns are shown in grey italics and carry the base model's source. The Recipe filter separates them.
  • Units differ by column, on purpose. SFT sorts by examples, preference by pairs or prompts, RL by distinct prompts; where a lab gives only tokens (Phi-4, SmolLM3, K2 Horizon) the value is shown but sorts with the undisclosed, and appears in the tokens chart instead. Compute as disclosed mixes A100, H100, H800 and GH200 hours; the 6ND column puts every row on one yardstick, and is derived unless the paper prints it.
  • Derived means recomputed here. Every derived cell shows its formula in the detail, and src/recipes/recompute.py recomputes all of them from the published inputs.
  • Dates matter. The two September 2026 rows rest on a model card (K2 Horizon, whose technical report is still pending) and a report PDF in the model repository (MiMo-V2.6); expect them to change. Everything was read on 3 October 2026.
