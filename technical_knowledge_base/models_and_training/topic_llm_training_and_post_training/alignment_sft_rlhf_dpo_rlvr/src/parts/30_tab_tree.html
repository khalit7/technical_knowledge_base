Method family tree

Every method on the Reading tab, placed by the date of its first arXiv version (from the identifier) and by what kind of signal it learns from. A line joins a method to the one it changed; most successors keep their parent and remove or fix one thing, which the detail names. The question it answers: what did each method take away, and what is the field still adding back?

Try: click a method to see its paper, what it removed or fixed, its successors, and where this knowledge base covers it in depth; its ancestors and descendants stay lit. Filter a lane to follow one line of descent. On a phone, scroll the chart sideways.

Dates are first arXiv versions (DeepSeek-R1's Nature version followed in September 2025; the cDPO note and the 2026 constitution are not on arXiv). Lanes: the signal the method trains on. The time axis is compressed before 2022, where there are few methods; placement within a lane is for legibility only.
