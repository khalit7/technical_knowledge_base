Further reading

Best resources

{{RLHF Book (Nathan Lambert)|https://rlhfbook.com/}}

Free, continuously updated, the best end-to-end treatment of modern post-training; published in print by Manning in July 2026 (seventeen short chapters), with an accompanying course. (6 h)

{{Reasoning RL in 2026: GRPO, DPO, RLVR, Agentic PO and beyond (Turing Post)|https://www.turingpost.com/p/reasoning-rl-in-2026}}

A current map of the algorithm zoo (Alyona Vert, 7 June 2026). About 1,900 words. (10 min)

{{Hugging Face TRL documentation|https://huggingface.co/docs/trl}}

Reference implementations of SFT, DPO and its loss variants, KTO, ORPO, PPO, GRPO and online DPO; the SFT, DPO and GRPO trainer pages are the core. (1 h)

{{Illustrating RLHF (Hugging Face)|https://huggingface.co/blog/rlhf}}

The canonical picture of the three-step SFT, reward model, PPO pipeline. (20 min)

{{Direct Preference Optimization (Cameron Wolfe)|https://cameronrwolfe.substack.com/p/direct-preference-optimization}}

DPO derived step by step, with its variants and practical lessons. (30 min)

{{The State of Reinforcement Learning for LLM Reasoning (Sebastian Raschka)|https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/the-state-of-llm-reasoning-model-training}}

GRPO, RLVR and the reasoning-RL papers of 2025 in one long read. (35 min)

{{Packing with Flash Attention 2 (Hugging Face)|https://huggingface.co/blog/packing-with-FA2}}

Padding-free packing with position ids and variable-length attention, with throughput and memory measurements. (10 min)

{{Reward Hacking in Reinforcement Learning (Lilian Weng)|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2024-11-28-reward-hacking/}}

What the KL penalty and a verifier defend against. (37 min)
