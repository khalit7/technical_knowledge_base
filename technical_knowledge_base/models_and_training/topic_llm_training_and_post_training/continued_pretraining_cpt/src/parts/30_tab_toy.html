Replay toy: nine continued-pretraining runs, three seeds each

Ibrahim et al. ran each configuration once, at 405M and 10B parameters. This page ran the same two levers on a model small enough to train on a laptop, three times each, so the effects can be told from noise. The question: when an English base model is continued on German, how much English does it lose, and how do replay and the re-warmed peak change that?

What the nine runs say

Code: src/toy/train.py (PyTorch, CPU, two threads); raw runs in src/toy/runs/, packed by src/recompute.py. Resetting AdamW at the start of CPT copies what an adapter of a released checkpoint must do; Ibrahim et al. found keeping or dropping optimizer states ends at similar loss after 40B tokens ({{Figure 13|https://arxiv.org/html/2403.08763v4#A1.F13}}). Toy scale throughout: a character-level model on twelve books is not a 405M model on the Pile, and German here shares English's alphabet; the direction of each effect is what transfers, not its size.
