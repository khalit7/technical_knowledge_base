Further reading

Best resources

{{Simple and Scalable Strategies to Continually Pre-train Large Language Models (Ibrahim et al. 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.08763}}

The reference empirical study: re-warming, re-decaying and replay match full retraining at 405M and 10B; infinite schedules; every table behind this page's frontier chart. (90 min)

{{Continual Pre-Training of Large Language Models: How to (re)warm your model? (Gupta et al. 2023)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04014}}

The predecessor: warmup length, peak and starting checkpoint on Pythia 410M continued on SlimPajama. (25 min)

{{CMR Scaling Law (Gu et al., EMNLP 2024 Findings)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17467}}

Predicting the critical domain/general mixture ratio from small pilots; the coefficients behind the calculator above. (45 min)

{{D-CPT Law (Que et al., NeurIPS 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01375}}

A Chinchilla-style law with the mixture ratio inside, its three uses, and a cross-domain version for new domains. (45 min)

{{Continued Pre-Training overview (Emergent Mind)|https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/continued-pre-training-cpt}}

A living survey of CPT papers, organised by method, scaling, forgetting and domains (last updated October 2025 when checked). (30 min)

{{Swallow: continual pre-training for Japanese (Fujii et al. 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.17790}}

The clearest public new-language recipe: vocabulary extension, replay ratio pilots, forgetting measured at three sizes. (40 min)

{{e-Llama: domain-adaptive CPT at 1T tokens (eBay, 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09706}}

Half general replay, the 10%-of-peak learning rate chosen by ablation, and merging to set the trade-off. (30 min)

{{Periodic Labs: Nature is our learning environment|https://periodic.com/news/nature-is-our-learning-environment}}

Neon: mid-training and RL on laboratory records, the operational-data argument. (10 min)
