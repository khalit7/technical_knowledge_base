# Continued Pretraining (CPT)

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3c65c17b0d0d8119b8f0e801caa09474
Site: https://khalit7.github.io/technical_knowledge_base/models_and_training/topic_llm_training_and_post_training/continued_pretraining_cpt/

Interactive page: `index.html`, built from `src/` (`sh src/build.sh`) and published to Notion as an HTML-only page. Taking a finished base model and training it further, with the same next-token objective, on a new domain, a new language or fresher data: what it forgets, the two levers that control it (replay and the learning-rate peak), how to choose the mix, what labs disclosed, and a small model trained here with and without replay. Tabs: Reading, Replay toy, Recipes compared, Further reading.
