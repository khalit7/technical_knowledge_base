# LLM-as-judge: design, biases, calibration, reliability

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3c65c17b0d0d810ba2a2ecc08c4c233b (child of Topic: evaluation-and-llm-judges)

Interactive page: `index.html`, built from `src/` (`sh src/build.sh`; see `src/README.md`). Judge design (pointwise, pairwise, reference-guided, checklist), prompt and output design, the bias catalogue with measured sizes and mitigation costs (with CALM's 6 judges by 9 biases grid), known exploits (null models), calibration against human gold (Critique Shadowing, kappa, the Rogan-Gladen correction with Lee et al.'s interval, reliability against validity), juries (PoLL, RoPoLL), fine-tuned judges, reward models, generative verifiers and agent-as-judge, meta-evaluations, list-level against instance-level reliability, an operational checklist and common mistakes. Tabs: Reading (with a before/after animation of the gold-slice correction on real MT-Bench data), Protocol lab (GPT-4 run five ways against 3,355 released human votes; reproduces Zheng et al. Table 5), Calibration lab (correct a judge's win rate with a seeded gold slice; 200 redraws; interval width against slice size), Further reading.
