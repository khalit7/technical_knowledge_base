Protocol lab: one judge, five protocols, 3,355 human votes

MT-Bench's authors released both sides of their calibration: 3,355 pairwise votes by experts and authors on six models (GPT-4, Claude-v1, GPT-3.5-turbo, Vicuna-13B, Alpaca-13B, LLaMA-13B), 80 questions, two turns, and GPT-4's verdicts on the same comparisons. Change how the judge is run and which votes count, and watch agreement with the humans, chance-corrected agreement (Cohen's kappa) and the ranking move. Agreement follows the paper: every human vote is compared with the judge's verdict on that question, turn and pair.

What the protocols are

  • One order, model shown first / shown second: a single pairwise call, the non-baseline model in slot A, or in slot B. From the MT-Bench leaderboard run (gpt-4_pair.jsonl, June 2023), which keeps each order's verdict; only pairs against GPT-3.5-turbo.
  • Both orders, leaderboard run: the same two calls combined; a disagreement becomes a tie.
  • Both orders, released with the votes: the GPT-4 verdicts published in lmsys/mt_bench_human_judgments (a separate run: on the 532 comparisons both runs cover, its combined verdict differs from the leaderboard run's in 83, or 15.6%; whether prompt or model snapshot also differed is not documented), all 15 pairs, order disagreements released as "tie (inconsistent)".
  • Pointwise 1 to 10: GPT-4 grades each answer alone (gpt-4_single.jsonl); the higher score wins, equal scores tie. That file grades Vicuna-13B v1.3 rather than the v1.2 the voters saw, so pairs with Vicuna are excluded.

Data: {{lmsys/mt_bench_human_judgments|https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmsys/mt_bench_human_judgments}} and the {{MT-Bench space's model_judgment files|https://huggingface.co/spaces/lmsys/mt-bench/tree/main/data/mt_bench/model_judgment}}, reduced by src/mk_data.py to 1,814 comparisons and 3,355 votes (about 100 KB, in the page).
