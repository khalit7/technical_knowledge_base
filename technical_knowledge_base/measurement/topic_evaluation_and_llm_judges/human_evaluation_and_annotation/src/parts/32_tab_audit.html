Audit lab

You have a review budget and a test set you suspect. Let 18 open models propose which questions are wrong, review them in order, and see how many real errors the queue finds. The "truth" is MMLU-Redux 2.0's human audit of 100 sampled questions per subject; the proposals come from the models' released 5-shot answer probabilities on the same questions (Open LLM Leaderboard v1 details, lm-evaluation-harness). Six subjects, from Virology (57 of 100 flawed) to High School Biology (5 of 100).

Try: Virology at a budget of 20, then High School Biology at the same budget: the same method, a very different hit rate. Switch the ranking to consensus votes, the version that works with API models that return no probabilities.

What the audit does to the scores

Accuracy of each model on the subject's 100 sampled questions against the original key, on the questions the audit found sound, and with the audit's corrected key restored where it gave one. Ranks are competition ranks (ties share a rank). The interval is ±1.96 binomial standard errors.

Model names are the Hugging Face repositories evaluated by the Open LLM Leaderboard (base models unless the name says Instruct, chat or a fine-tune). Questions are MMLU's, licensed MIT; MMLU-Redux 2.0 is CC BY 4.0. Prompts were the harness's 5-shot format; scores here use the argmax of the four answer log-likelihoods, which matches the leaderboard's "acc" on every question.
