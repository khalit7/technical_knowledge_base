Who annotates, and what it costs

The rater pool sets both the ceiling on quality and the bill: domain experts for gold (GPQA's PhD question writers and validators, MT-Bench's graduate students), trained contractors for scale (InstructGPT's about 40 contractors from Upwork and Scale AI, HelpSteer2's about 1,000 US raters via Scale AI), the crowd (Anthropic's HH crowdworkers, Mechanical Turk, Prolific), users (Arena voters, thumbs up and down in a product), and the team that built the system: cheapest and fastest, fine for a pilot, never the only source of gold, because they share the system's blind spots and know which output came from which version.

Crowd pairings sit lowest: agreement by who is paired with whom

Agreement between two raters on the same item, as each study measured it; the tasks differ, so compare within a row, not across. Arena (160 battles, {{Chiang et al. 2024, Table 3|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04132}}): crowd against two experts 72.8 to 83.1%, the experts with each other 79.4% and 89.8%. InstructGPT ({{Ouyang et al. 2022|https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155}}): 72.6 ± 1.5% between training labelers, 77.3 ± 1.3% for held-out labelers, against 73 ± 4% researcher to researcher in earlier summarisation work. Anthropic HH ({{Bai et al. 2022|https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05862}}): about 63% between crowdworkers and the researchers, on about 320 test comparisons. Arena's authors attribute the 5 to 10 point crowd gap mostly to users "overlooking factual errors": the gap is expertise and attention, not honesty.

Effort and cost evidence per pool (GPQA bonuses, HelpSteer2's rater counts, Prolific rates, LLM use)
  • Experts, GPQA ({{Rein et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12022}}): experts were right on 65% of each other's questions, 74% after discounting slips they admitted afterwards; skilled non-experts 34% with unrestricted web access, spending over 30 minutes per question. Writers got $10 per question plus bonuses ($20 per expert who answers correctly, $15 per non-expert who does not, $30 extra if both hold).
  • Contractors, HelpSteer2: at least 3 raters per response (3.41 on average), a fourth and fifth when helpfulness ratings spread by more than 2; about half of all annotations were excluded. InstructGPT chose its contractors by a screening test.
  • Crowd: Prolific's floor is £6 / $8 an hour and it recommends £9 / $12 ({{Prolific, July 2026|https://researcher-help.prolific.com/en/article/2273bd}}); 33 to 46% of MTurk workers on one summarisation task used an LLM ({{Veselovsky et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07899}}).
  • Users: free and plentiful, but the prompt mix and the voters are whoever shows up; how votes become a leaderboard is on {{Human preference and arenas|n:3ef5c17b0d0d814db49ff3b5c9fc3034}}.
