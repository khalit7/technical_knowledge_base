Agreement lab

Every agreement statistic from the Reading tab, computed in your browser on two released multi-rater sets: MT-Bench's expert votes on pairs of answers (A wins, tie, B wins; 2 to 6 experts per item) and HelpSteer2's individual 0 to 4 ratings on five attributes (2 to 6 raters per response, 23,652 responses). The coincidence matrix below is the whole input to alpha: each cell counts how often one label met another on the same item, an item with m labels contributing each of its pairs with weight 1/(m − 1).

Try: MT-Bench turn 1 with ties counted, then dropped; HelpSteer2 coherence against helpfulness (prevalence); any HelpSteer2 attribute with the paper's retention rule on and off (filtering raises agreement by construction).

How each number is computed
  • Raw agreement: matching rater pairs over all rater pairs on the same item (unweighted, as Zheng et al. count it; this reproduces their 63% and 81%).
  • Fleiss' kappa: (po − pe) / (1 − pe) with pe = Σ (share of label)². Strictly defined for a fixed number of raters per item; computed here on the pairs, as Scott's pi generalises.
  • Krippendorff's alpha: 1 − Do/De from the coincidence matrix; distances 0 or 1 (nominal), squared rank-sum distances (ordinal) and squared differences (interval). For MT-Bench, ordinal and interval place a tie halfway between the two wins.
  • Gwet's AC1: chance agreement Σ π(1 − π) / (q − 1) over the q labels used.
  • Checked against src/recompute.py, which computes the same quantities in Python from the same inputs.
