Primary sources

{{Pervasive Label Errors in Test Sets Destabilize ML Benchmarks|https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14749}}

Northcutt, Athalye and Mueller, NeurIPS 2021 Datasets track; browse the errors at {{labelerrors.com|https://labelerrors.com}}. 40 min

{{Confident Learning: Estimating Uncertainty in Dataset Labels|https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.00068}}

Northcutt, Jiang and Chuang, JAIR 2021: the method; {{cleanlab|https://github.com/cleanlab/cleanlab}} implements it. 60 min

{{Are We Done with MMLU? (MMLU-Redux)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04127}}

Gema et al., 2024: the taxonomy, the protocol and the re-annotated questions ({{dataset|https://huggingface.co/datasets/edinburgh-dawg/mmlu-redux-2.0}}). 45 min

{{Do Large Language Model Benchmarks Test Reliability? (PlatinumBench)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03461}}

Vendrow et al., 2025: model-proposed, human-decided cleaning of 15 benchmarks ({{site|http://platinum-bench.csail.mit.edu/}}). 45 min

{{Computing Krippendorff's Alpha-Reliability|https://repository.upenn.edu/handle/20.500.14332/2089}}

Krippendorff, 2011: the coincidence matrix and every metric, with worked examples. 30 min

{{Inter-Coder Agreement for Computational Linguistics|https://aclanthology.org/J08-4004/}}

Artstein and Poesio, 2008: the standard survey of kappa, pi and alpha for annotation. 60 min

{{The "Problem" of Human Label Variation|https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.02570}}

Plank, EMNLP 2022: why disagreement is signal. 25 min

{{What Can We Learn from Collective Human Opinions on NLI Data? (ChaosNLI)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.03532}}

Nie, Zhou and Bansal, EMNLP 2020: 100 annotations per item. 25 min

{{HelpSteer2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.08673}}

Wang et al., 2024: a multi-attribute rating collection with its guidelines and agreement by stage; individual ratings {{released|https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/HelpSteer2}}. 30 min

{{Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena|https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685}}

Zheng et al., NeurIPS 2023: expert votes and human agreement ({{votes|https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmsys/mt_bench_human_judgments}}). 40 min

{{Artificial Artificial Artificial Intelligence|https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.07899}}

Veselovsky, Ribeiro and West, 2023: crowd workers using LLMs. 20 min

{{All That's "Human" Is Not Gold|https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.00061}}

Clark et al., ACL 2021: untrained evaluators of generated text. 25 min
