Designing the task: rubrics and guidelines

Most disagreement between raters is manufactured by the task, not by the raters. The format, the rubric and the guidelines decide how much of a label is signal.

What a usable rubric contains

  • One definition per criterion, written as the question the rater answers ("Does every factual claim agree with the reference?"), not as an adjective ("accurate").
  • Anchors: at least one worked example per level, chosen from borderline cases rather than obvious ones. HelpSteer2's guidelines define every attribute and every rating level with examples (its Appendix G).
  • A decision order when criteria interact. MMLU-Redux's ordered protocol is why its kappa for "is there an error" (binary) and for "which error type" were almost the same, 0.67 and 0.67 on Virology and 0.92 and 0.95 on College Chemistry (Table 1): raters who agreed something was wrong also agreed what.
  • Allowed exits: "cannot judge", "needs an expert", "skip": HelpSteer2 told raters to skip anything with personal data and flag unsafe content. Forcing a label onto an item the rater cannot assess manufactures noise.
  • What to ignore: length, formatting and confident tone, unless they are the criterion. Arena's experts were asked to fact-check answers with a search engine, which took 3 to 5 minutes per label ({{Chiang et al. 2024, §6.3|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.04132}}).
  • Context the rater needs: showing AMT workers the human reference beside the model's story improved their ratings ({{Karpinska et al.|https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06835}}); HelpSteer2 had each rater score the two responses to a prompt one after the other for the same reason.

Pilot, measure, revise

Write the rubric, have two or three people label the same 50 to 100 items, measure agreement, read every disagreement, fix the guideline, repeat. HelpSteer2 published what this loop is worth: quadratic weighted kappa on helpfulness went from 0.465 in the initial collection to 0.706 after clarifying with the vendor how the guidelines apply to edge cases, and to 0.791 after post-processing.
