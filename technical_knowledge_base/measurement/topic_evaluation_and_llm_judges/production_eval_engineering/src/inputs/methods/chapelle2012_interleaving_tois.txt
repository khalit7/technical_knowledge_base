== 7.2 Summary ==
We find that the interleaving methodology is very sensitive and can reveal small differences in retrieval quality with relatively small query samples. In comparison, absolute
metrics typically need orders of magnitude more data to confidently detect any difference (in either the correct or the incorrect direction). We conjecture that the advantages
of interleaving over absolute measurements are due to the following two differences in
experiment design. First, interleaving appears to have increased sensitivity because it
is a paired test, paired on both queries and users. Second, by directly eliciting a preference between two alternatives, interleaving appears to more directly and reliably
measure differences in relevance.
