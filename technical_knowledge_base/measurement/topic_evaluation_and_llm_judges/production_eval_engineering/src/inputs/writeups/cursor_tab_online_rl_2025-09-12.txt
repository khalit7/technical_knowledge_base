Title: Improving Cursor Tab with online RL
Org: Cursor (Anysphere). Date on page: Sep 12, 2025

"Our Tab model runs on every user action, handling over 400 million requests per day."
"Our approach is unusual because it involves rolling out new models to users frequently throughout the day and using that data for training."
"Currently, it takes us 1.5 to 2 hours to roll out a checkpoint and collect the data for the next step."
Reward example: show if accept chance at least 25%: "reward of 0.75 for accepted suggestions, a reward of -0.25 for rejected suggestions, and a reward of 0 if no suggestion is shown."
Result: "This model makes 21% fewer suggestions than the previous model while having a 28% higher accept rate for the suggestions it makes."
Online metric: "We try to keep the accept rate of Tab suggestions high."
Copilot reference: "a logistic regression model taking 11 features as inputs" (contextual filter score, per Parth Thakkar 2022)
