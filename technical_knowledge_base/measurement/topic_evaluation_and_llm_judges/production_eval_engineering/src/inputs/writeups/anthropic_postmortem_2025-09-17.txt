Title: A postmortem of three recent issues
Org: Anthropic (engineering blog)
Date on page: September 17, 2025

"On August 5, some Sonnet 4 requests were misrouted to servers configured for the upcoming 1M token context window. This bug initially affected 0.8% of requests."
"At the worst impacted hour on August 31, 16% of Sonnet 4 requests were affected."
Output corruption: "This corruption affected requests made to Opus 4.1 and Opus 4 on August 25-28, and requests to Sonnet 4 August 25-September 2."
Approximately "30% of Claude Code users who made requests during this period had at least one message routed to the wrong server type."
Third bug: approximate top-k XLA:TPU miscompilation.

Why evals missed it:
"The evaluations we ran simply didn't capture the degradation users were reporting, in part because Claude often recovers well from isolated mistakes."
"We relied too heavily on noisy evaluations."

Changes:
"We've developed evaluations that can more reliably differentiate between working and broken implementations."
"We will run them continuously on true production systems to catch issues."
"We'll develop infrastructure and tooling to better debug community-sourced feedback without sacrificing user privacy."
