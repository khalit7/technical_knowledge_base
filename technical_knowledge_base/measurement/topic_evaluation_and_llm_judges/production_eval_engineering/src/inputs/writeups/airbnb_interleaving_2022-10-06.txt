Title: Beyond A/B Test : Speeding up Airbnb Search Ranking Experimentation through Interleaving
Authors: Qing Zhang, Michelle Du, Reid Andersen, Liwei He (Airbnb). Date on page: Oct 6, 2022

"In 2021, we built the interleaving experimentation framework ... and reached a 50x sensitivity in the development of our search ranking algorithm."
"Each interleaving experiment takes up about 6% of regular A/B test traffic, and one-third of running length."
"We achieve a 50x speedup over an A/B test given the same amount of traffic."
"we tracked eligible interleaving and A/B pairs and confirmed that the two are consistent with each other 82% of the time"
"we assign credits by bookings, which is a downstream activity." (team drafting, competitive pairs)
Uses: "ranker assessment, hyperparameter tuning as well as evaluating infra-level changes."
