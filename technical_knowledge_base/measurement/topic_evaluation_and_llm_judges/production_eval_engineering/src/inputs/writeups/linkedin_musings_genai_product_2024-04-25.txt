Title: Musings on Building a Generative AI Product
Org: LinkedIn Engineering. Date on page: April 25, 2024 (authors listed on page, not captured in extract)

"Our internal linguist team built tooling and processes by which we could evaluate up to 500 daily conversations"
Metrics: "overall quality score, hallucination rate, Responsible AI violation, coherence, style, etc."
"Without it, engineers are left with eye-balling results and testing on a limited set of examples, and having a 1+ day delay to know metrics."
Fig. 2: "Annotators give more granular feedback but have a ~1 day turnaround. Members are the final judges and give us scale, but some metrics can take 3+ days for a single change"
"getting it 80% was fast, but that last 20% took most of our work."
"achieved 80% of the basic experience ... within the first month and then spent an additional four months attempting to surpass 95%"
Structured output: "~10% of the time the LLM would make mistakes" in YAML; "We were ultimately able to reduce occurrences of these errors to ~0.01%."
Design: "Per-step specific evaluation pipelines, particularly for routing/retrieval"; "A centralized evaluation pipeline with multi-turn conversations"
Cost: "we even had to set timetables for when it was ok to test the product or not, as it'd consume too many tokens"
