Title: Innovating Faster on Personalization Algorithms at Netflix Using Interleaving
Authors: Joshua Parks, Juliette Aurisset, Michael Ramm (Netflix). Date on page: Nov 29, 2017

"we have devised a two-stage online experimentation process. The first stage is a fast pruning step ... The second stage is a traditional A/B test on the pared-down set"
A/B core metrics: "month-to-month subscription retention and member streaming hours."
"We find that interleaving is very sensitive: it requires >100x fewer users than our most sensitive A/B metric to achieve 95% power." (page uses the multiplication sign)
Fig. 5 caption: "interleaving can require >100x fewer subscribers to correctly determine ranker preference even compared to the most sensitive A/B metric."
Method: bootstrap subsampling, "N users to the interleaving cell or N/2 users to each cell of the traditional A/B experiment."
"there is a very strong correlation and alignment between the interleaving metric and our most sensitive A/B evaluation metric"
"The first stage finishes in a matter of days"
