6. Cost and latency budgets

The second gate reads tokens per request, p95 latency and projected spend from the runs the first gate already made, so it is nearly free. A model swap that wins on quality at 3 times the cost fails promotion unless someone explicitly waives it. Latency belongs in it from the start: Honeycomb's query assistant saw responses take "from two to 15+ seconds" ({{All the Hard Stuff Nobody Talks About|https://www.honeycomb.io/blog/hard-stuff-nobody-talks-about-llm}}, 26 May 2023); GitHub describes Copilot model choices as a trade between acceptance rate and latency (17 Jan 2025); Discord measures tokens per second during a limited release "to predict the costs of a complete launch" ({{Developing Rapidly with Generative AI|https://discord.com/blog/developing-rapidly-with-generative-ai}}, 12 Apr 2024).

Model choice is a Pareto problem: plot quality against cost per request (and latency) for every candidate; only points on the frontier are candidates. Predict: as your traffic becomes mostly prompt tokens, does the cheap Claude model join the frontier? Drag the share.

The frontier depends on your traffic's token mix. The four strongest models in the MT-Bench file at their mid-2023 list prices: at 75% prompt tokens the frontier is gpt-3.5-turbo and gpt-4; claude-instant-v1 joins it above a 90.5% prompt share (5.51 − 3.88 x share ≤ 2), where its blended price drops below gpt-3.5-turbo's (blended price = share x prompt price + (1 − share) x completion price; recomputed in src/recompute.py). A 2023 snapshot to show the method, not advice: re-run the frontier on every price or model-version change.

Three practical additions. Cascades and routing: a cheap model with escalation on low confidence or judge-flagged outputs often beats any single point (routers: {{Gateways and routing|n:3f15c17b0d0d81e4a964c129a3e520ae}}). Quality per dollar as the gate metric on cost-sensitive surfaces. And the frontier is re-run, never cached.

The evals' own bill

The gate costs money too, and judge calls dominate it. Section 3's run took 320 GPT-4 calls averaging 775 (617 prompt, 159 completion) tokens each (counted with GPT-4's tokenizer on the released prompts and judgments): $8.97 for the two models, or $0.028 per call at the 2023 price of $0.03 per 1,000 prompt tokens and $0.06 per 1,000 completion tokens ({{OpenAI pricing, May 2023 capture|https://web.archive.org/web/20230531155533/https://openai.com/pricing}}). Small per run, but a gate runs on every change, an online judge on a share of all traffic, and the champion must be re-scored whenever the judge changes.
