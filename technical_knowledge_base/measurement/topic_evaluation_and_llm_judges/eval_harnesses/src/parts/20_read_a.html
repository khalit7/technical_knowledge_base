Every harness does the same three things: load items, get the model to respond, turn the response into a number. They differ in what they treat as the unit of work, and that one choice decides what they can score, what they log, whether they can run an agent safely, and how easy it is to reproduce someone else's number.

The parent page, {{Topic: evaluation-and-llm-judges|n:3c65c17b0d0d8181b351e1b8fc6a1546}}, explains why an eval is a measuring instrument and shows, in its tab Same model, many harnesses, how far one model's score moves when only the harness changes (a 200-item reproduction on Qwen2.5-0.5B and published cases such as LLaMA-65B on MMLU). This page does not repeat that; it explains the harnesses themselves.
