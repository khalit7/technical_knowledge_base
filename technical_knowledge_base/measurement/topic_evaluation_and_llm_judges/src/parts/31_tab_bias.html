Judge bias lab

An LLM judge is an instrument, and these are the ways its reading moves when the answers do not change. Below, one real answer pair is graded again and again from released judgments: with the positions swapped, by a judge from the answer's own family, by five judges, and then with the usual fixes applied. A second real pair shows padding. Measured rates from the studies follow, each in its own panel because each study measures something slightly different. No judge was called for this page; every verdict is a released output.

This is the one place on this page where judge biases are shown hands-on. What the fixes can and cannot do
Swapping and averaging removes the position artefact; it turns a confident wrong verdict into an honest tie, which flags the item for a closer look. Length control removes the reward for length, not the reward for real content. A jury dilutes any one family's taste. None of them makes a judge right on a question it cannot solve: for a checkable answer, give the judge a verified reference or check the answer with a program, as the gold line in section 1 does.
