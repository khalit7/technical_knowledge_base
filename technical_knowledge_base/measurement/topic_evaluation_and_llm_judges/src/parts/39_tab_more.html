Further reading

The Reading tab is a map on one spine: an eval is a measuring instrument, with a thing it measures, a noise level, ways it breaks, and a calibration against people. The pages under this one own the depth. Links open in a new tab, with reading times as each page's header states them.

Best resources

{{Zheng et al. 2023, Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena|https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685}} (45 min)

The paper that named the pattern: position, verbosity and self-enhancement bias, reference-guided grading, agreement with experts. Source of the human-ceiling figures in Reading section 3.

{{Hamel Husain, Creating an LLM-as-a-Judge That Drives Business Results|https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/llm-judge/}} (about 35 min)

The best practitioner guide: Critique Shadowing, building a judge from one expert's pass/fail critiques.

{{Hamel Husain, evals FAQ|https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals-faq/}} (about 40 min)

Condensed answers on golden sets, CI gates and error analysis.

{{Evan Miller, Adding Error Bars to Evals|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640}} (45 min)

The statistics of evals: standard errors, clustering, paired differences, power.

{{Gu et al. 2024, A Survey on LLM-as-a-Judge|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15594}} (90 min)

The standard survey of judge designs and mitigations.

{{Reliability without Validity (arXiv:2606.19544)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19544}} (45 min)

21 judges, about 541,000 judgments: kappa deflation, ranking shifts across meta-benchmarks, and repeatable judges with position bias.

{{How to Correctly Report LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluations (arXiv:2511.21140)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21140}} (45 min)

Correcting a judge's pass rate with its measured sensitivity and specificity, with intervals.

{{Cheating Automatic LLM Benchmarks: Null Models Achieve High Win Rates|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07137}} (45 min)

The canonical judge-exploitation result.

{{JudgeBench|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12784}} (45 min)

Judges on hard pairs with objectively correct answers.

{{Replacing Judges with Juries (PoLL)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18796}} (45 min)

A panel of small judges against one large one, with Cohen's kappa against people.

{{Hugging Face, What's going on with the Open LLM Leaderboard?|https://huggingface.co/blog/open-llm-leaderboard-mmlu}} (about 20 min)

One model, one benchmark, three implementations, three scores: the clearest explanation of log-likelihood against generative scoring.

{{Are We Done with MMLU? (MMLU-Redux)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04127}} (45 min)

Gold-label errors in a benchmark everyone used, and how they move scores.

{{Inspect documentation|https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/}} (about 90 min for the core pages)

Tasks, solvers, scorers, sandboxing and agents, with the best log viewer of any harness.

{{Google DeepMind, Piloting the world's first double-blind AI evaluations|https://deepmind.google/blog/piloting-the-worlds-first-double-blind-ai-evaluations/}} (about 15 min)

The enclave pilot (August 2026); discussed on Topic: benchmarks.
