Hallucination spans

RAGTruth (Niu et al., ACL 2024; arXiv v2, 17 May 2024) had six 2023 models answer, summarise and write from given sources, then had annotators mark every hallucinated span in all 17,790 responses with one of four types. The prompted judges have high recall and low precision on QA (GPT-4-turbo: recall 90.6, precision 33.2): they call too many faithful answers hallucinated. A 13B model fine-tuned on the labels beats them, which is the paper's point and the reason the field moved to trained detectors (Reading, {{Detection benchmarks|#t-read}}).
