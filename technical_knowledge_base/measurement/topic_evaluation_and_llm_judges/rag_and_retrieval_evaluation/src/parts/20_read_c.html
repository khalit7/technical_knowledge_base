A real run, scored and reproduced

To see what the metrics say on real labels, we ran five retrievers over three BEIR test sets (Thakur et al., NeurIPS 2021 Datasets and Benchmarks) and scored them against the released qrels: SciFact (5,183 abstracts, 300 scientific claims), NFCorpus (3,633 medical documents, 323 queries, graded labels 1 and 2) and FiQA-2018 (57,638 financial forum posts, 648 questions). The systems: BM25 reimplemented the way Anserini's "flat" regression runs it (title and text as one field, Lucene's English stopwords, Porter stemming, k1 = 0.9, b = 0.4), the same BM25 with k1 = 1.2 and b = 0.75, the rank_bm25 package as installed, a small dense retriever (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, 22M parameters, cosine), and a hybrid fusing BM25 and dense with reciprocal rank fusion (k = 60). All runs took under 15 minutes on a laptop CPU, two threads (src/beir_run.py).

Defaults reproduce published numbers, independently

What the run teaches:

  • The winner depends on the corpus. BM25 beats the dense model on SciFact (nDCG@10 0.676 against 0.645), where claims reuse the abstracts' technical terms, and loses badly on FiQA (0.239 against 0.369), where forum questions paraphrase. A leaderboard average hides both; test on your corpus.
  • Fusion wins everywhere here. The RRF hybrid is best on all three sets (0.719, 0.356, 0.373) and lifts SciFact recall@100 from 0.925 to 0.962: the two retrievers miss different passages.
  • "BM25" is not one system. rank_bm25 as installed (no stemming, no stopwords, k1 = 1.5, b = 0.75) scores 0.652 on SciFact and 0.217 on FiQA, against 0.676 and 0.239 for the Lucene-style run; moving k1 and b alone takes FiQA from 0.239 to 0.253. Quote the implementation and parameters with the score.
  • Recall and hit rate tell different stories when labels are many. NFCorpus has 38 labelled passages per query on average, so BM25's recall@10 is 0.152 while 68% of queries have at least one relevant passage in the top 10 (hit@10 0.684), and precision@10 can never approach 1 on SciFact (1.1 labels per query).
  • Measure at the reranker's depth. On SciFact, BM25 recall@10 is 0.800 and recall@100 0.925: a reranker reading the top 100 has 12.5 more points of recall to work with than the prompt ever sees, and none beyond 0.925.
