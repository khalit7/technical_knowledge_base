Papers and guides

{{Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals" (Anthropic, 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640}}

The framework of this page: CLT and clustered standard errors, resampling, next-token probabilities, paired differences, the sample-size formula. Read first. 45 min

{{Anthropic, "A statistical approach to model evaluations"|https://www.anthropic.com/research/statistical-approach-to-model-evals}}

The five recommendations in plain words, with the 0.3 to 0.7 correlation range. 10 min

{{Bowyer, Aitchison and Ivanova, "Don't Use the CLT in LLM Evals With Fewer Than a Few Hundred Datapoints" (ICML 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01747}}

Coverage failures at small n; Wilson and Bayesian alternatives, with code. 40 min

{{Madaan et al., "Quantifying Variance in Evaluation Benchmarks" (2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10229}}

Seed variance of ten identical 7B runs against item intervals on 13 benchmarks; cloze against choice formats. 40 min

{{Card et al., "With Little Power Comes Great Responsibility" (EMNLP 2020)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06595}}

Power and Type-M error in NLP: GLUE, machine translation, human ratings, with notebooks. 50 min

{{Heineman et al., "Signal and Noise" (Ai2, 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13144}}

Signal-to-noise ratio of benchmarks across 375 models; which benchmarks support small-scale decisions. 45 min

{{Hochlehnert et al., "A Sober Look at Progress in Language Model Reasoning" (2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07086}}

Seed, hardware and prompt variance on AIME-size benchmarks. 35 min

{{Bean et al., "Measuring what Matters" (NeurIPS 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04703}}

Construct validity review of 445 benchmarks; the 16% statistics figure and a checklist. 40 min

{{statsforevals.com (Ian Arawjo)|https://statsforevals.com/}}

A living guide (updated August 2026) and the evalstats library: default methods chosen by simulation. 30 min

{{Dror et al., "The Hitchhiker's Guide to Testing Statistical Significance in NLP" (ACL 2018)|https://aclanthology.org/P18-1128/}}

Which significance test for which NLP metric. 40 min

{{Koehn, "Statistical Significance Tests for Machine Translation Evaluation" (2004)|https://aclanthology.org/W04-3250/}}

The paired bootstrap as practised in NLP. 20 min

{{Dietterich, "Approximate Statistical Tests for Comparing Supervised Classification Learning Algorithms" (1998)|https://doi.org/10.1162/089976698300017197}}

McNemar's test for classifiers and why resampled t-tests mislead. 40 min

{{Abadie, Athey, Imbens and Wooldridge, "When Should You Adjust Standard Errors for Clustering?" (QJE 2023)|https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjac038}}

Cluster on how the sample was drawn. 45 min

{{Gelman and Carlin, "Beyond Power Calculations" (2014)|https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614551642}}

Type-S and Type-M errors: why significant results from small studies exaggerate. 25 min

{{Chen et al., "Evaluating Large Language Models Trained on Code" (2021)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03374}}

Section 2.1: the unbiased pass@k estimator. 10 min
