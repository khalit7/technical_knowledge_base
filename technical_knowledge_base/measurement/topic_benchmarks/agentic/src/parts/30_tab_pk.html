pass^k on real trials

Every τ²-bench leaderboard run below comes with its trajectory file, four trials per task. This tab recomputes pass^1 to pass^4 and pass@1 to pass@4 from those trials (formulas in Reading, Best case or reliability) and checks them against the leaderboard. Sources: the submission files in {{sierra-research/tau2-bench|https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench/tree/main/web/leaderboard/public/submissions}} (MIT) and the trajectory files they point to, read 4 Oct 2026.

Try: pick Retail and move k to 4: three models that differ at pass^1 land on the same value. Then compare Claude Opus 4.5 with GPT-5.2 on Airline and look at the tasks where they disagree.

Two runs, task by task

One row per task, sorted by A's successes; each cell is one trial in file order (green passed, orange failed). Tasks where the two runs' success counts differ are marked. Different runs used the same tasks and the same user simulator version where stated in the table.

Beyond k = 4: the plug-in curve

With four trials, pass^k can be estimated without bias only up to k = 4. Treating each task's observed rate c/4 as its true success probability gives a curve for larger k, labelled plug-in, not an estimate: a task seen 4 times out of 4 counts as certain forever, so the curve flattens at the share of 4-of-4 tasks, and the true pass^8 is lower.

Files that do not reproduce the published figures

Counting a missing reward (an errored simulation) as a failure. Where the published value is higher, the leaderboard may have scored a different file or excluded errors; either way these runs are left out of the charts above.
