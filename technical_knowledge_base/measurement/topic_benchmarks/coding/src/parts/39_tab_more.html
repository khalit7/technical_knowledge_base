Primary sources

{{Chen et al. 2021, Evaluating LLMs Trained on Code|https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03374}} (90 min, 35 pages)

HumanEval, pass@k and its unbiased estimator (section 2.1, Appendix A), the Appendix B samples used on this page.

{{Liu et al. 2023, EvalPlus|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01210}} (40 min)

HumanEval+ and how generated tests catch wrong code; {{leaderboard|https://evalplus.github.io/leaderboard.html}} (5 min).

{{Austin et al. 2021, MBPP|https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.07732}} (40 min)

The 974 entry-level tasks.

{{Zhuo et al. 2024, BigCodeBench|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15877}} (40 min)

Library-heavy tasks with high-coverage tests.

{{LiveCodeBench|https://livecodebench.github.io/}} (15 min)

The board with its date slider; {{paper|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07974}} (45 min).

{{LiveCodeBench Pro paper|https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.11928}} (40 min)

Medalists' analysis of model failures. The site (livecodebenchpro.com) was offline on 4 October 2026.

{{OpenAI 2025, Competitive Programming with Large Reasoning Models|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06807}} (30 min)

The Codeforces ladder from o1 to o3, and IOI 2024.

{{Jimenez et al. 2023, SWE-bench|https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770}} (45 min)

Construction, fail-to-pass tests, Lite; {{swebench.com|https://www.swebench.com/}} (5 min).

{{OpenAI, Introducing SWE-bench Verified|https://openai.com/index/introducing-swe-bench-verified/}} (12 min)

The screening of 1,699 tasks into the 500.

{{Aleithan et al. 2024, SWE-Bench+|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.06992}} (30 min)

Solution leakage and weak tests, counted.

{{Liang et al. 2025, The SWE-Bench Illusion|https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12286}} (30 min)

File-path and function recall as memorisation probes.

{{Deng et al. 2025, SWE-Bench Pro|https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16941}} (45 min)

The V1 design; {{V1 board|https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public}} and {{V2 board|https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/swe_bench_pro_public_v2}} (10 min); {{V2 blog|https://labs.scale.com/blog/swe-bench-pro-v2}} (10 min).

{{SWE-rebench|https://swe-rebench.com/}} (10 min)

The rolling board; {{paper|https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20411}} (40 min); {{SWE-bench-Live|https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23419}} (30 min).

{{Real-SWE|https://withspecific.com/benchmarks/real-swe}} (15 min)

Licensed private codebases, per-task results and failure modes.

{{Berkeley RDI, How We Broke Top AI Agent Benchmarks|https://rdi.berkeley.edu/blog/trustworthy-benchmarks-cont/}} (15 min)

Including the 100% on SWE-bench Pro without solving anything.

{{Aider polyglot|https://aider.chat/2024/12/21/polyglot.html}} (8 min)

How the 225 exercises were chosen.

{{Vals AI, SWE-bench Verified|https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/swebench}} and {{DeepSWE|https://deepswe.datacurve.ai/}} (10 min)

Two independent fixed-harness boards.
