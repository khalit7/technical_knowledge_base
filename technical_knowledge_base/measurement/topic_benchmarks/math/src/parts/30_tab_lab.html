Sample lab: every MathArena answer, graded your way

MathArena publishes every final answer of every run on its contests. This tab loads all of them for three contests (7,914 answers on AIME 2025, 3,516 on AIME 2026 and 3,783 on HMMT February 2026, from 66, 32 and 32 model configurations; one AIME 2025 entry with a single answer is left out) and recomputes the scores under the rules the Reading tab compares: one sample, a vote of k, any of k, and three kinds of interval. Data: {{MathArena on Hugging Face|https://huggingface.co/MathArena}} (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, ETH Zurich SRI Lab), downloaded 4 October 2026; the problems are the contests' own.

Try: on AIME 2025, pick o1 (medium) and o3-mini (high) and look at the paired test; switch the interval to "per answer" and back; click problem 15 to see the shared wrong answer.

All models, with intervals

Dots: pass@1, the mean over all answers (it reproduces MathArena's table). Whiskers: 95% intervals. Per answer: 1.96·√(p(1−p)/answers), MathArena's own. Clustered: 1.96 × the standard error of the per-problem solve rates (problems as the unit). Binomial on problems: 1.96·√(p(1−p)/problems). Orange names: released after the contest, flagged by MathArena as possibly contaminated. A few configurations ran 2 times, not 4; some have a few answers missing in the released data; eight are in the released data but no longer on MathArena's tables, and pass@1 from the released answers matches the table exactly for 120 of the 122 that are.
