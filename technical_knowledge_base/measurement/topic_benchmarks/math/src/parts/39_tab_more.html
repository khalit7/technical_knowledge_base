Best resources

{{GSM8K paper (Cobbe et al., 2021)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.14168}} (45 min)

The dataset and the first sample-and-verify protocol on it.

{{GSM-Symbolic (Mirzadeh et al., 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05229}} (45 min)

The fragility critique: templates, P1, P2 and NoOp; read v2 for Table 1 and the authors' reply on statistics.

{{Towards more rigorous evaluations of language models (Ivanova et al., ICLR 2025 blog)|https://iclr-blogposts.github.io/2025/blog/towards-more-rigorous-llm-evals/}} (25 min)

The statistical re-test of GSM-Symbolic: what survives (NoOp) and what does not.

{{GSM1k (Zhang et al., 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00332}} (30 min)

A private look-alike set to measure overfitting to GSM8K.

{{MATH paper (Hendrycks et al., 2021)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03874}} (45 min)

Construction, subjects and levels, normalised exact match.

{{Let's Verify Step by Step (Lightman et al., 2023)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050}} (40 min)

Where MATH-500 and PRM800K come from.

{{FrontierMath (Glazer et al., 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04872}} (45 min)

Design rules (novelty, automatic checking, guessproofness) and the mathematicians' interviews.

{{Epoch AI's FrontierMath trackers|https://epoch.ai/benchmarks}} (15 min)

The hub with Tiers 1-3 and Tier 4, v1 and v2, the harness and its changelog; with {{the tiers page|https://epoch.ai/frontiermath/tiers-1-4/about}}.

{{Epoch AI: OpenAI and FrontierMath|https://epoch.ai/latest/openai-and-frontiermath}} (5 min)

The funding and access terms, January 2025.

{{MathArena|https://matharena.ai}} (15 min)

Contest-time evaluations (AIME, HMMT, USAMO, IMO) and the 2026 research sets; every table flags post-contest models.

{{MathArena paper (Balunović, Dekoninck et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23281}} (40 min)

The protocol, the AIME 2024 contamination finding, the rank-interval test.

{{Farewell to final-answer competition problems (MathArena, May 2026)|https://matharena.ai/no_final_answer/}} (10 min)

Why final-answer contests were retired and what replaced them.

{{Omni-MATH (Gao et al., 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07985}} (30 min)

Olympiad problems with a judge model instead of exact match.

{{PutnamBench leaderboard|https://trishullab.github.io/PutnamBench/leaderboard.html}} (10 min)

Formal results by track, with costs and caveats; the {{paper|https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11214}} for the construction.

{{Gemini Deep Think at IMO 2025 (Google DeepMind)|https://deepmind.google/blog/advanced-version-of-gemini-with-deep-think-officially-achieves-gold-medal-standard-at-the-international-mathematical-olympiad/}} (8 min)

The first certified gold, with the 2024 AlphaProof comparison.

{{IMO 2026 autonomous model comparison (deedy/imo-2026)|https://github.com/deedy/imo-2026}} (15 min)

Nine runs in one harness with full logs: effort settings, repair rounds, AI graders.

{{CritPt (Zhu et al., 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26574}} (40 min)

Research-level physics with private keys; read with the re-grading paper page.
