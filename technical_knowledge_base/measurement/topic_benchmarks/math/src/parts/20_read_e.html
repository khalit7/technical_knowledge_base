11. Reading a reported math score

Every section above reduces to a few questions to ask of any math number:

  • Which rule? pass@1, a vote and any-of-k differ by up to 17.5 points on the same samples; pass@k is an oracle's number (section 2).
  • Which checker? Exact match, a symbolic check, a GPT-4o judge, a self-submitted proof on a fixed formalisation and three kinds of IMO grading are different instruments (section 1).
  • Were the problems new? A contest is clean only for models released before it was sat; the full MATH test is not clean after May 2023. Contamination in general is the parent's {{Topic: benchmarks|n:3c65c17b0d0d811fb43fece56e40041a}}.
  • Which version, and who ran it? FrontierMath v1 and v2 are different measurements, and so are lab and Epoch runs (o3: about 25% claimed, about 10% measured).
  • How many problems, counted how? 30 problems give ±14 points at 80%; MathArena's ± counts answers, so a new set's interval is a median 1.7 times wider (section 6).
  • Is the key right? 42% of FrontierMath problems needed fixing, GSM8K-Platinum removed 110 test items, and CritPt's same model went from 32.3% to 87.5% on repaired keys: a low score on a hard set is not the model's limit until the key is audited.

Whatever the set, report the sampling rule, the number of runs, the effort level, who ran it, the set version and an interval at the problem level.

What to use now

Frontier claims

MathArena's research sets (ArXivMath, BrokenArXiv; ArXivLean for formal proofs), and proof-graded contests such as USAMO 2026, with the grader named. FrontierMath Tiers 1-3 v2 still separates by a few problems, run by Epoch, with its 1.4-point standard error in view; Tier 4 v2 no longer separates the top; FrontierMath Erdős is the one Epoch set far from its ceiling. A lab's release of machine-written papers is evidence of a different kind: count withdrawals and the formalised share, and check that the formal statements match the paper.

Milestones

The IMO each July, read with three questions: who graded (IMO coordinators, the lab's panel, an AI grader), under which time and tool limits, and first pass or after repair.

Small models and regressions

GSM8K (better: GSM8K-Platinum) and MATH-500 as cheap smoke tests, plus a perturbed variant (GSM-Symbolic P2 or a distractor set) to catch pattern matching. Not the full MATH test after May 2023.

History only

AIME and HMMT vintages (saturated; deprecated by MathArena in May 2026), Omni-MATH (board dormant since 2024), miniF2F and PutnamBench (solved).

The old page's advice, and why it changed

The old page's advice, "current-year AIME/HMMT via the MathArena protocol, FrontierMath tiers for frontier claims; GSM8K/MATH only as cheap regression smoke tests for small models", was right until spring 2026; its first two parts have since saturated.
