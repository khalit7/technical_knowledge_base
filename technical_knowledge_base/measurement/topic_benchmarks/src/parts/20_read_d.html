Reading any score

Seven questions for any model card, leaderboard or launch post, each with the case on this page that shows why it matters:

  1. Where is the benchmark in its life? Near the ceiling, deltas mean nothing: at 90% on GPQA Diamond's 198 questions the 95% interval is about ±4 points ({{Benchmark atlas|#t-atlas}}, {{Saturation timeline|#t-sat}}).
  2. Which version, split and index version? Terminal-Bench 2.x against 4.0, SWE-bench Pro public against private, Artificial Analysis v4.2 against v4.3: different numbers with the same name.
  3. What ran it, and who? Harness, tools, retrieval index, effort, and lab or independent re-runner. On SWE-bench, "bash only" and "full agent" columns differ by 10 to 20 points: never compare across leaderboard columns.
  4. How was it sampled? pass@1 or maj@k (consensus over 32 samples is many points above a single sample on AIME), runs, items, temperature, token budget; the same protocol for every model compared?
  5. Is the residual the model or the key? On HLE and the physics suites a large share of "failures" are wrong keys, ambiguous questions or grader bugs; an apparent ceiling below 100% may be the answer key.
  6. Could it have seen the test, or gamed it? A public static set is an upper bound; an agent score not run under an isolated evaluator with held-out checks deserves suspicion. An arena rating measures what voters prefer, style included, and selective private testing can inflate it: one noisy signal, filtered by category, style control on.
  7. Can anyone reproduce it, and is the subject really the model? A number no public harness reproduces deserves a discount; a vendor's claim with no technical report is carried in its own words, not reconstructed.
Why a garbled figure is carried in the source's words, and what cards lead with now

Carry an unverifiable figure in the source's own words so it stays matchable: Hyper-τ-bench's 23.9% against 82.2% first arrived from an aggregator as "tau^tau-Bench" and was matched a week later. Claims without a report (Shanghai AI Laboratory's "highest reported score on five" of 16 benchmarks for Atria Dawn Preview, five unnamed; StepFun's Step 5 Preview table) stay in the vendor's wording.

What model cards lead with in late 2026: HLE, the current AIME, SWE-bench Verified and Pro, Terminal-Bench 4.0 and Terminal-Bench-Science, Real-SWE, ARC-AGI-2 and 3, OSWorld, τ²-bench, MMMU-Pro, Arena ratings. GPQA Diamond is still printed but no longer separates the frontier.
