The families in brief

Benchmarks group by what they try to measure. Each family gets its intuition and what is active or retired; the {{Benchmark atlas|#t-atlas}} lists every benchmark with year, format, metric and status, and the child pages own construction, errata and scores.

Knowledge and reasoning

Can the model answer what experts can? These began as exams (MMLU's 57 subjects) and moved to questions chosen because models failed them: GPQA's "Google-proof" graduate science, where skilled non-experts with the web score about 34%, and HLE's roughly 2,500 expert questions. Abstract reasoning took its own road with ARC-AGI: novel puzzles that resist memorisation, interactive games since 2026. The recurring problem is the answer key.

Active: HLE, ARC-AGI-3, SimpleQA (factuality and abstention), AA-Briefcase, MMLU-Pro for mid-tier models. Saturated or retired: MMLU, GPQA Diamond at the frontier (still useful from 60 to 90%), HellaSwag, WinoGrande, BIG-Bench Hard, ARC-AGI-1 and ARC-AGI-2.

Go deeper: {{Knowledge and reasoning benchmarks|n:3c65c17b0d0d810f8574da3ffb860be5}} (33 min read): MMLU errata, GPQA, HLE's judge and tools effect, ARC-AGI 1 to 3.

Math

Math answers can be checked exactly, so the family is clean to grade and quick to saturate. GSM8K and MATH fell first (GSM-Symbolic showed part of GSM8K was pattern matching). The answer was freshness: each year's AIME is a benchmark the moment it is sat, and MathArena evaluates within days; by 2026 even fresh contests opened near the ceiling. At the top, FrontierMath's private research problems held out longest, but its hardest tier (Tier 4 v2, 41 private problems) reached 100% in Epoch's own run of GPT-6.1 Sol, started 29 September 2026 ({{Epoch AI|https://epoch.ai/benchmarks}}). Lean proofs (miniF2F, PutnamBench) remove grading ambiguity, and both are now solved.

Active: MathArena's research sets (ArXivMath, ArXivLean), proof-graded contests such as USAMO, CritPt. Saturating: FrontierMath Tiers 1-3. Saturated or retired: GSM8K, MATH and MATH-500, AIME and HMMT (every vintage; MathArena deprecated final-answer contests in May 2026), FrontierMath Tier 4, miniF2F and PutnamBench (solved), MGSM.

Go deeper: {{Math benchmarks: GSM8K to FrontierMath|n:3ef5c17b0d0d81ec942edf0040a2f596}} (24 min read): the GSM8K lineage, sampling traps on 30-problem contests, FrontierMath's versions and governance.

Coding

Coding moved from one function against unit tests (HumanEval, MBPP) to real GitHub issues in whole repositories (the SWE-bench family), which made coding benchmarks agent benchmarks: the score depends on the scaffold. Contamination is fought with dated contest problems (LiveCodeBench; LiveCodeBench Pro rates models against humans) and with repositories the model cannot have seen. Weak test suites overstate correctness, a lesson EvalPlus taught and every code benchmark relearns.

Active: SWE-rebench, Real-SWE, DeepSWE, Phi-Bench, Codeforces ratings (not comparable across labs). Saturating: Aider Polyglot; SWE-bench Verified, kept for continuity (eight models between 93.4% and 97.0% in Vals AI's bash-only run, updated 1 September 2026: {{Vals AI|https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/swebench}}). Saturated, frozen or retired: HumanEval, MBPP and EvalPlus, BigCodeBench, LiveCodeBench and LiveCodeBench Pro (boards frozen in 2025), SWE-bench Pro v1 (deprecated) and Pro V2 (public split at 99.4%).

Go deeper: {{Coding benchmarks: HumanEval to SWE-bench Pro|n:3ef5c17b0d0d81bfa09ad4ba5eef0c4e}} (27 min read): the HumanEval lineage, LiveCodeBench, the SWE-bench family and its splits.

Agentic

An agent benchmark hands the model tools and an environment (a terminal, a desktop, a website, a simulated customer) and checks the end state. The number belongs to model plus scaffold, and the environment can be attacked. τ²-bench's metric is worth knowing: pass^k, the probability that all k independent tries succeed, which measures reliability and falls far below pass@1 for most models. METR's time horizons measure the length of human task an agent completes half the time.

Active: Terminal-Bench 4.0 and Terminal-Bench-Science, OSWorld (Verified, 2.0), τ²-bench, BrowseComp, METR time horizons, GDPval and GDPval-AA (Artificial Analysis's agentic variant, graded head to head by model judges into an Elo scale), AutomationBench, Agents' Last Exam, EdgeBench, Hyper-τ-bench. Saturated or retired: GAIA and WebArena (saturating, shown gameable), AgentBench, τ-bench, Terminal-Bench 1.0.

Go deeper: {{Agentic benchmarks|n:3c65c17b0d0d811fb926d7b91ef8234e}} (35 min read): the environments, pass^k, time horizons, the 2026 integrity crisis.

Long context

Can the model use a million tokens, not just accept them? Finding one planted fact (needle in a haystack) was solved early, and partly a prompt artefact: one added sentence took Claude 2.1 from 27% to 98% ({{Anthropic, 6 Dec 2023|https://claude.com/blog/claude-2-1-prompting}}). Useful tests remove the literal match (NoLiMa) or make the model trace and aggregate across the input: MRCR's repeated requests, and GDP.pdf's 4,592 PDF pages, where GPT-6 Astra leads at 34.2% on Surge's harness and 32.2% on Artificial Analysis's, both read 4 October 2026 ({{Surge AI|https://surgehq.ai/benchmarks/gdp-pdf}}; {{Artificial Analysis|https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/gdp-pdf}}).

Active: GDP.pdf, MRCR (independent runs; vendor figures are near the ceiling), NoLiMa. Saturating: AA-LCR. Retired, dormant or saturated: needle in a haystack, RULER, LongBench v2, Fiction.LiveBench.

Go deeper: {{Long-context and multimodal benchmarks|n:3ef5c17b0d0d810bb4dfc98919c9be8a}} (23 min read): effective against advertised length, MRCR, GDP.pdf.

Multimodal

Questions that need an image, chart, document or video. MMMU saturated and its Pro version took over; chart, document and visual-math sets saturate fast because their answers are short, so successors are built by filtering for what current models fail; video is the open end.

Active: Video-MME-v2, CharXiv, ZeroBench, GDP.pdf. Saturating: MMMU-Pro, Video-MME and Video-MMMU. Saturated: MMMU, MathVista, ChartQA, DocVQA.

Go deeper: {{Long-context and multimodal benchmarks|n:3ef5c17b0d0d810bb4dfc98919c9be8a}} (23 min read): the blind baseline, MMMU to MMMU-Pro, charts, documents, video.

Safety

Does the model refuse what it should, resist jailbreaks, stay honest, and stay safe when it acts? Early tests scored the answer text (TruthfulQA); later ones score attack success (HarmBench, StrongREJECT, AgentHarm), regulation-derived risks (AIR-Bench), belief against claim (MASK), and now the monitor and the long run (MOLE, Emergence World): what a model does matters more than what it says. Two further kinds exist: dangerous-capability evaluations (biology, cyber, autonomy), scored against thresholds, and scheming and evaluation-awareness tests: does the model behave the same when it thinks no one is checking?

Active: AIR-Bench, MASK (saturating), the Virology Capabilities Test, scheming suites, MOLE, Emergence World. Research staples that left frontier cards: HarmBench, AgentHarm, StrongREJECT (filtered as saturated by OpenAI in December 2025, then replaced by a multi-turn red-teaming evaluation before GPT-5.4: {{GPT-5.2 card|https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/3a4153c8-c748-4b71-8e31-aecbde944f8d/oai_5_2_system-card.pdf}}). Mostly retired: TruthfulQA.

Go deeper: {{Safety and honesty benchmarks|n:3ef5c17b0d0d818da9d5efda1a443166}} (31 min read): refusal and over-refusal, jailbreaks, honesty, dangerous capabilities, scheming. Optimisers exploiting proxies: {{Reward Hacking|n:3c65c17b0d0d8196a6f1f29a817aa974}}.

Human preference and instruction following

Which answer do people prefer? Arena (LMArena until its renaming on 28 January 2026: {{Wikipedia|https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arena_(AI_platform)}}) fits Bradley-Terry (Elo-style) ratings to anonymous pairwise votes: live, contamination-free, and a measure of preference, not capability. Its text board held 413 models on 2 October 2026 ({{Arena|https://arena.ai/leaderboard/text}}). Votes reward confident, well-formatted, flattering answers, and style rivals substance (the 2025 GPT-4o sycophancy rollback showed the cost of optimising for approval), so style control is now the default view, with a factuality toggle since 14 July 2026 ({{Arena|https://arena.ai/blog/factuality-in-arena}}); Agent Arena (June 2026) ranks agents by causal tracing on real sessions, not votes. Arena-Hard and AlpacaEval swap voters for an LLM judge. Instruction following is checked mechanically: IFEval's verifiable constraints, MultiChallenge's multi-turn ones.

Active: Arena (with category boards, style control and factuality), MultiChallenge and IFBench. Saturated: IFEval. Dormant or retired: Arena-Hard v2 (newest entries April 2025), AlpacaEval 2, MT-Bench.

Go deeper: {{Human preference and arenas|n:3ef5c17b0d0d814db49ff3b5c9fc3034}} (25 min read): the Bradley-Terry fit, style control and factuality, the judge-graded stand-ins, and instruction following (IFEval, IFBench, MultiChallenge). Judges in general: {{Topic: evaluation-and-llm-judges|n:3c65c17b0d0d8181b351e1b8fc6a1546}}.

Multilingual

Does capability survive outside English? A translated English test measures translation as much as knowledge, so newer sets are culturally aware. Machine translation keeps chrF and BLEU on FLORES-200's 200 languages.

Active: FLORES-200. Saturating: Global-MMLU, MMMLU, INCLUDE. Saturated: MGSM.

Go deeper: no page of its own; the multilingual knowledge sets are on {{Knowledge and reasoning benchmarks|n:3c65c17b0d0d810f8574da3ffb860be5}} (33 min read), translation only here and in the {{Benchmark atlas|#t-atlas}}.

Tool use

Can the model pick the right tool and fill its arguments? BFCL began with function-call correctness and became agentic in v4; MCP-era suites call real Model Context Protocol servers rather than mocks. Young and fragmented, and its dedicated boards went quiet in 2026: tool use is now mostly measured inside agent benchmarks.

Still reported: τ²-bench (saturating). Dormant boards: BFCL v4 (last update 12 April 2026), MCP-Universe, MCPMark, MCP-Bench.

Go deeper: no page of its own; BFCL and the MCP evals are covered on {{Agentic benchmarks|n:3c65c17b0d0d811fb926d7b91ef8234e}} (35 min read).

Before these came the classic era, now historical: ImageNet (2009), SQuAD's reading comprehension, GLUE and SuperGLUE in the BERT years.
