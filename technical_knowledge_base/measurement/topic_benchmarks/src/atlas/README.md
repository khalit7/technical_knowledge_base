# Benchmark atlas (tab `t-atlas`, ids `at-`)

## Files
- `lib.py`: row schema and helpers. `EP(...)` and `GRID(...)` mark readings that `mk_atlas.py` copies from Epoch AI's hub CSVs and the Topic: llms grid (`models_and_training/topic_llms/src/data/bench_grid.json`), so those numbers are never retyped.
- `rows_kr.py`, `rows_mc.py`, `rows_ag.py`, `rows_ot.py`: the rows, by owning child page (knowledge and reasoning, math and coding, agentic, and the families no child owns yet).
- `rows_fix.py`: the readings and corrections from the 4 October 2026 research passes (official leaderboards and data files fetched that day) and the orchestrator's cross-checks against the other tabs. Applied last.
- `mk_atlas.py`: assembles and validates, then writes `../data/atlas.json` (for other tabs: `rows[].id`, `n`, `yr`, `fam`, `st`, `ev[0]`) and `../parts/32_js_atlas_a.js`. It fails on unknown enums, unknown sources, unverified arXiv ids, and quotes not found in their source.
- `verify_arxiv.py`: arXiv export API check of every cited paper (title, first-version date, abstract) into `inputs/arxiv_verified.json`.
- `check_quotes.py <cache dir>`: checks quotes that are not in an abstract against the arXiv full text or the fetched page, into `inputs/quote_checks.json`.
- `epoch_frontier.py <unzipped dir>`: top five rows per Epoch CSV into `inputs/epoch_frontier.json` (from https://epoch.ai/data/benchmark_data.zip, CC BY 4.0, downloaded 2026-10-04).
- `fetch_anim_inputs.py`: SWE-bench Verified issue dates (Hugging Face datasets-server) and LiveCodeBench release windows (README) into `inputs/anim_inputs.json`.
- `parse_old.py`: the old page's master table, verbatim, from `../live.md` into `inputs/old_table.json`.
- `check_atlas.mjs`: clicks every control at 390 px dark and 920 px light (chips on and off, search, owner, corrections filter, every sort, every row, ticks, presets, every correction link, every map chip, every defence dot, animation modes, steps, play, scrub); fails on errors, NaN, undefined or null text, and sideways scroll.
- `viz_ideas.md`: visuals built and rejected.

Rebuild: `python3 mk_atlas.py && sh ../build.sh`. Check: `node check_atlas.mjs` and `sh ../../../../html_utils/checkpage.sh ..`.

Status counts at build: 32 active, 17 saturating, 21 saturated, 24 retired; 90 rows carry at least one dated reading (152 readings), the other four rest on stated absence.

Definitions (shown in the legend): active = still separates frontier models or refreshes; saturating = top clustered near the ceiling but still separating the tier below; saturated = top at the ceiling or inside the answer key's noise; retired = replaced or no longer reported on frontier cards (several "retired" rows are dormant leaderboards, said in the row). "Contaminated" is a flag with its evidence, not a status.
