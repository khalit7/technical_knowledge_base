Best resources

{{Lost in the Middle (Liu et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172}} (40 min)

The position experiment, with full tables in the appendix.

{{RULER (Hsieh et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654}} (45 min)

The 13 tasks, the effective-length rule, the Yi-34B analysis; the {{repository|https://github.com/NVIDIA/RULER}} has the board.

{{NoLiMa (Modarressi et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05167}} (40 min)

Literal matching measured and removed; the {{README|https://github.com/adobe-research/NoLiMa}} carries later models and NoLiMa-Hard.

{{Michelangelo (Vodrahalli et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.12640}} (40 min)

Latent structure queries and the original MRCR.

{{HELMET (Yen et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02694}} (45 min)

Why synthetic tasks do not predict applications, from 59 models.

{{BABILong (Kuratov et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10149}} (40 min)

Reasoning over facts scattered in up to 10M tokens.

{{LongBench v2 (Bai et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15204}} (35 min)

Realistic long-context questions with a timed human baseline; {{leaderboard|https://longbench2.github.io}}.

{{OpenAI MRCR dataset card|https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/mrcr}} (5 min)

Construction, grader code, bins and the December 2025 fix; with {{Context Arena|https://contextarena.ai/?needles=8}} for independent runs.

{{Long context prompting for Claude 2.1 (Anthropic)|https://claude.com/blog/claude-2-1-prompting}} (5 min)

How one sentence moved a needle score from 27% to 98%.

{{GDP.pdf (Garre et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11192}} (35 min)

Long-document, multimodal professional tasks with all-pass rubric grading.

{{MMMU (Yue et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16502}} (40 min)

Construction, human experts and the text-only baselines.

{{Are We on the Right Way for Evaluating LVLMs? (MMStar)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.20330}} (35 min)

Blind answerability and multimodal leakage measured across six benchmarks.

{{MMMU-Pro (Yue et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02813}} (30 min)

Filtering, ten options and the vision-only setting.

{{CharXiv (Wang et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18521}} (30 min)

Why templated chart sets overstated progress.

{{ZeroBench (Roberts et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09696}} (25 min)

An "impossible" visual benchmark and a year of progress on it.

{{Video-MME-v2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05015}} (40 min)

Grouped, non-linear scoring and blind checks for video.

{{MMAU (Sakshi et al.)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.19168}} (30 min)

The main audio understanding benchmark; check the test-set version on the {{project page|https://sakshi113.github.io/mmau_homepage/}}.
