How to read a reported score

What a claim needs
Long context: the benchmark and version, the length bin, the number of needles or the task, the threshold if it is an effective length, and who ran it. "1M context" on a model card is the API limit; "effective to 16K on NoLiMa" and "63.5% on 8-needle MRCR at 1M (Context Arena)" are measurements. Multimodal: the split, the same model's blind score, the yes-rate or circular score where it applies, and for video the frames, subtitles and audio.

Then the item count: a 95% binomial half-width, 1.96·√(p(1−p)/n), says how far apart two scores must be before they are separated. On 100 items (AA-LCR, GDP.pdf) it is ±6 to ±9 points, so a two-point lead is not a ranking. And vendor and independent figures never sit on one axis: on 8-needle MRCR at 1M the best vendor figure and the best independent run differ by 33 points (different models: the vendor's is not yet independently listed).
