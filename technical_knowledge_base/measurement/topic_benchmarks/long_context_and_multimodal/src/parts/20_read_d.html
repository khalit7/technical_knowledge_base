Common mistakes

Reading the context window as capability
The window is what the API accepts. RULER, NoLiMa and MRCR all find effective lengths far below it: GPT-4.1's 1M window was effective to 16K on NoLiMa.
Quoting a needle heatmap as long-context evidence
Single-needle retrieval is a string match and was near perfect by 2024; it also moved from 27% to 98% for Claude 2.1 with one prompt sentence. Use a test without lexical overlap or with several identical needles.
Comparing "effective lengths" under different rules
RULER's is absolute (85.6%), NoLiMa's and BABILong's relative (85% of the model's own short-context score). The same model can be effective to 64K under one and 2K under the other; the rule is part of the number.
Splicing MRCR versions or variants
Google's Michelangelo MRCR, OpenAI's MRCR before 5 December 2025 and after, 2, 4 or 8 needles, and each length bin are different numbers. A vendor's best bin is not a model's 1M score.
Ignoring who ran a long-context number
8-needle MRCR at 1M: 96.3% in OpenAI's own report against 63.5% for the best independently run model. GDP.pdf: 34.2% and 32.2% for the same model under two harnesses.
Assuming the image was used
Language models answer a fifth or more of MMMU without the image, and vision models trained on leaked items do better still. A multimodal score means little without the same model's blind score beside it.
Comparing MMMU validation with test, or MMMU with MMMU-Pro
Validation (900, answers always public) tops at 85.4%; the test top was 65.9% before its answers were released in February 2026. MMMU-Pro is a different, harder set scored as the mean of two settings.
Treating a video score as a model property
Frames sampled, resolution, subtitles and audio are harness choices. Video-MME-v2's best model moves from 38.2 to 49.4 just by adding audio.
Reading small gaps on small sets
AA-LCR and GDP.pdf have 100 items each: ±6 to ±9 points at 95%. A two-point lead is not a ranking.
