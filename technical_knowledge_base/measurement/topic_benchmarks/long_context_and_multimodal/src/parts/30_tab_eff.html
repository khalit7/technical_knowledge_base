Effective length: advertised against measured

An "effective context length" turns a score-by-length curve into one number: the longest tested length at which the model still clears a threshold. Two published rules use different thresholds. RULER's is absolute: beat Llama 2 7B at 4K, 85.6% ({{Hsieh et al., Table 3|https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06654}}). NoLiMa's is relative: stay above 85% of the model's own short-context base score ({{Modarressi et al., Tables 3 and 10|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05167}}). Here are all 17 RULER rows and all 22 NoLiMa rows, with the claimed window beside the measured one. Change the rule and the threshold and watch the effective lengths, and the ranking, move.

Try: on NoLiMa, switch to the absolute rule at 85.6%: GPT-4o keeps 8K, but Claude 3.5 Sonnet falls below 1K and Gemini 2.0 Flash from 4K to 2K, because their base scores were lower. On RULER, the relative rule at 85% of each model's 4K score lifts the weak-but-flat LWM from under 4K to 16K and lets GLM4 clear every tested length. Click rows to compare their curves below.
