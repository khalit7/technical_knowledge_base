Fit the votes

Six thousand real votes from the 2024 arena, among ten models, and the arena's own rating machinery to run on them. Fit Bradley-Terry with or without style control, switch to online Elo and change K or the order of the votes, drop ties instead of counting them as half, use fewer votes, and compare sandwich intervals with a bootstrap. The orange tick on each row is the rating LMArena itself published for that model from all of its votes (board of 27 August 2024), so you can see how far a sample of 6,000 gets.

Try: tick all four style features: gpt-4o-mini falls from 4th to 7th and Llama 3.1 70B from 6th to 8th while Claude 3.5 Sonnet and Claude 3 Opus each rise two places, as on the full board. Then Online Elo with K = 32 and "shuffled": press reshuffle a few times and watch the ranks swap. Then cut the votes to 1,000 and see the intervals widen about two and a half times (√6).
