7. Aggregate indices, and preference against capability

An aggregate index is the other single number people quote. Artificial Analysis runs many answer-keyed evaluations itself, in its own harness, and combines them into the Intelligence Index: v4.3 includes, among others, Humanity's Last Exam, CritPt, AA-Omniscience, Terminal-Bench 4.0, GDPval-AA and AutomationBench-AA. Where an arena averages taste over users' prompts, an index averages correctness over a fixed suite, so its number is tied to its version: v4.2 (4 September 2026) removed GPQA Diamond, and v4.3 (7 September 2026) moved Terminal-Bench from 2.1 to 4.0, replaced τ³-Banking with AutomationBench-AA and raised the weight of privately held tests from 40% to 45% (versions as recorded in {{Topic: llm-landscape|n:3c65c17b0d0d812d9e00f6ec89965286}}'s snapshot of 1 October 2026, from {{Artificial Analysis|https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology/intelligence-benchmarking}}). Never compare index values across versions. Capability against price across labs is the landscape page's; here the question is only how the two kinds of single number relate.

Do they rank models alike? Across 35 models matched on both, Arena text score (style control, 2 October 2026) and the Intelligence Index v4.3 (1 October 2026) correlate with Spearman 0.71 (0.76 on hard prompts); restricted to the 25 with the same reasoning effort on both boards, 0.60; among the 19 frontier models with an index of 40 or more, only 0.33 derived.
