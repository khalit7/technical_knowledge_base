3. Style control and factuality: lenses on the same votes

Concrete first. On the 1.76 million votes of August 2024, one standard deviation of relative length is worth odds of e0.249 = 1.28 in a vote, a 56.2% win chance between otherwise equal answers derived. Is a model ahead because its answers are better, or because they are longer and bolder? Style control answers that inside the same regression: add columns for the difference in style between the two answers, so style gets its own coefficient and each model's rating is what is left ({{Arena, 29 Aug 2024|https://arena.ai/blog/style-control}}).

The formula, in the same names:

logit P(A wins) = ln10 · (RA − RB) / 400 + γ · z
z
four style features, each the difference over the sum, (xA − xB) / (xA + xB + 1), standardised over all votes: answer tokens, markdown headers, list items, bold spans (FastChat's STYLE_CONTROL_ELEMENTS_V1); the ratio makes 500 against 520 tokens nearly equal and 20 against 40 very different
γ
the style coefficients, fitted with the ratings; a small ridge penalty (0.5) as in FastChat
Reproduced

This page's fit gives γ = 0.249 for length, 0.024 for headers, 0.031 for lists and 0.019 for bold, the values LMArena published (0.249, 0.024, 0.031, 0.019), and 0.268 for length alone (published 0.267) reproduced. The ratings match LMArena's style-controlled board of 27 August 2024 to a mean of 1.1 points, and within 1.4 points for the top 20. Length dominates; markdown is second order.

Default since 16 May 2025. Style control became the default for the text and vision boards, with the offset adjusted so both views share a scale ({{leaderboard-dataset|https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset}}). It is observational: a longer answer may also be a better one (a worked derivation is both), and LMArena's post says as much ("possible unobserved confounders such as positive correlation between length and substantive quality").

Factuality is the second lens (14 July 2026). Arena samples battles, extracts web-verifiable atomic claims from both answers, has search agents assign calibrated truth probabilities, and turns the gap in average truthfulness into a soft "factuality vote"; a composite Bradley-Terry fit weights those votes 25% and human votes 75%, as a non-default toggle on the Text and Search boards ({{Arena|https://arena.ai/blog/factuality-in-arena}}). Over 2 million claims labelled, it found a "weak positive correlation" between preference and factuality, OpenAI and xAI models rising with the factuality weight, Claude and Gemini falling or flat, and Muse Spark 1.1 and ERNIE 5.1 dropping heavily. The factuality judge is itself a model; its calibration belongs to {{LLM-as-judge|n:3c65c17b0d0d810ba2a2ecc08c4c233b}}.
