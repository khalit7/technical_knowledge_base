XSTest, every prompt

All 450 XSTest prompts with the real answers of five model configurations released by the authors ({{paul-rottger/xstest|https://github.com/paul-rottger/xstest}}, prompts CC BY 4.0; read 4 October 2026), labelled three ways: by two human annotators (the final_label column), by the repository's string-match rule, and by its GPT-4 classifier. The Reading tab animates the two ends; this tab lets you check any cell.

Try: switch the grader to string match and watch Mistral's unsafe rows get worse (its refusals in its own words read as answers) while Llama 2's safe rows get better (partial refusals read as answers); then filter the list to prompts where the five configurations disagree.
