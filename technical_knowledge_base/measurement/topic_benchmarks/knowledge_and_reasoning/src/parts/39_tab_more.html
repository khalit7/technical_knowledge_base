Best resources

{{MMLU paper (Hendrycks et al., 2020)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03300}} (45 min)

Construction, the 57 subjects and the human baselines.

{{MMLU-Pro (Wang et al., 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01574}} (45 min)

Filtering, ten options, CoT against direct answering.

{{"Are We Done with MMLU?" (MMLU-Redux, 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04127}} (45 min)

The error taxonomy, 5,700 re-annotated questions, ranking changes; {{dataset|https://huggingface.co/datasets/edinburgh-dawg/mmlu-redux-2.0}}.

{{GPQA paper (Rein et al., 2023)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.12022}} (45 min)

The expert and non-expert validation pipeline; {{Epoch AI GPQA Diamond tracker|https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/gpqa-diamond}} (about 10 min).

{{HLE paper (Phan et al., 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14249}} (45 min)

Construction, the judge prompt, calibration, expert disagreement (appendix B.3). Leaderboards: {{lastexam.ai|https://lastexam.ai}} and {{Scale Labs|https://labs.scale.com/leaderboard/humanitys_last_exam}} (about 10 min each).

{{FutureHouse: about 30% of HLE chemistry and biology answers are likely wrong|https://www.futurehouse.org/research-announcements/hle-exam}} (about 15 min)

The audit, its examples and the HLE team's follow-up.

{{HLE-Verified (2026)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13964}} (45 min)

Component-wise verification and repair of all 2,500 items, and the re-evaluation; {{HLE-Diamond|https://lastexam.ai/blog/hle-diamond}} (about 5 min).

{{SimpleQA (Wei et al., 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04368}} (25 min)

Three-way grading, F-score, calibration; {{SimpleQA Verified|https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07968}} (25 min).

{{BIG-Bench Hard|https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.09261}} and {{BBEH|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.19187}} (30 min each)

Chain of thought as the protocol fix; harmonic-mean scoring.

{{On the Measure of Intelligence (Chollet, 2019)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.01547}} (90 min, 60+ pages)

The argument behind ARC: intelligence as skill-acquisition efficiency.

{{ARC-AGI-2 paper (2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11831}} (30 min)

ARC-AGI-1's history and limits, human calibration, prize rules.

{{ARC-AGI-3 technical report|https://arcprize.org/media/ARC_AGI_3_Technical_Report.pdf}} (40 min)

The game format, action space and the RHAE metric; {{ARC Prize site|https://arcprize.org}} (about 20 min) for rules and leaderboards.

{{GPT-6 Astra on ARC-AGI-3: two harnesses|https://arcprize.org/blog/astra}} (about 10 min)

ARC Prize on why the standard and provider-adapter harnesses are both published and never merged.

{{44% on ARC-AGI-1 in 67 cents|https://mvakde.github.io/blog/44-on-arc-1/}} (20 min)

A small transformer trained from scratch at test time, with ablations and its critics answered.

{{Expert re-grading of six physics benchmarks (2026)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13009}} (25 min)

How much of a benchmark residual is item defect rather than model failure.

{{Global-MMLU|https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03304}} and {{INCLUDE|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.19799}} (40 min each)

Cultural bias in translated MMLU, and knowledge from local exams in 44 languages.
