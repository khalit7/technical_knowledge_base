How far to trust it: Believe the allocation: parameters and tokens should grow together, about 20 tokens per parameter at these scales. Two independent methods in the paper agree, the 70B-against-280B test confirms it, and every replication since lands near 0.5 (Approach 2 on points read off the paper's own figure gives 0.51 here). Do not reuse the Approach 3 constants (E 1.69, A 406.4, B 410.7, α 0.34, β 0.28) or its intervals: the optimiser stopped early, and a correct fit gives a different law. The benchmark wins come from one pair of runs, with no seeds. The evidence, judged.
