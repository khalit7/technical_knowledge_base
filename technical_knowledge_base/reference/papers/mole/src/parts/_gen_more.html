MOLE: Detecting Insider Threats in AI Agents (arXiv HTML v1)

Preprint (cs.LG, cs.CL, cs.CR), the paper itself; the HTML version has the anchors this page links to. abstract page, PDF.

(1h 10m)

The simulator, the nine service backends, the twelve threat definitions with their personas, the monitors and the evaluation scripts. Facade: High-Precision Insider Threat Detection Using Deep Contextual Anomaly Detection

Kantchelian et al., Google, 2024: The production insider-threat detector MOLE uses as its strongest classical baseline, and the source of the ten-reviews-a-day budget.

(40 min)

AI Control: Improving Safety Despite Intentional Subversion

Greenblatt et al., Redwood Research, 2023: Where trusted-monitor protocols and the weaker-model-watches-stronger-model framing come from; MOLE's semantic monitor is the same idea applied to account-days.

(45 min)

Agentic Misalignment: How LLMs Could Be Insider Threats

Lynch et al., Anthropic, 2025: The red-team study MOLE cites for models choosing blackmail and exfiltration on their own; the useful contrast is that MOLE assigns the objective instead.

(25 min)

Chain of Thought Monitorability: A New and Fragile Opportunity for AI Safety

Korbak et al., 2025: Why the paper reports monitors with and without agent reasoning: reasoning may be unavailable or unfaithful in deployment.

(30 min)
