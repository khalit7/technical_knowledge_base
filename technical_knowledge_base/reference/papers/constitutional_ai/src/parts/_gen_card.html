In one line: Trains a harmless, non-evasive assistant with zero human harmlessness labels: critique-and-revise SFT against a written constitution, then RLAIF with AI preference labels distilled into a preference model, Pareto-dominating RLHF.

How far to trust it: Believe the mechanism and the qualitative findings: AI labels can stand in for human harmlessness labels, and the method removes canned refusals (in the released samples, 0 of 2,244 RL-CAI answers against 34% of HH RLHF's). Treat the size of the gain over RLHF as provisional: one run per setup, Elo judged by crowdworkers told to penalise exactly the evasion the RLHF baseline was trained into, no numbers printed in the text, and nothing outside Anthropic can rerun it.
