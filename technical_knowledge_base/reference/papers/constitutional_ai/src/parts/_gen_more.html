Connected papers in this knowledge base

InstructGPT

2022: the RLHF recipe CAI extends; CAI swaps the human preference labels for AI labels (harmlessness only) and keeps the preference model and RL machinery.

DPO

2023: removes the preference model and RL entirely; modern pipelines often feed CAI-style AI preference data to DPO-style direct optimisation.

DeepSeekMath (GRPO)

2024: the RL-for-LLMs line that CAI's RLAIF stage belongs to.

DeepSeek-R1

2025: replaces preference rewards with verifiable rewards, the other route around human labels.

GPT-3

2020: the few-shot prompting that every stage of CAI relies on to keep the model's output in format.
