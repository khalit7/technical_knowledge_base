Learn What's Left, Not What's Mastered: Saturation Aware Advantage Reweighting for Multi-Reward Policy Optimization (SA-MRPO)

Yixuan Wang*, Yifei Chen*, Haichao Zhang, Haozheng Luo, Xander Wu, Jie Ni, Yun Fu, Nuno Vasconcelos, Yijiang Li† (* equal contribution; † corresponding author and project lead)
University of Florida, UC San Diego, Northeastern University, Northwestern University, Stanford University, Zillion Network, Universität Innsbruck · 17 August 2026 (arXiv v1, the only version)

In one line: Drop-in advantage reweighting for multi-reward GRPO/RLVR: normalise each objective per group, then down-weight it by (1 - batch saturation)^γ so the update goes to objectives with headroom left; it beats GDPO (its γ = 0 case) on 12 of 15 math comparisons and by 3.8 points on average in adaptive reasoning, with γ = 0.5 best in the ablation.

How far to trust it: Believe the mechanism: the arithmetic is right and Figure 1 recomputes exactly. Treat the gains as unproven: every number is one training run with no error bars, several margins are inside the evaluation noise of 30- and 40-problem benchmarks, γ was chosen on the test sets, and the obvious rival, GDPO with a smaller fixed weight on the easy objective, is never run. In this page's toy that rival did as well or better. The evidence, judged.
