Best resources

GDPO: Group reward-Decoupled Normalization Policy Optimization for Multi-reward RL Optimization

Liu et al. (NVIDIA), 2026: the baseline and the γ = 0 case: why summing rewards before normalising collapses their resolution, and its own study of fixed reward weights and conditioned rewards (Section 3.2 and 4.2.1), the comparison this paper skips.

(45 min)

NVlabs/GDPO repository

NVIDIA: the open GDPO implementations (verl, TRL, NeMo RL; Apache-2.0) that SA-MRPO would be added to.

(20 min)

DVAO: Dynamic Variance-Adaptive Advantage Optimization for Multi-Reward RL

Jiang et al., 2026: a concurrent rival that adapts objective weights by reward variance rather than saturation.

(45 min)

GD2PO: Group-Dynamic Reward-Decoupled Policy Optimization

Liu et al., 2026: a concurrent rival that handles conflicts between reward-specific advantages.

(45 min)

A Sober Look at Progress in Language Model Reasoning

Hochlehnert et al., 2025: why single-seed gains of a few points on AIME-sized benchmarks need error bars before they are believed.

(40 min)

Policy Gradient Algorithms (RLHF Book)

Nathan Lambert: GRPO's group-normalised advantage in context, the quantity this paper reweights.
