Noise: each held-out gain against its standard error

Binomial standard error of a difference between two independent runs, √(p0(1 − p0)/n + p1(1 − p1)/n), from the benchmark sizes in Appendix A. Pairing on the same tasks would shrink it by an amount the paper does not report; trials of one Terminal-Bench task are not independent, which would widen it. The bar is the gain, the black mark the standard error. Evolve splits are shown for scale but are selected, so their gains are biased upward.
