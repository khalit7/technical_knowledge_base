Connected papers in this knowledge base

The Llama 3 Herd of Models

2024: the open-weights recipe OLMo 2 benchmarks against; annealing as a data test, and the RoPE θ = 500,000 OLMo 2 copies.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

2024: the other big open-weights recipe of the same month, MoE and FP8, with weights but not data.

Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models (Chinchilla)

2022: compute-optimal background; OLMo 2 trains far past it (the 7B sees about 550 tokens per parameter).

DeepSeekMath (GRPO)

2024: GRPO, the RL algorithm OLMo 2 used for RLVR at 1B and 32B.

Direct Preference Optimization

2023: the preference stage of the Tülu 3 recipe.

Training language models to follow instructions (InstructGPT)

2022: the PPO pipeline whose value-function and KL machinery RLVR reuses.

RoFormer (RoPE)

2021: rotary position embeddings, used here with θ = 500,000.

ZeRO

2019: the sharded data parallelism (FSDP) lineage behind OLMo-core.

Megatron-LM

2019: model parallelism; also where the reordered-LayerNorm question first came up for BERT.

Qwen3 Technical Report

2025: the concurrent work whose GQA choice inspired the 32B; v3 of this paper adds Qwen 3 base rows to Table 6.

WSM: Decay-Free Learning Rate Schedule via Checkpoint Merging

2025: later work that replaces the learning-rate decay itself with checkpoint merging, the two levers this paper combines.
