Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (arXiv HTML v4)

NeurIPS 2020 (Outstanding Paper award), the paper itself (75 pages (40 + 32 of appendices); very long, read §1, §2, §3.9, §4 and §5 first); the HTML version has the anchors this page links to. abstract page, PDF.

Calibrate Before Use: Improving Few-Shot Performance of Language Models

Zhao et al., 2021: the paper's missing error bar: on GPT-3 2.7B, the order of the same four examples moves SST-2 accuracy from 54.3% to 93.4%.

(40 min)

Rethinking the Role of Demonstrations: What Makes In-Context Learning Work?

Min et al., 2022: evidence on the paper's open question: random labels in the examples barely hurt, so the examples mostly teach format and label space.

(40 min)

In-context Learning and Induction Heads

Olsson et al., Anthropic, 2022: a candidate mechanism for in-context learning, from several of GPT-3's authors.

(1h)

Are Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models a Mirage?

Schaeffer, Miranda, Koyejo, 2023: re-scores the GPT-3 family's arithmetic with continuous metrics and argues the sharp jumps come from exact-match scoring.

(40 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Attention Is All You Need

2017: GPT-3 is the decoder half of the Transformer scaled about three orders of magnitude.

Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models

January 2020, the direct motivation (Kaplan and McCandlish are authors of both): GPT-3 extends the loss power law two more orders of magnitude and sized its models by it.

Chinchilla

2022: 175B parameters on 300B tokens is heavily undertrained; rebalanced the parameters-to-tokens ratio GPT-3 popularised.

BERT

2018: the rival paradigm (bidirectional, fine-tuned) this paper displaced; the authors blame part of GPT-3's comparison-task failures on lacking bidirectionality.

T5

2019: seven months earlier and pointing the other way, fine-tuning an encoder-decoder; T5-11B is the closed-book QA and compute baseline here.

InstructGPT

2022: RLHF on GPT-3 to fix the instruction-following and alignment gaps documented here.

Megatron-LM

2019: tensor parallelism within each matrix multiply, one of the two model-parallel axes GPT-3 used.

ZeRO

2019: the memory optimisations that make 100B-plus dense training feasible.
