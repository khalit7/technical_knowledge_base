Best resources

Φ-Bench leaderboard

the authors, faibench.org: the paper's tables with four decimals, a per-topic breakdown, a high-effort leaderboard the paper does not print, the effort study (labelled 30 tasks), and per-round trajectories for all 30 LHI and E2EO tasks; it also states the score-selection rules quoted on this page.

the authors, in the released package: the reward curve with worked anchors, the AB-BA measurement, the zeroing gates, the submission budget per subset and format, and the warning not to average performance and implementation tasks together.

ISO-Bench: Can Coding Agents Optimize Real-World Inference Workloads?

FlashInfer-Bench

Cohere: megakernels for serving
