Rerun the tournament

The chatbot results rest on judgments the authors released: every GPT-4 verdict on the Vicuna and OA benchmarks and every Mechanical Turk vote ({{artidoro/qlora/eval|https://github.com/artidoro/qlora/tree/main/eval}}). They are all on this page, so the Elo tournament of §5.2 can be replayed here, with its knobs, and with the uncertainty the paper did not report.

Try: replay once with one ordering (the ratings jump by tens of points between runs: that is the "ordering effect" the 10,000 replays average away); then resample the prompts (the honest interval); then keep only the matches where the stronger system's answer was shown second, or drop the coding and math prompts.
