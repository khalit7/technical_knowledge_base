Best resources

Making LLMs even more accessible with bitsandbytes, 4-bit quantization and QLoRA

Younes Belkada, Tim Dettmers, Artidoro Pagnoni, Sylvain Gugger, Sourab Mangrulkar (Hugging Face), 24 May 2023: the canonical practitioner guide, co-written with the paper authors; covers NF4, double quantization and the transformers and PEFT API (BitsAndBytesConfig, load_in_4bit).

(25 min)

A Visual Guide to Quantization

Maarten Grootendorst: the best visual intuition for absmax and blockwise quantisation, NF4, and where QLoRA sits among GPTQ and AWQ style methods.

(30 min)

Practical Tips for Finetuning LLMs Using LoRA

Sebastian Raschka: hands-on hyperparameter guidance (r, alpha, target modules, memory trade-offs) that reflects the paper's adapters-on-all-layers finding, with a measured QLoRA memory and runtime trade-off.

(30 min)

Finetuning LLMs with LoRA and QLoRA: Insights from Hundreds of Experiments

Sebastian Raschka, Lightning AI, 12 October 2023: empirical study of QLoRA's memory savings against runtime cost and quality across settings.

(30 min)

NF4 Isn't Information Theoretically Optimal (and that's Good)

Davis Yoshida, June 2023: a short note showing that absmax blocks change the distribution NF4 is built for, and the AF4 code it proposes (both are on the quantiser tab).

(15 min)

Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena

Lianmin Zheng et al., June 2023: the systematic study of position, verbosity and self-enhancement bias in GPT-4 judges that QLoRA's §6.2 anticipated.

(40 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models

the adapter method QLoRA builds on, with a LoRA you can train in the browser; QLoRA's contribution is making the frozen base 4-bit and showing that all-layer adapters are needed at scale.

Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback (InstructGPT)

the instruction-tuning paradigm Guanaco applies with supervised finetuning only.

Direct Preference Optimization

contemporaneous preference-tuning method; the common open-source recipe became QLoRA plus DPO.

ZeRO: Memory Optimizations Toward Training Trillion Parameter Models

the bytes-per-parameter accounting (weights, gradients, optimizer states) behind the 780 GB of 16-bit finetuning.

Efficient Memory Management for LLM Serving with PagedAttention

paging applied to the KV cache at serving time; QLoRA pages optimizer states during training.

Meta: Llama and Meta Superintelligence Labs

the LLaMA base models every Guanaco is built on.
