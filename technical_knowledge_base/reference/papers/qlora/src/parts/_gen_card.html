How far to trust it: Believe the method: NF4 is the best of the 4-bit types tested (we reproduce the ordering on five small open models and the exact NF4 values from the code), the memory arithmetic holds, and the recipe has been the default way to finetune on one GPU since. Read the chatbot claims as rough: 99.3% of ChatGPT is a GPT-4 score on 80 prompts, its interval is about ±3, the Elo intervals of ±1 measure only the order of matches (resampling the prompts gives about ±40), and the 16-bit match is shown at 65B only against 16-bit LoRA, not full finetuning.
