Connected papers in this knowledge base

Training Compute-Optimal Large Language Models (Chinchilla)

2022: the compute-optimal framework Llama 3 extends; its IsoFLOPs method is the one used here, and the 8B and 70B are deliberately trained far past it.

Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models

2020: the ancestor of the scaling-law method; Llama 3 adds a second stage from loss to benchmark accuracy.

Direct Preference Optimization

2023: the alignment loss Llama 3 scales up, with formatting tokens masked and an NLL term added.

InstructGPT

2022: the PPO-based RLHF pipeline Llama 3 explicitly moves away from.

PagedAttention (vLLM)

2023: used here to more than double rejection-sampling throughput.

RoFormer (RoPE)

2021: rotary position embeddings, with the base raised to 500,000 here for long context.

Megatron-LM

2019: the tensor parallelism that is the innermost of the four dimensions.

ZeRO

2019: the sharded data parallelism that FSDP implements, the outermost dimension.

FlashAttention

2022: the attention kernel family that makes 128K-token training affordable.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

December 2024: the counterpoint, mixture of experts, FP8 training and RL-heavy post-training, against Llama 3's dense-and-simple bet.
