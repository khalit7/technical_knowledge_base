The paper's tables, rebuilt

The tables that carry the argument, transcribed from the arXiv HTML by mk_tables.py with the printed precision kept, and every derived number recomputed by recompute.py. Where a number does not reproduce, the box says so.

Table 2: the instruction-tuned models on key benchmarks

Bold: the best in the size class as printed. Shaded: within the noise of the Llama model's score (the gap is smaller than √(h₁² + h₂²), the interval of a difference). Intervals: printed in {{Table 18|ax:S5.T18}} (code), {{Table 21|ax:S5.T21}} (long context) and {{Table 22|ax:S5.T22}} (tool use), otherwise computed with the paper's formula 1.96 √(S(1 − S)/N) from benchmark sizes (MMLU 14,042; MMLU-Pro 12,032; IFEval 541; GSM8K 1,319; MATH 5,000; ARC-C 1,172; GPQA 448 assumed; MGSM 2,750). Marks after a score: △ 5-shot without CoT, ⊲ without CoT, ♢ zero-shot. The formula reproduces the paper's printed intervals (ARC-C 96.1 ± 1.1, GSM8K 89.0 ± 1.7, MATH 53.8 ± 1.4 in Table 12; HumanEval 89.0 ± 4.8 in Table 18) to 0.01, which checks the benchmark sizes.

Table 3: hyperparameters, and the parameter count recounted

Per layer: attention 2d2 + 2 × d × 8 × 128 (query and output full width; keys and values for 8 heads of 128), SwiGLU 3 × d × dff, two RMSNorm vectors; plus untied embedding and output matrices of 128,256 × d and a final norm. Reproduces 8B, 70B and 405B independently: 8.03B, 70.55B, 405.85B.

Tables 6 and 7: preference data and SFT data

Shares recompute to 100% in both tables. The SFT tokens column is derived: share of examples × average tokens per example, renormalised. Table 7's printed average of 846.1 tokens per example recomputes as from the rows.

Table 15: contamination by 8-gram overlap, and its estimated effect

Contam. %: share of each evaluation set flagged, at the overlap threshold that showed the largest significant estimated gain. Gain: the score on the full set minus the score on the clean part, per model size. Dashes: the paper excludes results that were not significant or behaved erratically; for HumanEval, MBPP, MMLU and MMLU-Pro 8-gram overlap flagged too much to estimate a gain ({{§5.1.4|ax:S5.SS1.SSS4}}).
