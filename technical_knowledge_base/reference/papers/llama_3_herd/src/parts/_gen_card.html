The Llama 3 Herd of Models (Llama 3)

Llama Team, AI @ Meta (Aaron Grattafiori, Abhimanyu Dubey, Abhinav Jauhri and about 550 more; the full list is the paper's appendix)
Meta · July 2024 (dated 23 July 2024; arXiv v1 31 July 2024, v3 23 November 2024)

In one line: Meta's full playbook for a GPT-4-class open model: 405B dense on 15.6T tokens (50/25/17/8 data mix, 3-stage recipe with 128K long-context extension and annealing), six rounds of RM + rejection sampling + SFT + DPO, on 16K H100s at 38-43% MFU with 419 unexpected interruptions in 54 days (78% hardware) yet >90% effective training time.

Verdict: Believe the engineering record: the cluster, the parallelism layout, the failure log and the recipe are the most detailed public account of a frontier-scale run, and its scaling-law forecast reproduces from its own figures. Read the benchmark claims as approximate: rivals' scores mix self-run and reported numbers, many gaps sit inside the noise, and the paper's own human evaluation has the 405B behind GPT-4o rather than level with it.
