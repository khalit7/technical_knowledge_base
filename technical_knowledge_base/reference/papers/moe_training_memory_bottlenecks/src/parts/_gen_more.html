Best resources

Least-Loaded Expert Parallelism (LLEP)

Nguyen, Pandit, Xu, Xiong, Joty, arXiv 2601.17111: the authors' earlier paper that PipelinedLLEP extends: reroute an overloaded expert's excess tokens, and its weights, to the least-loaded ranks.

(40 min)

Mixture-of-Parallelisms (MoP)

Nguyen, Pandit, Zhao, Yavuz, Savarese, Joty, arXiv 2607.01844: the rank layout the four operators are composed in; it already reports 4.7 to 8.2 times FSDP2's throughput and 1M-token context, which matters for reading this paper's end-to-end numbers.

(40 min)

Cut Your Losses in Large-Vocabulary Language Models (Cut Cross-Entropy)

Wijmans et al., ICLR 2025, arXiv 2411.09009: the single-device fused cross-entropy that never forms the logit matrix; Ring-DTP is its multi-rank counterpart.

(30 min)

Online normalizer calculation for softmax

Milakov and Gimelshein, 2018, arXiv 1805.02867: the three-scalar running maximum and sum that Ring-DTP folds strips into (Eq. 3), and that FlashAttention also rests on.

(15 min)

ZeRO-Offload: Democratizing Billion-Scale Model Training

Ren et al., USENIX ATC 2021, arXiv 2101.06840: the CPU optimizer offload and AVX CPU Adam that OffloadStreamAdamW replaces as the update engine.

(40 min)

The Ultra-Scale Playbook

Hugging Face (nanotron): the clearest walkthrough of activation recomputation, ZeRO, context and expert parallelism, the components this paper schedules around (the relevant sections).

(1h)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

ZeRO

2019: the 16 bytes per parameter and the sharding of model state; the composed stack uses ZeRO-3 for dense weights, and OffloadStreamAdamW speeds up its offload descendant.

Megatron-LM

2019: vocabulary-parallel cross-entropy shards the output weight but needs the same batch on every rank; Ring-DTP keeps the shard and lets each rank keep its own batch.

Switch Transformers

2021: the expert-parallel MoE layer whose dispatch all-to-all and routing skew PipelinedLLEP bounds; Switch dropped overflow tokens with a capacity factor, this paper drops none.

Mixtral of Experts

2024: a top-2 sparse MoE trained at 32K context; the kind of model whose dispatch and logits this paper's bounds are about.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

2024: the auxiliary-loss-free bias balancing used in this paper's end-to-end runs, and the fine-grained MoE shape (top-8 of many small experts) its benchmarks use.

FlashAttention

2022: the same online-softmax trick applied to attention; Ring-DTP applies it to the vocabulary projection across ranks.

PagedAttention

2023: another memory paper whose gain comes from when and where tensors live rather than from changing the arithmetic, at serving time.

QLoRA

2023: the approximate route (4-bit weights, paged 8-bit optimizer state) this paper deliberately excludes so its results stay comparable with full BF16 training.
