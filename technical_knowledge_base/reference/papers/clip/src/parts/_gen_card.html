Verdict: Believe the method and the headline numbers: they come from released models that later work has re-run widely, and open reproductions matched them. Read "contrastive is 4x more efficient than predicting the words" and "robust because zero-shot" as findings at the paper's scale and on its data: later work traced the robustness mainly to the diversity of the training data, and found captioning competitive for learning representations once data and compute are matched.
