Run a tiny CLIP

A real CLIP, built as §2.4 says and trained with Figure 3's loss, small enough to run in this page: a ViT image encoder (24 × 24 colour images in 4 × 4 patches, width 32, 2 layers, 4 heads, the extra LayerNorm before the Transformer) and a causal Transformer text encoder (width 32, 2 layers, feature at [EOS]), both projected to a 32-dimensional shared space; parameters in all. It was trained offline in PyTorch on a toy web of 120,000 (image, caption) pairs, and this page runs the forward pass in plain JavaScript on the shipped 6-bit weights.

The toy web: one shape (circle, square, triangle, diamond, ring, cross) in one of six colours, small or big, on the left, in the middle or on the right, either as a photo (filled, on a muted textured background) or as a drawing (an outline on white; 30% of the web). Each caption mentions only some of that, in one of many phrasings, often with filler: "i drew a orange ring today so cool", "it , green", "nice picture today". The generator is part of this page; the test images below are new draws from it that training never saw.
