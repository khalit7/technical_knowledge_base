Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks (RAG)

Patrick Lewis, Ethan Perez, Aleksandra Piktus, Fabio Petroni, Vladimir Karpukhin, Naman Goyal, Heinrich Küttler, Mike Lewis, Wen-tau Yih, Tim Rocktäschel, Sebastian Riedel, Douwe Kiela
Facebook AI Research; University College London; New York University · May 2020 (arXiv v1, 22 May 2020); v4 April 2021; published at NeurIPS 2020

In one line: Coined RAG: a DPR retriever and a BART generator trained end to end, the retrieved documents treated as a latent variable and marginalised (RAG-Sequence against RAG-Token); state of the art on open-domain QA, and knowledge updated by swapping the index, not the weights.
