Alibaba (Qwen Team) · May 2025 (arXiv v1 14 May 2025, the only version; models released 29 April 2025)

In one line: One open Apache 2.0 family (0.6B dense to 235B-A22B MoE) fusing thinking and non-thinking modes with a token-budget dial, trained on 36T tokens plus 4-stage post-training, with on-policy distillation beating RL for small models at 1/10 the GPU hours.

Verdict: Believe the recipe and the controls it ships: one checkpoint that obeys /think and /no_think, a budget that trades tokens for accuracy, small models distilled cheaply. Read the leaderboards as Qwen's own single runs without error bars, and the two experiments that carry the report's ideas as one-model, one-run evidence: distillation beat RL in one 8B comparison on math and code, and the budget curves come from one model whose 32K points differ from its own Table 11.
