Connected papers in this knowledge base

DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning

The reasoning-RL paradigm Qwen3 adopts (cold start, then RL); its distilled models are the baselines Qwen3's small models beat, and Qwen3 answers its distillation-against-RL question for students.

DeepSeekMath (GRPO)

GRPO, the algorithm of Qwen3's reasoning-RL stage, and the Pass@K analysis that Table 21's pass@64 column echoes.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

The rival open MoE flagship: Qwen3-235B-A22B-Base beats DeepSeek-V3-Base on 14 of 15 benchmarks with a third of the parameters, without MLA, shared experts or multi-token prediction.

Switch Transformers

The MoE lineage behind Qwen3's 128 fine-grained experts with 8 active, and the auxiliary balancing loss that Qwen3 computes over the global batch.

Mixtral of Experts

The open MoE that made top-k routing standard; Qwen3 keeps the router and drops Mixtral's few large experts for many small ones.

RoFormer (RoPE)

Rotary embeddings, extended here by raising the base to 1,000,000 (ABF) and by YaRN and Dual Chunk Attention at inference.

The Llama 3 Herd of Models

The competing open-family report a year earlier; Qwen3's comparisons against Llama-4 Maverick and Scout mark the open-weight lead moving east.
