How the Vision Transformer works

How to train your ViT? (AugReg)

Steiner et al., 2021: the authors' follow-up: with augmentation, regularisation and more compute, ImageNet-21k pre-training matches JFT-300M; the main correction to this paper's data story.

(40 min)

Training data-efficient image transformers (DeiT)

Touvron et al., 2020: ViT-B trained on ImageNet alone to 83.1% with a modern recipe, two months after this paper.

(40 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Attention Is All You Need (Transformer)

2017: the encoder ViT reuses nearly unchanged.

BERT

2018: source of the [class] token, the Base and Large sizes, and the masked-prediction idea tried in section 4.6.

Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models

2020: the NLP scaling story whose vision counterpart this paper begins.

CLIP

2021: pairs a ViT image encoder with a text encoder; the main route by which ViT reached multimodal models.

DDPM

2020: the diffusion line that later swapped its U-Net for ViT-style DiT backbones.

Latent Diffusion (Stable Diffusion)

2021: diffusion in a latent space; DiT later ran a ViT on patches of those latents.

RoFormer (RoPE)

2021: rotary position embeddings, which 2D-RoPE vision towers (Qwen2-VL) use instead of ViT's learned table.
