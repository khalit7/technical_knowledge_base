Generate in latent space: a toy LDM against a toy pixel-space DM

Two text-conditioned diffusion models trained for this page the paper's way, running in your browser. The data are 32 × 32 images of one shape (circle, square or triangle) in one of four colours at one of nine positions, on a light background with faint grain; the prompt is three tokens, such as "red circle, top left". Stage 1 is a KL-regularised autoencoder with f = 4 (32 × 32 × 3 to 8 × 8 × 3, {{§3.1|ax:S3.SS1}}); stage 2 is a UNet with cross-attention to a one-block transformer τθ over the prompt ({{§3.3|ax:S3.SS3}}), trained with Eq. 3 and 10% empty prompts for classifier-free guidance. The pixel-space model is the same design on 32 × 32 × 3 with two more resolution levels, at a matched parameter count, trained for the same steps on the same data ({{§4.1|ax:S4.SS1}}'s protocol). Every sample is graded by a fixed rule that reads shape, colour and position from the pixels.
