High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models (LDM / Stable Diffusion)

Robin Rombach, Andreas Blattmann, Dominik Lorenz, Patrick Esser, Björn Ommer
Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich and IWR, Heidelberg University; Runway ML · December 2021 (arXiv v1 20 December 2021; v2 13 April 2022)

In one line: Runs DDPM in a mildly compressed autoencoder latent space with cross-attention conditioning, matching pixel-space quality at a fraction of the compute; became Stable Diffusion and made latent diffusion the default generative recipe.
