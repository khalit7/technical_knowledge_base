The paper's tables, rebuilt

Every results table of the paper as printed in the arXiv HTML v2 (transcribed by script, src/mk_tables.py), bold cells as in the paper, the paper's own rows highlighted. Click a column header to sort. Derived columns (ratios, A100-days) are computed in src/recompute.py and marked as derived.

Checks of the paper's own numbers

Each claim recomputed from the printed tables, or compared across the tables, figures and text where the paper reports the same model twice. "Holds" means the arithmetic agrees; the others say what differs.
