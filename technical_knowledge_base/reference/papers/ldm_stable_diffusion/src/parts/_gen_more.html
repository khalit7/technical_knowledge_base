Connected papers in this knowledge base

DDPM

2020-06: supplies the whole diffusion objective and the noise-predicting UNet; this paper changes only where the chain runs. Its page runs a toy DDPM and rebuilds the rate-distortion figure this paper's Figure 2 comes from.

CLIP

2021-02: this paper trains its own BERT-tokenised text transformer; Stable Diffusion swapped in CLIP's frozen ViT-L/14 text encoder as τθ.

ViT

2020-10: DiT and its successors replace this paper's UNet with a Transformer on latent patches while keeping the latent space.

Attention Is All You Need

2017-06: cross-attention conditioning is the Transformer's encoder-decoder attention grafted into a UNet.

LoRA

2021-06: the low-rank adapter that became the usual way hobbyists fine-tune Stable Diffusion's cross-attention layers.
