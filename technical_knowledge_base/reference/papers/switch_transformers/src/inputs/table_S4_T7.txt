Table 7: Distillation compression rates. We measure the quality when distilling large sparse models into a dense baseline. Our baseline, T5-Base, has a -1.636 Neg. Log Perp. quality. In the right columns, we then distill increasingly large sparse models into this same architecture. Through a combination of weight-initialization and a mixture of hard and soft losses, we can shrink our sparse teachers by 95%+ while preserving 30% of the quality gain.
However, for significantly better and larger pre-trained teachers, we expect larger student models would be necessary to achieve these compression rates.
