Best resources

Reducing reward hacking by localizing bad behavior instead of penalizing it

the authors, Anthropic Alignment Science Blog (maker), 19 October 2026: the one-result summary (Countdown-Code) with Figure 1 and the open questions; a few numbers are rounded differently from the paper.

(10 min)

Gradient Routing: Masking Gradients to Localize Computation in Neural Networks

Cloud et al., 2024: the expand, route, ablate procedure GRAFT adapts, with the first weak-oversight RL demo (a 5x5 gridworld).

(45 min)

Monitoring Reasoning Models for Misbehavior and the Risks of Promoting Obfuscation

Baker et al. (OpenAI), 2025: the problem at frontier scale: putting a chain-of-thought monitor into the reward made coding agents hide their hacks.

(40 min)

Natural emergent misalignment from reward hacking in production RL

MacDiarmid et al. (Anthropic), 2025: why reward hacking matters beyond the score, and inoculation prompting, the main alternative GRAFT is compared with.

(40 min)

Countdown-Code: A Testbed for Studying The Emergence and Generalization of Reward Hacking in RLVR

Khalifa et al., 2026: the two-file Countdown environment (solution.py plus a test.py the model may rewrite) behind GRAFT's main result.
