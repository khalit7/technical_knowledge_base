Then and now: BERT-Base becomes ModernBERT-base, one change at a time

The encoder recipe survived, the details did not. Each step makes one change that later work introduced, names the paper, and recounts the model from its configuration. The final step lands on ModernBERT-base's released {{configuration|https://huggingface.co/answerdotai/ModernBERT-base/blob/main/config.json}}, which reproduces the paper's stated 149M parameters ({{Warner et al. 2024, §2.1.3|https://arxiv.org/html/2412.13663}}).

BERT-Base (2018) to ModernBERT-base (2024). Counts are the encoder only: embeddings, layers and final norm, without pooler or heads.

Parameters: token embeddings VH, plus 512H learned positions and 2H segments while they exist, plus an embedding norm; per layer, attention 4H² and a feed-forward 2HI (GeGLU: 3HI with I = 1,152), plus biases and norm biases while they exist. "Keys per query" is the average over layers, at the longest context, of how many positions one query attends to.
