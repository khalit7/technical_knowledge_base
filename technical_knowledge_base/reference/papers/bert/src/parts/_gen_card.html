BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding (BERT)

Jacob Devlin, Ming-Wei Chang, Kenton Lee, Kristina Toutanova
Google AI Language · October 2018 (arXiv v1 11 October 2018; v2 24 May 2019)

In one line: Masked-LM pretraining of a deep bidirectional Transformer encoder plus per-task fine-tuning set SOTA on 11 NLP tasks and made pretrain-then-finetune the default recipe; the encoder lineage lives on in embeddings/retrieval via ModernBERT and Ettin.

How far to trust it: Believe the central claim: masked-LM pretraining of a deep bidirectional encoder beats the same model trained left to right, by margins many standard errors wide, and every later encoder confirmed it. Treat next sentence prediction as unproven (one run, one task outside the noise, reversed by RoBERTa) and the dev-set ablations as single, dev-selected runs. The evidence, judged.
