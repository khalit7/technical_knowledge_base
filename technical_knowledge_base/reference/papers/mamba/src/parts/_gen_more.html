The paper

Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces (arXiv HTML v2)

COLM 2024, the paper itself (v2; arXiv's HTML numbers several of the PDF's tables as figures, so this page uses the PDF's numbering and links the HTML anchor); the HTML version has the anchors this page links to. abstract page, PDF.

(1h)

state-spaces/mamba

code and checkpoints, Apache 2.0 (repo, about 20 minutes for the README and entry path); also holds Mamba-2 and Mamba-3.

(20 min)

Best resources

A Visual Guide to Mamba and State Space Models

Maarten Grootendorst: the best intuition-first walkthrough, from RNN/CNN duality through selectivity, with excellent diagrams.

(35 min)

The Annotated S4

Sasha Rush: executable notebook covering the S4 lineage Mamba builds on; essential background for the discretization and convolution-mode math.

(1h 30m)

Mamba: The Hard Way

Sasha Rush: reimplements the selective scan kernel step by step in Triton; the fastest route to actually understanding the hardware-aware algorithm.

(1h)

State Space Duality (Mamba-2) blog series

Tri Dao and Albert Gu: the authors' own follow-up explaining how selective SSMs and attention are two views of the same family of structured matrices (the series is four parts).

(1h 15m)

An Empirical Study of Mamba-based Language Models

Waleffe et al., NVIDIA (with Gu and Dao), 2024: the controlled test the paper could not run: 8B Mamba, Mamba-2, Transformer and a hybrid on the same 1.1T and 3.5T tokens; where pure SSMs match Transformers and where they do not (5-shot MMLU, Phonebook, long context).

(40 min)

Mamba in Hugging Face transformers

Hugging Face docs: how to load the released checkpoints (for example state-spaces/mamba-130m-hf) without the CUDA kernels; the quickest way to try one.

(10 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Attention Is All You Need (Transformer)

2017: the architecture Mamba positions itself against; Mamba trades exact-recall KV cache for compressed recurrent state.

FlashAttention

2022: same author (Dao) and same IO-aware philosophy; the selective scan kernel is the SSM analogue of FlashAttention's tiling and recomputation, and FlashAttention-2 is the baseline of Figure 8.

PagedAttention (vLLM)

2023: attacks the KV-cache cost at serving time that Mamba removes architecturally.

Mixtral of Experts

2024: MoE, the other big efficiency axis; hybrids like Jamba and Nemotron 3 Nano combine both.

Training Compute-Optimal LLMs (Chinchilla)

2022: the token budgets of Mamba's scaling-law protocol (Figure 4, Table 12).

Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (GPT-3)

2020: the model shapes and training recipe Mamba's scaling laws copy.

RoFormer (RoPE)

2021: rotary embeddings, part of the Transformer++ baseline and of the MHA-RoPE induction-heads baseline.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

2024: the other road to a small KV cache, compressing attention (MLA) instead of replacing it.
