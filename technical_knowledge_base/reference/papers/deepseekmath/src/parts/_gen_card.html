DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models (DeepSeekMath, GRPO)

Zhihong Shao*, Peiyi Wang*, Qihao Zhu*, Runxin Xu, Junxiao Song, Xiao Bi, Haowei Zhang, Mingchuan Zhang, Y.K. Li, Y. Wu, Daya Guo* (* core contributors; Shao, Wang and Zhu as interns at DeepSeek-AI)

In one line: GRPO replaces PPO's critic with a group-mean reward baseline (advantage = z-scored reward across G samples per question, KL moved into the loss), taking a 7B math model to 51.7% MATH and becoming the default RLVR algorithm.

How far to trust it: Believe the data pipeline and the headline scores: the gains are large against the noise. Treat GRPO's superiority as an argument, not a result: the paper never trains PPO, its memory saving is asserted rather than measured, and GRPO's lead over Online RFT in its own ablation is within one standard error. GRPO's standing rests on cost and on what later work found. The evidence, judged.
