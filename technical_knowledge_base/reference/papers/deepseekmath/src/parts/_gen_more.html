Best resources

A vision researcher's guide to some RL stuff: PPO & GRPO

Yuge (Jimmy) Shi: the clearest from-first-principles walkthrough of the PPO machinery and exactly what GRPO deletes from it.

(40 min)

Policy Gradient Algorithms (RLHF Book)

Nathan Lambert: GRPO in the context of REINFORCE and PPO variants, with the advantage formulas and the implementation caveats side by side.

(30 min)

GRPO Trainer documentation

Hugging Face TRL: the reference open implementation (docs, about 20 minutes for the core pages); its loss section documents where modern practice departs from the paper (loss aggregation, KL handling).

(20 min)

Understanding R1-Zero-Like Training: A Critical Perspective (Dr. GRPO)

Liu et al., 2025: the critical follow-up that identifies GRPO's length and difficulty biases; read it after the original.

(45 min)

On a few pitfalls in KL divergence gradient estimation for RL

Tang and Munos, 2025: why differentiating the k3 estimator as a loss, as GRPO does, does not give the gradient of the KL it estimates.

(25 min)

DeepSeekMath-RL 7B model card

DeepSeek-AI on Hugging Face: the released RL checkpoint (Base and Instruct sit beside it), with the chat template and the license.

Connected papers in this knowledge base

InstructGPT

2022: the PPO-based RLHF pipeline GRPO simplifies; GRPO keeps its clipping and its KL to a reference. Its page trains a PPO toy live.

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)

2023: the other way to remove a model from the RLHF stack; the unified paradigm here places DPO as offline, rule-rewarded, pairwise. Its page trains DPO against PPO on a toy with an exact optimum.

DeepSeek-R1

2025: GRPO at scale with rule (verifiable) rewards, the direct descendant; its Supplementary A.3 is the first GRPO against PPO comparison.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

2024: the model family whose post-training inherits this pipeline.

ZeRO

2019: the 16-bytes-per-parameter accounting used here to put a number on the memory GRPO saves.

Qwen3 Technical Report

2025: trained with GSPO, a sequence-level successor to GRPO's token-level ratio.

OLMo 2

2025: a fully open recipe ending in reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards.

Learn What's Left, Not What's Mastered

2026: advantage reweighting for multi-reward policy optimisation, a later turn of the advantage design GRPO started.
