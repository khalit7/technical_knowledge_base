Best resources

Dream-RSI project page

the authors: the method in prose, the result tables, and an animated one-lap demo whose numbers the page itself labels illustrative; it is also the only place that says what the dagger in the Lasso table means ("our reproduction").

(10 min)

Structured Scaling of AI Discovery Across Diverse Scientific Domains (SimpleTES)

Ye et al., arXiv 2604.19341 (v2, 27 July 2026; Dream-RSI cites it under its earlier title, "Evaluation-driven scaling for scientific discovery"): the strongest baseline and the source of the Lasso task, its 17 search instances and the 51,200-generation budget (C = 32 trajectories × L = 100 steps × K = 16 samples); its Supplementary Table 16 is where Dream-RSI's SimpleTES row comes from.

(45 min)

EvoX: Meta-evolution for automated discovery

Liu et al., arXiv 2602.23413: the online alternative Dream-RSI argues against: evolve the search strategy itself during discovery, paying a real rollout for each strategy tried.

(30 min)

AlphaEvolve: a coding agent for scientific and algorithmic discovery

Novikov et al., Google DeepMind, arXiv 2506.13131: the discovery loop (propose, evaluate, keep the best) with a hand-designed exploration strategy, and the source of the autocorrelation and circle-packing baselines in Table 1.

(40 min)

KernelBench: can LLMs write efficient GPU kernels?

Ouyang et al., arXiv 2502.10517: the benchmark behind the four kernel tasks (VGG16, LayerNorm, ConvDiv, ConvMax): correctness against a PyTorch reference, then speed.

(30 min)

World Models

Ha and Schmidhuber, 2018 (interactive article): the analogy the paper borrows: train a policy inside a model of the environment instead of the environment. Dream-RSI's "model" is not learned; it is the recorded tree itself.

(30 min)

Mastering diverse domains through world models (DreamerV3)

Hafner et al., arXiv 2301.04104: the Dreamer line the paper names: learn a dynamics model from experience and improve the policy on imagined trajectories.

(40 min)

Model-based reinforcement learning: a survey

Moerland et al., arXiv 2006.16712: for the general picture the paper's Section 2 sketches, including Sutton's Dyna (1990): plan with simulated experience between real steps, and the known risk that a policy exploits the model where the model is wrong.

(1h 30m)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Agora: Git as Shared Memory for Collective AutoResearch

One of the week's three auto-research papers. Its population converged prematurely and a human had to restore diversity, the opposite failure from a replay simulator that can only reward what the history already covers.

SoL-Pi: Recursively Scaling Auto-Research Loops for Efficient Agent Harness

The third of that week's auto-research papers: its memory lives in harness-layer techniques discovered once and reused, where Agora's lives in a shared commit graph and Dream-RSI's in replayable trees.

NeoHorse-1: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement via Agentic Post-Training with Routing Harness

Recursive self-improvement in the weights (interactions served through a routing harness become a post-training curriculum); Dream-RSI keeps every model fixed and changes only the exploration policy's code. Neither ablates its own loop.

JIT-Agent: Scaling Harness Intelligence via Just-in-Time Harness Evolution

Another way to change the layer around a fixed agent: write a new harness per task. JIT-Agent trains the writer; Dream-RSI scores rewrites of one policy against old traces.

Φ-Bench: Can Large Language Models Engineer the Infrastructure That Powers Them?

Kernel and hardware work is where frontier models score worst there (Hardware and Edge 5.4% for the best model), which is one of Dream-RSI's three demonstration domains.

HarnessDev: Can LLMs Create and Evolve Their Own Agent Harness?

The score a harness creator could see moved the same way as the held-out score in only 34 of 64 version changes: the same question of whether a self-edit judged on old runs generalises.

EnvHarness: Awakening Static Worlds for Agent Learning

Cited by Dream-RSI (Huang et al. 2026b) among self-evolving systems that build environments; there the world is generated, here it is the agent's own history.

RRSI: Regularized Recursive Self-Improvement of Agent Harnesses

Another recursive self-improvement loop over the layer around a fixed model, with a regulariser against drift.
