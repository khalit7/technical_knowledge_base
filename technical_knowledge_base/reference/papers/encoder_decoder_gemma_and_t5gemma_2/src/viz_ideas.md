## Considered and rejected

- **A live toy model in the browser** (weights shipped, JS forward pass): the toy's point is the comparison between training runs, which needs the training itself; a forward pass of one trained model would teach nothing the curves do not. No weights ship, so no forward check is needed.
- **Re-animating the three attention masks** (encoder-decoder, causal, prefix LM): already on the T5 page; linked instead.
- **UL2 denoiser animation on a sentence**: span corruption is animated on the T5 page; UL2's mixture is listed in text with its five settings.
- **Then and now tab**: the line is a year old; there is no later work yet that changed it. EmbeddingGemma is covered in Why it matters.
- **Refitting Figure 3's FLOPs**: the formula is not given and no standard estimate fits all thirteen points; reported under "does not reproduce" rather than drawn as a fit.

## What the methodology lacked for this page

- A page covering **two papers** needs two anchor namespaces: `build.sh` here adds `bx:` links and `[[B:...|...]]` margin labels for the second paper.
- Reading time: the paper method has no rule for opt-in detail. Here three `<details>` blocks (benchmark lists, two minor §6 findings, T5Gemma 2's sizes and training setup) are excluded from the reading-time count, since a reader must open them.
