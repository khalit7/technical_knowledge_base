The papers

Encoder-Decoder Gemma: Improving the Quality-Efficiency Trade-Off via Adaptation (arXiv HTML v1)

Biao Zhang, Fedor Moiseev, Joshua Ainslie, Paul Suganthan, Min Ma, Surya Bhupatiraju, Fede Lebron, Orhan Firat, Armand Joulin, Zhe Dong (Google). ICML 2025, April 2025 (arXiv v1, 8 April 2025): the method paper, called T5Gemma when the weights were released. Read Sections 3, 5 and 6; the discussion section is where the ablations that matter live (bidirectional attention, cross-attention warmup, adaptation against from scratch). Abstract page, PDF.

(45 min)

T5Gemma 2: Seeing, Reading, and Understanding Longer (arXiv HTML v2)

Biao Zhang, Paul Suganthan, Gaël Liu, Ilya Philippov, Sahil Dua, Ben Hora, Kat Black, Gus Martins, Omar Sanseviero, Shreya Pathak, Cassidy Hardin, Francesco Visin, Jiageng Zhang, Kathleen Kenealy, Qin Yin, Xiaodan Song, Olivier Lacombe, Armand Joulin, Tris Warkentin, Adam Roberts (Google DeepMind). technical report, December 2025 (arXiv v1 16 December, v2 23 December 2025): shorter, and the one to read if you care about long context or multimodality. Tables 4 and 5 are the whole argument. Abstract page, PDF.

(45 min)

Best resources

Encoder-Decoder or Decoder-Only? Revisiting Encoder-Decoder Large Language Model

Biao Zhang, Yong Cheng, Siamak Shakeri, Xinyi Wang, Min Ma et al., arXiv 2510.26622: the same group's scaling study, the companion that argues the architecture case on scaling grounds rather than on a released model.

(45 min)

T5Gemma: A new collection of encoder-decoder Gemma models

Google Developers Blog, 9 July 2025: the fast version if you only want the shape of the result, with the release of the first-generation weights.

(10 min)

T5Gemma 2: The next generation of encoder-decoder models

Google blog, 18 December 2025: the announcement of T5Gemma 2: tied embeddings, merged attention, 128K context, images.

(8 min)

EmbeddingGemma: Powerful and Lightweight Text Representations

Google, arXiv 2509.20354: the downstream user: an embedding model initialised from the encoder of a Gemma 3 model adapted with the T5Gemma recipe (Section 2).

(30 min)

UL2: Unifying Language Learning Paradigms

Tay et al., arXiv 2205.05131: the mixture-of-denoisers objective both papers adapt with, and its own encoder-decoder against decoder-only comparison.

(45 min)

T5Gemma 2 in Hugging Face Transformers

Hugging Face docs and source: the reference implementation of merged attention: keys and values of the decoder and the encoder concatenated into one softmax, with the cross-attention mask never windowed.

(20 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

T5

2019: the original encoder-decoder against decoder-only result being retested, the span corruption that UL2 extends, and the tied embeddings T5Gemma 2 returns to. Its page animates the three attention masks.

Seq vs Seq (Ettin)

2025: the compute-matched encoder against decoder comparison from the representation-learning side; T5Gemma is the same question from the generation side.

Bitune

2024: the other route to bidirectional prompt processing, a bidirectional pass inside a decoder-only model instead of splitting the model in two.

End-to-End Context Compression at Scale (Latent Context Language Models)

2026: the complementary answer for long inputs, compressing the context into latents for a decoder rather than restructuring the model around an encoder.

GPT-3

2020: the reason the field went the other way (few-shot prompting of one decoder stack), and the capability neither T5Gemma paper tests.

Attention Is All You Need

2017: the encoder-decoder this adapts back to, run live at toy scale on its page.

BERT

2018: the bidirectional encoder on its own; SuperGLUE as the test of representations.
