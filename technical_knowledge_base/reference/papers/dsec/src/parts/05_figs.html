The paper's figures, rebuilt

DSec's arXiv source ships every chart as a vector PDF. The curves below are those vectors, decoded by src/decode_figs.py: each axis is calibrated on its own tick marks (largest residual under 0.0001 of an axis unit), lines are read vertex by vertex and markers at their centres, then thinned for the page without moving any kept point. Nothing is read by eye. Pick a figure; the box under each says what the paper states and what the decoded curve gives.
