DeepSeek Elastic Compute (DSec): A Sandbox Infrastructure for Effective Agentic Training at Scale

Jialiang Huang (first author), Hongxuan Tang, Jingchang Chen, Yuxuan Liu and 126 more, with Mingxing Zhang, Liyue Zhang (corresponding), Panpan Huang and Wenfeng Liang: 131 authors, 16 of them marked as the DSec project developers
DeepSeek-AI, with Tsinghua University · 19 September 2026 (arXiv v1)

In one line: The sandbox layer under agentic reinforcement learning, published with production numbers for the first time: four isolation levels behind one SDK, about 3 million sandboxes a day and 5,000 created per second on roughly 160 nodes, co-designed so stateful rollouts survive preemptible GPU training.

How far to trust it: Believe the workload study and the four storage, memory and CPU mechanisms: their figures decode back to the text's numbers within a few percent. Treat the production scale as self-reported (no trace, no method) and the RL co-design, the paper's most original part, as described rather than measured: the evaluation leaves it out by design.
