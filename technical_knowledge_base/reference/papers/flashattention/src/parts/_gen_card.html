FlashAttention: Fast and Memory-Efficient Exact Attention with IO-Awareness (FlashAttention)

Tri Dao, Daniel Y. Fu, Stefano Ermon, Atri Rudra, Christopher Ré
Stanford University (Hazy Research) and University at Buffalo, SUNY · May 2022 (arXiv v1); v2 June 2022; published at NeurIPS 2022
