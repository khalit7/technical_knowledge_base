Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention (PagedAttention, vLLM)

Woosuk Kwon, Zhuohan Li, Siyuan Zhuang, Ying Sheng, Lianmin Zheng, Cody Hao Yu, Joseph E. Gonzalez, Hao Zhang, Ion Stoica
UC Berkeley, with Stanford, UC San Diego and an independent researcher · September 2023 (arXiv 2023-09-12); SOSP '23, Koblenz, 23 to 26 October 2023

In one line: PagedAttention pages the KV cache into fixed 16-token blocks with OS-style block tables, copy-on-write sharing, and swap/recompute preemption, lifting KV memory utilization from about 20 to 38% to 96% and serving throughput 2 to 4x over Orca; it became the industry-standard KV layout and the foundation of vLLM.

How far to trust it: Believe the mechanism and the memory accounting: the waste figures reproduce from Table 1 and the paper's own length distributions, and paged KV caches became the default of every major serving stack. Treat the throughput multiples as the top of a range: they come from single runs against the authors' own reimplementation of Orca, and read off the paper's figures the gain over the strongest baseline is 1.5 to 2.4x on ShareGPT, about 1.3 to 1.8x on Alpaca, and near 1x where memory is not the bottleneck.
