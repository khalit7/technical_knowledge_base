StateM: Reaching 95.3% Raw Accuracy, or a $15 Frontier Run, on Terminal-Bench 2.1 via Harness Scaling

Ziheng Qin, Yaxin Lu, Zhangyang "Atlas" Wang, Kai Wang
Independent work in the authors' personal time, not on behalf of any employer (Ziheng Qin and Yaxin Lu are equal first authors) · 15 August 2026 (arXiv v1, the only version so far)

In one line: Names and tests harness scaling: a versioned YAML state-machine runbook with enforced, checked transitions around a fixed CLI agent lifts GPT-5.5 from 83.1% to 92.1% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, transfers frozen to GPT-5.6 for a 95.3% raw submission, and after about $37 of adaptation lets DeepSeek-V4-Flash match the GPT frontier score at about 1/38 the evaluation cost.

How far to trust it: Believe the runtime: checked, logged transitions are a sound, reusable way to stop an agent handing off unverified work, and in the released DeepSeek trials a host-run check held back 56% of runs at least once. Treat the Terminal-Bench numbers as a benchmark-tuned upper bound: the runbook was developed on the same 89 tasks it is scored on, the 95.3% is the raw score of a submission the leaderboard closed without merging, and the only held-out test moved 0.55 to 1.34 points.
