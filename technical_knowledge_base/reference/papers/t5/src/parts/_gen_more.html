The paper

Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer (arXiv HTML v4)

JMLR 21(140), 2020, the paper itself (53 pages: read it as a survey rather than a paper); the HTML version has the anchors this page links to. abstract page, PDF.

(1h 30m)

google-research/text-to-text-transfer-transformer

code and checkpoints (repo, about 20 minutes for the README and entry path); span corruption lives in t5/data/preprocessors.py, ported line by line on this page.

(20 min)

Best resources

C4 on TensorFlow Datasets

TensorFlow Datasets: the corpus and its variants (en, en.noclean, realnewslike, webtextlike) with their sizes; the build script that regenerates it.

(5 min)

allenai/c4

Allen Institute for AI, Hugging Face: the processed C4 hosted as files, which is how most people download it rather than rebuilding it from Common Crawl.

(5 min)

Exploring Transfer Learning with T5

Google AI blog, Feb 2020: the authors' own short framing, the fastest way to get the shape of the paper before committing to it.

(12 min)

T5 model docs

Hugging Face: practical: covers the sentinel-token convention (<extra_id_0>, ...) and how to format inputs and targets, which the paper describes but never spells out at code level.

(25 min)

Released checkpoints

T5 repository: the map of what "T5" means in practice now, including T5 1.1 (GeGLU, no dropout in pretraining, C4 only with no multi-task mixing, untied embedding and classifier) and the LM-adapted variants; almost nobody should start from the original checkpoints.

(10 min)

mT5: A Massively Multilingual Pre-trained Text-to-Text Transformer

Xue et al., 2020: the same recipe on 101 languages via mC4, and the direct answer to T5's own weakest result.

(45 min)

Scaling Instruction-Finetuned Language Models (Flan-T5)

Chung et al., 2022: what happened when task prefixes were replaced by natural-language instructions across 1.8K tasks, on T5 checkpoints.

(45 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Attention Is All You Need

2017: the encoder-decoder T5 keeps, with three changes (rescale-only norm, norm outside the residual path, relative position bias).

BERT

2018: T5's denoising objective is a modified BERT masked LM, and the architecture ablation is largely a controlled test of BERT's encoder-only assumption.

GPT-3

2020, seven months later and pointing the other way: in-context learning on a decoder-only stack instead of fine-tuning; the two papers together are the fork in the road.

Switch Transformers

2021: built directly on this setup, T5-Base and T5-Large on C4 with span corruption as the dense control, and up to 7x faster pretraining against them.

Chinchilla

2022: T5's scaling section asks the same question (more compute: size or steps?) informally at a fixed 4x budget; Chinchilla answers it with a fitted law.

RAG

2020, contemporaneous: the counterpoint on knowledge, facts in a swappable index rather than in the weights T5 scaled up.

Seq vs Seq (Ettin)

2025: encoder-only against decoder-only with one recipe at matched sizes; finds the split task-dependent.

Encoder-Decoder Gemma and T5Gemma 2

2025 to 2026: decoder-only Gemma adapted into an encoder-decoder; the closest modern rerun of T5's encoder-decoder against decoder-only result.

Scaling Laws

2020: loss as a power law in parameters, data and compute; the fitted version of T5's "bigger model or more steps" question.
