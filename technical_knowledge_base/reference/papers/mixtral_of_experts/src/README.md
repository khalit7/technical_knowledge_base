# Mixtral of Experts: page source

`sh build.sh` writes `../index.html`, the whole body of the Notion row page (https://app.notion.com/p/3c65c17b0d0d81eba72af0ac91ddc6b3, a row of the Papers database; its properties Paper, Takeaway, Topics and Year stay in Notion). The row page has no child pages, databases or video. Built from the reference paper page (`../../attention_is_all_you_need_transformer/src/`, via the Switch Transformers page's copies of the shared pieces, which add the verdict line) and `html_utils/methods/papers.md`.

## Departures from `papers.md`, and why

- **The live ingredient is the routing analysis, not the architecture.** Mixtral's mechanism (a top-2 MoE layer) is already animated on the Mistral AI page, explained generically on Topic: llms (Deeper: inside an MoE) and trained live on the Switch Transformers page, so this page links those and builds what is specific to the paper: its cost claims (a roofline animation and chart) and its Section 5 findings (a toy trained to the paper's recipe, measured on held-out text). The page ships the toy's measured routing, not its weights (3.9M parameters, about 15 MB each, too large for the page or the repo), so there is no JS forward pass to check against PyTorch; every number in the tab is computed by `analyse.py` from the PyTorch model itself.
- **A systems before/after inside an architecture paper.** The paper's efficiency claims ("faster inference at low batch-sizes, higher throughput at large batch-sizes", the release post's "same speed as a 12.9B model") are testable with its own parameter counts, so the Reading tab has a decode-step animation (Mixtral, Llama 2 70B, Llama 2 13B; batch 1 to 2,048) on an idealised roofline.
- **No Then and now tab.** The lineage after Mixtral is covered with sources on the Mistral AI page, on Topic: llms and in the Switch Transformers page's Then and now; this page keeps a short sourced Why it matters instead. "What it takes to use this" is kept because the weights are still downloadable and used.
- **Decoded figures.** Figures 7, 9 and 10 are vector SVGs in the arXiv HTML; `decode_figs.py` reads them into numbers (Figure 10 matches Table 5 within 0.05 points; Figure 7's shares sum to 1.000), which let the page check the routing claims against Mixtral itself, not only against the toy.
- **Reading length**: about 20 minutes (the old page was 9). The paper page owns all of the paper plus the checks; the efficiency and routing sections carry most of the new length.

## Files

- `build.sh`: runs `recompute.py` and `mk_paper.py`, assembles `parts/`, expands link macros, fails on an em-dash or unexpanded macro, fills reading times.
- `paper.json`, `tables.json` (every table as printed, 229 cells, plus the Llama 2 paper's own numbers for Llama 2 70B), `check_tables.py` (every cell found in `inputs/paper_v1.txt` in order; the Llama 2 cells in `inputs/llama2_tables_extract.txt`).
- `mk_paper.py`: card, Further reading, `window.PAPER` (metadata, tables, `inputs/recompute.json`, decoded figures).
- `recompute.py` writes `inputs/recompute.json`: parameter counts from `inputs/configs.json` (Mixtral, Mistral 7B, Llama 2 13B and 70B), the decode cost model (expected experts read per layer 8 × (1 − (6/8)^B), bytes and FLOPs per step, roofline time on two H100s, crossover batches), Table 2 to 5 deltas and baselines, Figure 5 checks, Elo win probabilities, the magnet-link date from the tweet ID, and the decoded figures' distances and usage-adjusted repeat baselines.
- `decode_figs.py` writes `inputs/figs.json` from the three SVGs (download command in its docstring; the SVGs themselves are not kept).
- The toy: `data.py` (four domains into `model/data/`, not committed), `train.py` (tokenizer, then `main` and `noaux`, about 11 minutes each on two CPU threads; checkpoints in `model/ck/`, not committed), `analyse.py` (writes `model/routes.json` and `parts/20_route_data.js`). Logs: `model/train_main.log`, `model/train_noaux.log`; `model/tokenizer.json`. Rerun: `python3 data.py && uv run --with torch --with tokenizers python train.py tok && ... train.py main && ... train.py noaux && ... analyse.py`.
- `check_page.mjs` (from the repo root): every control in light 920 and dark 390, every toy domain, sample, layer and variant, the animation stepped end to end, text at least 11 px, no NaN, errors or sideways scroll. `shots_el.mjs`: screenshots of chosen elements for review.
- `mk_coverage.py` writes `coverage.json` (53 items of `live.md`, each verified against the built page); `save_live.py` copied the Notion fetch verbatim into `live.md`; `extract_paper.py` turned the arXiv HTML v1 into `inputs/paper_v1.txt` and `inputs/table_*.txt`.
- `inputs/`: also `mistral_blog_2023-12-11.txt`, `magnet_tweet_oembed.json`, `other_papers_extracts.txt` (GPT-4 report Table 2, DeepSeekMoE introduction, Hugging Face "What we don't know", RULER Appendix F, DBRX and Qwen1.5-MoE quotes), `mistral_docs_legacy.txt` (deprecation dates), `llama2_tables_extract.txt`, `configs.json`.
- `viz_ideas.md`: visualisations chosen and rejected, with scores.
