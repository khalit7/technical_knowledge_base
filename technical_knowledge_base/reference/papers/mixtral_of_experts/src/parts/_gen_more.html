Best resources

Mixture of Experts Explained

Hugging Face blog: the canonical MoE explainer, written for the Mixtral release; covers routing, load balancing, expert parallelism, and MoE fine-tuning gotchas (instability, router sensitivity) directly relevant to anyone tuning these models.

(30 min)

A Visual Guide to Mixture of Experts

Maarten Grootendorst: the best diagrams of the router, top-K gating, and capacity concepts; ends with a Mixtral walkthrough.

(30 min)

Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) LLMs

Cameron R. Wolfe: traces the lineage from sparsely-gated MoE and Switch through Mixtral to modern fine-grained designs, with the math for sparse vs active parameter counts.

(40 min)

Mixtral of experts (release post)

Mistral AI, 11 December 2023: concise statement of the cost-performance claim and the serving story (vLLM + Megablocks, Skypilot deployment); the paper's webpage link, and the source of the "6x faster inference" and "same speed as a 12.9B model" claims this page tests.

(10 min)

Welcome Mixtral

Hugging Face blog, 11 December 2023: release-day guide: what is unknown (pretraining data, fine-tuning data and hyperparameters), memory needed at each precision (over 90 GB in float16, over 23 GB in 4-bit), inference and fine-tuning code.

(15 min)

Fast Inference of Mixture-of-Experts Language Models with Offloading

Eliseev and Mazur, December 2023: the paper's reference [11]: runs Mixtral on consumer GPUs by caching recently used experts, the use of temporal locality that Section 5 points to; code at github.com/dvmazur/mixtral-offloading.

(30 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Switch Transformers

2021: the top-1 MoE predecessor, with a Switch layer you can train live; Mixtral's top-2, every-layer, no-fuss variant is what finally made the idea land in open weights.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

2024: the next generation of open MoE; fine-grained experts, shared experts and auxiliary-loss-free balancing, a different answer to the coarse-expert regime documented here.

Direct Preference Optimization

2023: Mixtral Instruct was among the first flagship models aligned with SFT plus DPO only.

PagedAttention (vLLM)

2023: Mistral upstreamed Megablocks-based MoE kernels into vLLM to make the model servable at release.

Attention Is All You Need

2017: the Transformer whose feed-forward sub-block the MoE layer replaces.

Qwen3 Technical Report

2025: an open MoE family two years on (128 experts, 8 active), the fine-grained design that replaced eight big experts.

Bounding the memory bottlenecks of large mixture-of-experts training

the memory side of training MoE models: total parameters set the bill, which Mixtral's 47B against 13B makes concrete.
