RocketEval: Efficient Automated LLM Evaluation via Grading Checklist (RocketEval)

Tianjun Wei*, Wei Wen*, Ruizhi Qiao, Xing Sun, Jianghong Ma (* equal contribution; Wei as an intern at Tencent; Qiao and Ma corresponding)
Tencent Youtu Lab, City University of Hong Kong, Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen) · 7 March 2025 (arXiv v1, the only version); OpenReview publication 22 January 2025

In one line: Move the judgment out of the judge: a frontier model compiles each query into 5-10 binary checklist questions once, then a 2B model answers them independently and you read the normalised Yes/No logit instead of the generated token, killing positional bias and preserving calibration; Gemma-2-2B hits 0.965 Spearman with Arena Elo (Mistral-Nemo 0.986, above GPT-4o's 0.979) at $27.70 per 1,000 tests versus $3,400, though instance-level agreement stays at 57.9% versus a 64.7% human ceiling.

How far to trust it: Believe the diagnosis and the cost arithmetic: both check out against the released data. Read "comparable to GPT-4o" as a claim about ranking 12 hand-picked, well-separated models, where 0.965 against 0.979 is one discordant pair out of 66; on single responses the small judges stay well short of humans and of GPT-4o.
