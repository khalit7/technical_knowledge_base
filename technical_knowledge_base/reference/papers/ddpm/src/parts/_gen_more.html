Best resources

What are Diffusion Models?

Lilian Weng: the canonical walkthrough of the full derivation, from ELBO to L_simple, plus DDIM and guidance follow-ups.

(1h)

Understanding Diffusion Models: A Unified Perspective

Calvin Luo (tutorial paper, arXiv 2208.11970): derives VAE, hierarchical VAE, DDPM and the score-based view as one framework; best for making the three equivalent parameterisations (x_0, epsilon, score) click.

(1h 30m)

The Annotated Diffusion Model

Hugging Face: a line-by-line PyTorch implementation of this exact paper, including the U-Net.

(1h)

Generative Modeling by Estimating Gradients of the Data Distribution

Yang Song (blog): the score-matching side of the story from the NCSN author; explains why DDPM's objective is denoising score matching in disguise.

(45 min)

Diffusion Explainer

Polo Club, Georgia Tech: an interactive walk through Stable Diffusion's denoising loop on real prompts: the latent-space descendant of this paper, step by step.

(20 min)
