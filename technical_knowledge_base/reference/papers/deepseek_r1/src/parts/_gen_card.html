DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning (DeepSeek-R1, R1-Zero)

DeepSeek-AI (core contributors include Daya Guo, Dejian Yang, Haowei Zhang, Junxiao Song, Peiyi Wang, Qihao Zhu, Runxin Xu, Ruoyu Zhang, Shirong Ma, Xiao Bi and Zhihong Shao; corresponding author of the Nature version Yu Wu)
DeepSeek-AI · arXiv v1 22 January 2025; submitted to Nature 14 February 2025, accepted 17 July 2025, published 17 September 2025; arXiv v2 4 January 2026

In one line: Pure GRPO RL with rule-based verifiable rewards on V3-Base makes reasoning (reflection, "aha moment", long CoT) emerge without SFT; a cold-start SFT, reasoning RL, rejection-sampling SFT, all-scenario RL pipeline fixes R1-Zero's readability and language mixing to match o1, and 800k R1 samples distill reasoning into 1.5B-70B models by SFT alone.

How far to trust it: Believe the central result: outcome-only RL on a strong base model produced long, self-checking reasoning, and many groups reproduced the effect within months. Treat the benchmark margins (R1 against o1, stage against stage) as single runs on small test sets, the cost as GPU rental for the RL alone, and the reflective behaviours as counted words rather than measured reasoning.
