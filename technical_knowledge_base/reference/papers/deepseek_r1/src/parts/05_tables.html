    Figures decoded from the PDF's vector drawings

    The arXiv HTML carries no images for most figures, but the v2 PDF draws them as vectors. decode_figs.py reads every path with PyMuPDF, fits each axis to its printed tick labels by least squares, and maps markers and lines to data units; nothing is read by eye. Figure 4 (PPO against GRPO), 11 and 12 are raster images and are not redrawn; Figures 10 and 17 are bar charts whose values are in the tables.
