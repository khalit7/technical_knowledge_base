The paper

DeepSeek-R1: Incentivizing Reasoning Capability in LLMs via Reinforcement Learning (arXiv HTML v2)

arXiv report (January 2025), then Nature 645, 633 to 638 (17 September 2025); arXiv v2 (4 January 2026) carries the Nature text and its supplementary information, the paper itself; the HTML version has the anchors this page links to. abstract page, PDF.

(2h)

deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1

the official repository: the README (models, evaluation settings, usage recommendations), the January paper as a PDF and the MIT licence; weights on Hugging Face (R1, R1-Zero, six distilled models). No training code, no RL code and no data, although the Nature paper's data statement points here. About 20 minutes for the README and the entry path.

(20 min)

Best resources

DeepSeek-R1 incentivizes reasoning in LLMs through reinforcement learning (Nature)

Guo et al., Nature 645, 633 to 638: the expanded, peer-reviewed version of the same work; its supplementary information is arXiv v2's appendix.

(1h 30m)

DeepSeek models on Hugging Face

DeepSeek-AI: R1, R1-Zero, R1-0528 and the six distilled models, with model cards and configs.

(10 min)

The Illustrated DeepSeek-R1

Jay Alammar: visual walkthrough of the three training paths (R1-Zero, R1, distillation); the fastest way to get the whole picture.

(20 min)

Understanding Reasoning LLMs

Sebastian Raschka: places R1 among the four ways to build reasoning models (inference scaling, pure RL, SFT plus RL, distillation), with the pipeline redrawn stage by stage.

(30 min)

DeepSeek R1's recipe to replicate o1 and the future of reasoning LMs

Nathan Lambert: the best analysis of what the four-stage recipe means for RL training practice and the o1 replication race.

(20 min)

Open-R1

Hugging Face: fully open reproduction with GRPO training code, data generation and evaluations; the natural starting point for building your own loop (README and training entry path).

(25 min)

Peer review file of the Nature paper

Nature, eight referees and the authors' replies: what the referees doubted (contamination, statistics, anthropomorphism, verbosity against skill) and what the authors added or declined; this page's evidence section quotes it.

(1h)

Understanding R1-Zero-Like Training: A Critical Perspective (Dr. GRPO)

Liu et al., Sea AI Lab: finds the aha moment already in DeepSeek-V3-Base and a length bias in GRPO's 1/|o| normalisation; the toy on this page tests that bias.

(45 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

DeepSeekMath (GRPO)

introduced GRPO; its page trains PPO, GRPO and the other methods side by side and traces the objective to DAPO and Dr. GRPO. R1 is GRPO scaled up with verifiable instead of learned rewards.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

the 671B MoE base both R1-Zero and R1 start from; its SFT data supplies the 200K non-reasoning samples, and its bill is the $5.576M the $294K sits on.

InstructGPT

the SFT-then-RLHF paradigm this paper partly inverts (RL before any reasoning SFT, rules before reward models); its page runs a toy PPO pipeline.

Direct Preference Optimization

the offline alternative for preference alignment; R1's fourth stage shows where online RL with learned preference rewards is still used, and how quickly it is hacked.

Qwen3 Technical Report

a descendant family trained with an R1-style long chain-of-thought RL pipeline; Qwen2.5 models are also the distillation students here.

The Llama 3 Herd of Models

Llama-3.1-8B and Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct are two of the six distillation students.
