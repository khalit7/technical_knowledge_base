Seq vs Seq: An Open Suite of Paired Encoders and Decoders (Ettin)

Orion Weller, Kathryn Ricci, Marc Marone, Antoine Chaffin, Dawn Lawrie, Benjamin Van Durme
Johns Hopkins University (CLSP) with LightOn (Antoine Chaffin) · July 2025 (arXiv v1 15 July 2025; v2 12 March 2026)

In one line: Compute-matched paired training (same data, recipe, 17M-1B) shows encoders beat decoders an order of magnitude larger on classification/retrieval and vice versa for generation, and 50B tokens of cross-objective adaptation cannot close either gap.

How far to trust it: Believe the direction: with data, order and recipe held fixed, encoders win classification and retrieval and decoders win generation, at every size. Treat the size of the gaps as provisional (single runs, no error bars), the retrieval claim as within noise at 1B, the 1B generation gap as measured on a third of the data and a third of the adaptation budget, and the SciQ and SIQA nuance as a table mix-up.
