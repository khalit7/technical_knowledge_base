The paper

Seq vs Seq: An Open Suite of Paired Encoders and Decoders (arXiv HTML v2)

ICLR 2026 (poster), the paper itself; the HTML version has the anchors this page links to. abstract page, PDF.

(45 min)

JHU-CLSP/ettin-encoder-vs-decoder

MIT-licensed repository with links to the code, data and batch order (repo, about 20 minutes for the README and entry path); the training code itself lives in the orionw/bert24 fork of the ModernBERT codebase.

(20 min)

Best resources

Ettin Suite: SoTA Paired Encoders and Decoders

Hugging Face blog, the authors: the authors' own release post; condensed walkthrough of the suite, the recipe, and the encoder-vs-decoder findings with usage snippets.

(15 min)

Ettin models and data on Hugging Face (jhu-clsp)

the authors: the 24 models (encoders, decoders, cross-objective), the raw checkpoints, the training data by phase and the batch order.

(10 min)

Smarter, Better, Faster, Longer: A Modern Bidirectional Encoder (ModernBERT)

Warner et al., Answer.AI and LightOn, December 2024: the recipe Ettin replicates with open data: the architecture of Table 12, the three training phases, the evaluation setup Ettin reuses.

(40 min)

LLM2Vec: Large Language Models Are Secretly Powerful Text Encoders

BehnamGhader et al., 2024: the decoder-to-encoder adaptation Ettin stress-tests: bidirectional attention, MNTP, then contrastive training; read section 2 and appendix D.1.1 for the MNTP budget.

(40 min)

BERTs are Generative In-Context Learners

David Samuel, NeurIPS 2024: the trick Ettin uses to run encoders on generative benchmarks: append three [MASK] tokens and an end token, fill the first mask, repeat.

(30 min)

Should We Still Pretrain Encoders with Masked Language Modeling?

Gisserot-Boukhlef et al., 2025: the concurrent study that reached the opposite verdict (CLM then MLM wins) at about 100B tokens; read it beside Ettin's section 6.

(30 min)

Pythia: A Suite for Analyzing Large Language Models Across Training and Scaling

Biderman et al., EleutherAI, 2023: the template for Ettin's release: one data order, many sizes, every checkpoint public.

(30 min)

mmBERT: A Modern Multilingual Encoder with Annealed Language Learning

Marone et al., JHU, September 2025: the authors' follow-up built on the Ettin recipe; the paper's section 6 cites it for encoders beating adapted decoders at scale.

(30 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

BERT

2018: the encoder lineage Ettin modernises; its page trains a toy bidirectional encoder against a left-to-right twin, the experiment this paper runs at 17M to 1B.

GPT-3

2020: the decoder-only shift this paper interrogates.

2 OLMo 2 Furious (OLMo 2)

2025: supplies the DCLM, Dolma and Dolmino data sources and the open-artifact philosophy.

RoFormer (RoPE)

2021: rotary positions; Ettin extends context by raising the RoPE base to 160,000.

Chinchilla

2022: training far past compute-optimal is what made decoder adaptation popular, and Ettin's 1B is still past it at 667B tokens.

Bitune

2024: bidirectional attention added inside a decoder during instruction tuning; Ettin cites it as mixed training.

T5

2019: the third architecture (encoder-decoder) that Ettin's pairs leave out; its page compares the three attention masks.

Encoder-Decoder Gemma and T5Gemma 2

2025: another way to reuse a decoder's weights, as an encoder-decoder rather than a pure encoder.

The Llama 3 Herd of Models

2024: Llama 3.2 1B is the decoder baseline Ettin's 1B decoder beats (59.0 against 56.6).

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

2020: dense retrieval, the job where this paper says a native encoder stays the right default.
