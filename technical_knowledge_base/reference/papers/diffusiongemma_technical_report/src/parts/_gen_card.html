Google DeepMind · August 2026 (arXiv v1 31 July 2026; the e-print is dated 4 August 2026); the weights were released on 9 June 2026

In one line: First open-weights text diffusion model that is fast and genuinely capable: Gemma 4 26B A4B warm-started from AR weights with under 10% of the AR token budget hits ~20 tokens per forward pass and ~1,500 TPS on one H100 (7.1x its AR baseline) via block-AR encoder-decoder decoding plus a unified SD-RL stage, at a real but bounded quality tax.

Verdict: Believe the speed at one request per GPU: it follows from the paper's own per-step timing and tokens per pass, and the code is public. Treat the comparisons with other systems (Mercury 2 through an API, LLaDA on 8 B200s, the MTP baseline measured on a different benchmark) and the crossover at about 32 users as rough, and the quality gap as real.
