Where diffusion behaves differently (Section 9, qualitative, no thinking)

  • Answer first ({{Figure 15|ax:S9.F15}}): for 7 × (√(111 + 10) − 3) − 9², Gemma 4 commits to "−1" in its first token and appends a correction; DiffusionGemma's canvas goes −1, −15, then −25 as the reasoning around it forms, in 5 steps (the frog puzzle of {{G.1|ax:A7.SS1}}: "Yes" revised to "No" in 6).
  • Adaptive compute ({{G.2|ax:A7.SS2}}): 40 tokens in 7 steps when each bit depends on the two before it, 4 when it depends only on the input; AR needs 40. The toy runs the 3-bit version of these two tasks.
  • Constrained outputs ({{G.3|ax:A7.SS3}}): a strict JSON extraction converges in 2 steps and a Python bug fix, mostly copy, in 3; AR pays the same per token for boilerplate.

Finetuning it yourself (Section 8)

The released toolkit, on Hackable Diffusion, sums the encoder's next-token loss and the decoder's denoising loss on one random canvas, half the batch self-conditioned ({{Eq. 13|ax:S8.E13}}; the toy trains the same way), with LoRA on every linear map, routers and self-conditioning included, on 2 × A100 80GB ({{§8|ax:S8}}). Sudoku (4,096 held-out puzzles): 0% before, 84.40% with LoRA rank 8 while effective steps fall from 40.65 to 10.72 ({{Table 5|ax:S8.T5}}); full finetuning passes 85%. PubMedQA (LoRA rank 4): BLEU 10.76 to 20.67, but steps rise from 18.09 to 31.57 ({{Table 6|ax:A3.T6}}). Early uses named by the paper: speech recognition (Interfaze) and radiology report drafting.

Limitations the authors state (Section 10)

A quality gap to the AR parent (no diffusion pretraining, a short SFT, a latency-targeted SD·RL, an AR-inherited architecture and data mix); conciseness multiplies speed but forgoes long-reasoning gains; occasional stuttering ("the the the"); a missing closing thought tag on multimodal prompts, which puts MMMU-Pro thinking (54.3) below non-thinking (66.0), found too late to fix; and AR regains the throughput lead around 32 concurrent requests, untuned for batching.
