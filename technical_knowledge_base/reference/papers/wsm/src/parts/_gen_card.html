WSM: Decay-Free Learning Rate Schedule via Checkpoint Merging for LLM Pre-training (WSM)

Changxin Tian*, Jiapeng Wang*, Qian Zhao*, Kunlong Chen, Jia Liu, Ziqi Liu, Jiaxin Mao, Wayne Xin Zhao, Zhiqiang Zhang, Jun Zhou (* equal contribution)
Ling Team, Ant Group; Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China · v1 23 July 2025; v2 11 August 2025 (adds citations of Hägele et al. 2024; no number changes). Added to the knowledge base 2026-08-31 on request

In one line: Proves checkpoint merging is algebraically equivalent to LR decay (merge weights invert to per-step gradient weights), so a constant LR plus a weighted merge of the last n checkpoints emulates cosine, linear, or 1-sqrt decay with no decay phase at all; beats a matched WSD anneal by ~1.3 points on a 16.3B MoE, with merge duration the dominant hyperparameter and EMA merging the worst.

How far to trust it: Believe the algebra and the recipe: keep the learning rate flat, save checkpoints, average the last stretch, and you get about what a decay gives, plus a cheap preview of the decayed model. Treat the size of the win as provisional: the +1.28 points is +1.18 at matched tokens, every comparison is one run with the best checkpoint picked on the test benchmarks, and the paper's merge-shape ranking reverses at matched tokens.
