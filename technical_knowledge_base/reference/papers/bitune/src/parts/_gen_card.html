Bitune: Leveraging Bidirectional Attention to Improve Decoder-Only LLMs (Bitune)

Dawid J. Kopiczko, Tijmen Blankevoort, Yuki M. Asano
v2: Fundamental AI Lab, University of Technology Nuremberg; Meta (Blankevoort). v1: Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, Meta, University of Amsterdam · arXiv v1 23 May 2024 ("Bitune: Bidirectional Instruction-Tuning"); v2 28 August 2025; EMNLP November 2025

In one line: The causal mask is required by generation, not by prompt processing. Bitune reads the prompt twice (causal and bidirectional, each with its own adapter), mixes the two sets of keys and values per block with learned weights, and generates causally as usual: +0.1 to +4.0 points over LoRA on five models, for about 3 times the finetuning time.

How far to trust it: Believe the direction: the gains are measured over 3 seeds, survive the noise on most models and in a parameter-matched baseline, and the learning rate was tuned for the baseline, not for Bitune. Treat the size as model-dependent (from +4.0 points on Gemma-2B to +0.1 on Gemma-7B), the claim that every component is needed as not shown (two of the ten ablation gaps are inside the noise), and the cost as larger than "negligible" whenever prompts are long and answers short.
