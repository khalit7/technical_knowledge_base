Ablations and examples

[[S4.SS2|§4.2, §4.3, Figures 2 to 4, Tables 5, 8, 9]]

Each ablation removes one design choice from the untrained JitMem-base or the trained JitMem, with all three executors ({{Figure 2|ax:S4.F2}}, {{Table 9|ax:A2.T9}}).

Drop = parent minus ablation, from Table 9; dots: Qwen3-8B, Gemini-2.5-Pro, GPT-5.4 executors. 3 orderings each; standard deviations of the rows run up to 4.7 points.

  • Task conditioning matters, more after training. Without the task in its input the curator is a query-independent summariser: up to 3.1 points lost on ALFWorld and 4.6 on WebShop untrained, 11.4 and 10.4 trained. RL learns to use the task ({{§4.2|ax:S4.SS2}}).
  • Store only successes. Storing everything with judged labels, as ReasoningBank and SkillOS do, costs 1.5 to 2.9 points on ALFWorld and 2.3 to 3.4 on WebShop.
  • Raw storage matters most on WebShop. Distilling ReasoningBank-style before storing costs 1.7 to 2.9 points on ALFWorld and 6.8 to 8.2 on WebShop (to 4.9 SR with Qwen3-8B, below no memory). Same curator, same task conditioning, only the write-time loss added: the cleanest test of the thesis.
  • RL learned to use the memory. Without retrieval, trained JitMem loses up to 14.8 points on ALFWorld and 15.2 on WebShop, falling to or below JitMem-base; with Qwen3-8B executing it stays above it (62.6 against 60.5; 17.6 against 11.7), the hint-writing effect of the {{question above|tab:t-read:pr-norag}}.
  • Small effects: rebuilding the training bank with curator-assisted trajectories and training 50 more steps adds 2.8 SR (Qwen3-8B), 0.9 (GPT-5.4) or nothing (Gemini); seeding the test bank with 100 training trajectories changes SR by at most 1.3; k = 5 instead of 3 by under 2 ({{Table 5|ax:S4.T5}}, {{Table 8|ax:A2.T8}}).
