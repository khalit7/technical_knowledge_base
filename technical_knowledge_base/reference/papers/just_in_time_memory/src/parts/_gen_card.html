Just-in-Time Memory: Learning to Curate Task-Adaptive Memory for LLM Agents (JitMem)

Yefan Zhou, Yang Li (equal contribution), Zeyu Leo Liu, Semih Yavuz, Shafiq Joty
Salesforce AI Research · 23 September 2026 (arXiv v1, the only version on 3 October 2026)

In one line: Curating an agent's memory at read time, once the task is known, beats distilling trajectories at write time by 16.2 and 16.3 points on ALFWorld and WebShop, and an untrained curator already wins, so the gain is the timing rather than the model.

How far to trust it: Believe the direction, not the headline's attribution. Untrained read-time curation beats untrained write-time memory with the same model in 15 of 17 comparisons, but the +16.2 and +16.3 come mostly from RL training (92% of the WebShop gain; the trained curator with no memory at all already beats every baseline there), 36 of the 60 baseline cells are SkillOS's published numbers rather than reruns, and the tau2-bench +3.9 is under two standard errors.
