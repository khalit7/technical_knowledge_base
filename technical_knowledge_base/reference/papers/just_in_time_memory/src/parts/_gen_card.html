Just-in-Time Memory: Learning to Curate Task-Adaptive Memory for LLM Agents (JitMem)

Yefan Zhou, Yang Li (equal contribution), Zeyu Leo Liu, Semih Yavuz, Shafiq Joty
Salesforce AI Research · 23 September 2026 (arXiv v1, the only version on 3 October 2026)

In one line: Curating an agent's memory at read time, once the task is known, usually beat distilling trajectories at write time in this paper's tests: untrained against untrained it won 15 of 17 comparisons on ALFWorld, WebShop and tau2-bench. The RL-trained curator leads the strongest baseline by 16.2 and 16.3 points on ALFWorld and WebShop with a Qwen3-8B executor, but most of that comes from the RL training (57% and 92% of the gain over no memory), the baselines in that Qwen3-8B block match SkillOS's published numbers to the decimal (taken from that paper, not rerun), and the tau2-bench +3.9 is under two standard errors.
