Results

[[S4.SS1|§4, §4.1, Tables 1 to 4]]

Setup. ALFWorld (household tasks, 140 test tasks), WebShop (shopping, 500 instructions; success rate and a 0 to 100 score) and tau2-bench (customer service with tools: airline, retail, telecom); frozen executors Qwen3-8B, Gemini-2.5-Pro and GPT-5.4. Baselines: no memory and three write-time memories, ReasoningBank, MemP and RL-trained SkillOS. "-base" is the same model as curator untrained; "-gpt" and "-gemini" a prompted frontier curator ({{§4|ax:S4}}). Success rate (SR) is in percentage points.

Mean over 3 task orderings, whiskers ±1 standard deviation ({{Table 1|ax:S4.T1}}). Hollow bars are numbers that match SkillOS's own published Tables 1 and 2 to the decimal, standard deviation included ({{SkillOS|https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06614}}), so they were taken from that paper, not rerun; the {{tables tab|tab:t-tables}} marks every cell.

Headline. With the RL-trained curator and Qwen3-8B as executor, JitMem reaches 77.4 SR on ALFWorld against 61.2 for RL-trained SkillOS (+16.2) and 32.8 SR on WebShop against 16.5 (+16.3; +20.5 on the score). Moved untouched to stronger executors, the margins shrink to +6.0 and +9.2 with Gemini-2.5-Pro and +8.8 and +10.9 with GPT-5.4 ({{Table 1|ax:S4.T1}}). And the training is simpler than SkillOS's: task reward only, no judge-model reward, no grouped streams ({{§4.1|ax:S4.SS1}}).

tau2-bench has no training split, so only untrained variants run there (GPT-5.4 executing): JitMem-gpt reaches 75.6 micro-averaged SR against 71.7 for ReasoningBank-gpt (+3.9; +3.0 macro), almost all from Telecom (72.6 against 61.6, +11.0). On Airline and Retail no memory method beats no memory beyond the variance, as the paper says: read-time curation helps most where tasks need procedure, not facts ({{Table 2|ax:S4.T2}}, {{§4.1|ax:S4.SS1}}).

Untrained against untrained. To separate timing from curator capacity, the paper compares untrained read-time and write-time memories with the same curator model: Qwen3-8B on ALFWorld, 60.5 against 55.7 and 53.1; Gemini-2.5-Pro on WebShop, 61.0 against 40.2 and 41.0; GPT-5.4 on tau2-bench, 75.6 against 71.7 and 66.4. A weaker curator can even win: on ALFWorld with GPT-5.4 executing, a Qwen3-8B curator (79.3) beats ReasoningBank and SkillOS curated by GPT-5.4 (77.9, 70.0). Hence the claim that an untrained curator "is already competitive with or surpasses" the baselines and that read-time curation "itself is a major source of the gain" ({{abstract|https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27334}}, {{§4.1|ax:S4.SS1}}).

The headline WebShop cell is Qwen3-8B as executor. There, does the untrained read-time curator (JitMem-base, Qwen3-8B curating) beat the untrained write-time memories built on the same model?

JitMem-base scores 11.7 SR against 12.0 (MemP) and 13.6 (SkillOS-base), and a 32.5 score against 33.3 for no memory at all ({{Table 1|ax:S4.T1}}). Across every like-for-like comparison the tables allow (untrained read-time against the best untrained write-time memory with the same curator model, all shown below), read time wins %%R:untrained_wins%% of %%R:untrained_n%%, and the only two losses are this cell, the one behind the headline +16.3.

Each row: read-time (filled dot) against the best untrained write-time memory with the same curator model (ring) and no memory (grey tick). Derived from Tables 1 and 2.

The ablations include the RL-trained curator with retrieval switched off: it gets no past trajectories at all and can only write hints from what it learned in training. On WebShop with the Qwen3-8B executor, where does it land?

17.6 SR, above SkillOS's 16.5, with no memory at all ({{Table 9|ax:A2.T9}}). On ALFWorld it is the same story: 62.6 against 61.2. So the headline gains split into three parts: what an untrained read-time curator adds over no memory, what RL training adds as a hint-writer even without memory, and what RL adds once it can read the retrieved trajectories. So the claim holds untrained against untrained (15 of 17 cells), but not for the headline numbers: with the Qwen3-8B executor most of the +16.2 and nearly all of the +16.3 come from RL training. With stronger executors the untrained curator supplies about half or more of the gain over no memory. The abstract's closing words, "training the curator further compounds the improvement", are fair; the Takeaway of this database row ("the gain is the timing rather than the model") overstates it for the cells it quotes.

Transfer. Trained with Qwen3-8B executing and moved to GPT-5.4, the curator scores 86.7 on ALFWorld, 1.4 points short of one trained with GPT-5.4 (88.1), and beats JitMem-base with both stronger executors (+6.2 and +7.4 on ALFWorld, +6.1 SR on WebShop) ({{Table 3|ax:S4.T3}}). But an untrained Gemini-2.5-Pro curator scores 61.0 SR on WebShop, above the trained curator's 50.5 with the same executor: curator strength matters a lot there ({{Table 1|ax:S4.T1}}).

Shorter context, fewer steps. On ALFWorld with GPT-5.4, JitMem-base adds 1.9K input tokens a task over no memory (9.0K) where ReasoningBank adds 10.7K and SkillOS-base 13.4K, and takes 18.5% to 21.9% fewer steps; training trims input by 10.1%, output by 13.0% and steps by 12.1% more. Trained JitMem uses 50.3% to 56.3% fewer input tokens and 28.4% to 31.4% fewer steps than the write-time methods ({{Table 4|ax:S4.T4}}, {{§1|ax:S1}}). The curator's own call is not counted.
