Best resources

The Illustrated Transformer

Jay Alammar: still the canonical visual walkthrough of Q/K/V, multi-head attention and the encoder-decoder data flow.

(30 min)

The Annotated Transformer

Harvard NLP, 2022 revision: the paper reimplemented line by line in PyTorch; the fastest route from equations to working code.

(1h 30m)

Attention in transformers, visually explained

3Blue1Brown: the best geometric intuition for what attention heads actually compute.

(30 min)

Let's build GPT from scratch

Andrej Karpathy: builds a decoder-only Transformer from an empty file; makes the causal masking and multi-head mechanics concrete.

(2h)

Transformer Explainer

Polo Club, Georgia Tech: a live GPT-2 small running in your browser: type a prompt and inspect every attention head and the sampling step. Its paper is arXiv 2408.04619. The idea this page's Run a Transformer tab borrows, at toy scale and encoder-decoder.

(20 min)

LLM Visualization

Brendan Bycroft: a 3D walk through every matrix multiply of a small GPT (nano-gpt) at true shape, step by step.

(30 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

BERT

2018: the encoder stack alone, pretrained bidirectionally.

GPT-3

2020: the decoder stack scaled; the parallelism this paper unlocked is what made those curves reachable.

Scaling Laws

2020: loss as a power law in parameters, data and compute, measured on Transformers.

RoFormer (RoPE)

2021: rotary embeddings replace the sinusoidal positional encoding.

FlashAttention

2022: exact attention made IO-aware; attacks the memory traffic this architecture created.

PagedAttention (vLLM)

2023: pages the KV cache like virtual memory to serve many sequences.

ViT

2020: the same encoder applied to image patches.

Mamba

2023: the main post-Transformer attempt to remove the O(n²) attention core.

T5

2019: the encoder-decoder kept and scaled, every task as text to text.
