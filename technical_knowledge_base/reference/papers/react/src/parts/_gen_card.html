ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models (ReAct)

Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, Nan Du, Izhak Shafran, Karthik Narasimhan, Yuan Cao
Princeton University (Yao, Narasimhan); Google Research, Brain team (the rest). Yao did the work as a Google intern · October 2022 (arXiv v1, 6 October 2022); v3, 10 March 2023, adds the GPT-3 appendix; published at ICLR 2023

In one line: Interleaving free-form thoughts with environment actions in one prompted loop beats reasoning-only and acting-only baselines (HotpotQA/FEVER grounding, 71% ALFWorld, +10% WebShop) and is the template every modern agent loop descends from.
