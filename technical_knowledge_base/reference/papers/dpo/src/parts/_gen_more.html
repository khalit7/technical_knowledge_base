Best resources

Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)

Cameron R. Wolfe, Deep (Learning) Focus: the full derivation walked through step by step, plus where DPO sits in the modern post-training pipeline, with TRL code.

(40 min)

DPO Trainer documentation

Hugging Face TRL: the production implementation: dataset format, the logged implicit-reward metrics (rewards/chosen, rewards/rejected, rewards/margins) and the catalogue of loss variants exposed as loss_type.

(25 min)

Do we need RL for RLHF?

Nathan Lambert, Interconnects: the DPO against PPO debate laid out with the evidence on both sides.

(20 min)

Direct-Alignment Algorithms (RLHF Book, chapter 12)

Nathan Lambert: a textbook chapter on DPO and its descendants, with the derivation, the failure modes and the variants side by side.
