LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models (LoRA)

Edward J. Hu*, Yelong Shen*, Phillip Wallis, Zeyuan Allen-Zhu, Yuanzhi Li, Shean Wang, Lu Wang, Weizhu Chen (* equal contribution)
Microsoft · June 2021 (arXiv v1); v2 October 2021

In one line: Freeze W0 and train a zero-initialized low-rank update W = W0 + BA (scaled alpha/r) on attention weights; matches full fine-tuning on GPT-3 175B with 10,000x smaller checkpoints and zero inference latency after merging.

How far to trust it: Believe the mechanism and the engineering: merging is exact, the parameter and storage savings are arithmetic, and five years of use have confirmed it. Treat the claim that LoRA beats full fine-tuning as unproven: the margins sit inside the noise, the baselines were not run under the same conditions, and the GPT-3 results come from runs nobody outside could repeat.
