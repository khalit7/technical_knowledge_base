Then and now: what was done to the spectrum

RoPE survived unchanged as a mechanism; what later work changed is which pairs rotate and how fast. Every long-context method of 2023 to 2025 is a rewrite of θi (or of the positions it multiplies) in a model that already uses RoPE. The animation steps through them on one picture: one bar per rotation pair of an attention head, its height the number of full turns that pair makes inside the context the model works at. A pair under one turn (below the dashed line) never shows its full circle in that context, so angles past what it reached in training are new to the model, the failure the {{toy|tab:t-run}} shows.

Each step's head size, base and context come from its paper or config.json.

Turns for pair i = L · θ′i / 2π on a log scale, where θ′ is the step's (possibly rescaled) frequency and L the context it runs at. Blue pairs rotate at their trained speed, orange ones are slowed by interpolation, gold ones are on YaRN's ramp, grey dimensions carry no rotation. For the per-model YaRN bands of current configs (gpt-oss, DeepSeek V3, Kimi K2), see the {{LLM Architecture Gallery|n:3c65c17b0d0d81be8a07f2562fa2030a}}.
