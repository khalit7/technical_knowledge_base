RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE)

Jianlin Su, Yu Lu, Shengfeng Pan, Ahmed Murtadha, Bo Wen, Yunfeng Liu
Zhuiyi Technology, Shenzhen · April 2021 (arXiv v1 20 April 2021; v5 November 2023; journal version in Neurocomputing 2024)

In one line: Rotate each query/key 2D pair by a position-proportional angle (theta_i = 10000^(-2i/d)) so the QK dot product depends only on relative offset; became the universal LLM positional encoding and the substrate for all context-extension methods (PI, NTK, YaRN, iRoPE).

How far to trust it: Believe the mathematics: the relative-offset property is exact and checkable in one line, and every major open LLM since 2023 has adopted it. Do not read the experiments as evidence that RoPE is better: they are single runs with gains of 0.2 BLEU and 1.5 to 1.7 points, the GLUE comparison sets RoFormer's validation scores against BERT's test-server scores, and the long-text result stays inside the length RoFormer was pretrained at.
