Best resources

Rotary Embeddings: A Relative Revolution

EleutherAI (Biderman, Black, Foster, Gao, Hallahan, He, Wang, Wang): the canonical English explainer; complex-number view, derivation, runnable code, and the early adoption story (GPT-NeoX, GPT-J).

(30 min)

Transformer升级之路：2、博采众长的旋转式位置编码

Jianlin Su (first author), kexue.fm: the first author's original blog derivation, which predates and is more readable than the paper (Chinese).

(30 min)

You could have designed state of the art positional encoding

Christopher Fleetwood, Hugging Face: rederives RoPE step by step from desiderata; the best intuition builder for why each design choice is forced.

(35 min)

Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE), annotated

labml.ai: line-by-line PyTorch of the efficient elementwise form.

(25 min)

RoFormer model documentation

Hugging Face Transformers: the model as integrated in Transformers, with the released checkpoints (docs).

(10 min)

Round and Round We Go! What makes Rotary Positional Encodings useful?

Barbero, Vitvitskyi, Perivolaropoulos, Pascanu, Veličković (2024): argues the long-term decay is not why RoPE works: for Gaussian queries and keys the expected score does not decay at all; Gemma 7B uses the highest frequencies for positional heads and the lowest as semantic channels.

(40 min)

Train Short, Test Long: Attention with Linear Biases (ALiBi)

Press, Smith, Lewis (2021): the paper that measured how sinusoidal and rotary encodings break when tested beyond the training length; the question this page's toy asks.

(40 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Attention Is All You Need (Transformer)

2017: the additive sinusoidal encoding whose frequency spectrum RoPE reuses multiplicatively.

BERT

2018: the pretraining baseline RoFormer swaps its absolute position embedding out of; its GLUE test-server row is the one RoFormer's Table 2 quotes.

The Llama 3 Herd of Models

2024: RoPE at base 500,000 plus staged long-context extension; the mainstream deployment pattern.

DeepSeek-V3 Technical Report

2024: MLA's decoupled RoPE keys, the trick that reconciles RoPE with latent KV compression.

Mixtral of Experts

2024: a representative RoPE-based open-weight architecture.

ViT

2020: learned absolute position embeddings on image patches; later vision models moved to 2D RoPE.
