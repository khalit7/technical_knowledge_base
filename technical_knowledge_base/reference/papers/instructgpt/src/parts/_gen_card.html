Paper · NeurIPS 2022 · arXiv 2203.02155

Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback (InstructGPT)

Long Ouyang, Jeff Wu, Xu Jiang, Diogo Almeida, Carroll L. Wainwright, Pamela Mishkin, Chong Zhang, Sandhini Agarwal, Katarina Slama, Alex Ray, John Schulman, Jacob Hilton, Fraser Kelton, Luke Miller, Maddie Simens, Amanda Askell, Peter Welinder, Paul Christiano, Jan Leike, Ryan Lowe
OpenAI (a joint project of the Alignment team; Ryan Lowe and Jan Leike team leads) · March 2022 (arXiv v1, 4 March 2022, the only version)

In one line: Defined the 3-stage RLHF recipe (SFT -> reward model -> PPO with KL penalty, plus PPO-ptx to cut the alignment tax) that made a 1.3B model preferred over 175B GPT-3 and became the ChatGPT-era industry standard.

52.7% against 24.0%
1.3B InstructGPT and 175B GPT-3, each against 175B SFT
the smallest InstructGPT beats GPT-3 with 135 times its parameters · Figure 1
85 ± 3% of the time
175B InstructGPT preferred to 175B GPT-3, head to head
71 ± 4% against GPT-3 given a well-chosen few-shot prompt · §4.1
60 petaflop/s-days
to train 175B PPO-ptx, against 3,640 to pretrain GPT-3
1.6% of the pretraining compute (SFT adds 4.9) · §5.1

How far to trust it: Believe the recipe and the ordering it produced (PPO above SFT above prompted GPT-3 above GPT-3, with tight intervals, held-out labelers agreeing, and the head-to-head numbers consistent with Figure 1). Read the 1.3B-over-175B headline as true for this prompt distribution judged by these 40 labelers, and the truthfulness and toxicity gains as smaller than the text states: about 1.4 to 1.5 times on TruthfulQA by human labels, not twice, and 16 to 20% less toxicity, not 25%.
