Tables 4 and 5: RL, and Chain

{{Table 4|ax:S5.T4}}: one GRPO run per arm; whole tasks of ALFWorld's 140 seen and 134 unseen games in brackets. {{Table 5|ax:S5.T5}}: SWE-bench Verified, success rate and average steps; whole tasks of 407 in brackets.

The RL recipe ({{Appendix F.1|ax:A6.SS1}}): Qwen3-8B-base trained with GRPO on one node of 8 H100s; vLLM rollouts (tensor parallel 1, GPU memory utilisation 0.5, eager mode); FSDP with parameter and optimizer offloading and gradient checkpointing; global batch 16, PPO mini-batch 256, PPO and log-probability micro-batches of 4 per GPU; prompts up to 4,096 tokens, responses up to 512; history length 50 and at most 50 steps per episode; seed 0; an invalid-action penalty with coefficient 0.1; rollout temperature 0.4; 150 epochs, which is 150 steps. The released rl/scripts/run_grpo.sh adds what the paper omits, a GRPO group of 8 rollouts per prompt, and uses micro-batches of 8 rather than 4.
