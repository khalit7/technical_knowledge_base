Best resources

{{We Must Pace the Frontier (Dario Amodei)|https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier}} (23 min)

The essay itself: why now, the three steps, what embedded evaluators get, and the four levels of agreement with China.

{{DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash: Pushing the Limits of KV Cache Compression|https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19969}} (~1h 50m)

The technical report: the causal encoder-decoder, CSA2's full, reindex and reuse modes, FP4 caching and bounded replay. Sections 2 and 3.2 carry the mechanism animated here.

{{DeepSeek-V4.1 Flash, the long breakdown (zartbot)|https://zartbot.github.io/blog/model_arch/dsv41flash_arch/en.html}} (2h 26m)

Where the 890 bytes per token come from, entry by entry, and the cache-hierarchy reading of cross-layer reuse.

{{Real-SWE (Specific Labs)|https://withspecific.com/benchmarks/real-swe}} (~13 min)

The benchmark, its per-task table, failure taxonomy and cost chart, as it reads now; the archived copies are linked on the card above.

{{Threat intelligence report, September 2026 (Anthropic)|https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026}} (~2h 40m)

Every case study in full, from the exploit foundries to the election-manipulation platform; the trends section alone is about 15 minutes.

{{Inside the megakernel serving engine for North Mini Code (Cohere)|https://cohere.com/blog/megakernels}} (22 min)

Why batch-1 decode is bandwidth-bound, and how one persistent kernel with continuous batching and paged attention gets to 62% of the bound.

{{Recurrent Looped Transformer (project page)|https://yifanzhang-pro.github.io/recurrent-looped-tranformer/}} (~11 min)

The state-carrying decoder, the execution schedule that makes prefill sequential, and the first experiments.

{{GPT-6 Astra, Looped Transformers, and Hidden Reasoning (Sebastian Raschka)|https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/gpt-6-astra-looped-transformers-and}} (~29 min)

The looped-transformer literature, and a careful argument about what shorter reasoning traces do and do not say about monitorability.

{{Scenarios for our Economic Future (Anthropic)|https://www.anthropic.com/institute/econ-scenarios}} (~14 min)

The interactive explorer behind the calculator; the {{working paper|https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/files/4zrzovbb/website/cf58f84d46a4a76bf5a5b039ac695fba6b80041c.pdf}} has the model and Table 3.

{{TPU Inference Externalization Full Steam Ahead (SemiAnalysis)|https://inferencex.semianalysis.com/blog/tpu-inferencex-full-steam}} (35 min)

Ironwood against B200 and B300 on performance per dollar, and the software Google has to hand outside customers.

{{Hyper-tau-bench: Evaluating agents that build agents (Sierra)|https://sierra.ai/blog/hyper-t-bench-evaluating-agents-that-build-agents}} (~5 min)
