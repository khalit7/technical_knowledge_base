Memory prices: two comparisons, two different headlines

The article's headline joins two numbers measured differently. Switch between its two tables. Each line runs from the earlier price to the August 2026 price, on a log scale so that equal ratios look equal.

Both tables from {{Tom's Hardware, Aug 17|https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/ram/memory-prices-climb-500-percent-in-12-months-up-to-10x-the-lowest-ever-tracked-prices-128gb-of-ddr5-now-usd3-399}}. Every percentage in its year-over-year table was recomputed from its two prices and matches to the rounding (src/recompute.py; the table prints one as "+$372%"). The prose calls the $72 32GB price "last year", but the table it comes from lists it as the lowest-ever price.
