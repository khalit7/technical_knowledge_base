Money moving: the week's figures by kind

The week's dollar figures are not one quantity. A price paid for a company and a funding-round valuation both put a value on the whole company, so they share an axis; a revenue run rate (dollars a year) and a financing guarantee (a commitment of credit) are different kinds and get rows of their own, never a bar beside the others. Each company's earlier value is shown too, because the multiple says more than the headline.

Two of the companies are this week's deals from Top stories: [[Stripe and OpenRouter|t-top|i-stripe-openrouter]] and [[Hugging Face|t-top|i-hf-offers]]; Micro1 and General Intuition are items on this tab, and the guarantee is [[Nvidia's, in Compute|t-compute|i-nvidia-guarantee]].

Sources: OpenRouter's price and May valuation, and the founders' and investors' shares, from {{TechCrunch (citing the New York Times)|https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/stripe-didnt-really-buy-openrouter-because-of-the-singularity/}}; Hugging Face's 2023 valuation, the rejected Nvidia investment and the reported offers from {{TechCrunch (citing Business Insider)|https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/hugging-face-reportedly-in-talks-to-be-acquired-for-13b/}}; the deal from {{TechCrunch, Sep 3|https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/}}; the rest from the items on this tab and in Compute. Multiples are derived: reported price divided by earlier valuation.
