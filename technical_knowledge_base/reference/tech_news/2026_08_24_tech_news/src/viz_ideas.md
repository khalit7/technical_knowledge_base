## Inspiration
The harness animation follows the DeepSeek MLA explainer and the OpenAI page's harness animation (O1): same input, same scale, a caption per step, counters, and the method it replaced as the first toggle. The diffusion lanes borrow the side-by-side clock of the reasoning page's "one chain against five chains" (R1). The memory dumbbells follow the "show both readings, never splice" rule.

## What the methodology lacked for a news page
- News items are not mechanisms; most need no visual. The scoring needed a rule for **the index visual** of a digest (the week strip): it scores low on every mechanism question but is what makes 33 items scannable, and it is reused by every issue.
- A rule for **dated follow-ups**: the issue's text is a dated record and must not be edited; later facts go in a separate, dated section linked both ways, and checks against the sources go in labelled boxes beside the original text.
- A rule for **sources that changed after publication** (Cerebras's page, DeepSeek's docs): read the Wayback capture nearest the issue date for the "as reported" check, and record the change as a follow-up.
- Data reuse: the issue is data (items.json parsed verbatim from live.md, annotations.json for dates, tags, numbers, notes, follow-ups and visual placement), so the next five issues reuse mk_items.py, mk_issue.py, the strip and the money parts unchanged.
