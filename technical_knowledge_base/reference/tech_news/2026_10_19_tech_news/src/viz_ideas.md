# Visual ideas for the 2026-10-19 issue

Chosen fresh for this window (`.claude/skills/explainer-page/visuals/choosing.md`).

## Built
- **Week strip** (`v_strip`, `12_js_strip.js`): kept from the series; axis widened to three weeks.
- **Score against cost per task** (`v_cost`, `13_js_cost.js`), under the open-weight story: the window's headline split ("open leader" by score or by cost) is a two-dimensional fact; one plane shows it. Artificial Analysis v4.3.2 scores and its cost per index task; models without a published per-task cost are named in the caption, not placed.
- **ARC-AGI-3, standard harness against Provider Adapter** (`v_arc`, `14_js_arc.js`), the before/after animation: the same models on the same tasks under two harnesses, model by model, using the shared step controller (`10b_js_anim.js`).

## Considered and rejected
- Revenue bars for OpenAI and Anthropic: the two figures measure different things (one counts cloud resale), so one axis would mislead (tech-news rule: never different kinds of figure on one axis).
- An egress diagram of the agent-containment incidents: the rules say never draw an escape route step by step; the text and the linked report carry it.
- Haiku 5.5 tiered pricing calculator: a one-line arithmetic fact (90% below up to 100K, 50% above); the card states it.
- Looped-model compute chart: the papers' compute figures are not on comparable setups.
