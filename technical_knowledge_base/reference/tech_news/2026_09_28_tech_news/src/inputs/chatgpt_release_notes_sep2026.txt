September 29, 2026
A new Pro plan with Astra Ultrafast
We’re introducing Pro 500, a new $500/month plan with the highest included usage of the Pro plans and access to Astra Ultrafast in ChatGPT Work and Codex. Select Ultrafast in the model picker to use a faster service tier for GPT-6 Astra. It uses your included allowance first, then your credit balance after that allowance is used.
Among Pro plans, Ultrafast is available only with Pro 500. Buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock it. Pro 500 subscriptions are available through ChatGPT on the web at launch.
Learn more about ChatGPT Pro tiers.
Codex Cloud
Codex Cloud lets you start and continue coding tasks from desktop, web, or mobile using reusable environments with your project’s repositories, tools, and dependencies. Each task gets its own isolated workspace, and tasks can continue while your computer is asleep. In managed workspaces, your workspace’s Codex cloud-access settings apply.
To connect repositories and prepare an environment, see: Set up and configure Codex Cloud. For availability and usage, see: Using Codex with your ChatGPT plan.
Create and collaborate on Pages
Turn a conversation into a Page, start from a template, or begin writing directly in Space. Edit the text yourself or ask ChatGPT to revise it, add charts, or create interactive content. Collaborators can work on the same Page and leave comments, with each person using their own ChatGPT.
Pages follow Space’s availability and sharing settings. Sharing a Page does not give other people access to your private chats or memory; information added to the Page is visible to people who can view it.
Creating, editing, and scheduling Sites
Site owners and editors on Plus and Pro can select Edit site from a published Site in a desktop browser to continue editing in ChatGPT, review changes, and publish when ready.
Site owners can also create recurring cloud schedules from Automations. These updates are available where Sites is supported. Learn more about creating and managing Sites.
GPT-6.1 Sol in ChatGPT Work and Codex
GPT-6.1 Sol improves on GPT-6 Sol in agentic coding, computer use, and professional work. We’re rolling it out in ChatGPT Work and Codex, starting with Pro users and expanding to Plus, Business, Enterprise, and Edu. Enterprise and Edu workspace owners can enable model access in Workspace settings > Permissions & roles, on the Workspace tab or through a custom role. Learn how to manage model access with role-based access control.
For availability and model selection, see: ChatGPT Work and Codex.
Meet your dot
We’re introducing dots: always-on agents in ChatGPT that can take on ongoing work and keep making progress between conversations. Give your dot a goal, connect the apps it needs, and define what it can do on its own. Powered by GPT-6 Astra, your dot has its own cloud computer and can bring results back for your review.
Dots are rolling out gradually to eligible Pro and Business Premium users aged 18 and older in supported markets. Pro access excludes the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the UK at launch. Enterprise access is a beta and is off by default. Create your dot in the ChatGPT desktop app or on desktop web.
For the next month, dots usage won’t count toward eligible Pro, Business, and Enterprise users’ plan allowances. After this period, we’ll share usage terms for each plan.
Learn more about getting started with your dot.

September 25, 2026
Security history in ChatGPT
We’re introducing security history, a new way to review recent security activity for your OpenAI account. You can see sign-ins, sign-outs, and changes to multi-factor authentication (MFA), passkeys, and other security settings. Events include the time, location, and device details. Some details may be approximate or unavailable.
To view your history in ChatGPT on the web, go to Settings > Security and login, then select Security history. For help reviewing unfamiliar activity and securing your account, see: Keeping your OpenAI account secure.
September 23, 2026
Use plugins in Voice and get work done by speaking
Live now supports plugins on web, iOS, and Android. You can use the plugins and connected apps available to your account during a Voice conversation and follow written responses in the chat.
Voice is also available in Work on web and mobile. Ask it to create documents, presentations, and spreadsheets, use connected apps, or work in a browser. When you end a Voice call in Work, an unfinished task can continue in text.
Free and Go users can use Voice in Chat with the plugins their plan supports. Voice in Work requires both Voice and Work access. Existing app connections, permissions, and usage limits apply.
Learn more: ChatGPT Voice.

Learn more about ChatGPT Work and Codex.
Create flashcards in ChatGPT
You can now create interactive flashcards in ChatGPT. Ask for flashcards on a topic you want to learn, or upload your notes and ask ChatGPT to turn them into flashcards.
Tap a card to flip it, then select ✔️ if you know the answer or ❌ to practice it again later. Shuffle your cards to practice in a different order.
Flashcards are automatically saved in your library. To practice again, open them there or ask ChatGPT to review them back.
Available on mobile and web for all ChatGPT plans.
September 21, 2026
Updated models and usage limits in ChatGPT Voice
ChatGPT Voice can now use GPT-5.6 or GPT-6 Astra when it needs to search or reason through harder questions. Choose your model and reasoning effort using the same controls as text chat. Available models and their usage limits depend on your plan.
We’re also simplifying GPT-Live daily usage limits:
Go: Up to 3 hours with GPT-Live-1 mini, replacing GPT-Live-1 access.
Plus: Up to 3 hours with GPT-Live-1.
Pro ($100/month): Up to 15 hours with GPT-Live-1.
Pro ($200/month): Unlimited GPT-Live-1 usage.
Plus and Pro no longer switch to GPT-Live mini after reaching a Voice limit.
With these updates, the separate Instant/Medium/High Voice intelligence levels are deprecated.
