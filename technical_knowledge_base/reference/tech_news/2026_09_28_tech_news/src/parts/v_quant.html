How many bits per weight fit the box: dlab's three claims

Weight memory is parameters × bits per weight ÷ 8. Each row is one of dlab's models at the bit width you choose; the line is the memory you have. A claim like "125B on one 24 GB GPU" fixes the bit width it needs, before any room for the KV cache and activations.

Source: {{dlab Open Source Week|https://timdettmers.com/2026/09/21/dlab-open-source-week/}} (Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B at 1.5 bits per weight, "roughly a tenth" of half-precision memory, 450 tokens per second on the Mac/Metal kernels; Qwen 3.8 Flash Next 125B on a single 24 GB GPU; DeepSeek V4.1 550B on AMD Strix, a DGX Spark or a 128 GB MacBook). The post states the bit width only for the 35B model; the widths for the other two are derived here as the most that fits, weights only. Defaults reproduce "roughly a tenth" independently: 1.5/16 = 9.4%. GB here are 10⁹ bytes; recomputed in src/recompute.py.
