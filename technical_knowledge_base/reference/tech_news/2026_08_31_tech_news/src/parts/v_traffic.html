"Less traffic, better traffic", on one scale

The three growth rates in the item are percentages of very different bases. Here the sessions are drawn in millions on one axis, before and after AI Overviews spread (Brainlabs' 54 clients), and the key events on a second axis of their own, because a visit and a conversion are different kinds of thing. AI referrals grew 163%, from a base so small that they replaced about a twentieth of the organic sessions that disappeared, and roughly a tenth of the conversions.

Sources: {{Brainlabs|https://www.brainlabsdigital.com/why-organic-traffic-is-declining-ai-seo/}} (organic 140.1 to 125.4 million sessions; AI referrals up "more than 163%", "just under 800,000" sessions; AI key events up 335%; key-event rates 13.4% for AI referrals and 10.4% for organic over the period) and {{Digiday|https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-how-ai-search-has-impacted-the-web-traffic-of-over-50-advertisers/}}. Derived in src/recompute.py: AI sessions before = 0.8 / 1.63 = 0.49 million and after = 1.29 million; AI key events split so that after = 4.35 x before and both together are 13.4% of all AI sessions. The organic key-event rate is an assumption (the slider), because Brainlabs gives organic's rate for the whole period, not for each window.
