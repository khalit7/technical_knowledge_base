Skills against size: WikiSkill's whole table

Each row is one model, smallest first, drawn from its score with no skills (grey) to its score with skills evolved by the chosen method (colour). The dashed line is the best score any model reaches without skills on this test; a coloured end to the right of it is a model that, with skills, beats every model without them. Pick a test and a method: the average hides how uneven the gains are.

Source: Table 1 of {{WikiSkill, arXiv 2608.27454|https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27454}} (every method starts from an empty skill set; scores in %). Defaults reproduce the paper's averages (Gemini-3.5-Flash 49.5 to 68.1, Qwen-3.6-27B 39.4 to 63.3) independently: each average is recomputed from its five columns in src/recompute.py. Gemini-3.5-Flash's size is not published; it is drawn last as the closed model the paper compares against.
