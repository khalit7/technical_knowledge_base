## Data, formulas and sources
- DFlash 2: [model card](https://huggingface.co/incoai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2) acceptance and throughput tables (SGLang, one H200, block 8, xhigh effort); [Inco AI post](https://inco.ai/blog/dflash2/) for the selector (top 16 candidates, pairwise scores), the two-tap convolutions, "around 1% added cycle latency" and DFlash's production use. Derived: time per verification = acceptance / throughput (plain 14.51 ms, MTP 28.12, DSpark 23.53, DFlash 2 23.13); draft cost = that minus one plain step (the assumption that a verification costs one decoding step is labelled); DFlash v1's cycle assumed 1% cheaper than DFlash 2's. The sentence, the guesses and where each block breaks are illustrative.
- MCP: [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/08/mcp-stateless-gateway/) for every mechanism; the instances, restart and calls are illustrative; counters are of the scenario (9 against 6 round trips).
- Parameters: Hugging Face API `safetensors.parameters`, `usedStorage` and `createdAt` for each repository (saved in `inputs/hf_meta.json`); headline counts from MarkTechPost, the Qwen card ("125B with 6B activated, plus 51B n-gram embedding and 4B MTP"), Tencent, Nvidia's 0813 card and Cohere's docs. NVFP4 = 4 bits + an 8-bit scale per 16 values = 4.5 bits.
- WikiSkill: [arXiv 2608.27454](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27454) Table 1, all five models and five methods.
- Traffic: [Brainlabs](https://www.brainlabsdigital.com/why-organic-traffic-is-declining-ai-seo/) and [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/in-graphic-detail-how-ai-search-has-impacted-the-web-traffic-of-over-50-advertisers/). Derived AI sessions 0.49 and 1.29 million (0.8 / 1.63), AI key events split 1 : 4.35 summing to 13.4% of AI sessions; the organic key-event rate is the slider's assumption.

## Inspiration
The two animations follow the DeepSeek MLA explainer: the same input, the method it replaced as the first mode, a caption per step, counters, and time drawn to the same scale across modes (the DFlash lanes keep every mode's total visible while one plays). The DFlash cells borrow Inco's own Figure 1 (top picks that stutter, then candidates and a selected path). The traffic chart applies the "never one axis for different kinds" rule to the item's three percentages. The parameter counter applies the Methodology's rule to "sum the parameter table every way and show which sum reproduces the headline".

## What the methodology lacked for this page
- **An issue assembled from parts.** Two runs and a backfill produced overlapping section lists. The layout rule "one tab per section" needed a reading: tabs follow the subject (all "Models" subsections in one tab), each card is labelled with its part, and the parts' own notes are kept verbatim in one place (At a glance) plus under the subsection they head (Security). The week strip gained a filter by part.
- **Links that point at the wrong thing.** Three items link to a source that is about something else (the original DeepSeek-V4-Pro NVFP4 card for the 0813 build; a TechCrunch piece on a different Anthropic paper; Notion auto-links of "Z.ai"). A Correction names the right source; auto-links are dropped in coverage with the reason.
- **Model repositories as a source of record.** The Hugging Face API gives exact parameter counts per number format, creation dates and storage, which settled three items (Qwen's 180B, Hy4's 1.56 TB, which NVFP4 build) without reading a card. Worth a line in the Methodology next to the refs API.
- **Unsourced items that can be sourced later.** Fifteen items carry no link. For eight a source was found and added in a labelled Links box (Brainlabs and Digiday, Dataconomy, Classmethod, The Decoder, The New Stack, ServeTheHome, NPR, and the Codex GitHub issue the backfill asked for); for two (the DALL-E retirement, the FT's "other income" story) a source was found but refused automated reading; Ternus's start is confirmed by the follow-up; the spot-price figure, the Hacker News counts and the base-models essay remain unconfirmed; two items only point at others in the issue.
