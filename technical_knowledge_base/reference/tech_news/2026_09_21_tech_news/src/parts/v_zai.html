Thirteen days, twelve steps: how the 3.22x accumulated

End-to-end throughput of GLM-5.3-Flash serving against the W8A8 baseline, day by day, as Z.ai's Figure 1 plots it, coloured by phase. No single change is the story: the largest jump is 1.41x to 1.97x (Layer Split), and three steps (the hierarchical cache, mixed-precision cache quantisation and chunked MQA) moved nothing end to end, which is exactly when only local measurements can tell whether a change helped. Correctness: compare execution paths

The KDA kernel's context-parallel state merge used tl.dot, which defaulted to TF32 even for FP32 inputs; the error grew with context length and showed up only when partitioned and unpartitioned runs were compared. Fixed with input_precision="tf32x3" and merged upstream ({{FLA PR #1180|https://github.com/fla-org/flash-linear-attention/pull/1180}}).

System behaviour: read the timeline

The target was prefill plus KV transfer within 5% of prefill alone; the gap exceeded 20% in some scenarios. A timeline showed DeepEP's intra-node dispatch and combine holding the Python GIL, so the Mooncake transfer thread could not submit. After releasing it the gap fell below 1%.

From {{Z.ai, 17 Sep|https://z.ai/blog/glm-built-its-inference-infrastructure}}: values transcribed from Figure 1 (no points at days 6 and 12); the text says "less than two weeks" and "tripling". The issue's "20% slowdown" is the 20% gap against a 5% criterion, not a 20% fall in throughput.
