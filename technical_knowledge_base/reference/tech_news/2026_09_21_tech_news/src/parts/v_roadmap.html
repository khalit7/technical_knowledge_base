Huawei's roadmap, 2025 against 2026

Each row is one part, by quarter. Hollow rings are the dates Huawei gave at Huawei Connect 2025; filled dots are the dates it gave on 17 September 2026; the arrow is the pull-in. The 2025 plan had one Ascend 960 in Q4 2027; the 2026 plan splits it into a training part (960DT) and an inference part (960PR), and brings both forward.

Sources: {{Huawei Connect 2025 keynote (18 Sep 2025)|https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2025/9/hc-xu-keynote-speech}}; {{Huawei Connect 2026 keynote (17 Sep 2026)|https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2026/9/hc-wang-keynote}} for the Ascend dates ("three quarters ahead", "one quarter ahead"); {{TrendForce|https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/09/17/news-huawei-speeds-up-ai-chip-roadmap-reportedly-pulls-ascend-960dt-forward-three-quarters-to-1q27/}} for the Atlas SuperPoD dates, which are not in Huawei's English release. The 2026 plan gives years only for the 970 and 980, drawn as a bar across the year. The pull-ins are reproduced independently from the two roadmaps (src/recompute.py).
