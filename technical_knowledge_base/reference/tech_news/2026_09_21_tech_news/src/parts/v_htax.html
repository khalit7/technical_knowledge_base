The harness tax: same model, three harnesses

Each row is one model run in Claude Code, Codex CLI and Pi on the same 30 tasks (three attempts each). Top: how many tasks it solved. Bottom: what an attempt cost, on a log scale, so equal gaps are equal ratios. The grey bar is the spread across harnesses: narrow on top, wide underneath, which is the post's finding. Switch the benchmark.

Data: {{Arena, The harness tax (16 Sep, updated 18 Sep)|https://arena.ai/blog/coding-agents-harness-tax}} and the project's {{chart data|https://harnesstax.github.io/}} (saved in src/inputs/harnesstax_pairs.json): mean success over 30 tasks x 3 attempts, mean cost per attempt at a fixed direct-API price list of 1 September 2026. The defaults reproduce Arena's 2.0x (Claude Code against Pi) and 1.6x (against Codex) on SWE-bench Lite and its 1.5x on Terminal-Bench 2.0 independently, as geometric means over the seven models (src/recompute.py). The authors caution that both benchmarks may be in training data.
