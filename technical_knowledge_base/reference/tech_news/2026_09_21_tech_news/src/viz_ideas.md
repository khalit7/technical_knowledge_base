## Data, formulas and sources

## Inspiration
The ternary animation follows the DeepSeek MLA explainer: same input, old and new layout, drawn to scale (one square per stored bit), a caption per step, counters, play/step/scrub/speed, animating only on screen in the visible tab and paused under reduced motion. The KV steps borrow the Architecture Gallery's "cache at context" bars. The Rubin chart follows the rule "never one axis for different metrics": per-MW ratios are slices of one chart, per-TCO and per-GW figures sit in their own rows.

## What the methodology lacked for this issue
- **Several ratios from one table.** A vendor-neutral benchmark quoted as "7x", "62.9x" and "67x" is three slices against two engines and two metrics; draw the curve and let each headline be a column.
- **Self-reported series beside an independent measurer.** When a company's rounded figures lean on a third party's measurements, plot both, labelled, rather than choosing one.
- **A ratio's denominator can be the wrong scope.** "2% of the token spend" compared one proof with a whole multi-problem run; the visual toggles the denominator.
- **Size budget for long issues.** At 73 items the verification notes must be condensed by hand (what was checked, what differs) or the page passes 300 KB; the strip's data now carries only each item's first sentence.
