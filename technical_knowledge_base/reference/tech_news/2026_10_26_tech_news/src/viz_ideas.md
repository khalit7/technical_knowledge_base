# Visual ideas for the 2026-10-26 issue

Chosen fresh for this week (`.claude/skills/explainer-page/visuals/choosing.md`).

## Built
- **Week strip** (`v_strip`, `12_js_strip.js`): kept from the series; axis 14 to 26 October, with an eighth row for the new Security section.
- **Score against cost per task** (`v_cost`, `13_js_cost.js`), under the Kimi K3.1 Pro top story: the week's model is a two-dimensional fact (fourth on score, a third of Opus 5.5's cost per task), so one plane shows it against the last two issues' models. Artificial Analysis v4.3.2; models without a published per-task cost are named in the caption, not placed.
- **GRAFT before and after** (`v_graft`, `14_js_graft.js`), the before/after animation: a reward penalty on flagged rollouts against routing flagged updates into a deletable adapter, four steps each on the shared step controller (`10b_js_anim.js`). Only the paper's stated figures appear (about 30%, up to 35%, near zero, about +20 points); the diagram shows where updates go and says it is not a training curve.

## Considered and rejected
- The LiteLLM attack chain as a step diagram: the tech-news rules say never draw an attack route step by step; the text and the linked defender's post carry it.
- Terminal-Bench 4.0 readings for Kimi K3.1 Pro as bars: three numbers from three harnesses; the card states them with their harnesses, and a bar chart would invite comparing them as one quantity.
- A capex chart: only Meta reported inside the window; next week's issue has Alphabet, Microsoft and Amazon.
- TorchTPU against JAX throughput: one vendor-run pair (1,828 against 1,937 tokens per second); the card states it.
