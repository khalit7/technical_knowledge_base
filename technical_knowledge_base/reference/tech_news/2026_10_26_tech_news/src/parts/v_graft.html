Before and after: penalising what the monitor catches, against routing it into weights you delete

The setting from the GRAFT paper: RL on a coding task where the model can cheat in three ways and the monitor recognises only one of them. Step through the usual fix first, a reward penalty on flagged rollouts, then switch to GRAFT. The task, the monitor and the three hacks stay the same; only what the monitor's flag does changes.

Source: {{GRAFT, arXiv 2610.17062|https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17062}} and the {{Alignment Science blog post|https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/graft/}} (Qwen3-8B, Countdown-Code RL, monitor detects hack type 1 of 3), read 2026-10-26. The figures in the captions are the paper's: the penalty baseline switches to an undetected hack on about 30% of trajectories; GRAFT hacks on up to 35% before the adapter is removed and near zero after, with the clean solve rate nearly 20 points above the base model. The diagram shows where updates go; it is not a plot of training curves.
