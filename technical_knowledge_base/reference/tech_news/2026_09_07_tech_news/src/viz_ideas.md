## Inspiration
The routing animation follows the template's harness animation (N2) and the DeepSeek MLA explainer: the same input in two lanes, one scale, a caption per step, counters, controls, paused under reduced motion and off screen. The index chart follows the Google round's rule "record the index version beside every benchmark index score" by giving each version its own axis rather than a slope between them.

## What the methodology lacked for this issue
- A rule for **ownership across the knowledge base**: three of the week's best mechanism visuals (ARC harness, recurrent depth, GLM-5.3-Flash's cache) were already built on lab and topic pages after the issue was written. For a news issue the right move is a correction note plus a link, and a fresh visual only where the week itself adds something (a version change, a derived total, a vendor post's mechanism).
- A rule for **launch-day superlatives** ("cheapest at its level", "first", "only"): check them against every model published the same day, not only against the vendor's comparison set; two such claims made on Sep 2 contradicted each other within hours.
- A rule for **counts that drift** (Hugging Face upvotes, Hacker News points): give the issue's figure and a dated reading side by side; never chart them.
