A 59.2% cut that moved the compute rather than removing it

OpenAI gave three numbers for the week after Aug 7, when preliminary evidence suggested Astra might reach the Critical cyber level: Astra-class GPU allocation fell 59.2%, allocation to other model classes rose 17.2%, and the rise offset about 85% of the decline. Those three numbers fix how big Astra-class work was beforehand, and so what happened to the total. Switch between the headline reading and what the numbers imply.

others / Astra-class = 0.85 × 0.592 / 0.172 = 2.93
share before
Astra-class = 1 / (1 + 2.93) = 25.5% of GPU allocation; other classes 74.5%
after
Astra-class 25.5 × (1 − 0.592) = 10.4; others 74.5 × 1.172 = 87.3; total 97.7, about 2% below the week before

Published: 59.2%, 17.2% and "about 85%" ({{The Neuron, citing OpenAI's research-acceleration post|https://www.theneuron.ai/news/openai-ai-research-acceleration-alignment-slowdown/}}). Derived: the shares and the total, which assume the three figures describe the same week and the same pool of GPUs; "about 85%" is rounded, so the 25.5% carries a point or so either way.
