Money moving: the week's figures by kind

The week's dollar figures are four different quantities. A funding-round valuation and an acquisition price both value a whole company, so they share one axis, with each company's earlier marks for scale. A decade of compute commitments, a yearly revenue run rate and a legal payout are different kinds and get rows of their own, never a bar beside the others.

Hugging Face's price is [[the Nvidia deal in Top stories|t-top|i-nvidia-hf]]; Cognition and Thinking Machines are in [[the single-sourced item|t-industry|i-single-sourced]] above; the compute figures are [[Anthropic's|t-industry|i-anthropic-compute]].
