Port drills

Each drill is a PyTorch snippet and two or three JAX ports of it. Pick the port you would write, then see what every candidate actually printed: each snippet and each candidate ran exactly as shown, on the running example's real block-0 activations where an input is needed (src/runs/pj_drills.py, jax 0.11.2, flax 0.12.10, optax 0.2.8, torch 2.14.1). Several wrong answers raise nothing: that is why they are drills.

Drill
"max |diff|" compares a candidate's output with the PyTorch snippet's output where the two are comparable (same shape, numbers). Random draws cannot match across frameworks by design, so that drill compares candidates with each other.
