Sharding and scale: Mesh, GSPMD, shard_map and multi-host

How JAX spreads one program over many devices: Mesh and PartitionSpec, who decides the layout of every other array (the compiler's propagation, sharding in the types, or you inside shard_map), what each parallelism strategy compiles to, collective costs checked against the compiler, choosing a mesh for a TPU slice, and multi-host runs. Measured on an Apple M1 Pro CPU with 8 simulated devices (jax 0.11.2); TPU numbers are published ones, kept apart. Data as of October 2026. Reading: 38 min, plus 26 in closed details.
