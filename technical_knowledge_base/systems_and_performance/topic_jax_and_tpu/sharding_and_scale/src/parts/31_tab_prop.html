One transformer block of the running example (forward and backward), five ways of annotating it, two propagators. Every sharding here was read from the HLO that XLA dumped right after its propagation pass, and every collective from the compiled program.

How it was made: src/runs/ss_prop.py compiles loss = mean(block(x)2) and its gradient with XLA_FLAGS=--xla_dump_hlo_pass_re=shardy-xla|sharding-propagation, once with Shardy (the default) and once with jax_use_shardy_partitioner=False (GSPMD); src/runs/ss_hlo.py reads each instruction's sharding (converting both notations to P(...) over the mesh) and the Python line it came from. Operations are grouped by source line and kind; the largest instance stands for the group. measured jax 0.11.2, 8 simulated CPU devices, Auto axes.
