Flash tiles

The flash forward kernel's inner loop, replayed on real numbers: one block of 32 queries (positions 96 to 127) of a trained attention head against the keys in 8 blocks of 16, exactly the arithmetic of flash_fwd_kernel. Before: the naive softmax, which stores the whole row of scores. After: the online softmax, which keeps three running quantities per row and rescales them whenever the row's maximum grows. And the version that forgets to rescale.

Try: play "Online (flash)" and watch row 100's maximum climb from block to block while α shrinks what was accumulated before; then switch to "Without rescaling" and compare row 100's output with the reference at the end. Then "Naive" for how much it has to hold at once.
