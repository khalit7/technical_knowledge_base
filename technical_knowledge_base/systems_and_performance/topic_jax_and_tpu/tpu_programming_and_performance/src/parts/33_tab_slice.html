Slice planner

Pick a generation, one of the slice shapes Google Cloud lists for it, how many slices (Multislice over DCN), and a model. The planner gives the slice's hardware, whether the training state fits, how many tokens per chip the batch gives against the roofline, the step time and run length at an MFU you assume, the cost at list price, the DCN time of a multislice gradient reduction, and what jax.make_mesh does with each model-axis size on this slice. Slice shapes and specs are published (Cloud pages, 2026-10-13; the scaling book), the mesh placement measured here on mock devices, everything else calculated. Per-device memory for each parallelism strategy, and collective bytes, are on {{Sharding and scale|n:3c65c17b0d0d8125b084e6720cc66b3d}} (its Mesh designer); this tab is the hardware side.

What jax.make_mesh((data, model)) does on this slice measured here

Each row ran jax._src.mesh_utils.create_device_mesh (jax-v0.11.2, the function jax.make_mesh calls) on mock devices with this slice's coordinates (src/runs/tq_slices.py). "Hops" is the largest ICI distance between neighbours on the model axis, counting wraparound where the slice has it (full cubes on 3D slices; an axis of 16 on v5e and v6e). Refused sizes raise NotImplementedError because they are not products of whole physical axes. On TPU7x the devices are TensorCores, two per chip. The 12-byte state option models bf16 weights and gradients (4 bytes) plus float32 Adam moments (8): add 4 more if you keep a float32 master copy.
