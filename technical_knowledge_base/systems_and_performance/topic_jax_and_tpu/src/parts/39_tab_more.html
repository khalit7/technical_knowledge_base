{{Reading|#t-read}}

The whole topic from zero for a PyTorch user: why JAX, transformations, the compiler, the training stack, sharding, TPUs and Pallas, codebases, an honest comparison with PyTorch, choosing, mistakes with real error messages, interview questions. about 27 min, plus 10 for the folded answers and code

{{Transforms lab|#t-xf}}

Pure functions, keys, pytrees, tracing into jaxprs, recompiles counted, grad checked against PyTorch, vmap against a loop, and a 30-line toy tracer. about 25 min

{{Compiler lab|#t-xla}}

The running example's train step from jaxpr to optimised HLO: fusion animated, donation, rematerialisation against memory, compile caching, against torch.compile. about 16 min

{{Training lab|#t-train}}

Flax NNX, Linen, Equinox and Haiku side by side, optax checked against PyTorch AdamW, orbax resume bit for bit, grain, the PyTorch-to-JAX port with loss curves overlaid. about 25 min

{{Sharding lab|#t-shard}}

Meshes and partition specs on 8 simulated devices, the collectives the compiler inserts for each strategy, shard_map and a hand-written ring all-reduce. about 19 min

{{TPU and Pallas lab|#t-tpu}}

The systolic array animated, a roofline calculator with published TPU specs, Pallas kernels run in interpret mode and checked against jnp. about 24 min

{{Tool map|#t-map}}

Every library, codebase and serving stack with its status, dates and licence; a picker and a lineage. about 5 min, plus the tables (about 15 in all)

Getting TPU time

Not used here (this page runs on a CPU); checked 2026-10-15 by the TPU and Pallas lab.

  • {{TPU Research Cloud|https://sites.research.google/trc/about/}} (confirmed): accepted researchers get Cloud TPUs "at no charge" from "a cluster of more than 1,000 Cloud TPU devices", with quota granted "on a temporary basis", in return for sharing their research and feedback; the VM, storage and other Google Cloud services are not free. Neither page names generations or a duration.
  • {{Colab|https://research.google.com/colaboratory/faq.html}}: its FAQ says the TPU types available "vary over time", "Colab does not publish these limits", and free notebooks "can run for at most 12 hours". Check the runtime menu on the day.
  • Kaggle unconfirmed: its TPU documentation could not be read (it renders only in a browser); third-party guides say 20 hours a week and 9-hour sessions and disagree on the chip (v3-8 or v5e-8). Your remaining quota shows in account settings. The old page's "20 h/month" matches no source.
