The map in four lines
JAX and XLA are the core; Flax NNX, optax, orbax and grain are the default training stack; MaxText and Tunix are Google's LLM codebases; vLLM TPU serves. Haiku and Linen are legacy, Objax, Trax and Penzai are archived, JetStream and XPK are retired by their owners, and PyTorch/XLA is being replaced by TorchTPU, open-sourced on 20 October 2026. The Training lab uses this stack; the Reading tab explains why it looks like this.
