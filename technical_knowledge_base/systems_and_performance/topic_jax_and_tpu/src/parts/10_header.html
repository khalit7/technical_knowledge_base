Topic: jax-and-tpu

JAX, the XLA compiler under it and the TPUs it was built for, taught from zero for someone who knows PyTorch: pure functions and transformations (jit, grad, vmap), what the compiler does with them, the training stack (Flax NNX, optax, orbax, grain), sharding a model over many devices, TPU performance and Pallas kernels, and the codebases worth reading. Run on an Apple M1 Pro CPU, with 8 simulated devices for sharding; TPU numbers are published ones, kept apart. Data as of October 2026.
