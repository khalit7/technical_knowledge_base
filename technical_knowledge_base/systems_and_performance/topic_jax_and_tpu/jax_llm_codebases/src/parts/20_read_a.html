A production JAX LLM codebase is the same five boxes as your own training loop (configuration, model, sharding, train step, data and checkpoints) with one difference that reshapes all of them: the model never names a device, so a config decides the shapes, the sharding and the remat policy, and one jitted pure step turns them into a single XLA program. The codebases differ in which box they invest in: MaxText in config and sharding for scale on TPU, Tunix in the train step for post-training, Levanter in named axes and reproducible data.

Same five boxes, different investments: the highlighted cell is what each codebase is built for

The running example is the topic's 826,368-parameter character model (268 lines: model_jax.py plus common.py); MaxText holds the same boxes in 213,293 lines of Python. Reading a large codebase is finding these five boxes and the one or two ideas it adds to each. The {{Codebase map|#t-lcmap}} tab has every part of the three repositories with its size and the symbols worth reading at their lines.

You need: tracing and jax.jit ({{JAX core|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b7a0d1faba6cef5561}}), meshes and PartitionSpec ({{Sharding and scale|n:3c65c17b0d0d8125b084e6720cc66b3d}}), Flax NNX split and merge, optax and Orbax ({{The JAX training stack|n:3c65c17b0d0d817ea0ace9a2ca8677ec}}). New terms:

Logical axis names: one name per dimension of a weight or activation (embed, mlp, heads), never a device.
Logical axis rules: an ordered table in the config mapping each name to mesh axes; the first free rule wins.
scan_layers: run one traced layer over weights stacked on a layers axis.
remat_policy: which named intermediates the backward pass keeps instead of recomputing.
role_to_mesh: Tunix's map from an RL role (actor, reference, rollout) to a device mesh.
Named axis (Haliax): an array dimension addressed by name in every operation, not by position.

Provenance: commits, machine, and what ran

Everything marked measured ran on an Apple M1 Pro CPU (jax 0.11.2, flax 0.12.10, shared machine at load 30 to 60, low priority, 4 threads): MaxText at main 1a95d1e5 (2026-10-15), Tunix at v0.1.7 (2026-06-09), Levanter at Marin main 1290cfb5 (2026-10-15, lib/levanter) and the frozen Levanter repository 982cef7f. Configurations, shapes, shardings, losses and differences are deterministic; times are rough. Every file and line quoted is located by pattern in src/runs/lc_source.py; runs are in src/runs/, outputs in src/out/. The root page's {{Reading tab, section 7|n:3c65c17b0d0d81ecbc04eea6aa45ea1a}} lists the codebases with MaxText's MFU table, and its {{Tool map|n:3c65c17b0d0d81ecbc04eea6aa45ea1a}} (tab "Tool map") dates every library and its lineage; this page goes inside them.
