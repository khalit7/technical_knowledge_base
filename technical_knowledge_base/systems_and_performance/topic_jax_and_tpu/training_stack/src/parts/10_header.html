The JAX training stack: Flax NNX, optax, orbax and grain

The four libraries around JAX that turn a model into a training run, in depth, for someone who knows PyTorch: how Flax NNX holds and moves state, Linen and Equinox for reading other code, optax transformation by transformation, orbax checkpoints that resume bit for bit, grain pipelines that stay deterministic, float16 loss scaling, metrics and a complete script. Every result was run on the parent topic's tiny character LM on an Apple M1 Pro CPU (8 simulated devices for sharding). Data as of October 2026.
