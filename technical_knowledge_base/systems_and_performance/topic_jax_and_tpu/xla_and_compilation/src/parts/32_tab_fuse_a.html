Fusion explorer

Every fusion in the running example's optimised train step, the fusion pass's own reasons for what it refused, and thirteen small programs that keep or break fusion, each compiled three ways. Everything here was compiled on an Apple M1 Pro, CPU only, JAX 0.11.2 (src/lab/fusion_lab.py); kernel counts, kinds and bytes are deterministic. The mechanics are explained in {{Reading, section 4|#t-read}}.
