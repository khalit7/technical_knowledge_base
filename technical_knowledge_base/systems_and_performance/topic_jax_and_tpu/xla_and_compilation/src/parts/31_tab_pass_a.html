Pass diff viewer

XLA's CPU pipeline replayed on two real programs of the running example, one pass at a time: what each pass changed in the program text, opcode by opcode, and where the pass is added in the CPU and GPU compilers at the pinned XLA commit. Dumped with XLA_FLAGS="--xla_dump_to=DIR --xla_dump_hlo_pass_re=.*" on an Apple M1 Pro, CPU only (src/lab/passes_diff.py). The Reading tab's {{section 3|#t-read}} explains passes; this tab is for looking.





How to read a diff: - lines left the program, + lines entered it; metadata (source locations) is stripped before comparing, and lines are cut at 170 characters. The attention block's diffs are shown up to 50 lines, the train step's up to 14 (its full dumps are 64 MB). Instruction counts are top-level: a fusion counts as one. Passes that ran without changing the text are not dumped.
