Custom derivatives: where autodiff of a correct formula fails, and the fixes

Each case was swept over its input in float32 with jax.vmap(jax.grad(f)) under jit and compared with a float64 NumPy reference of the true function (src/runs/run_custom.py). Red ticks along the top of a chart mark inputs where a variant returned NaN or inf. Reading section 6 explains the rules.
