Rules and sources. Wraparound: "Any multiple of a full cube (e.g. 4x4x4 or 4x4x8) will have wraparounds provided by the optical switches"; "a 2x2x4 won't have any wraparounds"; without one "typically doubles the time of most communication" ({{How To Scale Your Model, TPUs|https://jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book/tpus/}}). Here an axis wraps when every dimension is a multiple of 4 (or the axis has length 1 or 2 and the slice is otherwise whole cubes, which this tab does not allow). Per-link bandwidth one way / both ways: v4 45 / 90 GB/s, v5p and TPU7x 90 / 180 GB/s (scaling book; the TPU v4 paper gives "6 links @ 50 GB/s"). All-reduce time = 2V / Σ W over the chosen axes, W = both-way bandwidth with wraparound and one-way without, and at least 1 μs per hop along the axes (the book's Tmin) derived; real collectives reach about 95% of the link figure (the book's measurement on v5e). Hosts hold 4 chips (2 × 2 × 1). Optical links: 96 per cube (16 per face, TPU v4 paper). Largest pods: v4 4,096 chips (16 × 16 × 16), v5p 8,960, TPU7x 9,216.
