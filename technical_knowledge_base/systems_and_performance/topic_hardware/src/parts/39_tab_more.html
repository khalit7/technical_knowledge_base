Best outside resources

{{How To Scale Your Model|https://jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book/}}

Google DeepMind's book on systems for LLMs: rooflines, sharding, TPUs, and a GPU chapter (Aug 2025). The single best text for this topic. about 6 h; GPUs chapter 1 h

{{Scaling book: All About Rooflines|https://jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book/roofline/}}

Rooflines for compute, memory and network, done properly. 45 min

{{Modal GPU Glossary|https://modal.com/gpu-glossary/readme}}

Short, accurate definitions of every GPU term, hardware and software. 30 min for the core pages

{{Making Deep Learning Go Brrrr|https://horace.io/brrr_intro.html}}

Horace He: compute-bound, memory-bound and overhead-bound, with intuition. 25 min

{{Transformer Math 101|https://blog.eleuther.ai/transformer-math/}}

EleutherAI: training FLOPs and memory budgets. 35 min

{{Transformer Inference Arithmetic|https://kipp.ly/transformer-inference-arithmetic/}}

kipply: the inference side, KV cache and latency math. 40 min

{{PaLM, Appendix B|https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02311}}

Where MFU was defined. 15 min for the appendix

{{Programming Massively Parallel Processors|https://shop.elsevier.com/books/programming-massively-parallel-processors/hwu/978-0-323-91231-0}}

Hwu, Kirk and El Hajj (PMPP), 4th edition on the publisher's page: the textbook for GPU architecture and CUDA. a book; 4 h for the architecture chapters
