At what scale was that number measured?

Hardware numbers are quoted per chip, per server, per rack, per pod and now per gigawatt, and they are not interchangeable: per chip decides what fits, per scale-up domain which parallelism is affordable (section 6), per megawatt how much a power connection can host. Before comparing two figures, put them at the same scale and the same format, dense on both sides. What the scales look like:
