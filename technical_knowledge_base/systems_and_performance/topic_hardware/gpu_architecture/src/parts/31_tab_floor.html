Chip floorplans: each GPU drawn from its published counts

Each drawing places a chip's dies, GPCs, SMs (enabled and disabled), L2 and memory interfaces from the counts NVIDIA publishes (or, for the B200, an independent paper). It is a schematic drawn to the counts, not to silicon area or position: NVIDIA does not publish which SMs are disabled on a given chip, so their positions are illustrative, and where a count is not published the drawing says so instead of guessing.

Try: A100 against H100 (same 8-GPC plan, one more TPC (two more SMs) per GPC, same 5 of 6 HBM sites); H100 against B200 (one die to two); B200 against Rubin; H100 against RTX 5090 (12 GPCs, GDDR7 controllers around the edge instead of HBM stacks). Cells that differ are highlighted in the table.

Sources: {{A100 in depth|https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-ampere-architecture-in-depth/}} (GA100: 8 GPCs, 8 TPCs per GPC, 128 SMs, 6 HBM2 stacks, 12 512-bit controllers; A100: 108 SMs in 7 GPCs, 5 stacks, 10 controllers; 40 MB L2 in two partitions), {{Hopper in depth|https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/nvidia-hopper-architecture-in-depth/}} (GH100: 8 GPCs, 9 TPCs per GPC, 144 SMs, 6 stacks, 60 MB L2; H100 SXM5: 66 TPCs, 132 SMs, 5 HBM3 stacks, 10 controllers, 50 MB), {{Jarmusch and Chandrasekaran 2025|https://arxiv.org/html/2512.02189}} (B200: 148 SMs in 8 GPCs, four L2 partitions, eight HBM3e stacks; independent), {{Inside Blackwell Ultra|https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/inside-nvidia-blackwell-ultra-the-chip-powering-the-ai-factory-era/}} (160 SMs in 8 GPCs, two reticle-sized dies, eight 12-Hi stacks, 16 512-bit controllers, 208B transistors, TSMC 4NP), {{Inside Rubin|https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/inside-nvidia-rubin-gpu-architecture-powering-the-era-of-agentic-ai/}} (two dies, 224 SMs, 336B transistors, HBM4 12-Hi), {{RTX Blackwell whitepaper|https://images.nvidia.com/aem-dam/Solutions/geforce/blackwell/nvidia-rtx-blackwell-gpu-architecture.pdf}} (GB202: 12 GPCs, 96 TPCs, 192 SMs, sixteen 32-bit controllers, 128 MB L2; RTX 5090: 11 GPCs, 85 TPCs, 170 SMs, 96 MB L2), and the parent page's Chip atlas for process, transistors and area. vendor spec
