Fabric explorer

Servers of 8 GPUs, each GPU with one 400 Gb/s NIC (50 GB/s each way) and NVLink 4 (450 GB/s each way) inside its server. Choose how the NICs are wired, what traffic runs, how many servers and how much the spine is oversubscribed, and see which class of link limits the operation. Each GPU sends 1 GB. The drawing shows the first few servers; the numbers are for the whole cluster.

Fluid model (src/fabric.py, mirrored here and checked on 216 cases): perfect load balance across spine switches, no congestion control or flow collisions (real ECMP hashing does worse; Meta's Llama 3 cluster opens 16 flows per GPU pair to balance it), all GPUs sending at once. Time = the largest load / capacity over link classes. Switch ports count leaf downlinks plus leaf uplinks and the spine ports they need, a rough cost proxy; the rail-only paper reports 38 to 77% lower network cost for the same idea ({{Wang et al. 2023|https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12169}}). derived
