Run and serving planner

Two planning questions the parent's {{Performance calculator|n:3c65c17b0d0d8118beeefaed56da6f8e}} leaves open, built on its functions (copied unchanged, so the two pages agree): how many accelerators and how much money a training run needs to finish by a date, and what serving costs per million tokens as the batch grows. Every result is a ceiling or an estimate at the efficiency you set; prices are on-demand list prices fetched 2026-10-05 (Lambda for GPUs, Google Cloud for TPUs), and only chips with a published price show a cost.

A training run against a deadline

Try: Llama 3.1 8B on 15,000B tokens finishes in 30 days on about 780 H100s; drop MFU to 25% or goodput to 70% and watch the count and the bill; switch to DeepSeek-V3 to see active parameters set the compute.

Serving: tokens per second against cost

Try: start at batch 1 and follow the curve: total throughput rises almost linearly while each user's speed barely falls, until the KV cache or compute takes over. Raise the context and watch the knee move left.
