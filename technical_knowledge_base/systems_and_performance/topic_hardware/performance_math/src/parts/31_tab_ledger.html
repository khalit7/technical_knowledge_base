FLOP and memory ledger

Every FLOP and every saved byte of one training step of a Llama-family model, part by part, for any sequence length. The formulas are the Reading tab's sections 1 and 3; for Llama 3.1 8B at 2,048, 8,192, 32,768 and 131,072 tokens, and Llama 3.1 70B at 8,192, they equal PyTorch's own count of the real model (the green badge says when the point you chose is one of them).

Try: slide the sequence length up and watch attention overtake the MLP; switch the attention convention to see what causal masking saves; switch checkpointing and the GPU to see what fits.

Activation memory per GPU

Model state is not included here (the Reading tab's section 2 and the parent's calculator add it). Per-layer bytes per token: 16h (two RMSNorms in FP32 and their outputs) + 4 × heads × head size (Q and the output) + 4 × KV heads × head size (K and V) + 8 × MLP width + small terms; plain attention adds 6 × heads × sequence length; full recompute keeps 2h. Logits: 4 bytes × vocabulary per token. Tensor parallel with sequence parallel divides the layers' share by the TP degree (the logits are left whole, a simplification). Counted on Transformers' reference code; fused kernels save less.
