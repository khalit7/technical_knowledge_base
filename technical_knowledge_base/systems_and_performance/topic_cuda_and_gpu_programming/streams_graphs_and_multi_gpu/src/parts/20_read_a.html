A GPU is fast only while it has work. Between the kernels this topic teaches you to write, a training step leaves the GPU idle in three ways: it waits for the CPU to launch the next kernel, it waits for a copy over PCIe, and it waits for other GPUs to exchange gradients. This page is about the tools that remove those waits: streams (run things side by side), CUDA graphs (launch many kernels with one call) and NCCL on its own stream (communicate while computing).

Where this sits. {{The CUDA programming model|n:3c65c17b0d0d8162865eec15d9dae87e}} taught what one launch is and what it costs, and left streams, events, pinned-memory pipelines and NCCL streams to this page; {{GPU memory hierarchy|n:3c65c17b0d0d814a8edef7bd21c303d3}} has pinned, mapped and managed memory. Below the API, {{Virtualization and the GPU driver stack|n:3f05c17b0d0d8158b029e9b2ba2e161b}} follows a launch down to the doorbell write, and the wires (PCIe, NVLink, InfiniBand) and NCCL's algorithms are on {{Interconnects and scaling|n:3c65c17b0d0d81808563fd41fb163af0}}. This page is the programmer's side.

What could run here. There is no NVIDIA GPU. So: every CUDA program on this page was compiled with CUDA 13.4.2 and NCCL 2.31.2 in a container compiled here, and the runs fail at the first runtime call with "CUDA driver version is insufficient for CUDA runtime version (35)", which is shown rather than hidden. The overlap of gradient all-reduce with backward was run with real PyTorch DDP on CPU processes (Gloo instead of NCCL) measured on CPU. Launch and queueing costs were measured on the Apple M1 Pro's GPU through Metal measured on Apple M1 Pro GPU. NVIDIA timings come from NVIDIA's posts and docs published; the training-step timelines are a model built from stated inputs derived.

The one picture to keep
A step's time is the sum of the parts that do not overlap. Overlap hides the smaller of two things under the larger; it never makes the larger one shorter, and the two often slow each other down a little (both use the GPU's SMs and memory). Section 6's measurement shows both effects on real DDP.
