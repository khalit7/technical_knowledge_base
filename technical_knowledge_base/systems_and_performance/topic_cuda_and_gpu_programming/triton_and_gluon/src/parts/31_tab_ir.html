The four kernels of the Reading tab compiled by Triton 3.8.0 for six GPUs, at every stage. Pick a kernel, a target and a stage; the operations the Reading tab discusses are highlighted.



All output is real and compiled without a GPU (src/code/compile_tg.py in the kb-gpu-lab:1 image: Triton 3.8.0, its bundled ptxas and LLVM, CUDA 13.4.2's cuobjdump for SASS and resource usage). To keep the page small, long stages show only the layout definitions and the lines carrying the operations discussed (one line per distinct instruction form for PTX, AMDGCN and SASS); debug locations are removed and long lines cut. TTIR does not depend on the NVIDIA target, so one copy is kept for NVIDIA and one for AMD. Full files: src/out/ir/ (the large ones are regenerated by src/run_all.sh). AMD targets have no SASS; their machine code is the AMDGCN stage.
