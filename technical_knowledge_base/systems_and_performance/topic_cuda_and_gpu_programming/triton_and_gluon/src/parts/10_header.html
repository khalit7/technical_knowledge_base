Triton and Gluon: block-level kernels, autotuning, what the compiler does

Writing GPU kernels in Triton from zero (vector add, softmax, matmul, FlashAttention), what num_warps, num_stages and autotuning do, every stage and pass of the Triton compiler with real output for NVIDIA sm_80, sm_90a, sm_100a, sm_120 and AMD gfx942, gfx950, layouts, how torch.compile writes Triton, and Gluon, the lower-level language on the same compiler. Kernels checked on the CPU with Triton's interpreter and compiled with Triton 3.8.0 without a GPU; nothing here ran on an NVIDIA or AMD GPU.
