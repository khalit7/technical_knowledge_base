Best outside resources

Official Triton tutorials

01 vector add, 02 fused softmax, 03 matmul, 06 fused attention: the core curriculum, each with a benchmark against PyTorch. Sources for v3.8.0 are in the repository's python/tutorials/. about 3 h for 01, 02, 03, 06

Gluon tutorials (14)

From layouts to TMA, wgmma, tcgen05, warp specialisation and multi-CTA kernels; read 01 and 02 first. about 4 h

Triton paper (Tillet, Kung and Cox, MAPL 2019)

The block-level design rationale in 10 pages. 45 min

GPU MODE lectures 14 and 29

"Practitioner's Guide to Triton" and "Triton Internals" (also 34, low-bit Triton kernels, and 78, multi-GPU Triton); recordings on the GPU MODE YouTube channel. about 1 h 30 min each

Triton-Puzzles and GPU Puzzles

Sasha Rush's exercises; Triton-Puzzles runs on the interpreter, so it works on this laptop. about 3 h each

triton-lang/triton releases

The 3.8.0 notes list what the compiler gained (Rubin, multi-CTA, FpSan and GSan sanitisers, Gluon APIs). 20 min

GPU MODE kernel leaderboard

Competitive Triton and CUDA kernel problems for practice. 20 min to look, hours to play

Liger Kernel

Production Triton kernels for LLM training (RMSNorm, RoPE, SwiGLU, fused linear cross-entropy): the best real code to read after the tutorials. 1 h to read two kernels
