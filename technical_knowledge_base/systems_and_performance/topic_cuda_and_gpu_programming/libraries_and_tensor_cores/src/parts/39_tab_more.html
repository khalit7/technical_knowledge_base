Best resources

{{CUTLASS documentation|https://docs.nvidia.com/cutlass/}}

C++ templates and the CuTe DSL; start with CuTe's layout and layout-algebra pages ({{layout algebra|https://docs.nvidia.com/cutlass/latest/media/docs/cpp/cute/02_layout_algebra.html}}). 2 h for the core pages

{{NVIDIA/cutlass on GitHub|https://github.com/NVIDIA/cutlass}}

Read examples/cute/tutorial (Ampere, Hopper and five Blackwell steps), then examples 48 and 70; all compiled on this page. 45 min for the entry path

{{Colfax Research blog|https://research.colfax-intl.com/blog/}}

The clearest written series on CuTe layouts, {{wgmma|https://research.colfax-intl.com/cutlass-tutorial-wgmma-hopper/}}, {{TMA|https://research.colfax-intl.com/tutorial-hopper-tma/}} and FlashAttention-3-style pipelines. 6 h for the series

{{GPU MODE lectures|https://github.com/gpu-mode/lectures}}

Video lectures, several on CUTLASS and CuTe by their developers. 1 h 30 min each; about 4 h 30 min for the CUTLASS set

{{cuBLAS documentation|https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cublas/}}

Especially the {{cuBLASLt API|https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cublas/index.html#using-the-cublaslt-api}}: descriptors, heuristics, epilogues, scale modes. 1 h 30 min

{{cuDNN documentation|https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/cudnn/}}

The graph API and the frontend; the attention section. 1 h 30 min

{{PTX ISA|https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/parallel-thread-execution/index.html}}

Ground truth for mma, wgmma, tcgen05, TMA and mbarriers; read the matrix-instruction sections. 1 h for the mma sections

{{How to Optimize a CUDA Matmul Kernel|https://siboehm.com/articles/22/CUDA-MMM}}

Simon Boehm's ten-step FP32 ladder to 93.7% of cuBLAS. 1 h

{{Stream-K paper|https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.03598}}

Work-centric GEMM decomposition (section 3). 1 h

{{Benchmarking and Dissecting the Nvidia Hopper GPU Architecture|https://arxiv.org/html/2402.13499v1}}

Independent measurements of mma against wgmma, DSMEM and TMA. 1 h

{{DeepGEMM|https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepGEMM}}

A readable FP8 GEMM with fine-grained scaling for Hopper and Blackwell. 1 h

{{FlashAttention repository|https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention}}

FlashAttention-2 in CUDA, -3 for Hopper, -4 in the CuTe DSL. 1 h to find your way
