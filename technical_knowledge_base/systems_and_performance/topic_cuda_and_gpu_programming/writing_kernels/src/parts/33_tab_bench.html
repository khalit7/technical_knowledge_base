Kernel bench: every measurement on the M1 Pro GPU

Every timing quoted on this page, with its spread, the machine's load, the derived rate and the kernel source that was timed. All measured on Apple M1 Pro GPU (16-core GPU, MLX 0.32.3, custom Metal kernels through mx.fast.metal_kernel), each kernel first checked against a float64 NumPy reference at the measured size. Each case: 2 warm-up calls, then 7 trials of enough calls to last about 60 ms; the bar is the median of the three runs' medians, the thin line the fastest and slowest of all 21 trials. Times include MLX's per-call launch-and-wait overhead (about 0.2 ms).
