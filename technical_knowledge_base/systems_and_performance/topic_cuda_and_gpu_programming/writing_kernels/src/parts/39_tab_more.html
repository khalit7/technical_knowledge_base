Best resources

{{Simon Boehm, How to Optimize a CUDA Matmul Kernel for cuBLAS-like Performance|https://siboehm.com/articles/22/CUDA-MMM}} 1 h 30 min

The canonical worked example, ten kernels from 1.3% to 93.7% of cuBLAS on an RTX A6000 (December 2022). Read it, then reproduce it.

{{salykova, Advanced Matrix Multiplication Optimization on NVIDIA GPUs|https://salykova.github.io/sgemm-gpu}} 1 h 30 min

The sequel: vectorisation, double buffering and autotuning, beating cuBLAS SGEMM on an RTX 4090 by the author's account.

{{Mark Harris, Optimizing Parallel Reduction in CUDA|https://developer.download.nvidia.com/assets/cuda/files/reduction.pdf}} 45 min

The seven-step reduction deck (2007). Read rung 5 with the Volta correction of section 1 in mind.

{{Programming Massively Parallel Processors, 5th edition|https://shop.elsevier.com/books/programming-massively-parallel-processors/hwu/978-0-443-43900-1}} 2 h 15 min

Hwu, Kirk and El Hajj: chapter 6 (tiled matmul), chapters 10 and 11 (reduction, scan), later chapters for patterns.

{{GPU Gems 3, chapter 39: Parallel Prefix Sum (Scan) with CUDA|https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-vi-gpu-computing/chapter-39-parallel-prefix-sum-scan-cuda}} 40 min

Harris, Sengupta and Owens: Hillis-Steele, Blelloch, bank-conflict padding.

{{Merrill and Garland, Single-pass Parallel Prefix Scan with Decoupled Look-back|https://research.nvidia.com/publication/2016-03_single-pass-parallel-prefix-scan-decoupled-look-back}} 45 min

The algorithm inside CUB's DeviceScan (NVIDIA, 2016).

{{CCCL (CUB, Thrust, libcu++)|https://nvidia.github.io/cccl/}} 20 min

Block- and device-wide reduce, scan, sort and histogram you call instead of writing; source at {{NVIDIA/cccl|https://github.com/NVIDIA/cccl}}.

{{FlashAttention-2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.08691}} · {{FlashAttention-3|https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08608}} · {{FlashAttention-4|https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05451}} 1 h each

Work partitioning; Hopper asynchrony and FP8; Blackwell's asymmetric scaling. Code: {{Dao-AILab/flash-attention|https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention}}.

{{Flash-Decoding for long-context inference|https://crfm.stanford.edu/2023/10/12/flashdecoding.html}} 15 min

Split-KV decode explained with animations (Dao, Haziza, Massa, Sizov, 2023).

{{Triton tutorial: fused softmax|https://triton-lang.org/main/getting-started/tutorials/02-fused-softmax.html}} · {{fused attention|https://triton-lang.org/main/getting-started/tutorials/06-fused-attention.html}} 30 min each

The two kernels of the path's last steps, as reference solutions.

{{Marlin|https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.11743}} 45 min

Frantar et al., 2024: 4-bit weight kernels that keep the speedup up to batch 16 to 32.

{{GPU MODE lectures|https://github.com/gpu-mode/lectures}} 3 h for lectures 8 and 12

Lecture 8 (CUDA performance checklist), lecture 12 (FlashAttention), and guest lectures by kernel authors.

{{Compute Sanitizer|https://docs.nvidia.com/compute-sanitizer/ComputeSanitizer/index.html}} · {{cuBLAS and cuBLASLt|https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cublas/index.html}} 20 min each

The four sanitizer tools of section 8; the epilogue options of section 5.
