Making training fast: profiling, memory, attention, torchao

Find the bottleneck in a real training step, fix that one, and measure again: the profiler and honest timing, data loading, optimizers, mixed precision, where memory goes and how checkpointing and fused losses cut it, attention backends and FlexAttention, and torchao's low-precision formats. Every number measured on an Apple M1 Pro (CPU and MPS) with PyTorch 2.14.1 unless labelled published. Data as of October 2026.
