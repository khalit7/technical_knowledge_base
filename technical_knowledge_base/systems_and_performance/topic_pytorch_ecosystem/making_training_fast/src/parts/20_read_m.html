12. What carries over to NVIDIA GPUs

Every number above was measured on a laptop. This table says, technique by technique, what transfers to a data-centre NVIDIA GPU and which way the result is likely to move. "Likely" means reasoning from how the hardware differs, not a measurement; published GPU figures are quoted where they exist.
