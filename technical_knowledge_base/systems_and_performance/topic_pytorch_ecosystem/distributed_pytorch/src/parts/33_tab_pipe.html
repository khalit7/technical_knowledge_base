Pipeline schedules

Every schedule in torch.distributed.pipelining at 2.14.1, run on the same model and batch: what each rank computed, in which order, when, and how long it sat idle. Compare any two, on the measured clock or on an ideal one.

Recorded with src/exp/pp_sched.py: the 8-block, dmodel 512 variant of the running example (25.3M parameters) cut into 2 stages (GPipe, 1F1B) or 4 (two per rank), batch 8 × 64 tokens in 4 or 8 micro-batches, 2 CPU processes (4 for GPipe and 1F1B at 8 micro-batches), gloo, one thread each, under the shared bench lock; the median of 5 timed steps is drawn. Every forward (F), backward (B), input-gradient (I) and weight-gradient (W) action was timed by wrapping the stage's methods. Bubble = 1 − time in actions / step time. Measured on Apple M1 Pro, CPU. The ideal clock replays each rank's recorded order with F = 1, B = 2, I = 1, W = 1 units and no communication cost, respecting what each action waits for (the previous stage's forward, the next stage's backward); it shows the schedule's own bubble, separate from this machine's noise.
