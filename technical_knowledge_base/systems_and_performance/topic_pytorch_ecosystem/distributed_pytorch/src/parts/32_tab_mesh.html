Mesh explorer

Six ways of laying the running example over 4 or 8 processes, each run for real: pick a layout, click a rank, and see the groups it belongs to, the piece of each parameter it holds, every collective it issued in one training step, and its memory.

Recorded with src/exp/fsdp_more.py (DDP, FSDP2, HSDP, reshard_after_forward=2), fsdp_tp.py and compose3d.py; CPU processes, gloo, torch 2.14.1, Apple M1 Pro. Collectives are step 1's (step 0 for the 8-process run, which records one step), as the flight recorder saw them, grouped by the mesh dimension of their process group. On gloo a gradient reduce-scatter appears as an all-reduce of the whole tensor (Reading tab, section 2). Measured, deterministic.
