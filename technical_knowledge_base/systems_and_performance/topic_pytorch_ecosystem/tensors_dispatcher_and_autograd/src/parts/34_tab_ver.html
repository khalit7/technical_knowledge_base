The mechanism in four lines
Every storage-sharing family of tensors has one version counter. Every in-place op bumps it (the ADInplaceOrView kernel, even under no_grad). A node that saves a tensor records the counter's value. When backward unpacks the tensor, a different value raises the error, unless saved-tensor hooks are installed, in which case nothing is checked.
