Custom-op lab

Ten ways of putting the same C++ GELU into the running example's MLP, from a direct call to a fully registered operator, each run for real with torch 2.14.1 on the CPU (src/exp/custom_ops.py, src/exp/graphs.py). For each: what is registered, what happens in eager training, what Dynamo captures, whether torch.compile(fullgraph=True) trains correctly, and what opcheck's four tests say. Pick two variants to compare; the defaults are the first and last steps of section 1 of the Reading tab.

What to take away
A direct call costs graph breaks and silently loses gradients; a custom op needs a kernel, a fake and an autograd formula, in that order, and only all three pass the four tests and train under compile. A C++ op is the same once its fake and formula are registered, at import. The lab's last two columns are the Reading tab's section 2.3: same code, different registration time.
