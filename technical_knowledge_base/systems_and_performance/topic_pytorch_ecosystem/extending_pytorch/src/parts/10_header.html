Extending PyTorch: custom ops, tensor subclasses, kernels

How to add what PyTorch does not ship without breaking autograd, torch.compile or export, taught from zero on the running example: torch.library custom operators, C++ extensions, Metal, Triton and Helion kernels plugged in, tensor subclasses, dispatch modes, and how to choose. Every graph count, opcheck result, trace and error here was recorded with torch 2.14.1 on an Apple M1 Pro (CPU and MPS); source quoted at pinned tags. Data as of October 2026.
