9. The ecosystem: what sits on top, and what is alive

Every library above PyTorch is a different answer to one question: how much of the training loop do you want to own? They mostly call the same core pieces (FSDP2, DTensor, torch.compile, SDPA), so the choice is about defaults, recipes and how much code you read when something breaks. Statuses below were read from each project's own repository or release page on 2026-10-12; the {{Ecosystem map|#t-map}} has 42 entries with sources, licences, what each calls in core (from its released source) and a chooser.

Inside core, what was replaced. FSDP2's fully_shard (2.4) replaces the FullyShardedDataParallel wrapper, which still ships at 2.14 with no formal deprecation notice; torch.compile and torch.export replace TorchScript, whose torch.jit now raises a FutureWarning (2.14); torchao replaces torch.ao.quantization (deprecated 2.8, its PT2E flow moved out in 2.11); torch.distributed.pipelining replaced torch.distributed.pipeline; torch.load defaults to weights_only=True since 2.6 ({{release notes|https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/releases}}).

Who governs what. The PyTorch Foundation now hosts PyTorch, vLLM and DeepSpeed (May 2025), Ray (October 2025), Helion and Safetensors (April 2026) ({{Foundation year in review|https://pytorch.org/blog/pytorch-foundation-in-2025-a-year-in-review/}}, {{PyTorch Conference Europe 2026|https://pytorch.org/blog/pytorch-conference-europe-2026-a-landmark-moment-for-open-source-ai-in-paris/}}). Meta-owned satellites (torchtune, torchforge, monarch) live in the separate meta-pytorch GitHub organisation, while torchtitan stays under pytorch. Hugging Face, home of transformers, Accelerate, PEFT, TRL and the model Hub, signed a definitive agreement on 2026-09-02 to be acquired by NVIDIA for about $11.9 billion to its stockholders plus up to about $1.0 billion of retention equity, expected to close in the first half of 2027 subject to regulatory approval ({{NVIDIA Form 8-K|https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001045810/000104581026000078/nvda-20260902.htm}}); NVIDIA's announcement the next day said the Hub "will remain an open platform for the entire AI ecosystem" ({{Semafor|https://semafor.com/article/09/03/2026/nvidia-to-buy-hugging-face-for-nearly-13-billion}}). Until it closes, Hugging Face is independent.
