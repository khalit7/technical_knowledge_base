11. Dropping down a level

Every library on this page lets you take back one duty at a time, and going down one level when something is wrong is cheaper than switching libraries. From a config tool, start by printing trainer.args, trainer.optimizer and trainer.lr_scheduler: every one of them ends in a transformers.Trainer (or a TRL subclass) holding a transformers model, and those three show every effective default.
