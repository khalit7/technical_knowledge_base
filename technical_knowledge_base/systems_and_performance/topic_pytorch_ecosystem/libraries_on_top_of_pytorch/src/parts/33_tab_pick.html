Two tools. The chooser turns a task, a scale, the hardware and how much of the loop you want to own into a stack, with the reason for each pick taken from this page or the root's {{Ecosystem map|n:3c65c17b0d0d8150aa65ca44b6ed2fd5}} (whose chooser covers more tasks, such as serving and kernels; this one goes deeper on training). The porting checklist lists every default that differs between two ways of training, so a move between them changes only what you meant to change. Both are judgements built on recorded facts, not measurements of speed.
