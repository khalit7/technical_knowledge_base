torch.compile from the user's side

What happens between torch.compile(model) and the fused kernels, taught from zero on the topic's running example: Dynamo reading bytecode and writing guards, graph breaks and how to remove each kind, recompiles and dynamic shapes, AOTAutograd's partitioner, Inductor's fusion and code, modes, compiled autograd, regional compilation, caches, torch.export and AOTInductor, and the debugging tools. Every graph, log and timing here was recorded with torch 2.14.1 on an Apple M1 Pro (CPU unless marked); GPU numbers are quoted and labelled. Data as of October 2026.
