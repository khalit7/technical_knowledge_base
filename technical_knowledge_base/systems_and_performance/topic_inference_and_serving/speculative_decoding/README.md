# Speculative decoding: draft models, EAGLE, MTP, n-gram, the batch-size crossover

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3f35c17b0d0d81e086fde85940ebac1c (child of Topic: inference-and-serving)
Site: https://khalit7.github.io/technical_knowledge_base/systems_and_performance/topic_inference_and_serving/speculative_decoding/

Interactive page: `index.html`, built from `src/` (`sh src/build.sh`). A new page, taught from zero: why decode leaves the chip idle, draft then verify, the acceptance rule and why it is exact, tokens per step, the cost model and the batch-size crossover, every kind of drafter (separate model, Medusa, EAGLE 1 to 3, MTP, DFlash and DSpark, n-gram and suffix decoding), draft trees, engine support, recent developments. Tabs: Reading, Acceptance rule lab (real Qwen3 distributions; an exactness test of five verification rules), Batch crossover lab (cost model from measured pass times against real llama-server runs at 1 to 16 users, and a derived H100 curve), Drafters measured (one Qwen3-4B target with a 0.6B draft, EAGLE-3, DFlash, DSpark and five n-gram modes), Further reading.
No NVIDIA GPU was available: every run is on an Apple M1 Pro (MLX and llama.cpp Metal), labelled; published GPU results are labelled by who measured them. Sources, scripts and compact results are in `src/` (see `src/README.md`); `src/coverage.md` lists what was carried from the parent and corrected.
