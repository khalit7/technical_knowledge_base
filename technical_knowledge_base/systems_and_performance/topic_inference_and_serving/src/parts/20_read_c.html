Along the frontier or outward. Baseten's framing is the test for any of these: batch size, quantisation level or speculation settings move a deployment along the latency-throughput frontier, while better kernels, cache management or architecture push the frontier outward ({{Baseten, 2026-09-02|https://www.baseten.co/blog/the-efficient-frontier-of-llm-inference/}}). Only the second is a free win.
