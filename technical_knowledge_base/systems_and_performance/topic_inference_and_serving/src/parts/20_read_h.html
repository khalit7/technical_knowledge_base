7. More than one GPU

One GPU stops being enough for three different reasons, and each has its own fix: the weights do not fit, the cache for enough users does not fit, or one GPU is too slow for the latency target. The running example shows the second: Llama 3.1 70B in BF16 is 141 GB, so on H100s (80 GB) it needs two GPUs, and the two then have room left for the cache of only 2 conversations {{Capacity planner|#t-plan}}, TP 2 on H100 in BF16. Fitting the weights is not the same as serving.

Tensor parallelism is Megatron's split of each matrix across GPUs; its cost and the links it needs are on {{Interconnects and scaling|n:3c65c17b0d0d81808563fd41fb163af0}}. A model's KV heads are split too, so Llama's 8 KV heads cap useful TP at 8. Expert parallelism is how the largest open models are served: SGLang's DeepSeek-V3 deployment ran decode across 9 nodes with 72-way expert parallelism and 256 sequences per GPU ({{LMSYS|https://lmsys.org/blog/2025-05-05-large-scale-ep/}}, vendor).

Disaggregated serving puts prefill and decode on different GPUs because they want different things (compute against bandwidth, section 1) and because a prefill in a decode step stalls every stream (section 4). The prefill GPU sends the finished cache to a decode GPU: 2,000 tokens of the running example are 625 MiB, about 13 ms over a 400 Gb/s network link or under 1 ms over NVLink 2,000 × 320 KiB / link speed. The papers report large gains, each the authors' own measurement: DistServe 7.4 times more requests within both latency targets ({{Zhong et al. 2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09670}}), Splitwise 1.4 times the throughput at 20% lower cost ({{Patel et al. 2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18677}}), Mooncake 75% more requests for Moonshot's Kimi in production ({{Qin et al. 2024|https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.00079}}). Open implementations: NVIDIA {{Dynamo|https://github.com/ai-dynamo/dynamo}} (v1.5.1), {{llm-d|https://github.com/llm-d/llm-d}} (v0.10.0, on vLLM), and SGLang's and vLLM's own prefill/decode modes. It is worth its complexity at scale with strict TPOT targets and long prompts; for a few GPUs, chunked prefill gets much of the benefit.
