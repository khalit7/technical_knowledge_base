How quality was measured (depth)

How quality was measured. Each file was compared with the F16 file on the same text: the first 40 windows of 512 tokens of WikiText-2's test split, the last 255 tokens of each window scored (10,200 tokens), with llama-perplexity --kl-divergence. Three numbers: perplexity (exp of the average negative log-probability of the true next token; lower is better), KL divergence against the F16 model's whole next-token distribution, and same top token (how often both pick the same most likely token, what greedy decoding sees). Rank by KLD: perplexity can improve by luck on one text, while KLD only grows as the model drifts from what was trained.

What the ladder teaches

  1. The importance matrix is the cheapest quality there is. Same size, same speed: KLD fell by % at Q4_K_M, % at Q3_K_M and % at Q2_K. Prefer GGUFs made with one; makers usually say so on the card or in the file name.
  2. The averages hide the bad tokens. At Q4_K_M with an imatrix the mean KLD is , but the worst 1% of tokens exceed and the top token changes on % of tokens, often where the model was uncertain: what users notice as an occasional odd word or wrong digit.
  3. Perplexity can point the wrong way. On Qwen3-4B, Q4_K_M with an imatrix has the lower KLD ( against ) but the higher perplexity ( against ; F16 ). Perplexity rewards being confidently right on this one text, even after moving away from the original; KLD measures the move.
  4. Who made the file matters. The Q4_K_M GGUF that Qwen publishes ({{Qwen/Qwen3-4B-GGUF|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-GGUF}}, revision bc640142), measured the same way against this page's F16 conversion, drifted : twice this page's Q4_K_M without an imatrix and three times the one with (made with an older llama.cpp or from different source weights; the card does not say). Measure before you trust a download.

Newer, more heavily trained models suffer more. The same help text listed LLaMA-1-7B in July 2023 (commit 6cbf9dfb32) and Llama-3-8B now, and each type costs 2 to 6.5 times more perplexity on the newer model (Q2_K +0.87 against +3.52, Q4_K_M +0.05 against +0.18, Q6_K +0.004 against +0.022, Q8_0 +0.0004 against +0.0026). Fewer weights, or more training per weight, means each carries more of the function. Perplexity is not the whole story either: a unified 2026 evaluation of llama.cpp types on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct finds "schemes with similar perplexity can diverge meaningfully on instruction-following and reasoning benchmarks"; there Q4_K_M averages 69.15 against FP16's 69.47 and Q5_0 scores best of all, 69.92 (Kurt 2026, Table 2).
