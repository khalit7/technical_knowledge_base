Three real files holding the same model, Qwen3-0.6B, read field by field: the Hugging Face safetensors file, a GGUF quantized to Q4_K_M, and an MLX 4-bit safetensors file. Every offset, byte and value below was read from the files on 2026-10-11 by src/formats/scripts/extract_fields.py, a from-scratch reader cross-checked against the safetensors library and gguf-py.

Try: play the GGUF and watch the field offsets: almost all of the header is three tokenizer arrays. Then switch to MLX and compare its tensor entries with the safetensors file: one linear layer becomes three tensors.
