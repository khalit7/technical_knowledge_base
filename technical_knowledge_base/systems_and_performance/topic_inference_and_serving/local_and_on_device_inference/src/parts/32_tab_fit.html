Fit and speed: will a model fit, and how fast will it stream?

The arithmetic of Reading sections 1 and 2, for any model and machine. Memory = weights + KV cache + a margin; generation speed ≈ efficiency × bandwidth / bytes read per token. Model shapes come from the released config.json files through the parent's {{Capacity planner|n:3c65c17b0d0d81c08b3bc95ff45c7b13}} model (the same functions, imported unchanged); bits per weight are the averages measured in Reading section 5 (embeddings and the quantizer's mix included); bandwidths are the makers' figures. Derived: an estimate, checked against this laptop's measurements where they exist.

Not modelled: prompt reading time on machines without a calibration point, speculative decoding, the extra memory a long prompt's processing buffers need, and partial offload to the CPU (section 2). The usable share of a Mac's memory is what Metal reports as its recommended working set; it was measured only on this 16 GB laptop, and the same share is assumed for other Macs.
