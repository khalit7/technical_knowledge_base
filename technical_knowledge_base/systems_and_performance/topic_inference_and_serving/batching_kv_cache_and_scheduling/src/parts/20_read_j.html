Reading the metrics together. Waiting requests growing while kv_cache_usage_perc sits near 1.0 means memory is the limit: quantize the cache, shorten max_tokens, add GPUs or a host tier. Waiting requests growing while cache use is low means compute or the token budget is the limit. Preemptions mean admission is too optimistic for the answer lengths you get. A low prefix hit rate on chat or agent traffic with short reuse gaps means the cache is too small; check vllm:kv_block_reuse_gap_seconds against vllm:kv_block_idle_before_evict_seconds (with --kv-cache-metrics). Throughput, latency and cost planning across GPUs and models is the parent's {{Capacity planner|n:3c65c17b0d0d81c08b3bc95ff45c7b13}}; routing conversations back to the replica that holds their cache is {{LLM serving in production|n:3f35c17b0d0d818aa406da1f7a784c62}}.
