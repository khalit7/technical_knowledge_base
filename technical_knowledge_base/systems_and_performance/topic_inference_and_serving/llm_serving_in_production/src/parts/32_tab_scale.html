An autoscaler that follows the Kubernetes Horizontal Pod Autoscaler's rules reads one metric every 15 seconds and adds or removes replicas; a new replica serves only after its cold start. What the model is, and what it leaves out

Inputs, all from the parent page's {{Capacity planner|n:3c65c17b0d0d81c08b3bc95ff45c7b13}} for the running example derived: a request holds one of 32 sequence slots for its prefill (0.42 s) plus 300 tokens at 46.8 ms each (14.5 s), so one replica completes about 2.2 requests a second; the TTFT SLO is the planner's 1.05 s. Arrivals are Poisson (seeded). One shared queue feeds every serving replica, which is what a good router approximates. The autoscaler applies {{HPA's rule|https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/autoscaling/horizontal-pod-autoscale/#algorithm-details}}: desired = ceil(current × metric / target), skipped when the ratio is within 10% of 1, scale-up by at most 100% or 4 replicas per 15 s period, scale-down to the highest recommendation of the last 300 s; replicas still starting count as zero usage. Left out: the per-replica queues a real router creates, prefix caching, the time a removed replica spends draining, GPU node provisioning (folded into the cold start), and quota limits. The page's JavaScript is a port of src/sim/autoscale.py and gives the same 26,088 numbers on 24 reference runs.
