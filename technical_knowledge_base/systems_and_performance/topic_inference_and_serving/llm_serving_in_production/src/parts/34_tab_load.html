Load-test lab: a 5-second stall, seen by two load generators

One llama-server (Qwen3-0.6B Q4_K_M, 4 slots) on the M1 Pro, driven by the parent page's load generator (loadgen.py from the {{Engine bench|n:3c65c17b0d0d81c08b3bc95ff45c7b13}}): 120 requests of about 150 words with 48-token answers, either from 2 users who each wait for their answer before sending the next (closed loop) or arriving at random at 3 per second regardless of answers (open loop, Poisson). In the "stall" runs the server process was frozen (SIGSTOP) 15 seconds after the start and resumed 5 seconds later, the kind of pause a garbage collection, a page-cache miss on the weights, a noisy neighbour or a network hiccup causes. measured on Apple M1 Pro
