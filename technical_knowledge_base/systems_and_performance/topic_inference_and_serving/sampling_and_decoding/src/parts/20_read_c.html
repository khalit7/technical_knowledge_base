2. Sampling: temperature, then truncation

Pure sampling at T = 1 lets the long tail in, and a few bad draws derail the text (Holtzman et al. measured perplexity 22.73 for pure sampling against 12.38 for human text). So the stack reshapes first: temperature, then truncation, then renormalise, then sample.

  • Temperature T divides the logits. T < 1 sharpens towards greedy, T > 1 flattens towards uniform. Reasoning models are usually run at T 0.6 to 1.0 so they keep exploring (section 6).
  • Top-k keeps the k most probable tokens ({{Fan et al., 2018|https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.04833}}, k = 10 for stories). A fixed k ignores the shape: on a sharp step it lets junk in, on a flat one it cuts good options.
  • Top-p (nucleus) keeps the smallest set whose cumulative probability reaches p, the smallest V(p) with Σx∈V(p) P(x) ≥ p ({{Holtzman et al.|https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.09751}}, Eq. 2). The set adapts to the distribution; it was the long-time default for open-ended text.
  • Min-p keeps tokens with probability at least pmin × P(top token), typically 0.05 to 0.1 ({{Nguyen et al., ICLR 2025 oral|https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01082}}). The cut follows the model's confidence: confident steps become near-greedy, uncertain ones stay diverse. It is in Hugging Face transformers, vLLM, SGLang and llama.cpp, which enables it by default (min-p 0.05, {{common.h|https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/common/common.h}}).

Disputed: "min-p beats top-p"
The min-p paper reports better quality and diversity on GPQA, GSM8K and AlpacaEval creative writing, and a human preference for min-p. A re-analysis by {{Schaeffer, Kazdan and Denisov-Blanch (June 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13681}} found omitted data and incorrect statistical tests in the human evaluation, found that "min-p did not outperform baselines in quality, diversity, or a trade-off between quality and diversity" once hyperparameter counts were controlled, and noted that the community-adoption figures were removed from the paper as unsubstantiated. The mechanism above is not in dispute; whether it buys quality is. Tune both on your own task.

The order is not universal

{{Hugging Face transformers|https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/main/src/transformers/generation/utils.py}} applies its warpers as temperature, top-k, top-p, min-p, typical; {{vLLM|https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/vllm/v1/sample/sampler.py}} as penalties, temperature, min-p, then top-k and top-p. {{llama.cpp|https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/common/common.h}} applies temperature last by default (penalties, DRY, top-n-sigma, top-k, typical, top-p, min-p, XTC, temperature), so its truncation sees the T = 1 distribution. With llama.cpp's defaults (top-k 40, top-p 0.95, min-p 0.05) at T 1.5, the uncertain step keeps 14 tokens with temperature last and 34 with temperature first measured here. The {{Sampler lab|#t-lab}} has the switch, every knob on this page and 32 real distributions.
