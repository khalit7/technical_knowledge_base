The idea in one screen

A model only scores every token in its vocabulary; a separate rule, the decoder, edits those scores, reshapes them, cuts the unlikely tail and picks one token, and which tokens survive that rule decides whether the same model writes loops, sensible prose, noise or valid JSON.

One real next-token distribution through the decoder: 151,936 scores in, one token out

Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct writing the opening of a lighthouse story, second token (after "The"); each box draws the 8 most likely tokens. At T 1 the top token " l" (the start of "lighthouse") has 22.0%; dividing the logits by T 1.5 flattens it to 5.4%, with 77.3% of the mass outside the top 12. Min-p 0.1 then keeps 22 of 151,936 tokens (top-p 0.9 would keep 6,102), and renormalising lifts the top token to 18.6% measured here (src/reading_numbers.mjs). Greedy skips steps 3 to 5 and takes the argmax; beam search keeps the B best whole sequences instead of one token.

You need: logits and softmax (assumed), and what a token is ({{Tokenizers|n:3c65c17b0d0d817187bcd94a1b0f15ea}}), which matters for constrained decoding. Where the sampler runs inside a serving engine's step is on {{vLLM: inside the default engine|n:3c65c17b0d0d8113b976d8bcaf2c0722}}.

Decoder
the rule that turns one next-token distribution into the next token.
Logit processor
a function that edits the logits before the choice: penalties, logit bias, a grammar mask.
Temperature T
divides the logits; below 1 sharpens, above 1 flattens.
Truncation
dropping the tail before sampling: top-k (a fixed count), top-p or nucleus (a probability mass), min-p (a share of the top token).
Beam search
keeps the B most probable partial sequences instead of one.
Constrained decoding
masking every token that would break a grammar (a JSON schema, a regular expression) at every step.
Perplexity
exp of minus the mean log-probability per token: how surprised a model is by a text.

Numbers are published, with the source linked, unless marked derived (formula shown), measured here (computed offline on a small open model, scripts in the page source) or unconfirmed. Training draws from the same stack for RL rollouts and rejection sampling ({{Topic: llm-training-and-post-training|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b6876ee72b7056793b}}).

The formula behind steps 1 to 3: one forward pass ends in a vector of logits z, one per vocabulary entry (151,936 for {{Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct|https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct}}, 50,257 for {{GPT-2|https://huggingface.co/openai-community/gpt2}}), and the softmax with temperature turns them into probabilities:

pi = exp(zi / T) / Σj exp(zj / T)
zi
logit of token i (only differences between logits matter)
T
T = 1 is the model's own distribution; T → 0 puts all the mass on the top token (greedy)

Decoders fall into two families: search is deterministic and looks for a high-probability sequence (greedy, beam); sampling draws at random from a reshaped distribution. Constraints sit before either, as a logit processor.
