Updated: closed reasoning models now reject sampling parameters
The original page said many providers "remap or ignore extreme values". As of October 2026 the large closed APIs refuse them. On Claude Opus 4.7 and later, "setting temperature, top_p, or top_k to any non-default value ... returns a 400 error" ({{Anthropic, Opus 5.5 migration guide|https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/opus-5-5/migration-guide}}). OpenAI's reasoning models other than GPT-6 Astra do not support temperature, top_p, presence_penalty, frequency_penalty, logprobs or logit_bias; GPT-6 Astra supports temperature and top_p ({{Azure OpenAI reasoning docs, 21 September 2026|https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/foundry/openai/how-to/reasoning}}). The knobs on this page are now mainly for open models you serve yourself; structured outputs remain available.

7. Built on sampling (owned elsewhere)

  • Self-consistency and best-of-n sample n answers and return the majority answer ({{Wang et al., 2022|https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171}}) or the one a verifier or reward model rates highest; best-of-n is also a baseline alignment method and a reward-hacking amplifier ({{Reward Hacking|n:3c65c17b0d0d8196a6f1f29a817aa974}}). The maths of pass@k, voting and best-of-n, with a sampling lab: {{Topic: llm-landscape, Deeper: test-time compute|n:3c65c17b0d0d812d9e00f6ec89965286}}.
  • Speculative decoding drafts several tokens cheaply (a draft model, n-gram lookup, or extra heads such as Medusa and EAGLE) and verifies them in one target pass with an acceptance rule that leaves the output distribution exactly the target's, so every sampler on this page works unchanged. The rule, every kind of drafter and the batch size where it stops paying: {{Speculative decoding|n:3f35c17b0d0d81e086fde85940ebac1c}}.
  • Test-time compute scaling, longer chains of thought and parallel sampling with a verifier, is the decoding-side half of the reasoning-model story; the training-side half (RL with verifiable rewards) is on {{Alignment: SFT, RLHF, DPO Family, RLVR|n:3c65c17b0d0d81d5bed7e608d4061c7a}}.
