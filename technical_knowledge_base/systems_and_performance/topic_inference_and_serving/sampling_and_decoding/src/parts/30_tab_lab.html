Sampler lab

Every sampler from the {{Reading|#t-read}} tab, applied to 32 real next-token distributions: four prompts to Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, eight positions each along the model's own greedy reply. Pick a prompt and a position, move the controls, and see which tokens survive and how likely each becomes. "Draw 200" samples from the result.

Rows

Rows are the most probable tokens at T = 1, before any penalty (␣ is a space, ↵ a newline). Grey: probability after penalties and temperature, before truncation; blue: final probability; the label names the filter that cut a token; an orange border marks a token already in the context. Penalties use the counts the frameworks use: the repetition penalty counts every token in the context including the chat template and system prompt (as transformers does); presence and frequency count only tokens generated so far (as the OpenAI formula does). Tokens beyond the top 60 are grouped in 0.1-logit bins; counts that include them match the exact full-vocabulary result within 1% (recompute.py --exact). Penalties are not applied to tokens beyond the top 60.

What to try

  • Sharp against flat. Capital, position 6 ("Canberra" at 96%) against Cat, position 3 (a name, top token 7%). Set top-k 40: on the sharp step it keeps 39 tokens the model barely considers; on the flat one it may cut good names. Top-p and min-p adapt.
  • High temperature. Preset "T 2, top-p 0.95" against "T 2, min-p 0.1" on any uncertain step: 58,000 to 77,000 tokens against 16 to 177 at the steps tried measured here.
  • Order. llama.cpp defaults, then flip the order to "First" at T 1.5: the same numbers keep a different set.
  • Penalties hurt correct repeats. Pattern, position 7: the right token " blue" is already in the context. Repetition penalty 1.3 cuts its probability from 99.9% to 73.6%, because it appears three times in the context (the prompt counts). A frequency penalty of 1 barely moves it (99.7%): the margin is large and it was generated only once so far.
  • Typical sampling at 0.5 drops the single most likely token on four of the five uncertain steps tried (Lighthouse 1 and 2, Kitten 1 and 3), because its surprisal is far below the entropy.
