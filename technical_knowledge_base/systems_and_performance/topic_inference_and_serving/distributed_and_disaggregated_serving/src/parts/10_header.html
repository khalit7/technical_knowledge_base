Distributed and disaggregated serving: Dynamo, llm-d, TensorRT-LLM, Triton

What happens when one GPU or one engine is not enough: replicas, tensor, pipeline and expert parallelism for inference, splitting prefill from decode and moving the KV cache between them, and the serving layer above the engines (NVIDIA Dynamo, llm-d, TensorRT-LLM, Triton Inference Server, KServe, Ray Serve). Real splits run on an Apple M1 Pro and in vLLM's CPU image, a validated simulator for the GPU cases, published GPU results kept apart. Data as of October 2026.
