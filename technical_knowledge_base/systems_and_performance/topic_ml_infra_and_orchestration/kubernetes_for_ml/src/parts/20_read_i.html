8. Kueue: quota, flavors, topology

Several teams share a fixed pool of GPUs. Each needs a guaranteed share, idle shares should be usable by others, jobs should wait in an orderly queue rather than as Pending pods, and a 16-GPU job should get 16 GPUs that are close together. Kueue is a controller plus webhooks with six kinds of object (all kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta2; v0.20 no longer serves v1beta1):

ResourceFlavorA kind of capacity, mapped to nodes by labels (and tolerations): "H100 in block 1", "spot A100". Optional topologyName for topology-aware placement.
ClusterQueueA team's quota: for each flavor and resource a nominalQuota (plus borrowingLimit, lendingLimit), a queueing strategy, preemption rules, and the cohort it shares with.
LocalQueueA namespace's door into a ClusterQueue. Jobs say kueue.x-k8s.io/queue-name: <localqueue>.
WorkloadKueue's record of one job: its pod sets (count and requests per pod), created by Kueue's integration for Jobs, JobSets, TrainJobs, RayJobs and others. Admission is a property of the Workload.
CohortA group of ClusterQueues that lend each other unused quota; cohorts can form a tree.
TopologyAn ordered list of node labels (block, rack, host) for topology-aware scheduling.

Admission, step by step. A labelled job is made to start suspended (spec.suspend: true: no pods). Kueue's loop takes the head of each queue, assigns flavors (the flavors in order, checking the workload's total request against unused quota, borrowing and preemption), reserves the quota, runs any admission checks (for example a ProvisioningRequest that asks the cluster autoscaler for nodes first), then admits: it writes the chosen flavors into the Workload, injects the flavor's node labels as a nodeSelector and unsuspends the job. From there the normal scheduler places pods. The parent's {{Scheduling lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b5925bfd1d8665dd4b}} shows queueing and cohort borrowing and reclaim; here are the two things it did not run, one decision per step.

What Kueue decided for each workload: a full flavor falls through to the next; a full block refuses with quota free measured

Seven workloads as Kueue v0.20.2 recorded them (16 Oct 2026): four ordinary jobs against a ClusterQueue with two flavors, and three topology-aware jobs against a ClusterQueue whose flavor carries a Topology. Each step replays one decision of the flavor assigner from the Workload objects (src/runs/e10a.workloads.yaml, e10b*.workloads.yaml): the requests summed over the pod set, each flavor's quota before and after, and the message or nodeSelector Kueue wrote.

Flavors in order measuredA ClusterQueue with flavor km-b1 (8 GPUs of quota, nodes labelled block b1) listed before km-b2 (4 GPUs, block b2). Four jobs of 2 pods x 2 GPUs: the first two got km-b1, the third km-b2 (pods got nodeSelector: mi.example.com/block: b2 injected), the fourth waited with insufficient unused quota for example.com/gpu in flavor km-b1, 4 more needed, insufficient unused quota for example.com/gpu in flavor km-b2, 4 more needed.
Topology-aware scheduling measuredA flavor with a Topology (block, then host). Three pods of 2 GPUs with kueue.x-k8s.io/podset-required-topology: mi.example.com/block were admitted inside block b1 (two pods on worker, one on worker2). Three pods of 4 GPUs, the same annotation, 12 GPUs of quota free: refused, topology "km-topo" allows to fit only 2 out of 3 pod(s); all pods must fit in a single "mi.example.com/block" topology domain. With podset-preferred-topology instead, admitted across both blocks.

The cost. Kueue reasons about quota, not nodes (except with topology): with 12 GPUs of quota and 12 GPUs in the cluster, an admitted job can still be unplaceable if a node is cordoned or fragmented. Keep quotas equal to what is really schedulable, and mind the webhook: as section 7 found, while Kueue is down no pod can be created anywhere it watches.

Topology-aware scheduling is beta and on by default since v0.14 (pkg/features/kube_features.go lines 975-978). Kueue then pins each pod with a nodeSelector on the host label (the pods above got kubernetes.io/hostname: mi-kind-worker), so the default scheduler only confirms the placement.

The rest of Kueue (described, not run here):

  • Flavor fungibility: by default the search stops at the first flavor where the workload fits or fits by borrowing (whenCanBorrow: MayStopSearch), but tries the next flavor before preempting in the current one (whenCanPreempt: TryNextFlavor) (apis/kueue/v1beta2/clusterqueue_types.go lines 526-549).
  • Preemption: withinClusterQueue (by priority inside a team), reclaimWithinCohort (take back lent quota; the parent's lab measured it), borrowWithinCohort (preempt to borrow). Kueue preempts whole workloads, which is the right unit for training.
  • Fair sharing: in the Kueue configuration, share cohort capacity by weight rather than first come; and admission fair sharing orders a queue by each LocalQueue's recent usage ({{Kueue fair sharing|https://kueue.sigs.k8s.io/docs/concepts/fair_sharing/}} docs).
  • waitForPodsReady: evict and requeue an admitted workload whose pods are not all ready within a timeout (default 30 minutes in v0.20), closing the gap between "quota reserved" and "pods really running".
  • MultiKueue: one manager cluster dispatching workloads to several worker clusters.
