Kueue admission: what Kueue decided for each workload, and why

Seven workloads, as Kueue v0.20.2 recorded them on this topic's kind cluster (16 Oct 2026): four ordinary jobs against a ClusterQueue with two flavors, and three topology-aware jobs against a ClusterQueue whose flavor carries a Topology. Each step replays one decision of Kueue's flavor assigner from the Workload objects (src/runs/e10a.workloads.yaml, e10b*.workloads.yaml): the requests summed over the pod set, each flavor's quota before and after, and the message or nodeSelector Kueue wrote. Reading section {{7|#t-read}} explains the objects; the parent's {{Scheduling lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b5925bfd1d8665dd4b}} has queueing, borrowing and reclaim.

The objects that ran

apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta2
kind: ResourceFlavor
metadata: {name: km-b1}
spec:
  nodeLabels: {mi.example.com/block: b1}     # km-b2: b2
---
apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta2
kind: ClusterQueue
metadata: {name: km-cq}
spec:
  cohortName: km-cohort
  namespaceSelector: {}
  queueingStrategy: BestEffortFIFO
  resourceGroups:
  - coveredResources: ["cpu", "memory", "example.com/gpu"]
    flavors:
    - name: km-b1
      resources:
      - {name: cpu, nominalQuota: 4}
      - {name: memory, nominalQuota: 2Gi}
      - {name: example.com/gpu, nominalQuota: 8}
    - name: km-b2
      resources:
      - {name: cpu, nominalQuota: 2}
      - {name: memory, nominalQuota: 1Gi}
      - {name: example.com/gpu, nominalQuota: 4}
---
apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta2
kind: Topology
metadata: {name: km-topo}
spec:
  levels:
  - nodeLabel: mi.example.com/block
  - nodeLabel: kubernetes.io/hostname
---
apiVersion: kueue.x-k8s.io/v1beta2
kind: ResourceFlavor
metadata: {name: km-tas}
spec:
  nodeLabels: {km.example.com/tas: "true"}
  topologyName: km-topo                      # ClusterQueue km-cq-tas: 4 CPUs, 3Gi, 12 GPUs of km-tas

Abridged from src/k8s/e10-kueue.yaml; jobs from src/k8s/kueue-job.sh (an Indexed Job labelled kueue.x-k8s.io/queue-name, the TAS ones annotated kueue.x-k8s.io/podset-required-topology or podset-preferred-topology on the pod template). The label km.example.com/tas=true was put on the three workers for the TAS runs and removed after.
