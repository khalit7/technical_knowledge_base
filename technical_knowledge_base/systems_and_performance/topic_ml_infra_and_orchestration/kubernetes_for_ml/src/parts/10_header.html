Kubernetes for ML: scheduling, device plugins, gang scheduling and operators

How a training job becomes running pods on Kubernetes, taught from zero and run for real: the control loop (API server, etcd, controllers, scheduler, kubelet), Jobs and their failure policies, the scheduler's filter and score cycle, GPUs as extended resources, device plugins and DRA, placement and preemption, gang scheduling (Kueue, JobSet, Volcano, KAI, and the new native PodGroup), Kueue and JobSet in depth, operators (Kubeflow Trainer v2), and volumes. Every manifest ran on a kind cluster (Kubernetes v1.37.0) with the topic's running example. A child of Topic: ml-infra-and-orchestration. Data as of October 2026.
