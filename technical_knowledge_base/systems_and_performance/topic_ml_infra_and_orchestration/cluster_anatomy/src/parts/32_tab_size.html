Sizing calculator

From a GPU count to nodes, network switches, cables, racks, power and shared-storage bandwidth, with the fat-tree arithmetic of section 4: switches of k ports; each leaf uses half its ports down, half up; two layers while the network cards fit in k2/2, otherwise three, with the chosen oversubscription where the pods meet the top layer. Rail-optimised means one leaf per GPU index (rail) in every group of k/2 nodes. Start from a preset: three of them are published designs, and the line under the results says whether the formula reproduces their switch counts.

What the model leaves out: management, storage and out-of-band networks (NVIDIA's 4-SU SuperPOD adds separate storage and management switches), the fabric manager's own ports, spare ports for growth, switch and cooling power, and modular (chassis) switches whose radix is far above 144. Storage bandwidth follows NVIDIA's SuperPOD guidance per 32 nodes ({{storage guidance|https://docs.nvidia.com/dgx-superpod/reference-architecture-scalable-infrastructure-b200/latest/storage-architecture.html}}), write half of read. Power is node maximum power times nodes; site power multiplies by PUE. All outputs derived.
