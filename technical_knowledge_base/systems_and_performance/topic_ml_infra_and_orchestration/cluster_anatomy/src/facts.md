# Verified facts (ca, 2026-10-16)

- DGX H100 user guide: 8 x H100 (640 GB); 4th-gen NVLink "900 GB/s GPU-to-GPU"; 2 x Xeon 8480C 56 cores; 2 TB in 32 DIMMs; "4 x OSFP ports for 8 x ConnectX-7 Single Port InfiniBand Cards" up to 400 Gb/s; "2 x ConnectX-7 Dual Port Ethernet Cards" (storage and in-band); dual 100 GbE + 10 GbE; BMC 1 GbE; 2 x 1.92 TB M.2 RAID 1; 8 x 3.84 TB U.2 RAID 0; 6 x 3.3 kW 4+2; 10.2 kW max. https://docs.nvidia.com/dgx/dgxh100-user-guide/introduction-to-dgxh100.html
- DGX B200 page: 1,440 GB, 64 TB/s HBM3e, 14.4 TB/s NVLink aggregate, 2 x Xeon 8570 (112 cores), 2 TB, 8 CX-7 + 2 BlueField-3, ~14.3 kW max. https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/dgx-b200/
- DGX GB200 guide: 18 compute trays (2 Grace + 4 Blackwell), 9 NVLink switch trays, ~120 kW, liquid; per tray 4 x CX-7 400G (cluster), 2 x BlueField-3 (storage/mgmt), 4 x 3.84 TB E1.S RAID 0 + 1.92 TB M.2. GB200 NVL72 page: 13.4 TB HBM3E, 576 TB/s, 130 TB/s NVLink.
- SuperPOD B200 RA: "four network fabrics" (compute, storage, in-band, OOB); "two DGX B200 systems per rack"; SU = 32 DGX; Table 4: 1 SU 31 nodes 8 leaf 4 spine; 2 SU 63/16/8; 3 SU 95/24/16; 4 SU 127/32/16, 1,020 compute+UFM cables, 1,024 spine-leaf cables (one DGX slot to UFM); storage per node "must exceed 40 GBps", node-side ~4:3; Table 6 storage read per SU 40 (standard) / 125 (enhanced) GB/s, 4 SU 160/500; write about half.
- Meta RSC (2022-01-24): 760 DGX A100 = 6,080 GPUs phase 1, 16,000 planned; Quantum 1600 Gb/s two-level Clos no oversubscription; 175 PB FlashArray, 46 PB cache, 10 PB FlashBlade; 16 TB/s target.
- Meta GenAI (2024-03-12): two clusters of 24,576 H100; RoCE (Arista 7800, Wedge400, Minipack2) and InfiniBand Quantum-2; 400 Gbps endpoints; Grand Teton; Tectonic FUSE + Hammerspace NFS; 350,000 H100 by end 2024.
- Meta RoCE post (2024-08-05): FE network "data ingestion, checkpointing, and logging"; BE non-blocking RDMA fabric; AI Zone, RTSW, CTSW, ATSW; "cross-AI Zone connectivity is oversubscribed by design".
- Fire-Flyer 2 (arXiv 2408.14158v2, 2024-08-31): 10,000 PCIe A100 40 GB, ~1,250 nodes, 2 x EPYC 32-core, 512 GB, 1 x CX6 200 Gb/s per node; QM8700 40 ports; two-layer 800-port fat tree "20 spine switches and 40 leaf switches"; two zones (~600 nodes each), "122 switches"; storage on same fabric (2 NICs, one per zone), traffic isolated with SL/VL (VI-A1); 3FS 180 nodes (III-B says nearly 200), 8 TB/s; node 2,500 W vs 4,200 W DGX-A100 (Table II); under 4 MW, "approximately just over 3 MW"; abstract "costs by half ... energy 40%" vs III-C "60% of the GPU cost", ~83% performance.
- xAI Colossus (NVIDIA press release 2024-10-28): 100,000 Hopper, Spectrum-X, SN5600 800 Gb/s, BlueField-3 SuperNICs, "95% data throughput" vs "only 60%", 122 days, doubling to 200,000. Vendor.
- AWS Project Rainier (Amazon, activated 2025-10-29): "nearly half a million Trainium2"; UltraServer = 4 servers x 16 = 64 chips on NeuronLink; EFA between; Indiana named.
