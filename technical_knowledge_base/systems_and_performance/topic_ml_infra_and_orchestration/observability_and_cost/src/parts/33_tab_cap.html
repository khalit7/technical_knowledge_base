Capacity planner: from a token budget to a fleet, and whether to own it

Three questions in order. How many GPUs does one run need to meet its deadline at a realistic failure rate? How big a fleet does a year of that work need, once experiments and the scheduler's own slack are counted? And is that fleet cheaper owned or rented? Every input says where its default comes from; the ones marked assumption are yours to change.

The run model (6 FLOPs per parameter per token, peak times MFU, failures, restarts, checkpoint stalls, Young/Daly interval) is the parent page's Cost lab calculator, copied unchanged so the two agree ({{Cost lab|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b5925bfd1d8665dd4b}}); its Llama 3.1 405B check lands 2.6% above the model card's 30.84M H100-hours on both pages.
