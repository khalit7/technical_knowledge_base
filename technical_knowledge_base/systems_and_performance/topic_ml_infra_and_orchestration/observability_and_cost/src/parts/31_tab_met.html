Metrics explainer: what each GPU counter would read

One GPU, one sample window, seven situations a training job gets into. Each counter is computed from its own definition (quoted below the grid) on the same simulated chip: 132 SMs, each able to hold 64 warps, the H100 SXM's numbers ({{GPU architecture|n:3c65c17b0d0d810682aae39bd614ec64}}). Pick a situation, play the window, then switch to after the fix and watch which counters move.

Derived, not measured
There is no GPU on this machine. The activity patterns are illustrative (the SM counts, warps per SM and pipe shares are chosen to be typical, not read from a profiler); the readings are exact consequences of the definitions applied to them. The point is which counters can tell two situations apart, not the second decimal. Measured counters from the local clusters are on the {{Cluster dashboard|#t-dash}} tab.
