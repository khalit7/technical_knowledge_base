Topic: ml-infra-and-orchestration

The machinery around a training run, taught from zero: what a GPU cluster is made of, how jobs get machines (Slurm, Kubernetes, Ray), how a multi-node job starts and finds its peers, what fails at scale and how runs survive it, where data and checkpoints live, and what the time and money go on. Run on an Apple M1 Pro with real Slurm, Kubernetes and Ray clusters in containers, with published cluster numbers kept apart. Data as of October 2026.
