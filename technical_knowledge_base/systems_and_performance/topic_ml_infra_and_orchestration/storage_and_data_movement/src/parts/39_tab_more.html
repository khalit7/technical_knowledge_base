How this page was made

Measured on an Apple M1 Pro with Docker Desktop's Linux VM, on a shared machine, 2026-10-16: the toy parallel file system (src/runs/tpfs.py), SeaweedFS 4.48 (image chrislusf/seaweedfs pinned by digest sha256:4e61d15f...), strace in a torch 2.14.1 container, and DCP and safetensors natively with torch 2.14.1 and safetensors 0.8.0. Scripts, raw records and notes are in the page's src/ folder. Nothing here ran on Lustre, GPFS, WEKA, VAST, 3FS or S3 itself: their behaviour is quoted from their documentation, labelled.
