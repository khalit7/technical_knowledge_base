Timeline implied by defaults (my note): a hung collective is declared by the watchdog after 10 min;
if the watchdog itself is wedged, the heartbeat monitor kills the process after 8 min without
heartbeat; on timeout, the flight recorder (2000 entries) is dumped with up to 15 s extra wait;
then SkipCleanUp tears the process down; torchrun notices within 0.1 s and restarts the group
if --max-restarts allows (CLI default 0).
