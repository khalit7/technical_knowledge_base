RATES AND OTHER NUMBERS (verbatim)
- "RSC-1 has an rf of 6.50 failures per thousand node-days and RSC-2 has a significantly lower rf of 2.34
  failures per thousand node-days."
- "failure rate is ~2.5 failures per 1000 node-days on RSC-1 and periods where failure rate spikes as high as
  ~17.5 failures per 1000 node-days (an order of magnitude higher)."
- "the mean-time-to-failure (MTTF) of 1024-GPU jobs is 7.9 hours, roughly 2 orders-of-magnitude lower than 8-GPU
  jobs (47.7 days)." [dash in original replaced by comma]
- "we project the MTTF for 16384 GPU jobs to be 1.8 hours and for 131072 GPU jobs to be 0.23 hours."
  Model: MTTF = (N_nodes r_f)^-1. CHECK: 16384 GPUs = 2048 nodes; 1/(2048 x 6.50/1000) = 0.0751 days = 1.80 h. OK.
  131072 GPUs = 16384 nodes; 1/(16384 x 0.0065) = 0.00939 days = 0.225 h, printed 0.23. OK.
  1024 GPUs = 128 nodes; model gives 28.8 h vs observed 7.9 h (observed is the measured number, not the model).
- "Already at 4k scale, MTTF is around 10 hours" (Observation 8). Model check: 512 nodes x 0.0065 -> 7.2 h.
- "Attributed hardware failures impact 19% of GPU runtime and less than 1% of jobs."
- "In late 2023, XID errors from a driver bug were the dominant source of job failures on RSC-1"; "GSP Timeouts
  were caused by a code regression that was fixed with a driver patch".
- "Failures may co-occur, 3% and 5% of hardware failures on RSC-1/RSC-2 have co-occuring events of similar
  priority." "we observe 43% (63%) of PCI errors co-occur with XID 79 and 21% (49%) have all 3 event types."
- Overlapping checks: a PCIe failure without the GPU's own XID "occurs in our logs 57% of the time on RSC-1 (37%
  on RSC-2)".
- Crash loop: "we see a 1024 GPU job NODE_FAIL and subsequently requeue 35 times, causing a total of 548
  preemptions (over 7k GPUs)."
- "16% of the total lost goodput resulting from hardware failures on RSC-1 is due to second-order preemptions".
- Lemon nodes: "The identified lemon nodes represent 1.2% of RSC-1's footprint, 13% of daily jobs, and 1.7% of
  RSC-2's footprint. Our lemon node detection mechanism led to 10% reduction in large job failures (512+ GPUs),
  from 14% to 4%." CHECK: 14% to 4% is 10 percentage points (a 71% relative reduction).
  RSC-1 24 nodes / 2000 nodes (16k GPUs / 8) = 1.2% OK; RSC-2 16 / 1000 = 1.6%, printed 1.7% (minor mismatch,
  possibly a node count slightly under 1000).

ETTR DEFINITION (verbatim)
"ETTR is defined as the ratio of productive runtime to available wallclock time of a job run. A job run consists
of one or more scheduler jobs related to the same logical job." "(ETTR attempts to ignore the impact from
userspace failures to focus on impact from cluster stability only). The available wallclock time of a job run is
defined as the total time a job in the multi-job run was either (1) scheduled or (2) eligible to be scheduled but
waiting in the queue." Unproductive scheduled time: "1) Catching up from last saved checkpoint ... 2) Restart
overhead ... 3) Checkpoint overhead". "ETTR varies from 0 ... to 1".
Observation 10: "The RSC clusters are highly efficient (ETTR 0.85-0.9) for their largest and highest priority
jobs." ETTR of ~0.9 for 2+ day 2048-4096 GPU runs assumes Daly-Young optimal checkpoint interval and 5 min restart
and 5 min checkpoint write overhead. For ~12,000 GPUs: "checkpoint write time overhead needs to be on the order of
~10 seconds or failure rate needs to dramatically improve from 6.50 to ~1."
