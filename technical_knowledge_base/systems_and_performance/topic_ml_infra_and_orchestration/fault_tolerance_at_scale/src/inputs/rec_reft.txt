SOURCE: Wang, Kang, Shi, He, Tang, Pan, Zheng, Wu, Zhou, ... "Fault-Tolerant Hybrid-Parallel Training at Scale with Reliable and Efficient In-memory Checkpointing" (REFT). arXiv:2310.12670v4, 19 Aug 2024. HKBU, HKUST(GZ), HIT Shenzhen, Huawei.
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12670
LABEL: vendor (authors). Read 2026-10-16. Venue not confirmed from the PDF.

MECHANISM (my words): Distributed in-memory checkpointing for hybrid-parallel training: snapshots of parameter/optimizer shards are taken asynchronously to host memory with three-level on-device scheduling to avoid competing with training (Hierarchical Asynchronous Snapshotting). For hardware failures, it adds intra-node redundancy rather than cross-node copies: Asynchronous Redundant Copying, Asynchronous Erasure Coding, and Asynchronous Optimizer Recomputing. Restart loads directly from distributed host memory (Distributed In-memory Checkpoint Loading) instead of NFS.

VERBATIM
- "During pretraining Llama-2-6.7B on 512 V100-SXM2-16GB GPUs, REFT-save achieves 5% to zero overhead compared to the state-of-the-art in-memory protection method. It also achieves zero in-memory checkpoint saving overhead on Frontier while training Llama-2-34B on 256 MI250X devices, 512 GPUs. REFT has been integrated into an industrial LM training platform".
- "on-device erasure coding achieves up to 12x to 15x higher throughput than snapshotting on V100 and A100 GPUs."
- Motivation: existing checkpoint protection time "can be even higher than that of training time (up to 6.63x of iteration time)".
- DataStates-LLM (independent of REFT) cites REFT as reporting "38% PCIe bandwidth utilization at 6 GB/s".

CAVEATS
- 512 V100 16GB and 512 MI250X GCDs (Frontier); "zero overhead" is the authors' measurement. Recovery time numbers not extracted.
