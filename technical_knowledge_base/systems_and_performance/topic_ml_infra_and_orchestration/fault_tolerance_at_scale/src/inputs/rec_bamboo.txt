SOURCE: Thorpe, Zhao, Eyolfson, Qiao (UCLA), Jia (CMU), Zhang (MSR), Netravali (Princeton), Xu (UCLA). "Bamboo: Making Preemptible Instances Resilient for Affordable Training of Large DNNs". NSDI 2023, Apr 17-19, 2023.
URL: https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi23/presentation/thorpe (PDF https://www.usenix.org/system/files/nsdi23-thorpe.pdf)
LABEL: vendor (authors). Read 2026-10-16.

MECHANISM (my words): For pipeline-parallel training on spot instances, each node also holds and computes the layers of its pipeline successor (redundant computation), scheduled into pipeline bubbles. When a node is preempted, its neighbour already has the state and can take over its stage immediately, so training continues without a checkpoint restart; full reconfiguration is needed only when consecutive nodes are lost, which Bamboo makes unlikely by placing consecutive pipeline nodes in different zones. Needs about 1.5x more nodes than an on-demand pipeline for memory.

VERBATIM
- Abstract: "introducing redundant computations into the training pipeline, i.e., whereby one node performs computations over not only its own layers but also over some layers in its neighbor ... Bamboo carefully fills redundant computations into these bubbles, providing resilience at a low cost. Across a variety of widely used DNN models, Bamboo outperforms traditional checkpointing by 3.7x in training throughput, and reduces costs by 2.4x compared to a setting where on-demand instances are used."
- Intro results: "evaluated it by training 6 representative DNN models using EC2 spot clusters comprised of p3 instances. Compared to a baseline using on-demand instances, Bamboo delivers a 3.6x cost reduction. Bamboo also outperforms a checkpointing approach by 3.7x." Simulation: value (performance-per-dollar) "much higher (2.48x)".
- Zones: consecutive nodes "come from different zones, minimizing the chance of consecutive preemptions at a small (<5%) overhead".
- Size: "Bamboo's pipeline should be ~1.5x (see 6.4) longer than an on-demand pipeline"; spot "much cheaper (e.g., 3-4x on EC2)"; "the hourly rate of a GPU-based spot instance is only ~30% of that for its on-demand counterpart on Amazon EC2".
- Motivation run: "train GPT-2 over 64 p3.2xlarge GPU spot instances on EC2" (V100).

CAVEATS
- Cost reduction reported as 2.4x in the abstract and 3.6x in the intro (different baselines/settings in the paper). V100 spot instances; models small by 2026 standards (Oobleck reports Bamboo OOMs on GPT-3 sizes).
