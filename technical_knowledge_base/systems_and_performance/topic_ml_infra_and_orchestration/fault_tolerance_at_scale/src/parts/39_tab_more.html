Primary sources and the best resources

  • {{Llama 3, section 3.3.4|https://arxiv.org/html/2407.21783v3#S3.SS3.SSS4}}: the most quoted failure table, and how Meta kept over 90% effective training time. 15 min
  • {{Training LLMs with Fault Tolerant HSDP on 100,000 GPUs (Meta, 2026)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00277}}: the successor table and fault-tolerant HSDP at scale. 40 min
  • {{Kokolis et al., Revisiting Reliability in Large-Scale ML Research Clusters (HPCA 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21680}}: failure rates per node-day, lemon nodes, the ETTR model. 45 min
  • {{ByteRobust (SOSP 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16293}}: detection, warm standbys, replay localisation across 778,135 jobs. 45 min
  • {{MegaScale (NSDI 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15627}}: fault tolerance and straggler hunting on more than 10,000 GPUs. 40 min
  • {{Hu et al., Characterization of LLM Development in the Datacenter (NSDI 2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07648}}: failures by job and by GPU time. 40 min
  • {{Jeon et al., Philly (USENIX ATC 2019)|https://www.usenix.org/system/files/atc19-jeon.pdf}}: the first large study of failures in a GPU cluster. 40 min
  • {{Gemini: fast failure recovery with in-memory checkpoints (SOSP 2023)|https://doi.org/10.1145/3600006.3613145}}: peer CPU-memory checkpoints and a measured recovery breakdown. 30 min
  • {{Lin et al., Understanding Stragglers in Large Model Training Using What-if Analysis|https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05713}}: how common stragglers are and why. 40 min
  • {{FALCON|https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12588}}: detecting and mitigating fail-slow at scale. 30 min
  • {{Ma et al., Understanding Silent Data Corruption in LLM Training|https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12340}}: SDC measured on real unhealthy nodes. 30 min
  • {{Gemini 1.0 report, section 3|https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11805}}: goodput, in-memory replicas, SDC every week or two. 10 min
  • {{torchft|https://github.com/pytorch/torchft}} and {{NVIDIA Resiliency Extension|https://nvidia.github.io/nvidia-resiliency-ext/}}: the two open libraries for in-job fault tolerance and straggler detection. 20 min each
  • {{Google Cloud: ML Productivity Goodput|https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/goodput-metric-as-measure-of-ml-productivity}} and {{ml-goodput-measurement|https://github.com/AI-Hypercomputer/ml-goodput-measurement}}: goodput as three factors and the badput buckets. 15 min
  • {{DCGM diagnostics|https://docs.nvidia.com/datacenter/dcgm/latest/user-guide/dcgm-diagnostics.html}} and the {{Xid catalogue|https://docs.nvidia.com/deploy/xid-errors/analyzing-xid-catalog.html}}: what each health check runs and what each error asks you to do. 20 min
