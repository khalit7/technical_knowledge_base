How training teams organise this: what they have published (six organisations)

The pattern: the scheduler is often custom at the largest scale (MAST, Beaker, HAI Platform), the tracker is usually bought or adopted (OLMo's logs are on Weights & Biases), and workflow engines came out of product companies with many recurring pipelines (Netflix, Lyft, Airbnb) rather than out of pretraining labs, whose main "pipeline" is one long job plus evaluation. Design: the platform for a 1,000-GPU training lab (one reasonable answer, with the trade-off at each choice)

1,000 GPUs (125 nodes of 8), about 50 researchers, a few long pretraining runs, hundreds of fine-tuning and ablation runs a week, nightly evaluation of new checkpoints.

  1. Scheduler: Slurm, or Kubernetes with Kueue and JobSet (the root page and its Scheduling lab compare them); on AWS, HyperPod gives either with node replacement (section 10). The platform layer must work with either: every job goes through one submission wrapper.
  2. Submission wrapper (the piece most teams underbuild): a small CLI that refuses a dirty git tree, resolves the dataset version and container image digest, creates the tracker run with those as tags, then calls sbatch or creates the JobSet. Lineage then exists for every run, not just the careful ones.
  3. Tracker: hosted Weights & Biases (no operations, live system metrics, cost per seat, data leaves your network, and since May 2025 owned by CoreWeave, a GPU cloud) or self-hosted MLflow on PostgreSQL plus object storage (free and open, you run and back it up, simpler UI). Choose by data policy and who will operate it. Either way, a nightly export of runs and the registry to your own storage: Neptune's shutdown is the reason.
  4. Registry: the tracker's registry, with aliases per purpose (champion, eval-candidate) and version tags for evidence; checkpoints stay in object storage and the registry points at them.
  5. Workflow engine: for the recurring pipelines only: data preparation, the nightly evaluation of every new checkpoint, registration and promotion. Airflow if a data team already runs it; Flyte or Kubeflow Pipelines on a Kubernetes estate. The training job itself stays a scheduler job that the pipeline submits and waits for.
  6. Gate: promotion scored on a frozen evaluation set whose hash is checked, champion and challenger in the same step (section 6); a human approval for anything that serves users.
  7. Retention: keep every registered version's checkpoint, keep the last few checkpoints of each run, delete the rest on a schedule; the tracker's records cost little and are kept.

What breaks first, and how to check: the tracker's database under many runs logging many metrics at a high rate (load-test it with the expected points per second: for example 200 concurrent runs x 50 metrics every 10 s is 1,000 points per second derived, illustrative inputs; SageMaker's managed tracker documents its ceiling as transactions per second, section 10); artifact storage filled by checkpoints nobody registered; pipelines that hang without timeouts; and lineage gaps from runs started outside the wrapper.

A workflow step with no timeout that hangs (the real messages)
torchrun --standalone inside a Metaflow step on macOS kept printing [c10d] The IPv6 network addresses of (1.0.0.0...ip6.arpa, 55935) cannot be retrieved (gai error: 8 - nodename nor servname provided, or not known) and then The client socket has timed out after 300000ms, retrying; the step ran for 6 minutes until killed, because Metaflow steps have no timeout by default. Fixed with static rendezvous on 127.0.0.1 and @timeout(minutes=5). Put a timeout on every step that waits on something else (rendezvous itself: sibling page {{Launching distributed training|n:3fc5c17b0d0d81b3a8a5c58224bff9fa}}).
Interview rehearsal (one angle the sections do not already give)

A step failed after a 3-day training step. What does the engine need to avoid rerunning training? Stored outputs of the finished steps (a datastore or cache) and a resume or cache mechanism keyed on step identity or inputs: Metaflow resume, a cleared Airflow task, a KFP or Flyte cache hit. And the training step's output must be the checkpoint in durable storage, not a local file on a node that is gone.
