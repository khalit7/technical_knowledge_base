Hang triage: four broken runs, every rank's stack

The running example's model, data and optimiser (imported from its train.py), in its training loop with one classic distributed bug injected at step 5, 4 ranks under torchrun in one container, process-group timeout 60 s (src/scripts/ld_hang.py, ld_hang.sh). At 25 s, if the job was still alive, py-spy dump read every rank's stack from outside (the container had CAP_SYS_PTRACE, not --privileged); a watchdog thread inside each rank also wrote its own stacks and flight recorder after 20 s without a step. Each case is first shown the way you meet it, the logs only, with the cause hidden: diagnose it, then reveal the stacks.

All measured on Apple M1 Pro, Docker Desktop VM, shared machine, torch 2.14.1+cpu, gloo. Times are seconds since the processes finished init_process_group; addresses scrubbed. The timeouts and messages are what matter, not the seconds.
