Why pending? Decode a reason

Every pending job carries one reason, the first check it failed. Slurm 24.11.5 knows reason strings (src/common/job_state_reason.c at slurm-24-11-5-1), and of them are accounting limits of an association or a QOS. The green ones below were produced on the lab cluster for this page (or by the root's labs); click one, or type any reason you have seen.

How to read a limit name

The 170 limit reasons follow one grammar, and the decoder above splits any of them: who (Assoc: your user or account association; QOS: the job's QOS; ...PerAccount/...PerUser without a prefix: a QOS limit counted per account or user), kind (Grp: the sum over all running jobs of that association or QOS; Max: one job, or one user or node, as the suffix says; Min: a minimum the job does not meet), resource (Cpu, Mem/Memory, Node, GRES (GPUs), Billing, Energy, License, BB (burst buffer), Jobs, SubmitJobs, Wall), and measure (nothing: the amount held now; Minutes: resource-minutes used, including finished jobs; RunMinutes: resource-minutes still to run for jobs running now, from their time limits).

The three questions to ask of any reason
  1. Will it ever start? A limit the job exceeds on its own (QOSMaxGRESPerUser on an 8-GPU job when the limit is 4), PartitionTimeLimit, DependencyNeverSatisfied and JobHeldAdmin do not clear by waiting.
  2. Is it about me or the cluster? Resources, Priority, ReqNodeNotAvail are the cluster; Assoc*, QOS* and Dependency are your own usage, limits and chain.
  3. What does the plan say? squeue --start -j N gives the backfill plan, an upper bound that usually moves earlier (Reading, section 2); N/A means no plan at all.
