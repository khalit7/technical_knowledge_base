Slurm from zero: allocation, gang scheduling, fairshare and preemption

How the scheduler most training clusters run decides who gets which nodes, and how to drive it: the daemons, the life of a job, resources and steps, partitions and QOS, the main loop and backfill, priority, preemption, arrays, dependencies, reservations and accounting. Every command was run on a real Slurm 24.11.5 cluster in containers (3 nodes, 12 fake GPUs) with the topic's running example. Data as of October 2026.
