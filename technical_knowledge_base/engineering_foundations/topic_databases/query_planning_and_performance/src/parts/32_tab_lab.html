Plan reading lab: eight real plans

Every plan here is real output of EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS) from the chat database used on this page (PostgreSQL 16.2, measured 2026-10-04). Each exercise asks you to find something by clicking a line of the plan, then to choose a fix. Wrong clicks are fine: the feedback says what that line means. The skills are those of Reading, {{section 6|#t-read}}: read bottom up, compare estimated rows with actual rows times loops, count pages, look for spills and wasted rows.
