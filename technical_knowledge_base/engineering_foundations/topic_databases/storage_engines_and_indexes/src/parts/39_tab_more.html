Best resources

{{Database Internals|https://www.databass.dev/}}

Alex Petrov, O'Reilly 2019. Part I (storage engines) is the clearest walk through B-tree mechanics, page layout and LSM compaction. Start here. About 9 h 25 min (Part I about 5 h).

{{Designing Data-Intensive Applications, 2nd ed.|https://dataintensive.net/}}

Kleppmann and Riccomini, O'Reilly, March 2026. The storage and retrieval chapter covers the same ground at a higher altitude, with judgement about when each engine wins (the chapter number in the 2nd edition is unconfirmed). About 1 h 30 min for the chapter.

{{The Internals of PostgreSQL|https://www.interdb.jp/pg/}}

Hironobu Suzuki, free online. Chapters on the heap, the buffer manager, the WAL and VACUUM, with diagrams of real structures. About 6 h for the storage chapters.

{{PostgreSQL docs: Database Physical Storage|https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/storage.html}} and {{Reliability and the WAL|https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/wal.html}}

The primary source for sections 1 to 4. About 1 h.

{{nbtree README|https://github.com/postgres/postgres/blob/REL_18_STABLE/src/backend/access/nbtree/README}}

How Postgres's B-tree handles concurrency, splits, deduplication and deletion, from the people who wrote it. About 45 min.

{{Use The Index, Luke|https://use-the-index-luke.com/}}

Markus Winand, free. Practical indexing: composite order, covering indexes, functions in predicates, reading plans. About 4 h.

{{Designing Access Methods: The RUM Conjecture|https://openproceedings.org/2016/conf/edbt/paper-12.pdf}}

Athanassoulis et al., EDBT 2016: the six-page paper behind section 7. About 30 min.

{{LSM-based Storage Techniques: A Survey|https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.07527}}

Luo and Carey, VLDB Journal 2020: compaction strategies, bloom filters and cost analysis. About 1 h 30 min.

{{The Log-Structured Merge-Tree|https://doi.org/10.1007/s002360050048}}

O'Neil, Cheng, Gawlick and O'Neil, Acta Informatica 1996: the original paper. About 1 h.

{{RocksDB wiki: Leveled Compaction|https://github.com/facebook/rocksdb/wiki/Leveled-Compaction}} and {{Universal Compaction|https://github.com/facebook/rocksdb/wiki/Universal-Compaction}}

How a production LSM engine sizes levels and picks files, with diagrams. About 30 min.

{{CMU 15-445/645 Database Systems|https://15445.courses.cs.cmu.edu/}}

Andy Pavlo's course; the lectures on storage, buffer pools, B+trees and logging and recovery are the best free videos on this page's material. About 6 h for those lectures.

{{Interactive latency numbers|https://colin-scott.github.io/personal_website/research/interactive_latency.html}}

Colin Scott's version of Jeff Dean's list, with a slider for the hardware year (extrapolated, not measured; see the Numbers section). About 10 min.

How this page was made

Measurements: PostgreSQL 16.2 (the pgserver wheel's build, plus contrib modules built from the 16.2 source) and 18.6 (built from source), and RocksDB through rocksdict, on an Apple M1 Pro laptop on 2026-10-04; every script is in the page's src/measure/ and each run takes under 15 minutes. The old page's facts, where each now lives, and the corrections are listed in src/coverage.json.
