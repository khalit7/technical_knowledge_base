Inside real pages

Everything on this tab was printed by PostgreSQL 16.2 about its own files, using the {{pageinspect|https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/pageinspect.html}} extension (functions page_header, heap_page_items, bt_metap, bt_page_stats, bt_page_items) on the 1M-message table of the Reading tab measured. Nothing is drawn from imagination: the offsets, lengths, transaction numbers and keys are the real ones (script src/measure/m1_pages.py).

1. A table page, slot by slot

Try: click a stripe or a row. The strip is page 0 drawn to scale: header, line pointers, free space, then the tuples, which are written from the end of the page backwards (slot 1 sits at the far right).

3. The B-tree path to chat 42000

Try: click each page in the chain to list its entries; the entry that leads to chat 42000 is highlighted. Keys are stored little-endian, so the bytes d7 61 00 00 00 00 00 00 are 0x61d7 = 25,047.
