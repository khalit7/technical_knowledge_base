Caching: every layer, eviction, invalidation, and the LLM caches

Caching taught from zero on the chat product: why a small cache catches most traffic, every layer from the CPU to the browser, how a cached copy goes stale and the mechanisms that stop it, Redis as a cache measured under real traffic, Postgres's own caches, HTTP caching, and the three caches of an LLM product (the KV cache, provider prompt caching, semantic caching). Everything measured here was run for real on a laptop (Redis 8.8, PostgreSQL 16.2, two sentence-embedding models) on real data (an hour of Wikipedia traffic, Quora and PAWS question pairs).
