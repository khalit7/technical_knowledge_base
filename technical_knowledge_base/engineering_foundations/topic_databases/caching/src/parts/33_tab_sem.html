Threshold lab: what a semantic cache serves, and how much of it is wrong

The curves are measured on real labelled pairs with two small embedding models (section 12 of the Reading tab). Here you choose the traffic: what share of incoming questions really has an equivalent question already in the cache. The lab combines that share with the measured true-hit and false-hit rates at your threshold, and with what a model call and a wrong answer cost you.

Try: MiniLM on Quora pairs at 0.90 with 37% true matches (Quora's own share); then lower the true-match share to 10%, as on traffic where few questions repeat, and watch the wrong share of served answers climb; then switch to PAWS, where every non-paraphrase shares almost all its words.
