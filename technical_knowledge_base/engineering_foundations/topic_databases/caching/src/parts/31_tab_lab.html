Cache lab: Redis policies on real traffic

Every number here comes from a real run: Redis 8.8 on a laptop replaying 2,000,000 cache-aside requests per run, hit ratio counted after 500,000 warm-up requests (section 5 of the Reading tab explains the setup). Policies marked "simulated" are exact algorithms run offline on the same requests for comparison; Redis does not offer them. The cost-weighted hit ratio uses the miss costs you set: one key in ten is expensive, chosen at random.

Try: the real hour at 50k keys, LRU against LFU; then Zipf α 1.1, where both hit about 85% and the gap almost closes; then GreedyDual, whose plain hit ratio is the worst of all and whose work saved is the best. Set both costs equal and the two ratios coincide.
