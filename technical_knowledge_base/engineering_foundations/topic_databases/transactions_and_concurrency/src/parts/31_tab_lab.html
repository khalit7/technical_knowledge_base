Isolation lab

Pick an anomaly, a database and an isolation level, and step through the real recording, one statement at a time. Nothing here is simulated: each run is two or three live sessions on PostgreSQL 16.2 or MySQL 9.7.2, recorded on this page's laptop by src/measure/, with the statements, the results, the errors and the lock waits exactly as they happened.

The anomalies are explained in the Reading tab, section 3, and the matrix of all of them in section 4. The lock scenarios (deadlocks, the lock queue, job queues, optimistic updates, advisory locks) are from sections 6 to 8 and exist for Postgres only.
