Best resources

Designing Data-Intensive Applications, 2nd edition (Kleppmann and Riccomini, O'Reilly, 2026)

Data models, storage engines, replication and partitioning: the book behind this whole topic. A book; the data models and partitioning chapters about 3 hours.

Alex DeBrie, The What, Why, and When of Single-Table Design (2020)

The clearest account of single-table design and when not to use it; his book, The DynamoDB Book, goes further. About 25 minutes.

Elhemali et al., Amazon DynamoDB: A Scalable, Predictably Performant, and Fully Managed NoSQL Database Service (USENIX ATC 2022)

How DynamoDB's partitions, Multi-Paxos replication and admission control actually work. About 1 hour.

Discord, How Discord Stores Billions of Messages (2017) and Trillions of Messages (2023)

A real wide-column design for this page's exact question, its tombstone incident, and the move to ScyllaDB. About 30 minutes for both.

Apache Cassandra, data modelling

Query-first modelling with worked examples, partition size guidance. About 40 minutes.

Redis, persistence and scripting with Lua

What RDB and AOF really guarantee; how scripts run atomically and what that blocks. About 30 minutes.

MongoDB, data modelling

Embedding against referencing, and the design patterns (bucket, subset, extended reference). About 45 minutes.

Jepsen analyses

Independent tests of what Redis, MongoDB, Cassandra, ScyllaDB, etcd and others really do under failures; the root atlas summarises the relevant ones. About 30 minutes per analysis.

Prometheus, storage and naming and labels

The TSDB's blocks and WAL, the disk formula, and the cardinality rule. About 20 minutes.

Robinson, Webber and Eifrem, Graph Databases, 2nd edition (O'Reilly, 2015)

Property graphs, Cypher and how a native graph store lays out records (chapter 6). A book; chapters 3 and 6 about 2 hours.
