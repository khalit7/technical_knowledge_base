Primary sources

{{pgvector README|https://github.com/pgvector/pgvector}}

The reference for everything pgvector on this page: types, operators, HNSW and IVFFlat options, filtering, iterative scans, quantisation. About 30 minutes.

{{Malkov and Yashunin, HNSW|https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09320}}

The paper behind nearly every vector index in production. About 45 minutes.

{{Subramanya et al., DiskANN|https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2019/hash/09853c7fb1d3f8ee67a61b6bf4a7f8e6-Abstract.html}}

Billion-scale graph search from SSD (NeurIPS 2019). About 40 minutes.

{{Jégou, Douze and Schmid, Product quantization|https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2010.57}}

The compression behind IVF-PQ (TPAMI 2011). About 45 minutes.

{{Robertson and Zaragoza, BM25 and beyond|https://doi.org/10.1561/1500000019}}

The theory of BM25 from its authors (2009). About 2 hours.

{{Cormack et al., Reciprocal rank fusion|http://cormack.uwaterloo.ca/cormacksigir09-rrf.pdf}}

Four pages, the fusion formula with k = 60 (SIGIR 2009). About 10 minutes.

{{PostgreSQL docs, Full Text Search|https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/textsearch.html}}

tsvector, tsquery, configurations, ranking and GIN, chapter 12. About 60 minutes.

{{Elasticsearch, near real-time search|https://www.elastic.co/docs/manage-data/data-store/near-real-time-search}}

Segments, refresh and why a write is searchable a second later. About 10 minutes.

{{ANN-Benchmarks|https://ann-benchmarks.com/}}

The standing recall against queries-per-second comparison across ANN libraries; read the Pareto curves. About 20 minutes.

{{Hugging Face, embedding quantization|https://huggingface.co/blog/embedding-quantization}}

Binary and int8 quantisation measured, with re-scoring (2024-03-22). About 25 minutes.

{{Manning, Raghavan and Schütze, Introduction to Information Retrieval|https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/}}

The textbook: chapters 1 to 7 cover inverted indexes, tokenisation, scoring. Free online. Several hours; chapter 1 about 40 minutes.

{{Qdrant, filterable HNSW|https://blog.vasnetsov.com/posts/categorical-hnsw/}}

How a graph index keeps filtered regions connected (2019). About 15 minutes.
