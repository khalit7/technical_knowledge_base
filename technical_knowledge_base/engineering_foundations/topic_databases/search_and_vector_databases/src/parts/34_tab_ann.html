ANN lab: recall, latency and memory, measured

Every point is a real measurement on PostgreSQL 18.6 with pgvector 0.8.7: 522,931 Quora questions embedded with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (384 dimensions), 1,000 test queries, recall@10 against exact search, latency of the whole SQL query from a client on the same machine (median and 95th percentile of the second pass), all indexes in memory; Apple M1 Pro, 2026-10-04 measured. Other jobs shared the machine: treat times as plus or minus 30%, and the tails (p95) as noisier.

Try

  • Slide HNSW m 16 from ef_search 10 to 640: recall climbs fast, then slowly, while latency keeps growing.
  • Compare IVFFlat at 23 probes with HNSW m 16 at 40: HNSW is faster and more accurate, but IVFFlat built faster.
  • Pick "binary index": without re-ranking it is fast and poor; re-ranking 100 or 400 candidates buys recall back.
