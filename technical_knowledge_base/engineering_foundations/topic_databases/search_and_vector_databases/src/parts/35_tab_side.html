Retrievers side by side

Real test questions from BEIR Quora (522,931 stored questions) and BEIR SciFact (5,183 abstracts), each answered by five retrievers in PostgreSQL 18.6 with pgvector 0.8.7; results that human annotators labelled relevant are in green measured. Questions were picked at random among those where BM25 clearly beat vectors, where vectors clearly beat BM25, where fusion beat both, and where all failed, so this is a gallery of differences, not an average: the averages are in the table at the bottom. Two randomly drawn Quora questions on adult topics were left out of the gallery; they remain in the averages.

Averages over all test questions

fts_and: plainto_tsquery (every word required), ranked by ts_rank. fts_or: the query's lexemes OR-ed, ranked by ts_rank (no idf). BM25: k1 0.9, b 0.4 (the values Anserini uses for BEIR) over the same Postgres lexemes, computed outside the database. Vector: exact cosine on all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Hybrid: RRF (k 60) of BM25 and vector, top 100 of each. Retrieval metrics are defined on the {{RAG and retrieval evaluation|n:3ef5c17b0d0d8194a914d1843ee26f13}} page.
