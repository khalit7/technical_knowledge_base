8. The ML and LLM angle

The chat product adds retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): before answering, it finds the passages of a user's documents closest in meaning to the question and puts them in the prompt. Each passage now has an embedding plus the facts any app needs: owner, who may see it, source document, and a way to delete it. The team also trains and evaluates models on chat logs. Four database ideas carry most of this, each owned by a child.

IdeaWhat it means hereOwner
Vectors next to the rows: pgvector firstpgvector adds a vector column and ANN indexes (HNSW, a layered graph walked greedily, {{Malkov and Yashunin|https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.09320}}; IVFFlat) to Postgres, trading a little recall (the share of the true nearest neighbours returned) for speed. The passage, its owner, its permissions and its embedding become one row: one transaction writes them, one delete removes them, one backup holds them. Its limits are memory (a 1,536-dimension vector of 4-byte floats is 6,152 bytes as pgvector stores it, about 615 GB per hundred million before any index, derived) and filters applied after the index scan. A dedicated store earns its place when vectors outgrow one server or filtered search dominates; measure recall and latency on your own data rather than trusting a vector count.{{Search and vector databases|n:3ef5c17b0d0d815eb4caef9b4cd4d732}} (sections 7 to 12); {{Vector indexes from the inside|n:3f85c17b0d0d81f29707c130b2ebcfb1}}
Feature stores are two databasesA feature store (Feast, Tecton) is an offline store of feature history for training sets plus an online store of each entity's latest values for serving: a column store (section 5) plus a key-value store (section 6), fed by the same code so training and serving do not drift. Training examples must join features as of their own timestamp (a point-in-time or ASOF join), or labels leak.{{Analytics: columnar engines, DuckDB and the lakehouse|n:3ef5c17b0d0d8157814ec5dd8a0d4fb5}} (section 11); {{ML platforms and workflows|n:3c65c17b0d0d81b9943cf6c72600f96b}}
DuckDB on Parquet for datasets and evalsTraining data, eval results and logs are mostly Parquet files; DuckDB queries them in place with SQL and no server (section 5's two columns of a million rows in 2.6 ms).Analytics (sections 5, 6); {{Storage and loading at scale|n:3f95c17b0d0d81398782efcb29d950d4}}
A language model that writes query plansA fine-tuned 4-billion-parameter model writing pg_hint_plan hints beat Postgres's planner by 1.40 times geometric mean on the Join Order Benchmark (Rohan Bansal, {{2026-09-16|https://rohanbansal.com/qorl}}), on a set also watched during development, so not a held-out result; executing candidate plans costs far more than planning once, so it pays only for one heavy query run again and again.{{Query planning and performance|n:3ef5c17b0d0d817f9e15f445b079e9c8}} (closed block: Learned optimizers)
