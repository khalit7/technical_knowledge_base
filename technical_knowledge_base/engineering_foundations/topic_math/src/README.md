## What changed in the Reading tab
The old Reading was a 29-minute course from zero (vectors to statistics), re-teaching every child with its own animations (matrix-vector, softmax, slope, one step, learning-rate limit, noise). Now:
- **Top**: the core sentence; the hero is the training step as seven boxes, each one area of maths with the tiny model's value and a link to the owning child's section, plus two bands (floating point under every box, variational inference where they meet); the running example every child uses; six new terms.
- **1 The map**: fourteen parts of ML against the maths, the one object to know, the owning child and section, and where it is applied outside the topic.
- **2 One training step**: each move's number, what it teaches and where it is explained (score, bet, pay, gradient, step with the 2/λmax limit, error bar, overflow).
- **3 Many hats**: cross-entropy four ways and curvature three ways (kept: only visible across children), the Fisher/Gauss-Newton correction closed.
- **4 Which area**: symptom to area to owner.
- **5 Reading an equation**: the five steps on attention (kept).
- **6 Child cards**: current title, one line, header time; the glossary is a closed reference block with each term linked to its owner.
- Removed: the six from-zero sections and their six animations (`23_` to `28_js_*` in `retired/parts/`); their units are `owned:` by the children.
