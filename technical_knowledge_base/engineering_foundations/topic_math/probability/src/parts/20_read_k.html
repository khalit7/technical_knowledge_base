Two mistakes the sections above do not show (depth)

Assuming i.i.d. when it fails. Near-duplicate items, many items from one document, or test data leaked into training make examples dependent, so the likelihood is not a clean product and every error bar is too narrow (handled on {{Statistics for ML|n:3f05c17b0d0d810f896cf688ce9aed1b}}).

Forgetting that decoding changes the distribution. Text sampled at top-p 0.9 or temperature 0.7 is not a sample from the model; probabilities computed under the model do not describe it (section 6).
