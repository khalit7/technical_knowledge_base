Books, from rebuilding to research

Seeing Theory (Kunin et al., Brown University)

Interactive chapters on basic probability, compound probability, distributions and Bayesian inference. When: first, if sections 1 to 4 felt rusty. About 1 h for chapters 1 to 3 and 5.

Introduction to Probability (Blitzstein and Hwang, 2nd ed., 2019; Harvard Stat 110)

The best book for rebuilding intuition: conditional probability (ch. 2), random variables (ch. 3 to 5), joint distributions (ch. 7), with the free lecture videos. When: to make sections 1 to 6 automatic. About 10 to 15 h for ch. 1 to 7.

Mathematics for Machine Learning (Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong, 2020)

Chapter 6 (probability and distributions, conjugacy, the exponential family) and section 8.3 (MLE and MAP) at exactly this page's level. Free PDF. About 1 h 10 min for ch. 6, 30 min for 8.3.

Probabilistic Machine Learning: An Introduction (Murphy, 2022)

Chapters 2 and 3 (univariate and multivariate models, the exponential family in 3.4), chapter 4 (MLE, MAP, Bayesian, conjugate priors). Free PDF. When: the reference for every section here. About 6 h for ch. 2 to 4.

Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning (Bishop, 2006)

Section 1.2 (probability theory), chapter 2 (distributions, 2.4 the exponential family), appendix B (every distribution's moments), chapter 11 (sampling). Free PDF. About 4 h for ch. 1.2 and 2.

All of Statistics (Wasserman, 2004)

The fastest serious route through estimation: chapter 9 for MLE's properties, chapter 11 for Bayesian inference. Carried from the old page. About 3 h for ch. 9 and 11.

CS229 probability review and main notes (Stanford)

A compact refresher; the main notes derive the losses as GLM maximum likelihood (part III). Carried from the old page. About 1 h; main notes part III about 1 h.

Monte Carlo theory, methods and examples (Owen, 2013)

Chapters 2 (simple Monte Carlo), 4 (random variable generation, inverse CDF) and 9 (importance sampling). When: research depth on sections 13 and 14. About 4 h.

Papers behind sections 9, 13 and 15

Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation (Gneiting and Raftery, JASA 2007)

The theory of section 9: which scores reward honesty, and why. About 1 h 30 min.

On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks (Guo et al., ICML 2017)

Reliability diagrams, ECE with 15 bins, temperature scaling. About 40 min.

Monte Carlo Gradient Estimation in Machine Learning (Mohamed et al., JMLR 2020)

Score-function, pathwise and measure-valued estimators compared, with variance analyses: the research version of section 15. About 2 h.

Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (Kingma and Welling, 2013)

The reparameterisation trick, section 2.4. About 40 min.

A Review of the Gumbel-max Trick and its Extensions (Huijben et al., 2022)

Gumbel-max, Gumbel-top-k and Gumbel-softmax, with their uses. About 1 h.

Categorical Reparameterization with Gumbel-Softmax (Jang et al., 2017)

The relaxation of section 13. About 30 min.
