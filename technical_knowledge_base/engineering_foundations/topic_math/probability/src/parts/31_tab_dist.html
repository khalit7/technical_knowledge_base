Distribution explorer

Pick a distribution and move its parameters. The top panel draws its PMF or PDF (and CDF), samples from it with a fixed seed, and checks the sample mean and variance against the formulas of the Reading's {{section 6|#t-read}}. The bottom panel reads the same distribution as a loss: fix an observed target \(y\), and see the negative log-likelihood as a function of what the network outputs (the natural parameter where there is one, section 7), with its gradient checked against a numerical derivative.

Try: Bernoulli with \(y = 1\): the loss is \(\ln(1 + e^{z}) - z\), flat for confident right answers and a straight line of slope \(-1\) for confident wrong ones, and the gradient is exactly \(p - y\). Gaussian against Laplace with the same target: a parabola with a linear gradient against a V with a constant one. Poisson: the loss is not symmetric in the log-rate.

PMF

\(P(Y = y) = p^y (1 - p)^{1 - y}\), mean \(p\), variance \(p(1 - p)\)
\(P(Y = k) = p_k = e^{z_k} / \sum_j e^{z_j}\), one-hot mean \(p\), covariance \(\operatorname{diag}(p) - pp^\top\)
\(P(K = k) = \binom{n}{k} p^k (1 - p)^{n - k}\), mean \(np\), variance \(np(1 - p)\)
\(P(Y = k) = \lambda^k e^{-\lambda} / k!\), mean \(\lambda\), variance \(\lambda\)
\(p(y) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} e^{-(y - \mu)^2 / 2\sigma^2}\), mean \(\mu\), variance \(\sigma^2\)
\(p(y) = \frac{1}{2b} e^{-|y - \mu| / b}\), mean \(\mu\), variance \(2b^2\)
\(p(x) = \lambda e^{-\lambda x}\) for \(x \ge 0\), mean \(1/\lambda\), variance \(1/\lambda^2\)
\(p(x) = x^{a - 1}(1 - x)^{b - 1} / B(a, b)\), mean \(\frac{a}{a + b}\), variance \(\frac{ab}{(a + b)^2(a + b + 1)}\)
\(p(x) = \frac{1}{B(\alpha)} \prod_k x_k^{\alpha_k - 1}\) on the triangle \(x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = 1\), mean \(\alpha_k / \alpha_0\)

As a loss: minus the log-probability of an observed target

\(\eta = z\) (logit), \(\ell = \ln(1 + e^{z}) - yz\) (BCE), \(d\ell/dz = \sigma(z) - y\)
\(\ell = -z_y + \ln \sum_j e^{z_j}\) (cross-entropy), \(\partial\ell/\partial z_k = p_k - [k = y]\); the plot varies one score \(z_k\)
\(\eta = z\) (logit of \(p\)), \(\ell = n\ln(1 + e^{z}) - kz - \ln\binom{n}{k}\), \(d\ell/dz = np - k\)
\(\eta = \ln\lambda\), \(\ell = e^{\eta} - y\eta + \ln y!\), \(d\ell/d\eta = \lambda - y\)
\(\eta = \mu\), \(\ell = \frac{(y - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} + \frac12\ln(2\pi\sigma^2)\) (MSE), \(d\ell/d\mu = (\mu - y)/\sigma^2\)
\(\ell = |y - \mu|/b + \ln 2b\) (L1), \(d\ell/d\mu = \operatorname{sign}(\mu - y)/b\); not an exponential family
\(\eta = \ln\lambda\), \(\ell = -\eta + e^{\eta} y\), \(d\ell/d\eta = \lambda y - 1 = \lambda\,(y - 1/\lambda)\)
\(\ell(a) = -(a - 1)\ln y - (b - 1)\ln(1 - y) + \ln B(a, b)\), \(d\ell/da = \psi(a) - \psi(a + b) - \ln y\) (\(\psi\): digamma; computed here by central difference)
A Dirichlet is used as a prior over probability vectors, not as a target distribution: its negative log-density appears as the regulariser \(-\sum_k (\alpha_k - 1)\ln p_k\) in MAP (Reading, section 11). No loss panel.

Samples use a seeded generator (mulberry32) with Box-Muller for Gaussians, inverse CDF for the exponential and Laplace, Knuth's multiplication method for the Poisson, sums of Bernoullis for the binomial, Gamma draws (Marsaglia and Tsang, 2000) for Beta and Dirichlet. Formulas: Bishop, PRML, appendix B. Defaults and every formula value are checked against src/recompute.py by src/check_page.mjs.
