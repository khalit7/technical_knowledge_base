The idea in one screen

A model's output is a probability distribution; training scores it by minus the log-probability it gave the truth, generation draws from it, and the rest of probability says how to reshape it (temperature, priors, evidence), how sure to be about averages of it, and how to compute with it without overflowing.

One distribution, three things done with it

The root page's tiny model: scores \(z = (2, 1, 0)\) for cat, dog, sat become, by softmax, the bet \(p = (0.665, 0.245, 0.090)\). Score it: the right word was sat, so the loss is \(-\ln 0.090 = 2.408\) (sections 7 and 8). Draw from it: lay \(p\) end to end as the CDF strip and drop a uniform number on it; \(u = 0.7\) lands in dog's slice (section 10). Reshape it: divide the scores by a temperature, or multiply by a prior and renormalise (sections 5, 6 and 9).

You need: the running example, from {{Topic: math|n:3c65c17b0d0d81cdb851c0835b25a538}} (its section 2 is this page in brief). On ground an MSc already covers (axioms, PMF, expectation), this page states only what ML uses differently and puts the rest in closed blocks.

Density
probability per unit of \(x\); only its areas are probabilities, so it can exceed 1 and its NLL can be negative.
Exponential family
distributions of the form \(h(y)\,e^{\eta T(y) - A(\eta)}\); why every standard loss has a "prediction minus target" gradient.
Proper scoring rule
a score whose expectation is best when you report your true belief (the log loss, Brier).
ECE
expected calibration error: the size-weighted gap between confidence and accuracy over confidence bins.
Conjugate prior
a prior whose posterior has the same form; for a coin, Beta plus counts is Beta.
Gumbel-max
sampling a categorical as \(\arg\max_i (z_i + g_i)\) with Gumbel noise \(g\); Gumbel-softmax is its differentiable relaxation.
Reparameterisation
writing a sample as a function of the parameters and parameter-free noise, \(x = \mu + \sigma\varepsilon\), so gradients pass through it.
Log-sum-exp
\(\ln \sum_k e^{z_k}\), the log of the softmax normaliser, computed as \(m + \ln\sum_k e^{z_k - m}\) with \(m = \max_k z_k\).

The one idea to keep
Pick a distribution for the target, let the network output its parameters, and minus the log of the probability it gives the truth is the loss. Every standard loss falls out of that move (section 7), its gradient is always "prediction minus target" (section 7), and the probabilities it trains are honest because the log loss is a proper scoring rule (section 8).
