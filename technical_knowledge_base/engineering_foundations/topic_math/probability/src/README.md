## The 2026-10-24 rebuild to the teaching contract

- **Order and scope.** Fifteen from-zero sections ("the question", plain words, "used for" on every one) became twelve sections in the order an ML engineer meets the ideas: distributions and densities, the multivariate Gaussian, expectation algebra, LLN and CLT, conditioning and Bayes, softmax and temperature, distribution to loss (MLE), proper scoring and calibration, priors (MAP and posterior), sampling, reparameterisation, log space. - **Ownership per the 2026-10-24 brief.** New: LLN and CLT shown by simulation (section 4), softmax as a distribution and temperature (section 6), log-probabilities and log-sum-exp (section 12), Gumbel-softmax at the level of why.
