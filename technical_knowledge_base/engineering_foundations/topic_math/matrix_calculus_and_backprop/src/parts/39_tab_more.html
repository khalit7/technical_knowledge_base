A reading path, from rusty to research

Olah, Calculus on Computational Graphs: Backpropagation 20 min

The forward against reverse mode picture with small graphs; the gentlest start (31 Aug 2015).

Karpathy, The spelled-out intro to neural networks and backpropagation: building micrograd about 2.5 h

Builds a scalar reverse-mode engine from nothing; the code is micrograd.

CS231n, Backpropagation, intuitions 30 min

Local gradients, gates and the patterns in backward flow, with the matrix-product rule derived from shapes.

Parr and Howard, The Matrix Calculus You Need For Deep Learning about 1.5 h

Jacobians and the vector chain rule from scratch, in numerator layout (also arXiv 1802.01528).

Deisenroth, Faisal and Ong, Mathematics for Machine Learning, chapter 5 about 4 h

Vector calculus: gradients (5.2), Jacobians and the numerator layout (5.3), gradients of matrices (5.4), backpropagation and automatic differentiation (5.6), higher-order derivatives (5.7). Free PDF.

PyTorch, Autograd mechanics 25 min

The graph, saved tensors, in-place checks, multithreading; with Extending PyTorch (custom Functions, 30 min) and Hooks for autograd saved tensors (20 min).

JAX, The Autodiff Cookbook 45 min

grad, jvp, vjp, jacfwd, jacrev, Hessian-vector products, with the cost of each.

Baydin, Pearlmutter, Radul and Siskind, Automatic differentiation in machine learning: a survey about 2 h

JMLR 2018. Sections 2 and 3 (what AD is and is not, forward and reverse mode, costs) are the core, about 45 minutes.

Minka, Old and New Matrix Algebra Useful for Statistics 40 min

The differential method of section 6, with a table of canonical forms.

Blondel and Roulet, The Elements of Differentiable Programming about 6 h for chapters 2, 7, 8 and 9

The modern textbook: differentiation (ch. 2), finite differences (ch. 7), automatic differentiation including checkpointing and linear transposition (ch. 8), second-order AD (ch. 9). 2024, free on arXiv.

Griewank and Walther, Evaluating Derivatives (2nd ed., SIAM 2008) reference

The standard reference on AD: complexity bounds, checkpointing (revolve), higher derivatives.

Papers cited on this page

Chen, Xu, Zhang and Guestrin 2016, Training Deep Nets with Sublinear Memory Cost 40 min

The \(O(\sqrt n)\) checkpointing argument (section 4.3).

Pearlmutter 1994, Fast exact multiplication by the Hessian 45 min

Hessian-vector products at the cost of a gradient. Neural Computation 6(1).

Kaplan et al. 2020, Scaling Laws, section 2.1 10 min

The 6N accounting.

Dao et al. 2022, FlashAttention, appendix B 30 min

The memory-efficient attention backward pass.

Ba, Kiros and Hinton 2016, Layer Normalization 20 min

The definition (section 3, Eq. 3; the paper's version has no \(\epsilon\)).

Griewank 2012, Who Invented the Reverse Mode of Differentiation? 25 min

The history, and the cheap gradient principle.

Research directions 35 to 50 min each

RevNet (Gomez et al. 2017), Deep Equilibrium Models (Bai et al. 2019), Efficient and Modular Implicit Differentiation (Blondel et al. 2022), Gradients without Backpropagation (Baydin et al. 2022), Zero Bubble Pipeline Parallelism (Qi et al. 2023).
