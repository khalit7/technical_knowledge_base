A VAE on MNIST

The 2-D VAE of Reading section 8, trained for this page on binarised MNIST (784-400-2 encoder, 2-400-784 Bernoulli decoder, 30 epochs). Left: where the encoder puts 1,500 test images (the mean of each \(q(z\mid x)\)), coloured by the digit's true label, which the model never saw. Right: what the decoder draws at a 10 by 10 grid of latent points. Click a grid cell to mark its \(z\) on the map; click a digit to highlight it. The grid covers the prior's central 92% (quantiles 0.04 to 0.96 per axis); the codes themselves spread wider than the prior (standard deviations 1.55 and 1.08 across these images), because the KL term pulls each image's \(q\) towards \(\mathcal{N}(0, I)\) only on average, against the reconstruction term.

Reconstructions

Top: a binarised test image (the first of each digit in the test set). Bottom: the decoder's pixel probabilities at the encoder's mean code. With only 2 latent numbers the decoder draws a typical version of the digit, not this exact one: the 144 nats of reconstruction error per image.
