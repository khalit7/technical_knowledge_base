Best resources, in reading order

{{Blei, Kucukelbir and McAuliffe, "Variational Inference: A Review for Statisticians" (2017)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00670}}

Sections 2 to 4: the ELBO, mean field, CAVI, stochastic VI. The clearest first text. 2 h

{{Bishop, Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning (2006), chapter 10|https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/pattern-recognition-machine-learning/}}

10.1 to 10.4: mean field, the Gaussian example of section 4, variational mixtures. Free PDF from Microsoft Research. 5 to 6 h

{{Kingma and Welling, "An Introduction to Variational Autoencoders" (2019)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.02691}}

The VAE by its author: chapters 2 and 3 (ELBO, reparameterisation, flows as posteriors). 3 h

{{Kingma and Welling, Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (2013)|https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6114}}

The original: sections 2 and 3, Appendix B (the Gaussian KL). 1 h

{{Luo, "Understanding Diffusion Models: A Unified Perspective" (2022)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.11970}}

The diffusion ELBO derived in full from the hierarchical VAE, then the score view: a longer version of D3 to D5. 2 to 3 h

{{Lilian Weng, "What are Diffusion Models?" (11 July 2021, updated)|https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2021-07-11-diffusion-models/}}

Dense survey with every derivation; good for review. 1 h

{{Yang Song, "Generative Modeling by Estimating Gradients of the Data Distribution" (5 May 2021)|https://yang-song.net/blog/2021/score/}}

Score matching, Langevin, the SDE view, by its author. 1 h

{{Ho, Jain and Abbeel, DDPM (2020)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.11239}}

Sections 2 and 3 and Appendix A: what D4 derives. 1 h

{{Holderrieth and Erives, "An Introduction to Flow Matching and Diffusion Models" (MIT, 2025)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02070}}

Course notes building flows and diffusion from ODEs and SDEs. 4 to 6 h

{{Lipman et al., "Flow Matching Guide and Code" (2024)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06264}}

The reference treatment of flow matching, with a PyTorch library. 4 h

{{Karras et al., "Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models" (2022)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00364}}

Schedules, preconditioning and samplers as separate design choices: the research-level view of D3 and D6. 2 h

{{Murphy, Probabilistic Machine Learning: Advanced Topics (2023)|https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book2.html}}

Reference chapters on variational inference, Monte Carlo, VAEs, flows and diffusion, with code. reference

How this page was checked

Every closed form on the page (model A's evidence, posterior and ELBO terms, the Gaussian KL, \(q(x_t\mid x_0)\), the reverse-step posterior, Tweedie's slope, the change of variables) is recomputed and checked by Monte Carlo or by an independent route in src/recompute.py; the page's JavaScript is compared with it, and the in-browser samplers with the NumPy reference sampler, by src/check_js.mjs. Equation numbers and quotations are from the arXiv versions linked (Kingma and Welling v11, Ho et al. v2, Song et al. DDIM v4, Ho and Salimans v1, Lipman et al. v2, Blei et al. v9).
