8. The measured runs, and where these ideas sit in real models

This page trained three models whose runs teach the models more than the maths; each now sits on the page that owns it:

  • The MNIST VAE (2-D latent, 30 epochs: training curves of the two terms, test numbers, latent map, decoded grid, reconstructions) is a closed block in section 3 of {{Autoencoders and VAEs|n:3c65c17b0d0d814da93bdb159b7c1e11}}; its importance-weighted bound and gap split stay here (sections 3 and 4).
  • The \(\beta\) sweep (five 16-D MNIST VAEs, best log-likelihood near \(\beta = 1\), half the dimensions dead at \(\beta = 8\)) is a closed block in section 5 of {{Autoencoders and VAEs|n:3c65c17b0d0d814da93bdb159b7c1e11}}.
  • The two tiny 2-D diffusion and flow networks (noised then generated back, step positions, straightness, guidance, the sampler lab you can run) are closed blocks in sections 5 and 6 of {{Diffusion from zero|n:3c65c17b0d0d81cf9bf0d94e57ce9164}}.

The pattern: the VAE survives as the compressor in front of nearly every image and video generator; the generator itself moved from DDPM's noise prediction to flow-matching velocity with transformer backbones; and diffusion is being tried for text, where the latents are masked tokens rather than Gaussian noise. {{Topic: generative-and-multimodal|n:3c65c17b0d0d817ab6ade318917bff55}} compares these systems (its Model map); this page owns the maths they share.
