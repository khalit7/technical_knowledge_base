Bottom strip: where the chosen steps sit on the log-SNR axis \(\lambda = \log(\bar\alpha/(1-\bar\alpha))\) (for flow matching, \(\lambda = 2\log((1-t)/t)\)). Steps spent at very negative \(\lambda\) see almost pure noise; a large jump near the data end is where few-step samplers lose quality. "On data": within 0.2 of a moon's centre line (98% of real points are). Both diffusion samplers clip the predicted \(x_0\) to \([-3, 3]\) (the data range), as Ho et al.'s code does with \([-1, 1]\) for images: DDPM then takes the posterior mean \(\tilde\mu_t(x_t, \hat x_0)\) of Reading section 13, and DDIM re-derives the noise from the clipped \(\hat x_0\), as GLIDE and diffusers do. Without the clip, large \(w\) or the end of the cosine schedule throws samples far off (Reading section 20).
