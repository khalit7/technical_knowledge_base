In one screen

A generative model explains each data point \(x\) (an image, a sentence) by a hidden cause \(z\) that we never observe. Training wants \(\log p(x)\), the log-probability the model gives the data, but that needs an average over every possible \(z\): an integral nobody can compute. Everything on this page is one trick for getting around that integral, and its consequences.

The trick: a lower bound

Pick a simple distribution \(q(z)\) and compute the ELBO (evidence lower bound) instead. Always \(\log p(x) = \text{ELBO} + D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q \,\Vert\, p(z \mid x))\), so the ELBO is below \(\log p(x)\) by exactly how far \(q\) is from the true posterior. Raising it trains the model and fits \(q\) at once (sections 1 to 4).

Gradients through samples

The ELBO is an average over samples of \(q\). Writing the sample as \(z = \mu + \sigma\varepsilon\) (the reparameterisation trick) lets backprop pass through it, with far less noise than the score-function (REINFORCE) estimator: on this page's example, 6.2 against 71 at one dimension and 400,000 at a hundred (section 6).

The VAE

A network outputs \(q(z \mid x)\) (encoder), another outputs \(p(x \mid z)\) (decoder); the loss is reconstruction error plus \(D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q(z\mid x) \,\Vert\, \mathcal{N}(0, I))\), whose Gaussian closed form is derived in section 7. Trained here on MNIST in under two minutes (sections 7 to 10).

Diffusion is a VAE with a fixed encoder

The "encoder" adds Gaussian noise in 1,000 small steps; only the decoder (the denoiser) is learned. Its ELBO, worked out term by term, simplifies to "predict the noise that was added": the one-line loss of Ho et al. 2020 (sections 12 to 14).

Scores, speed, control

Predicting the noise is the same as estimating the score \(\nabla_x \log p(x)\) (section 15). DDIM samples in 10 to 50 steps instead of 1,000 (16); classifier-free guidance is one line of arithmetic on two predictions (17).

Flow matching

Draw a straight line from a data point to a noise sample and train a network to predict its direction; integrate backwards to generate. The objective behind Stable Diffusion 3, FLUX.1 and Movie Gen (sections 18, 19).
