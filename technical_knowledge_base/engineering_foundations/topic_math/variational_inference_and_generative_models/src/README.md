# Source of the Variational inference and generative-model maths page

`sh build.sh` writes `../index.html`: it generates `parts/23_js_data.js` from `inputs/numbers.json`, `inputs/vae.json` and `inputs/dm.json`, concatenates the parts, expands `{{text|url}}` links, and converts LaTeX to MathML with `html_utils/tex2mathml.mjs` (which wraps display formulas in a scrolling `div.dm`).

- `parts/`: `01_head` (copied from the information theory sibling, plus `.dm` styles), `10_header`, `20_read_a` to `20_read_m` (Reading: one screen and sections 1 to 20), `31_tab_elbo` (ELBO lab), `32_tab_diff` (Diffusion lab), `33_tab_vae` (A VAE on MNIST), `39_tab_more` (Further reading). JS: `21_js_rd_common` (the root's animation controller), `22_js_core` (all maths and both trained networks as pure functions: model A and B, Gaussian KL, CAVI, schedules, int8 network, DDPM/DDIM/flow samplers, the on-data score), `24_js_read_a` (CAVI animation, estimator chart, VAE curves, beta table, schedules), `25_js_read_b` (forward-then-back animation, straightness), `31_js_elbo`, `32_js_diff`, `33_js_vae`, `99_js_tabs`.
- `recompute.py` (NumPy): every closed-form number on the page with a Monte Carlo or independent check, written to `inputs/numbers.json`. `check_js.mjs`: runs `22_js_core.js` in Node and compares 42 values with `numbers.json` and the NumPy sampler (`inputs/sampler_ref.json`; largest sample difference 5e-11), and prints the straightness numbers quoted in section 18. `test_page.mjs` (run from `html_utils/`): clicks every control on every tab at 390 dark and 920 light.
- `data/`: `vae.py` (MNIST VAE, 2-D latent, then a beta sweep at 16-D; about 3 minutes on 2 CPU threads; MNIST from PyTorch's mirror `ossci-datasets.s3.amazonaws.com/mnist/`, not committed), `vae_gap.py` (log p(x) by 2-D quadrature, approximation and amortisation gaps), `diffusion.py` (two-moons; eps-prediction and flow-matching networks, width 64, 30,000 steps each, under a minute each), `sampler.py` (NumPy float64 reference sampler, same RNG as the page), `sampler_ref.py` (references and the measured table, `inputs/sampler_ref.json`), `extract.py` (builds `inputs/vae.json` and `inputs/dm.json`). Raw outputs: `vae_out.json`, `vae_gap.json`, `dm_w64.json`.
  Rerun: `cd data; OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 uv run --no-project --with torch --with numpy python vae.py 30` (needs the four MNIST .gz files in `data/`), then `vae_gap.py`, `diffusion.py 64 30000`, `python3 extract.py`, `python3 sampler_ref.py`, then `python3 ../recompute.py`, `node ../check_js.mjs`, `sh ../build.sh`.
- `coverage.json`: what this new page took over from the root and the siblings (the information theory handoff, the root's DDPM equation and drill).
- `viz_ideas.md`: the visuals, scored, with rejected ones.

SDE 2011.13456v2, Rezende and Mohamed 1505.05770v6, Blei et al. 1601.00670v9, Burda et al. 1509.00519v4, Cremer et al. 1801.03558v3, Bowman et al. 1511.06349v4, Liu et al. 2209.03003v1, Esser et al. 2403.03206v1, Song and Ermon 1907.05600v3, Nichol and Dhariwal 2102.09672v1, Kingma et al. VDM 2107.00630.

Departures from the child-page method: ordered as a course (each section builds on the last) because the reader asked to be taught from the basics; Reading is about 50 minutes, above the 30 to 45 suggested, because the brief's scope runs from latent-variable models to flow matching; sections stand alone. The page is about 540 KB, mostly native MathML for about 680 formulas plus 120 KB of model weights and VAE data; nothing was cut to meet the 300 KB guide. Two choices in the toy diffusion model differ from Ho et al. and are stated on the page: the network is conditioned on log-SNR (so one network serves both schedules) and on a class label dropped 15% of the time (for guidance).
