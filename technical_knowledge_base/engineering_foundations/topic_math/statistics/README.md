# Statistics for ML: estimation, bias and variance, bootstrap, hypothesis tests

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3f05c17b0d0d810f896cf688ce9aed1b (child of Topic: math)

Interactive page: `index.html`, built from `src/` (`sh src/build.sh`; see `src/README.md`). Statistics from samples and estimators to research tools, on the root's tiny three-word model (whose true mean loss, 0.832 nats, is known, so every tool's success can be counted): populations, samples and the estimate as a random number; bias, variance, MSE, consistency, efficiency and three entropy estimators computed exactly; why divide by n - 1, derived; the law of large numbers and the central limit theorem with simulations of when it fails (skew, infinite variance); confidence intervals and what 95% does not mean, with a 100-interval before/after animation; the bootstrap, percentile against BCa, the paired bootstrap; tests, p-values, errors and power; t-tests, McNemar and permutation tests; Bonferroni, Holm and Benjamini-Hochberg; Markov, Chebyshev and Hoeffding with proofs and generalisation bounds; bias-variance for models and double descent reproduced against Belkin, Hsu and Xu's exact formula; cross-validation and its variance; credible intervals and Bayes factors; common mistakes (peeking, p-hacking, comparing intervals by overlap). Tabs: Reading (about 42 minutes), CI and test lab, Double descent lab, Further reading. New page (October 2026): takes the statistics facts of the old "Probability and statistics for ML" page from the probability page's handoff, with its corrections; eval-specific measurements stay on Eval statistics. No child pages, databases or video.
