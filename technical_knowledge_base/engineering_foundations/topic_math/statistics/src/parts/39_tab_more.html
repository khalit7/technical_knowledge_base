Books

All of Statistics (Wasserman, 2004)

The fastest serious route through estimation and testing; the reference for MLE properties and hypothesis testing. Chapters 4 (inequalities), 5 (convergence: LLN, CLT), 6 to 9 (estimation, the bootstrap, parametric inference), 10 (tests and p-values, including Bonferroni and Benjamini-Hochberg), 11 (Bayesian inference). About 4 h for the estimation and testing chapters.

Computer Age Statistical Inference (Efron and Hastie, 2016; free PDF)

Chapters 10 and 11: the jackknife, the bootstrap and bootstrap confidence intervals including BCa, by the bootstrap's inventor; chapter 15 on large-scale testing and false discovery rates. About 3 h.

The Elements of Statistical Learning (Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman, 2nd ed.; free PDF)

Chapter 7 (model assessment: bias-variance, cross-validation, the bootstrap) and chapter 15 (random forests, eq. 15.1 for the variance of an average). About 2 h.

Understanding Machine Learning (Shalev-Shwartz and Ben-David, 2014; free PDF)

Chapters 2 to 6: PAC learning, the finite-class bound of section 10, uniform convergence, VC dimension. About 3 h.

An Introduction to the Bootstrap (Efron and Tibshirani, 1993)

Chapters 12 to 14 and 22: bootstrap intervals, BCa and why it is second-order accurate. About 2 h.

Papers

Belkin, Hsu, Ma, Mandal: Reconciling modern machine-learning practice and the classical bias-variance trade-off (PNAS 2019)

The double-descent curve named. 30 min.

Belkin, Hsu, Xu: Two models of double descent for weak features (SIAM J. Math. Data Science 2020)

Theorem 1, reproduced by this page's experiment. 40 min.

Nakkiran et al.: Deep Double Descent (2019; ICLR 2020)

Model-wise, epoch-wise and sample-wise double descent in deep networks. 45 min.

Nakkiran, Venkat, Kakade, Ma: Optimal Regularization Can Mitigate Double Descent (2020)

Tuned ridge makes the curve monotonic. 30 min.

Schaeffer et al.: Double Descent Demystified (2023)

Three interpretable factors, in plain linear regression. 30 min.

Yang, Yu, You, Steinhardt, Ma: Rethinking Bias-Variance Trade-off for Generalization of Neural Networks (ICML 2020)

Measured bias and variance of real networks against width. 30 min.

Bates, Hastie, Tibshirani: Cross-validation: what does it estimate and how well does it do it? (JASA 2023)

What CV estimates, why naive intervals undercover, nested CV. 45 min.

Bengio and Grandvalet: No Unbiased Estimator of the Variance of K-Fold Cross-Validation (JMLR 2004)

The impossibility result of section 12. 30 min.

Wasserstein and Lazar: The ASA Statement on p-Values (The American Statistician 2016)

The six principles quoted in section 7. 15 min.

Benjamini and Hochberg: Controlling the False Discovery Rate (JRSS B 1995)

Efron: Better Bootstrap Confidence Intervals (JASA 1987)

Zhang, Bengio, Hardt, Recht, Vinyals: Understanding deep learning requires rethinking generalization (ICLR 2017)

The first non-vacuous PAC-Bayes numbers. 40 min.

Miller: Adding Error Bars to Evals (2024)
