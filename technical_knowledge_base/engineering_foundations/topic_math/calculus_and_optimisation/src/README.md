## Numbers
- `recompute.py` (`uv run --no-project --with numpy --with sympy python recompute.py`) recomputes every hand-worked number and summarises the real runs into `numbers.json` and `recompute.out`.
- `inputs/lr_real.py` (numpy, scipy, scikit-learn; seconds): logistic regression race, gradient noise, SGD, the 1D and 2D slices. Outputs `lr_out.json`, `lr_data.json`.
- `inputs/eos.py`, `inputs/eos_extra.py` (torch 2.14.1, float64, 2 threads; about 1 min each): the edge-of-stability runs and the Lanczos cross-check. Outputs `eos_out.json` (450 KB, per-step losses), `eos_extra.json`; logs beside them.
- `check_page.mjs` (from the repo root) opens every closed block, clicks every control at 390 dark and 920 light with reduced motion, compares the page with `numbers.json` (359 checks, including the step-list numbers of Figures 1, 4 and 8 against `hero2_*`, `gd_steps_*`, `hb_steps_best`, `cvx_*` and against the scenes' own computations), confirms the Newton animation plays once scrolled into view, and writes screenshots to `../.shots/check/`.
- `check_stages.mjs [shots dir]` (2026-11-06, from Storage engines and indexes): at 1400 light, 920 light and 390 dark, every 3D stage and the 2D animation plays when scrolled into view, steps to its last step with one list item per step, flows stop off screen, splits are two columns at 1400 (lab included); under reduced motion nothing moves.
