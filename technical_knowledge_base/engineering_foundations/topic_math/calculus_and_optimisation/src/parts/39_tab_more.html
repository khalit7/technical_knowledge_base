Books, with the chapters this page uses

Deisenroth, Faisal and Ong, Mathematics for Machine Learning ch. 5 and 7: about 6 h

Vector calculus (Taylor series, gradients, Hessians) and continuous optimisation (gradient descent, momentum, constrained optimisation, convexity). Free PDF.

Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization ch. 3, 5, 9: about 15 h

Convex functions and Jensen (3.1), duality and KKT (5.2, 5.5), gradient descent and Newton's method with their analyses (9.3, 9.5). Free PDF.

Nocedal and Wright, Numerical Optimization (2nd ed., 2006) ch. 2, 3, 6, 7: about 12 h

Taylor's theorem and optimality conditions, line searches (Wolfe), quasi-Newton and BFGS, L-BFGS. The reference for how real solvers work.

Bottou, Curtis and Nocedal, Optimization Methods for Large-Scale Machine Learning (SIAM Review 2018) sections 3 and 4: about 3 h

Why SGD works, with the fixed-step floor and the diminishing-step rate of section 16.

Parr and Howard, The Matrix Calculus You Need For Deep Learning about 1 h 30 min

A deep-learning-focused refresher on derivatives of vector functions.

Papers and articles

Goh 2017, Why Momentum Really Works (Distill) 45 min

Momentum on a quadratic, eigenvalue by eigenvalue, with live figures; the source of section 9's formulas.

Cohen et al. 2021, Gradient Descent on Neural Networks Typically Occurs at the Edge of Stability 1 h

Progressive sharpening and sharpness at 2/η (section 17).

Dauphin et al. 2014, Identifying and attacking the saddle point problem 45 min

Why saddles, not local minima, dominate in high dimensions; saddle-free Newton.

Chen and Guestrin 2016, XGBoost section 2: 20 min

The second-order expansion, leaf weights and split gain of section 12. Also the Introduction to boosted trees tutorial (20 min).

Schulman et al. 2015, Trust Region Policy Optimization appendix C: 15 min

The KL-constrained step solved with conjugate gradient (section 15).

Martens 2020, New insights and perspectives on the natural gradient method 2 h

Fisher, Gauss-Newton and the Hessian; natural gradient as a second-order method.

Kingma and Ba 2014, Adam 30 min

The update rule and, in section 5, its relation to the diagonal Fisher.

Sutskever et al. 2013, On the importance of initialization and momentum in deep learning 40 min

Nesterov momentum in the form used by deep-learning libraries.

Liu and Nocedal 1989, On the limited memory BFGS method 1 h

L-BFGS.

Foret et al. 2021, Sharpness-Aware Minimization 40 min

Seeking flat minima on purpose; read with Dinh et al. 2017 on why sharpness is not reparameterisation-invariant (30 min).
