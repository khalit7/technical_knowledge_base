# Calculus and optimisation for ML: derivatives to convexity, Hessians, Newton, Lagrange

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3c65c17b0d0d81eb8b49ed6a0f897f3b (child of Topic: math)

Interactive page: `index.html`, built from `src/` (`sh src/build.sh`; see `src/README.md`). The derivative as a slope and as the best linear approximation, rules of differentiation, partial derivatives, the gradient and directional derivatives, Taylor models (and why every optimiser is one plus a trust rule), the gradient of every standard loss (prediction minus target, both MSE conventions, the canonical-link view), Hessians and critical points, gradient descent derived from the first-order model, the 2/lambda_max limit and the (kappa - 1)/(kappa + 1) rate derived, momentum and Nesterov (Goh 2017), Newton's method (animated against gradient descent on a real logistic loss, including a start where Newton diverges), BFGS and L-BFGS with a real race on breast-cancer logistic regression, XGBoost's leaves as Newton steps (Chen and Guestrin), convexity and Jensen, Lagrange multipliers, KKT and duality, weight decay, SVMs, softmax as maximum entropy and TRPO's KL-constrained step, stochastic gradients (noise measured against batch size, noise floors), and deep-network landscapes (saddles; the edge of stability reproduced on a small real network). Tabs: Reading, Optimisation lab (eight methods on a quadratic, Rosenbrock, a saddle and a real logistic loss, with the Hessian and local Taylor model at every step), Further reading.
