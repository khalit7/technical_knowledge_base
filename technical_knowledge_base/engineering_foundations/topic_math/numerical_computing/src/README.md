## Numbers
- `recompute.py` (`uv run --no-project --with numpy --with ml_dtypes python recompute.py`) recomputes every worked number with NumPy and ml_dtypes and writes `numbers.json`, `recompute.out` and the emulator test vectors `inputs/fp_vectors.json`.
- `check_page.mjs` (run from the repo root with node) compares the page's emulator with NumPy and ml_dtypes on 2,940 values, the softmax and Kahan animation traces, the Summation lab on 9 cases and 36 numbers quoted in the Reading text with `numbers.json`; it clicks every control at 390 px dark and 920 px light with reduced motion and writes screenshots to `../.shots/check/`.
- Measurements on this laptop (Apple M1 Pro, Python 3.13.5, NumPy 2.5.3, ml_dtypes 0.6.0, PyTorch 2.14.1, MPS), 2026-10-05:
  - `inputs/measure_a.py` (about 1 min): bit patterns, format constants, summation error to 10^6 terms, Thinking Machines' 8-number snippet, 20 orders, `torch.sum` at 1/2/4/8 threads, MPS `index_add_` and `sum`. Output `inputs/out_a.json`, log `inputs/measure_a.log`.
  - `inputs/measure_b.py` (about 2 min): one training step of HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M on 4 x 128 tokens of Tiny Shakespeare in float32 (CPU) and pure float16 at 7 loss scales and bfloat16 (MPS); gradient exponent histogram, underflow and non-finite counts, lost updates. Output `inputs/out_b.json`, log `inputs/measure_b.log`.
  - `inputs/nan_demo.py`: the naive softmax NaN, `detect_anomaly`, the fused loss. Output `inputs/nan_demo.out`.
- Library source at a pinned version: `inputs/pytorch_v2.14.1/excerpts.txt` (logsumexp, BCE with logits, log_sigmoid, softplus, softmax kernel, GradScaler defaults, LayerNorm and RMSNorm eps, Welford) and the notes `randomness.md`, `numerical_accuracy.md`, `amp.md`. OFP8 spec text extract: `inputs/ofp8_spec_extract.txt` (fetched through the Wayback Machine; opencompute.org blocks scripts).
