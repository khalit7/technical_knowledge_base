Numerical computing for ML: floating point, precision formats, stable softmax

How a computer stores a number, bit by bit, and what that does to training: IEEE 754, machine epsilon and rounding, the formats ML uses (float64 to FP8 and 4-bit), cancellation, summation error and non-deterministic reductions, the stable softmax and its relatives, mixed precision and loss scaling, and hunting NaNs.
