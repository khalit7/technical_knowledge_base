# Numerical computing for ML: floating point, precision formats, stable softmax

Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/3f05c17b0d0d81d6af0be4a9a2edba8d (child of Topic: math)

Interactive page: `index.html`, built from `src/` (`sh src/build.sh`; see `src/README.md`). Binary and two's complement, IEEE 754 bit by bit (bias, hidden bit, subnormals, signed zero, infinity, NaN), ulp, machine epsilon and unit roundoff, round to nearest even and stochastic rounding, the ML formats with dated sources (float64, float32, TF32, float16, bfloat16, FP8 E4M3 and E5M2, MX and NVFP4 in brief), the standard model and backward stability, cancellation and condition numbers of functions, log1p and expm1, one-pass, two-pass and Welford variance, summation error (recursive, pairwise, Kahan, float32 accumulation; measured to 10^6 terms), non-associativity and non-deterministic reductions (thread counts, MPS atomics, batch invariance, PyTorch determinism settings), the stable softmax, log-sum-exp, log-softmax and online softmax (animated naive against stable with every intermediate's bits), logsigmoid, BCE with logits, softplus and normalisation epsilons with PyTorch v2.14.1 source, mixed-precision training measured on one real SmolLM2-135M step (lost updates, gradient underflow, loss scaling at 7 scales, bf16), NaN hunting, research directions and common mistakes. Tabs: Reading, Float explorer (any number in 7 formats, flippable bits), Summation lab (4 algorithms, 3 formats, shuffled orders), Further reading. New page (2026-10-05); quantisation formats and methods in depth stay on the training topic's Quantization and precision page. No child pages, databases or video.
