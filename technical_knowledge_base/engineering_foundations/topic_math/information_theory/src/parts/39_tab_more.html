Books and papers, from rusty to research

1. Visual Information Theory (Chris Olah, 14 October 2015)

Entropy, cross-entropy, KL and mutual information drawn as codes. When: first, for pictures, before sections 3 to 8. 45 min to 1 h.

2. MacKay, Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms (Cambridge UP 2003, free PDF)

Ch. 2 (entropy, Gibbs' inequality, Jensen; §2.6, §2.7), ch. 4 (the source coding theorem, §4.4), ch. 5 (symbol codes, Kraft, Huffman, eq. 5.23 on the wrong code), ch. 6 (arithmetic coding), ch. 8 (mutual information, data processing), ch. 33 (variational methods and the KL direction). When: the backbone text for this page. 8 to 12 h for ch. 2, 4, 5, 6, 8; 2 h more for ch. 33.

3. Cover and Thomas, Elements of Information Theory (2nd ed., Wiley 2006)

The standard reference: ch. 2 (entropy, relative entropy, mutual information, data processing), ch. 5 (data compression, Huffman), ch. 12 (maximum entropy). When: for proofs and exact statements. Reference; about 6 h for ch. 2 and 5.

4. Shannon, A Mathematical Theory of Communication (Bell System Technical Journal, 1948)

Part I: the three requirements and Theorem 2 (why \(-\sum p \log p\)), Theorem 9 (the coding theorem). When: once sections 1 to 3 are comfortable. 1 h 30 min for Part I.

5. Shannon, Prediction and Entropy of Printed English (1951)

The first language model evaluation: humans predicting the next letter, entropy bounds with 100 letters of context. 40 min.

6. Delétang et al., Language Modeling Is Compression (ICLR 2024)

Models as arithmetic-coding compressors; raw against adjusted rates; read Table 1 rather than the abstract (section 11). 1 h.

7. van den Oord et al., CPC (2018) and Poole et al., On Variational Bounds of Mutual Information (ICML 2019)

InfoNCE and its bound (CPC §2.3), then the family of bounds and their bias-variance trade-off (Poole §2). 1 h 30 min together.

8. McAllester and Stratos, Formal Limitations on the Measurement of Mutual Information (AISTATS 2020)

Why no estimator certifies much more than \(\ln N\). 45 min for the introduction and main theorem.

9. Paninski, Estimation of Entropy and Mutual Information (Neural Computation, 2003)

The plug-in bias (eq. 4.6), Miller-Madow, and why no estimator is unbiased for all distributions. 1 h for sections 1 to 4.

10. Nowozin, Cseke and Tomioka, f-GAN (NeurIPS 2016)

The f-divergence family (eq. 2, Table 1) and the variational bound behind GAN training (eq. 4). 45 min.

11. Schulman, Approximating KL Divergence (blog, 7 March 2020)

The \(k_1, k_2, k_3\) estimators of section 13. 15 min.

12. Rafailov et al., DPO (NeurIPS 2023), Appendix A.1; Kingma and Welling, Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (2013), §2 and Appendix B

The two derivations of section 13 in their original form. 1 h together.
