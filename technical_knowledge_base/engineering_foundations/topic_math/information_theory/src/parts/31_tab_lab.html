Entropy and KL lab

Edit two distributions over the same outcomes: \(p\), the truth, and \(q\), the model or the code. Everything on the Reading tab's sections 2 to 7 and 12 is computed live: entropies, both cross-entropies, both KL divergences, their symmetric relatives, and the codes each distribution implies. The sliders set weights; each distribution is normalised to sum to 1.

Try: make \(q\) equal to \(p\) (every divergence goes to 0); give \(q\) almost nothing on an outcome \(p\) uses (forward KL explodes, reverse KL barely moves); pick "Stream" and check that the Huffman lengths reproduce the 2.375 bits of the Reading tab's animation; pick "Tiny model vs sat" and find the root page's loss, 2.408 nats.
