5. Temperature: one dial on the entropy of a distribution

Sampling at temperature \(T\) divides the scores by \(T\) before the softmax, \(q_T = \mathrm{softmax}(z / T)\). On the tiny model, \(T = 1\) is the bet itself, 1.201 bits; \(T = 0.5\) sharpens it to (0.867, 0.117, 0.016), 0.636 bits; \(T = 2\) flattens it to (0.506, 0.307, 0.186), 1.472 bits. \[ \frac{dH(q_T)}{dT} = \frac{\mathrm{Var}_{q_T}(z)}{T^3} \;\ge\; 0 \quad \text{(nats)} \]

(write \(H = \ln Z - \mathbb{E}_{q_T}[z]/T\) with \(Z = \sum_j e^{z_j/T}\) and differentiate). At \(T = 1\) the variance of the scores under the bet is 0.424, so the entropy climbs 0.424 nats per unit of temperature there. Temperature is the one dial that sets how uncertain the sampler is without changing what it prefers; truncation samplers (top-k, top-p, min-p) change the support instead, and their effects on real next-token distributions are on {{Sampling and Decoding|n:3c65c17b0d0d8115bafcda29a2c7086e}}. In RL fine-tuning, the entropy of the policy's own next-token distribution is the quantity watched for entropy collapse ({{RL for LLMs|n:3c65c17b0d0d818c9bcff7177325fe56}}).

Distillation read as information theory

A teacher's soft probabilities carry more bits than a one-hot label: the relative sizes of the wrong answers. Distillation trains the student on cross-entropy against the teacher's distribution at a raised temperature, which is the teacher's entropy (fixed) plus the forward KL from teacher to student (removable), exactly the split of section 3. Raising \(T\) raises the teacher's entropy and so how much of that wrong-answer structure the target exposes. The gradient on the student's logits is \((q_{T} - p_{T})/T\), and at high temperature the difference itself shrinks like \(1/T\), so the soft term's gradients scale as \(1/T^2\) and are multiplied back by \(T^2\) (Hinton, Vinyals and Dean 2015, §2). On-policy distillation instead scores the student's own samples: MiniLLM minimises the reverse KL from student to teacher, with the mode-seeking behaviour of section 4, and GKD allows a family of divergences between the two. The recipes, the measured teacher-student runs and when each direction wins are on {{Distillation and Small Language Models|n:3c65c17b0d0d81efa2a8c3b3b623eb09}}.
