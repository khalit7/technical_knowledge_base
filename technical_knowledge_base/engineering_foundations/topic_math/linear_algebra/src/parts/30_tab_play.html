Matrix playground

Type any 2x2 or 3x3 matrix, or pick a preset from the page, and read off everything the Reading tab defines: what it does to the plane, its eigenvalues and eigenvectors, its SVD, rank, determinant, norms and condition number. The 2x2 picture can be played through the SVD's three steps, and the orthogonalise button runs the Newton-Schulz iteration of Reading section 13 one step at a time.

Computed in the page with closed forms for 2x2 and Jacobi rotations on \(A^\top A\) for 3x3, in double precision. Because singular values come from the eigenvalues of \(A^\top A\), any below about \(10^{-7}\) of the largest are reported as 0, so a matrix with condition number above about \(10^{7}\) reads as rank-deficient here (a library SVD, which works on \(A\) directly, resolves down to about \(10^{-16}\)).
