A reading path, from rusty to research

1. 3Blue1Brown, Essence of Linear Algebra

16 animated videos: vectors, matrices as maps, determinants, change of basis, eigenvectors. The source of this page's "columns are where the axes land". When: first. About 3 h.

2. Boyd and Vandenberghe, Introduction to Applied Linear Algebra (VMLS)

Cambridge University Press 2018, free PDF. Chapters 1 to 5 (vectors, norms, independence), 6 to 10 (matrices, products), 12 to 13 (least squares). Gentle and applied. When: to rebuild technique. About 10 to 12 h for those chapters.

3. Strang, MIT 18.06 Linear Algebra

34 recorded lectures. For ML: 1 to 3, 9 to 11 (independence, the four subspaces), 14 to 17 (orthogonality, projections, least squares, Gram-Schmidt), 21 to 22 and 25 to 27 (eigenvalues, symmetric and PD matrices), 29 to 30 (SVD). When: alongside sections 5 to 12. About 12 h for the subset (25 h for all).

4. Mathematics for Machine Learning (Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong)

Cambridge University Press 2020, free PDF. Chapter 2 (linear algebra), 3 (analytic geometry: norms, inner products, projections), 4 (matrix decompositions: determinant, eigen, Cholesky, SVD, matrix approximation), 10 (PCA). When: for derivations in ML notation. About 12 to 15 h for chapters 2 to 4 and 10.

5. Strang, MIT 18.065 Matrix Methods in Data Analysis and Machine Learning

Spring 2018, with the book Linear Algebra and Learning from Data (2019). Lectures 1 to 9 (factorisations, SVD, PCA, norms) and 21 to 27 (optimisation, backprop). When: after step 3, to connect to ML. About 12 h.

6. Trefethen and Bau, Numerical Linear Algebra

SIAM 1997 (not free). Lectures 4 and 5 (SVD), 7 to 11 (QR, Gram-Schmidt, Householder, least squares), 12 to 15 (conditioning and stability), 20 to 23 (LU, Cholesky). The best short account of why algorithms lose digits; the operation counts in section 17 are from here. When: for section 13 in depth. About 10 h for those lectures.

7. Axler, Linear Algebra Done Right

4th edition, Springer 2024, open access. Proofs without determinants: chapters 2 and 3 (bases, rank-nullity), 5 (eigenvalues), 7 (spectral theorem, SVD). When: for research, when you need the proofs. About 20 h for those chapters.

8. The Matrix Cookbook (Petersen and Pedersen)

Version of 15 November 2012, 72 pages of identities. Do not read; look things up. Reference.
