# Linear algebra for ML: page source

`sh build.sh` writes `../index.html` (LaTeX in the parts is converted to MathML by `html_utils/tex2mathml.mjs`; display formulas are wrapped in `.dm` boxes that scroll at phone width). Child of Topic: math (Notion 3c65c17b0d0d81d89c26e1dab2fcb701); no child pages, databases or video of its own.

## Numbers
- `recompute.py` recomputes every hand-worked number and summarises the GPT-2 and LoRA data (`numbers.json`, `recompute.out`).
- `check_page.mjs` (run from the repo root) clicks every control at 390 dark and 920 light with reduced motion, compares the page's numbers with `numbers.json` (196 checks) and writes screenshots to `../.shots/check/`.
- `inputs/la_gpt2.py` made `inputs/gpt2_la.json` offline (`uv run --no-project --with torch --with transformers --with pyarrow`, 2 threads, about 7 min; log in `inputs/la_gpt2.log`).

## Coverage
`live.md` is the old page (copy of the root's saved fetch); `coverage.json` (made by `mk_coverage.py`) maps its 66 facts; the matrix-calculus facts are in `handoff_matrix_calculus_and_backprop.md`.

## Departures from the child method
The Reading tab is longer than most children (about 45 min) because it rebuilds linear algebra from zero for a rusty reader and every other maths child is written in its notation. No "what is in production now" section as such: the production content is section 14 (LoRA, MLA, GaLore, Muon) measured on real weights. Page size is about 520 KB, over the usual 300 KB: about 95 KB is the real GPT-2 spectra and error curves, and most of the rest is MathML for 940 formulas.
