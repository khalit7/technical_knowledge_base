One training loop, every combination of PyTorch threads and DataLoader workers, under three CPU quotas. measured

The loop (src/exp/grid.py): a Dataset whose every sample costs real CPU (four FFT round trips over 8,192 floats, standing in for decode and augmentation), batches of 64, and a "model step" of six pairs of 64 × 1024 by 1024 × 1024 matmuls on torch.set_num_threads(T) threads (there is no GPU here, so the step runs on the CPU too). For T in 1, 2, 4, 5 (5 is PyTorch's default on this VM) and W in 0, 1, 2, 4 workers, 3 warm-up steps then 20 timed steps, inside containers started with --cpus 1, 2 and 4; the whole grid was run three times. Try: predict the best cell for each quota before you look; then switch the metric to throttling and to involuntary switches.

Under a 1-CPU quota, which configuration gave the fastest steps?

What the grid says

Each cell: the median over the three repeats of that run's median step time (or of the chosen metric). Spread: the detail panel shows every repeat. The VM's 1-minute load at the start of each repeat: (other agents' experiments ran at the same time). "Periods throttled" is nr_throttled / nr_periods from cpu.stat over the 20 timed steps; "waiting for data" is the share of step time the main loop spent in next(iterator).
