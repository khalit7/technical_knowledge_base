Best resources

{{OSTEP, the concurrency piece (ch. 26 to 33)|https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/}}

Arpaci-Dusseau and Arpaci-Dusseau; free. The spine of sections 1 to 6. about 5 hours

{{Futexes Are Tricky|https://www.akkadia.org/drepper/futex.pdf}}

Ulrich Drepper, 2011: three mutex designs on futexes, two of them broken, and why. The source of section 4's protocol. 45 minutes

{{futex(2)|https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man2/futex.2.html}}, {{epoll(7)|https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man7/epoll.7.html}}, {{io_uring(7)|https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man7/io_uring.7.html}}

The interfaces, with the edge-triggered pitfalls spelled out in epoll(7). 20 minutes each

{{Efficient IO with io_uring|https://kernel.dk/io_uring.pdf}}

Jens Axboe, 2019: why io_uring exists and how the rings work. 40 minutes

{{Is Parallel Programming Hard, And, If So, What Can You Do About It?|https://mirrors.edge.kernel.org/pub/linux/kernel/people/paulmck/perfbook/perfbook.html}}

Paul McKenney: locking, memory barriers and RCU from the Linux kernel's point of view; the pro-level follow-up. a book; chapters of 1 to 2 hours

{{The Little Book of Semaphores|https://greenteapress.com/wp/semaphores/}}

Allen Downey: synchronisation puzzles with semaphores; good practice for interviews. puzzles, 15 minutes each

{{Understanding the Python GIL|https://www.dabeaz.com/python/UnderstandingGIL.pdf}}

David Beazley, 2010: the GIL's switching and the convoy effect, measured. 30 minutes

{{Learning from Mistakes|https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1346281.1346323}}

Lu, Park, Seo and Zhou, ASPLOS 2008: 105 real concurrency bugs classified. 40 minutes

{{Learnings from kCTF VRP's 42 Linux kernel exploits|https://security.googleblog.com/2023/06/learnings-from-kctf-vrps-42-linux.html}}

Why io_uring is disabled in many production environments. 10 minutes
