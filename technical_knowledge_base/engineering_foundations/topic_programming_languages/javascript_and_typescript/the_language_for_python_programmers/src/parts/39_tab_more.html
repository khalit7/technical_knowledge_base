The language page and its siblings

  • {{JavaScript and TypeScript: the essentials|n:3cd5c17b0d0d81dbac7be3b8686be023}}: running JavaScript, Node essentials (files, processes, fetch, worker threads), packages, Bun and Deno, and the traps that cut across the pages.
  • {{JavaScript: the event loop, promises and streams|n:4055c17b0d0d81fe857de8b34db109ea}}: what this page leaves out on purpose: callbacks, promises, await, the two queues, async iterators and streamed LLM tokens.
  • {{TypeScript: the type system, narrowing and runtime validation|n:4055c17b0d0d81519278df19753ab5ec}}: types on top of these values, erased before they run; publishing a package with types.
  • {{TypeScript: LLM apps and agents|n:4055c17b0d0d8109aad6e69a87b3bd6f}}: what most of this is for.
  • {{Topic: programming-languages|n:3c65c17b0d0d81a2bb1ac0bc8aeb6aa9}}: Design space (JavaScript false friends), Rosetta (the running program in four languages), Benchmark (Node and Bun on the token counter), Toolchain atlas (release dates), and How code runs (V8 against CPython and compilers).

Neighbouring pages

  • {{Python: the data model and protocols|n:4055c17b0d0d816a8705e4d3f20b3da7}}: attribute lookup, the MRO and dunder methods, the Python side of sections 4 and 7.
  • {{Python: CPython internals and performance|n:4055c17b0d0d8111872ad7101b7e3c22}}: CPython's interpreter, reference counting and garbage collector, the Python side of section 8.

Outside sources

  • {{MDN JavaScript|https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript}}: the reference. about 1 h for the guide, then lookup
  • {{javascript.info|https://javascript.info/}}: the best free structured course. about 25 h in full, 15 min per chapter
  • {{You Don't Know JS Yet|https://github.com/getify/You-Dont-Know-JS}} (Kyle Simpson), books 1 and 2 on scope, closures and objects. about 8 h
  • {{Eloquent JavaScript|https://eloquentjavascript.net/}} (Marijn Haverbeke), with exercises. about 10 h
  • {{V8 blog: Maglev|https://v8.dev/blog/maglev}}: the tiers and why they exist. 20 min
  • {{Node.js docs: Modules, ECMAScript modules|https://nodejs.org/api/esm.html}}: the rules of section 5 in full. 30 min
  • {{ECMAScript specification|https://tc39.es/ecma262/}} (look things up, do not read through) and {{TC39 proposals|https://github.com/tc39/proposals}} (where the language is going). 30 min to browse
