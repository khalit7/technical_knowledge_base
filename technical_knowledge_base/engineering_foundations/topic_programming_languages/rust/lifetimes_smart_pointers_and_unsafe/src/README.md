- **`ra/gen.py`** also writes `parts/22_js_lsp_data.js` (`window.LSP_DATA`): line numbers read from the sources (life_err2.rs, life.rs, smart.rs), loop counts from threads.rs (8 threads x 100,000), the vectors and indexes of unsafe_ok.rs and ub/src/main.rs, and every printed value the stages draw, read from `ra/out/`. It asserts that the line numbers agree with the four points of rustc's E0597, that 8 x 100,000 is the printed 800000, that the printed `second` is `vals[idx]`, and that Miri's "size 12 bytes" and "+0xc" agree with three u32s and `p.add(3)`. A new directive `[[x:PATH]]` prints a value of that data in the step lists, so the prose and the stage cannot drift apart. Nothing was rerun: every value is from the 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-26 recordings.
- **Four 3D stages**, each with an `ol.sx` step list on the left:
  `31_js_sc_life.js` (section 1, 6 steps): the lines of `main` along x; each value a slab from its declaration to the end of its scope, an inner block's value stacked on the outer one, the borrow `w` on top of what it borrows; the glass plate is how far `'a` may reach; the use after the block overhangs with nothing under it (E0597, its three points on the line ruler); the last step morphs into life.rs, where `w` rests on `b` along its whole length. Replaces the 2D before/after toggle. The stacking is a picture of "must fit inside", not of memory, and the page says so.
  `32_js_sc_rc.js` (section 2, 7 steps): handles on a stack plate, blocks on the heap floor (the C++ ownership page's layout): the Box tree of `e`, then `Rc::new` and its count tower rising 1, 2, 3 and falling to 2 (3 and 2 printed), the `Cell` change, then `Rc<RefCell<Vec>>` with its borrow flag and the panic.   `33_js_sc_thr.js` (section 3, 8 steps): the main thread in front, threads on an arc behind it: `spawn` + `move`, `thread::scope`, the Rc stopped at a glass wall (E0277), `Arc<Mutex<u64>>` with its strong count of 9 (from the code, labelled not printed), one guard holder at a time, the joined result, the atomic and the channel, every result printed by threads.rs. Replaces the toolbox table (its rows are the step list).
  `34_js_sc_ub.js` (section 4, 6 steps): the stack plate behind an `unsafe { }` glass gate, the heap in front: making `p` (dashed, nothing read), `*p.add(1)` through the gate, `split_at_mut(2)` on the stack array, `*p.add(3)` landing past the 12-byte allocation (the release build reads 0), Miri's wall at the same byte, and the use after free that Miri never reached.
