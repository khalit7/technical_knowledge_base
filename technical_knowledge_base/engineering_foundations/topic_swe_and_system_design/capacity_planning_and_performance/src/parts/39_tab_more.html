Primary sources, in the order to read them

Dean and Barroso, "The Tail at Scale" (CACM, 2013)

Fan-out, hedged and tied requests, with Google's measurements. {{PDF|https://www.barroso.org/publications/TheTailAtScale.pdf}}. About 25 min.

Gil Tene, "How NOT to Measure Latency" (Strange Loop, 2015)

Coordinated omission, percentiles, HdrHistogram, the talk that changed load-testing tools. {{Video|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lJ8ydIuPFeU}}. About 45 min.

Schroeder, Wierman and Harchol-Balter, "Open Versus Closed: A Cautionary Tale" (NSDI 2006)

Eight principles on workload models, measured. {{PDF|https://www.usenix.org/legacy/event/nsdi06/tech/full_papers/schroeder/schroeder.pdf}}. About 45 min.

Mitzenmacher, "The Power of Two Choices in Randomized Load Balancing" (IEEE TPDS, 2001)

The supermarket model; read sections 1 and 4 first. {{PDF|https://www.eecs.harvard.edu/~michaelm/postscripts/tpds2001.pdf}}. About 40 min for the parts that matter.

Nishtala et al., "Scaling Memcache at Facebook" (NSDI 2013)

Look-aside caching at scale: leases, Gutter, regions. {{PDF|https://www.usenix.org/system/files/conference/nsdi13/nsdi13-final170_update.pdf}}. About 50 min.

Vattani, Chierichetti and Lowenstein, "Optimal Probabilistic Cache Stampede Prevention" (VLDB 2015)

XFetch and why it is optimal; sections 1 to 3 are enough. {{PDF|https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~avattani/papers/cache_stampede.pdf}}. About 25 min.

Brinkley and Chhabra, "Caching challenges and strategies" (Amazon Builders' Library)

Side caches, cold starts, request coalescing, from Amazon's practice. {{Article|https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/caching-challenges-and-strategies/}}. About 25 min.

Google SRE book, ch. 20 and 21

"Load Balancing in the Datacenter" and "Handling Overload": weighted round robin, subsetting, client-side throttling. {{Ch. 20|https://sre.google/sre-book/load-balancing-datacenter/}}, {{ch. 21|https://sre.google/sre-book/handling-overload/}}. About 1 h.

Einziger, Friedman and Manes, "TinyLFU" (ACM ToS, 2017)

The admission policy behind Caffeine. {{arXiv|https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00727}}. About 40 min.

Brendan Gregg: flame graphs and the USE method

{{Flame graphs|https://www.brendangregg.com/flamegraphs.html}}, {{USE method|https://www.brendangregg.com/usemethod.html}}. About 30 min.

HikariCP, "About Pool Sizing", and the PgBouncer configuration reference

{{Pool sizing|https://github.com/brettwooldridge/HikariCP/wiki/About-Pool-Sizing}}, {{PgBouncer|https://www.pgbouncer.org/config.html}}. About 25 min.

k6 open and closed models; wrk2 README

{{k6|https://grafana.com/docs/k6/latest/using-k6/scenarios/concepts/open-vs-closed/}}, {{wrk2|https://github.com/giltene/wrk2}}. About 20 min.

Python free-threading how-to (3.14) and PEP 779

{{How-to|https://docs.python.org/3/howto/free-threading-python.html}}, {{PEP 779|https://peps.python.org/pep-0779/}}. About 20 min.

Little, "Little's Law as Viewed on Its 50th Anniversary" (2011)

{{PDF|https://people.cs.umass.edu/~emery/classes/cmpsci691st/readings/OS/Littles-Law-50-Years-Later.pdf}}. About 30 min.

Harchol-Balter, Performance Modeling and Design of Computer Systems (Cambridge, 2013)

The textbook for everything in the queueing section, with proofs. Weeks; dip in by chapter.
