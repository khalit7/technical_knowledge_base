Best resources

{{AI Engineering (Chip Huyen, O'Reilly 2025)|https://huyenchip.com/books/}}

The best single book on building products on foundation models; chapter 10 (architecture: context, guardrails, router and gateway, caches, agents, monitoring, feedback) is this page's subject. About 13 h for the book, 1 h 30 for chapter 10.

{{Building A Generative AI Platform (Huyen, 2024)|https://huyenchip.com/2024/07/25/genai-platform.html}}

The free blog post the chapter grew from. About 40 min.

{{Machine Learning Systems Design booklet (Huyen)|https://huyenchip.com/machine-learning-systems-design/toc.html}}

Free; the classic ML interview framing (project setup, data pipeline, modelling, serving). About 1 h 30.

{{Machine Learning System Design Interview (Aminian and Xu)|https://bytebytego.com/intro/machine-learning-system-design-interview}}

Worked ML interview cases in a standard structure; pair with Huyen. About 7 h 30.

{{GKE: Best practices for autoscaling LLM inference|https://cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/docs/best-practices/machine-learning/inference/autoscaling}}

Why queue size and batch size beat GPU and CPU signals, with thresholds to start from. About 20 min.

{{Amazon Builders' Library: Timeouts, retries and backoff with jitter|https://builder.aws.com/content/3EumjoZascWd1oZiEgL8ORlv3qE/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter}}

The retry discipline every gateway needs. About 25 min.

{{Google SRE book, ch. 21 Handling Overload and ch. 22 Cascading Failures|https://sre.google/sre-book/handling-overload/}}

Criticality, load shedding, degraded responses, client-side throttling; ch. 4 on SLOs alongside. About 1 h for the two chapters.

{{vLLM production stack|https://github.com/vllm-project/production-stack}} and {{llm-d|https://github.com/llm-d/llm-d}} docs

What the open-source serving and routing layer looks like in 2026: routers, autoscaling recipes, Helm charts. About 1 h for the core pages.

{{LiteLLM docs: routing, reliability, budgets|https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/routing}}

A real gateway's configuration surface, readable in an afternoon. About 45 min.

{{Anthropic: A postmortem of three recent issues|https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/a-postmortem-of-three-recent-issues}}

How serving bugs degrade quality without errors, and what monitoring caught them. About 15 min.

{{Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems (Sculley et al., 2015)|https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/hash/86df7dcfd896fcaf2674f757a2463eba-Abstract.html}}

Why ML systems are mostly glue, and where they rot. About 40 min.

{{Rules of Machine Learning (Zinkevich)|https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/guides/rules-of-ml}}

43 rules from Google, including training-serving skew. About 1 h.
