{{Design drill|#t-drill}}

Twelve prompts (the seven worked designs plus a notification system, typeahead, a web crawler, ticket booking and a key-value store), a 45-minute clock, the method revealed step by step with self-check lists. 45 min a drill.

The parent topic and its children (each technique used here is taught there)

{{Topic: swe-and-system-design|n:3c65c17b0d0d81048ae0dd14ac399b4d}}

The root: one LLM chat product from 1 user to 10 million; Step 6 is the method this page applies; Scale simulator, Numbers to know (the constants used in every estimate here) and Case files (Discord, Slack, Twitter, Instagram, Stripe, cited here). About 25 min.

{{Building a backend API|n:3c65c17b0d0d8190ade6c34e8f653e79}}

HTTP, errors, cursors, idempotency keys, SSE, versioning: every API sketch on this page. About 45 min.

{{Distributed systems fundamentals|n:3c65c17b0d0d8115bc45c31d915b4d33}}

Replication, partitioning, consistent hashing, quorums, leases and fencing: the storage and scheduler deep dives. About 45 min.

{{Queues, streams and async work|n:3ef5c17b0d0d81aebf91df927f215b6e}}

At-least-once delivery, idempotent consumers, backpressure, scheduling and cron: clicks, fan-out, ingestion and job runs. About 50 min.

{{Reliability engineering|n:3ef5c17b0d0d8152b8dececf34196174}}

Timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, rate-limiting algorithms (animated), fail-open, safe deploys: every failure-modes table. About 45 min.

{{Observability|n:3ef5c17b0d0d811e8490c54dd42c2b9b}}

How you would know any of these designs works in production. About 45 min.

{{Capacity planning and performance|n:3ef5c17b0d0d81458291d55d7443700c}}

Little's law, caching strategy, load balancing and load testing behind the estimates. About 50 min.

{{ML system design|n:3c65c17b0d0d8117b057dc2586836ec8}}

The depth behind design 5: gateway duties, routing, token limits, caching, autoscaling GPUs, its Gateway lab and section 14 (an ML interview run with the method). About 50 min.

{{Testing and quality|n:3c65c17b0d0d81548848e2ebdd2f63ee}}

Unit, property-based, data and model tests and CI. About 45 min.

{{Code design|n:3ef5c17b0d0d817b99a0ff9701ff30cd}}

Inside one of these boxes: readable code, abstractions, review. About 40 min.

The primary sources behind the seven designs

{{Stripe: Scaling your API with rate limiters (2017)|https://stripe.com/blog/rate-limiters}}

Four limiters, token buckets in Redis, fail open, dark launches. 15 min.

{{Cloudflare: Counting things, a lot of different things (2017)|https://blog.cloudflare.com/counting-things-a-lot-of-different-things/}}

The sliding window counter at Cloudflare scale, with its measured error. 15 min.

{{GitHub: a sharded, replicated rate limiter in Redis (2021)|https://github.blog/engineering/infrastructure/how-we-scaled-github-api-sharded-replicated-rate-limiter-redis/}}

Lua for atomicity and the replica expiry bug. 10 min.

{{Discord: How Discord stores billions of messages (2017)|https://discord.com/blog/how-discord-stores-billions-of-messages}}

Choosing Cassandra and the (channel, bucket) key. Then {{the trillions sequel (2023)|https://discord.com/blog/how-discord-stores-trillions-of-messages}}. 20 min each.

{{Slack: Real-time messaging (2023)|https://slack.engineering/real-time-messaging/}}

Gateway, channel, admin and presence servers on a hash ring. 15 min.

{{Timelines at Scale (Krikorian, QCon 2012)|https://www.infoq.com/presentations/Twitter-Timeline-Scalability/}}

The talk behind design 4; {{High Scalability's notes|http://highscalability.com/blog/2013/7/8/the-architecture-twitter-uses-to-deal-with-150m-active-users.html}} are the readable summary. 50 min video, 20 min notes.

{{Uber: GenAI Gateway (2024)|https://www.uber.com/blog/genai-gateway/}}

An LLM gateway at a large company, with its numbers. 15 min.

{{Anthropic: Introducing Contextual Retrieval (2024)|https://www.anthropic.com/news/contextual-retrieval}}

Hybrid retrieval and reranking measured. 15 min.

{{Google SRE book: Distributed Periodic Scheduling with Cron|https://sre.google/sre-book/distributed-periodic-scheduling/}}

Paxos-backed cron and the skip-or-double-launch decision. 25 min.

{{Dropbox: ATF, asynchronous task scheduling (2020)|https://dropbox.tech/infrastructure/asynchronous-task-scheduling-at-dropbox}}

A real scheduler's components and guarantees. 20 min.

{{Bitly: lessons from 6 billion clicks a month (High Scalability, 2014)|http://web.archive.org/web/2015/http://highscalability.com/blog/2014/7/14/bitly-lessons-learned-building-a-distributed-system-that-han.html}}

A shortener's real architecture, summarised from a talk. 15 min.

{{Netflix: RENO, rapid event notifications (2022)|https://netflixtechblog.com/rapid-event-notification-system-at-netflix-6deb1d2b57d1}}

The reference for the notification prompt in the drill. 15 min.
