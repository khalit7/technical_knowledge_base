Design drill

Practise the method under a clock. Pick a prompt, start the timer, and for each step say (or write) your answer before revealing the model answer. Then tick what your answer covered. Seven prompts are the worked designs of the Reading tab (the reveal is their summary, with a link back); five are new, with a reference answer and the sibling sections to read afterwards. Your ticks are saved in this browser only, if it allows storage.

How to score yourself

Most strong answers tick about two thirds of the items; the point is to see which kind you miss repeatedly (numbers, failure modes, trade-offs said aloud) and practise that. The traps are listed in the Reading's {{How to practise|#t-read}} section.
