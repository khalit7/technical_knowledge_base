Best resources

{{pytest documentation|https://docs.pytest.org/}}

Fixtures, parametrize, markers: the substrate everything here runs on. 1 h for the how-to pages

{{Hypothesis documentation|https://hypothesis.readthedocs.io/en/latest/}}

Quick start, strategies, stateful testing, the numpy and pandas extras. 45 min

{{Software Engineering at Google, ch. 11 to 14|https://abseil.io/resources/swe-book/html/ch11.html}}

Testing overview, unit testing, test doubles, larger tests; free online. 2 h

{{The Practical Test Pyramid|https://martinfowler.com/articles/practical-test-pyramid.html}}

Ham Vocke on martinfowler.com: every layer with real code. 45 min

{{Mocks Aren't Stubs|https://martinfowler.com/articles/mocksArentStubs.html}}

Martin Fowler; the classic on test doubles and the two schools of TDD. 30 min

{{How to Test Machine Learning Code and Systems|https://eugeneyan.com/writing/testing-ml/}}

Eugene Yan (2020); pre-train and post-train tests with worked code. 30 min

{{Made With ML: Testing|https://madewithml.com/courses/mlops/testing/}}

Goku Mohandas; code, data and model tests with pytest. Its Great Expectations code uses the old 0.x API. 40 min

{{The ML Test Score|https://research.google/pubs/the-ml-test-score-a-rubric-for-ml-production-readiness-and-technical-debt-reduction/}}

Breck et al., Google (2017): 28 tests and monitoring needs; still the best checklist. 45 min

{{Practical Mutation Testing at Scale|https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11378}}

Petrović et al.: mutants in code review at Google. 40 min

{{An Empirical Analysis of Flaky Tests|https://mir.cs.illinois.edu/marinov/publications/LuoETAL14FlakyTestsAnalysis.pdf}}

Luo et al., FSE 2014: the causes, with fixes. 40 min

{{Coverage Is Not Strongly Correlated with Test Suite Effectiveness|https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rtholmes/papers/icse_2014_inozemtseva.pdf}}

Inozemtseva and Holmes, ICSE 2014. 30 min

{{Beyond Accuracy: CheckList|https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442.pdf}}

Ribeiro et al., ACL 2020: behavioural testing of models. 40 min

{{Refactoring Hermes with 1,393 agents|https://nousresearch.com/refactoring-hermes-with-1393-agents}}

Nous Research: what a green suite missed in a million-line refactor. 20 min

{{mutmut|https://github.com/boxed/mutmut}} and {{coverage.py|https://coverage.readthedocs.io/}}

The two tools behind the Mutation lab. 20 min
