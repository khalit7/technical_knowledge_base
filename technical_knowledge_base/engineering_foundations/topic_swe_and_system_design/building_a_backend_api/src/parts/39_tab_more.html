When to write async def and when plain def, from the framework's author. About 25 min.

Workers, containers, HTTPS, running behind a proxy. About 1 h.

Models, fields, validators and pydantic-settings: the contract layer of Python services. About 1 h 30 min for the core pages.

Config, backing services, processes and disposability (fast startup, graceful shutdown) in twelve short pages. About 30 min.

SIGTERM, the grace period, preStop and how endpoints change during a stop. About 30 min for the termination part.

{{OWASP API Security Top 10 (2023)|https://owasp.org/API-Security/editions/2023/en/0x11-t10/}}

The ten ways APIs get broken into; API1, object-level authorisation, is the one a handler fixes. About 45 min.

{{Alexis King: Parse, don't validate|https://lexi-lambda.github.io/blog/2019/11/05/parse-don-t-validate/}}

Why validation should produce types. Examples in Haskell; the idea is language-free. About 25 min.

A whole handler designed around transactions, atomic phases and recovery points; the contract side is on Service APIs. About 40 min.
