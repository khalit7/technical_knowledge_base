Primary sources, in the order to read them

{{Timeouts, retries, and backoff with jitter (Brooker, Builders' Library)|https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter/}}

The single best short essay on this page's core. Archived copy (the new site renders text by script). About 15 min.

{{Exponential Backoff And Jitter (Brooker, 2015)|https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/architecture/exponential-backoff-and-jitter/}}

The simulation behind full jitter. About 10 min.

{{Metastable Failures in Distributed Systems (HotOS 2021)|https://sigops.org/s/conferences/hotos/2021/papers/hotos21-s11-bronson.pdf}}

Six pages that name the failure mode behind most long outages. About 25 min.

{{Metastable Failures in the Wild (OSDI 2022)|https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi22-huang-lexiang.pdf}}

22 real incidents dissected; retries sustain more than half. About 1 h.

{{Using load shedding to avoid overload (Builders' Library)|https://builder.aws.com/content/3Eun1EEyX6p2e3VYNyRLSJzLuMV/using-load-shedding-to-avoid-overload}}

Goodput, cheap rejection, priorities. About 20 min.

{{Implementing health checks (Builders' Library)|https://builder.aws.com/content/3Ev53O39izHCtWLzp4XU6t8PC1O/implementing-health-checks}}

Liveness, local and dependency checks; failing open. About 20 min.

{{Automating safe, hands-off deployments (Liguori, Builders' Library)|https://builder.aws.com/content/3ErTKQOTKc5NIw031UePBPxTQ6I/automating-safe-hands-off-deployments}}

One-box, waves, bake time, automatic rollback, as AWS does it. About 25 min.

{{Google SRE book: ch. 3, 4, 14, 15, 21, 22|https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/}}

Embracing risk, SLOs, managing incidents, postmortem culture, handling overload, cascading failures. About 3 h for these six.

{{Google SRE workbook: ch. 2, 5, 9 and the error budget policy|https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/}}

Implementing SLOs, alerting on SLOs (the burn-rate tables), incident response. About 2 h.

{{The Calculus of Service Availability (ACM Queue 2017, archived)|https://web.archive.org/web/20231218074029/https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3096459}}

Why dependencies need an extra nine, and why detection time dominates. About 25 min.

{{The Tail at Scale (Dean and Barroso, 2013)|https://www.barroso.org/publications/TheTailAtScale.pdf}}

Fan-out, tail latency and hedged requests. About 30 min.

{{Scaling your API with rate limiters (Stripe, 2017)|https://stripe.com/blog/rate-limiters}}

Four limiters in production, with code. About 15 min.

{{Counting things, a lot of different things (Cloudflare, 2017)|https://blog.cloudflare.com/counting-things-a-lot-of-different-things/}}

The sliding window counter and its measured error. About 10 min.

{{gRFC A6: gRPC client retries|https://github.com/grpc/proposal/blob/master/A6-client-retries.md}}

A complete, precise retry design: policy, throttling, hedging, commit points. About 30 min.

{{PagerDuty Incident Response|https://response.pagerduty.com/}}

Free, practical handbook: severities, roles, what to do during and after. About 1 h.

{{Blameless PostMortems and a Just Culture (Allspaw, 2012, archived)|https://web.archive.org/web/20231230191519/https://www.etsy.com/codeascraft/blameless-postmortems/}}

Why blame makes systems less safe. About 10 min.

{{Fail at Scale (Maurer, Facebook, ACM Queue 2015)|https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=2839461}}

Controlled delay and adaptive LIFO queues under overload. Not checked for this page (the site refused access). About 25 min.

Release It!, 2nd ed. (Nygard, Pragmatic, 2018)

The book that named circuit breakers and bulkheads; its stability patterns chapter is the classic. {{Publisher page|https://pragprog.com/titles/mnee2/release-it-second-edition/}}. About 10 h.
