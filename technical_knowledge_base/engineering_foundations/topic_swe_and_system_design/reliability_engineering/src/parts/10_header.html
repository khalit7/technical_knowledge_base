Reliability engineering: timeouts, retries, circuit breakers, SLOs, incidents

How a service keeps working when the things it depends on do not, taught from zero: what "reliable" means and costs, how systems fail, the defences (timeouts, retries, idempotency, circuit breakers, bulkheads, load shedding, rate limits, health checks, safe deploys), how much failure to allow (SLOs and error budgets), and what to do when it happens anyway (incidents and postmortems).
