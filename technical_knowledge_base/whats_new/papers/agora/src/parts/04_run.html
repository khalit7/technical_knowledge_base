Rebuild the winning prior

Nothing from the run is released, so this tab rebuilds the part of the agents' recipe that did almost all the work, Stage A ({{§4.4|ax:S4.SS4}}), from the paper's description, and measures it with the paper's own metric. We asked GPT-2 small for its next-token distribution after every one of the 50,257 tokens, under the paper's 28 contexts (and, separately, with no context), averaged the clipped log-probabilities into a 50,257 × 50,257 table, split off the column mean as the unigram anchor u, factorised the rest by randomized SVD exactly as Algorithm 1 says (rank 671, oversampling 32, one power iteration, fixed seed), and scored the resulting model on 200 FineWeb-Edu texts in 512-token chunks, in bits per byte.

Same as the paper, and not
Same: the main donor (GPT-2 small carries weight 0.725 in the winning blend), the 28 contexts of {{Table 5|ax:A2.T5}}, the ±25 clip, averaging per-context log-softmaxes, the unigram anchor, the SVD settings, the temperatures Tb = 0.9314 and Tu = 1.00459, the corpus, chunking and metric. Different: one donor instead of six; equal context weights instead of the variance and naturalness row weights; no Stage B; logits computed directly as u/Tu + (rank-r part of C)/Tb, which §4.4 says is approximately what the target network computes; and our 200 texts (rows 0 to 199 of FineWeb-Edu's sample-10BT) are not theirs, which were never pinned ({{§4.8|ax:S4.SS8}}).

A finding of the rebuild: with 28 contexts the rank-671 model (@@R:ours.stage_a_c28.ranks.671.T1:4@@ at T = 1) scores better than the full table it approximates (@@R:ours.stage_a_c28.full_bpb:4@@); with one context the two are the same (@@R:ours.stage_a_c1.ranks.671.T1:4@@ against @@R:ours.stage_a_c1.full_bpb:4@@). So the factorisation is not only a way to fit the table into a 672-wide network: on the context-averaged table, dropping the smallest singular directions smooths it and helps. That is our reading of two measurements, not something the paper reports or we tested further.

Energy captured is the share of the squared Frobenius norm of the centred table C carried by the top r singular values. The full table is scored without SVD, at T = 1 (no temperature split exists without the factorisation).
