Replay the released runs

The authors published every round of four evolution runs on their {{project page|https://regularized-rsi.com/}}: each candidate's summary, the components it edits, the critic's verdict, its measured score and tokens per trial, and the decision with its reason. This tab replays them round by round. Two runs use the released code as the paper describes it (coding with Claude Opus 4.8; agentic workspace on Harvey LAB); the other two are earlier or adapted procedures, as their notes say. Nothing here is simulated: every dot is a recorded evaluation.

Try: watch the coding run play and see the floor (dashed) hold the incumbent near its best while most candidates fall below it. Then switch to "keep the best score" to see, round by round, where an unregularised selector would have chosen differently from the same candidates.

      Vertical axis: the evolve-set count (passes or rubric criteria) of each evaluation. Step line: the incumbent. Dashed: the noise-adjusted floor S* − δ for the next round. "Keep the best score" compares, in each round, the recorded candidates against the recorded incumbent only; the real run would have proposed different candidates afterwards, so no alternative path is drawn.

      The step list is generated from the run chosen: its rounds come in up to seven stretches, each played round by round; the box under the chart lists every candidate of the round on screen, with its summary and the critic's note on its first draft.

      Re-judge the recorded candidates

      Algorithm 2 ported from the released rrsi/selection.py and applied to every recorded round of the two runs that used it, one round at a time against the incumbent the run actually had. At the released settings it reproduces the recorded decisions; move the noise band or the cost weights and see which decisions would have gone the other way.

      The plane plots each measured candidate's score change against the incumbent (horizontal, in passes or criteria) and its relative token change (vertical). Left of the dashed line: below the floor when the incumbent is the run's best (when it is not, the floor sits further left, which the verdicts account for). Between the floor and the band: the shaped rule, Eq. 17. Right of the band: the cost line of Eq. 7. Filled dots are the recorded winners.
