Best resources

Jay Alammar: the classic visual walkthrough of how BERT relates to ELMo and GPT and how fine-tuning works.

(30 min)

Hugging Face: practical intro with code, good for the fine-tuning workflow.

Finally, a Replacement for BERT: Introducing ModernBERT

Answer.AI, December 2024: where the encoder lineage stands today and why it still matters; the paper is arXiv 2412.13663.

(30 min)

Open Sourcing BERT: State-of-the-Art Pre-training for Natural Language Processing

Google AI blog, the authors: concise author framing of the contribution, written for the code release.

(10 min)

RoBERTa: A Robustly Optimized BERT Pretraining Approach

Liu et al., Facebook AI, 2019: the replication that found BERT undertrained, dropped NSP and made masking dynamic; read sections 4 and 5.

(40 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

Attention Is All You Need (Transformer)

2017: BERT is this paper's encoder stack, transplanted to transfer learning; its page runs a toy of the full encoder-decoder.

Seq vs Seq (Ettin)

2025: paired encoders and decoders trained with one recipe (the ModernBERT one); a controlled comparison of the BERT and GPT lineages.

GPT-3

2020: the rival left-to-right lineage scaled; in-context learning replaced per-task heads for generation.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

2020: its dense retriever (DPR) is a pair of BERT encoders; retrieval is the encoder's main living habitat.

RoFormer (RoPE)

2021: rotary positions replaced BERT's learned absolute positions in modern encoders (ModernBERT) as well as decoders.

T5

2019: masked-span denoising carried into an encoder-decoder, every task as text to text.

Bitune

2024: gives a decoder-only LLM bidirectional attention over the prompt, BERT's argument applied inside a decoder.

ViT

2020: the same encoder and a [CLS]-style class token, applied to image patches.
