The idea in one sentence

[[S1|§1]]

RL with verifiable rewards is usually trained with a fixed rollout cap, and that cap does not only save compute: the trained model learns to finish inside it, so it beats the base model at the cap and falls below it once both may think longer ({{abstract and §1|ax:S1}}). The authors call this budget internalization.

Fig. 1 is the whole result. Qwen3.5-4B trained with GRPO under a 4k-token cap scores 35.93% on AIME 2024 and 2025 at a 4k budget, against 1.11% for the base model, which almost never finishes in 4k tokens. Give both 64k tokens and the base model climbs to 78.33% while the trained model stops at 48.15%: +34.8 points at the cap becomes −30.2 ({{§2.1|ax:S2.SS1}}, {{Table 8|ax:A4.T8}}). Every trained curve in the figure goes flat soon after its own cap; pick another model to see the same shape.

You need: GRPO and the RLVR loop (group-standardised advantage, PPO-style clipping, a KL term, rewards from a verifier), owned by {{RL for LLMs|n:3c65c17b0d0d818c9bcff7177325fe56}}; test-time scaling, serial thinking budgets and budget forcing, owned by {{Test-time compute and reasoning|n:4035c17b0d0d813f9fb2d62cff1f09ef}}.

New terms

Rollout cap B
the most tokens a training rollout may generate; a rollout that reaches it without an answer is truncated and, in standard GRPO here, gets reward 0.
Evaluation budget
the generated-token limit at test time; an answer cut off by it scores 0. The paper sweeps it from 512 tokens to 64k.
Budget internalization
above the training cap, the base model keeps using more tokens and gaining accuracy while the trained model's length and accuracy flatten ({{§2.4|ax:S2.SS4}}).
avg@k
accuracy averaged over k sampled answers per problem: avg@3 on the 60 AIME problems for the main curves, avg@16 for the ablations.
Answer forcing, forcing up
two decoding interventions: close the thinking at the budget and demand an answer, or delete the answer, append "Wait" and make the model keep reasoning.
RFT
rejection fine-tuning: supervised fine-tuning on the model's own correct answers.
