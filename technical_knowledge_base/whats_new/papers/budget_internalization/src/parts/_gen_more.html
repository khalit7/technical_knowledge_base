Budget Internalization: Fixed-Budget RLVR Trades Test-Time Scaling for In-Budget Accuracy (arXiv HTML v1)

JetBrains-Research/budget-internalization (Hugging Face collection)

Every trained checkpoint, ablations included, released with the paper under the base models' licences (78 model repositories on 23 November 2026). No training or evaluation code and no per-problem scores are released; the runs used the public NeMo RL framework and Inspect AI.

Best resources

s1: Simple test-time scaling

Muennighoff et al., arXiv 2501.19393: budget forcing, the decoding trick both of this paper's controls use: cut the reasoning and force an answer, or suppress the end of thinking and append "Wait".

(30 min)

Understanding R1-Zero-Like Training: A Critical Perspective (Dr. GRPO)

Liu et al., arXiv 2503.20783: the argument that GRPO's 1/|o| normalisation biases length, and the fix this paper's normalisation ablation runs (it changes nothing here).

(40 min)

DAPO: An Open-Source LLM Reinforcement Learning System at Scale

Yu et al., arXiv 2503.14476: the soft overlong penalty (0 down to −1 over the last tokens before the cap) that this paper tests as a truncation treatment.

(40 min)

L1: Controlling How Long A Reasoning Model Thinks With Reinforcement Learning

Aggarwal and Welleck, arXiv 2503.04697: training a model to follow a length stated in the prompt, the explicit budget conditioning this paper suggests as a way out.

(30 min)

Inverse Scaling in Test-Time Compute

Gema et al., arXiv 2507.14417: tasks where longer reasoning lowers accuracy: the overthinking side that this paper's GPQA and LiveCodeBench results touch.

(35 min)
