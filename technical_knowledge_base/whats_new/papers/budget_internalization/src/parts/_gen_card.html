Budget Internalization: Fixed-Budget RLVR Trades Test-Time Scaling for In-Budget Accuracy

Ilia Zavidnyi, Alexander Kovrigin, Arina Puchkova, Kirill Gelvan, Sergey Titov
JetBrains Research · 19 November 2026 (arXiv v1, the only version as of 23 November 2026)

In one line: On three small models (Qwen3.5-4B, Gemma4-E2B, Nemotron-3-Nano-4B) trained with GRPO on math under a fixed rollout cap (three seeds for the main comparison), AIME accuracy at the cap rose 10.9 to 42.4 points over the base model while accuracy at the largest budget tested fell 13.1 to 30.2 points below it, and none of the tested fixes (single runs on Qwen3.5-4B) kept both the in-budget gain and the base model's test-time scaling.

How far to trust it: Believe the crossover: it appears in all 18 seeded runs on three model families, the tables reproduce every section 2 number, and the collapse in output length (27.3k to 5.0k tokens for Qwen) explains it. Treat the ranking of the fixes as suggestive: each is one run on one model, most of their 64k differences sit inside the printed 95% intervals and the 5-point spread between seeds, two levers of the objective were never varied, and the introduction's headline ranges do not match the paper's own tables. The evidence, judged.
