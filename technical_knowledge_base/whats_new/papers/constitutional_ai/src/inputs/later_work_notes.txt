Sourced notes for the "beyond the paper" claims (fetched 2026-10-02).

https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback
  Anthropic research post, 15 December 2022: official summary of the paper.

https://www.anthropic.com/news/claudes-constitution  (9 May 2023)
  "During the first phase, the model is trained to critique and revise its own responses using the set of principles and a few examples of the process."
  "During the second phase, a model is trained via reinforcement learning, but rather than using human feedback, it uses AI-generated feedback based on the set of principles to choose the more harmless output."
  Sources of principles: UN Declaration of Human Rights, trust and safety best practices, DeepMind's Sparrow Principles, Apple's Terms of Service, non-western perspectives, Anthropic's own research.
  "Since launching Claude, our AI assistant trained with Constitutional AI, ..."
  Update note: "Update, Jan 21, 2026: We've published a new version of Claude's constitution".

https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-new-constitution  (dated 22 January 2026 on the page)
  Explanation over rules: "AI models like Claude need to understand why we want them to behave in certain ways."
  Released under CC0 1.0. Used at various stages of training, to generate synthetic training data and response rankings.
  Priorities in order: broadly safe, broadly ethical, compliant with Anthropic's guidelines, genuinely helpful.

https://www.anthropic.com/news/collective-constitutional-ai-aligning-a-language-model-with-public-input  (17 October 2023)
  About 1,000 American adults on Polis; 1,127 statements, 38,252 votes. Two Claude Instant-sized models trained, public vs standard constitution:
  "People interacting with the models found the Public model as helpful and harmless as both the Standard model and Claude Instant 1.2."
  "The Public model shows lower bias scores across all nine social dimensions than the Standard model".

https://huggingface.co/blog/constitutional_ai  (1 February 2024; Huang, Tunstall, Beeching, von Werra, Sanseviero, Rasul, Wolf)
  Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.1 as the helpful model; Anthropic's constitution; dataset HuggingFaceH4/cai-conversation-harmless built from hh-rlhf harmless-base; SFT then DPO.
  Models HuggingFaceH4/mistral-7b-anthropic (DPO) and mistral-7b-grok.
https://github.com/huggingface/alignment-handbook/tree/main/recipes/constitutional-ai  (Apache-2.0)
  "You will require 8 GPUs (80GB of VRAM) to train the full model." Step 1 SFT, Step 2 DPO. Dataset generation with llm-swarm.
