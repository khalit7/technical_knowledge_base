## Departures from `.claude/skills/explainer-page/page-kinds/paper.md`

- **No Then and now tab**: the report is two months old; its lineage (DDPM, BERT, T5, block diffusion) is linked instead.
- **No Connections section in the Reading tab**: the connections are the Further reading cards (same text as the old page's Connections list), to keep the Reading tab shorter.
- **The live ingredient is the paper's sampler, not its quality**: an architecture-and-recipe paper at 26B scale cannot be reproduced; the toy runs the mechanism the report actually specifies (Algorithm 1, block-AR with a shared-weights encoder and decoder, self-conditioning, Eq. 13's losses) and tests the claims that make sense at toy scale (adaptive steps on sequential against parallel tasks, Appendix G.2; revision under multinomial against masked noise; dual mode). SD·RL is not built, because the report gives no objective to implement.
- **The AR-against-diffusion pass-count animation is not rebuilt**: it is on the 2026-08-24 tech news page and linked.
- **A third verdict, "context", in the checks table**, for recomputed numbers that frame a claim without testing a printed value.
- **Reading length**: about 21 minutes against the old page's 13 (the page now owns every detail of the report, including the evidence judgement and the toy's results).

- Toy: `train.py` (`train multinomial|masked`, `eval` writes `model/results.json`, `export` writes `parts/20_model_data.js` and `model/*_q.pt`), `check_forward.py` (JS forward pass against PyTorch, `model/check_forward.json`), `mk_toydata.py` (`parts/_gen_toydata.js`: results, logs, the Reading tab's demo problem picked by running the page's own JS in node, the findings text). `model/` keeps the float and quantised checkpoints, logs and JSON results; `model/old/` keeps the logs of the three abandoned first designs (a numeric rule table and learned absolute positions: the parallel task stayed at chance for 2,400 steps; then explicit rule-window tokens; then rotary positions, which learned both tasks within 1,200 steps; the 6,000-step run was stopped and retrained at 3,000 steps to save CPU on the shared machine).
  - `uv run --with torch --with numpy python train.py train multinomial` (about 13 minutes at 2 threads on a loaded machine), the same for `masked`, then `... train.py export`, `... train.py eval`, `... check_forward.py`, then `sh build.sh`.
- `check_page.mjs` (from the repo root with node): every control in light 920 and dark 390, the stage playing once in view, the Run widget stepped in each mode, the in-browser test run; fails on text under 11 px, NaN/undefined, errors or sideways scroll. `check_stages.mjs`: the layout and motion rules and the step list's numbers (see the 2026-11-13 section).
- `mk_coverage.py`: `coverage.json`, every fact of `live.md` with where the HTML carries it, verified against the built page, plus the corrections to the old summary.
- `save_live.py` (Notion fetch copied from the session transcript into `live.md`), `extract_paper.py` (arXiv HTML v1 to `inputs/paper_v1.txt` and table extracts), `inputs/hf_model.json` (model card metadata, 3 October 2026), `inputs/vllm_blog.txt`.

## The 2026-11-13 layout rollout (split layout, renderer v2 stage, autoplay)

What changed and why:
- **Split layout** (template `02_split.html`, unchanged). The paper tab: the top (core sentence, You need, New terms) beside the hero; Idea beside Table 1 (moved out of the prose into a side card); Discrete diffusion beside the corruption widget; Block by block and The sampler beside the 3D stage, with its step list, the problem line and counters in the left column; Speed beside the Figure 11 calculator (new `s-speed2` for the experts prediction and the vLLM block, full width); More than one user and Towards real traffic beside Figure 12 (new `s-speed3`); Results' quality paragraphs beside the dot plot (new `s-res2` for the rest, full width). Run the toy: the intro, Try and a new paragraph explaining the widget beside the controls and the decoder card (`r-intro`); the frontier paragraph beside its chart (`r-front`); the training-curve note moved out of the card to the left of it (`r-curves`); the test table and findings full width. Tables and Further reading: tables and lists, full width.
- **One 3D stage** (template `25_js_s3.js`, renderer v2, unchanged): `31_js_sc_samp.js` replaces the Reading tab's 2D step animation (`#dgx`). Data: the page's own toy, run in the browser by `22_js_model.js` on `TOY.demo` (task seq, held-out rule table 96, start 1 0 1; picked by `mk_toydata.py`) with the paper's sampler defaults, seed 1, exposed as `SAMPDATA()`. Across: the 24 answer positions (three canvases of 8); into the depth: forward passes, one row per pass; bar height: the decoder's confidence in its top token; blue kept by the entropy budget, faint re-noised, orange the one revision; teal at the front the KV cache (prompt, then each appended canvas), with flows from the cache into the pass being computed and from each finished canvas into the cache; the last step adds AR mode's 24-pass diagonal (green). Seven steps (`sx-samp`), each stretch of passes explained (the run is 13 passes plus prefill, so passes are grouped: 1, 2 to 4, 5 to 7, the append, canvases 2 and 3, AR). The camera moves in on canvas 1 for steps 2 to 5 and on canvases 2 and 3 for step 6. The masked twin is not a mode of the stage: on this problem (picked partly because the twin fails on it) it gets 10 of 24 bits, while over 512 problems it is about as accurate on seq (74% against 77% exact) and more accurate on conv (100% against 59%), so showing it here would mislead; it stays on Run the toy with the toy's measured findings beside the stage.
- **The Run the toy decoder is a widget** (long-replay rule, case b): one step per pass (15 frames on the default problem, far more with a loose stopping rule) of a run the reader configures, so no autoplay and no step list; `makeAnim` (`11_js_ui.js`) takes `openEnd` and the widget opens on its finished answer, also after a rerun or a mode switch; a new paragraph on its left explains its rows and colours (the legend that sat under the old Reading animation).
- **Kept 2D**: the hero (a loop diagram plus times on an axis), Table 1, the corruption widget (words in a sentence), the Figure 11 calculator (milliseconds on an axis), the experts and answer-length reveals, Figure 12's ratios, the quality dot plot, the frontier and training curves, every table.
- **Motion and templates**: `05z_errbox.js.html` and `99_js_tabs.js` are the templates (storage key `diffusiongemma-tab`; a `data-to` link opens the closed block holding its target, as DeepSeekMath's). `check_page.mjs` now asserts that the stage plays when scrolled into view and that the Run widget never moves before a click; `check_stages.mjs` (after DeepSeekMath's) checks splits, the stage's autoplay, step count, off-screen pause, stop on a control and reduced motion at 1400 light, 920 light and 390 dark, the widget's rules, and that every number in the step list (the noise tokens, 5 kept, 7, 7, 8, the revision at pass 5, position 7, 0.57, 7 passes, 7, 2, 2, 13 passes, 24 / 13 = 1.85, AR 24 of 24) matches `SAMPDATA()`.
- The Figure 11 calculator's axis now runs to 15.6 ms so the 12.63 ms total no longer runs into the legend in the narrower column.
