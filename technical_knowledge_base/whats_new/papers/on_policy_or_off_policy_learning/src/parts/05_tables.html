The paper's figures, rebuilt

The paper's results are figures, not tables, so the numbers here are read from figure: decoded from the vector SVGs in the arXiv HTML (every bar, point and error cap is a path with exact coordinates, and the axis ticks carry their characters), with the axis fitted on the printed tick labels (residuals under 10−4 of a unit). Decoded values match every value label the figures print. The script is src/decode_figs.py; the claims below are checked by src/recompute.py. Printed tables (2, 6) and in-text lists (Appendix A.12) are transcribed at their printed precision.
