Best resources

On-policy distillation

Thinking Machines (Lu and Thinking Machines Lab), 2025: The recipe that made on-policy distillation popular: per-token reverse KL from one sampled token, used as an RL advantage. The paper's sampled-KL ablation (Appendix A.2) tests this estimator.

(25 min)

On-Policy Distillation of Language Models (GKD), arXiv 2306.13649

Agarwal et al., Google DeepMind, 2023: Where student-generated rollouts for distillation, and the idea of choosing the divergence separately, come from.

(40 min)

When Do We Need On-Policy Distillation? (Semi-OPD), arXiv 2610.11291

Zhao et al., NVIDIA, 8 October 2026: Rollouts the initial student wrote once, reused, beat full on-policy distillation in 14 of 17 teacher-student pairs; on-policy helps only when the pair is well aligned. Same direction as this paper, from a different angle.

(40 min)

Gains and Collapse in On-Policy Distillation, arXiv 2610.03185

Cui et al., 2 October 2026: On-policy distillation read as RL against the teacher's implicit reward: it makes correct answers easier to sample, and collapses into long repetitive text when that reward is wrong.

(30 min)

The Teacher Is a Direction, Not a Destination (RIDE), arXiv 2609.36484

Li et al., 29 September 2026: Distils in representation space along the RL-induced shift of the teacher instead of matching its next-token distribution.

(30 min)

Reinforcement Learning Finetunes Small Subnetworks in Large Language Models, arXiv 2505.11711

Mukherjee et al., NeurIPS 2025: The sparse-update claim this paper tests, and whose checkpoints' learning rates its Appendix E lists.

(30 min)

RL's Razor: Why Online Reinforcement Learning Forgets Less, arXiv 2509.04259

Shenfeld, Pari and Agrawal, 2025: The forgetting claim this paper tests: on-policy training forgets less than SFT.

(30 min)

SFT Memorizes, RL Generalizes, arXiv 2501.17161

Chu et al., 2025: The generalisation claim this paper tests, made by comparing SFT with RL.

(35 min)
