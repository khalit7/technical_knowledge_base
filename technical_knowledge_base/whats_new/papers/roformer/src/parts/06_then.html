Then and now: what was done to the spectrum

Each step's head size, base and context come from its paper or config.json.

Turns for pair i = L · θ′i / 2π on a log scale, where θ′ is the step's (possibly rescaled) frequency and L the context it runs at. Blue pairs rotate at their trained speed, orange ones are slowed by interpolation, gold ones are on YaRN's ramp, grey dimensions carry no rotation. For the per-model YaRN bands of current configs (gpt-oss, DeepSeek V3, Kimi K2), see {{Positional Encodings|n:3c65c17b0d0d81a5b7c9e578fcdfac00}}.

RoPE survived unchanged as a mechanism; what later work changed is which pairs rotate and how fast. Every long-context method of 2023 to 2025 is a rewrite of θi (or of the positions it multiplies) in a model that already uses RoPE. The animation beside steps through them on one picture: one bar per rotation pair of an attention head, its height the number of full turns that pair makes inside the context the model works at. A pair under one turn (below the dashed line) never shows its full circle in that context, so angles past what it reached in training are new to the model, the failure the {{toy|tab:t-run}} shows.

  1. 2017: sinusoids, added once. Vaswani et al. add sin and cos of position at these 256 frequencies to the input, once, across dmodel = 512 (Attention Is All You Need). RoFormer keeps the spectrum and changes how it is applied.
  2. 2021: RoFormer, rotated into q and k. Same geometric frequencies, now rotations of each 64-dimension head's query and key, in every layer (RoFormer Chinese base: 768 wide, 12 heads, pretrained up to 1,536 tokens; shown at stage 1's 512). 16 of its 32 pairs never complete a turn in 512 tokens.
  3. 2021: GPT-J, partial rotary. GPT-J rotates only the first 64 of each 256-dimension head (rotary_dim 64, config.json); GPT-NeoX-20B followed with 25% (2022). The grey dimensions match on content alone.
  4. 2023: LLaMA, every dimension, base 10,000. LLaMA removes absolute embeddings and adds RoPE at each layer (Touvron et al.): 128-dimension heads, base 10,000, context 2,048. This is the starting point the extension methods below rewrite.
  5. 2023: Position Interpolation, 2,048 to 32,768. Run at 32,768 tokens but divide every position by s = 16, so each pair turns exactly as often as it did in 2,048 trained tokens: no angle is new. The cost: neighbouring tokens are now 1/16 of a step apart, so a short fine-tune (within 1,000 steps) is needed (Chen et al.).
  6. 2023: NTK-aware, change the base instead. Raise the base to b · sd/(d−2) = 167,199: the fastest pair keeps its speed (local order stays sharp), the slowest is slowed by the full 16 (YaRN, Appendix A.2).
  7. 2023: YaRN, by wavelength, plus a temperature. Pairs that turn more than β = 32 times in the original 2,048 tokens are kept, pairs under α = 1 turn are interpolated by 16, those between are ramped; logits are scaled by 1/t with √(1/t) = 0.1 ln 16 + 1 = 1.277 (Peng et al.). DeepSeek-V3 and gpt-oss ship this.
  8. 2024: Llama 3, base 500,000 from the start. Train with a base 50 times larger, "effective for context lengths up to 32,768", pretraining on 8,192-token sequences (Llama 3). More pairs sit below one turn, so their angles change slowly enough to stay informative far out.
  9. 2024: DeepSeek-V3, a decoupled RoPE key. MLA compresses keys into a latent that cannot carry a position-dependent rotation, so each head gets 128 position-free dimensions plus a 64-dimension RoPE part (qk_nope_head_dim 128, qk_rope_head_dim 64, YaRN factor 40 from 4,096; config.json, DeepSeek-V2 §2.1.3).
  10. 2024: Qwen2-VL, three position indices. M-RoPE splits the 64 pairs into 16 for time, 24 for height and 24 for width (mrope_section, config.json); for text all three indices are equal and it is plain RoPE (Qwen2-VL).
  11. 2025: Llama 4, interleaved layers without positions. iRoPE: most layers use RoPE over local chunks; interleaved global layers have no positional embedding at all, plus inference-time temperature scaling, for length generalisation (Meta). Maverick: 36 RoPE and 12 NoPE layers (from the Architecture Gallery). The bars show a RoPE layer; the strip shows the layer pattern.
