The paper's figures, rebuilt

The paper prints almost all its results as charts. Its arXiv source ships each chart as a vector PDF, so every bar here is read from the PDF's geometry, calibrated on the tick marks, and checked against the label the authors printed above it where there is one: 91 printed labels, all agree to their printed precision (src/decode_figs.py, inputs/figs.json). Bars without a printed label (Figures 2b and 9, the intervals of Figure 13c) are marked "decoded". Tables 1 to 3 are transcribed from the HTML.

Choose a figure or table on the right: the chart is redrawn from tables.json, with the table of values under it.
