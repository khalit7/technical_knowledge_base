Best resources

The Expressive Power of Transformers with Chain of Thought

Merrill and Sabharwal, ICLR 2024: the result this paper leans on: logarithmically many decoding steps add little to a transformer, linearly many reach all regular languages, polynomially many reach exactly P. It is why a student that writes a long trace escapes Theorem 1.

(45 min)

Chain of Thought Empowers Transformers to Solve Inherently Serial Problems

Li, Liu, Zhou and Ma, ICLR 2024: the circuit view of chain of thought: constant-depth transformers sit in AC0 or TC0 depending on precision, and T steps of chain of thought buy T serial steps.

(45 min)

The Parallelism Tradeoff: Limitations of Log-Precision Transformers

Merrill and Sabharwal, TACL 2023: the upper bound used for the student: a fixed-depth, log-precision transformer is a uniform TC0 circuit, so it cannot solve NL-complete problems unless NL is in TC0.

(40 min)

A Little Depth Goes a Long Way: The Expressive Power of Log-Depth Transformers

Merrill and Sabharwal, 2025: the other side of the same coin: a transformer whose depth grows like log n can decide directed reachability, the problem Theorem 1 uses; Lemma 1 borrows its transcript-enumeration argument.

(40 min)

Algorithmic Thinking Theory (the Branching harness)

Bateni et al., 2025: the tree-shaped harness of Section 5: leaves sample answers, internal nodes synthesise one from their children.

(40 min)

Taming Imperfect Process Verifiers: A Sampling Perspective on Backtracking (VGB)

Rohatgi et al., ICLR 2026: the backtracking harness of Section 2, with the Metropolis-style retract rule and the learned verifier.

(45 min)

Beyond A*: Better Planning with Transformers via Search Dynamics Bootstrapping

Lehnert et al., 2024: where the A* trace format (CREATE, CLOSE, PLAN) comes from; its transformers learn search from such traces at larger sizes.

(35 min)

Stream of Search (SoS): Learning to Search in Language

Gandhi et al., 2024: the optimistic counterpart: a model trained on serialised search traces learns to search inside one rollout.

(35 min)

QED-Nano: Teaching a Tiny Model to Prove Hard Theorems

Qu et al., 2026: the 4B prover of Section 5's second experiment, and its own harness (RSA: 40.0% to 56.9% on IMO-ProofBench at about 22 times the tokens, as this paper quotes it).

(40 min)

Learning through Internalization

Tsilivis et al., 2026: the closest related work by the paper's own account: a transformer absorbing its own chain of thought into its weights, provably for sparse parities.

(35 min)
