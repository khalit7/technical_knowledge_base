Replay the 42 cells

The authors released the success count of every task in every cell: 14 base and post-trained pairs on three benchmarks, 128 attempts per task per model, 41,160 rows ({{data/outcomes_42cells.csv.gz|https://github.com/changdaeoh/sharpening-tax}}, commit 789ae06c, read 19 October 2026, CC BY-NC 4.0). This tab runs the paper's estimators on those counts in your browser: nothing here is read from a figure. Pick a cell; every number below is computed from its counts and set beside the paper's printed {{Table 7|ax:A4.T5}}.

The tax across budgets, for this cell

The paper's {{Figure 6|ax:S4.F5}} plots TaxS(k) against the budget for eight pairs, and {{Figure 26|ax:A4.F25}} both taxes for all 14. Here is TaxS for the cell above, with TaxA in the note under it. Raw TaxA usually grows with k; calibrated TaxS can rise or fall, because it divides by the first attempt's failure rate.

All 42 cells on one map

Two different questions, one per axis. Across: does the base model reach more tasks at k = 128 (coverage gap, base minus post-trained)? Up: did post-training lower what extra attempts are worth (TaxS(128))? The vertical count (36 of 42) is the tax; the horizontal one (26) is smaller. Tap a point to load that cell above.

The bootstrap intervals are the paper's (the {{tables tab|tab:t-tables}} also has this page's own bootstrap, within 0.010 of every printed bound).
