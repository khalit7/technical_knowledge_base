Every check

CheckWherePaperRecomputed
What reproduces, and how. Every check is independent arithmetic on the paper's own printed numbers: the per-stage seconds of Table 14 against the hours, shares and percentages in the text, the S25 update counts and token shares against the budget, Table 8 against the account, the studies against their pooled estimates. Those marked approximate rest on a stated simplification and say so. Nothing here reruns a model: the trainer and run records were not public on 16 November 2026, so no number could be checked against code or logs.
