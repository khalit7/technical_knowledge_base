Held-out accuracy at the first evaluation at or after the token budget $B$ , or after $2B$ or at outcome-only GRPO’s training time at $B$ where marked, minus outcome-only GRPO on the same seeds and evaluation draws. Evaluation uses the setting’s $200$ test problems ( $8$ samples each), except in the rows marked “further test problems” (the $1{,}543$ numeric-answer MATH test problems of levels 3–5 outside every other evaluation set) and “GSM8K” (its $1{,}319$ test problems). Intervals are $95\%$ paired- $t$ CIs over seeds; the bars draw them on the scale above each block, over a gray band at $\pm 0.02$ , blue when the interval lies above zero and red when below. The per-size Monte-Carlo rows give three means without intervals. “Holm” marks a preregistered primary contrast with its Holm-adjusted $p$ ; “TOST” gives the equivalence $p$ at $\pm 0.02$ (studies on Qwen2.5-1.5B that compare against its gain of $0.008$ preregistered $\pm 0.01$ , reported in the text). Secondary, estimate (seeds pooled over several studies of the same configurations), and exploratory rows carry no multiplicity correction. The Monte-Carlo reward scores six segments from $k$ continuations at each cut; learned critics are ridge regressions on hidden states that credit the change in predicted value (TD form) or subtract it from the outcome (baseline form); the critic filter keeps the $6$ of $24$ candidates whose predicted success is nearest $1/2$ , plus $2$ at random.
