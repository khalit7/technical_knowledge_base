Benchmark sizes, read from the scores

A score printed to two decimals that is always a whole number of items out of n fixes the benchmark's size. Checked against every model in both tables (and Table 3 where present):

LiveCodeBench v6's 175 problems and VitaBench's 400 tasks are inferred this way, not stated in the paper; HumanEval (164), IFBench (300) and IFEval (541 prompts, prompt-level) match the benchmarks' published sizes. The other five benchmarks (BFCL v4, τ²-Bench, PinchBench, WorkBuddy, QwenClawBench) are composites or averages over runs, so their size cannot be read this way. Standard errors are binomial, √(p(1 − p)/n) per model, combined as independent.
