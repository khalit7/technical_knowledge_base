Then and now: the recipe, line by line

The paper's legacy is a recipe, so this tab follows its seven lines through the work that came after, one step per paper, from Bai et al. (April 2022, a month after InstructGPT) to DeepSeek-R1 (January 2025). Each step shows only what that source says it changed, quoted; lines it does not mention keep their previous value and are marked as carried over, not as confirmed. Every source is beyond the paper.

The recipe, one source per step

  1. March 2022: {{InstructGPT (this paper)|ax:S1}}. The starting recipe, as {{§3.5 and Appendix C|ax:S3.SS5}} describe it.
  2. April 2022: {{Bai et al., Anthropic|https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.05862}}. Helpful and harmless assistants with RLHF, a month after InstructGPT; also "a roughly linear relation between the RL reward and the square root of the KL divergence between the policy and its initialization".
  3. November 2022: {{ChatGPT, OpenAI|http://web.archive.org/web/20221202010338/https://openai.com/blog/chatgpt/}}. "using the same methods as InstructGPT, but with slight differences in the data collection setup"; "a sibling model to InstructGPT".
  4. December 2022: {{Constitutional AI, Anthropic|https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073}}. RL from AI Feedback (RLAIF): the same skeleton, with the harmlessness labels made by a model.
  5. May 2023: {{DPO|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290}}. Steps 2 and 3 collapse into one supervised loss on the same comparisons.
  6. July 2023: {{Llama 2, Meta|https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09288}}. An open model card for the whole pipeline at scale.
  7. February 2024: {{DeepSeekMath (GRPO)|https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03300}}. The value network (a model as large as the policy) goes; DeepSeekMath names this paper as the source of the per-token KL it replaces.
  8. November 2024: {{Tülu 3, Ai2|https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15124}}. A fully open recipe: "supervised finetuning (SFT), Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and ... Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR)"; OLMo 2 adopts it.
  9. January 2025: {{DeepSeek-R1|https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948}}. For reasoning, the learned reward model is replaced by checking the answer.
