The paper

Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback (arXiv HTML v1)

NeurIPS 2022, the paper itself (a long paper: 20 pages of main text, then references and 43 pages of appendices); the HTML version has the anchors this page links to. abstract page, PDF.

(1h 30m)

following-instructions-human-feedback

no training code or weights: the model card, samples from GPT-3 and InstructGPT on the public NLP benchmarks, and links to the labeling instructions (about 10 minutes to skim).

Best resources

Aligning language models to follow instructions

OpenAI blog, 27 January 2022 (before the arXiv version): the announcement: InstructGPT replaced GPT-3 as the default API model, with side-by-side samples.

(15 min)

RLHF: Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

Chip Huyen: the whole pipeline (pretrain, SFT, reward model, PPO) with the maths, the data economics and practical failure modes; the best single walkthrough of what this paper standardised.

(40 min)

Illustrating Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

Lambert, Castricato, von Werra, Havrilla (Hugging Face): the canonical diagram-first explanation of the three stages and the KL-penalised reward.

(25 min)

RLHF Book

Nathan Lambert: book-length treatment of RLHF and post-training; the reward-modelling and policy-optimisation chapters (about 2 hours) put InstructGPT in the context of everything after it (DPO, GRPO, RLVR).

(2h)

The N+ Implementation Details of RLHF with PPO

Huang, Noukhovitch, Hosseini, Rasul, Wang, Tunstall (arXiv 2403.17031): the first open reproduction of OpenAI's RLHF scaling results, with over 20 implementation details that decide whether PPO on a language model works at all; read it before implementing the recipe.

(45 min)

Scaling Laws for Reward Model Overoptimization

Gao, Schulman, Hilton (arXiv 2210.10760): the measurement this paper's KL penalty guards against: a gold reward model stands in for humans, and the true score rises and then falls as a proxy reward model is optimised harder.

(40 min)

Connected papers in this knowledge base

GPT-3

Constitutional AI

2022: replaces the human harmlessness labels with AI feedback (RLAIF) on the same RLHF skeleton.

DPO

2023: collapses the reward model and PPO stages into one classification loss on the same comparisons.

DeepSeekMath (GRPO)

2024: GRPO drops the value function this paper initialised from the reward model and moves the KL term into the loss.

DeepSeek-R1

2025: rule-based (verifiable) rewards instead of a learned reward model for reasoning data.

The Llama 3 Herd of Models

2024: an open production pipeline built on the SFT plus preference-tuning template.

OLMo 2

2025: a fully open pipeline (the Tülu 3 recipe: SFT, DPO, RLVR).

Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models

2020: the scaling this paper argues post-training beats, for usefulness, by 100 times.
