The paper's tables and figures, rebuilt

Tables 2 and 3 are parsed from the arXiv HTML at their printed precision; Figures 4 and 5 are transcribed from their printed labels; Figures 6 to 8 are decoded from the vector graphics; the leaderboard values come from the authors' {{bench.json|https://faibench.org/data/bench.json}}. Every derived number is recomputed by src/recompute.py.

Table 2: score by category

{{Table 2|ax:Sx3.T2}}. Click a column heading to sort. Under each category, its number of tasks from {{Figure 4|ax:Sx3.F4}}. Bold: best in column. The bound is √(m(1 − m)/n), the largest standard error a mean of n rewards in [0, 1] can have. Defaults reproduce the Full column independently: Σ nc × scorec / 85 lands within 0.03 of every printed Full value.
