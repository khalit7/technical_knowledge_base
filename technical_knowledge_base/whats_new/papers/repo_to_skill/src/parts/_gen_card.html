Repo-To-Skill: Distilling GitHub Repositories Into AI4AI Skills

Jianlyu Chen, Yuyang Hu, Hongjin Qian, Jiawei Liu, Wenqing Wei, Xiaolong Chen, Defu Lian, Zhicheng Dou, Chaozhuo Li, Qiwei Ye, Zheng Liu
Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI), University of Science and Technology of China, Renmin University of China, Hong Kong Polytechnic University (corresponding authors Defu Lian, Zhicheng Dou, Zheng Liu) · September 2026 (arXiv v1, 2 September 2026; the only version so far)

In one line: Names the thing agent skills usually lack, operational knowledge (the know-how of making a published method run), and distils it with one agent, DisCo, from repositories, papers or a task into checked skill graphs; the released AREX-Skill Library holds 5,353 skills from 1,000 ML repositories in 20 areas and 178 capability families (checked for structure and against each package's own tests, not for helping an agent succeed). With Codex on GPT-5.5 at a fixed run-time budget, skills built for each benchmark, not the library (which no result uses), gave +134.3% relative on MLE-bench (31.1% to 72.9% medalled, three runs; the skills came from up to 24 GPU-hours of trials on each competition, not counted), +34.4% on PaperBench, +9.2% on FrontierCS (with 1.8 times the tokens) and +14.0% on PassNet, the last three single runs.

Softened on 2026-10-25 from the database property, which credits the gains to the library "at fixed compute" and says the knowledge lives in READMEs and issues: none of the four results uses the library, the fixed budget excludes skill construction, and the paper's repository pipeline reads source, docs, examples, tests, scripts and configs, not issue threads.

How far to trust it: Believe that the released library is real and counts out exactly (5,353 skills, 2,209 routes, the FrontierCS graph's 42 links), and that task-specific skills helped Codex on all four benchmarks. Do not read the headline numbers as the library's effect or as free: none of the four results uses the 1,000-repository library, the MLE-bench skills came from up to 24 GPU-hours of exploration on each competition that the "fixed budget" leaves out, the FrontierCS skill runs used 1.8 times the tokens, and only MLE-bench reports repeated runs.
