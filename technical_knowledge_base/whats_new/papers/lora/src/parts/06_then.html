Then and now

What later work changed in how LoRA is used, one step at a time, and what survived untouched. Each step marks the matrices that get adapters in a decoder block of today (orange; its SwiGLU MLP has three matrices where GPT-3's had two) and how the base is stored; every change carries its source. All of this is beyond the paper.

Step by step, 2021 to 2025

  1. 2021, the paper. LoRA on Wq and Wv only, MLP frozen; α/r with α fixed; FP16 base; merge for zero latency (§4.1, §4.2, Table 12).
  2. Feb 2023: Hugging Face PEFT. The repository points to PEFT, which becomes the standard interface (LoraConfig, target_modules) ({{repository README|https://github.com/microsoft/LoRA}}).
  3. May 2023: QLoRA. Attention-only LoRA could not match full fine-tuning on large models; LoRA on all linear layers could. A frozen 4-bit NormalFloat base with double quantisation and paged optimisers fine-tunes 65B on one 48 GB GPU ({{QLoRA|https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314}}).
  4. Nov 2023: S-LoRA, then vLLM and SGLang. Instead of merging, serve many adapters over one base in one batch: up to 4× the throughput of naive serving, thousands of adapters on one GPU ({{S-LoRA|https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03285}}); the paper's "hard to batch" limitation, solved by not merging.
  5. Nov 2023: rsLoRA. Dividing by r stunts high ranks; dividing by √r lets larger r help ({{rsLoRA|https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03732}}); PEFT's use_rslora. The {{Train a LoRA|tab:t-run:runSweep}} tab compares both at one learning rate.
  6. Feb 2024: DoRA. Decompose each weight into a magnitude and a direction and apply LoRA to the direction; consistently better than LoRA on LLaMA, LLaVA and VL-BART ({{DoRA|https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09353}}).
  7. Jun 2024: adapters on a phone. Apple's about 3B on-device model loads, caches and swaps rank-16 adapters of tens of megabytes per feature, and uses LoRA adapters to recover accuracy after compressing the base ({{Apple|https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/introducing-apple-foundation-models}}).
  8. Sep 2025: LoRA Without Regret. All layers, especially MLP; the optimal LoRA learning rate is about 10× full fine-tuning's; RL matches even at rank 1; large batches cost LoRA more ({{Thinking Machines|https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/lora/}}); their {{Tinker|https://thinkingmachines.ai/tinker/}} service is LoRA underneath.
