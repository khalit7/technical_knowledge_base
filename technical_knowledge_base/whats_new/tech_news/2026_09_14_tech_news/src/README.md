# 2026-09-14: tech news, source

`../index.html` is the whole Notion page "2026-09-14: tech news" (https://app.notion.com/p/3db5c17b0d0d8135a34af60966c5ecc1, child of Tech news). The page has no child pages, databases or video. Build with `sh build.sh`.

## Layout
Nine tabs, as in the 2026-08-24 template: **At a glance** (`t-read`: lead, theme, week strip, one row per section, one-line follow-ups), one tab per section (`t-top`, `t-models`, `t-research`, `t-industry`, `t-compute`, `t-dev`, `t-talk`) with its own lead, items as cards (verbatim text, Checked / Correction / Unconfirmed / Links / Related boxes, follow-ups under the item) and its visual, then **Further reading** (`t-more`).

Visuals: `v_ced` + `31_js_sc_ced.js` (one agent turn through DeepSeek V4-Flash and V4.1-Flash, a 3D stage since 2026-11-13) and `14_js_ced.js` (the cache waterfall), `v_rswe` + `15_js_rswe.js` with `data/realswe.json` (Real-SWE's three readings), `v_rlt` + `16_js_rlt.js` (Transformer against the Recurrent Looped Transformer, animated, with step lists), `v_econ` + `17_js_econ.js` (labour share x GDP), `v_sol` + `18_js_sol.js` (decode speed-of-light), and the week strip (`12_js_strip.js`, generalised here: day labels from the axis, an "earlier" column, crowded cells in two rows).

## The issue is data
- `live.md`: the verbatim Notion fetch, saved by script from the fetch result.
- `mk_items.py` parses it into `data/items.json` (changed from the template: Notion's bold-inside-link and adjacent bold runs are normalised, `\^` is unescaped, each item gets an `html` field keeping bold and code marks, and links inside the text are kept as sources).
- `mk_annotations.py` writes `data/annotations.json` (sections, leads, dates, feeds, notes, follow-ups, visual placement) and merges `data/factcheck_notes.json`, written by `mk_factcheck.py` from a separate search for the items the issue gives no source for (raw results in `inputs/factcheck_raw.json`).
- `mk_issue.py` renders `parts/03_issue.html` and `parts/11_data.js` (the strip data now carries a short excerpt of each item, not its full text).
- `mk_coverage.py` writes `coverage.json` after the build.
- Order: `python3 mk_factcheck.py && python3 mk_annotations.py && sh build.sh && python3 mk_coverage.py`.

## Files
- `recompute.py`: every derived number (DeepSeek cache bytes from the configs, 437x, layer passes; all three Real-SWE readings and their correlations; 3.44x, 3.24x; speed-of-light 472 tok/s, 62%, 39%, 1.58x; Table 3 shortcut; RLT chain lengths), with asserts.
- `viz_ideas.md`: candidates, scores, data, rejections, and what the methodology lacked.
- `inputs/`: sources saved on 2026-10-02 (`i<n>_*` the issue's own links, `wb_*` Wayback captures nearest the issue date, `x_*` other primary sources, `fu_*` follow-up sources, `fc_*` spot checks of the search results).
- Checks: `sh html_utils/checkpage.sh technical_knowledge_base/whats_new/tech_news/2026_09_14_tech_news` and `node technical_knowledge_base/whats_new/tech_news/2026_09_14_tech_news/src/check_stages.mjs` from the repo root.

## The 2026-11-13 layout rollout (split layout, renderer v2 stage, autoplay)

The issue's words, checks, corrections and follow-ups are unchanged; only the layout, the visual boxes and their motion changed. The machinery follows the 2026-08-24 issue, so the issues converge.
- **Split in `mk_issue.py`, never in the generated part** (ported from the 2026-08-24 issue: `vsplit`, the markers and `viz_with`). An item with a visual (`viz_after`) becomes `.s3sp`: the item card in `.s3L`, the visual in `.s3R`. A visual part is cut by markers: `<!--L-->` the visual's explanation for the left column, `<!--R-->` the right column, `<!--A-->` anything after the split, `<!--RFIRST-->` to put the right column first (the stage and the RLT animation, so on a phone they sit at the top). Each visual's lede moved into the left column as `.vz-l`; static cards sit in `.s3side`. CSS added at the end of `01_css.html` (`.vz-h`, `.vz-l`, `.vz-cnt`, the 60em cap on full-width cards).
- **Splits**: At a glance (lead and theme | week strip); Top stories (DeepSeek V4.1-Flash item, explanation, step list and counters | the 3D stage; then a second split, the factor of four explained | the cache waterfall; Real-SWE item and its explanation | the three-readings card); Research (RLT item, explanation and two step lists | the RLT animation; Anthropic's scenarios item | the labour-share calculator); Dev tools (Cohere's megakernel item | the speed-of-light calculator). Every other item, and Models, Industry, Compute, Talk of the town and Further reading, stay full width (no visual).
- **One 3D stage**, `31_js_sc_ced.js` (`#stg-ced`, replacing the 2D layer grid of `14_js_ced.js` and its V4-Flash / V4.1-Flash toggle): both models side by side, each a tower of its layers to scale (43 against 20 + 20), one slab per layer whose depth is the prompt; a layer that keeps a global cache carries a plate on its outer side whose length is its bytes per prompt token (V4-Flash: 21 plates of (584 + 68) / 4 and 20 of 584 / 128; V4.1-Flash: three of (288 + 68) / 2 and the decoder's 288 + 68 as a glass plate, written by projection). Teal dots show cache reuse inside V4.1-Flash's encoder, orange dots the projection, the purple sliver the 128 replayed tokens, the floor blocks the SSD store (the report's "nearly half" and "about 1/8", published, not recomputed), and the blue columns one generated token reading every cache. Eight steps in one list (`sx-ced`) merge the two old caption sets; steps 4 and 5 move the camera to V4.1-Flash. The counters became a table of both models at the end of the current step; the prompt-length menu sits on the left and stops the stage.
- **The RLT animation stays 2D** (a grid of tokens by layers with one chain drawn through it reads best flat). Its own controller in `16_js_rlt.js` now follows the motion rules of `RD.anim`: plays once at least half in view, holds each step 3.8 s, pauses off screen, stops for good on any control (including a click in the step list) or at the last step, never under reduced motion, and sets `data-playing` while it plays. Its captions moved into two step lists (`sx-rlt-tf`, `sx-rlt-rlt`), one shown per mode; switching mode swaps the list and replays.
- **Kept 2D**: the week strip (a timeline), Real-SWE's three readings (dot plot, task grid, scatter), the labour-share and speed-of-light calculators (sliders over a bar chart: the outcome of a setting the reader drives, so widgets, no autoplay), the cache waterfall (bars on one axis).
- Templates copied unchanged: `02_split.html`, `25_js_s3.js`, `05z_errbox.js.html`, `99_js_tabs.js` (storage key `tn0914-tab`, replacing `90_js_tabs.js`). `21_js_rd_common.js` is not used (no `RD.anim`).
- `build.sh` finds the repo root by walking up to `.venv` or `html_utils` (no git), and writes its long-dash check as bytes.
- `check_stages.mjs` (from the repo root): the stage and the RLT animation at 1400 light, 920 light and 390 dark (autoplay on view, one list item per step, last step reached, stops after a control, flows stop off screen, the RLT list per mode and list clicks), splits two columns at 1400, no errors, NaN or sideways scroll, reduced motion, and the stage's counters against the configs' arithmetic (3,514 and 890 bytes per token, N x 43 and N x 20 + 128 x 20 passes) at all four prompt lengths.

Length: "N min to read all tabs" went from 60 to 64 (14,810 words). The growth is the step lists: the stage's eight steps (the old captions showed one at a time, drawn by script) and the RLT's two lists (one hidden until its mode is chosen). Nothing was cut; a tech-news issue has no ledger.
