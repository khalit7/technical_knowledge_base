 Alibaba launches Wan3.0, a 30-second AI video generation model
 by Kerem Gülen 
 August 24, 2026 
 in 
 Artificial Intelligence , News 
 Home 
 News 
 Artificial Intelligence 
 Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on LinkedIn Share on WhatsApp Share on e-mail 
 Alibaba launched Wan3.0, its latest AI video generation model, on August 24, 2026, following a $10.2 billion share placement in Hong Kong. The share sale’s proceeds are earmarked for investments in artificial intelligence, underscoring the company’s commitment to AI development. The launch of Wan3.0 marks an expansion of access to a model that has been in public beta since August 6, 2026, via Alibaba Cloud’s Model Studio and Qwen Cloud platform.
 Wan3.0 significantly enhances video generation capabilities, allowing users to create clips of up to 30 seconds in a single pass, which is double the output length of its predecessor, Wan2.7, which had a limit of 15 seconds. The new model generates videos at resolutions up to 1080p and maintains character detail, spatial layout, and motion consistency throughout the video. It is designed to produce synchronized micro-expressions in faces and multilingual voice output.
 A key feature of Wan3.0 is its ability to accept a variety of input formats, including text, images, audio, videos, web pages, and documents such as PDFs and PowerPoint files. This versatility allows users to convert structured content, like slide decks and spreadsheets, into videos, making it particularly appealing for marketing departments and corporate communications teams.

 The pricing structure for Wan3.0 is set at $0.05 per second for 480p, $0.10 for 720p, and $0.20 for 1080p, translating to $12 per minute at the highest resolution. This competitive pricing strategy positions Wan3.0 against other models in the market, including Google’s Veo 3.1, which costs $0.40 per second for standard service. However, the model’s performance has not yet been independently benchmarked, leaving its quality claims primarily reliant on company assertions.
 Access to Wan3.0 is controlled, as it is currently available through an application process on the Model Studio and Qwen Cloud platforms, with a consumer-facing site at wan.video planned as a members-only platform. Alibaba’s decision to close the model weights marks a shift from its previous open-source approach, which has been a hallmark of the Wan series. The company previously built its reputation on open-sourcing, but this change coincides with a focus on monetizing its AI capabilities.
 In its financial results for the June quarter of 2026, Alibaba reported a 45% year-over-year increase in cloud and AI revenue, totaling 48.44 billion yuan. However, the company’s quarterly net profit fell by 75%, attributed to a significant rise in capital expenditure, which reached 67.68 billion yuan. Alibaba had committed 380 billion yuan to AI infrastructure over three years in early 2025, and reports suggest it may increase this commitment to 480 billion yuan, highlighting the substantial financial pressure associated with advancing its AI initiatives.
 Although the initial launch of Wan3.0 does not provide specific performance metrics, its introduction is positioned as a strategic move to consolidate Alibaba’s presence in the competitive AI video generation market. As the company navigates the challenges of pricing and performance evaluation, the success of Wan3.0 will depend on its ability to attract and retain users in a rapidly evolving landscape.
