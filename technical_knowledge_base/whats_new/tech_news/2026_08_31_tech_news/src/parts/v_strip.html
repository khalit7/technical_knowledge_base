The week at a glance

One mark per item on the day the issue (or its source) dates it; rows are the tabs, the shaded band is the issue's window. Hollow rings joined by a line are an item's other dates (first report, paper posted); marks drawn as rings with a coloured edge are backfill items, added after publication. Undated items sit in the last column. Click a mark for its summary and sources, and a link to the item in its own tab. The first row of buttons filters by tab, the second by the part of the issue an item came from.
