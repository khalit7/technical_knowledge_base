# 2026-09-21: tech news, source

`../index.html` is the whole Notion page "2026-09-21: tech news" (https://app.notion.com/p/3e25c17b0d0d813dba03fa81f5d8cc39, child of Tech news). The page has **a video block** (`<video>` block `3e45c17b-0d0d-81a4-a26c-d732344a2c81`, file `tech_news_2026_09_21.mp4`, caption "Tech news, week to 21 September 2026: four stories, and the rest of the week at the end"); it stays on the Notion page under the HTML, and the header carries its caption and description and links the block. No child pages or databases. Build with `sh build.sh`.

## Layout
Nine tabs, as in the approved 2026-08-24 issue: **At a glance** (`t-read`, open by default: lead, the week's theme, week strip, one row per section, follow-ups in one line each), one tab per section (`t-top`, `t-models`, `t-research`, `t-industry`, `t-compute`, `t-dev`, `t-talk`) with its own lead, its items as cards (verbatim text; Checked, Correction, Unconfirmed, Links and Related boxes; Hacker News points from the issue and comment counts from 2026-10-02 as separate badges; follow-ups in full under the item; the topic pages the item feeds) and its visuals, then **Further reading** (`t-more`).

Visuals, each beside the item it explains (the split layout since 2026-11-13, below): task horizon (`v_horizon`, `14_js_horizon.js`), two proofs to scale (`v_proofs`, `16_js_proofs.js`), Z.ai's 13 days (`v_zai`, `19_js_zai.js`), Rubin by interactivity (`v_vr`, `13_js_vr.js`) in Top stories; ternary packing animation (`v_tern`, `15_js_tern.js` on `RD.anim`, data `11c_tern_data.js`) and the KV cache 3D stage (`v_kv`, `31_js_sc_kv.js`, with the per-session chart `20_js_kv.js`) in Research; Huawei roadmap (`v_roadmap`, `17_js_roadmap.js`) in Compute; harness tax (`v_htax`, `18_js_htax.js`) in Dev tools; the week strip (`v_strip`, `12_js_strip.js`) in At a glance.

## The issue is data
- `live.md`: the verbatim Notion fetch (extracted by script from the fetch result, never retyped).
- `mk_items.py` parses it into `data/items.json`. Changes from the 2026-08-24 version: the leading `# Video` section, the read-time line and the intro are recognised; Notion auto-links that split a bold title (`[**Z.ai**](http://Z.ai)** says ...**`) are joined; per-link time estimates after the trailing sources are kept; `\{ \}` escapes are undone.
- `data/annotations.json`: dates, short strip labels, tags, feeds, Hacker News badges, the condensed check notes, cross-tab links, follow-ups and where each visual goes. It was written from four source re-checks made on 2026-10-02 (the extracts are in `inputs/`).
- `mk_issue.py` renders `parts/03_issue.html` and `parts/11_data.js`. Changes: the issue's inline `**bold**` and `` `code` `` are rendered; short labels come from annotations; source read times are shown; Hacker News comment badges link the thread; the strip data carries only each item's first sentence (size).
- `12_js_strip.js`: dates generic (axis and window from annotations), an "earlier" column, row heights by the fullest cell.
- `01_css.html` was pruned of the template's unused rules.

## The 2026-11-13 layout rollout (split layout, renderer v2 stage, autoplay)

The issue's words, checks, corrections and follow-ups are unchanged; only the layout, the visual boxes and their motion changed.
- **Split in `mk_issue.py`, never in the generated part** (the scheme of the 2026-08-24 issue, added to this issue's `mk_issue.py`, which keeps its inline bold and code, short labels from annotations and comment badges). An item with a visual (`viz_after`) becomes `.s3sp`: the item card in `.s3L`, the visual in `.s3R`. A visual part is cut by markers: `<!--L-->` the visual's explanation for the left column, `<!--R-->` the right column, `<!--A-->` anything after the split, `<!--RFIRST-->` to put the right column first (the animation and the stage, so on a phone they sit at the top of their item). `"viz_with": [n]` would group earlier items of a section beside one visual; this issue does not need it. At a glance puts the lead and theme beside the week strip.
- **Splits (nine visuals, eleven splits)**: At a glance (lead and theme | week strip); Top stories (each of the four first items | its chart: task horizon, two proofs, Z.ai's 13 days with the two method boxes moved to the left, Rubin by interactivity); Research (BITCOS item, explanation, zero-share slider and step lists | the packing animation; then the bits-per-weight chart as its own split; DeepSeek-V4.1 Flash item, explanation and step list | the 3D stage; then "What one session holds" as its own split); Compute (Huawei item | roadmap); Dev tools (harness-tax item | chart). Every other item, Models, Industry, Talk of the town and Further reading stay full width (no visual).
- **One 3D stage**, `31_js_sc_kv.js` (`#stg-kv`, replacing the 2D step bars of `20_js_kv.js`): the three factors of zartbot's formula as the three axes (layers left to right, a 16-token window front to back, each stored entry a box as tall as its bytes, the 68-byte indexer key on top). Four steps: V4-Flash's 21 CSA layers at m = 4 and 20 HCA layers at m = 128 (3,514 B per token); cross-layer reuse in V4.1, four source layers (2, 8, 14, 20) with dots streaming to the layers that read them (652); the compression relaxed to m = 2 and 1 (1,630); FP4 main entries, 584 to 288 B (890). Numbers from zartbot's breakdown and the model card (the extracts in `inputs/`); illustrative and said under the explanation: the 16-token window, V4-Flash's layers grouped by kind, and which later layer reads which source. On a phone the camera looks down more steeply.
- **The ternary animation stays 2D** (a block of 128 weights and the bits that store them is a flat bit layout), now on `RD.anim` from the template: two step lists (`sx-tn-trit`, `sx-tn-bitcos`, the old per-step captions moved into them), one shown per mode, five steps each; the 26 bytes and 8 chunks are grouped into stretches (long-replay rule) that write frame by frame when reached by moving forward. The sample-dependent counts in the lists are filled by script from defaults written in the HTML. Moving the zero share stops the animation; switching mode swaps the list and replays.
- **Kept 2D**: the week strip (a timeline), task horizon (a log-scale time series), the two proofs (a unit grid and hours on their own axis), Z.ai's 13 days (a step curve), Rubin by interactivity (log-scale curves), bits per weight against z (lines and a scatter), the per-session chart (log bars), Huawei's roadmap (a timeline), the harness tax (paired values on a log axis).
- `02_split.html`, `21_js_rd_common.js`, `25_js_s3.js`, `05z_errbox.js.html` copied from the templates; `99_js_tabs.js` (template, storage key `tn0921-tab`) replaces `90_js_tabs.js`. `build.sh` finds the repo root by walking up to `.venv` or `html_utils` (no git). Long dashes in `inputs/` extracts were replaced with `--`.
- `check_stages.mjs` (from the repo root): the stage and the animation in both modes at 1400 light, 920 light and 390 dark (autoplay on view, one list item per step, last step reached, stops after a control, flows stop off screen), splits two columns at 1400, no errors, NaN or sideways scroll, reduced motion, the stage's four totals and three ratios against the sources' bytes and the scene's labels, and the ternary lists' sample counts against the drawn sample at two zero shares.

Length: "N min to read all tabs" went from 70 to 74 (16,933 words). The growth is the step lists (the stage's four and the ternary's two, one of them hidden until BITCOS is chosen) and the explanations moved beside the visuals. Nothing was cut; a tech-news issue has no ledger.
