Which "x" is which: Vera Rubin against GB300 by interactivity

Total tokens per second per megawatt of all-in utility power, on a log scale, against how fast each user is served (tokens per second per user, the interactivity). Every ratio in the headlines is one vertical slice of this chart against one particular GB300 software engine. At the 60 to 100 tokens per second most agentic serving runs at, Rubin is 1.45x to 2.1x per megawatt (the issue's 1.4x to 3x is per dollar of total cost, a different metric); the gap grows as GB300 runs out of headroom, peaking near 7x against its best engine at 150; the 62.9x is against the engine that collapses first.
