Why two models in one session can cost less: the prompt cache

One illustrative coding session of twelve turns, drawn three ways to the same scale. Each bar is the input a model reads on that turn: the light part comes from that model's prompt cache, the dark part is processed at full price. Cached work belongs to one model, so switching models mid-session re-reads the whole history; Fusion gives each model its own context and passes short briefs and results between them, so neither cache breaks.

Illustrative session: the turn sizes (20K to start, 4K a turn, 2K briefs and results) are invented to show the mechanism. The mechanism is Cognition's; the measured runs are in the table below.
