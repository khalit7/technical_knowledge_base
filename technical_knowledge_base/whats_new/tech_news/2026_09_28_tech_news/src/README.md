# 2026-09-28: tech news, source

`../index.html` is the whole Notion page "2026-09-28: tech news" (https://app.notion.com/p/3e95c17b0d0d81e69f97de1d3b6960f4, child of Tech news). The page has no child pages, databases or video. Build with `sh build.sh`.

## Layout
Nine tabs: **At a glance** (`t-read`, open by default: lead, theme, week strip, one row per section, one-line follow-up list), one tab per section (`t-top`, `t-models`, `t-research`, `t-industry`, `t-compute`, `t-dev`, `t-talk`), each with its own lead, its items as cards (verbatim text, Checked / Correction / Links / Unconfirmed / Related boxes, follow-ups in full under the item) and its visuals, then **Further reading** (`t-more`). Items with a visual are splits (see the rollout section).

## Machinery (from the 2026-08-24 template, with the 2026-09-21 improvements)
- `live.md`: the verbatim Notion fetch, extracted by script from the session's saved tool result (never retyped).
- `mk_items.py` (unchanged from 2026-09-21) parses it into `data/items.json`.
- `data/annotations.json`: issue window and strip axis, glance texts, sections with leads, per item dates, short label, tags, feeds, Hacker News points (as stated) and comments (counted 2026-10-02), notes, follow-up and `viz_after`; `followups` with the dated "What happened next" entries. It was composed from the four source re-checks of 2026-10-02.
- `mk_issue.py`: as 2026-09-21, plus `relink()`, which puts the issue's own inline links (arXiv ids, github.com/docker/skills) back on their text; only Notion's bare-name auto-link (claude.ai) is dropped. The strip data no longer carries tags and first lines are cut at 150 characters (page size).
- `mk_coverage.py`: as 2026-09-21, also checking those inline links.
- `parts/10b_js_anim.js`: a shared step-animation controller (`makeAnim`, `animCtl`): modes, play, pause, step, scrub, speed, step lists, autoplay on view and reduced motion (see the rollout section). Reusable by later issues.
- Visuals: `v_price` + `13_js_price.js`, `v_share` + `15_js_share.js`, `v_sci` + `14_js_sci.js` (Top stories), `v_jit` + `16_js_jit.js` (Research), `v_fusion` + `31_js_sc_fusion.js` (3D stage), `v_quant` + `17_js_quant.js` (Dev tools), `v_ord` + `19_js_ord.js` (Talk), `v_strip` + `12_js_strip.js` (At a glance). `01b_css.html` adds table and checkbox styles missing from the trimmed CSS.

## Files
- `recompute.py`: every derived number, with asserts on the published ones.
- `coverage.json`: every item, its numbers and source links checked against the built page (`all_found` true); the dropped auto-link; corrections; follow-ups.
- `viz_ideas.md`: candidates, scores, data, rejections.
- `inputs/`: source text saved on 2026-10-02.
- Checks: `sh html_utils/checkpage.sh technical_knowledge_base/whats_new/tech_news/2026_09_28_tech_news` from the repo root.
- Long dashes in the saved source extracts were replaced by `--` (the repo allows no em-dashes anywhere).

## The 2026-11-13 layout rollout (split layout, renderer v2 stage, autoplay)

The issue's words, checks, corrections and follow-ups are unchanged; only the layout, the visual boxes and their motion changed (machinery as on the 2026-08-24 issue).
- **Split in `mk_issue.py`, never in the generated part.** `vsplit()` and `viz_with` were added to this page's own `mk_issue.py` (it keeps its `relink()`, markdown and `short` labels): an item with a visual (`viz_after`) becomes `.s3sp`, the item card in `.s3L`, the visual in `.s3R`. Markers in `parts/v_<name>.html`: `<!--L-->` left text, `<!--R-->` right column, `<!--A-->` after the split, `<!--RFIRST-->` visual first in the DOM (the stage and the JitMem animation). Each visual's lede moved to the left as `.vz-l` (with its "Illustrative" note); static and widget cards sit in `.s3side`. CSS added at the end of `01b_css.html` (`.vz-h`, `.vz-l`, `.an-st`, `.fu-tab`, the 60em cap on full-width cards). No item uses `viz_with`.
- **Splits**: At a glance (lead and theme | week strip); Top stories (three frontier releases | price changes and the one-task calculator; open weights' share | token and spend bars and Harvey's margin; nine loops | cost per checked result); Research (JitMem item, explanation and two step lists | the memory-curation animation); Dev tools (Devin Fusion item, explanation, step list, per-step table and the measured runs | 3D stage; dlab item | bits-per-weight calculator); Talk (Ord's post | swarm calculator). Every other item, Models, Industry, Compute and Further reading stay full width (no visual).
- **One 3D stage**, `31_js_sc_fusion.js` (`#stg-fusion`, replacing the 2D three-mode lane animation `18_js_fusion.js`): the same illustrative twelve-turn session three ways at once, one row each in depth (one model throughout at the back, switching models mid-session, then Fusion's lead and sidekick in front), one column per turn whose height is that turn's input on one token scale, pale where it is read from the model's prompt cache and solid where it is processed at full price (red for the two full re-reads). A glass plate at each row's head is that model's cache, dim when cold or expired; dots stream from it into the turn being read, red dots carry the history into a miss, purple dots carry Fusion's 2K brief and 2K result. Eight steps in one list (`sx-fusion`), the old three caption sets merged into stretches of turns (1; 2 to 4; 5 and 6; 7; 8 and 9; 10; 11 and 12; the sums); steps 4 and 6 move the camera close to the switching row on wide screens. The counters became a table under the list (from cache, full price, hit rate, re-reads, after the step's last turn). Numbers: the same illustrative session as the 2D lanes (20K start, 4K a turn, 8K sidekick prompt, 2K brief and result; sums 504K / 440K cached / 87%, 504K / 156K full price / 69%, 296K / 228K / 77%).
- **The JitMem animation stays 2D** (four short rows of text in three columns read best flat). `makeAnim` in `10b_js_anim.js` now follows the motion rules of `RD.anim`: plays once at least half in view (each step tweens, then rests 2.4 s), pauses off screen, stops for good on any control (including a click in the step list) or at the last step, never under reduced motion, sets `data-playing` while it plays. Its captions moved into two static step lists (`sx-jt-write`, `sx-jt-read`), one shown per mode; switching mode swaps the list and replays. The card keeps a one-line status.
- **Kept 2D**: the week strip (a timeline), the price chart (log-axis dumbbells) and its task calculator, the token and spend split (two 100% bars and a three-point line) and Harvey's margin slider, the cost per checked result (log dollar axis), the bits-per-weight and swarm calculators (sliders over bars: widgets the reader drives, no autoplay).
- Templates copied unchanged: `02_split.html`, `25_js_s3.js`, `05z_errbox.js.html`; `99_js_tabs.js` (storage key `tn0928-tab`) replaces `90_js_tabs.js`. `build.sh` finds the repo root by walking up to `.venv` or `html_utils` (no git) and checks for long dashes as bytes.
- `check_stages.mjs` (from the repo root): the stage and the JitMem animation in both modes at 1400 light, 920 light and 390 dark (autoplay on view, one list item per step, the other mode's list hidden, last step reached, list clicks, stops after a control, nothing moving off screen), the expected splits per tab and two columns at 1400, no errors, NaN or sideways scroll, reduced motion, and every number in the stage's step list against the scene's own columns.

Length: "N min to read all tabs" went from 81 to 85 (19,654 words). The growth is the step lists: JitMem's two lists in Research (one hidden until its mode is chosen; about 430 words more on that tab) and the stage's eight steps and table in Dev tools (about 480 words more). Nothing was cut; a tech-news issue has no ledger.
