# 2026-08-24: tech news, source

`../index.html` is the whole Notion page "2026-08-24: tech news" (https://app.notion.com/p/3c65c17b0d0d81ca8c8af3c2fc0c3ced, child of Tech news). The page has no child pages, databases or video. Build with `sh build.sh`.

## Layout
Nine tabs: **At a glance** (`t-read`, open by default: lead, theme, week strip, one row per section linking to its tab, one-line follow-up list), one tab per section (`t-top`, `t-models`, `t-research`, `t-industry`, `t-compute`, `t-dev`, `t-talk`), each with its own lead paragraph, its items as cards (verbatim text, Checked / Correction / Links / Unconfirmed / Related boxes, follow-ups in full under the item) and its visual, then **Further reading** (`t-more`). Each item lives in one tab only; other tabs link to it with in-page tab links (`[[text|tab|target id]]` in the data). The strip's click and every follow-up link open the item's tab.

## The issue is data (reuse this for the next issues)
- `live.md`: the verbatim Notion fetch (saved by script from the fetch result, never retyped).
- `mk_items.py` parses it into `data/items.json`: one record per item with its section, title (top stories), verbatim text and source links. It works on any issue in the same markdown shape.
- `data/annotations.json` adds what the text does not hold: the issue window and strip axis, `glance` (At a glance lead, theme, follow-up heading), the sections with their colours, tab id, tab label, lead paragraph and At a glance line, the knowledge base pages an item feeds, and per item `d` (date), `also` (other dates), `dnote`, `tags`, `numbers` (value, unit, kind), `hn` (points and comments, kept separate), `notes` (`check`, `corr`, `link`, `unc`, `rel` for cross-tab links), `follow` (id of a follow-up) and `viz_after` (which visual part follows the item). `followups` holds the dated "What happened next" entries.
- `mk_issue.py` renders `parts/03_issue.html` (the tab bar and all section tabs, readable without JavaScript) and `parts/11_data.js` (the same data for the week strip) and includes `parts/v_<name>.html` after an item when `viz_after` names it.
- Reusable unchanged for another issue: `mk_items.py`, `mk_issue.py`, `mk_coverage.py`, `parts/01_css.html`, `parts/10_js_common.js`, `parts/11b_js_jump.js`, `parts/12_js_strip.js` and `parts/v_strip.html` (week strip), `parts/13_js_money.js` (values by kind; edit its small data block), and the templates `parts/02_split.html`, `21_js_rd_common.js`, `25_js_s3.js`, `05z_errbox.js.html`, `99_js_tabs.js` (storage key `tn0824-tab`). Issue-specific: `parts/v_*.html` with `14_js_harness.js`, `16_js_mem.js` (memory and image-token calculator) and `31_js_sc_diff.js` (the 3D stage).
- For the next issue: copy `src/`, save its `live.md`, write its `annotations.json` (dates, short labels are in `SHORT` in `mk_issue.py`), drop or replace the issue-specific visuals in `build.sh`, build, then `python3 mk_coverage.py`.

## Files

- `coverage.json` (from `mk_coverage.py`): every item, its numbers and source links checked against the built page; the one dropped link (Notion's auto-link of AGENTS.md); corrections and follow-ups.

## The 2026-11-13 layout rollout (split layout, renderer v2 stage, autoplay)

The issue's words, checks, corrections and follow-ups are unchanged; only the layout, the visual boxes and their motion changed.
- **Split in `mk_issue.py`, never in the generated part.** An item with a visual (`viz_after`) becomes `.s3sp`: the item card (and, with `"viz_with": [n]`, earlier items of the same section; Industry puts General Intuition and Micro1 together beside the money chart) in `.s3L`, the visual in `.s3R`. A visual part is cut by markers: `<!--L-->` the visual's explanation for the left column, `<!--R-->` the right column, `<!--A-->` anything after the split, `<!--RFIRST-->` to put the right column first (animations and stages, so on a phone they sit at the top). A part without markers is included whole, full width, as before. At a glance puts the lead and theme beside the week strip. Full-width item cards and section rows are capped at 60em on the widened page.
- **Splits**: At a glance (lead and theme | week strip); Top stories (the harness item | What the real runs measured, then the harness explanation and step lists | the harness animation); Models (DeepSeek vision item | image-token calculator); Research (DiffusionGemma item, explanation, step list and sliders | 3D stage); Industry (General Intuition and Micro1 | money by kind); Compute (memory prices item | the two price tables). Every other item, and Dev tools, Talk of the town and Further reading, stay full width.
- **One 3D stage**, `31_js_sc_diff.js` (`#stg-diff`, replacing the 2D three-lane grids of `15_js_diff.js`): the same 256-token block decoded autoregressively, by speculative decoding and by DiffusionGemma, each lane 16 x 16 cells whose bars rise to the forward pass in which the token became final (step 5: to milliseconds at the measured speeds). A glass plate behind each lane stands for the model; dots stream from it while the lane is decoding, more for more tokens per pass. Five steps, the pass clock grouped into stretches (long-replay rule): the start, passes 1 to 12 (camera close on the diffusion lane), 13 to 57, 58 to 256, then milliseconds. Numbers: the report's as the issue's check quotes them (256, about 12 and at most 48 passes, 3 to 6 kept per pass from a draft of 8, 1,479 and 303 tokens per second); the settling order, revised cells and the 4.5 default are illustrative (said under the list). The two sliders stay, on the left; moving one stops the stage and rewrites the numbers in the step list.
- **The harness animation stays 2D** (a turn timeline and three panels read best flat), now on `RD.anim` from the template: three step lists (`sx-hs-alone`, `sx-hs-statem`, `sx-hs-avo`, the captions moved into them), one shown per wrapper; a step reached by moving forward draws its turns one by one. Plays once half in view, stops on any control or at the last step, never under reduced motion; switching wrapper swaps the list and replays.
- **Kept 2D**: the week strip (a timeline), money by kind (values on one axis plus separate rows), memory prices (log-scale lines), the image-token calculator (a product of two numbers), the measured harness scores (paired bars per benchmark).
- `build.sh` finds the repo root by walking up to `.venv` or `html_utils` (no git), and writes its long-dash check as bytes. Long dashes in `inputs/` extracts were replaced with `--`.
- `check_stages.mjs` (from the repo root): the stage and the animation in every mode at 1400 light, 920 light and 390 dark (autoplay on view, one list item per step, last step reached, stops after a control, flows stop off screen), splits two columns at 1400, no errors, NaN or sideways scroll, reduced motion, and the stage's step-list numbers against the scene's arithmetic at three slider settings.

Length: "N min to read all tabs" went from 21 (as last built) to 26 (5,909 words). The growth is the step lists: the harness's three lists (two hidden until their wrapper is chosen, about 3 minutes of the count) and the stage's list. Nothing was cut; a tech-news issue has no ledger.
