Responses, in date order

checkpoint-forensics-repro: a CPU-only reimplementation

Peter J. Liu (GitHub peterjliu), 12 November 2026, independent replication: Written from PyTorch's source and posted about 85 minutes after the report, before the author's code. A blind search of all 2^32 seeds finds one hit per model at the report's layouts: V4-Flash at the report's seed, MiniMax-M3 (2760) and Ling-3.1-flash (3952), seeds the report never printed. It also reads V4-Pro and K3.1-Pro from untouched rows (1,831 and 317 sigma), far stronger for V4-Pro than the report's 49. It takes the SM-to-chip table from the report and does not test the trained-weight trace, the AMD test or the GPU counts.

Hacker News: one Blackwell is enough to initialise a model

adrian_b and loneboat, Hacker News, 13 November 2026, discussion: The commonest objection: draw the weights on one developer's Blackwell, train elsewhere. The report states this limit itself; its own facts (Kimi K3's 64 seeded processes, MiniMax-M3's four seeded shards) point to initialising inside a sharded job, which weighs against one machine without excluding it.

What it rests on

PyTorch: DistributionTemplates.h (calc_execution_policy and the grid-stride kernel)

PyTorch (maker): Lines 49 to 61 set the grid to min(SMs times maxThreadsPerMultiProcessor / 256, the tensor's size / 256) blocks of 256 threads; lines 73 to 81 give thread idx Philox subsequence idx and its values at idx, idx + G, idx + 2G and so on. The whole fingerprint is in these 30 lines.

(10 min)

Parallel Random Numbers: As Easy as 1, 2, 3 (Philox)

Salmon, Moraes, Dror and Shaw, SC 2011 (maker): The counter-based generator behind cuRAND's Philox4x32-10: any thread can compute its own stream from (key, counter) with no shared state, which is why the numbers are identical on every chip and only their placement differs.

DeepInfer (DeepSeek's inference engine), README

DeepSeek (maker): Supported hardware: NVIDIA SM100 GPUs and Huawei Ascend 950; benchmarks measured on SM100 GPUs. Independent of the forensics, and about serving, not training.
