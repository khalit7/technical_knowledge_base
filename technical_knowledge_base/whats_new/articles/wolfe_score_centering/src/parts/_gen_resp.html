• earlier analysisBaselines and Score Centering in Policy Gradient Estimation
    Shuo Han and Ziqing Hu, 15 October 2026

    Treats both the group-mean baseline and score centering as centering in a covariance estimate (a sample mean against a population mean). With sequence-level importance sampling, score centering adds nothing once the group-mean baseline is used; with token-level importance sampling and a clipped or masked ratio, the group baseline alone is biased and score centering is needed. The article, which centres token-level scores throughout, does not cite it.

  • reader comments, answeredThree readers' questions, answered by the author
    readers, Substack comments, 9 November 2026

    On the 30B result: uncorrected training was stable at FP8, and Wolfe notes FP8 is the common setting (Qwen3 releases FP8 checkpoints). On mixture-of-experts models: score centering does not cover different expert choices, so keep routing replay (the paper did not replay routing at 30B and says it should be). On stale rollouts: stack importance sampling for the version lag and centering for the precision gap, "probably", as the paper recommends.

  • experimentRegularized Score Centering
    Hao Bai, Chenlu Ye, Xuanchang Zhang, Aviral Kumar, Tong Zhang, 16 November 2026

    Finds two failure modes: centering from the top 128 tokens plus a modelled tail scored below Monte Carlo centering (auxiliary tokens drawn from the sampler) in every tested variant (math runs single-seed), and score centering fell behind when the sampler was refreshed rarely or on agentic tasks such as Crafter, where a reverse-KL penalty toward the sampler (their RSC) helped most. On math at the same refresh interval, adding the penalty changed final accuracy by under 1 point.

  • alternativeGumbel Substitution: Removing Sampler Drift from RL Without Trusting the Sampler
    Romain Fanucci and Malik El Hassani, 20 November 2026

    Accepts the drift diagnosis and removes the need for sampler-reported probabilities, which every correction including score centering trusts: with shared Gumbel noise the trainer recomputes the token its own policy would have drawn. On a 2B model under strong mismatch it was comparable to masked importance sampling with score centering given the true q.
