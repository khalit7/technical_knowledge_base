• Noise records at 10M tokens (archived) (22 October 2026): In ten days outside entrants took the validation-loss track from 4.167 (Dust with Adam) to 3.676, past backprop with Adam (3.769), and the speedrun to loss 4.2 from 1 h 46 min to 1.6 s. The fast entries replace Dust's random draws with structured probes (a Hadamard cycle measuring every neuron's credit at every token; credit handed down from the top of the network), allowed because a measured error may take one step through a nonlinearity: Dust's successors, not Dust.
  • Noise at 30M tokens (25 November 2026): From 22 October the benchmark trains on 30M tokens. Dust with Adam at 24k draws scores 3.767 in 5 h 17 min against backprop with Adam's 3.419; the best forward-only entry, 3.300, is below that, though backprop with a stronger optimiser (ANVIL II) scores 3.340. One run each, scored by the repository's own script.
  • Q Labs Dust and Noise: is the 4,000x claim verified? (22 October 2026): Checks the viral "4,000x faster" figure: the arithmetic holds (1.76 h to 1.6 s), but it is a community speedrun to a loose target (4.2, where backprop with Adam reaches 3.769), and "the fast entries are not Dust as published".
