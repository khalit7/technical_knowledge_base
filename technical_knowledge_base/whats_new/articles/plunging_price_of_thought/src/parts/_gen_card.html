The plunging price of thought

Luke Emberson, David Roodman (Epoch AI) · published 22 September 2026

In one line: On five benchmarks (AIME, FrontierMath tiers 1-3, GPQA Diamond, chess and a secret game's puzzles), from 2023 to mid-2026, the cheapest per-question cost of reaching a fixed score (tokens used times archived list prices, lower budgets simulated by truncating runs) fell about 47% a quarter, 13x a year, averaged over a lattice of dates and scores; 43% to 53% by benchmark, with no uncertainty reported by design.

Read the exact 47% and the technology multiples as one averaging choice among several, the article's dated examples as partly wrong (o3 is dated to o3-mini's release), and "fastest right after SOTA" as true on three of five benchmarks.
