The article page for Divyansh Agrawal's research post on divagr.com, https://divagr.com/blog/qwenputer (published 2026-10-22, unchanged since the first archived copy of 28 October). Built 2026-11-27: the post's argument in its order, a live delta-rule RAM toy, the benchmark redrawn with intervals, and its claims checked against the released design log (`experimental/riscv/docs/rv32.md`) and the repository's commit history.
