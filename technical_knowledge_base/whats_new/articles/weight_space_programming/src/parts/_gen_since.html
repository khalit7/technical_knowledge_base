• WeightBench: Evaluating Language Models as Weight-Space Programmers (arXiv 2611.18553) (13 November 2026): 42 tasks in five tracks on networks of 2K to 7B weights: agents read small networks and write control policies, but cannot yet patch language models, compress without data, or tell an impossible edit from a hard one. Macro score: Claude Opus 5.5 0.535; GPT-6.1-sol 0.568 against Opus 0.546 on the 38 tasks both ran.
  • Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights (arXiv 2611.21885) (16 November 2026): An open 27B model, with its weights, its graded answers and the ability to run itself, edits its own weights: +6.6 points on unseen problems; only the largest model of one family improves, smaller ones make no edit or break themselves.
  • TrojanMaxxing: One-shotting VLM Backdoors with Weight-Space Programming (arXiv 2611.27425) (20 November 2026): Claude Opus 5.5 and Mythos 5.2 agents implant backdoors in vision-language models by editing weights, with no training: attack success 93% to 100% on content moderation, 78% to 100% on OCR, clean accuracy kept.
