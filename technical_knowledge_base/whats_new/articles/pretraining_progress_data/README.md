The article page for Dwarkesh Patel and Jerry Han's research post, https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/pretraining-progress-is-mostly-data (Dwarkesh Podcast, published 2026-09-08). Built 2026-11-27: the post's experiment in its order (seven yearly recipes crossed with seven yearly corpora, compute multipliers on OLMES), every multiplier recomputed from the data behind the post's Datawrapper charts, the finding that the two gains overlap in compute (15x together against a 45x product), the evidence judged, the responses on X and Substack, and what the owner pages (Pretraining, the data-curation root, the training root, Quality classifiers) should change. `index.html` is the whole page, built from `src/` (see `src/README.md`), published with the `sync-kb-github` skill.
