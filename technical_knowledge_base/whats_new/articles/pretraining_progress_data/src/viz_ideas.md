# Visuals: built and rejected

## Built
- **Hero: the four corners as a square, a compute multiplier on each edge, all at one score.** The left and bottom edges are the post's 3.7x and 12.0x, the diagonal its joint 15x; the top and right edges (4.05x, 1.25x), recomputed, show at a glance that the gains overlap. The page's main correction in one picture.
- **The four-corner curves with a reference-budget slider** (the live ingredient): a multiplier as a horizontal gap, the crossings marked, the three multipliers read out. Defaults reproduce 3.7x and 12.0x independently; moving the reference shows the multipliers are level-dependent (the recipe is 0.66x at 3.16e17).
- **Per-year multipliers of both axes** (the post's two tables on one log axis with their intervals), with rings where the recomputation from the curves differs: shows when each gain arrived and that the corpus table does not reproduce.
- **The 7 x 7 grid as a heatmap**, with a toggle to the additive fit's residuals: the corpus columns dominate; residuals look like noise.

## Rejected
- Redrawing the post's two per-axis curve charts (14 curves): the per-year figure carries what they show; the post's interactive versions are better for browsing.
- A slider for the number of seeds or a noise simulation of the grid: the bootstrap numbers fit in one sentence.
- A 3D surface of the grid: a heatmap reads the same, faster.
- An Ho et al. comparison chart: two rates and four differences fit a small table.
