Responses, in date order

Why partial RoPE at all, if the memory win is so small?

a reader, Substack comments, 16 November 2026, reader comment: Asks whether a quality reason or legacy code explains partial RoPE in 2026 models. The author answers that he knows of no study; two readers then point to MLA, where rotating only a small decoupled key lets the compressed latent stay compressed (the key up-projection can be absorbed into the query). That is the better reason, and the knowledge base already teaches it.

(3 min)

Check the loaded rope settings against config.json

a reader, Substack comments, 16 November 2026, reader comment: When Llama 3.1 shipped with rope_type llama3, older transformers versions refused it, and a workaround rewrote it as dynamic, which loads but runs a different scaling. The author replies that this scaling took him a long time to get right in LitGPT.

What about PoPE, which removes RoPE's content and position cross term?

a reader, Substack comments, 18 November 2026, reader comment: Points to Gopalakrishnan et al.'s polar coordinate positional embedding (arXiv 2509.10534, v3 June 2026), which argues RoPE entangles what and where; another reader notes its code is released. The author did not reply by 27 November.

What it rests on

RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding

Su et al., 2021 (maker): The RoPE paper; the article's Section 3 follows its Section 3.3 with smaller numbers and the split-half layout instead of the paper's interleaved one.

(40 min)

The Impact of Positional Encoding on Length Generalization in Transformers

Kazemnejad et al., 2023 (maker): NoPE: the construction behind the article's 1/prefix-length toy in Section 6.2, the ten-task length-generalisation ranking (NoPE first) and the 1.3B code model that degrades at the longest lengths.

(40 min)

Fractional Rotation, Full Potential? Investigating Performance and Convergence of Partial RoPE

Khan, Gummadi, Gupta, Ravichander, 2026 (maker): The paper behind Section 6.1: rotating about 10% of each head converged like full RoPE in their runs; NoPE alone can cause loss spikes, which minimal RoPE or QK-Norm mitigate. It frames the gain as RoPE-cache memory, which the article shows is small.

(35 min)

Round and Round We Go! What makes Rotary Positional Encodings useful?

Barbero et al., 2024 (third-party analysis): The quality argument for partial RoPE the article leaves out: Gemma uses the fastest pairs for positional patterns and prefers the lowest frequencies, which the authors suspect carry semantic information.

(40 min)

What Language Model to Train if You Have One Million GPU Hours?

Le Scao et al. (BigScience), 2022 (maker): The true source of the appendix's 1.3B zero-shot comparison of learned, rotary and ALiBi positions (Table 2), which chose ALiBi for BLOOM.
