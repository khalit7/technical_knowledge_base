What it rests on

A Reduction of Imitation Learning and Structured Prediction to No-Regret Online Learning (DAgger)

Ross, Gordon and Bagnell, AISTATS 2011 (third-party): The method the self-repair step adapts: train on the states the learner reaches, labelled by an expert. Here the recorded session plays the expert.
