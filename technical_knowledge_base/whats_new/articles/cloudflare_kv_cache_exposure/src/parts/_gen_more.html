Since the article (the engine and the fix, checked 27 November 2026)

PR #45973: failed NIXL and MORI sends no longer acknowledged as drained

23 October 2026: The last fix in SGLang's disclosure timeline, merged 23 October and listed in the v0.5.23 release notes (2 November): the race's protection for the two transfer backends #43719 did not cover.

Draft #46435: optionally zero KV pages on free

17 November 2026: Opened by Kevin Flansburg on 25 October (an opt-in flag that zeroes freed pages, so a read of a page a request does not own returns zeros, not another request's KV; MLA and DSA pools only) and closed unmerged on 17 November. No merged zero-on-free by 27 November.

Cloudflare's #38704: honor admission aborts before KV handoff

10 October 2026: Closed unmerged on 10 October; #38935 took its admission approach, and the transferred-length check it added was declined upstream.

What it rests on

Issue #30233: aborted prefill requests (input too long) cause decode to generate garbage from incomplete KV cache

an SGLang user, 6 July 2026 (third-party): The same bug, reported seven weeks before the launch on a GLM-5.2 deployment with a 1,048,576-token context and a 284,928-token pool, with the root cause traced line by line and a proposed patch; closed for inactivity on 5 September.

(10 min)

PR #36988: Retire aborted disaggregated prefill results

SGLang, merged 6 September 2026 (maker): Written for a failing vision encoder, not this incident; it retires an aborted request when its prefill result returns, so its stub KV never reaches decode. The change that stops the exposure, released in v0.5.20 on 18 September.

(15 min)

PR #38935: Do not admit intake-rejected requests to a PD handoff

SGLang, merged 16 September 2026 (maker): Retires a rejected request at both admission doors. Its description calls itself "a waste-and-latency fix, not a correctness fix": after #36988 the stale-KV symptom no longer reproduced on main. Its tests cover the doors, re-entering preempted requests, and single-node mode left alone.

(15 min)

Smaller, faster, safer: running Kimi and GLM at scale

Cloudflare, 3 August 2026 (maker): The earlier post describing the disaggregated GLM deployment and the page-tag integrity check (under 1% overhead), which the incident post says was disabled after mid-August tests.
