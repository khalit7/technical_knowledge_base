Language Models for Text Classification: From Bag-of-Words to Jev

Sebastian Raschka · published 29 September 2026

In one line: A free long explainer (about 36 minutes, 39 figures, linked code) that follows text classification on IMDb reviews from bag-of-words logistic regression (89.9%) through LSTMs, CNNs and fine-tuned transformers to TypeSafe's Jev decision model, which scored 96.47% on the 25,000 test reviews with no IMDb training for $0.65 in one run by the author; it sketches a Jev-like API on any transformer and relates Jev's unpublished training method to RLCR, a published reward for calibrated confidence.

How far to trust it: A clear, correct history and an honest hands-on test of Jev; read the IMDb numbers as single runs on three different test sets of a benchmark Jev may have seen. The Jev-like design and the guess of a small ModernBERT-like model are not how Jev works: earlier measurements show it reads its options together and has frontier-scale knowledge, and the benchmark the article cites found Jev badly calibrated on one of three datasets.
