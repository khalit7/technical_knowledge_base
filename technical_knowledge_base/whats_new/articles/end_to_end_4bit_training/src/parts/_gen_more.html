End-to-End 4-Bit Training

Its interactive figures (the bit-level NVFP4 widget, the 16 x 16 tile figures, the Hadamard scatter) are worth opening there; this page redraws none of them.

Responses, in date order

4bit-training-b200: 4-bit training, tested on one NVIDIA B200

Raj Dandekar and Vizuara AI Labs, 15 November 2026, replication: An 840M GPT on TinyStories, 2,000 steps: NVFP4 forward with an 8-bit backward reached validation loss 1.6912 and 1.6910 (two seeds) against 1.6886 and 1.6873 in BF16; steps 1.006 to 1.035 times faster than all-8-bit at widths 2,048 to 8,192. Backward FP8 with per-tensor scales, not MXFP8; no Four Over Six.

(15 min)

[Feature] End-to-end 4-bit MoE training: NVFP4 forward, MXFP8 backward, quantized EP comm (radixark/miles #4952)

HJSang (Miles, the RL framework), 19 November 2026, port plan: Plans the recipe in six phases for Miles (Megatron, Transformer Engine); the stochastic MXFP8 backward needs a Transformer Engine fork. Its current Inkling trainer runs experts in BF16 with fake-quantised weights, part of a 0.019 train-rollout log-probability gap. Open, no pull request, on 27 November.

(10 min)

looped-transformer-4bit: replicating Inkling 2 Mini with 4-bit forward passes

Saurabh Ghatnekar, 23 November 2026, replication: A 10.6M model on TinyStories, laptop, emulated: 4-bit forward with MXFP8 backward 1.214 nats per byte at step 300 against 1.196 in full precision (one run each). Rounds the backward re-quantisation to nearest, because Transformer Engine's MXFP8 quantiser has no stochastic option.

(15 min)

RL on the released NVFP4 weights (a comment on miles #4952)

LejsDeen, 24 November 2026, question: RL on Inkling 2 Mini from the release weights (fp32 master, fake-quantised experts): Adam steps of about 1e-6 against NVFP4 grid steps of 1e-3 to 1e-2, so almost no expert code flips, and learning about as slow as with experts frozen. Asks for full-precision master weights; unanswered on 27 November.

(5 min)

Since the article (papers that build on it)

Inkling 2 Small model card

12 November 2026: Apache 2.0 weights, NVFP4 the main release and an MXFP8 checkpoint beside it; no full-precision weights. It says the model "was pretrained, midtrained and post-trained in NVFP4", which is shorter than the post: only the expert layers' forward matmuls were NVFP4.

(10 min)

Inkling 2 Small in NVIDIA NeMo AutoModel

27 November 2026: Lists the NVFP4 release as NVFP4 experts in layers 0 to 39 and MXFP8 in layers 40 to 47: the last 8 of 48 layers, 16.7%, the post's "last 16.6%". The native implementation is not yet merged.

(5 min)

The Miles port (radixark/miles #4952)

27 November 2026: The first open-source framework to plan the full recipe; open with no linked pull request when checked.

What it rests on

Pretraining Large Language Models with NVFP4

NVIDIA, September 2025 (maker of the format): The recipe this one departs from: NVFP4 in all three matmuls of a 12B model for 10T tokens, with 16 x 16 weight tiles, Hadamard transforms on the weight gradient and stochastic rounding on gradients.

(40 min)

Quartet II: Accurate LLM Pre-Training in NVFP4 by Improved Unbiased Gradient Estimation

Panferov et al., 2026 (third-party): Re-quantises the forward pass's tensors for the backward pass so the gradient estimate stays unbiased; one of the two works the post credits.

(40 min)

TetraJet-v2: Accurate NVFP4 Training for Large Language Models with Oscillation Suppression and Outlier Control

Chen et al., 2025 (third-party): The other credited source of re-quantisation from the forward tensors.

(40 min)

Four Over Six: More Accurate NVFP4 Quantization with Adaptive Block Scaling

Cook et al., 2025 (the post's author is its first author): Lets each block choose whether its largest value maps to 4 or to 6, whichever loses less; the recipe uses it on activations, not weights.

(30 min)

NVFP4 for RL (Four Over Six for RL weights and activations)

Ziang Li and others at humans&, July 2026 (third-party): The write-up the post recommends: a dequantised backward, Four Over Six on weights too, and selected layers in high precision removed gradient-norm spikes in five RL runs.

(20 min)

Stable FP4 Training via Transposition-Invariant Block Quantization

Rahimifar et al., July 2026 (third-party): Fixes the same transpose mismatch the other way, with 2D blocks that are the same in both directions, plus stochastic rounding: under 1.3% degradation up to 7B dense and a 30B MoE, 100B tokens. Not cited by the post.

(35 min)

Recipes for Pre-training LLMs with MXFP8

Mishra et al., 2025 (NVIDIA): The 8-bit baseline every number in the post is measured against.

(30 min)

OCP Microscaling Formats (MX) Specification v1.0

Open Compute Project, 2023 (maker): MXFP8 and MXFP4: blocks of 32 values sharing one power-of-two (E8M0) scale.

(30 min)
