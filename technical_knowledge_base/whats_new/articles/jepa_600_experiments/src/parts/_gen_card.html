After 600 experiments, here's what I discovered about JEPA world models

Haddy Alchaer (independent) · published 11 November 2026

In one line: On Push-T, in one author's single runs without retraining, LeWM (a 15M-parameter JEPA world model) planned as well as the simulator when both were given expert subgoals every 25 steps (78% against 77%, 250 tasks with the goal 75 steps away); subgoals found in a graph of its own embedded training demonstrations, with a cost on each jump between demonstrations, took such tasks from 22% to 85%. On the author's harder test the best planner reaches 56%. No code released.
