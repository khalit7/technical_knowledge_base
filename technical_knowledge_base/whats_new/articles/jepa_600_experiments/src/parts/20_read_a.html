The idea in one sentence: on Push-T, LeWM's learned predictor plans as well as the simulator over 25 steps, so far goals fail because one search cannot reach 75 steps; chaining short searches through subgoals found among its own embedded training demonstrations lifts 75-step success from 22% to 85%, on a test whose goals come from those demonstrations.

You need: JEPA ({{Contrastive and self-supervised learning|n:3c65c17b0d0d811db6a7d02d0ee7215c}}, the JEPA section); model-predictive control with the cross-entropy method, and compounding model error ({{Model-based RL and planning|n:3ee5c17b0d0d8116be5fd39e10bd94c5}}, sections 11 to 13).

New terms: Push-T (a 2D simulation: a round pusher shoves a T-shaped block to the pose in a goal image); LeWM (LeWorldModel, 2026: a 15M-parameter JEPA world model from pixels whose CEM planner looks 25 steps ahead); subgoal (an intermediate goal image); memory graph (embedded training frames linked into a graph); jump (an edge between two demonstrations); harder test (the author's: start and goal from different demonstrations, block held on target for the last 10 steps).
