# Visuals: built and rejected

## Built
- **Hero: every JOB evaluation in the post on one log axis**, coloured SFT, RL, best of three, with the 1.33x to 1.47x band of repeated searches behind it. It carries the page's main point (training gives about 1.4x; 1.81x is search) and the noise caveat in one picture.
- **One search of the harness** (static flow): where the single feedback timing and the three-pair score sit, which the evidence section relies on.
- **Toy: the no-op fooling rate on job-13b**, exact over every draw, timings per side and tie zone movable, the post's 20 runs as dots above. Defaults land on the post's "~40%" independently (computed from the runs, nothing fitted).
- **GRPO against anchored advantages on the post's four rollouts** (before/after on the same input), recomputed.

## Rejected
- Redrawing the post's search-space counter or calibration queue: the post's own versions are better and the numbers fit in a sentence.
- A per-query scatter of the 113 pooled results from the repository CSV: tempting, but the question it answers (is the gain a few outliers?) is answered by one number from the author's analysis (1.61x without the top five).
- A 3D view of anything: nothing here has spatial structure.
- A toy of the reward formula with a slider for speedup: the soft threshold is one sentence.
