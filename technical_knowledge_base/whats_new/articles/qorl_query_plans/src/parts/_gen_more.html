Responses, in date order

The repository's results for the final run (experiment 033)

Rohan Bansal (the author), 14 September 2026, author's analysis: Separates the model's choice (1.4048x), the within-search rule (1.4355x) and best-of-three (1.8054x); notes that 1.805x "is not an 80.5% latency reduction", that comparing it with a one-search SFT baseline "would mix policy improvement with additional search compute", that JOB "has been inspected during development", and that only 17 queries win in all three searches.

An 8 GB in-memory dataset, warmed, read-only

refibrillator, Hacker News (the top comment), 16 September 2026, critique: Warns of overfitting and asks whether the plans would hold at scale and on more realistic workloads; database statistics and workloads drift.

(2 min)

Planning time is the constraint a live planner works under

perrygeo and Someone, Hacker News, 16 September 2026, critique: A real planner must minimise planning plus execution on live statistics, and the 95 hours of training and the search are outside the benchmark numbers. The post's own framing (repeated analytic queries) answers part of this.

(2 min)

pg_hint_plan's debug log shows whether a hint was used

polyphilz (the author), Hacker News, 16 September 2026, author's reply: Answering whether a hinted plan still computes the query: the hints only choose among plans for the same SQL, and the debug log was used during evaluation to confirm Postgres applied them. The author's only comment in the thread.

(1 min)

Skeptical of these results (random_page_cost)

zacmps, Hacker News, 17 September 2026, critique: The model's favourite setting, random_page_cost=1.1, suggests the baseline's cost settings were not tuned for fast storage, which "could easily explain the difference by itself". The repository confirms the baseline ran at the default 4.0; no reply.

What it rests on

How Good Are Query Optimizers, Really?

Leis et al., PVLDB 2015 (third-party): The Join Order Benchmark and the evidence that cardinality estimates, not cost models, decide plan quality; the test set here.

(1 hour)

Bao: Making Learned Query Optimization Practical

Marcus et al., SIGMOD 2021 (third-party): The same idea with a small model: keep the planner and learn which hint set to give it per query. The post lists a Bao-style sweep as its next experiment; no comparison was run.

(45 min)

Flow-Loss: Learning Cardinality Estimates That Matter

Negi et al., PVLDB 2021 (third-party): Introduces the Cardinality Estimation Benchmark, the 13,646 training queries.

(45 min)

pg_hint_plan

NTT OSS Center (maker): The extension the model writes for: scan, join, Leading, Rows, Parallel and Set hints.

(15 min)

DeepSeekMath (GRPO)

Shao et al., 2024 (third-party): Where GRPO's group-normalised advantage comes from; the anchored variant here drops the standard deviation and uses a leave-one-out mean floored at zero.
