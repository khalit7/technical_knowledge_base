GPT-6 Astra, Looped Transformers, and Hidden Reasoning

Sebastian Raschka · published 9 September 2026

In one line: An explainer of looped transformers written against GPT-6 Astra's reported use of them: reusing a stack of blocks saves weights but not compute or KV cache, so Raschka argues a looped model behaves like a bigger one and does not by itself hide its chain of thought. It rests on published papers and on a reading of OpenAI's system card, not on any measurement of Astra.

his analogy: shorter traces come from capability, not concealment · in the article: section 5.2

How far to trust it: Believe the mechanics, which we checked against Nanbeige4.2-3B's config and the papers. Treat "looping does not hide reasoning" as half right: it does not remove the written chain, but the equivalence with a bigger model breaks when keys and values are shared across passes, and later measurements found looping raises reasoning without a chain of thought more than it raises general ability.
