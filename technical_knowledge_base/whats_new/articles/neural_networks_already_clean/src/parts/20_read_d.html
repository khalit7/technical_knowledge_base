How much of this to believe

What holds up. The strongest part is the author overturning his own canonical examples with mechanisms anyone can check against the essay's microscope: the V neuron's negative pole and its flame inputs, the car circuit's readers seen through guided feature visualization, InceptionV1's last layer read again and retrained. The squeezing experiments test a clear prediction (Figure 1) and came out the way generalization predicts. Exceptions are reported rather than hidden: the bow-tie neuron, the unsettled Korean firings, the 30 October softening of "it's a V-shape neuron". The near-miss test wrote its examples before measuring and includes a distant-concept control. Outside checks so far point the same way in direction: Julian Bradshaw's spot checks of the 1,000 released Qwen3 neurons found the claims "hold up reasonably well", and Jasper Blank's GPT-2 XL rerun found a modern model explains far more neurons than GPT-4 did (8% against 0.3% on the 2023 metric).

Where the evidence is weaker than the wording.

  • The 90% cannot be checked from the essay. It comes from "preliminary audits" with no rubric, sample size or judge stated, and the essay itself says thresholds move it. The companion paper's measured figure, on a different model, is 69% of 5,468 neuron poles dominated by one mode (47% with exactly one). "1.7% in 2023, 90% now", as secondary coverage put it, compares different models, judges and definitions (Figure 6).
  • "No superposition" is argued from not finding it, with a narrow definition. The essay concedes representing more things than dimensions; Nanda's point that memorised facts outnumber neurons and may sit in weak activations is answered with an argument (rare facts ride related circuits) and the umbrella experiment, not a measurement in a language model.
  • Weak activations: cheap for loss, not for reasoning. The essay's evidence is mostly vision (69.47% against 70.08%) and one Qwen neuron. The companion paper measures that keeping only the strongest 7.8% of routed activations in Qwen3.6-35B-A3B moves web-text loss from 1.886 to 1.940, but reasoning collapses into loops until a light fine-tune, which it ties to the double-negative quadrant. leogao reports that ablating small activations substantially degrades models (his data not yet shared).
  • Agents judging agents, on the authors' own models. Neuron papers, labels, near-miss texts and their scoring are LLM-written; the ConvNeXts and their squeezed variants were trained by the author, and no code is released. leogao's objection applies: a pattern that fits the top examples can still miss which inputs of the pattern never fire the neuron, which a downstream-KL simulation would catch (the near-miss test covers part of this, at five texts each way).
  • Recursive generalization explains after the fact. The Colorado neuron that also fires on the Python framework Tornado is causally real (asked where Tornado was developed, Qwen3-32B gives Colorado 30% and California 0.7%) though rare (about 1.2% of the neuron's effect, companion paper). It fits "rare, unimportant offshoots", but no one predicted the combination; the essay says it has not tested recursive generalization across a whole model.

Verdict. The diagnosis of the old examples is convincing and the method lessons (both poles, circuits, guided visualization, explanations that can lose) are worth adopting now. "Already clean" for frontier language models is a well-argued working hypothesis, as the essay's own byline calls it, not a measured result: the numbers that would decide it (what share of a frontier model's computation its neurons explain, measured by downstream effect) are only beginning to appear, in the companion paper, and are lower and more qualified than the essay's headline.

What the responses said

Hacker News, submitted 15 October 2026 as "Neural networks are clean": 849 points and 344 comments (read 27 November). Substantive threads: the claims overreach what is shown; "cats and cars" against "V" may only rename the concept; the Zoom In error deserved a formal correction; and the 90% does not cover emergent behaviour spread across many neurons ({{thread|https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50105647}}). The LessWrong linkpost (220 karma, 51 comments) carries most expert replies, and Julian Bradshaw's analysis there of the Colorado neuron from the essay's public data: about 87% of its activations above half its maximum are directly about Colorado, and the Tornado firings are small (about 8.6% of maximum in the example he traced).

Where it moves the knowledge base

The knowledge base has no interpretability page and is not getting one; the essay touches the pages below.

  • {{Activation functions|n:3c65c17b0d0d819f8049c255e6f206e2}} (gated units): SwiGLU's double-negative quadrant (gate and value both negative give a positive output, at most about 0.278 × |value|) dominates the top activations of about 4% of Qwen3-32B neurons and about a third of all activations in Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, and is tied to long reasoning; a property of the gate the page does not yet name.
  • {{Activation functions|n:3c65c17b0d0d819f8049c255e6f206e2}} (Inside real models, "sparse activations"): in a large SwiGLU MoE most weak activations are functionally removable for loss (strongest 7.8% kept: 1.886 to 1.940 on web text) but not for reasoning.
  • {{Mixture of experts|n:3fd5c17b0d0d81fda06bf955af7c2b58}} (what the router learned): beside "routing tracks the token, not the topic", the essay's agent-judged finding that Qwen3.6's 10,240 experts are narrowly themed and narrow with depth, and its argument that experts resist superposition. Different measures (router predictability from the token against what an expert's neurons respond to), so an extension, not a contradiction.
  • {{The transformer from zero|n:3fd5c17b0d0d8174980ddde4a20c7b3c}}: a feed-forward hidden unit has a negative pole that carries meaning (Santa lives on one) and can act as a gate between routes.
  • {{Sparks of Weight-Space Programming|n:4255c17b0d0d81c4923ac58982f35f2f}}: the two articles meet at steering; that page's Qwen edit was a direction written into one matrix, this essay checks single neurons by steering them.
