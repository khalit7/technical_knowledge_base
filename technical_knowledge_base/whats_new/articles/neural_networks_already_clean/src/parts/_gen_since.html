• Neurons as an Interpretable Basis (Goodfire, the companion paper) (10 November 2026): Tigges, Kowal, McGrath, Lewis and Cammarata on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (about 5 million neurons): keeping the strongest 320 of 4,096 routed neuron activations per token moves web-text loss from 1.886 to 1.940 (reasoning needs a light fine-tune to recover); 47% of 5,468 neuron poles have one mode and 69% are dominated by one mode; judged descriptions detect firing with 95% accuracy against random text. Its appendix answers the Colorado and Tornado counterexample: about 98.8% of the neuron's effect is places, the Tornado mode is causally real but rare.
